USCIS Announces FY2027 Inflation Adjustment to H.R. 1 Immigration Fees

Published Oct 1, 2026Effective Oct 16, 2026

USCIS has published the Fiscal Year 2027 inflation adjustment to immigration fees established under H.R. 1 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act). The adjustment reflects a 3.36 percent increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) between July 2025 and July 2026. The new fees take effect for requests postmarked on or after October 16, 2026.

Key points

  • The I-765 fee for an initial asylum applicant EAD rises from $560 to $570.
  • Initial TPS EAD and initial parole EAD fees also rise from $560 to $570.
  • The I-821 TPS fee increases from $510 to $520, and the I-131 immigration parole fee increases from $1,020 to $1,050.
  • Fees such as the initial I-589 asylum application fee, the I-360 SIJ fee, the I-94 fee, and renewal or extension EAD fees remain unchanged.
  • Requests postmarked on or after October 16, 2026 without the correct fee amount will be rejected.

Who is affected

Asylum applicants, TPS applicants, parolees, and others who pay H.R. 1 immigration fees are affected.

What to do

Anyone filing an affected form on or after October 16, 2026 should check the updated fee table and submit the correct amount.
