DHS Says New York Refused Over 16,000 ICE Detainers Since 2025

Published Oct 12, 2026

The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released data claiming New York State failed to honor 16,070 ICE detainers between January 20, 2025 and October 8, 2026. DHS says these detainers were linked to 52,503 criminal charges or convictions and used the data to criticize New York's sanctuary policies.

Key points

  • DHS reports New York declined 16,070 ICE detainers from January 20, 2025 to October 8, 2026.
  • The agency says these cases involve 52,503 total criminal charges or convictions, including 131 homicides and 9,502 assaults.
  • The release follows a recent vehicle attack on ICE officers in New York City and other publicized incidents involving noncitizens.
  • New York enacted a law in May 2026, effective August 25, 2026, restricting local police cooperation with ICE.
  • This is a DHS political statement and data release, not a new rule, regulation, or legal action.

Who is affected

New York State and City officials, law enforcement agencies, and immigrant communities affected by detainer and sanctuary policies.
