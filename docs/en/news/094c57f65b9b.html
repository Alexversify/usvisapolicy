ICE Announces Arrests of Five Criminal Illegal Aliens Convicted of Assault, Drug and Burglary Offenses

Published Nov 17, 2026

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), part of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), reported on November 17, 2026 that it arrested five individuals described as criminal illegal aliens the previous day. The release states each had prior convictions, including aggravated assault, drug importation and distribution, and burglary.

Key points

  • Jose Antonio Leon-Avalos, from Mexico, was convicted of aggravated assault and attempted indecent assault in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania.
  • Lorena Espinoza-Murillo, from Mexico, was convicted of importing methamphetamine and cocaine in the Southern District of California.
  • Daniel Martinez Espana, from Venezuela, was convicted of conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute narcotics in the District of Puerto Rico.
  • Abraham Valdivia-Valladolid, from Mexico, was convicted of transporting and selling narcotics in Santa Ana, California.
  • Biyou Makaula, from South Africa, was convicted of burglary twice in Las Vegas, Nevada.
  • DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin said the arrests reflect the administration's focus on removing criminal illegal aliens.

Who is affected

The release concerns individuals identified by ICE as criminal illegal aliens with prior convictions.

What to do

No application or filing action is indicated in the source.

WORST OF THE WORST: ICE Arrests Violent Assailants, Drug Traffickers, and Burglars
Release Date: November 17, 2026
Thanks to President Trump and Secretary Mullin, ICE continues to break deportation records
WASHINGTON – Yesterday, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested more worst of the worst criminal illegal aliens, including those convicted for aggravated assault, importation of methamphetamine and cocaine, burglary, and other horrible crimes.
“Yesterday, the heroic men and women of ICE arrested violent assailants, drug traffickers, and burglars from our communities,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Every arrest of a criminal illegal alien saves American lives. America can never return to open borders chaos. The Trump Administration will always put the safety of the American people first.”
Yesterday’s arrests include:
Jose Antonio Leon-Avalos, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for aggravated assault and criminal attempted indecent assault in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania.
Lorena Espinoza-Murillo, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for importation of methamphetamine and cocaine in the Southern District of California.
Daniel Martinez Espana, a criminal illegal alien from Venezuela, convicted for conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute narcotics in the United States District Court of Puerto Rico.
Abraham Valdivia-Valladolid, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for transport/sell narcotics in Santa Ana, California.
Biyou Makaula, a criminal illegal alien from South Africa, convicted for burglary TWICE in Las Vegas, Nevada.
Americans can see more public safety threats arrested in their communities on our webpage at WOW.DHS.Gov.
