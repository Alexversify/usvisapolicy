DHS Highlights Worst Illegal Aliens Arrested in Kansas
Release Date: November 2, 2026
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement highlighting recent examples of the worst illegal aliens arrested in the state of Kansas, including those convicted for crimes such as indecent liberties with a child, aggravated battery, distribution of narcotics, and burglary.
“Every day, the men and women of ICE work tirelessly to arrest and remove illegal aliens from all across the country,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “In the great state of Kansas, ICE has arrested pedophiles, violent assailants, drug traffickers, and burglars. For four years, an open-border administration and sanctuary politicians tried to destroy this country. But the Trump Administration is putting America first again, and we will never apologize for arresting and deporting illegal aliens.”
Recent ICE arrests of illegal aliens in Kansas include:
Daniel Cruz-Garcia, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in Ellsworth on October 23. His criminal history includes convictions for aggravated indecent liberties with a child and sexual intercourse with a child between the ages of 14 and 16.
Juan Pablo Rios-Flores, an illegal alien from Honduras, arrested by ICE in Leavenworth on October 20. His criminal history includes convictions for TWO counts of sexual battery and illegal entry and an arrest for domestic assault.
Orgel Marel Izaguirre Valladares, an illegal alien from Honduras, arrested by ICE in Harvey County on October 28. His criminal history includes convictions for felony aggravated battery knowingly using weapon to cause great bodily harm and felony aggravated assault with intent to commit a felony and an arrest for kidnapping.
Jesus Alejandro Valenzuela Zamarripa, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in Johnson County on October 14. His criminal history includes convictions for distribution of narcotics, fleeing or attempting to elude, criminal damage to property, and driving under the influence.
Javier Esteffano Rifo Luman, an illegal alien from Chile, arrested by ICE on October 7. His criminal history includes a conviction for burglary.
