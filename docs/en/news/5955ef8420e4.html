  • Those deported are from Mexico, Honduras and Guatemala, with ties to gangs including Surenos, Tango Blast and Paisas.

Release Date: October 9, 2026
America is for Americans

DHS is delivering on President Trump’s mandate from the American people to deport illegal aliens, because that’s how we save America.”
