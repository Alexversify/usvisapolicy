ICE Announces Arrests of Illegal Aliens Convicted of Child Abuse, Money Laundering, Drug Trafficking

Published Oct 6, 2026

DHS announced on October 6, 2026 that ICE arrested several illegal aliens with prior convictions, including lewd acts with a child, money laundering, and drug trafficking. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said ICE will continue to find and remove individuals who entered the country illegally and broke the law.

Key points

  • A Mexican national was arrested after conviction for lewd or lascivious acts with a child under 14 in California.
  • A South Korean national was arrested in Boston, Massachusetts for money laundering and related conspiracy charges.
  • A Dominican national was arrested in Massachusetts for trafficking heroin and morphine.
  • A Colombian national was arrested in Texas for manufacture or delivery of a controlled substance.
  • ICE stated that more cases of public safety threats are listed on its WOW.DHS.Gov webpage.

Who is affected

This concerns illegal aliens with criminal convictions who were arrested by ICE, and the public following U.S. immigration enforcement actions.

What to do

No action is required for the general public; those seeking more detail can consult ICE's public case listings.
