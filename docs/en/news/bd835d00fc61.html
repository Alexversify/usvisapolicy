ICE Re-Arrests Illegal Alien Freed by Illinois Officials After Fatal DUI Sentence

Published Nov 13, 2026

DHS announced on November 13, 2026 that ICE arrested Vicente Torres-Vasquez, a Mexican national, on November 11 after Illinois state corrections officials released him on parole without honoring an ICE detainer. Torres-Vasquez had served part of a 25-year sentence for a 2011 DUI crash that killed one person and injured two others.

Key points

  • Torres-Vasquez was convicted in 2012 of aggravated DUI resulting in death and two counts of aggravated DUI resulting in great bodily injury, receiving a 25-year sentence.
  • ICE lodged detainers in January 2017 and again in 2026, but Stateville Correctional Center released him on parole on November 2, 2026 without notifying ICE.
  • ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO) Chicago arrested him on November 11, 2026, and he remains in ICE custody pending removal proceedings.
  • He had previously been arrested four times for DUI, with three convictions, and had entered the U.S. illegally multiple times before receiving a final removal order in 2010.
  • DHS included a statement from Secretary Markwayne Mullin criticizing Illinois sanctuary policies, and the release listed several other recent cases involving illegal aliens in Illinois.

Who is affected

Affects individuals in ICE custody or removal proceedings, and relates to ICE's enforcement interactions with state and local jurisdictions that limit cooperation with immigration detainers.

ICE Arrests Illegal Alien Responsible for Fatal DUI After Illinois Sanctuary Politicians Released Him
Release Date: November 13, 2026
This illegal alien had previously been arrested FOUR TIMES for driving under the influence
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement announcing that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested an illegal alien who had been RELEASED by sanctuary politicians in Illinois, after serving a prison sentence for causing a fatal accident while driving under the influence.
Vicente Torres-Vasquez, an illegal alien from Mexico, was arrested in Wauconda, Illinois after causing a fatal crash on July 24, 2011, while driving under the influence (DUI). His victim was 55-year-old Gregory Errol Homola, a veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps, who was riding his motorcycle when he was struck by Torres-Vasquez. Two other women in another vehicle were also injured in the accident.
The victim: Gregory Errol Homola
Torres-Vasquez was convicted in 2012 for aggravated driving under the influence resulting in the death of another person and TWO counts of aggravated driving under the influence resulting in great bodily injury. He was sentenced to 25 years in prison.
Torres-Vasquez had previously been arrested FOUR TIMES for driving under the influence, being convicted on three of the four counts.
The killer: Vicente Torres-Vasquez
On January 16, 2017, ICE lodged a detainer with the Illinois River Correctional Center (IRCC) in Canton, Illinois. On March 3, 2026, IRCC transferred Torres-Vasquez to the Stateville Correctional Center (SCC) in Crest Hill, Illinois, in anticipation of his parole release date. ICE then lodged another detainer. But on November 2, 2026, SCC refused to honor the ICE detainer and RELEASED him on parole without notifying ICE.
On November 11, ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO) Chicago arrested Vicente Torres-Vasquez at large. He will remain in ICE custody pending removal proceedings.
“This illegal alien was sentenced to 25 years in prison after killing a man while drunk behind the wheel. When ICE lodged a detainer ahead of his release on parole, sanctuary politicians in J.B. Pritzker’s Illinois refused to cooperate with us and released him without notifying ICE,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Thankfully, the brave patriots of ICE managed to arrest this killer anyway, and he will now be deported from our country. These heartless sanctuary politicians need to stop prioritizing political power over the safety of their constituents. They owe it to the Angel Families to work with us so that we can prevent any more American lives from being lost to illegal alien crime.”
Torres-Vasquez illegally entered the United States through Arizona TWICE in March 2004, and was granted voluntary departures both times. He then illegally entered the country for a third time at an unknown date and location. An Immigration Judge issued him a final order of removal on November 11, 2010.

He had been released by sanctuary politicians in Will County who refused to honor ICE’s detainer, and ICE arrested him two days later.
In June, ICE lodged a detainer for Janio Gionel Velasquez-Muro, an illegal alien from Venezuela, after he was arrested for kidnapping and sexually assaulting a 9-year-old girl in Kane County, Illinois.
