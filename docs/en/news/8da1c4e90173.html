DHS Says ICE Rearrested Man After California County Released Him Despite Detainer

Published Nov 19, 2026

DHS said that on November 19, 2026, ICE arrested Ronaldo Barbosa Ortiz, a Mexican national, after Riverside County, California released him instead of honoring an ICE detainer filed on November 10. DHS said Barbosa Ortiz has convictions including child molestation, sexual battery and DUI causing injury, and has been deported four times since a 2009 removal order. The release is part of a new DHS series called "Sanctuary Insanity," which also cites other cases in California and other states.

Key points

  • DHS said ICE lodged a detainer with Riverside County on November 10, 2026, asking it not to release Barbosa Ortiz.
  • According to DHS, Barbosa Ortiz's convictions include molesting a child under 18, sexual battery, and DUI causing bodily injury.
  • DHS said Riverside County released him instead of honoring the detainer, and ICE later arrested him; he remains in ICE custody.
  • DHS said Barbosa Ortiz entered the country illegally, received a final removal order on March 4, 2009, and has been deported four times.
  • Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin criticized California officials and called on jurisdictions nationwide to honor ICE detainers.
  • The release, part of DHS's new "Sanctuary Insanity" series, also lists numerous other cases in California and other states.

Who is affected

Noncitizens with criminal convictions subject to ICE detainers, and local jurisdictions that decide whether to cooperate with them.

SANCTUARY INSANITY: ICE Arrests Previously-Deported Pedophile After He was RELEASED by California Sanctuary Politicians
Release Date: November 19, 2026
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement after U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested an illegal alien who had been RELEASED by California sanctuary politicians despite being convicted for molesting a child, sexual battery, and driving under the influence of alcohol.
On November 10, ICE lodged a detainer asking Riverside County, California authorities not to release Ronaldo Barbosa Ortiz, an illegal alien from Mexico, whose criminal history includes convictions for annoying or molesting a child under 18, sexual battery, driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs causing bodily injury, driving with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.08 percent or more causing bodily injury, driving without a license, and illegal entry.
Ronaldo Barbosa Ortiz
Instead of honoring the ICE detainer, Riverside County officials RELEASED this heinous criminal onto the streets. ICE arrested him after his release, and he remains in ICE custody.
“This illegal alien is a convicted pedophile and drunk driver. Instead of honoring our detainer, California sanctuary politicians chose to RELEASE this pedophile back onto their streets, where he could harm more Americans,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Thanks to the hard work of the brave men and women of ICE, we have arrested this criminal and will remove him from our country. The Department of Homeland Security has a mission to protect the backyard of every American citizen across the country. When sanctuary politicians release criminals like this pedophile back onto the streets, it makes that mission needlessly harder. We are calling on sanctuary politicians not only in California, but across the country, to honor ICE detainers and work with us to secure the Homeland.”
Barbosa Ortiz first illegally entered the United States at an unknown date and location. He was issued a final order of removal by an Immigration Judge on March 4, 2009, and has since been deported FOUR times.
