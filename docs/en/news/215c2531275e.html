Nigerian National Indicted for Falsely Claiming Citizenship to Vote

Published Sep 25, 2026

A federal grand jury in Kansas indicted George Olawunmi Johnson, a Nigerian national and lawful permanent resident, for falsely claiming U.S. citizenship to register and vote in elections. USCIS assisted Homeland Security Investigations in the case.

Key points

  • Johnson allegedly falsely claimed U.S. citizenship when registering to vote in Johnson County, Kansas, in February 2010.
  • He allegedly cast a ballot in November 2024 in a federal election involving president, vice president, Senate, and House races.
  • He faces four charges: false claim to citizenship, false claim to citizenship to vote, fraudulent election conduct, and voting by an alien in a federal election.
  • Homeland Security Investigations led the investigation, with assistance from USCIS.
  • The indictment was announced by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Kansas.

Who is affected

The case concerns a lawful permanent resident accused of falsely claiming U.S. citizenship to vote in federal elections.

What to do

Anyone suspecting immigration benefit fraud or abuse can report it to USCIS using the USCIS Tip Form.
