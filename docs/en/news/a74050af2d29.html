DHS Publicizes Five ICE Arrests of Immigrants in Michigan With Criminal Records

Published Oct 26, 2026

DHS issued a press release describing five noncitizens arrested by ICE in Grand Rapids and Detroit, Michigan between October 12 and 18, 2026, listing their alleged criminal histories and immigration status. The release announces no new policy, fee, or eligibility change.

  • DHS named five individuals from Venezuela, the Philippines, and Guatemala arrested by ICE in Michigan.

  • DHS stated some individuals had previously been released by the Biden Administration after being taken into custody by CBP, or had final removal orders from an immigration judge.

DHS Highlights Worst Illegal Aliens Arrested in Michigan
Release Date: October 26, 2026
If You Seek a Pleasant Peninsula, Deport More Illegal Aliens
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement highlighting examples of the worst illegal aliens arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in Michigan recently, with criminal histories that include armed robbery, identity theft, and driving under the influence of liquor.
“Every day, the steadfast patriots of ICE are arresting and deporting illegal aliens from all across the country, including the beautiful state of Michigan,” said Secretary of Homeland Security Markwayne Mullin. “In recent days, they’ve arrested armed robbers, scammers, and drunk drivers. At DHS, we will always enforce the law and protect the American people. Arresting and removing illegal aliens from our country is how we save lives and make America safe again.”
Among the worst illegal aliens arrested in Michigan are:
Amilcar Jose Suarez Barreto, an illegal alien from Venezuela, arrested by ICE in Grand Rapids, Michigan, on October 12. His criminal history includes arrests for felony armed robbery, FOUR counts of assault, FOUR counts of trespassing, TWO counts of property damage, felony possession of weapon, shoplifting, larceny, resisting officer, obstructing justice, and escape/violate electronic monitoring. He illegally entered the United States in July 2023 through Texas, and was RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration. He was issued a final order of removal by an Immigration Judge on January 27, 2025.
Sarah Jane Hulbert, a criminal illegal alien from the Philippines, arrested by ICE in Detroit, Michigan, on October 16. Her criminal history includes convictions for aggravated identity theft from $10,000 to $100,000 against a person 60 years or older and domestic violence, as well as arrests for driving under the influence of liquor, TWO counts of intimidation/physical harm, FOUR counts of forgery, TWO counts of harassment, obstructing police, and computer fraud. She was first lawfully admitted to the United States in August 2004, but became inadmissible after her criminal convictions. She was subsequently taken into custody by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), but was RELEASED back into the country by the Biden Administration.
Kelvin Ramon Castillo Rodriguez, an illegal alien from Venezuela, arrested by ICE in Detroit, Michigan, on October 18. His criminal history includes an arrest for assault. He illegally entered the United States in April 2023 near Hidalgo, Texas, and was RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration.
Elmer Domingo Lopez Tomas, an illegal alien from Guatemala, arrested by ICE in Detroit, Michigan, on October 18. His criminal history includes an arrest for a felony weapons offense. He was lawfully admitted to the United States in March 2025 but failed to depart after his authorized period expired in July 2025.
Anderson Jesus Rosales Ramos, an illegal alien from Venezuela, arrested by ICE in Grand Rapids, Michigan, on October 16. His criminal history includes an arrest for second-degree retail fraud. He illegally entered the United States in August 2023 through Texas, and was RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration. He was issued a final order of removal by an Immigration Judge on January 19, 2024.
