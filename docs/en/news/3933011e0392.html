WORST OF THE WORST: ICE Arrests Murderers, Sexual Predators, and Kidnappers
Release Date: October 8, 2026
The Trump Administration will always put the safety of the American people first and remove every illegal alien we encounter from our country
WASHINGTON – Yesterday, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested more worst of the worst illegal aliens, convicted of heinous crimes such as murder, statutory rape, and abduction.
“Yesterday, the brave patriots of ICE arrested murderers, sexual predators, kidnappers, and other public safety threats from our communities,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Under President Trump, there will be no sanctuary for illegal aliens. If you are in this country illegally, we will find, arrest, and remove you from our nation.”
Yesterday’s arrests include:
Ian Clive Burton, a criminal illegal alien from Jamaica, convicted for murder, assault on a law enforcement officer, resisting arrest, and possession of a firearm in Monmouth County, New Jersey.
Angel Ramirez-Castillo, a criminal illegal alien from Cuba, convicted for attempted murder and aggravated assault in Miami, Florida.
Cesar Funes-Oliva, a criminal illegal alien from El Salvador, convicted for statutory rape and larceny in Gwinnett County, Georgia.
Irvin Roblero-Ortiz, a criminal illegal alien from Guatemala, convicted for abduction and strangulation in Accomack, Virginia.
Josue Fuentes-Argueta, a criminal illegal alien from El Salvador, convicted for conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute in the Northern District of Texas.
Americans can see more public safety threats arrested in their communities on our webpage WOW.DHS.Gov.
