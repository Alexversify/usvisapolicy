CBP Proposes Changes to Low-Value Shipment Customs Entry Rules, Not Immigration-Related

Published Oct 8, 2026

U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has proposed a rule modifying filing requirements for informal customs entries of goods valued at $2,500 or less, including a new electronic entry type for mail shipments. This is a customs and trade rulemaking and does not concern visas, green cards, or citizenship.

Key points

  • CBP proposes to modify filing requirements for informal entries of goods valued at $2,500 or less.
  • A new electronic informal entry type, Entry Type 13, would be created for merchandise entering through the mail.
  • The proposal adds data requirements for carriers and bonding requirements for certain informal entries.
  • This is only a proposed rule, not yet final or in effect.
  • Comments are due by December 7, 2026.

Who is affected

Importers, customs brokers, carriers, and e-commerce sellers handling low-value shipments into the United States.

What to do

This notice does not affect immigration applicants; parties involved in customs entries may review the Federal Register notice and submit comments by the deadline.
