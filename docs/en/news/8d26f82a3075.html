ICE Announces Arrests of Illegal Aliens Convicted of Murder, Child Abuse, Rape

Published Oct 9, 2026

DHS announced that ICE arrested several illegal aliens with criminal convictions for murder, child sexual abuse, vehicular homicide, and rape. DHS said the arrests are part of ongoing efforts under the Trump administration to remove criminal aliens from communities.

Key points

  • Ricardo Gonzalez, from the Dominican Republic, was convicted of murder in the Bronx, New York.
  • Maykel Ramos-De La Torre, from Cuba, was convicted of vehicular homicide in Key West, Florida.
  • Amador Rodriguez-Garcia, from Mexico, was convicted of aggravated criminal sexual abuse of a victim under 13 in Cook County, Illinois.
  • Angel Paguada-Banegas, from Honduras, was convicted of statutory rape in Wake County, North Carolina.
  • Jose Lemus, from El Salvador, was convicted of assault with a deadly weapon and hit-and-run with injury in Long Beach, California.
  • DHS says more enforcement cases can be viewed on its WOW.DHS.Gov webpage.

Who is affected

This announcement concerns illegal aliens with criminal convictions who were arrested by ICE, and the communities where they had resided.

What to do

No action is required from the public; additional cases are listed on WOW.DHS.Gov.

WORST OF THE WORST: ICE Arrests Murderers, Pedophiles, and Rapists
Release Date: October 9, 2026
Under President Trump and Secretary Mullin’s leadership, America will never become a sanctuary for illegal aliens
WASHINGTON – Yesterday, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested more worst of the worst illegal aliens, convicted of heinous crimes such as murder, child sexual abuse, and rape.
“Yesterday, the brave patriots of ICE arrested murderers, pedophiles, rapists, and other illegal aliens who should have never been in our country at all,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Under President Trump, we are laser-focused on arresting and removing illegal aliens from our communities to make America safe again. DHS will never apologize for putting the safety of the American people first.”
Yesterday’s arrests include:
Ricardo Gonzalez, a criminal illegal alien from the Dominican Republic, convicted for murder in the Bronx, New York.
Maykel Ramos-De La Torre, a criminal illegal alien from Cuba, convicted for vehicular homicide in Key West, Florida.
Amador Rodriguez-Garcia, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for aggravated criminal sexual abuse of a victim under the age of 13 in Cook County, Illinois.
Angel Paguada-Banegas, a criminal illegal alien from Honduras, convicted for statutory rape/sex offense in Wake County, North Carolina.
Jose Lemus, a criminal illegal alien from El Salvador, convicted for assault with a deadly weapon and hit-and-run with injury in Long Beach, California.
Americans can see more public safety threats arrested in their communities on our webpage WOW.DHS.Gov.
