USCIS to Issue New Form I-485 Edition, Rejecting Older Versions From Sept. 18

Published Aug 19, 2026Effective Sep 18, 2026

USCIS will publish a revised edition of Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status, dated 09/18/26, effective Sept. 18, 2026. The revision aligns the form with the recently announced Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility Final Rule, and there is no grace period between editions.

Key points

  • The 09/18/26 edition replaces the current 01/20/25 edition.
  • The 09/18/26 edition cannot be filed before Sept. 18, 2026.
  • USCIS will accept the 01/20/25 edition only if postmarked or electronically submitted before Sept. 18, 2026.
  • The 01/20/25 edition will be rejected if submitted on or after Sept. 18, 2026.
  • The 09/18/26 edition will be accepted only if submitted on or after that date.
  • A preview of the new edition and instructions is already available on the Form I-485 webpage.

Who is affected

Applicants filing Form I-485 to adjust status to permanent resident, and attorneys representing them, are affected.

What to do

Applicants should confirm which edition matches their filing date and review the preview of the 09/18/26 edition and instructions before submitting.
