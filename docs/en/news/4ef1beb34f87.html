DHS Says ICE Arrested Guatemalan National Earlier Released Under LA Sanctuary Policy

Published Oct 29, 2026

DHS announced that ICE arrested Guatemalan national Juan Jose Leonardo De Leon in Los Angeles on October 19, 2026. The release also lists numerous other cases in California and other states where DHS says sanctuary policies led to the release of noncitizens subject to ICE detainers.

Juan Jose Leonardo De Leon

That’s what it means to put America first.”
