DHS Criticizes New York Sanctuary Officials Over Release of Alien Charged in Officer Assault

Published Oct 28, 2026

DHS issued a statement condemning New York City officials for declining an ICE detainer and releasing Alpha Bah, an alien from Guinea charged with aggravated assault on a police officer with a deadly weapon. The release is a political statement about sanctuary policies, not a change to immigration law, rule or policy.

Key points

  • ICE lodged a detainer for Alpha Bah on October 7 after his arrest for aggravated assault on a police officer with a deadly weapon, among other charges; NYPD released him without notifying ICE.
  • Bah had been arrested twice before in New York and released each time without ICE being notified; he entered the U.S. through Arizona in November 2023 and was released into the country.

  • New York enacted a law in May 2026 restricting local police cooperation with ICE, which took effect August 25, 2026.
  • DHS called on Governor Kathy Hochul and Mayor Zohran Mamdani to end sanctuary policies.

Who is affected

New York residents, local law enforcement agencies and those following U.S. immigration enforcement policy.

SANCTUARY INSANITY: DHS Slams New York Sanctuary Politicians for Releasing Illegal Alien from Jail After Assaulting a Police Officer
Release Date: October 28, 2026
This illegal alien was RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement condemning sanctuary politicians in New York for denying a detainer lodged by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) against an illegal alien arrested for aggravated assault on a police officer with a deadly weapon.
On October 7, ICE lodged a detainer with Queens Central Booking for Alpha Bah, an illegal alien from Guinea, after he had been arrested the same day for the charges of aggravated assault on a police officer with a deadly weapon, resisting arrest, obstruct governmental administration, and intent to obtain transportation without paying.
The New York City Police Department (NYPD) did not honor the detainer and chose to RELEASE Bah from jail without notifying ICE. He remains at large.
Bah had previously been arrested by the NYPD twice before. He was arrested on June 15, 2026, and charged with intent to obtain transportation without paying. ICE lodged a detainer with Manhattan Central Booking, but the NYPD refused to honor the detainer and released him. More than a year earlier, Bah was arrested on January 5, 2025, and charged with criminal trespass. He was released before ICE could lodge a detainer.
“This illegal alien from Guinea was arrested for assaulting a police officer with a deadly weapon. Despite these violent charges, New York City sanctuary politicians refused to honor ICE’s detainer and RELEASED him back onto the streets, and he remains at large,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “This is the vision of sanctuary politicians in a nutshell: the open-border policies of the Biden Administration let this criminal into our country, and then sanctuary politicians in New York released him after arresting him without notifying ICE. DHS is calling on Governor Kathy Hochul and Mayor Zohran Mamdani to knock it off with these evil sanctuary policies before another American gets hurt, or worse.”
Bah illegally entered the United States through Arizona in November 2023 and was arrested by the U.S. Border Patrol. He was then RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration.

In response, New York Governor Kathy Hochul punished local law enforcement who did cooperate with ICE by proposing legislation that would bar local police departments from partnering and cooperating with ICE law enforcement, which she signed into law in May 2026. The law took effect on August 25, 2026.
