ICE Announces Arrests of Criminal Illegal Aliens Convicted of Child Sex Abuse, Drug Trafficking

Published Oct 7, 2026

DHS announced that ICE arrested several criminal illegal aliens with convictions including sexual conduct against a child, sexual abuse of an animal, and drug trafficking. The release lists five individuals, their countries of origin, convictions, and arrest locations across several U.S. states.

Key points

  • ICE arrested individuals convicted of crimes including sexual conduct against a child, child pornography possession, sexual abuse of an animal, and drug manufacturing or distribution.
  • Those arrested are nationals of Honduras, Mexico, El Salvador, and Guatemala, arrested in New York, Illinois, Virginia, Texas, and New Jersey.
  • DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin stated assaults against ICE officers have increased by more than 1,600 percent, but enforcement will continue.
  • DHS says additional arrests of public safety threats can be viewed on its WOW.DHS.Gov webpage.

Who is affected

This concerns individuals with criminal convictions who are in the country without legal immigration status, and the broader public.

What to do

No action is required by the general public; further details are available on the DHS website.
