DHS Highlights Worst Illegal Aliens with Criminal Histories in the State of Kansas
Release Date: October 19, 2026
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement highlighting examples of the worst illegal aliens arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) with criminal histories in the state of Kansas, including convictions for murder, indecent liberties with a child, rape, drug trafficking, and robbery.
“The disastrous open-border policies of the Biden Administration led to millions of illegal aliens flooding into our country. They brought crime and destruction into every state, including the great state of Kansas,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “In recent months, the brave patriots of ICE have arrested murderers, pedophiles, rapists, drug traffickers, and robbers. Thanks to President Trump’s leadership, ICE has been unleashed to arrest these dangerous illegals and get them off our streets. No matter where they are, no matter which state they try to hide in, we will not stop until we have arrested and removed as many illegal aliens as possible so that we can make America safe again.”
Recent arrests of illegal aliens with criminal histories in Kansas include:
Omar Israel Hernandez, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in July. His criminal history includes a conviction for first-degree murder in Wyandotte County, Kansas.
Alberto Estrada, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in October. His criminal history includes convictions for aggravated indecent liberties with a child and rape in Ellsworth, Kansas.
Greg Chukwuka Orji, a criminal illegal alien from Nigeria, arrested by ICE in September. His criminal history includes convictions for dangerous drugs and possession with intent to distribute fentanyl in Hutchinson, Kansas.
Oscar Sanchez-Corona, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in Shawnee, Kansas in August. His criminal history includes convictions for use or possess drug paraphernalia to introduce in human body, criminal damage to property, and operating a motor vehicle without a license, as well as arrests for driving under the influence of liquor and illegal entry. ICE deported him on September 4.
Jose Luis Rauda-Diaz, a criminal illegal alien from El Salvador, arrested by ICE in July. His criminal history includes convictions for aggravated robbery and TWO counts of possession of a weapon in Emporia, Kansas.
