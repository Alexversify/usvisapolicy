ICE Arrests Nigerian National Working on PA Congressional Campaign After Indecent Exposure Case

Published Oct 7, 2026

DHS announced that ICE arrested Chimaobim Adedayo Igbojekwe, a Nigerian national unlawfully present in the United States, after he pleaded guilty to disorderly conduct in Pennsylvania. He had been working as a canvasser for a congressional campaign.

Key points

  • Igbojekwe was arrested on September 27 in East Pennsboro Township for indecent exposure and disorderly conduct.
  • He pleaded guilty to the disorderly conduct charge.
  • ICE arrested him on October 1 following the conviction.
  • He was lawfully admitted in June 2017 but overstayed after his authorized period expired in November 2019.
  • The Biden Administration granted him deferred action in September 2021.

Who is affected

Individuals unlawfully present in the United States and organizations or campaigns that employ them are affected.
