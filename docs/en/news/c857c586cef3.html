USCIS to Open New International Field Office in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Published Sep 3, 2026Effective Sep 9, 2026

USCIS is opening a new international field office in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, beginning operations on September 9, 2026. The office will directly handle document verification, fraud investigations, and DNA collection for family relationship confirmation, functions previously coordinated with the Department of State.

Key points

  • The new field office begins operations on September 9, 2026, in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
  • Staff will verify the authenticity of foreign documents and investigate suspected fraud.
  • The office will collect DNA samples to confirm family relationships.
  • These functions were previously handled through coordination with the Department of State.
  • The move is part of a broader effort to align USCIS overseas operations with agency priorities.
  • Services will be available only by appointment, and appointment details will be posted on USCIS' International Immigration Offices webpage.

Who is affected

Applicants and petitioners with immigration matters in Ethiopia, including those requiring document verification, fraud investigations, or DNA testing.

What to do

Check the USCIS International Immigration Offices webpage for updates on services and appointment scheduling at the Addis Ababa office.
