DHS Announces ICE Arrests of Five Individuals With Prior Criminal Convictions

Published Nov 19, 2026

DHS said in a November 19, 2026 press release that ICE arrested five noncitizens with prior criminal convictions in Idaho, North Carolina, Arizona, Tennessee and Virginia, describing them as among the "worst of the worst." Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin was quoted saying the arrests reflect ongoing efforts to remove people with criminal histories and to reverse enforcement policies from the previous administration. The release does not announce any change to immigration law, policy or eligibility.

Key points

  • DHS said ICE arrested the individuals the day before the release, in five separate states.
  • According to DHS, Diego Arellanes-Carillo, from Mexico, was convicted of sexual assault on three occasions and cruelty toward a child, and was arrested in Boise, Idaho.
  • According to DHS, Eldmond Saye Boley, from Liberia, was convicted of assault with a firearm on a law enforcement officer, kidnapping and larceny, and was arrested in Rowan County, North Carolina.
  • According to DHS, Francisco Javier Ruiz-Nieblas, from Mexico, was convicted of conspiracy to distribute amphetamine and other drug offenses, and was arrested in Phoenix, Arizona.
  • According to DHS, Maynor Arevalo-Garcia, from Guatemala, was convicted of robbery and was arrested in Rutherford County, Tennessee, and Jose Luis Gutierrez-Rocha, from Honduras, was convicted of carrying a concealed firearm and drug offenses and was arrested in Alexandria, Virginia.
  • The release does not describe any new rule, fee or process; it reports specific enforcement actions.

Who is affected

The announcement concerns the five named individuals with prior criminal convictions who were arrested by ICE; it does not change rules for other immigration applicants.

WORST OF THE WORST: ICE Arrests Sexual Deviants, Violent Assailants, Drug Dealers, and Other Public Safety Threats
Release Date: November 19, 2026
DHS will never apologize for upholding the law and doing our job to protect the Homeland
WASHINGTON – Yesterday, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested more worst of the worst criminal illegal aliens convicted for horrific crimes, including sexual assault, assault with firearm on law enforcement officer, and conspiracy to distribute amphetamine.
“Every day, ICE is arresting and removing criminal illegal aliens from across the country. Just yesterday, ICE arrested sexual deviants, violent assailants, drug dealers, and other public safety threats,” said HomelandSecurity Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Under President Trump, DHS is reversing dangerous Biden-era open border polices that allowed illegal aliens to flood into our nation. We will never apologize for enforcing our nation’s laws and removing criminal illegal aliens from our streets in an effort to Make America Safe Again. Our border is and will remain secure.”
Yesterday’s arrests include:
Diego Arellanes-Carillo, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for sexual assault THREE times and cruelty toward a child in Boise, Idaho.
Eldmond Saye Boley, a criminal illegal alien from Liberia, convicted for assault with firearm on a law enforcement officer, kidnapping, and larceny in Rowan County, North Carolina.
Francisco Javier Ruiz-Nieblas, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for conspiracy to distribute amphetamine and dangerous drugs in Phoenix, Arizona.
Maynor Arevalo-Garcia, a criminal illegal alien from Guatemala, convicted for robbery in Rutherford County, Tennessee.
Jose Luis Guitierrez-Rocha, a criminal illegal alien from Honduras, convicted for carrying a concealed firearm and drugs: possess with intent to manufacture/sell in Alexandria, Virginia.
Americans can see more public safety threats arrested in their communities on our webpage at WOW.DHS.Gov.
