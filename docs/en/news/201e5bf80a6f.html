USCIS Opens New Asylum Office in Charlotte, North Carolina

Published Oct 9, 2026Effective Oct 13, 2026

USCIS is opening a new asylum office in Charlotte, North Carolina. Starting October 13, 2026, asylum applicants residing in North Carolina or South Carolina will fall under this office's jurisdiction and may be scheduled for interviews there.

Key points

  • The Charlotte Asylum Office is located at 128 South Tryon Street, Suite 600, Charlotte, North Carolina 28202.
  • Beginning October 13, 2026, asylum applicants in North Carolina or South Carolina fall under the new office's jurisdiction.
  • Interviews and services are by appointment only; walk-ins are not accepted.
  • Applicants should carefully check their interview notice for the correct interview location.
  • Applicants needing to reschedule an interview should call the USCIS Contact Center at 1-800-375-5283.

Who is affected

Affects individuals who have filed or are filing for asylum and reside in North Carolina or South Carolina.

What to do

Review your asylum interview notice carefully for the correct location, and contact the USCIS Contact Center if you need to reschedule.
