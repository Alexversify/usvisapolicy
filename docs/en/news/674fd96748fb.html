USCIS Updates Policy Manual on LPR Registration for Children of Foreign Government Employees

Published Sep 9, 2026

USCIS has issued new guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual addressing regulatory changes to the registration of lawful permanent residence for children born in the United States to foreign government employees. The update also clarifies related key terminology.

Key points

  • The update affects 7 USCIS-PM O.3, Chapter 3, on children born in the U.S. to foreign government employees.
  • It also affects 12 USCIS-PM A.2, Chapter 2, on becoming a naturalized U.S. citizen after being born in the U.S.
  • The guidance addresses regulatory changes to lawful permanent residence registration for this group.
  • The source does not specify an effective date or further detail on the underlying regulatory change.

Who is affected

Children born in the United States to foreign government employees, and attorneys handling their immigration status, are affected.

What to do

Those affected should review the updated chapters of the USCIS Policy Manual and consult an immigration attorney if applicable.
