DHS Criticizes Texas Officials Over Plan to End Cooperation with ICE

Published Nov 18, 2026

DHS issued a statement criticizing Texas officials reportedly planning to end cooperation with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) once Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa takes office. Secretary Markwayne Mullin called such a move a dereliction of duty and urged continued cooperation. The statement listed recent cases of individuals arrested, convicted or removed from Texas with criminal histories including murder, child sex trafficking and drug trafficking.

Key points

  • DHS warned that if Texas stopped cooperating with ICE and became a sanctuary state, it would be a serious dereliction of duty.
  • Secretary Mullin urged Governor-elect Hinojosa and other Texas leaders to continue cooperating with ICE.
  • The statement cited recent cases of aliens arrested, convicted or removed from Texas, including convictions for murder, sexual assault and drug trafficking.
  • It also referenced the first case decided by the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, involving a deportation tied to a planned ISIS-inspired attack.
  • The statement does not include any new immigration policy, rule or fee change.

Who is affected

Texas state and local officials, immigration enforcement agencies, and the public following immigration enforcement policy in Texas.

DHS Denounces Texas Officials for Allegedly Planning to End Cooperation with ICE
Release Date: November 18, 2026
Illegal aliens arrested in Texas have criminal histories that include murder, child sex trafficking, sexual assault, and drug trafficking
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement denouncing sanctuary politicians in Texas who plan to end cooperation with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) once Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa takes office.
“If Texas were to stop cooperating with ICE and become a sanctuary state, it would be one of the worst derelictions of duty we’ve seen in a very long time," said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Recent illegal alien arrests in Texas include murderers, child sex traffickers, terrorists, gang members, sexual assailants, and drug traffickers. Texas has long been a great partner with DHS in the fight to arrest and remove illegal aliens from our communities, so that we can save lives and make America safe again. We are calling on Governor-elect Hinojosa and other leaders throughout the state to continue cooperating with us. If they do not, then crime and violence will plague the Lone Star State, and innocent Americans will suffer as more sanctuary politicians put illegal alien criminals over their own constituents. It’s no coincidence that 7 of the 10 safest cities in America cooperate with ICE law enforcement.”
Recent examples of aliens arrested, convicted, or removed from Texas include:
Nazira Haji Zada
In September, ICE deported Nazira Haji Zada, an alien from Afghanistan who had been residing in Fort Worth, Texas, after she conceded that she is an alien terrorist who supported a plot to commit an ISIS-inspired mass shooting on Election Day 2024. This marked the first-ever case before the United States Alien Terrorist Removal Court (ATRC). She is now permanently inadmissible to the United States.
Giannys Alexandra Ramirez-Fernandez (left) and Nelson Adrian Perez-Martinez (right)
In June, an investigation by ICE Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) San Antonio led to the sentencing of two illegal aliens from Venezuela on charges related to child sex trafficking. Giannys Alexandra Ramirez-Fernandez and Nelson Adrian Perez-Martinez were both convicted on charges of conspiracy to traffic a child and transportation of a minor with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity, while Perez-Martinez was also convicted on charges of benefiting from sex trafficking of children and aiding and abetting coercion and enticement. Ramirez-Fernandez was sentenced to 12 and a half years in prison, while Perez-Martinez was sentenced to 20 years.
Isaias Jose Rodriguez-Manzanares
In June, ICE deported Isaias Jose Rodriguez-Manzanares, an illegal alien from Honduras, self-admitted MS-13 gang member, and associate of the Cartel del Noreste drug cartel, to his country of origin. He had been arrested on June 16, 2025, by the Colleyville Police Department for manufacturing or distributing a controlled substance and unlawfully carrying a weapon. On December 19, 2025, the charges were reduced and he was convicted of felony possession of a controlled substance. He was sentenced to two years in prison. The Texas Department of Criminal Justice transferred Rodriguez-Manzanares into ICE custody on May 20 following the completion of his sentence.
Rufino Varela-Flores
In May, ICE arrested Rufino Varela-Flores, an illegal alien from Mexico and convicted sex offender who had previously been deported. He was arrested on May 24 by the Randall County Sheriff’s Office for public intoxication, who then cooperated and turned him over to ICE custody on May 27. His criminal history includes convictions for TWO felony counts of sex assault and FOUR counts of drug possession. After serving a four-year sentence for the sex assault conviction, he was deported in 2015. He then illegally re-entered the country – a felony – at an unknown date and location.
Dinh Quy Nguyen
In May, ICE arrested Dinh Quy Nguyen, an illegal alien from Vietnam, in Houston. He had remained free in the local community for over 15 years despite having a final order of removal and prior convictions for attempted capital murder of a police officer and burglary.
Juan Fernando Lara Saavedra, an illegal alien from Honduras, arrested by ICE on October 29 in Austin. His criminal history includes a conviction for second-degree murder.
Javier Hernandez Alcon, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE on October 20 in Houston. His criminal history includes convictions for homicide – willful killing of a police officer with a weapon, burglary – forced entry of a residence, and illegal entry.
Apolinar Santibanez Avellanea, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE on October 16 in Houston. His criminal history includes convictions for murder and driving while intoxicated.
Carlos Ovidio Culgua-Alas, an illegal alien from El Salvador, deported by ICE on October 2. His criminal history includes convictions for homicide and alien in possession of a firearm, as well as arrests for public intoxication and disorderly conduct in Texas.
Victor Falfan, an illegal alien from Mexico and Tango Houstone gang member, arrested by ICE on September 10 in Corpus Christi. His criminal history includes manslaughter, TWO counts of possession of marijuana, and TWO counts of possession of a controlled substance.
David Alejandro Vera Belandia, an illegal alien from Venezuela, arrested by ICE on October 15 in Austin. His criminal history includes a conviction for second-degree sexual assault – rape.
Victor Andres Ayala Rosales, an illegal alien from El Salvador, arrested by ICE on October 12 in Houston. His criminal history includes a conviction for indecency with a child by sexual contact.
Santiago Lopez Diaz, an illegal alien from Honduras, arrested by ICE on November 1 in Austin. His criminal history includes a conviction for aggravated battery on a pregnant female.
Sergio De Anda-Rodriguez, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE in October. His criminal history includes convictions for domestic violence, burglary – forced entry, and unauthorized use of a vehicle in Palmview, Texas.
Alejandro Falfan Acosta, an illegal alien from Mexico and Tango Corpito gang member, arrested by ICE on October 5 in Corpus Christi. His criminal history includes convictions for manufacturing/delivering a controlled substance, possession of a controlled substance, possession of dangerous drugs, and property theft.
Daniel Torres Rodriguez, an illegal alien from Mexico and Los Zetas gang member, deported on September 25. His criminal history includes convictions for manufacture/delivery of a controlled substance, possession of marijuana, illegal re-entry, and evading arrest/detention, and arrests for assault causing bodily injury to family member and liquor violation in Texas.
Jonathan Josue Rivera-Caceres, an illegal alien from Honduras and MS-13 gang member, deported on September 22. His criminal history includes convictions for aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, driving while intoxicated, and an arrest for terroristic threats against peace officer/judge in Texas.
Yarlee Freddy Acosta Salmeron, an illegal alien from Honduras and Southwest Cholos gang member, deported on September 21. His criminal history includes convictions for assault of a family member, theft from person, and failure to identify a fugitive with the intent to give false information in Texas.
Victor Gonzalez-Ibarra, an illegal alien from Mexico, arrested by ICE on July 15. His criminal history includes convictions for conspiracy to distribute 500 grams or more of cocaine and possessing/carrying a firearm during and in relation to a drug trafficking crime in San Antonio, Texas.
