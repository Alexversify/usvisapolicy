DHS Spotlights Illegal Alien Arrests and Convictions in San Antonio, Texas

Published Oct 7, 2026

On October 7, 2026, DHS released a statement highlighting illegal aliens arrested by ICE or convicted of crimes in San Antonio, Texas, including cases involving child sex trafficking, drug trafficking and theft. The release frames these cases as examples of ongoing enforcement efforts following what DHS described as prior border policy failures.

Key points

  • DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin criticized the previous administration's border policies and said Texas was heavily affected.
  • Two Venezuelan nationals were convicted of child sex trafficking related charges, receiving sentences of 12.5 and 20 years respectively.
  • A Mexican national was convicted of conspiracy to distribute over 500 grams of cocaine and firearm possession during drug trafficking, arrested by ICE in July 2026.
  • Another Mexican national was convicted of indecency with a child and possession of a controlled substance, arrested in June 2026.
  • A third Mexican national was convicted of multiple charges including illegal re-entry, making a terrorist threat and theft, also arrested in June 2026.

Who is affected

This release concerns specific individuals arrested or convicted in the San Antonio area and does not change rules or eligibility for visa applicants or immigrants generally.

What to do

No action is required by the general public; this is an enforcement highlight, not a policy change.
