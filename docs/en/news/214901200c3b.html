USCIS Notice to Clients of Former Immigration Attorney Alexandra Lozano

Published Jun 26, 2026

Alexandra Lozano resigned from the State Bar of Washington in lieu of discipline on May 26, 2026, and is permanently barred from practicing law in Washington. The Board of Immigration Appeals suspended her from practice before DHS, USCIS, the Immigration Courts, and the Board on June 18, 2026. USCIS is notifying her former clients with pending cases that they need to update their contact information.

Key points

  • Lozano resigned from the State Bar of Washington in lieu of discipline on May 26, 2026, and is permanently barred from practicing law there.
  • She closed her law firm on June 10, 2026.
  • The Board of Immigration Appeals suspended her from practice before DHS (including USCIS), the Immigration Courts, and the Board on June 18, 2026.
  • Clients with petitions or applications pending before USCIS need to submit an updated mailing address to keep their case moving.
  • VAWA self-petitioners, and T or U nonimmigrant status applicants or recipients, should follow separate address change procedures noted by USCIS.
  • Clients may withdraw a pending application or petition by submitting a written request to the USCIS office handling their case.

Who is affected

Former clients of attorney Alexandra Lozano who have petitions or applications pending with USCIS.

What to do

Update your mailing address with USCIS, or submit a written withdrawal request to the office handling your case, call the USCIS Contact Center at 800-375-5283 if you do not know which office that is, or file a Freedom of Information Act request for a copy of your alien file.

Release Date
06/26/2026
On May 26, 2026, Alexandra Lozano resigned from the State Bar of Washington in lieu of discipline and is permanently barred from practicing law in Washington. She closed her law firm on June 10, 2026. On June 18, 2026, the Board of Immigration Appeals (Board) suspended Lozano from practice before the Department of Homeland Security (including USCIS), the Immigration Courts, and the Board.
If you were represented by Alexandra Lozano and have petitions or applications pending before USCIS, we need an updated mailing address to continue processing your case. Visit our How to Change Your Address page for more information.
If you are a Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) self-petitioner, applicant or recipient of T nonimmigrant status, or petitioner or recipient of U nonimmigrant status, visit our Change of Address Procedures for VAWA/T/U Cases and Form I-751 Abuse Waivers page for more information about updating your address.
You can also withdraw your application or petition by submitting a request in writing to the USCIS office processing your case. If you do not know which office is processing your case, you should call the USCIS Contact Center at: 800-375-5283 (TTY 800-767-1833). If you need a copy of your alien file, please file a Freedom of Information Act Request.
