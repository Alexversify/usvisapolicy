ICE Reports Weekend Arrests of Convicted Murderers and Sex Offenders

Published Oct 26, 2026

DHS said ICE arrested multiple noncitizens with prior convictions for murder, child sexual assault and other serious crimes during a nationwide enforcement operation over the weekend of October 24 to 26, 2026. The agency named 15 individuals from countries including Mexico, Guatemala, Cuba, El Salvador, the Dominican Republic and Micronesia.

Key points

  • DHS said the arrests targeted individuals it describes as public safety threats who already have criminal convictions.
  • Listed convictions include murder, sexual assault of minors, sexual assault, assault, robbery, conspiracy to kidnap, arson, drug trafficking and illegal reentry.
  • DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin said ICE will continue to arrest and remove noncitizens who are in the country illegally.
  • DHS directed the public to its WOW.DHS.Gov webpage for additional cases of individuals arrested in local communities.

Who is affected

The release concerns noncitizens with prior criminal convictions who were arrested in ICE enforcement actions, not routine visa, green card or citizenship applicants.

DHS will always put America first and remove illegal aliens from our streets
