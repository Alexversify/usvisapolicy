ICE Announces Arrests of Illegal Immigrants Convicted of Murder, Sexual Assault, Violent Crimes

Published Nov 18, 2026

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) announced the arrest of five individuals it describes as illegal aliens with prior criminal convictions, including murder, sexual assault, aggravated assault and a firearms-related offense. DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin framed the arrests as part of the administration's broader immigration enforcement effort. The release reports completed enforcement actions and does not involve any new rule, fee or policy change.

Key points

  • ICE reported the arrest of five people it identifies as illegal aliens with existing criminal convictions.
  • Cited convictions include murder, aggravated manslaughter, criminal sexual conduct against a mentally impaired victim, aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, and aiding unlawful firearm possession tied to a violent crime.
  • The individuals named are nationals of Honduras, Mexico (two individuals), Zambia and the Dominican Republic.
  • DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin linked the arrests to a cited 2025 nationwide drop in violent crime and described them as part of ongoing deportation efforts.
  • The release announces completed arrests only; it does not propose or change any immigration rule, eligibility criterion or fee.

Who is affected

The notice concerns specific named individuals already convicted of crimes who are described as lacking legal immigration status, as well as communities affected by ICE enforcement activity.

What to do

No application, deadline or filing is affected; this is an enforcement announcement, not a policy change.

WORST OF THE WORST: ICE Arrests Murderers, Sexual Predators, and Violent Assailants
Release Date: November 18, 2026
Thanks to President Trump’s America First agenda, violent crime dropped 9.3% in 2025, including an 18.1% drop in murder and nonnegligent manslaughter
WASHINGTON – U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) announced the arrest of more worst of the worst criminal illegal aliens, convicted for murder, criminal sexual conduct – victim mentally impaired, aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, and other obscene crimes.
“From day one, President Trump made it clear: illegal aliens are NOT welcome in the U.S. Just yesterday, ICE arrested murderers, sexual predators, and violent assailants,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Under President Trump, DHS is deporting illegal aliens at a historic pace, delivering the most secure border in American history, and stopping deadly drugs from pouring into our communities. We will never apologize for putting Americans first.”
Yesterday’s arrests include:
Osman Velesquez-Suarez, a criminal illegal alien from Honduras, convicted for murder in Riverhead, New York.
Santos Garcia-Lira, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for aggravated manslaughter in the U.S. District Court of New Jersey.
Mainza Lombe Malambo, a criminal illegal alien from Zambia, convicted for criminal sexual conduct – victim mentally impaired in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
Cristian Vazquez-Jantes, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico, convicted for aggravated assault with a deadly weapon in Fort Worth, Texas.
Joel Duarte-Hierro, a criminal illegal alien from the Dominican Republic, convicted for aiding/abetting unlawful possession of a firearm related to a crime of violence in the U. S. District Court of Puerto Rico.
Americans can see more public safety threats arrested in their communities on our webpage at WOW.DHS.Gov.
