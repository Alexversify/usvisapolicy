DHS Sets FY 2027 Inflation Adjustments for Parole, I-94, ESTA and EVUS Fees

Published Oct 1, 2026Effective Oct 16, 2026

DHS has announced Fiscal Year 2027 inflation-adjusted amounts for certain immigration fees required by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (HR-1), including the parole fee, ESTA fee and EVUS enrollment fee. The new amounts take effect October 16, 2026. The Form I-94 fee at land border ports of entry will not change for FY 2027 due to the statutory rounding rule.

Key points

  • The HR-1 parole fee will rise from $1,020 to $1,050, effective October 16, 2026.
  • The ESTA authorization fee will rise from $40.27 to $40.62.
  • The EVUS enrollment fee will rise from $30.75 to $31.75.
  • The Form I-94 fee at land border ports of entry stays at $30 total, as the HR-1 portion rounds to $0 increase.
  • The parole fee applies when parole is actually granted and effectuated, not when the request is filed.
  • These are final fee adjustments under existing law, not a proposed rule.

Who is affected

This affects aliens granted parole into the United States, and travelers using ESTA, EVUS, or Form I-94 at land border ports of entry.

What to do

Travelers and applicants should budget for the new fee amounts if their parole, ESTA application, or EVUS enrollment occurs on or after October 16, 2026.
