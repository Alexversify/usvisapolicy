USCIS Issues Correction to Typos in 'Signatures on Immigration Benefit Requests' Rule

Published Jun 30, 2026

DHS published a correction to a rule document titled 'Signatures on Immigration Benefit Requests' that originally appeared in the Federal Register on May 11, 2026. The correction fixes several instances where 'PM' (referring to a USCIS Policy Memorandum) was mistakenly printed as 'p.m.'.

Key points

  • The correction applies to the rule document originally published on page 25479 of the May 11, 2026 Federal Register.
  • Multiple sentences on pages 25481 and 25482 are corrected to change 'p.m.' to 'PM'.
  • The errors involved references to 2016 and 2018 USCIS Policy Memoranda discussed in the rule's preamble.
  • This correction notice does not change any substantive requirement, eligibility rule, or procedure.

Who is affected

This notice is relevant to attorneys and others closely tracking the text of the USCIS rule on signatures for immigration benefit requests.

What to do

No action is needed, as this is a technical correction only; readers should refer to the original rule for substantive requirements.

Correction
In rule document 2026-09289, beginning on page 25479 in the issue of Monday, May 11, 2026, make the following changes:
1. On page 25481, third column, second sentence of the last paragraph,
“The 2016 p.m. stated” should read “The 2016 PM stated”.
2. On page 25482, first column, second sentence of the first paragraph continuing from the prior page, “The 2016 p.m. also provided” should read “The 2016 PM also provided”.
3. On the same page, first column, third sentence of the first paragraph continuing from the prior page, “The 2016 p.m. did not address” should read “The 2016 PM did not address”.
4. On the same page, first column, second sentence of the second paragraph,
“The 2016 p.m. provided” should read “The 2016 PM provided”.
5. On page 25482, first column, third sentence of the second paragraph,
“The 2016 p.m. also provided that” should read “The 2016 PM also provided that”.
6. On the same page, first column, fourth sentence of the second paragraph,
“The 2016 p.m. was incorporated” should read “The 2016 PM was incorporated”.
7. On the same page 25482, first column, fifth sentence of the second paragraph,
“Consistent with the 2018 p.m., USCIS” should read “Consistent with the 2018 PM, USCIS”.
