DHS Says ICE Deported Convicted Gang Members, Murderers and Other Criminal Aliens Last Week

Published Oct 8, 2026

DHS issued a press release on October 8 stating that ICE deported several criminal illegal aliens last week, including individuals convicted of murder, sexual assault, robbery and DUI. The release names about ten individuals and details their criminal histories and nationalities.

Key points

  • DHS said the deportations reflect the administration's 'Make America Safe Again' enforcement priorities.
  • Those deported were nationals of Honduras, El Salvador and Mexico, most with final removal orders.
  • Several individuals had prior deportations and had illegally reentered the United States multiple times.
  • Listed convictions include second-degree murder, sexual assault of a minor, armed robbery and DUI.
  • This is a factual enforcement announcement, not a new policy or rule change.

Who is affected

This announcement concerns individuals with criminal convictions who were removed by ICE, and is of interest to the public tracking immigration enforcement activity.

What to do

No action is required from the general public or applicants as this is a report on past enforcement actions.
