State Department Publishes October 2026 Visa Bulletin, First of Fiscal Year 2027

Published Oct 1, 2026Effective Oct 1, 2026

The U.S. Department of State released the Visa Bulletin for October 2026, the opening bulletin of fiscal year 2027, with final action dates and dates for filing for family sponsored and employment based immigrant visa categories. China and India continue to face the longest waits in several employment categories, while EB-1 and most EB-5 unreserved categories remain current for most countries.

Key points

  • Family-sponsored F2A final action date moved to 22 September 2026 for most countries, though the filing date remains current.
  • F1 final action date for Mexico remains the longest among family categories, at 8 September 2008.
  • EB-1 final action date is current for all countries except China (1 July 2023) and India (1 February 2023).
  • EB-2 final action date is 1 January 2025 for most countries, 1 October 2021 for China, and 1 November 2013 for India.
  • EB-3 final action date is 15 May 2024 for most countries, with longer waits for China, India, and the Philippines.
  • EB-5 unreserved categories remain current for most countries, with China at 1 December 2016 and India at 1 December 2023.

Who is affected

Applicants with pending family sponsored or employment based immigrant visa petitions, and the attorneys and employers tracking their priority dates.

What to do

Compare your priority date to the applicable chart to determine whether you may file an adjustment of status application or whether your case is ready for final action.
