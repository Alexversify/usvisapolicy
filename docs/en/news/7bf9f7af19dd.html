ICE Reports Weekend Arrests of Convicted Murderers, Child Sex Offenders and Drug Traffickers

Published Oct 5, 2026

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) announced the arrest of multiple illegal aliens with prior convictions for murder, child rape, sexual assault and drug trafficking over the weekend. DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin said the operation reflects the administration's public safety priorities despite a reported sharp rise in assaults on ICE officers.

Key points

  • ICE arrested illegal aliens from Mexico, Cuba, El Salvador, Honduras, Guatemala and Morocco.
  • Those arrested had prior convictions including murder, homicide, child rape, aggravated sexual abuse of a minor, and drug possession or distribution.
  • DHS said assaults against ICE officers have increased more than 1,600 percent.
  • The release lists each individual's conviction, nationality and the jurisdiction where they were convicted.
  • DHS said additional cases of public safety threats arrested in local communities are posted on its WOW.DHS.Gov webpage.

Who is affected

This concerns individuals with prior criminal convictions who were in the United States illegally, as well as the public following immigration enforcement activity.

What to do

No action is required; this is a report on completed enforcement arrests, not a policy change affecting pending applications.
