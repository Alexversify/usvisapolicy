    USCIS Announces FY 2027 Inflation Adjustment for Certain H.R. 1 Immigration Fees

    USCIS has published a Federal Register notice raising certain H.R. 1 immigration-related fees for fiscal year 2027 to account for inflation from July 2025 through July 2026. The new fees take effect on Oct. 16, 2026, and apply to requests postmarked on or after that date.

    Effective: Oct 16, 2026

    USCIS Helps Florida Charge 16 Noncitizens With Unlawful Voting

    USCIS assisted a Florida investigation that led to charges against 16 noncitizens accused of registering to vote by falsely claiming U.S. citizenship and then voting. Four additional individuals were charged with voting while on felony probation. USCIS confirmed the alien status of the individuals involved, according to the Florida Attorney General's Office.

  3. Sep 29, 2026

    USCIS to Adjust EB-5 Fees to Cover Program Costs and Boost Integrity Measures

    USCIS announced it is updating fees for the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program so that fees fully recover the cost of running the program, meet statutory processing time goals, and support integrity measures such as audits and site visits. The change finalizes a proposed rule published by DHS in October 2025. The source does not specify exact new fee amounts.

    Effective: Nov 30, 2026

    Cuban National Convicted in International Alien Smuggling and Money Laundering Case

    USCIS supported an investigation that led to the conviction of Lazaro Alain Cabrera-Rodriguez, a Cuban national, for conspiring to run an international alien smuggling organization and launder its proceeds. The organization charged between $1,500 and $40,000 per smuggled migrant and used fraudulent ESTA applications and false claims of European citizenship.

    Antonia-Beatrice Mircea, a Romanian citizen, admitted to conspiring with a U.S. citizen to enter a sham marriage to obtain lawful permanent residence. USCIS assisted the federal investigation, announced by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of New Jersey.

    Two Aliens Charged in New Jersey With Illegal Voting and False Statements on N-400

    USCIS assisted an investigation that led to charges against a Jamaican national and a Venezuelan national living in New Jersey, accused of voting in federal elections while not U.S. citizens and lying about it on their naturalization applications.   8. Sep 23, 2026

    Trump Proclamation Extends $100,000 H-1B Entry Restriction Through September 2027

    Proclamation 11069, signed September 18, 2026, extends the 2025 H-1B entry restriction under Proclamation 10973 for an additional 12 months. H-1B specialty occupation workers outside the United States remain barred from entry unless their petition is accompanied by a $100,000 payment, except where DHS grants a national interest exception.

    Effective: Sep 21, 2026

    USCIS Reports Naturalization Fraud Charge and Three Denaturalization Cases in North Carolina

    A federal grand jury in Charlotte indicted a naturalized citizen from Bhutan for naturalization fraud, alleging he concealed prior child sexual abuse when applying for citizenship. Separately, the U.S. Attorney's Office filed civil denaturalization complaints against three other individuals accused of obtaining citizenship through fraud or concealment of immigration violations.

  10. Sep 23, 2026

    Executive Order Strengthens Interagency Review of H-1B Visa Petitions

    President Trump signed Executive Order 14431 on September 18, 2026, published in the Federal Register on September 23, 2026, directing closer coordination among federal agencies in reviewing H-1B petitions, labor condition applications and visas. The order responds to concerns about wage suppression, layoffs of American workers and outsourcing abuses linked to the H-1B program.

    Effective: Sep 18, 2026

    State Department Updates FOIA Rules, Clarifies Requirements for Visa Record Requests

    The State Department has revised its public access to information regulations at 22 CFR Part 171 to reflect changes in agency organization and procedures, including electronic submission of requests. The rule updates how requesters should describe records sought and consolidates identity verification requirements. It takes effect on October 21, 2026.

    Effective: Oct 21, 2026

    State Department Delegates Visa Bond Waiver Authority to Senior Consular Officials

    The State Department issued a notice delegating its authority to waive visa bond requirements under 22 CFR 41.11(c)(3) to the Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary and the Deputy Assistant Secretary for Visa Services. This is an internal administrative change specifying who may authorize such waivers on behalf of the Assistant Secretary for Consular Affairs.

    USCIS Announces H-2B Cap Reached for First Half of FY 2027

    USCIS has received enough petitions to meet the congressionally mandated H-2B cap for the first half of fiscal year 2027. September 4, 2026 was the final receipt date for new cap-subject H-2B petitions requesting an employment start date before April 1, 2027. Petitions received after that date requesting such start dates will be rejected.

    Effective: Sep 4, 2026
