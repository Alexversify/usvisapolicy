Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09

You can use this site without signing up or logging in. We do not require your name or contact details to use the site.

Display settings such as your language and the number of items per page are kept only in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers. You can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

Send privacy questions and requests for access or deletion to info@usvisapolicy.com. If this policy changes, we will post the update here and revise the effective date.
