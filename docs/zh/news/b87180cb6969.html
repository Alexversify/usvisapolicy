USCIS称自2025年1月以来已采取220万次移民执法行动

发布日期 2026年10月29日

美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)宣布,自2025年1月20日以来,已采取约220万次执法行动,撤销或终止外国人的移民身份。这些行动包括改变临时保护身份(TPS)的管理方式、拒绝缺乏法律依据的非移民身份延期或变更申请、签发出庭通知书(NTA)启动遣返程序、终止工作许可证(EAD)自动延期,以及终止类别性假释项目。

要点

  • USCIS表示,自2025年1月20日以来,已通过约220万次执法行动撤销或终止外国人的移民身份。
  • 该机构称已结束对临时保护身份(TPS)的滥用。
  • USCIS正在拒绝其认为缺乏法律依据的非移民身份延期或变更申请。
  • USCIS正向应被遣返人员签发出庭通知书(NTA),启动移民法庭遣返程序。
  • 工作许可证(EAD)自动延期已终止,续期前须重新进行审查。
  • 类别性假释(parole)项目已被终止,改为逐案审查。

影响对象

临时保护身份(TPS)持有人、非移民身份延期或变更申请人、EAD持有人及假释项目受益人,以及雇用或代理他们的雇主和律师将受到影响。

建议行动

受影响人士应核实自身案件进展,并就这些执法变化可能产生的影响咨询移民律师。

WASHINGTON — Under the leadership of President Donald J. Trump and Secretary of Homeland Security Markwayne Mullin, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services is delivering on the Administration’s agenda and putting the American people first. The agency has restored integrity and order to the nation’s immigration system, reversing the downstream effects of the Biden border crisis. Since Jan. 20, 2025, USCIS has carried out immigration enforcement actions to revoke or terminate the status of approximately 2.2 million illegal aliens. These actions are in addition to the millions of visa revocations and denials issued by the U.S. Department of State and removals carried out by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

USCIS’s enforcement actions include:
终止临时保护身份（TPS）的滥用现象，使TPS再次真正成为一项临时性安排；
Ending the misuse of Temporary Protected Status so that TPS is once again truly temporary;
在不存在合法依据的情况下，拒绝延长或变更非移民身份的申请；
Denying applications to extend or change nonimmigrant status when no legal basis exists;
签发出庭通知书，将应被驱逐的外国人移交移民法官进行遣返程序；
Issuing Notices to Appear to place removable aliens into removal proceedings before an immigration judge;
终止工作许可文件的自动延期，确保在任何工作许可续期之前，每一名外国人都经过适当的审查和核实；以及
Ending the automatic extension of employment authorization documents, ensuring every alien is properly screened and vetted before any work authorization is renewed; and
终止类别性假释项目，使假释恢复到国会原本设想的严格个案审查方式。
Terminating categorical parole programs and returning parole to strict case-by-case review as Congress intended.

These actions have real consequences. Every fraudulent application identified, every ineligible benefit denied, and every national security threat flagged represents an immigration system working to uphold the law and protect the American people. By strengthening screening and vetting, preventing the exploitation of immigration benefits, and initiating removal proceedings against those who violate our laws, USCIS is closing the gaps that undermine public safety and national security.

USCIS is exercising its full legal authorities to deny meritless applications, terminate unlawfully granted immigration benefits, initiate removal proceedings, and revoke improper approvals—all while promoting accountability and preventing the misuse of public benefits.
