摘要:
SUMMARY:
美国国务院(“本部”)通过《联邦公报》向公众提出本年度请求,征集书面信息,以协助评估美国政府及外国政府在多大程度上达到2000年《贩运受害者保护法》所规定的消除人口贩运最低标准(“最低标准”)。
The Department of State (“the Department”) submits this annual request to the public, through the Federal Register , seeking written information to assist in reporting on the degree to which the United States and foreign governments meet the minimum standards for the elimination of trafficking in persons (“minimum standards”) that are prescribed by the Trafficking Victims Protection Act of 2000.
日期:
DATES:
须以书面形式提交至美国国务院监测和打击人口贩运办公室(TIP),提交截止时间为2026年2月27日美国东部标准时间下午5时。
Submissions must be made in writing to the Office to Monitor and Combat Trafficking in Persons (TIP) at the Department of State by 5 p.m. EST on February 27, 2026.

背景
Background
定义:《贩运受害者保护法》(TVPA)将“严重形式的人口贩运”定义如下:
Definitions: The TVPA defines “severe forms of trafficking in persons” as:
为商业性性行为之目的而招募、窝藏、运输、提供、获取、光顾或招揽他人,且该行为系通过武力、欺诈或胁迫手段促使从事,或被诱使从事该行为之人未满18周岁。
The recruitment, harboring, transportation, provision, obtaining, patronizing, or soliciting of a person for the purpose of a commercial sex act that is induced by force, fraud, or coercion, or in which the person induced to perform such act has not attained 18 years of age.
○ 未满18周岁而从事商业性行为的人,无论是否涉及武力、欺诈或胁迫手段,均属于人口贩运受害者。
○ Persons under age 18 in commercial sex are trafficking in persons victims regardless of whether force, fraud, or coercion were involved.
为劳动或服务之目的,通过武力、欺诈或胁迫手段招募、窝藏、运输、提供或获取他人,致使其遭受非自愿劳役、劳役偿债、债务奴役或奴役。
The recruitment, harboring, transportation, provision, or obtaining of a person for labor or services, through the use of force, fraud, or coercion, for the purpose of subjection to involuntary servitude, peonage, debt bondage, or slavery.
○ 强迫劳动可表现为家庭奴役、强迫乞讨、强迫从事犯罪活动(例如走私毒品),以及并非法院定罪后果的监狱劳动等形式。
○ Forced labor may take the form of domestic servitude, forced begging, forced criminal activity ( e.g., drug smuggling), and prison labor that is not a consequence of a conviction in a court of law.
被招募或利用作为士兵、或用于劳动或服务的儿童,若该活动涉及武力、欺诈或胁迫,即可构成严重形式的人口贩运。无论性别如何,儿童均可能成为受害者。
Children recruited or used as soldiers or for labor or services can be a severe form of human trafficking when the activity involves force, fraud, or coercion. Children may be victims regardless of gender.
《人口贩运问题报告》:《人口贩运问题报告》评估世界各国政府打击人口贩运的努力情况。它是对人口贩运的性质和范围以及各国政府为应对和消除人口贩运所采取的广泛行动的年度全球评估。美国政府利用《人口贩运问题报告》为外交工作提供依据,鼓励各方合作制定和实施打击人口贩运的法律和政策,并将资源用于预防、保护和起诉项目。起诉贩运者、保护受害者和预防贩运是《人口贩运问题报告》以及美国政府反贩运政策的最终目标。
The TIP Report: The TIP Report assesses governments' efforts worldwide to combat trafficking in persons. It represents an annual global assessment of the nature and scope of trafficking in persons and the broad range of government actions to confront and eliminate it. The U.S. government uses the TIP Report to inform diplomacy, encourage partnership in creating and implementing laws and policies to combat trafficking, and target resources on prevention, protection, and prosecution programs. Prosecuting traffickers, protecting victims, and preventing trafficking are the ultimate goals of the TIP Report and of the U.S government's anti-trafficking policy.
《人口贩运问题报告》侧重于各国政府为打击人口贩运所采取的具体行动,包括对贩运者的起诉、定罪和量刑,以及受害者识别与保护措施和预防工作。等级评定仅考虑政府打击人口贩运的行动,不考虑非政府组织(NGO)或国际组织的活动。每份《人口贩运问题报告》国别叙述均包含针对该国的优先建议。这些建议用于协助国务院衡量各国政府逐年的进展情况,并确定各国政府是否符合消除人口贩运的最低标准,或是否正在为达到该标准作出重大努力。
The TIP Report focuses on concrete actions made by governments to fight trafficking in persons, including prosecutions, convictions, and sentences for traffickers, as well as victim identification and protection measures and prevention efforts. Rankings only consider government actions to combat human trafficking, not the activities of nongovernmental organizations (NGOs) or international organizations. Each TIP Report country narrative includes prioritized recommendations for each country. These recommendations are used to assist the Department in measuring governments' progress from one year to the next and determining whether governments meet the minimum standards for the elimination of trafficking in persons or are making significant efforts to do so.
《贩运受害者保护法》确立了四级评级制度。等级评定主要基于政府打击人口贩运的具体行动程度。国务院首先评估该国政府是否完全符合经修订的《贩运受害者保护法》第108条(《美国法典》第22编第7106节)所规定的消除人口贩运的最低标准。完全符合标准的政府被列为第一级。对于其他政府,国务院则考虑其所作努力的程度。正在为达到最低标准作出重大努力的政府被列为第二级。未完全达到最低标准且未作出重大努力的政府被列为第三级。最后,国务院还会考虑观察名单标准,并在适用情况下将有关国家列入第二级观察名单。欲了解更多信息,可查阅2025年《人口贩运问题报告》(第1593页),网址为:www.state.gov/​reports/​2025-trafficking-in-persons-report。
The TVPA creates a four-tier ranking system. Tier placement is based principally on the extent of concrete government action to combat trafficking. The Department first evaluates whether the government fully meets the TVPA's minimum standards for the elimination of trafficking, as set forth in Sec. 108 of the TVPA, as amended (22 U.S.C. 7106). Governments that do so are placed on Tier 1. For other governments, the Department considers the extent of such efforts. Governments that are making significant efforts to meet the minimum standards are placed on Tier 2. Governments that do not fully meet the minimum standards and are not making significant efforts to do so are placed on Tier 3. Finally, the Department considers Watch List criteria and, when applicable, places countries on the Tier 2 Watch List. For more information, the 2025 TIP Report can be found at ( printed page 1593) www.state.gov/​reports/​2025-trafficking-in-persons-report.
《2025年人口贩运问题报告》涵盖了188个国家和地区。在世界各地，《人口贩运问题报告》推动了立法、国家行动计划、政策实施、项目资助、用于补充起诉工作的保护机制，以及对这一犯罪行为更深入的全球性认识的形成。
The 2025 TIP Report included 188 countries and territories. Around the world, the TIP Report has inspired legislation, national action plans, policy implementation, program funding, protection mechanisms to complement prosecution efforts, and a stronger global understanding of this crime.
自2010年以来，《人口贩运问题报告》通过跨部门协作程序，依据《贩运受害者保护法》(TVPA)所规定的消除人口贩运最低标准，纳入了对美国政府反贩运努力的评估。
Since 2010, the TIP Report, through a collaborative interagency process, has included an assessment of U.S. government anti-trafficking efforts in light of the minimum standards to eliminate trafficking in persons set forth by the TVPA.
国务院将继续遵守其法定职责，但可能会进一步精简《人口贩运问题报告》。提交材料可包括关于国务院应如何实现这一目标的意见，这些意见将在编制未来的《人口贩运问题报告》时予以考虑。
The Department will maintain adherence to its statutory mandates but may further streamline the TIP Report. Submissions may include input on how the Department could achieve this, which will be considered in preparing for future TIP Reports.

概况
Overview
1. 自2025年4月1日以来，该国政府在打击人口贩运方面取得了哪些重大成就？在过去一年中，该国政府打击人口贩运的努力发生了哪些重大变化？新的法律、法规、政策或实施策略（例如，实体刑法及程序、民事救济机制，以及针对法庭诉讼程序的被害人证人保护项目）对其反贩运应对措施产生了怎样的影响？
1. What were the government's major accomplishments in addressing human trafficking since April 1, 2025? In what significant ways have the government's efforts to combat trafficking in persons changed in the past year? How have new laws, regulations, policies, or implementation strategies ( e.g., substantive criminal laws and procedures, mechanisms for civil remedies, and victim-witness programs, generally and in relation to court proceedings) affected its anti-trafficking response?
2. 在过去一年中，该国政府反贩运工作中最严重的不足是什么？该国政府在实践中应对人口贩运问题的能力存在哪些限制？
2. Over the past year, what were the greatest deficiencies in the government's anti-trafficking efforts? What were the limitations on the government's ability to address human trafficking problems in practice?
3. 请就现行法律、政策和程序的实施情况提供观察意见。反贩运立法中是否存在可通过修订加以改进政府应对措施的漏洞？是否存在任何削弱或以其他方式对该国反贩运工作产生负面影响的政府政策？
3. Provide observations regarding the implementation of existing laws, policies, and procedures. Are there gaps in anti-trafficking legislation that could be amended to improve the government's response? Are there any government policies that have undermined or otherwise negatively affected anti-trafficking efforts within that country?
贩运概况
Trafficking Profile
4. 请描述报告期内人口贩运的趋势、驱动因素、手段、来源地/目的地动态、涉及的行业和部门、受影响的人口群体、招募手段等。请报告面临性贩运或劳动贩运特定风险的受害者概况，包括自上一报告期以来的任何变化（例如，男性、女性、儿童、移民、特定族裔群体）。请说明是否存在由毒品贩运者、参与移民偷渡或其他移民计划者以及帮派实施的劳动贩运或性贩运行为。
4. Describe human trafficking trends, drivers, methods, source/destination dynamics, industries and sectors, impacted demographics, recruitment methods, etc. during the reporting period. Please report the profile of victims at particular risk of sex trafficking or labor trafficking, including any changes since the last reporting period ( e.g., men, women, children, migrants, certain ethnic groups). Please note whether there was labor trafficking or sex trafficking by narcotics traffickers, those involved in migrant smuggling or other migration schemes, and gangs.
5. 是否有中国、古巴或朝鲜籍人员作为政府间协议和/或外国政府关联项目的一部分出现在该国境内？如果存在，这些人员是否遭受强迫劳动或面临强迫劳动的高风险？
5. Are any Chinese, Cuban, or North Korean persons present in the country as part of government-to-government agreements and/or in foreign government-affiliated projects? If present, are these individuals subjected to or at high risk of forced labor?
6. 如适用，请描述贩运者如何利用技术招募和剥削受害者，以及政府如何应对技术助推的人口贩运行为。
6. If applicable, describe how traffickers used technology to recruit and exploit victims and how the government responded to tech-facilitated human trafficking.
起诉
Prosecution
7. 包括法官在内的政府官员是否了解各种形式人口贩运的性质？如果不了解，请举例说明。政府是否有效提供或支持了面向官员的反贩运培训，包括关于政策和法律执行的培训，和/或关于受害者识别措施或程序的培训？如果没有，应如何改进？
7. Do government officials, including judges, understand the nature of all forms of trafficking? If not, provide examples. Did the government effectively provide or support anti-trafficking trainings for officials, including on enforcement of policies, and laws; and/or on victim identification measures or procedures? If not, how could it be improved?
