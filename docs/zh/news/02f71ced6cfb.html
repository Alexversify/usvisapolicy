美国国务院最终规则要求多元化移民签证申请人提交护照扫描件

发布日期 2026年3月11日生效日期 2026年4月10日

美国国务院发布最终规则，修改多元化移民签证(DV)项目相关规定，以打击欺诈行为。该规则要求申请人在电子申请表中提供有效护照信息并上传护照个人信息页扫描件,除非符合豁免条件。规则还更新了部分用语,将“性别(gender)”改为“性(sex)”,将“年龄”改为“出生日期”。

要点

  • 这是一项最终规则,而非提案,已于2026年3月11日发布,将于2026年4月10日生效。
  • DV项目申请人须提供有效期内护照信息,并上传护照个人信息及签名页扫描件,或说明其符合豁免条件。
  • 国务院指出欺诈现象普遍,例如DV-2025项目中发现250万份欺诈申请,以及有组织欺诈团伙在当事人不知情的情况下代为提交申请。
  • 规则还统一了法规用语,新增“应当(shall)”以明确指引,将“性别”改为“性”,将“年龄”改为“出生日期”。
  • 该规则将从DV-2027项目开始实施,具体取决于管理和预算办公室(OMB)对DS-5501申请表更新的批准。
  • 国务院就该规则的提案版本收到399条公众意见,并按原提案内容采纳了最终法规文本。

影响对象

计划从DV-2027申请期开始注册多元化移民签证(DV)项目的个人将受到影响。

建议行动

有意申请DV项目者应确保持有有效期内护照,并在DV-2027申请期开放后准备上传护照个人信息页扫描件。

摘要:
SUMMARY:
美国国务院(以下简称“国务院”)正在修订有关多元化移民签证项目(“DV项目”)的法规,以提高该项目的完整性并打击其中的欺诈行为。这些修正案要求DV项目申请人提供有效、未过期的护照信息,并在电子申请表中上传护照个人信息页及签名页的扫描件,或以其他方式说明其可免于该要求。此外,国务院正在对相关法规进行标准化和修订,包括加入“应当”一词以简化领事官员指南;确保使用“性别(sex)”一词而非“性别(gender)”;并将DV项目法规中的“年龄”一词替换为“出生日期”,以准确反映国务院在移民签证办理过程中所收集和保存的信息。
The Department of State (“Department”) is amending regulations governing the Diversity Immigrant Visa Program (“DV Program”) to improve the integrity of, and combat fraud in, the program. These amendments require a petitioner to the DV Program to provide valid, unexpired passport information and to upload a scan of the biographic and signature page in the electronic entry form or to otherwise indicate that he or she is exempt from this requirement. Additionally, the Department is standardizing and amending its regulations to add the word “shall” to simplify guidance for consular officers; ensure the use of the term “sex” in lieu of “gender”; and replace the term “age” in the DV Program regulations with the phrase “date of birth” to accurately reflect the information collected and maintained by the Department during the immigrant visa process.

一、执行摘要
I. Executive Summary
A. 监管行动的目的
A. Purpose of the Regulatory Action
本规则修订了外国人申请加入DV项目的方式,要求申请人在电子申请表中提供其有效、未过期护照的信息,并上传护照个人信息页及签名页的扫描件,但有限豁免情形除外。本规则旨在提高DV项目的完整性并打击其中的欺诈行为。本规则还包含其他澄清说明及更新用语。
This rule amends how an alien petitions for entry into the DV Program, requiring that a petitioner provide information from his or her valid, unexpired passport and upload a scan of the passport biographic and signature page to the electronic entry form, subject to limited exemptions. This rule improves the integrity of, and combats fraud in, the DV program. The rule also contains additional clarifications and updated language.
B. 法律授权摘要
B. Summary of Legal Authority
《移民与国籍法》(INA)第204(a)(1)(I)条,即8 U.S.C. 1154(a)(1)(I),规定了提交DV项目申请的程序,并授权国务卿实施这些法规修正案。INA第104(a)条,即8 U.S.C. 1104(a),进一步授权国务卿制定其认为实施其在INA项下职权所必需的法规。
Section 204(a)(1)(I) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), 8 U.S.C. 1154(a)(1)(I), governs the procedures for filing a petition to the DV Program and authorizes the Secretary of State to carry out these regulatory amendments. Section 104(a) of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1104(a), further authorizes the Secretary to establish such regulations as he or she deems necessary for carrying out the Secretary's authority under the INA.
C. 拟议规则摘要
C. Summary of Proposed Rule
国务院于2025年8月5日发布了一项拟议规则制定通知(NPRM),题为《签证:加强多元化移民签证项目的审查并打击欺诈》(90 FR 37437)。该NPRM提议要求DV项目申请人提供有效、未过期的护照信息,并在电子申请表中上传护照个人信息页及签名页的扫描件,或以其他方式说明其可免于该要求。此外,该NPRM提议对《联邦法规汇编》第22篇第42部分中的用语进行标准化和修订,包括加入“应当”一词以简化领事官员指南;按照第14168号行政命令的要求确保使用“性别(sex)”一词而非“性别(gender)”;并将22 CFR 42.33(h)(1)(i)中的“年龄”一词替换为“出生日期”,以准确反映国务院在移民签证办理过程中所收集和保存的信息。
The Department published a notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM), Visas: Enhancing Vetting and Combatting Fraud in the Diversity Immigrant Visa Program (90 FR 37437), on August 5, 2025. The NPRM proposed requiring a DV Program petitioner to provide valid, unexpired passport information and upload a scan of the passport's biographic and signature page to the electronic entry form or otherwise indicate that he or she is exempt from this requirement. Additionally, the NPRM proposed to standardize and amend language in 22 CFR part 42, including by adding the word “shall” to simplify guidance for consular officers; ensuring the use of the term “sex” in lieu of “gender” as mandated by Executive Order 14168; and replacing the term “age” in 22 CFR 42.33(h)(1)(i) with the phrase “date of birth” to accurately reflect the information collected and maintained by the Department during the immigrant visa process.
D. 国务院为何提出本规则?
D. Why did the Department propose this rule?
美国国务院(以下简称“国务院”)在《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中解释说,拟议的修改对于解决欺诈问题和提高多元化签证项目(“DV项目”)的完整性是必要的。国务院历来发现有数以百万计的DV项目欺诈性申请,其中包括由第三方代为提交的申请,其中一些是犯罪团伙,在当事人不知情或未经同意的情况下代为提交。在DV-2025年度,国务院发现了250万份欺诈性申请。未经授权的第三方经常联系这些个人,告知他们申请多元化移民签证(“DV”)的机会,并在当事人支付高额费用或同意参与欺诈活动之前,不向其提供申请信息。申请人提交申请后(原文第11892页),会收到一个唯一的确认号码,没有该号码就无法确认申请人是否被选中以继续DV程序。国务院的年度说明建议申请人保留确认号码,并指出国务院今后将不会补发或提供该号码。
The Department explained in the NPRM that the proposed changes are necessary to address fraud and improve the integrity of the DV Program. The Department has historically encountered millions of fraudulent DV Program entries, including entries submitted by third parties, some of them criminal enterprises, on behalf of individuals without their knowledge or consent. In DV-2025, the Department discovered 2.5 million fraudulent entries. Unauthorized third parties often contact these individuals, inform them of the opportunity to apply for a diversity immigrant visa (“DV”), and withhold the entry information unless the individual pays a large fee or agrees to participate in fraudulent activities. Upon submitting an entry, an entrant ( printed page 11892) receives a unique confirmation number, without which it is impossible to confirm whether the entrant was selected to continue the DV process. The Department's annual instructions advise entrants to retain the confirmation number and note that the Department will not replace or provide it in the future.
护照要求还使国务院能够在DV程序的早期阶段更有效地确认申请人的身份,这有助于筛查和审查,并增强国务院维护美国国家安全的能力。
The passport requirements also allow the Department to more effectively confirm petitioners' identities earlier in the DV process, which facilitates screening and vetting and enhances the Department's ability to protect U.S. national security.

二、背景
II. Background
A. DV项目申请
A. Petitioning for the DV Program
DV项目由美国国务院管理。《移民与国籍法》(“INA”)第203(c)条,8 U.S.C. 1101及其后条款,规定在符合一定数量限制的前提下,向“低录取率”国家的“本国公民”提供多元化签证。INA将“低录取率国家”定义为在最近五年期间被录取进入美国的本国公民人数等于或少于50,000人的国家。参见INA第203(c)(1)(B)(ii)条。每年有数百万申请人通过电子申请表登记参加DV项目。[1]
The DV Program is administered by the Department of State. Section 203(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (“INA”), 8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq., makes diversity visas available to aliens who are “natives” of “low-admission” states, subject to certain numerical limitations. The INA defines “low-admission states” as those with equal to or fewer than 50,000 natives admitted to the United States during the most recent five-year period. INA section 203(c)(1)(B)(ii). Millions of petitioners register annually for the DV Program through an electronic entry form.[1]
根据INA第204(a)(1)(I)(iii)条的规定,DV项目的申请(也称为“申请件”)必须采用国务卿(“国务卿”)在法规中规定的表格形式,并包含国务卿所要求的全部信息,且须有国务卿要求的证明文件支持。根据国务院法规22 CFR 42.33的规定,申请表收集申请人及其配偶和/或子女的相关信息。该表格还要求填写距其住所最近的领事馆地点,如果申请人被选中并通过DV项目安排面谈,通常将在该领事馆受理其DV申请。
Under section 204(a)(1)(I)(iii) of the INA, petitions (also referred to as “entries”) for the DV Program must be in the form prescribed in regulations by the Secretary of State (“Secretary”) and contain all information and be supported by documentary evidence that the Secretary requires. As provided in Department regulations at 22 CFR 42.33, the entry form collects information on the petitioner and his or her spouse and/or children. It also requests the location of the consular post nearest to his or her residence, where the application for a DV should generally be adjudicated if the petitioner is selected and scheduled for an interview through the DV Program.
DV项目申请期结束后,[2] 部分申请人将通过计算机随机抽签方式被选中(“中签者”),中签者可以申请DV签证,或者如果其本人身处美国境内且符合其他资格条件,可申请以多元化移民身份调整身份,参见INA第245(a)条。根据INA第201(e)条的规定,每年可提供的DV签证数量为55,000个。[3] 多元化移民必须依照INA和国务院法规的规定,证明其符合签证资格和条件。
After the close of the DV Program entry period,[2] certain petitioners are selected through a randomized computer drawing (“selectees”), and selectees may apply for a DV or, if physically present in the United States and otherwise eligible, may apply to adjust status as a diversity immigrant, see INA section 245(a). Under section 201(e) of the INA, the number of available DVs each year is 55,000.[3] A diversity immigrant must establish his or her qualifications and eligibility for a visa in accordance with the INA and Department regulations.
B. 此前对DV申请的要求
B. Previous Requirements for DV Petition
希望根据DV项目提交申请的外国人须在规定的登记期内填写DS-5501表,即电子多元化签证申请表。在以往的项目年度中,DS-5501表要求提供以下个人身份信息:姓名、性别、出生日期、出生城市和国家、邮寄地址和居住地址、电子邮件地址、电话号码、受教育程度、婚姻状况、子女人数,以及申请人照片。如果该外国人有符合资格的随行家属(配偶和/或子女),DS-5501表还会收集每名随行家属的姓名、性别、出生日期、出生城市和国家以及照片。
An alien seeking to submit a petition under the DV Program completes the DS-5501, Electronic Diversity Visa Entry Form, during the specified registration period. In previous program years, the DS-5501 required the following personal identification information: name, gender, date of birth, city and country of birth, mailing and physical address, email address, phone number, educational attainment, marital status, number of children, and entrant photograph. If the alien had eligible derivatives (spouse and/or child[ren]), the DS-5501 also collected the name, gender, date of birth, city and country of birth, and photograph of each derivative.
C. DV项目中普遍存在的欺诈活动
C. Fraudulent Activities Prevalent in the DV Program
多元化签证(DV)项目欺诈现象十分普遍,以至于联邦贸易委员会(FTC)在2012年发布的一段关于DV欺诈的YouTube视频中专门对此进行了说明。[4] 2012年发生的一起较为严重的大规模第三方欺诈案例中,美国驻达卡(孟加拉国)大使馆通过官方渠道报告称,有一个IP地址提交了超过634,000份申请。孟加拉国当局对协助者展开调查,发现有电脑中存有数千份申请材料,以及伪造的教育文件和摆拍的婚礼照片。
DV Program fraud is so prevalent that the Federal Trade Commission addressed it in a 2012 YouTube video about DV fraud.[4] One of the more egregious examples of large-scale third-party fraud occurred in 2012 when U.S. Embassy Dhaka (Bangladesh) reported through official channels that one IP address was responsible for more than 634,000 entries. Bangladeshi authorities investigated the facilitators and found computers with thousands of applications, along with fake education documents and staged marriage photos.
同样,国务院监察长办公室(OIG)的一份报告[5]披露了美国驻基辅(乌克兰)大使馆的相关情况:有组织的欺诈团伙伪装成旅行社,控制了乌克兰的DV项目。该欺诈团伙通过购买、窃取或从公开渠道获取乌克兰公民的个人信息,将这些乌克兰公民的姓名登记进DV项目在线申请网站,且往往未经本人同意或在其不知情的情况下进行。随后,该欺诈团伙联系数百名被抽中的乌克兰申请人,要求其支付高达15,000美元以换取确认号码。若申请人无力支付,欺诈团伙通常会坚持要求其与某个希望移民美国的人进行假结婚。在这种情况下,该“假配偶”会支付大笔金钱,以便与被抽中的DV申请人配对。
Similarly, a Department Office of the Inspector General (OIG) report [5] highlighted U.S. Embassy Kyiv's (Ukraine) account of organized fraud rings masquerading as travel agencies taking control of the DV Program in Ukraine. By buying, stealing, or otherwise obtaining from public sources, personal information about Ukrainian citizens, the fraud ring entered these Ukrainian citizens' names in the online DV Program website, often without their permission or awareness. The fraud ring then contacted hundreds of Ukrainian selectees and required them to pay up to $15,000 to obtain the confirmation number. If the selectee could not pay, the fraud ring often insisted that he or she enter a sham marriage with a person who had expressed interest in immigrating to the United States. In such a case, the “sham spouse” would pay a substantial amount of money to be paired with a DV selectee.
要求申请人提供有效且未过期护照的信息以及护照个人信息页的扫描件,使未经授权的第三方更难以代表不知情的个人提交DV申请,因为此类第三方通常难以获得这些信息。这些措施同时也减少了DV项目中出现的虚假婚姻数量。在申请阶段即识别出欺诈性申请人,可为审核官员节省大量时间和精力,否则他们将不得不投入大量资源以厘清申请人DV申请表与签证申请之间的差异。
Requiring that entrants include information from a valid, unexpired passport and a scan of the biographic information page makes it significantly more difficult for unauthorized third parties to submit a DV petition on behalf of an unwitting individual, because such parties are less likely to have access to this information. These measures also decrease the number of fraudulent marriages encountered in the DV program. Identifying fraudulent petitioners at the petition stage saves time and effort on the part of adjudicating officers who otherwise dedicate significant resources to clarify discrepancies between a petitioner's DV entry form and the visa application.

E. Implementation

As required by the Paperwork Reduction Act, the Department is securing approval from OMB's Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs to finalize the necessary updates to the DV Entry Form (DS-5501) upon this rule's effective date.
F. 成本与收益
F. Costs and Benefits
一、概述
i. Overview
本规则会为申请人带来新的成本,同时为国家安全带来收益,国务院已确定收益大于成本。这些要求使国务院能够更有效地识别并取消重复申请,这是《移民与国籍法》(INA)第204(a)(1)(I)(i)条所要求的。国务院的反欺诈专家确认,在2025财年的DV项目中,超过250万份重复申请被取消资格。相比之下,在实行此前护照要求的2022财年DV项目中,仅有760,079份重复申请被取消资格。这表明,护照要求能够有效减少欺诈性申请。
The rule results in new costs for petitioners and benefits for national security, and the Department has determined that the benefits outweigh the costs. These requirements allow the Department to more effectively identify and disqualify duplicate entries, as required under INA section 204(a)(1)(I)(i). The Department's fraud prevention experts confirmed that over 2.5 million duplicate entries were disqualified in the FY25 DV Program. Comparatively, during the FY22 DV Program, during which a previous passport requirement was in effect, only 760,079 duplicate entries were disqualified. This is indicative that a passport requirement effectively reduces fraudulent entries.
二、收益
ii. Benefits
本规则通过使国务院能够在申请阶段确认申请人身份并遏制欺诈性申请,从而维护美国国家安全。要求提供护照扫描件可降低伪造护照号码的风险,并使审核人员能够将申请人本国文字的姓名与其英文音译进行比对。这有助于支持安全审查工作,并确保准确识别与申请人姓名相关联的各种音译形式。
This rule protects U.S. national security by enabling the Department to confirm a petitioner's identity at the entry stage and deterring fraudulent entries. Requiring a passport scan reduces the risk of fabricated passport numbers and allows adjudicators to compare the applicant's name in his or her native alphabet with its English transliteration. This supports security vetting and ensures accurate identification of transliterations associated with the applicant's name.
此外,护照信息有助于领事官员根据申请人的出生地及其应计入的“低签发量”外国国籍,核实其是否符合《移民与国籍法》(INA)第203(c)条所要求的资格。此前,多样化签证项目的参加者在DS-5501表格中自行选择其应计入的外国国籍,除非该人提出签证申请,否则无法核实该信息。本规则使国务院能够在流程的更早阶段更好地评估资格。
Additionally, passport information helps consular officers verify the applicant's eligibility based on his or her place of birth and chargeability to a “low-admission” foreign state, as required under INA section 203(c). Previously, an entrant to the Diversity Visa program self-selected his or her foreign state of chargeability on the DS-5501 form, and this information could not be verified unless the individual applied for a visa. This rule allows the Department to better assess eligibility earlier in the process.
最后,收集护照签发日期和到期日期,可提供有关申请人在其本国地位的关键信息,而这些信息若非如此,国务院在审查申请人案件时将无从获得。例如,签发期限有限的护照可能提示领事官员认为与多样化签证(DV)裁定相关的潜在调查方向。所有这些信息都可能引出与安全、经济或INA项下其他不合格事由相关的信息。要求收集护照信息页同样具有若干安全功能,并确保护照的真实性以及护照是签发给正确个人的。
Finally, collection of the dates of issue and expiration provides critical information about the status of the person in his or her own country that would not otherwise be available to the Department in the review of the applicant's case. For example, passports issued for a limited duration period may indicate potential lines of inquiry that a consular officer finds relevant to a DV adjudication. All this information may lead to information relevant to security, economic, or other grounds of ineligibility under the INA. The requirement to collect the signature page likewise serves several security functions and ensures both authenticity and that the passport was issued to the correct individual.
三. 成本
iii. Costs
本法规要求尚未持有有效、未过期护照的申请人在进入多样化签证项目之前获得护照,而非在被选中参加面谈之后再获得护照,但有限例外情形除外。根据国务院领事事务局签证处开展的研究,符合参加多样化签证项目资格的国家中,护照的平均价格为74.43美元。[6] 国务院征求公众意见,以更好地为本成本效益分析提供依据,具体征求公众对以下方面的意见:该估算数字的准确性、符合多样化签证资格国家中护照持有率,以及在这些国家获得护照的未量化负担。国务院在下文中就公众意见作出回应。在公众参与之前,国务院估计,响应新的信息要求平均将额外花费二十分钟。该时间负担估算包括获取护照、在申请中提供所需信息、拍摄或扫描护照,以及上传图像文件。如下文意见回应部分所述,国务院正将估计时间负担修订为额外60分钟,从而使每份回复的估计总时间负担为90分钟。
This regulation requires petitioners who do not already possess a valid, unexpired passport to obtain a passport prior to entry into the DV Program, rather than after being selected for an interview, with limited exceptions. According to research conducted by the Visa Office of the Department's Bureau of Consular Affairs, the average price of a passport in countries that are eligible to participate in the DV Program is $74.43.[6] The Department sought public comment to better inform this cost-benefit analysis, specifically requesting public input on the accuracy of this estimate, rates of passport ownership in DV-eligible countries, and the unquantified burdens of obtaining a passport in those countries. The Department addresses comments from the public below. Prior to public participation, the Department estimated that, on average, responding to the new information requirements would take an additional twenty minutes. This time burden estimate included gathering the passport, supplying the required information in the application, photographing or scanning the passport, and uploading the image file. As discussed in the comment response section below, the Department is revising the estimated time burden to an additional 60 minutes, resulting in a total estimated time burden of 90 minutes per response.
国务院估计,DV-2027项目的参加者人数约为1000万人。该数字基于类似护照规则在2021至2023财政年度多样化签证登记期间实施时的历史数据。在此期间,国务院收到的参加申请为:670万份(2021财政年度)、730万份(2022财政年度)以及960万份(2023财政年度)。
The Department estimates that it will see approximately 10 million entrants for DV-2027. This number is based on historical data from when a similar passport rule was in effect during the Diversity Visa registration periods that took place for the program fiscal years of 2021-2023. During that time frame, the Department received 6.7 million entries (in FY 2021), 7.3 million entries (in FY 2022), and 9.6 million entries (in FY 2023).
综合考虑这些历史数据、近期多样化签证参加申请的趋势,以及新增的1美元参加费,国务院确定DS-5501表格年度总回复数的最准确估算为1000万份。为对该表格修订后的时间负担进行成本量化,国务院依据此前对国际劳工组织[7]针对符合DV-2025项目参加资格的国家[8]的工资数据所作分析,该分析计算出的平均时薪为4.56美元。由于国务院在时间负担中增加了一小时,每份回复因此增加的小时成本负担为4.56美元。将该数字乘以预计的潜在回复人数(1000万),国务院估计额外时间成本为4560万美元,全球年度估计总成本为6840万美元。
Taking into consideration this historical data, recent DV entry trends, and the addition of the new $1 entry fee, the Department determined the most accurate estimate for total annual responses to the DS-5501 is 10 million. To place a cost value on the revised time burden for the form, the Department relies on a prior analysis of wage data from the International Labor Organization [7] for countries that were eligible to participate in the DV-2025 Program [8] which calculated an average hourly wage of $4.56. Since the Department is adding one additional hour to the time burden, each response has an additional hour cost burden of $4.56. Multiplied by the projected number of potential respondents (10,000,000), the Department estimates the additional time cost to be $45,600,000, for a total annual estimated cost of $68,400,000 globally.

A. Comments Expressing General Support for Passport Requirements
许多评论者支持该规则,认同其能够防止大规模欺诈并提升项目诚信度。他们明确指出防止欺诈是支持多元化签证申请人在登记时须随附有效且未过期护照扫描件这一要求的关键原因。这些评论者常常描述其亲身经历或目睹的签证欺诈行为,并强调护照要求能够减少重复申请、未经授权的申请、身份盗用以及第三方代理人的剥削行为。
Many commenters support the rule, agreeing that it prevents widespread fraud and improves program integrity. They explicitly cite fraud prevention as the key reason for supporting the requirement that DV entrants include a scan of a valid, unexpired passport with their registration. These commenters often describe personally experiencing or witnessing visa fraud and emphasize the ability of the passport requirements to reduce duplicate entries, unauthorized entries, identity theft, and exploitation by third-party agents.
部门回应:本部门感谢评论者对护照要求表示的支持,并认可其在防止欺诈和提高多样化签证(DV)项目完整性方面的重要作用。正如《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中所述,欺诈行为一直是该项目面临的持续性挑战,存在重复申请、身份盗用以及第三方代理人滥用等已有记录的案例,这些行为损害了该项目的公平性和有效性。护照要求是旨在解决这些薄弱环节、遏制欺诈行为并确保提交申请者是认真对待其参与资格的重要保障措施。本部门认识到评论者分享的亲身经历,他们曾目睹或受到签证欺诈的影响,这进一步凸显了实施相关措施以保护该项目完整性并确保其按预期运作的重要性。
Department Response: The Department appreciates the support expressed by commenters regarding the passport requirement and their recognition of its importance in preventing fraud and improving the integrity of the DV program. Fraud has been a persistent challenge in the program, with documented cases of duplicate entries, identity theft, and exploitation by third-party agents undermining the fairness and effectiveness of the program, as discussed in the NPRM. The passport requirement is a critical safeguard intended to address these vulnerabilities, deter fraudulent practices, and ensure that individuals submitting entries are serious about their participation. The Department acknowledges the personal experiences shared by commenters who have witnessed or been affected by visa fraud, which underscore the importance of implementing measures to protect the integrity of the program and ensure that it operates as intended.
B. 对护照要求表示普遍反对的评论意见
B. Comments Expressing General Opposition to Passport Requirements
一些评论者普遍反对该规则,理由是其无法防止欺诈或提高项目完整性。这些评论者指出,本部门未能证明护照上传功能具备检测伪造文件所需的技术要素。评论者认为,意图利用毫无戒心的申请人的行为者仍可使用虚假或其他可获取的文件提交未经授权的申请。少数评论者声称,本部门未能证明存在广泛的签证欺诈(第11895页),以此作为该规则的正当理由。其他评论者承认个人曾经历或目睹过欺诈行为,但批评本部门认为具体统计数据不足,成本效益分析也不够充分。
Some commenters generally oppose the rule on the basis that it would not prevent fraud or improve program integrity. These commenters note that the Department failed to establish that the passport upload feature had the technical elements needed to detect fraudulent documents. Commenters argue actors seeking to exploit unsuspecting registrants can still use false or otherwise available documentation to submit unauthorized entries. A few commenters claim the Department failed to establish the ( printed page 11895) existence of widespread visa fraud to justify the rule. Others acknowledge personally experiencing or witnessing fraud but criticize about what they viewed as insufficient concrete statistics and a lackluster cost-benefit analysis.
部门回应:本部门研究了遏制欺诈性申请的其他可能途径,并确定有效护照扫描件是最具成本效益的方法。虽然没有任何单一措施能够消除所有欺诈行为,但本部门认为,护照要求是解决DV项目中最普遍薄弱环节的关键一步,将遏制身份盗用以及第三方代理人的滥用行为。历史上,这些代理人曾代表毫不知情的个人提交未经授权的申请。这种方法使本部门能够在抽签前对护照信息进行进一步核查验证,这与本部门目前用于检测重复申请和不符合标准照片的流程类似。评论者认为,恶意行为者将能够提交虚假文件或获取申请人的护照,从而提交虚假申请,本部门承认这种可能性的存在。然而,这项附加要求使不法分子更难提交多份或未经授权的申请,因为现在每份申请都必须与唯一、有效且未过期的护照相关联。申请人还将被告知应保留其用于申请DV项目的护照的扫描件或复印件,如果他们无法提供充分证据证明其申请表上的护照号码在登记时是其有效护照,则其申请可能被拒绝。
Department Response: The Department examined alternative possible avenues for deterring fraudulent entries and determined that a valid passport scan is the most cost-effective approach. While no single measure can eliminate all instances of fraud, the Department believes that the passport requirement is a critical step in addressing the most pervasive vulnerabilities in the DV program and will deter identity theft and exploitation by third-party agents, who have historically submitted unauthorized entries on behalf of unsuspecting individuals. This approach allows the Department to conduct further checks verifying the passport information before selection, similar to the process the Department currently conducts to detect duplicate entries and photos that do not meet the required standards. Commenters argue that mala fide actors will be able submit false documents or obtain the passports of applicants allowing them to submit fake entries, and the Departments acknowledges this possibility. However, this additional requirement makes it more difficult for bad actors to submit multiple or unauthorized entries, as each entry must now be tied to a unique, valid, and unexpired passport. Entrants will also be advised to retain a scanned copy of photocopy of the passport they use to apply for the DV program and may be denied if they are unable to produce sufficient proof that the passport number on their application was their valid passport at the time of registration.
本部门已充分记录了DV项目中存在广泛欺诈的案例,其中包括2012年孟加拉国超过60万份欺诈性申请案件、2013年乌克兰欺诈团伙在以申请人名义提交虚假申请后向其收取15,000美元的案件,以及2023年柬埔寨欺诈团伙采用类似手法,向中签申请人收取5,000至30,000美元的案件,这些内容在NPRM及本规则随附的补充信息中均有详细说明。此外,正如评论意见中所述,多位评论者描述了其亲身经历或目睹签证欺诈的情况。
The Department has well documented cases of extensive fraud in the DV Program, to include cases of over 600,000 fraudulent entries in Bangladesh in 2012, Ukrainian fraud rings charging DV applicants $15,000 after submitting a fake entry in their name in 2013, and a Cambodian fraud ring using a similar practice and charging selected applicants $5,000-$30,000 in 2023, as detailed in the NPRM and supplementary information accompanying this rule. Furthermore, as stated in the comments, multiple commenters describe personally experiencing or witnessing visa fraud.

C. Comments Proposing Alternative Measures To Address Fraud
一些评论者建议,可以通过替代或补充措施来应对欺诈问题,例如在后续阶段加强身份核验或在面谈期间实施更严格的审查。评论者还建议通过生物识别验证、面部识别或数字身份工具来加强防欺诈措施。
Some commenters suggest that fraud can be addressed through alternative or additional measures, such as enhanced identity verification at later stages or stricter vetting during interviews. Commenters also suggest enhancing fraud prevention through biometric verification, facial recognition, or digital identity tools.
部门回应:本部门同意,健全的身份核验程序对于维护该项目的完整性至关重要,并已经实施了许多建议中提到的措施。例如,本部门在签证面谈期间采用了强化的身份核验程序,由领事官员综合评估申请人提供的全部信息,包括个人基本资料、人际关系以及辅助证明文件。此外,本部门还使用人脸识别技术以侦测身份欺诈,并辅以生物识别技术及其他可供本部门使用的类似工具。尽管这些工具带来显著益处,但当与流程早期阶段的防范措施(例如本规则所拟议的护照要求)相结合时,其效果最为显著。多元化签证项目中的大多数欺诈行为都源于登记阶段,表现为重复或近似重复登记,或由第三方代表不知情的申请人进行登记。本部门预计,要求在登记参加该项目时扫描护照将能够遏制此类登记行为,并在申请人进入面谈阶段之前降低欺诈发生的可能性。在抽签结果公布后,如上文所述,本部门会在签发签证之前核实所有签证申请人的身份,但这些努力并不能阻止例如第三方提交欺诈性或未经授权的登记这类行为。
Department Response: The Department agrees that robust identity verification processes are essential to maintaining the integrity of the program and has already implemented many of the measures suggested. For example, the Department employs enhanced identity verification procedures during visa interviews, where consular officers assess the totality of information provided by the applicants, including biographical details, relationships, and supporting documentation. Additionally, facial recognition technology is used to detect identity fraud, alongside biometric technology and other similar tools available to the Department. While these tools offer significant benefits, they are most effective when combined with safeguards at earlier stages of the process, such as the passport requirement contemplated in this rule. Most fraud in the Diversity Visa program originates at the time of entry, in the form of duplicate or near duplicate entries, or entries by third parties on behalf of unwitting applicants. The Department anticipates that requiring a passport scan at the time of registering to participate in the Program will deter such entries and reduce the likelihood of fraud before applicants reach the interview stage. Following selection, the Department verifies the identities of all visa applicants before issuance of a visa as described above, but these efforts do not deter, for example, third parties from submitting fraudulent or unauthorized entries.
D. 关于可及性与公平性的意见
D. Comments Related to Accessibility and Equity
部分评论者对在低收入和政局不稳定国家,以及在服务资源有限的农村或偏远地区获取护照所面临的经济和后勤方面的困难表示担忧。这些评论者指出,无国籍人士或来自不签发护照国家的人员可能会被不公平地排除在外。其他评论者强调,该规则可能使参与者偏向较富裕的城市人口,从而降低申请人群体在地域和社会经济方面的多样性。该规则的支持者承认存在这些困难,但认为认真的申请人本应已持有护照,而该规则可确保只有真正有诚意且符合条件的人员参与该项目。
Some commenters raise concerns about the financial and logistical challenges of obtaining passports in low-income and politically unstable countries as well as in rural/remote areas with limited access to services. These commenters note that stateless individuals or those from countries that do not issue passports may be unfairly excluded. Others emphasize that the rule may skew participation toward wealthier, urban populations, reducing geographic and socioeconomic diversity in the applicant pool. Proponents of the rule acknowledge these challenges but argue that serious applicants should already have passports and that the rule ensures only committed and qualified individuals participate.
部门回应:根据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第222(b)条,在签发任何移民签证之前,申请人通常必须出示有效且未过期的护照或其他适当的旅行证件,或根据22 CFR 42.2(d)、(e)或(g)(2)条所授权的规定,符合该要求的豁免条件。在制定本规则时,本部门仅是要求申请人在登记参加多元化签证项目时出示其护照,此举依据《移民与国籍法》第204(a)(1)(I)(iii)条获得授权。尽管本部门理解获取护照可能对某些人构成困难,但此项要求对于保护该项目免遭滥用,并确保其按预期运作而言是必要的。本部门还采取措施减轻该要求带来的影响,包括根据上述授权规定,为无国籍人士等群体提供特定豁免。
Department Response: Before the issuance of any immigrant visa, an applicant must generally present a valid, unexpired passport or other suitable travel document pursuant to INA 222(b), or qualify for an exception to this requirement, as authorized under 22 CFR 42.2(d), (e), or (g)(2). In adopting this Rule, the Department is merely requiring that applicants present their passports at the time of entry into the Diversity Visa program, as authorized under INA 204(a)(1)(I)(iii). While the Department understands that obtaining a passport may pose challenges for some individuals, this requirement is necessary to protect the program from exploitation and to ensure that it operates as intended. The Department has also taken steps to mitigate the impact of this requirement by allowing for certain exceptions, including for individuals who are stateless, consistent with the authorities above.
本部门指出,多元化签证项目的设计初衷是促进移民的多样性,而非保证特定地区或特定群体获得准入资格。护照要求平等适用于全世界所有申请人,其实施旨在为全体参与者的利益而加强该项目的完整性。尽管本部门承认该要求可能导致未持有护照者提交的登记数量减少,但如上文所述,根据以往年度项目的现有数据,本部门预计仍将继续收到足够数量的登记,以便按地区分配使用全部可用签证名额。
The Department notes the Diversity Visa program was designed to promote diversity in immigration, not to guarantee access for specific regions or demographics. The passport requirement applies equally to all applicants worldwide, and its implementation is intended to strengthen the program's integrity for the benefit of all participants. While the Department acknowledges the requirement could result in fewer entries submitted by persons who do not possess a passport, the Department anticipates continuing to receive sufficient entries to facilitate issuance of all available visa numbers, including on a regional basis, based on data available from prior program years, as explained above.
E. 关于附加要求和/或建议的意见
E. Comments Related to Additional Requirements and/or Suggestions
许多评论者建议对多元化签证项目的登记者增加附加要求,包括以下内容:
Many commenters recommend additional requirements for entrants to the DV program, to include the following:
一、登记费
i. Entry Fee
部分评论者建议收取1至250美元的登记费,以遏制轻率申请并为项目管理提供资金支持。其中若干评论者还建议对来自低收入国家的申请人给予费用减免。另有评论者则表示不应收取任何登记费用,理由是担心经济门槛和费用可能会损害该项目对低收入人群的可及性。
Some commenters suggest introducing an entry fee of $1-$250 to deter frivolous applications and fund program administration. Several of these individuals also recommend waivers for applicants from low-income countries. Other commenters express that there should be no entry fees, citing concerns regarding the risk that financial barriers and fees may undermine the program's accessibility for low-income individuals.
部门回应:所有寻求参加多元化签证(DV)项目的申请人必须在提交申请时缴纳1美元费用,且该名义费用不设任何豁免。( printed page 11896)本部门已于2025年9月16日发布一项最终规则,宣布收取1美元的多元化签证登记费。[13]该费用使本部门能够更公平地分摊管理DV项目日益增长的成本,包括DV申请系统的年度维护、数据存储、自动随机抽签处理以及相关安全审查。成本负担现将由所有申请人共同分担,而不再仅由被选中继续办理DV程序的一小部分成功登记者承担。此外,本部门预计此项变更还将有助于减少企图利用毫不知情的潜在申请人的不法分子提交的可疑登记。
Department Response: All entrants seeking to participate in the DV program must pay a $1 fee at the time of submitting their entry, and no waivers ( printed page 11896) will be available for the nominal fee. The Department published a final rule announcing a $1 Diversity Visa Registration Fee on September 16, 2025.[13] This fee allows the Department to more fairly distribute the rising costs of managing the DV program, including annual maintenance of the DV entry system, data storage, automated randomized selection processing, and associated security reviews. The cost burden will now be shared among all petitioners, rather than by only the small percentage of successful DV registrants who are selected to continue the DV process. Additionally, the Department anticipates this change will also help reduce specious registrations by actors seeking to exploit unsuspecting potential entrants.
二、教育证明
ii. Education Proof
许多评论人士主张在申请阶段要求提供高中文凭或同等学历证明,以防止不符合资格的申请人占用抽签名额。
Many commenters advocate for requiring proof of a high school diploma or equivalent at the entry stage to prevent ineligible applicants from taking lottery slots.
部门回应:本部门在发布年度申请须知时,已告知所有潜在DV申请人须具备高中文凭或同等学历的要求,申请人须在DV申请中注明其所获得的教育程度。由于全球范围内满足此类要求的文件种类繁多,本部门通常依靠在当地熟悉并具备各国特定文件经验的领事官员,在面谈时确定申请人是否符合该要求。若要求在登记时即提供文凭或其他教育证明文件,将大幅增加本部门预选程序的复杂性和成本。此外,尽管此类要求可能在一定程度上遏制部分不实申请,但本部门认为,申请人有效且未过期护照中的信息可能更为安全可靠,因而更难以伪造,或被用于提交未经授权的第三方申请。
Department Response: The Department informs all potential DV entrants of the requirement to have a high school diploma or equivalent when it publishes the yearly instructions, and entrants must specify the level of education obtained in their DV entry. Due to the variety of documents globally that fulfill these requirements, the Department generally relies on consular officers in the field, who have knowledge of and experience with country-specific documents, to determine whether the applicant meets these requirements at the time of interview. Requiring diplomas or other educational documentation at the time of registration would substantially increase the complexity and cost of the Department's pre-selection process. Additionally, while it is possible that some frivolous entries may be deterred by such a requirement, the Department considers that the information from an entrant's valid, unexpired passport is likely to be more secure, and therefore more difficult to falsify or otherwise be used to submit unauthorized third-party entries.
三、英语能力
iii. English Proficiency
部分评论人士建议引入基本英语语言要求,以确保被选中者能更好地融入美国社会。另有评论人士建议本部门提供多语言资源,以确保全球范围内对DV项目的理解,并避免成为DV项目欺诈行为的受害者。
Some commenters suggest introducing basic English language requirements to ensure selectees are better prepared to integrate into U.S. society. Others suggest the Department provide multilingual resources to ensure global understanding of the DV program and of how to avoid falling victim to DV program fraud.
部门回应:DV项目依据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第203(c)条设立,旨在为来自历史上赴美移民比例较低国家的个人提供移民机会。该法定框架并未将英语语言能力列为资格条件之一,增加此类要求亦无法遏制或以其他方式解决DV项目中存在的欺诈问题的根本原因。欺诈性申请主要与身份盗用、提交多份申请以及第三方中介的不当利用等问题有关,而要求英语能力并不能缓解上述任何一种问题。
Department Response: The DV program was established under INA 203(c) to provide immigration opportunities to individuals from countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States. The statutory framework does not include English language proficiency as a criterion for eligibility, and adding such a requirement would not deter or otherwise address the underlying problem of fraud in the DV program. Fraudulent entries are primarily tied to issues such as identity theft, submission of multiple entries, and exploitation by third-party facilitators, none of which would be mitigated by requiring English proficiency.
四、结婚证明
iv. Marriage Certificate
部分评论人士建议本部门要求声称已婚的申请人提交结婚证书作为证明,以防止虚假婚姻申报。
Some commenters recommend the Department require entrants claiming to have a spouse to submit a marriage certificate as proof to prevent fraudulent marriage claims.
部门回应:根据《联邦法规汇编》第22篇第42.33(b)条的规定,所有申请均须包含申请人配偶及所有亲生子女的姓名、照片、出生日期和出生地,以及所有合法收养的子女和继子女(须为未婚且截至初次申请之日未满21周岁)的相关信息。即使配偶目前并未与申请人共同居住,和/或不会随申请人一同移民,配偶随行家属信息仍须予以包含,除非双方已合法分居或已办理离婚手续。由于全球范围内表明婚姻状况的文件种类繁多,且某些国家和文化中普遍存在缺乏官方认证的习俗性或宗教性婚姻,本部门依靠在当地熟悉并具备各国特定要求经验的领事官员,在面谈时确定婚姻在移民用途上的有效性。
Department Response: Department regulations at 22 CFR 42.33(b) require that all entries include the name, photograph, date, and place of birth of the entrant's spouse and all natural children, as well as all legally adopted children and stepchildren who are unmarried and under the age of 21 as of the date of the initial entry. Spousal derivatives must be included even if the spouse does not currently reside with the entrant and/or will not immigrate with such entrant, unless they are legally separated from the entrant or have obtained a divorce. Due to the variety of documents globally that indicate marital status, and the prevalence in certain countries and cultures of customary or religious marriages that lack official certification, the Department relies on consular officers in the field, who have knowledge of and experience with country-specific requirements to determine the validity of a marriage for immigration purposes at the time of interview.
国务院还建立了完善的机制,以在签证申请过程中处理虚假婚姻申报问题。被选中接受进一步处理的申请人必须提供证明所申报婚姻关系的证据,包括在可获得的情况下提供结婚证,并在某些情况下提供其他文件以证明该关系的真实性。如怀疑存在欺诈行为,领事官员可要求提供进一步证据或开展额外审查。国务院认为,现行措施足以应对有关虚假婚姻申报的担忧,无需在登记阶段施加额外要求。这与国务院对多样化签证(DV)项目的管理方式相一致,该项目旨在实现便捷和简化,使来自代表性不足国家的人员能够在不承受过度行政负担的情况下参与该项目。若在登记时即要求提供结婚证,将大幅增加该流程的复杂性,并可能阻碍某些符合条件的外国人申请。
The Department also has robust mechanisms in place to address fraudulent marriage claims during the visa application process. Applicants who are selected for further processing must provide evidence of the claimed marital relationship, including a marriage certificate where available and, in some cases, additional documentation to establish the bona fides of the relationship. Consular officers may request further evidence or conduct additional review if fraud is suspected. The Department believes these existing measures are sufficient to address concerns about fraudulent marriage claims without imposing additional requirements at the registration stage. This aligns with the Department's administration of the DV program, which is designed to be accessible and straightforward, allowing individuals from underrepresented countries to participate without undue administrative burdens. Requiring a marriage certificate at the time of registration would significantly increase the complexity of the process and could deter certain eligible aliens from entering.

F. Comments Related to Potential Implementation Challenges
一、时间安排
i. Timing
许多评论者对DV-2027项目年度实施护照规定的通知时间过短表示担忧。评论者指出,一些国家的护照办理时间可能需要数月,并声称对于尚未持有护照的潜在申请人而言,可能没有足够的时间在本项目年度内办理护照。部分评论者建议将该规定推迟至DV-2028或以后年度实施,以便申请人有充足时间做准备。
Many commenters express concerns about the short notice for implementing the passport rule for the DV-2027 program year. Commenters note that processing times for passports in some countries could take several months, and claim there may not be enough time for prospective entrants without passports to obtain them for this program year. Some commenters recommend postponing the rule to DV-2028 or later to allow entrants sufficient time to prepare.
国务院答复:本规定是一项早该实施的防欺诈措施。国务院认识到,全球各地护照签发流程差异很大,部分个人可能因后勤或行政障碍而面临延误。然而,护照要求是提升多样化签证项目完整性的关键措施,有助于减少欺诈性申报及项目完整性方面的其他薄弱环节。国务院预计,推迟2027财年多样化签证项目申请期的开放时间,将有助于减轻本规定对那些原本尚未取得护照人员的影响。国务院不会将该要求推迟至DV-2028项目年度实施,因为那样将不必要地再给予恶意行为者一年的时间去利用潜在申请人,并将延误旨在促进美国国家安全的关键审查措施的实施。最后,国务院指出,在某一特定年度无法参加多样化签证项目的个人,仍可在其所属国家符合资格的未来项目年度继续参加。(印刷页第11897页)
Department Response: This rule is a long-overdue fraud prevention effort. The Department recognizes that passport issuance processes vary widely across the globe and that some individuals may face delays due to logistical or administrative barriers. However, the passport requirement is a critical measure to enhance the integrity of the DV program by reducing fraudulent entries and other vulnerabilities in the program's integrity. The Department anticipates the deferral of the opening of the entry period for the 2027 Diversity Visa program year will mitigate the impact of this Rule on persons who would not yet have obtained a passport otherwise. The Department is not postponing this requirement until the DV-2028 program year because that would unnecessarily allow another year of mala fide actors to take advantage of potential entrants and would delay the implementation of critical screening intended to advance the U.S. national security. Finally, the Department notes that individuals who are unable to participate in the DV program in a given year remain able to participate in future program years for which their country is eligible. ( printed page 11897)
二、技术问题
ii. Technical Issues
一些评论者担心,在登记阶段要求扫描护照个人信息页可能会在技术上造成系统瓶颈。另一些评论者要求国务院确保基础设施能够处理此类上传而不出现系统故障或延误,他们声称系统目前已存在此类问题。少数评论者建议国务院在全面实施该规定之前,先在高欺诈风险国家开展试点测试。

A few commenters suggest that the Department conduct pilot testing in high-fraud countries before attempting to implement the rule globally.
国务院答复:国务院在每个选择周期结束后都会对多样化签证登记和选择系统进行审查,然后根据新要求对系统进行更新,并开展测试以确保系统正常运行。国务院同样已对系统的护照扫描上传功能(以及接受1.00美元名义费用的功能)进行了测试,以确保系统在实施该项目年度能够按预期运行。根据此次测试结果所显示的系统能力,国务院认为无需进一步延迟即可适应这些变更。
Department Response: The Department reviews the DV entry and selection system after each selection period, and then updates the system based on new requirements and conducts testing to ensure that the system operates properly. The Department has likewise tested the system's passport scan upload capability (as well as the acceptance of the $1.00 nominal fee) to ensure the system will function as intended for the program year of implementation. Based on the systems capabilities demonstrated in the results of this testing, the Department believes no further delay is necessary to accommodate these changes.

一些评论者建议,鉴于这些额外要求,国务院应努力改善多样化签证项目网站的功能性,确保移动端优化、符合《网页内容无障碍指南》(WCAG)标准,并为上传护照扫描件提供清晰指引,以提升整体用户体验。

部门回应:国务院在每个多元化签证年度申请期开始前,每年都会对多元化签证申请网站进行审查,并将就新申请年度引入的护照扫描件上传及其他要求提供明确指导。此外,国务院将继续遵守美国电子和信息技术无障碍法律,例如经修订的1973年《康复法》第508条(29 U.S.C. 794d)中规定的相关法律。

Additionally, the Department will continue to comply with the United States' electronic and information technology accessibility laws, such as those codified in Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended (29 U.S.C. 794d).
G. 关于隐私与安全的意见
G. Comments on Privacy and Security
许多评论者对护照扫描件将如何存储、加密以及如何防止数据泄露或滥用表示担忧。他们认为,国务院必须确保采取隐私和安全保护措施来保护护照文件。
Many commenters express concerns about how passport scans will be stored, encrypted, and protected from data breaches or misuse. They argue the Department must ensure privacy and security measures are in place to protect passport documents.
部门回应:国务院依照美国法律,每日在其所有领事平台上收集个人身份信息(PII)。具体而言,对于多元化签证申请人,通过多元化签证项目提交的护照信息存储在符合严格联邦信息安全标准的安全系统中,包括《联邦信息安全现代化法案》(FISMA)所确立的标准。这些系统旨在保护敏感数据免遭未经授权的访问、滥用或泄露。国务院采用先进的加密协议,以确保护照数据的安全传输和存储,并严格限制只有经授权的人员才能访问该信息。
Department Response: The Department collects Personally Identifiable Information (PII) in accordance with U.S. law on a daily basis across all of its consular platforms. Specifically, for DVs entrants, passport information submitted through the DV program is stored in secure systems that comply with stringent federal information security standards, including those established by the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA). These systems are designed to protect sensitive data from unauthorized access, misuse, or breaches. The Department employs advanced encryption protocols to ensure that passport data is securely transmitted and stored, and access to this information is strictly limited to authorized personnel.
此外,国务院持续监测其系统的漏洞,并定期进行安全评估,以识别和应对潜在风险。如发生数据泄露事件,国务院已建立相应的应对协议,以迅速作出反应并减轻对受影响个人的影响。
Additionally, the Department continuously monitors its systems for vulnerabilities and conducts regular security assessments to identify and address potential risks. In the event of a data breach, the Department has established protocols to respond swiftly and mitigate any impact on affected individuals.
H. 建议护照规则豁免的意见
H. Comments Suggesting Passport Rule Exemptions
许多评论者建议对无法获得护照的个人给予护照规则豁免。一些人认为,目前针对无国籍人士和冲突地区居民的豁免范围过于狭窄。评论者建议为处于冲突地区或政局不稳定地区的个人授权临时护照豁免,并为无国籍人士或面临极端经济困难的人提供豁免。一些评论者建议国务院允许获得豁免的申请人提交其他形式的身份证明文件,例如国民身份证或出生证明。
Many commenters suggest passport rule exemptions for individuals who are unable to obtain passports. Some feel that existing exemptions for stateless individuals and conflict zone residents are too narrow. Commenters propose authorizing temporary passport waivers for individuals in conflict zones or politically unstable regions and exemptions for stateless individuals or those facing extreme financial hardship. Some commenters suggest the Department allow exempted entrants to submit alternative forms of identification, such as national IDs or birth certificates.
部门回应:该规则包含以下护照豁免情形:无国籍申请人;属于共产党控制国家国民且无法从该共产党控制国家政府获得护照的申请人;以及在面谈时经逐案认定,已经获得或预计将获得国土安全部部长与国务卿批准的个别豁免的受益人的申请人。这些要求与根据22 CFR 42.2(d)、(e)和(g)(2)规定无需出示护照的移民签证申请人类别保持一致。申请豁免的申请人必须提供证据以支持其主张,此类申请将经过仔细审查,以确保符合既定标准。
Department Response: The rule includes passport exemptions for: petitioners who are stateless; petitioners who are nationals of a Communist-controlled country and unable to obtain a passport from the government of the Communist-controlled country; and petitioners who are, or expect to be, the beneficiary of an individual waiver approved by the Secretary of Homeland Security and the Secretary of State, as determined on a case-by-case basis at the time of interview. These requirements are consistent with the categories of immigrant visa applicants who are not required to present passports pursuant to 22 CFR 42.2(d), (e), and (g)(2). Applicants seeking an exemption must provide evidence to support their claim, and such requests are carefully reviewed to ensure they meet the established criteria.
国务院认为,扩大这些豁免范围或允许使用替代形式的证明文件,可能会削弱护照要求作为防止欺诈措施的有效性。接受国民身份证等其他形式的身份证明文件,会违背护照规则的初衷,因为在某些国家,这类文件更难以可靠核实,也更容易被伪造。此外,仅凭经济困难并不构成护照规则豁免的合理理由,因为赴美移民程序费用高昂,如果申请人无力负担护照费用,那么即便其获选,也不太可能有经济能力完成移民程序。此外,为2027财年多元化签证项目获得的护照,只要在有效期内,也可用于未来的多元化签证年度。
The Department believes expanding these exemptions or allowing for alternate forms of documentation would risk undermining the effectiveness of the passport requirement as a fraud prevention measure. Accepting other forms of identification, such as national IDs, defeats the purpose of the passport rule because these documents are more difficult to reliably verify and are easier to alter in some countries. Additionally, financial hardship alone does not constitute reasonable justification for a passport rule exemption, as the immigration process to the United States is expensive and an entrant unable to afford a passport is unlikely to have the financial means to complete the immigration process if he or she is selected. Further, a passport obtained for DV-2027 could be used for future DV years for which it remains valid and unexpired.
I. 关于法律风险的意见
I. Comments Related to Legal Vulnerabilities
一些评论者对重新引入多元化签证护照规则的合法性提出质疑。
Some commenters question the legality of re-introducing a DV passport rule.
一、E.B诉国务院案
i. E.B v. Department of State
多位评论者指出,法院过去曾推翻类似的护照规则,许多人特别提及E.B诉国务院案,并认为该规则可能会因类似理由受到质疑。
Several commenters note that the courts struck down a similar passport rule in the past, many specifically referring to E.B v. Department of State and argue this rule may be challenged on similar grounds.
部门回应:在E.B.诉国务院案(E.B. v. Department of State, 583 F. Supp. 3d 58, 61 (D.D.C. 2022))中,哥伦比亚特区联邦地区法院认定,国务院2019年要求多样化签证(DV)入选者提供有效的、未过期护照信息的临时最终规则系非法制定,因为该规则未按照《行政程序法》(Administrative Procedures Act, APA)的规定经过公众通知和评议程序。尽管国务卿已认定,与签证业务及签发相关的所有政策(除其他事项外)构成美国在《行政程序法》下的外交事务职能,[14]但国务院仍就本规则制定征求了公众意见,并通过本最终规则作出回应,从而降低了该规则可能因类似程序性理由而受到质疑的风险。
Department Response: In E.B. v. Department of State, 583 F. Supp. 3d 58, 61 (D.D.C. 2022), the District Court for the District of Columbia found that the Department's 2019 interim final rule requiring DV entrants to provide information from a valid, unexpired passport was unlawfully promulgated because it did not undergo public notice and comment under the Administrative Procedures Act (APA). While the Secretary of State has determined that all policy related to visa operations and issuance, among other matters, constitutes a foreign affairs function of the United States under the Administrative Procedures Act,[14] the Department has nonetheless sought public comment on this rulemaking, to which the Department is responding via the present final rule, mitigating the risk that the rule may be challenged on similar procedural grounds.
二、武断和任意性审查
ii. Arbitrary and Capricious Review
《行政程序法》指示法院“应认定并撤销被认定为武断、任意、构成裁量权滥用或以其他方式不符合法律规定的机构行为、认定和结论。”(印刷版第11898页)一些评论指出,该规则可能因在未能实现其既定目标的同时不成比例地排斥弱势群体而被指控为根据《行政程序法》属于“武断和任意”的规定。这些评论强调该规则对来自低收入国家、农村地区和冲突地区的申请人造成了不成比例的负担,且没有明确证据表明该规则能够有效遏制欺诈行为,并批评该规则是一项过于宽泛的措施,未能达到相称性标准。
The APA instructs courts to “hold unlawful and set aside agency action, findings, and conclusions found to be arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of ( printed page 11898) discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.” Some comments express that the rule may be challenged as “arbitrary and capricious” under the APA because it disproportionately excludes vulnerable populations while failing to achieve its stated goals. They highlight the disproportionate burden on participants from low-income countries, rural areas, and conflict zones, without clear evidence that the rule will effectively deter fraud and criticize the rule as an overbroad measure that fails to meet the standard of proportionality.
部门回应:国务院已认真考虑了这些问题,并仍然确信该规则符合《行政程序法》的要求,且该入境要求对公众造成的成本与其既定目标相称适当。
Department Response: The Department has carefully considered these issues and remains confident the rule is consistent with the APA's requirements and that the costs of the entry requirements on the public are proportionally appropriate to its stated goals.
《行政程序法》要求各机构确保其规则不具有武断性、任意性或其他违法情形。在制定本规则的过程中,国务院对DV项目的脆弱性进行了全面分析,包括已记录在案的广泛欺诈、身份盗用、重复申请以及第三方代理人滥用等案例。护照要求通过将每份申请与唯一、有效且未过期的护照相关联,直接应对了这些脆弱性,从而减少了此类欺诈活动的发生机会。
The APA requires agencies to ensure that rules are not arbitrary, capricious, or otherwise unlawful. In developing this rule, the Department conducted a thorough analysis of the DV program's vulnerabilities, including documented cases of widespread fraud, identity theft, duplicate entries, and exploitation by third-party agents. The passport requirement directly addresses these vulnerabilities by tying each entry to a unique, valid, and unexpired passport, thereby reducing opportunity for such fraudulent activity.
部分评论者担忧,获取护照可能对某些人造成困难,特别是对低收入国家、农村地区和冲突地区的个人而言。为缓解这些担忧,如上文所述,本规则包含有限的豁免规定,旨在确保该规则不会不成比例地排斥无国籍人员等弱势群体,同时维护该项目的完整性。与此同时,国务院认为,更广泛的豁免既会削弱该规则按预期打击欺诈的能力,也最终无法使此类个人(如其获选)免于按照《移民与国籍法》(INA)第222(b)条的一般要求在签证申请时出示护照。基于上述原因,国务院认为该规则体现了其对《行政程序法》要求的审慎考量,以及在维护项目完整性与照顾弱势群体可及性之间取得平衡的必要性。
Certain commenters raised concerns that obtaining a passport may pose challenges for some individuals, particularly those in low-income countries, rural areas, and conflict zones. To mitigate these concerns, the rule includes limited exemptions, as discussed above, which are designed to ensure the rule does not disproportionately exclude vulnerable populations such as stateless individuals, while maintaining the integrity of the program. At the same time, the Department considers that broader exemptions would both restrict the rule's ability to address fraud as intended, and would not ultimately shield such individuals, if selected, from the need to present a passport with their visa application, as generally required under INA 222(b). For these reasons, the Department believes the rule reflects its careful consideration of the APA's requirements, and the need to balance program integrity with accessibility for vulnerable populations.
三、国会立法意图
iii. Congressional Intent
评论者认为,由于该护照规则不成比例地将依据《移民与国籍法》第203(c)(1)(F)条确定的较贫困国家或地区的申请人、农村地区申请人以及冲突地区申请人排除在外,因此直接损害了多样化签证项目的宗旨。部分评论者认为,该规则与多样化签证项目法定目的之间的不一致,可能导致基于国会立法意图提出的法律挑战。
Commenters argue that because the passport rule disproportionately excludes applicants from poorer nations or regions established pursuant to INA 203(c)(1)(F), rural areas, and conflict zones, it directly undermines the spirit of the Diversity Visa program. Some argue that the rule's inconsistency with the statutory purpose of the Diversity Visa program may lead to legal challenges based on congressional intent.
部门回应:该规则并未损害国会的意图,因为该项目仍面向历史上赴美移民率较低的任何国家的本国公民全球开放。《移民与国籍法》(INA)第203(c)(1)(B)(ii)条将“低录取州”定义为在最近五年期间内被美国录取的本国公民人数等于或少于50,000人的国家。2019年,国务院颁布了一项临时最终规则,要求申请人在电子申请表中填写有效且未过期护照的某些信息。该要求在与2021年度、2022年度和2023年度多元化签证项目相关的申请期间内生效。在此期间,国务院得以比较外国人被要求随多元化签证申请一并提交护照信息前后的参与水平。在所有年份中,国务院收到的来自《移民与国籍法》第203(c)条所划定的六个地理区域中每个区域的申请人数,均超过了该区域可获得的多元化签证数量。因此,该临时最终规则并未对任何区域的本国公民造成过度负担,也未影响国务院按照国会意图全面实施该项目的能力。
Department Response: The rule does not undermine congressional intent, as the program is still available globally to the natives of any countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States. INA 203(c)(1)(B)(ii) defines “low-admission states” as those with equal to or fewer than 50,000 natives admitted to the United States during the most recent five-year period. In 2019, the Department promulgated an interim final rule that required entrants to include certain information from a valid, unexpired passport in the electronic entry form. This requirement was in effect for the entry periods associated with the 2021, 2022, and 2023 DV Program years. During this time, the Department was able to compare participation levels both prior to and during the period in which aliens were required to include this passport information with their DV entry. In all years, the Department received entries from more individuals from each of the six geographic regions created by INA section 203(c) than there were diversity visas available for each region. The interim final rule was therefore not unduly burdensome on natives of any region and did not affect the Department's ability to fully implement the program as Congress intended.

i. 时间负担
i. Time Burden
若干意见对与扫描和上传护照信息相关的预估时间负担的准确性表示担忧。这些意见强调了低收入地区申请人面临的挑战,因为这些地区获取扫描设备和技术支持的渠道有限,往往需要出行才能获得必要的资源。评论者认为国务院低估了时间负担,并提出实际时间负担可能为每位申请人一到三小时不等。

They emphasize challenges faced by applicants in low-income regions, where access to scanning equipment and technical support is limited, often requiring travel to obtain access to the necessary resources. Commenters argue that the Department underestimated the time burden, suggesting it could range from one to three hours per applicant.
部门回应:国务院建议在原有的(30分钟)时间负担基础上增加20分钟,以计入收集护照信息并将其添加至DS-5501表所需的时间。原先的时间估算未能包括个人收集、扫描护照文件并将其上传至DS-5501表所需的时间,也未考虑到部分申请人需要出行才能获取扫描设备。国务院感谢公众提出这些担忧,并正在调整时间负担以解决这些问题。
Department Response: The Department recommended adding 20 minutes to the original (30-minute) time burden to account for the time it would take to gather and add passport information to the DS-5501. The original time estimate neglected to include the amount of time it would take an individual to gather, scan, and upload the passport document to the DS-5501 and did not consider that some entrants would need to travel to access scanning equipment. The Department thanks the public for raising these concerns and is adjusting the time burden to address them.
国务院正将此前批准的DS-5501表的30分钟时间负担修改为新的90分钟时间负担。此新估算计入了获取和操作设备以扫描及上传护照文件所需的时间;同时也计入了鉴于近期公布的护照费用规则而收集并填写付款信息所需的时间。所有与每位申请人时间负担相关的估算均已相应调整。
The Department is modifying the previously approved 30-minute time burden on the DS-5501 to a new time burden of 90 minutes. This new estimate accounts for the time it takes to access and operate equipment to scan and upload passport documentation; it also accounts for the amount of time it takes to gather and input payment information in light of the recently announced passport fee rule. All estimates related to the per-applicant time burden are adjusted accordingly.
ii. 费用负担
ii. Cost Burden
部分评论者对获取护照所涉及的费用负担表示担忧。一些评论者认为,国务院估算的74.43美元护照费用并不现实,因为某些国家的护照费用超过200美元。另一些评论者则支持国务院提供的估算。在评论者提供具体护照费用金额时,数字因国家不同而在50美元至250美元之间不等。
Some commenters raise concerns about the cost burden associated with obtaining a passport. Some commenters suggest the Department's estimate of $74.43 for passport costs is unrealistic, as costs in some countries exceed $200. Others supported the estimate the Department provided. When commenters provide specific dollar amounts for passport costs, figures range from $50 to $250, depending on the country.
部门回应:2023年,国务院对多元化签证项目合格国家的护照费用进行了全面分析。利用这些数据,国务院确定多元化签证项目合格国家护照的平均费用为74.43美元。国务院理解某些国家的护照费用可能超过200美元,但同样属实的是,其他国家的护照费用极低,从而拉低了全球平均水平。
Department Response: In 2023, the Department conducted a comprehensive analysis of DV Program-eligible country passport fees. Using this data, the Department determined that the average cost of a passport in DV-eligible countries was $74.43. While the Department understands that passport fees may exceed $200 in some countries, it is also true that other countries have minimal passport costs that bring down the global average.
国务院进一步指出,护照有效期范围各异,通常每次签发为5年至10年。如果将护照费用按其有效期逐年分摊,则年度费用负担将低于所提供的估算值。例如,一本费用为250美元、有效期为五年的护照,其年度费用估算仅为每年50美元。
The Department further notes that passport validity ranges, typically between 5 and 10 years per issuance. If the cost of a passport is distributed annually for the duration of its validity, the annual cost burden is less than the estimate provided. For example, a passport costing $250 with a validity of five years would have an annual cost estimate of only $50 annually.
iii. 对DS-5501表的修改
iii. Changes to the DS-5501
a. 文件上传
a. Document Upload
评论人对提交过程中护照扫描件可能出现的问题表示担忧,包括图像被裁剪、页面模糊等技术性错误,以及5兆字节文件大小限制不足等问题。评论人建议在文件大小和格式方面提供灵活性,例如允许上传PDF文件或多张图像。部分评论人指出,国务院(印刷页第11899页)未能列明分辨率要求,总体上也未就如何上传文件提供充分指导。一位评论人请求国务院提供表内图像合并功能、文件压缩指导以及测试图像工具。

Within the published instructions, the Department provides detailed information, and this includes information about the requirements for the passport scan. The Department has no plans at this time to provide an in-form image combiner, file compression guidance, or test image tool, and these are items not generally provided during diversity or other U.S. visa applications.
b. 性别(Gender)与生理性别(Sex)
b. Gender vs Sex
两位评论人对在多元化签证申请表中将“gender(性别)”一词替换为“sex(生理性别)”表示担忧,理由涉及包容性问题,以及法规文本、操作系统与既往裁决记录之间可能出现的不匹配。一位评论人指出,在某些国家,“gender(性别)”与“sex(生理性别)”具有不同的法律定义。他们请求国务院澄清“sex(生理性别)”一词究竟指生理性别还是官方文件所列的法定性别。
Two commenters raise concerns about replacing the word “gender” with “sex” on the Diversity Visa application form, citing concerns around inclusivity and potential mismatches between regulatory text, operational systems, and prior adjudication records. One commenter noted that in some countries, “gender” and “sex” have different legal definitions. If two or more petitions for any single fiscal year are submitted by, or on behalf of, any alien, all such petitions will be void pursuant to INA 204(a)(1)(I)(i) and the alien by or for whom the petition has been submitted will not be eligible for consideration for diversity visa issuance during the fiscal year in question.
