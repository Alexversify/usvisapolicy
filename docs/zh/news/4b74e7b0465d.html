美国劳工部废除实施数十年的农场工人执法协调规定

发布日期 2026年8月31日生效日期 2026年9月30日

美国劳工部(DOL)发布最终规则,废除《联邦法规》第29编第42部分(29 CFR Part 42),该规定原本要求工资和工时司(WHD)、职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)与就业培训署(ETA)之间就移民和季节性农场工人保护事项建立正式协调机制。劳工部表示,该规定制定于1980年,已经过时,涉及已撤销的机构和已废止的法律。新规将于2026年9月30日生效。

要点

  • 被废除的29 CFR Part 42曾要求建立全国和地区委员会,以协调对农场工人的执法。
  • 劳工部指出,该规定提及已不存在的机构(ESA)和已废止的法律(FLCRA),已经过时。
  • 劳工部表示,过去几十年来通过工作组、专责小组以及《移民和季节性农业工人保护法》(MSPA)和H-2A签证项目等机制,已实现有效协调。
  • 共收到8份反对废除该规定的公众意见,但劳工部仍按原提案最终完成废除。
  • 此次废除仅取消了正式委员会和会议要求,并未改变MSPA、职业安全与健康法或《公平劳动标准法》下的实质性劳工保护。
  • 该规则将于2026年9月30日生效。

影响对象

雇用移民和季节性农场工人的农业雇主(包括使用H-2A签证项目的雇主)以及相关工人权益倡导团体。

建议行动

相关雇主及倡导团体应持续关注劳工部在移民和季节性农场工人保护方面的最新协调措施和执法指引。

摘要:
SUMMARY:
美国劳工部(劳工部)正在废止确立工资与工时司(WHD)、职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)和就业与培训管理局(ETA)之间就移民和季节性农场工人执法活动开展协调的正式程序的法规。劳工部废止这些法规的原因是:这些法规已经过时,不再反映劳工部的组织结构或运作方式,且对于相关下属机构之间的有效协调已无必要。此举将消除不必要的监管负担,并使劳工部的执法策略与已在使用的现代化、有效且灵活的协调模式保持一致。
The Department of Labor (Department) is rescinding the regulations that established formal procedures for coordination of enforcement activities among the Wage and Hour Division (WHD), Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and Employment and Training Administration (ETA) with respect to migrant and seasonal farmworkers. The Department is rescinding these regulations because they are obsolete, no longer reflect the Department's organizational structure or operational practices, and are not needed for effective coordination among the relevant component agencies. This action will remove unnecessary regulatory burden and align the Department's enforcement strategy with modern, effective, and flexible coordination models already in use.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
一、背景与历史
I. Background and History
该部于1980年颁布了29 CFR第42部分（第42部分）的法规，旨在改善其下属机构在执行迁徙农场工人保护措施方面的协调工作。[1] 该法规的目的是协调下属机构的活动，确保在“保护性法规”（包括《农场劳工承包商注册法》（FLCRA，后已废止）[2]、《职业安全与健康法》以及《公平劳动标准法》）下的有效执法工作，以“最大限度地提高……效力，同时最大限度地减少不必要的重复”。29 CFR 42.2。具体而言，该法规要求该部建立并维持若干流程和机构，包括：（1）成立由劳工部副部长（现为副部长职位）、[3]劳工部法律顾问、就业与培训管理局（ETA）、职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）以及就业标准管理局（ESA，一个现已不存在的机构，在该部颁布第42部分时该机构下辖工资与工时司（WHD）[4]）组成的全国农场劳工协调执法委员会（全国委员会），并由一个工作人员层级的工作组提供支持，以审查各下属机构的政策和执法策略；（2）成立地区农场劳工协调执法委员会（地区委员会）；以及（3）制定地区和全国层面的执法策略和协调计划。此外，该法规要求该部在工资与工时司（WHD）指定农场劳工专员，并在职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）指定合规官员，作为农场劳工联络人，并收集和审查与执行保护性法规相关的特定数据，供全国委员会审查，并用于指导未来的机构工作。该法规还规定了全国委员会和地区委员会召开会议的具体频率，以及公众参加其中某些会议的要求。
The Department promulgated the regulations at 29 CFR part 42 (Part 42) in 1980 to improve coordination among its component agencies in enforcing protections for migrant farmworkers.[1] The purpose of the regulations is to coordinate activities of the component agencies and to ensure effective enforcement efforts under “the protective statutes,” which included the Farm Labor Contractor Registration Act (FLCRA) (later repealed),[2] the Occupational Safety and Health Act, and the Fair Labor Standards Act, in order to “maximize . . . effectiveness, yet minimize unnecessary duplication.” 29 CFR 42.2. Specifically, the regulations require the Department to establish and maintain several processes and structures, including: (1) a National Farm Labor Coordinated Enforcement Committee (National Committee) with membership by the Under Secretary of Labor (now the Deputy Secretary),[3] the Solicitor of Labor, ETA, OSHA, and the Employment Standards Administration (ESA, a now-defunct agency that incorporated WHD at the time the Department promulgated Part 42),[4] and supported by a staff level working group, to review the subagencies' policies and enforcement strategies; (2) Regional Farm Labor Coordinated Enforcement Committees (Regional Committees); and (3) the development of enforcement strategies and coordination plans, both regionally and nationally. In addition, the regulations require the Department to designate Farm Labor Specialists in WHD and Compliance Officers in OSHA to serve as farm labor contact persons, and to collect and review specific data pertaining to enforcement of protective statutes to be reviewed by the National Committee and to be used to inform future agency efforts. The regulations also prescribe specific frequency of meetings for the National Committee and the Regional Committees, and public attendance at certain of these meetings.
自1980年颁布第42部分后的至少十年间，该部按照法规规定维持了全国委员会和地区委员会。此后，该部在很大程度上停止维持这些正式委员会，但现有记录并未明确确定具体发生的时间。实际上，正如本序言后文将更详细说明的那样，该部此后已实施了更为有效的机制，无需依赖第42部分的具体架构，即可确保工资与工时司（WHD）、就业与培训管理局（ETA）和职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）协调执行现行的迁徙农场工人法律。
For at least a decade after promulgating Part 42 in 1980, the Department maintained a National Committee and Regional Committees as prescribed in the regulations. The Department subsequently largely ceased maintaining those formal committees, although available records do not establish precisely when that occurred. Rather, as explained in more detail later in this preamble, the Department has since implemented more effective mechanisms that do not rely on the specific structures of Part 42 to ensure coordinated enforcement of the current migrant farmworker laws by WHD, ETA, and OSHA.
2024年，包括评论提交者“无边界组织项目”（Sin Fronteras Organizing Project）在内的多个维权组织提起诉讼，指控该部未能遵守第42部分的规定。该诉讼最终达成和解协议，该部同意采取某些措施重新组建全国委员会和地区委员会，并召开公开（第55745页）会议，“只要29 CFR第42部分的法规仍然有效且未作修改”。[5] 该和解协议并未禁止该部通过制定规则的程序废止或修改该法规。自和解协议生效之日起，该部一直遵守该协议。
In 2024, a number of advocacy organizations—including commenter Sin Fronteras Organizing Project—filed a lawsuit alleging that the Department had failed to comply with Part 42. That litigation resulted in a settlement agreement in which the Department agreed to take certain steps to reconstitute the National Committee and Regional Committees and hold public ( printed page 55745) meetings “for as long as the regulations at 29 CFR part 42 are in effect and unchanged.” [5] The settlement agreement does not bar the Department from rescinding or modifying the regulation through the rulemaking process. The Department has complied with the settlement agreement since its effective date.
二、废止提案
II. Proposal for Rescission
2025年7月1日，该部发布了一项拟议规则制定通知（NPRM），拟废止第42部分，因为该法规施加了过时、不必要且重复的内部程序；限制了该部的自由裁量权；并阻碍了该部下属机构以更高效、更有效的方式就迁徙农场工人事务进行协调。90 FR 28247。如该拟议规则制定通知所详述，该部认定第42部分施加了僵化的程序性要求和组织性规定，这些规定已经过时，数十年来未被采用，且已不再反映该部各机构协调执法活动的实际方式。例如，第42部分提及了诸如就业标准管理局（ESA）等历史性机构，而该机构已于2009年撤销；该法规对已撤销的就业标准管理局助理部长办公室及就业标准管理局地区管理员办公室施加了职责；依赖于已废止的法规，如《农场劳工承包商注册法》（FLCRA，已于1983年废止）；并强制要求建立诸如全国和地区执法委员会、季度会议以及指定联络人员等正式架构，而这些架构早已被弃置不用。
On July 1, 2025, the Department issued a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) to rescind Part 42 because the regulation imposed outdated, unnecessary, and duplicative internal procedures; limited the Department's discretion; and prevented the Department's agencies from coordinating with regard to migrant farmworkers in more efficient, effective ways. 90 FR 28247. As detailed in the NPRM, the Department determined that Part 42 imposed stilted procedural mandates and organizational requirements that were outdated, had not been utilized in decades, and no longer reflected how the Department coordinated enforcement activity across its agencies. For instance, Part 42 referenced historical entities such as ESA, which was eliminated in 2009, imposed duties on the abolished offices of the Assistant Secretary for ESA and the ESA Regional Administrator, relied on repealed statutes such as the FLCRA (repealed in 1983), and mandated formal structures—such as national and regional enforcement committees, quarterly meetings, and designated contact personnel—that had long ago fallen into disuse.
尽管《第42部分》中规定的部分结构和组成部分已处于闲置状态,但劳工部仍通过现代化协调机制持续开展针对流动农场工人的有力且有效的执法工作,包括跨部门工作组、专项工作组,以及《流动和季节性农业工人保护法》(MSPA)和《移民与国籍法》H-2A类临时或季节性农业工人非移民签证项目项下的新法定框架。参见,例如,《美国法典》第29编第1812条、第1852条;《美国法典》第8编第1188条。
Notwithstanding the dormancy of some structures and components set forth in Part 42, the Department has continued to conduct robust and effective enforcement related to migrant farmworkers through modern coordination mechanisms, including interagency working groups, task forces, and new statutory frameworks under the Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act (MSPA) and the Immigration and Nationality Act's H-2A nonimmigrant visa program for temporary or seasonal agricultural workers. See, e.g.,29 U.S.C. 1812, 1852; 8 U.S.C. 1188.
正如公告中所述,职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)定期主持一个农业专项工作组,该工作组与工资和工时司(WHD)及就业和培训管理局(ETA)的代表协调,处理农业相关事务和问题。该专项工作组旨在识别、审查、更新和制定OSHA的农业指导性文件,包括法规和网页内容。多年来,该工作组推动了多项新的农业指导性文件的出台,包括2017年对OSHA“农业作业安全与健康主题”网页的更新,其中包含从预防中暑到农业青年就业等多方面信息的链接。[6] 同样,公告指出,劳工部的其他举措也体现了部门在国家和地区层面持续致力于跨部门协调的承诺。例如,ETA地区监察倡导员经常为相关的州劳动力机构举办会议,WHD的代表也会参加。
As described in the NPRM, OSHA has regularly hosted an agriculture task force that has engaged with agricultural concerns and issues in coordination with representatives from WHD and ETA. The task force was designed to identify, review, update, and develop OSHA's agricultural guidance products, including regulations and web pages. The task force contributed to several new agricultural guidance products over the years, including a 2017 update to OSHA's Agricultural Operations Safety and Health Topics web page with links to information ranging from heat illness prevention to youth employment in agriculture.[6] Likewise, the NPRM stated that other Departmental initiatives reflect the Department's ongoing commitment to cross-agency coordination at the national and regional levels. For example, ETA Regional Monitor Advocates often host meetings for the relevant State Workforce Agencies, which representatives from WHD attend.
此外,公告指出,劳工部持续与农场劳工团体及公众保持联系,并就涉及流动和季节性农场工人就业的问题进行信息交流。作为劳工部定期利益相关方互动的一部分,OSHA、WHD和ETA均在国家和地区层面与农场工人团体保持联系并举行会议,包括公众外联活动、圆桌会议和研讨会。ETA国家监察倡导员定期与农场工人团体会面,并在每年收获季节期间,前往流动和季节性农场工人人数众多的各州,与州劳动力机构及代表农场工人的组织会面。
Additionally, the NPRM stated that the Department maintains contact and exchanges information with farm labor groups and the public on issues relating to the employment of migrant and seasonal farmworkers on an ongoing basis. As part of the Department's regular stakeholder engagement, OSHA, WHD and ETA all maintain contacts and meet with farmworker groups, as part of public outreach, roundtables, and conferences, at both the national and the regional level. The ETA National Monitor Advocate regularly meets with farmworker groups and, each year during harvest season, visits States with high numbers of migrant and seasonal farmworkers to meet with State Workforce Agencies and organizations that represent farmworkers.
《第42部分》反映了一种过时的法律环境,并依赖于早已不复存在的部门结构,这使得按原条文实施该法规的要求变得日益困难。劳工部进一步认为,OSHA、ETA和WHD目前的协调工作能够更有效地解决流动农场工人所面临的就业相关问题;这些工作经过协调,以实现效果最大化并尽量减少不必要的重复;并确保雇用流动农场工人的雇主遵守劳工部所执行的各项法律。因此,劳工部在公告中提出,《第42部分》施加了行政负担,阻碍了有效和高效的协调成果的实现,其继续留存于《联邦法规汇编》中已无必要。
Part 42 reflects an outdated legal landscape and depends on a bygone Departmental structure that made it increasingly difficult to implement the requirements of the regulation, as drafted. The Department further believes that the current coordination efforts of OSHA, ETA, and WHD more effectively address the employment-related problems faced by migrant farmworkers; are coordinated to maximize their effectiveness and minimize unnecessary duplication; and assure that employers of migrant farmworkers are complying with the laws that the Department enforces. Therefore, the Department suggested in the NPRM that Part 42 imposed bureaucratic obligations that did not allow for effective and efficient coordination outcomes, and that its continued presence in the Code of Federal Regulations was no longer necessary.
因此,劳工部依据《美国法典》第5编第301条规定的一般内部管理和程序性职权,并依照第14192号行政命令(《通过放松监管释放繁荣》),《联邦公报》第90卷第9065页(2025年1月31日)的要求,该行政命令指示各机构取消不必要或过时的法规,提议全面废止该规则。正如本序言后文所述,在审查了收到的全部八条意见后,劳工部决定按提议内容最终确定该规则。
Accordingly, the Department proposed to rescind the rule in its entirety under its general housekeeping and procedural authority at 5 U.S.C. 301 and consistent with Executive Order (E.O.) 14192 (Unleashing Prosperity Through Deregulation), 90 FR 9065 (Jan. 31, 2025), which directs agencies to eliminate unnecessary or obsolete regulations. As discussed later in this preamble, after reviewing all eight comments received, the Department is finalizing the rule as proposed.
三、最终规则的讨论
III. Discussion of Final Rule
该公告邀请公众就废止协调流动农场工人劳动法执法活动相关法规一事提交意见;意见征询期于2025年9月2日截止。在为期60天的公众意见征询期内,劳工部共收到八份意见提交。有关该公告的意见可通过访问https://www.regulations.gov网站,输入案卷编号ETA-2025-0003查阅。
The NPRM invited comments from the public concerning this rescission of regulations for coordinated migrant farmworker labor law enforcement activities; the comment period closed on September 2, 2025. During the 60-day public comment period, the Department received a total of eight comment submissions. The comments received on the NPRM may be viewed at https://www.regulations.gov by entering docket number ETA-2025-0003.
劳工部已对上述意见进行了审议,并决定按提议内容最终确定废止《第42部分》。在审查该规则的历史沿革、监管记录以及评论者提出的各项考量因素后,劳工部得出结论:《第42部分》已不再具有实质性的实际作用,它造成了不必要的工作重复和过时的程序性要求,并且对于确保有效执行针对流动农场工人的劳动保护而言并非必需。
The Department has considered these comments and has decided to finalize the rescission of Part 42 as proposed. After reviewing the rule's history, the regulatory record, and the considerations raised by commenters, the Department concludes that Part 42 no longer serves a meaningful operational purpose, it imposes unnecessary duplication of efforts and outdated procedural requirements, and it is not needed to ensure effective enforcement of labor protections for migrant farmworkers.
共收到八份反对拟议废止的意见。这些意见包括来自法律维权组织、一个州际机构间委员会、非营利工人维权团体、一个州劳动力机构以及一名个人的提交意见。尽管提交意见者表示担忧废止第42部分可能削弱执法或协调工作,但本部认定,如本序言后文所述,其数十年来在不依赖第42部分的情况下成功协调执法的能力已经证明,这打消了废止第42部分可能削弱执法或协调工作的任何担忧。
Eight comments were submitted in opposition to the proposed rescission. These included submissions from legal advocacy organizations, a State interagency committee, nonprofit worker advocates, a State Workforce Agency, and one individual. While the commenters expressed concern that rescinding Part 42 might weaken enforcement or coordination, the Department finds that its proven ability to coordinate enforcement for decades without reliance on Part 42, as described later in this preamble, allays any concern that rescinding Part 42 might weaken enforcement or coordination.
农场工人机会计划协会(AFOP)、法律与社会政策中心(CLASP)、全国就业法项目(NELP)、得克萨斯州格兰德河法律援助(TRLA)以及密歇根州跨机构移徙服务委员会(IMSC)等提交意见者断言,第42部分对于保护农场工人及确保跨机构执法协调至关重要。本部不予认同,(印刷页55746)特别是因为提交意见者未承认或考虑本部随时间推移所形成的某些活动与做法,正如本规则所述,也未考虑所要求的官僚程序在实质上的低效性。正如公告拟议规则制定通知(NPRM)中所指出并在此重申的那样,多年来,本部未依赖第42部分所列举的部分职能,因为本部已通过其他更有效的途径成功实现了这些内部及与外部利益相关方开展活动的既定目的。尽管提交意见者援引2017年中西部地区会议作为该规则下活动的最新实例,但本部直至和解协议要求开展与该法规所列名称类似的部分活动后,才较为持续地实施这些机制。全国委员会和地区委员会、协调计划以及要求召开的公开会议在实践中并未得以维持。相反,本部在履行本部最初制定这些法规时所欲实现的执法与协调活动类型方面发挥了关键作用。本部协调工作的实例包括但不限于:参加每月举行的部内农业工作组会议,各成员机构在会上就执法以及农业教育推广活动分享信息;参加每季度举行的地区农业协调电话会议,各成员机构的地区及全国代表均参与信息共享;以及各机构间定期举行协调电话会议,讨论持续及新出现的问题。此外,各机构还从事执法战略规划、开展利益相关方外联与互动、制定及审查政策、向适当机构提供转介,并开展去冲突工作,以防止重叠、解决冲突,确保协调一致、高效的运作。
Commenters such as the Association of Farmworker Opportunity Programs (AFOP), the Center for Law and Social Policy (CLASP), the National Employment Law Project (NELP), Texas RioGrande Legal Aid (TRLA), and Michigan's Interagency Migrant Services Committee (IMSC) asserted that Part 42 is essential to protect farmworkers and ensure cross-agency enforcement coordination. The Department disagrees, ( printed page 55746) particularly because the commenters did not acknowledge or take into account certain activities and practices that the Department has developed over time, as set forth in this rule, or the substantive inefficacy of the bureaucratic requirements required. As noted in the NPRM and reiterated here, for several years the Department has not relied on some of the functions enumerated in Part 42 as it has successfully fulfilled the intended purposes of those engagements, both internal to the Department and with external stakeholders, through other more effective avenues. Although commenters cited a 2017 Midwest regional meeting as the most recent example of activity under the rule, the Department did not consistently implement these structures until the settlement agreement required some activities similar to those named in the regulation. The National Committee and Regional Committees, coordination plans, and required public meetings were not maintained in practice. Instead, the Department has played a key role in carrying out the types of enforcement and coordination activities intended by the Department when first creating these regulations. Examples of Departmental coordination include, but are not limited to, participation in monthly Departmental Agriculture Taskforce meetings in which component agencies share information about enforcement as well as education and outreach activities in agriculture; quarterly Regional Agriculture Coordination calls that include regional and national representation of each component agency for information sharing; and recurring coordination calls among agencies to discuss ongoing and emerging issues. Additionally, each agency engages in planning enforcement strategies, conducting stakeholder outreach and engagement, developing and reviewing policies, providing referrals to appropriate agencies, and conducting deconfliction to prevent overlap, resolve conflicts, and ensure coordinated, efficient operations.
例如,职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)持续维护并定期更新其“农业作业安全与健康主题”网页,为雇主和工人提供有关农业危害、适用于农业的OSHA标准、危害控制、出版物、培训材料以及合规协助资源的信息。OSHA的农业指南涉及的危害包括高温暴露、谷仓与筒仓、危险机械、跌落、化学品、呼吸危害、噪声暴露、车辆危害以及青年工人安全。这些资源包括涉及拖拉机与收割机危害、谷物处理危害、中暑预防、农场受限空间危害、触电危害、应急准备、个人防护装备以及农业青年工人保护等方面的材料。OSHA以多种形式提供这些资源,包括情况说明书、速览卡、危害警示、培训材料以及多语种出版物。
For example, OSHA continues to maintain and regularly update its Agricultural Operations Safety and Health Topics web page, which provides employers and workers with information regarding agricultural hazards, OSHA standards applicable to agriculture, hazard controls, publications, training materials, and compliance assistance resources. OSHA's agricultural guidance addresses hazards including heat exposure, grain bins and silos, hazardous machinery, falls, chemicals, respiratory hazards, noise exposure, vehicle hazards, and youth worker safety. These resources include materials addressing tractor and harvester hazards, grain handling hazards, heat illness prevention, confined-space hazards on farms, electrocution hazards, emergency preparedness, personal protective equipment, and youth worker protections in agriculture. OSHA makes these resources available in multiple formats, including fact sheets, QuickCards, hazard alerts, training materials, and multilingual publications.
此外,劳工部在该领域的规则制定方式体现了其对持续协调的承诺。就业和培训管理局(ETA)与工资与工时司(WHD)定期联合发布针对农场工人需求的法规,并在此过程中与利益相关方就该等法规的实施和执行进行会商。例如,2025年,ETA与WHD联合提议修订有关H-2A工人从事的农业劳动或服务的认证以及此类非移民工人雇主应承担义务的执行相关法规。参见90 FR 28919(2025年7月2日)。2022年,WHD与ETA联合发布了一份关于劳工部针对农场劳务承包商执法情况的国会报告。参见https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/WHD/h2a/H-2A_Report2022.pdf。此外,WHD定期举办面向公众开放的虚拟农业研讨会,就与农业行业相关的联邦劳工规则提供指导,且有时邀请ETA代表参加。
Furthermore, the Department's approach to rulemaking in this space reflects its commitment to ongoing coordination. ETA and WHD regularly issue joint regulations addressing the needs of farmworkers, and in the process meet with stakeholders about the implementation and enforcement of those regulations. For example, in 2025, ETA and WHD jointly proposed to amend the regulations governing the certification of agricultural labor or services performed by H-2A workers and the enforcement of the obligations applicable to employers of such nonimmigrant workers. See90 FR 28919 (July 2, 2025). In 2022, WHD and ETA issued a joint report to Congress about the Department's enforcement related to farm labor contractors. See https://www.dol.gov/​sites/​dolgov/​files/​WHD/​h2a/​H-2A_​Report2022.pdf. Additionally, WHD regularly hosts a Virtual Agricultural Seminar that is open to the public, provides guidance on Federal labor rules pertaining to the agricultural industry, and has at times included representatives from ETA.
尽管第42部分规定的部分结构和组成部分已处于休眠状态,劳工部仍持续通过不依赖第42部分的机制开展执法行动[7](例如调查、审计、检查、现场访查),并在WHD、职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)和ETA之间有效协调,这些机制包括第42部分制定时尚不存在的制定法和法规所提供的途径。这些机制包括非正式工作组、联合调查、战略性转介以及有针对性的跨部门举措,均不依赖第42部分的授权规定,也不因其而得到加强。劳工部持续有效执法以保障本国劳动力,其执法行动所呈现的高违规率即为明证,其中许多行动涉及有针对性的举措,旨在将有限的调查资源集中用于查处实施严重违规行为的雇主。
Despite the dormancy of some structures and components set forth in Part 42, the Department has continued to conduct enforcement actions [7] ( e.g., investigations, audits, inspections, site visits) and coordinate effectively across WHD, OSHA, and ETA through mechanisms that do not rely on Part 42, including those provided by statutes and regulations that did not exist when Part 42 was promulgated. These include informal working groups, joint investigations, strategic referrals, and targeted interagency initiatives, none of which are dependent on or enhanced by the mandates in Part 42. The Department continues to carry out effective enforcement to safeguard the nation's workforce as illustrated by the high violation rate yielded by its enforcement actions, many of which involve targeted initiatives to focus limited investigative resources on employers that commit egregious violations.
自1980年第42部分颁布以来,国会已制定了多项专门旨在保护流动农场工人的重大法律框架。其中包括《移民农业工人保护法》(MSPA),该法为农场工人确立了全面的劳动保护措施,并大幅扩展了WHD的执法权限。国会还根据1986年《移民改革和控制法》设立了H-2A签证项目,为临时外籍农业工人的雇用建立了正式程序,此类工人须遵守劳工部管理的劳工标准,这必然需要ETA(负责认证方面)与WHD(负责执法方面)在H-2A项目中紧密协作。为此,劳工部已根据MSPA和H-2A制定了详细的法规、执法策略和人员配置结构,而这些在第42部分制定时尚不存在。劳工部已成功依靠这些法规和项目,即MSPA和H-2A相关法规,而非第42部分,包括相关的外展工作,来保护流动农场工人。劳工部始终致力于利益相关方参与和外展工作,将其视为获得有意义反馈、建立信任、维持良好关系及强化成果的关键要素。为推进这些努力,劳工部的活动包括但不限于:下属机构参加例行会议;与农场工人维权团体及其他利益相关方举行临时会议;以及农业专项外展工作。例如,WHD每年主办并参与数百场外展活动,其中大多数由社区外展与资源规划专员(CORPS)主持。CORPS是利益相关方参与及公众外展联络的主要联系人。他们定期在全国范围内安排线下和线上活动。ETA的就业服务相关法规继续要求若干协调环节,包括将投诉转介至执法机构、交叉核对取消资格名单,以及因执法机构作出最终裁定而终止相关事项。
Since Part 42 was promulgated in 1980, Congress has enacted major statutory frameworks specifically aimed at protecting migrant farmworkers. These include MSPA, which established comprehensive labor protections for farmworkers and significantly expanded the enforcement authority of WHD. Congress also enacted the H-2A visa program under the Immigration Reform and Control Act of 1986, creating a formal process for the employment of temporary foreign agricultural workers subject to Department-administered labor standards, which necessarily involves close collaboration between ETA on the certification side and WHD on the enforcement side of the H-2A program. In response, the Department has developed detailed regulations, enforcement strategies, and staffing structures under both MSPA and H-2A that did not exist when Part 42 was adopted. The Department has successfully relied on these regulations and programs—that is, the MSPA and H-2A regulations, not Part 42—including related outreach, to protect migrant farmworkers. The Department maintains its commitment to stakeholder engagement and outreach as essential to obtaining meaningful feedback, building trust, maintaining strong relationships, and strengthening outcomes. In furtherance of those efforts, the Department's activities include, but are not limited to, component agencies participating in recurring conferences; ad hoc meetings with farmworker advocacy groups and other stakeholders; and agricultural initiative outreach work. For example, WHD hosts and participates in hundreds of outreach events each year where most are led by Community Outreach and Resource Planning Specialists (CORPS). CORPS are the primary contacts for stakeholders and public engagement and outreach. They schedule in-person and virtual events regularly throughout the nation. ETA's Employment Service regulations continue to require several coordination points, including the referral of complaints to enforcement agencies, cross-referencing debarment lists, and discontinuation due to final determinations from enforcement agencies.
包括美国农场工人机会计划(AFOP)、法律与社会政策中心(CLASP)和跨国移民团结联盟(IMSC)在内的多位评论者敦促劳工部保留并修订第42部分，更新过时的法规和机构引用(例如，替换对《农场劳动合同登记法》(FLCRA)或《就业服务法》(ESA)的过时引用)，而不是完全废除该(printed page 55747)法规。劳工部考虑了这一方案，但认为修订并不适当。第42部分存在的问题不仅限于术语问题，而是涉及整个监管框架。该规则要求建立的组织结构和程序并不反映劳工部数十年来的实际运作方式。这些程序也未考虑到国会为保护农场工人而颁布的新法规。更新过时的名称并不能解决该法规核心内容数十年来一直未被使用、且与当前执法工作无关这一事实。事实上，废除第42部分使劳工部能够最大限度地减少不必要的重复工作，并能够根据跨机构协调与公众利益相关方互动中固有的不断演变的流程、项目需求和现实情况进行调整，而不受40多年前设计的流程和结构的限制。
Several commenters, including AFOP, CLASP, and IMSC, urged the Department to retain and revise Part 42 by updating outdated statutory and organizational references ( e.g., replacing outdated references to the FLCRA or ESA) rather than eliminating the ( printed page 55747) regulation entirely. The Department considered this approach but concluded that revision is not appropriate. The problems with Part 42 are not limited to terminology; they extend to the entire regulatory framework. The rule mandates organizational structures and processes that do not reflect the way the Department has operated for decades. Nor do these processes account for new statutes enacted by Congress to protect farmworkers. Updating outdated names would not address the fact that the core features of the regulation have not been needed in decades and are not relevant to current enforcement operations. In fact, the rescission of Part 42 enables the Department to minimize unnecessary duplication, and to adapt to evolving processes, program demands, and realities inherent to the cross-agency coordination and engagements with public stakeholders without being limited by processes and structures designed over 40 years ago.
部分评论者，特别是得克萨斯农村法律援助会(TRLA)和法律与社会政策中心(CLASP)认为，废除第42部分可能违反劳工部2024年在Farm Labor Organizing Committee诉Su案中经法院批准的和解协议的条款。劳工部对此不予认同。该和解协议明确规定，其条款仅在“29 CFR第42部分法规有效且未作更改期间”适用。因此，该和解协议明确保留了劳工部通过通知评议规则制定程序修改或废除该法规的裁量权。自和解协议生效之日起，劳工部已遵守该协议的所有条款，且在法律上并无义务保留第42部分。因此，废除该法规完全符合和解协议的规定。
Some commenters, notably TRLA and CLASP, argued that rescinding Part 42 could violate the terms of the Department's 2024 court-approved settlement agreement in Farm Labor Organizing Committee v. Su. The Department disagrees. The settlement agreement expressly states that its terms apply only “for as long as the regulations at 29 CFR part 42 are in effect and unchanged.” Therefore, the settlement agreement explicitly preserves the Department's discretion to modify or rescind the regulation through notice-and-comment rulemaking. The Department has complied with all terms of the settlement agreement since its effective date and is under no legal obligation to retain Part 42. Rescission is therefore fully consistent with the settlement agreement.
全国就业法律项目(NELP)、得克萨斯农村法律援助会(TRLA)及其他评论者认为，废除第42部分属于武断和反复无常的行为，并指出劳工部在《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中未能充分说明该法规如何对劳工部的执法能力构成了不可逾越的限制，并建议劳工部对第42部分进行更新和修订，而非将其完全废除。劳工部对此不予认同。正如《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)和本最终规则所明确表明的，废除决定是基于合理且有充分文件支持的认定做出的：第42部分数十年来未得到持续实施；其结构已过时，与当前以保护移民农场工人为核心的法规体系(这些法规和规章均在第42部分颁布之后制定)不符；数十年来该法规未对执法成效作出任何贡献；而且在没有该法规的情况下，就业和培训管理局(ETA)、工资与工时处(WHD)以及职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)之间的协调工作仍能有效开展。《行政程序法》并未要求各机构保留过时且不必要的程序性法规，尤其是在该法规缺席情况下执法活动依然切实有力的情况下。只要机构提供合理的解释说明变更理由，即可对规则进行修订或废止。参见Encino Motorcars, LLC诉Navarro案，579 U.S. 211, 221 (2016)。在本案中，劳工部已认定第42部分过时、程序繁琐且无必要，并已通过第42部分之外的现代协调机制证明了其对切实有效执法工作的坚定承诺，因此劳工部认为废除第42部分是合理的。
NELP, TRLA, and others argued that rescission of Part 42 is arbitrary and capricious, stating that the Department did not sufficiently justify in the NPRM how the regulation posed insurmountable restrictions on the Department's enforcement capabilities and suggesting that the Department update and revise Part 42 instead of removing it in its entirety. The Department disagrees. As the NPRM and this final rule make clear, the rescission is based on a reasoned and well-documented determination: Part 42 has not been consistently implemented for decades; its structures are outdated and inconsistent with current agency operations, which are centered around statutes and regulations that protect migrant farmworkers and were enacted after Part 42 was promulgated; it has not contributed to enforcement outcomes for decades; and coordination among ETA, WHD, and OSHA continues to occur effectively without it. The Administrative Procedure Act does not require agencies to retain outdated and unnecessary procedural regulations, particularly where enforcement activities are demonstrably robust in the regulation's absence. Agencies may amend or repeal a rule so long as they provide a reasoned explanation for the change. See Encino Motorcars, LLC v. Navarro, 579 U.S. 211, 221 (2016). Here, where the Department has determined that Part 42 was outdated, overly bureaucratic, and unnecessary, and where the Department has demonstrated its commitment to robust and effective enforcement through modern coordination mechanisms outside of Part 42, the Department believes that rescission of Part 42 is reasonable.
一些评论者,包括法律与社会政策中心(CLASP)和跨国移民团结联盟(IMSC),表示担忧,认为废止该法规将取消公开区域会议,并减少利益相关方与劳工部直接接触的机会。尽管劳工部重视公众意见和利益相关方的参与,但强制要求1980年制定的特定会议结构,并非确保有效沟通所必需。相反,劳工部日益依赖更加灵活和包容的参与方式,包括利益相关方圆桌会议、意见听取会、直接外联、电子通讯以及虚拟论坛。与源于数字化之前做法的会议结构所形成的形式相比,这些现代方式更能反映当前的沟通实践,增强可及性,并能与农场工人权益倡导者及其他利益相关方进行更及时、更具响应性的互动。无论第42部分是否废止,当前的参与方式都将继续实施。此外,劳工部参与这些活动的代表包括其政策助理部长办公室的人员,他们能够直接听取利益相关方的意见,并有效落实符合劳工部优先事项和目标的政策变更。此外,倡导团体及任何其他第三方一直以来,并将继续,享有接触劳工部的渠道,包括代表工人提出申诉的可能性。在劳工部遍布全国的地区办事处中,许多利益相关方在地方层面保持着协作、互惠的工作关系。
Some commenters, including CLASP and IMSC, expressed concern that rescission would eliminate public regional meetings and diminish opportunities for stakeholders to engage directly with the Department. While the Department values public input and stakeholder engagement, mandating a specific meeting structure codified in 1980 is not necessary to ensure meaningful communication. Instead, the Department has increasingly relied on more flexible and inclusive forms of engagement, including stakeholder roundtables, listening sessions, direct outreach, electronic communications, and virtual forums. These modern approaches reflect current communication practices, enhance accessibility, and allow for more timely and responsive interaction with farmworker advocates and other stakeholders than formalities born of a meeting structure with roots in pre-digital practices. Current methods of engagement will continue regardless of the rescission of Part 42. Further, the Department's representatives at these engagements include personnel from its Office of the Assistant Secretary for Policy who can hear firsthand from stakeholders and effectively implement policy changes consistent with the Department's priorities and objectives. Moreover, advocacy groups and any other third party have always had, and continue to have, access to the Department, including the potential to file a complaint on a worker's behalf. In the Department's district offices throughout the nation, many stakeholders have collaborative working, symbiotic relationships at the local level.
反对废止的意见已获得全面考虑,但并未提供令人信服的理由,证明有必要保留一项既无必要、又不再反映劳工部内部结构或执法策略的法规。劳工部认定,废止第42部分将促进行政明晰性,消除过时且未使用的程序性要求,并支持执法协调的现代化,从而使劳工部在应对新挑战和新机遇方面继续保持灵活性和敏捷性。
The comments opposing rescission were considered in full but do not provide a compelling basis for retaining a regulation that is unnecessary and no longer reflects the Department's internal structure or enforcement strategy. The Department finds that rescinding Part 42 will promote administrative clarity, eliminate obsolete and unused procedural mandates, and support modernized enforcement coordination, allowing the Department to continue to be flexible and nimble in addressing new challenges and opportunities.
因此,劳工部最终决定全面废止第42部分。
Accordingly, the Department is finalizing the rescission of Part 42 in its entirety.
四、程序性事项及其他事项
IV. Procedural and Other Matters
A. 根据第12866号行政命令进行的审查
A. Review Under Executive Order 12866
第12866号行政命令《规制计划与审查》(58 FR 51735,1993年10月4日)要求各机构在法律允许的范围内:(1)仅在经过合理判断确认其收益大于成本的情况下才提议或采纳法规(同时认识到部分收益和成本难以量化);(2)在实现监管目标的前提下,结合考虑包括累积法规成本在内的各项因素,尽可能将法规对社会造成的负担降至最低;(3)在可供选择的监管方式中,选择能够实现净收益最大化的方式;(4)在可行的情况下,明确规定履行目标,而非规定受监管实体必须采取的具体行为或合规方式;(5)识别并评估可替代直接监管的可行方案,包括提供经济激励措施以鼓励预期行为,例如使用费或可交易许可证,或向公众提供可据以作出选择的信息。
E.O. 12866, “Regulatory Planning and Review,” 58 FR 51735 (Oct. 4, 1993), requires agencies, to the extent permitted by law, to (1) propose or adopt a regulation only upon a reasoned determination that its benefits justify its costs (recognizing that some benefits and costs are difficult to quantify); (2) tailor regulations to impose the least burden on society, consistent with obtaining regulatory objectives, taking into account, among other things, and to the extent practicable, the costs of cumulative regulations; (3) select, in choosing among alternative regulatory approaches, those approaches that maximize net benefits; (4) to the extent feasible, specify performance objectives, rather than specifying the behavior or manner of compliance that regulated entities must adopt; and (5) identify and assess available alternatives to direct regulation, including providing economic incentives to encourage the desired behavior, such as user fees or marketable permits, or providing information upon which choices can be made by the public.
第12866号行政命令第6(a)条还要求各机构将“重大监管行动”提交信息与监管事务办公室(OIRA)审查。信息与监管事务办公室已确定,本最终规则不构成第12866号行政命令第3(f)条所指的“重大监管行动”。因此,本最终规则未依据第12866号行政命令提交信息与监管事务办公室审查。
Section 6(a) of E.O. 12866 also requires agencies to submit “significant regulatory actions” to the Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) for review. OIRA has determined that this final rule does not constitute a “significant regulatory action” under section 3(f) of E.O. 12866. Accordingly, this final rule was not submitted to OIRA for review under E.O. 12866.
B. 根据《监管灵活性法》进行的审查
B. Review Under the Regulatory Flexibility Act
《监管灵活性法》(《美国法典》第5编第601条及其后各条)要求,对于依法必须公开征求意见的任何法规,均须编制初步监管灵活性分析和最终监管灵活性分析(FRFA),除非该机构证明该法规一旦颁布,不会对大量小实体产生重大经济影响。(印刷页第55748页)
The Regulatory Flexibility Act (5 U.S.C. 601 et seq.) requires preparation of an initial regulatory flexibility analysis and a final regulatory flexibility analysis (FRFA) for any rule that by law must be proposed for public comment, unless the agency certifies that the rule, if promulgated, will not have a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities. ( printed page 55748)
劳工部根据《监管灵活性法》(Regulatory Flexibility Act)的规定对本次废止进行了审查。劳工部所废止的法规涉及劳工部内部关于工资与工时司(WHD)、职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)和就业与培训管理局(ETA)就移徙农场工人执法活动进行协调的程序,因此对小实体没有影响。因此,劳工部得出结论认为,本次废止的影响不会对“相当数量的小实体产生重大经济影响”,无需编制最终监管灵活性分析(FRFA)。
The Department reviewed this rescission under the provisions of the Regulatory Flexibility Act. The regulation the Department is rescinding pertains to procedures within the Department for the coordination of enforcement activities by WHD, OSHA, and ETA relating to migrant farmworkers, so there is no impact on small entities. Therefore, the Department concludes that the impacts of the rescission would not have a “significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities,” and that the preparation of an FRFA is not warranted.

C. Review Under the Paperwork Reduction Act
本次废止不施加任何信息收集或记录保存要求。因此,根据《文书削减法》(44 U.S.C. 3501 及以下)的规定,无需获得管理和预算办公室(OMB)的批准。
This rescission imposes no information collection or record-keeping requirements. Accordingly, Office of Management and Budget clearance is not required under the Paperwork Reduction Act (44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq.).
D. 根据第13132号行政命令进行的审查
D. Review Under Executive Order 13132
第13132号行政命令“联邦制”,64 FR 43255(1999年8月10日),对联邦机构在制定和实施优先于州法律或对联邦制具有影响的政策或法规时施加了某些要求。第13132号行政命令要求各机构审查支持任何将限制各州决策自由裁量权的行动的宪法和法定权限,并仔细评估采取此类行动的必要性。第13132号行政命令还要求各机构建立可问责的程序,以确保州和地方官员能够对具有联邦制影响的监管政策的制定提供有意义且及时的意见。
E.O. 13132, “Federalism,” 64 FR 43255 (Aug. 10, 1999), imposes certain requirements on Federal agencies formulating and implementing policies or regulations that preempt State law or that have federalism implications. E.O. 13132 requires agencies to examine the constitutional and statutory authority supporting any action that would limit the policymaking discretion of the States and to carefully assess the necessity for such actions. E.O. 13132 also requires agencies to have an accountable process to ensure meaningful and timely input by State and local officials in the development of regulatory policies that have federalism implications.
劳工部已审查本次废止,并确定其不会对各州、联邦政府与各州之间的关系,或政府各级之间权力与责任的分配产生重大直接影响。
The Department has examined this rescission and has determined that it does not have a substantial direct effect on the States, on the relationship between the Federal government and the States, or on the distribution of power and responsibilities among the various levels of government.
E. 根据《无资金授权改革法》进行的审查
E. Review Under the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act
1995年《无资金授权改革法》(UMRA)第二章要求各联邦机构评估联邦监管行动对州、地方和部落政府以及私营部门的影响。公法104-4,第201条(编入2 U.S.C. 1531)。对于可能导致某项规则使州、地方和部落政府合计或私营部门在任何一年内(每年根据通货膨胀进行调整)支出1亿美元或以上的监管行动,UMRA第202条要求联邦机构发布书面声明,估计由此产生的成本、收益及对国民经济的其他影响。2 U.S.C. 1532(a)、(b)。UMRA还要求联邦机构建立有效程序,允许州、地方和部落政府的民选官员就“重大政府间授权”及时提供意见,并要求机构在制定可能对潜在受影响的小型政府产生重大或独特影响的任何要求之前,制定通知计划并提供及时意见的机会。
Title II of the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995 (UMRA) requires each Federal agency to assess the effects of Federal regulatory actions on State, local, and Tribal governments and the private sector. Public Law 104-4, sec. 201 (codified at 2 U.S.C. 1531). For a regulatory action likely to result in a rule that may cause the expenditure by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector of $100 million or more in any one year (adjusted annually for inflation), section 202 of UMRA requires a Federal agency to publish a written statement that estimates the resulting costs, benefits, and other effects on the national economy. 2 U.S.C. 1532(a), (b). The UMRA also requires a Federal agency to develop an effective process to permit timely input by elected officers of State, local, and Tribal governments on a “significant intergovernmental mandate,” and requires an agency plan for giving notice and opportunity for timely input to potentially affected small governments before establishing any requirements that might significantly or uniquely affect them.
劳工部根据UMRA及其政策声明对本次废止进行了审查,确定本次废止不包含联邦政府间授权,预计也不会要求州、地方和部落政府合计或私营部门在任何一年内支出1亿美元或以上。因此,UMRA的分析要求不适用。
The Department examined this rescission according to UMRA and its statement of policy and determined that the rescission does not contain a Federal intergovernmental mandate, nor is it expected to require expenditures of $100 million or more in any one year by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector. As a result, the analytical requirements of UMRA do not apply.
F. 第13175号行政命令(印第安部落政府)
F. Executive Order 13175 (Indian Tribal Governments)
劳工部已根据第13175号行政命令及劳工部的部落协商政策对本最终规则进行了审查,并得出结论认为,法规文本的变更不会对部落产生影响。这些变更不会对一个或多个印第安部落、联邦政府与印第安部落之间的关系,或联邦政府与部落政府之间权力与责任的分配产生实质性直接影响。
The Department has reviewed this final rule under the terms of E.O. 13175 and the Department's Tribal Consultation Policy and has concluded that the changes to regulatory text will not have Tribal implications. These changes do not have substantial direct effects on one or more Indian Tribes, the relationship between the Federal government and Indian Tribes, nor the distribution of power and responsibilities between the Federal government and Tribal governments.
G. 简明语言
G. Plain Language
第12866号行政命令、第13563号行政命令以及1998年6月1日的总统备忘录(《政府写作中的简明语言》)指示各行政部门和机构在《联邦公报》上发布的所有规则制定文件中使用简明语言。其目标是使政府在与公众的沟通中更具响应性、可及性和可理解性。因此,劳工部以简明语言起草了本最终规则。
E.O. 12866, E.O. 13563, and the Presidential Memorandum of June 1, 1998 (Plain Language in Government Writing), direct executive departments and agencies to use plain language in all rulemaking documents published in the Federal Register . The goal is to make the government more responsive, accessible, and understandable in its communications with the public. Accordingly, the Department drafted this final rule in plain language.
