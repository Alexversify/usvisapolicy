DHS公布在佐治亚州被捕的五名有犯罪记录外国人信息

发布日期 2026年10月24日

DHS发布声明,介绍ICE于2026年10月13日至18日在佐治亚州逮捕的五名外国人及其犯罪记录。声明由DHS部长马克韦恩·穆林发布,并提及“莱肯·莱利法案”。

要点

  • DHS公布了ICE于2026年10月13日至18日在佐治亚州多地逮捕的五名外国人身份。
  • 被捕者分别为洪都拉斯、秘鲁、危地马拉、洪都拉斯和墨西哥国籍。
  • DHS列出的犯罪记录包括严重袭击、家庭暴力、酒后驾驶和虐待儿童等,其中部分为逮捕记录而非定罪。
  • 其中一名洪都拉斯籍人士曾于2021年被遣返,DHS称其后非法再次入境美国,并指出非法再入境属重罪。
  • 穆林部长在声明中提及2024年发生在佐治亚州的莱肯·莱利遇害事件及“莱肯·莱利法案”。

影响对象

涉及在佐治亚州被ICE执法行动影响的外国人及相关社区。

国土安全部重点通报在佐治亚州被捕的最恶劣非法外国人
DHS Highlights Worst Illegal Aliens Arrested in Georgia
发布日期:2026年10月24日
Release Date: October 24, 2026
华盛顿 - 美国国土安全部(DHS)发布以下声明,重点通报美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)在佐治亚州逮捕的最恶劣非法外国人,其中包括暴力袭击者、家庭暴力施暴者、酒后驾驶者和绑架者。
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement highlighting the worst illegal aliens arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in Georgia, including violent assailants, domestic abusers, drunk drivers, and kidnappers.
“拜登鲁莽的开放边境政策使每一个州都变成了边境州,佐治亚州也不例外,”国土安全部部长马克韦恩·马林(Markwayne Mullin)表示。“近几周来,ICE的勇敢工作人员一直在从佐治亚州社区中将因虐待儿童、酒后驾驶、加重伤害罪和家庭暴力等可怕罪行而被捕的非法外国人予以遣返。自本届政府执政第一天起,特朗普总统就采取了迅速而果断的行动,让美国再次安全。他在上任后签署的第一项法案便以莱肯·莱利(Laken Riley)命名,她是佐治亚州的一名护理专业学生,被一名来自委内瑞拉、属于阿拉瓜火车帮(Tren de Aragua)成员的犯罪非法外国人无端杀害。”
“Biden’s reckless open border policy turned every single state into a border state, and that includes Georgia,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “In recent weeks, the brave men and women of ICE have been removing illegal aliens from Georgia communities arrested for such horrific crimes as cruelty to children, driving under the influence of alcohol, aggravated assault, and family violence. Since day one of this administration, President Trump has taken swift and decisive action to Make America Safe Again, centering his first bill signed in office on Laken Riley, a Georgia nursing student who was senselessly killed by a criminal illegal alien from Venezuela and member of Tren de Aragua.”
奥尔宾·奥马尔·洛佩兹-奥雷利亚纳(Olbin Omar Lopez-Orellana)是一名此前已被驱逐出境的洪都拉斯籍非法外国人,于10月14日在佐治亚州伊顿顿(Eatonton)被ICE逮捕。其犯罪记录包括三项加重伤害罪定罪、两项刑事毁坏财产罪定罪以及逃避或企图逃避警察追捕定罪,并曾因三级虐待儿童罪和无有效驾照驾驶被逮捕。他最初非法进入美国的时间和地点不详。他于2021年4月被签发最终驱逐令,并于2021年6月被遣返回洪都拉斯。随后他在不明时间和地点再次非法入境美国,此行为构成重罪。
Olbin Omar Lopez-Orellana, a previously-deported illegal alien from Honduras, was arrested by ICE in Eatonton, Georgia on October 14. His criminal history includes a conviction for THREE counts of aggravated assault, TWO counts of criminal damage to property, and fleeing or attempting to elude a police officer, and arrests for third-degree cruelty to children and driving without a valid license. He first entered the United States illegally at an unknown time and location. He was issued a final order of removal in April 2021 and was removed to Honduras in June 2021. He then illegally re-entered the United States – a felony – at an unknown time and location.
豪尔赫·奇波科(Jorge Chipoco)是一名秘鲁籍非法外国人,于10月13日在佐治亚州劳伦斯维尔(Lawrenceville)被ICE逮捕。其犯罪记录包括两项普通伤害罪逮捕记录、三级虐待儿童罪、家庭暴力殴打罪、刑事损害财产罪、妨碍司法行政罪以及骚扰(殴打或冒犯性行为)罪。奇波科在不明时间和地点非法进入美国。
Jorge Chipoco, an illegal alien from Peru, was arrested by ICE in Lawrenceville, Georgia on October 13. His criminal history includes arrests for TWO counts of simple assault, third-degree cruelty to children, battery – family violence, criminal mischief – property damage, obstruction of the administration of law, and harassment – striking or offensive conduct. Chipoco illegally entered the United States at an unknown time and location.
里戈贝托·加布里埃尔·托赫(Rigoberto Gabriel Toj)是一名危地马拉籍非法外国人,于10月18日在佐治亚州迪凯特(Decatur)被ICE逮捕。其犯罪记录包括一项重罪级家庭暴力型强力加重伤害罪逮捕记录。他在不明时间和地点非法进入美国。
Rigoberto Gabriel Toj, an illegal alien from Guatemala, was arrested by ICE in Decatur, Georgia on October 18. His criminal history includes an arrest for felony aggravated assault family-strongarm. He illegally entered the United States at an unknown time and location.
塞尔文·克鲁斯·加约(Selvin Cruz Gallo)是一名洪都拉斯籍非法外国人,于10月13日在佐治亚州梅布尔顿(Mableton)被ICE逮捕。其犯罪记录包括酒后驾驶、家庭暴力普通殴打罪、制造、销售或持有伪造驾驶证罪、无有效驾照驾驶以及未能出示身份证件等逮捕记录。他在不明时间和地点非法进入美国。
Selvin Cruz Gallo, an illegal alien from Honduras, was arrested by ICE in Mableton, Georgia on October 13. His criminal history includes arrests for driving under the influence of alcohol, simple battery – family violence, manufacturing, sale, or possession of fictitious operator's license, driving without a valid license, and failure to show identification. He entered the United States illegally at an unknown time and location.
赫克托·古铁雷斯·埃斯皮诺萨(Hector Gutierrez Espinoza)是一名墨西哥籍非法外国人,于10月14日在佐治亚州迪凯特(Decatur)被ICE逮捕。其犯罪记录包括加重伤害罪、刑事侵入罪、非法拘禁罪、家庭暴力殴打罪以及入店行窃罪的逮捕记录,此外还有三次因无驾照驾驶被逮捕的记录。他最初非法进入美国的时间和地点不详。
Hector Gutierrez Espinoza, an illegal alien from Mexico, was arrested by ICE in Decatur, Georgia on October 14. His criminal history includes arrests for aggravated assault, criminal trespass, false imprisonment, battery-family violence, and theft by shoplifting, and THREE separate arrests for driving without a license. He first entered the United States illegally at an unknown time and location.
