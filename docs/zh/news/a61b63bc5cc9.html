DHS批评纽约庇护政策,涉谋杀未遂指控的非法移民被释放后在逃

发布日期 2026年11月17日

美国国土安全部(DHS)批评纽约市当局无视ICE的拘留请求(detainer),释放了一名被控谋杀未遂和袭击罪的非法移民。来自圭亚那的德隆·雷蒙德·琼杜于11月7日从莱克斯岛监狱获释,目前仍在逃。

要点

  • ICE于2026年4月29日向纽约州惩教部门发出拘留请求,但纽约市在11月7日释放琼杜,未通知ICE。
  • 琼杜在奥巴马政府时期合法入境,但在2015年1月授权居留期限届满后非法滞留。
  • 纽约州于2026年5月通过法律,禁止地方警方与ICE合作,该法已于当年8月25日生效。
  • 国土安全部长马克韦恩·穆林批评州长凯西·霍楚尔的庇护政策,称纽约已成为'非法移民犯罪温床'。
  • DHS在声明中还列举了纽约及其他州涉及非公民的多起刑事案件,作为庇护政策后果的例证。
  • 该声明是DHS新推出的每日系列'Sanctuary Insanity'的一部分。

影响对象

涉及纽约州的移民庇护政策、ICE拘留令的执行,以及面临相关刑事指控的非法移民。

SANCTUARY INSANITY: DHS Calls Out Sanctuary Politicians in New York for Releasing Illegal Alien Arrested for Attempted Murder, Assault, and Other Crimes
发布日期：2026年11月17日
Release Date: November 17, 2026
庇护政客肆意妄为，危害美国民众生命安全
Sanctuary politicians recklessly endanger American lives
华盛顿，纽约庇护政客拒绝配合美国移民及海关执法局（ICE），释放一名因涉嫌企图谋杀、袭击及其他暴力犯罪而被捕的非法外国人后，美国国土安全部（DHS）发表以下声明。
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement after sanctuary politicians in New York refused to cooperate with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and released an illegal alien who had been arrested for attempted murder, assault, and other violent crimes.
2026年4月29日，ICE向纽约市惩教局发出拘留请求，要求其不要释放德隆·雷蒙德·琼杜，一名来自圭亚那的非法外国人，此前他因在布鲁克林被捕，面临以下指控：企图谋杀；一级袭击罪：持械蓄意造成严重伤害；二级袭击罪：蓄意造成严重人身伤害；一级非法使用枪支罪：持有已装弹的致命武器；二级非法持有武器罪：持有已装弹的枪支。其犯罪记录还包括此前因三级袭击罪（蓄意造成人身伤害）被捕的记录。
On April 29, 2026, ICE lodged a detainer asking the New York Department of Corrections to not release Delon Raymond Jaundoo, an illegal alien from Guyana, after he had been arrested in Brooklyn for the charges of attempted murder, first-degree assault: intent to cause serious injury with weapon, second-degree assault: with intent to cause serious physical injury, first-degree criminal use of a firearm: possession of a loaded deadly weapon, and second-degree criminal possession of a weapon: loaded firearm. His criminal history includes a prior arrest for third-degree assault: with intent to cause physical injury.
纽约庇护政客拒绝遵从ICE的拘留请求，于11月7日将琼杜从莱克斯岛释放，重新放回街头。他目前仍然在逃。
New York sanctuary politicians refused to honor ICE’s detainer and RELEASED Jaundoo from Rikers Island back onto the streets on November 7. He currently remains at large.
国土安全部部长马克韦恩·马林表示：“这名非法外国人有暴力犯罪记录，包括企图谋杀、袭击和非法持有武器。尽管面临这些暴力指控，纽约庇护政客仍拒绝遵从ICE的拘留请求，将这名罪犯重新释放回街头，使更多美国民众的生命安全受到威胁。在州长凯西·霍楚尔的治理下，纽约已成为非法外国人犯罪的温床。我们越早终结这些鲁莽的庇护政策，就能越早共同努力，让美国重归安全。”
“This illegal alien has a violent criminal history that includes attempted murder, assault, and criminal possession of a weapon,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Despite these violent charges, sanctuary politicians in New York refused to honor ICE’s detainer and RELEASED this criminal back onto the streets to put more American lives in danger. Under Governor Kathy Hochul, New York has become a hotbed of illegal alien crime. The sooner we put an end to these reckless sanctuary policies, the sooner we can all work together to make America safe again.”
琼杜在奥巴马政府执政期间合法入境美国，但在其授权停留期限于2015年1月届满后非法滞留。
Jaundoo lawfully entered the United States under the Obama Administration, but illegally overstayed his authorized period after it expired in January 2015.
纽约市市长佐兰·马姆达尼签署了一项行政命令，将纽约市变成暴力犯罪非法外国人的庇护所，使其得以再次犯罪，制造更多无辜受害者。
New York City’s Mayor Zohran Mamdani signed an Executive Order turning New York City into a shield for violent criminal illegal aliens to reoffend and create more innocent victims.
2025年12月，时任ICE代理局长托德·莱昂斯致函纽约州总检察长莱蒂希娅·詹姆斯，以及加利福尼亚州和伊利诺伊州的总检察长，就该州未能遵从ICE拘留请求一事向其发出警告。作为回应，纽约州州长凯西·霍楚尔惩罚了确实与ICE配合的地方执法部门，提出立法禁止地方警察部门与ICE执法部门建立伙伴关系及开展合作，该法案已于2026年5月由其签署成为法律。该法律于2026年8月25日正式生效。
In December 2025, then-Acting ICE Director Todd Lyons sent a letter to New York Attorney General Letitia James, along with the attorneys general of California and Illinois, putting her on notice for the state’s failure to honor ICE detainers. In response, New York Governor Kathy Hochul punished local law enforcement who did cooperate with ICE by proposing legislation that would bar local police departments from partnering and cooperating with ICE law enforcement, which she signed into law in May 2026. The law took effect on August 25, 2026.

Under Governor Hochul, New York has become a hotbed of illegal alien crime. Some of the recent illegal alien crimes in the sanctuary state include:
10月，ICE逮捕了来自罗马尼亚的非法外国人夫妇鲁迪和米拉贝拉·拉卡图苏，此前二人因在四个州实施十余起珠宝盗窃案而被控重大盗窃罪。拿骚县警方将二人移交ICE拘押。
In October, ICE arrested Rudi and Mirabela Lacatusu, an illegal alien couple from Romania, after they were charged with grand larceny for carrying out more than a dozen jewelry thefts across four states. Nassau County Police turned them over to ICE custody.
10月，两名来自多米尼加共和国的非法外国人，丹赫洛·拉斐尔·让·卡拉巴略和斯奈德·梅希亚·包蒂斯塔，在一次企图抢劫中多次刺伤一名休班纽约市警察局警探。两名嫌疑人此前均曾在纽约因鲁莽危害罪、鲁莽驾驶罪、持有盗窃工具罪、重大盗窃罪以及非法持有管制物质罪等多项罪名被捕，但二人均在ICE能够发出拘留请求之前已被释放。
In October, two illegal aliens from the Dominican Republic, Dangelo Rafael Jean Caraballo and Sneider Mejia Bautista, repeatedly stabbed an off-duty NYPD detective in an attempted robbery. Both suspects had previously been arrested in New York for crimes including reckless endangerment, reckless driving, possession of burglar’s tools, grand larceny, and criminal possession of a controlled substance, among other crimes, but both had been released before ICE could lodge detainers.
6月，ICE对来自萨尔瓦多的非法外国人奥雷利亚诺·安东尼奥·梅伦德斯·雷耶斯发出拘留请求，此前他因在纽约州亨廷顿强奸一名16岁女孩而被捕。
In June, ICE lodged a detainer for Aureliano Antonio Melendez Reyes, an illegal alien from El Salvador, after he was arrested for raping a 16-year-old girl in Huntington, New York.
4月，纽约市惩教局拒绝遵从针对来自墨西哥的非法外国人罗曼·塞隆·阿马蒂特拉的ICE拘留请求，此人在纽约州皇后区被控犯有谋杀罪和纵火罪。
In April, the New York City Department of Corrections refused to honor an ICE detainer for Roman Ceron Amatitla, an illegal alien from Mexico, who was charged with murder and arson in Queens County, New York.
3月，ICE逮捕了来自厄瓜多尔的非法外国人、拉丁国王帮派成员布莱恩·大卫·塔西瓜诺·莱昂，此人此前曾因袭击罪被起诉，却仍被纽约庇护政客释放。
In March, ICE arrested Bryan David Tasiguano Leon, an illegal alien from Ecuador and Latin Kings gang member, who had been released by New York sanctuary politicians despite being charged with assault.
今年3月,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)对来自洪都拉斯的非法外国人拜龙·波萨达·埃尔南德斯(Bairon Posada-Hernandez)发出拘留令。此人曾四次被驱逐出境,此前因在纽约市将包括一名83岁的空军退伍军人在内的两名旁观者推下地铁轨道而被捕。该空军退伍军人理查德·威廉姆斯(Richard Williams)后因伤势过重去世。
In March, ICE lodged a detainer for Bairon Posada-Hernandez, a four-time deported illegal alien from Honduras, after he was arrested for shoving two bystanders, including an 83-year-old Air Force veteran, onto subway tracks in New York City. The Air Force veteran, Richard Williams, later died of his injuries.
今年1月,ICE逮捕了来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人拉斐尔·安德烈斯·鲁比奥·博霍尔克斯(Rafael Andres Rubio Bohorquez)。此人在纽约市议会任职期间仍有犯罪记录,其中包括因袭击罪被捕的记录。
In January, ICE arrested Rafael Andres Rubio Bohorquez, an illegal alien from Venezuela, who was working as a New York City Council employee despite having a criminal history that includes an arrest for assault.
今年1月,ICE逮捕了来自墨西哥的非法外国人赫拉尔多·米格尔·莫拉(Gerardo Miguel-Mora)。此人此前已被纽约州的庇护政客释放,尽管其犯罪记录中包括因勒颈、强奸、性侵、入室盗窃、重大盗窃及持有毒品等罪名而被捕的记录。
In January, ICE arrested Gerardo Miguel-Mora, an illegal alien from Mexico, after he had been released by New York sanctuary politicians despite having a criminal history that includes arrests for strangulation, rape, sexual assault, burglary, grand larceny, and drug possession.
2025年4月,ICE对来自墨西哥的非法外国人费利克斯·赫罗尼莫·罗哈斯(Felix Jeronimo-Rojas)发出拘留令,此前他因在纽约市地铁强奸并盗窃一具尸体而被捕。赫罗尼莫·罗哈斯已于2026年6月被判处五年监禁。
In April 2025, ICE lodged a detainer for Felix Jeronimo-Rojas, an illegal alien from Mexico, after he was arrested for raping androbbing a corpse on a New York City subway. Jeronimo-Rojas was sentenced in June 2026 to five years in prison.

In addition to the crimes committed by illegal aliens in New York, the state’s sanctuary policies have had disastrous consequences in other states as well. These include:
今年9月,ICE在宾夕法尼亚州逮捕了来自印度的非法外国人西姆兰帕尔·辛格(Simranpal Singh)。此人曾获霍楚尔(Hochul)治下的纽约州颁发商业驾驶执照(CDL)。
In September, ICE arrested Simranpal Singh, an illegal alien from India, in Pennsylvania who had been issued a Commercial Driver’s License (CDL) by Hochul’s New York.
今年3月,ICE逮捕了来自印度的非法外国人苏克德夫·辛格(Sukhdev Singh)。此人在印第安纳州印第安纳波利斯驾驶半挂卡车时引发车祸,导致一名美国公民重伤入院,生命垂危。辛格曾获霍楚尔治下的纽约州颁发商业驾驶执照。
In March, ICE arrested Sukhdev Singh, an illegal alien from India, after he caused a crash while driving a semi-truck that left a U.S. citizen hospitalized in critical condition in Indianapolis, Indiana. Singh had been given a CDL by Hochul’s New York.
2025年10月,ICE在俄克拉荷马州逮捕了来自印度的非法外国人安莫尔·安莫尔(Anmol Anmol)。霍楚尔治下的纽约州曾为此人颁发驾照,上面将其姓名登记为“未提供姓名安莫尔(No Name Given Anmol)”。
In October 2025, ICE arrested Anmol Anmol, an illegal alien from India, in Oklahoma. Hochul’s New York had given Anmol a CDL that listed him as “No Name Given Anmol.”
2023年8月,纽约市警察局(NYPD)警员逮捕了来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人、“阿拉瓜列车帮”(Tren de Aragua)成员何塞·安东尼奥·伊瓦拉(Jose Antonio Ibarra),原因是他驾驶摩托车搭载一名未戴头盔的两岁儿童,行为鲁莽危险。由于纽约州的庇护政策,他获释放。六个月后,即2024年2月,伊瓦拉在佐治亚州杀害了护理专业学生莱肯·莱利(Laken Riley)。
In August 2023, New York City Police Department (NYPD) officers arrested Jose Antonio Ibarra, an illegal alien from Venezuela and Tren de Aragua gang member, for recklessly driving a motorcycle with a 2-year-old child who was not wearing a helmet. He was released due to New York’s sanctuary policies, and six months later, Ibarra went on to murder nursing student Laken Riley in Georgia in February 2024.
