美国海岸警卫队为特拉华河疏浚工程设立安全区,与移民政策无关

发布日期 2026年7月20日生效日期 2026年7月20日

美国海岸警卫队在宾夕法尼亚州马库斯胡克附近的特拉华河设立了三个临时安全区,以便在维护性疏浚作业期间保护人员和船只安全。该规定属于航运管理事项,与签证或移民政策无关。

要点

  • 该规定限制船只在特拉华河特定区域的通行和锚泊,有效期至2026年10月31日。
  • 该规定由海岸警卫队发布,旨在保障疏浚作业期间的安全。
  • 文件中提到需接受移民当局检查的船只,但这仅涉及港口锚泊程序,与移民申请无关。
  • 该通知不涉及签证、绿卡或入籍事务。

影响对象

在宾夕法尼亚州马库斯胡克附近特拉华河航行的船只操作者。

摘要:
SUMMARY:
海岸警卫队正在特拉华河水域设立三个临时安全区,分别位于马库斯胡克航道、第7号锚地(宾夕法尼亚州马库斯胡克附近)以及第9号锚地(靠近曼图亚溪入口)的部分区域。在进行维护性疏浚作业期间,这些安全区将临时限制船舶在特拉华河部分水域通行或抛锚。设立安全区旨在保护人员、船舶及海洋环境免受疏浚作业所造成的危害。除非经港口管制官(COTP)或其指定代表特别授权,否则禁止船舶或人员进入这些区域。
The Coast Guard is establishing three temporary safety zones on the waters of the Delaware River, in portions of Marcus Hook Range, Anchorage 7, off Marcus Hook, PA, and Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek. The safety zones temporarily restrict vessel traffic from transiting or anchoring in portions of the Delaware River while maintenance dredging is being conducted. The safety zones are needed to protect personnel, vessels, and the marine environment from hazards created by dredging operations. Entry of vessels or persons into these zones is prohibited unless specifically authorized by the COTP or his designated representatives.

二、背景与授权依据
II. Background and Authority
海岸警卫队依据《美国法典》第5编第553(b)(B)条所赋予的权力发布本临时规则。该法律条款授权机构在因正当理由认定按通常程序进行事先通知并征求意见“不可行、无必要或违反公共利益”时,可无需事先通知及征求意见程序而发布规则。海岸警卫队认定,就本规则而言存在不发布拟议规则制定通知(NPRM)的正当理由,因为鉴于疏浚作业将于2026年7月15日开始,时间不足以留出合理的意见征求期并在此之前发布最终规则,故此做法不可行。
The Coast Guard is issuing this temporary rule under the authority in 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B). This statutory provision authorizes an agency to issue a rule without prior notice and opportunity to comment when the agency for good cause finds that those procedures are “impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest.” The Coast Guard finds that good cause exists for not publishing a notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM) with respect to this rule because it is impracticable to do so given that there is insufficient time to allow for a reasonable comment period and publish a final rule prior to July 15, 2026, the start date for dredging operations.
此外,依据《美国法典》第5编第553(d)(3)条,基于上述同样原因,海岸警卫队认定存在正当理由使本规则在《联邦公报》刊登后不足30天即生效。
Also, under 5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3), the Coast Guard finds that good cause exists for making this rule effective less than 30 days after publication in the Federal Register for the same reason provided above.
三、法律授权与规则制定的必要性
III. Legal Authority and Need for Rule
海岸警卫队依据《美国法典》第46编第70034条所赋予的权力发布本规则。特拉华湾港口管制官(COTP)已认定,疏浚作业存在潜在危险,例如存在水下及漂浮管道、增压泵、接头段以及操纵能力受限的船舶等。本规则制定的目的在于确保疏浚作业及所有相关管道和设备250码半径范围内(印刷页第45205页)人员、船舶及海洋环境的安全,并确保较大型船舶在无法于正在疏浚的区域抛锚时,仍有安全锚地可供使用。
The Coast Guard is issuing this rule under authority in 46 U.S.C. 70034. The Captain of the Port, Delaware Bay (COTP) has determined that dredging operations present potential hazards, such as the presence of submerged and floating pipelines, booster pumps, head sections, and vessels with a restricted ability to maneuver. The purpose of this rulemaking is to ensure the safety of ( printed page 45205) personnel, vessels, and the marine environment within a 250-yard radius of dredging operations and all associated pipeline and equipment, and to ensure safe anchorage availability for larger vessels which are not able to anchor in areas being dredged.
四、规则讨论
IV. Discussion of the Rule
本规则自2026年7月15日至2026年10月31日设立三个安全区。设立其中两个安全区是为了便利对特拉华河位于马库斯胡克航道（Marcus Hook Range）附近以及马库斯胡克外的第7号锚地（如33 CFR 110.157(a)(8)所述）进行维护性疏浚。第三个安全区设立于曼图亚溪（Mantua Creek）入口附近的第9号锚地（如33 CFR 110.157(a)(10)所述），以确保因疏浚作业而无法在第7号锚地锚泊的较大型船舶能够获得可供其使用的安全锚泊区域。疏浚作业最有可能使用“查尔斯顿”号（CHARLESTON）挖泥船进行，但也可能使用其他挖泥船，以及配套的疏浚管道和增压泵。该管道由紧随挖泥船之后的浮动软管与通往陆上处置区的潜没管道组合而成。鉴于疏浚作业、配套管道以及潜没管道所在位置所带来的危险，现在以下区域设立安全区：
This rule establishes three safety zones from July 15, 2026, through October 31, 2026. Two safety zones are being established to facilitate maintenance dredging of the Delaware River in the vicinity of Marcus Hook Range, and Anchorage 7, off Marcus Hook, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(8). The third safety zone is being established in Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(10), to ensure safe anchorage availability for larger vessels that cannot anchor in Anchorage 7 because of the dredging. Dredging will most likely be conducted with the dredge CHARLESTON, though other dredges may be used, along with associated dredge pipeline and boosters. The pipeline consists of a combination of floating hoses immediately behind the dredge and submerged pipeline leading to upland disposal areas. Due to the hazards related to dredging operations, the associated pipeline and the location of submerged pipeline, safety zones are being established in the following areas:
（一）安全区一包括挖泥船在马库斯胡克航道和第7号锚地作业期间，挖泥船250码范围内的所有可通航水域，以及所有相关疏浚设备所在水域。挖泥船将按照我方《内河航行规则》（33 CFR 83.27所述）展示表示操纵能力受限船舶的灯光和号型。该安全区将在维护项目持续期间设立。请求通过该安全区的船舶必须至少提前1小时通过甚高频（VHF）13频道或16频道与挖泥船联系，以安排安全通行。在该安全区附近，主航道的至少一侧将保持畅通，以便船舶安全通行。任何时候都不会将整条主航道全部封闭，禁止船舶通行。在主航道一侧保持开放的区域，船舶应避免相遇会船，并应按照本规则及《航行规则》（33 CFR第E分章）行驶。
(1) Safety Zone One includes all navigable waters within 250 yards of the dredge, and all related dredge equipment when the dredge is operating in Marcus Hook Range, and Anchorage 7. The dredge will be displaying lights and shapes for vessels restricted in ability to maneuver, as described in our Inland Navigation Rules at 33 CFR 83.27. This safety zone is being established for the duration of the maintenance project. Vessels requesting to transit the safety zone must contact the dredge on VHF channel 13 or 16 at least 1 hour prior to arrival to arrange safe passage. At least one side of the main navigational channel will be kept clear for safe passage of vessels in the vicinity of the safety zone. At no time will the entire main navigational channel be closed to vessel traffic. Vessels should avoid meeting in these areas where one side of the main navigational channel is open and proceed per this rule and the Rules of the Road (33 CFR subchapter E).
（二）安全区二包括马库斯胡克航道外第7号锚地（如33 CFR 110.157(a)(8)所述）的全部水域。本规则生效期间，拟在马库斯胡克航道外第7号锚地锚泊的船舶，须至少提前24小时拨打电话（215）271-4807征得港口安全主任（COTP）的许可。请求获准在马库斯胡克外第7号锚地锚泊的船舶，船舶总长须不小于650英尺。港口安全主任最多准许两艘船舶同时锚泊，按“先到先得”原则安排。船舶仅可锚泊12小时。需接受公共卫生署、海关或移民当局检查的船舶，将由港口安全主任指派至相应锚地接受所需检查。总长不足650英尺的船舶，如欲在马库斯胡克航道外第7号锚地锚泊,可致电（215）271-4807联系港口安全主任提出申请，并可能视具体情况获得批准。鼓励船舶改用以下替代锚地：曼图亚溪入口附近的第9号锚地、格洛斯特（Gloucester）与费城卡姆登（Camden）之间的第12号锚地，以及深水点航道（Deepwater Point Range）外的第6号锚地。
(2) Safety Zone Two includes all the waters of Anchorage 7 off Marcus Hook Range, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(8). Vessels wishing to anchor in Anchorage 7 off Marcus Hook Range while this rule is in effect must obtain permission from the COTP at least 24 hours in advance by calling (215) 271-4807. Vessels requesting permission to anchor within Anchorage 7 off Marcus Hook must be at least 650 feet in overall length. The COTP will permit, at maximum, two vessels to anchor at a time, on a “first-come, first-served” basis. Vessels will only be allowed to anchor for a 12-hour period. Vessels that require an examination by the Public Health Service, Customs, or Immigration authorities will be directed to an anchorage by the COTP for the required inspection. Vessels under 650 feet in overall length may request to anchor in Anchorage 7, off Marcus Hook Range by contacting the COTP via phone at (215) 271-4807 and may be approved on a case-by-case basis. Vessels are encouraged to use Anchorage 9 near the entrance to Mantua Creek, Anchorage 12 between Gloucester and Camden, Philadelphia, and Anchorage 6 off Deepwater Point Range as alternative anchorages.
（三）安全区三包括曼图亚溪入口附近第9号锚地（如33 CFR 110.157(a)(10)所述）的全部水域。欲在曼图亚溪入口附近第9号锚地锚泊的船舶，总长须不小于500英尺。总长不足500英尺的船舶,如欲在曼图亚溪入口附近第9号锚地锚泊,可致电（215）271-4807联系港口安全主任提出申请，并可能视具体情况获得批准。
(3) Safety Zone Three includes all the waters of Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(10). Vessels must be at least 500 feet in overall length to anchor in Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek. Vessels under 500 feet in overall length may request to anchor in Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek by contacting the COTP via phone at (215) 271-4807 and may be approved on a case-by-case basis.
本规则生效期间，使用第7号锚地的船舶总长须不小于650英尺，使用第9号锚地的船舶总长须不小于500英尺。之所以实行此项限制，是因为此类尺寸的船舶因吃水限制，利用其他锚地的能力有限，且第9号锚地需用作总长在500英尺至700英尺之间船舶的溢流锚泊区。较小型船舶则仍可选用其他多种锚地，包括但不限于上文建议的第6号和第12号锚地。
Vessels must be at least 650 feet in overall length to use Anchorage 7 and 500 feet in overall length to use Anchorage 9 while this rule is in effect. We are instituting this restriction because vessels of this size are limited in their ability to utilize other anchorages due to draft and Anchorage 9 needs to be utilized as overflow for vessels between 500 feet and 700 feet in overall length. Smaller vessels maintain a host of other options to include, but not limited to, Anchorage 6 and 12, as recommended above.
进入、穿越或锚泊于安全区一律属禁止行为,除非船舶获得港口安全官(COTP)许可,或根据本规则及《航行规则》(33 CFR 第E分章)与作业挖泥船达成令人满意的通行安排。港口安全官可通过《海上安全信息公告》、《航行通告广播》及《地方航行通告》发布有关用于此类挖泥作业的船舶及设备的更新信息。
Entry into, transiting, or anchoring within safety zone one is prohibited unless vessels obtain permission from the COTP or make satisfactory passing arrangements with the operating dredge per this rule and the Rules of the Road (33 CFR subchapter E). The COTP may issue updates regarding the vessel and equipment being utilized for these dredging operations via Marine Safety Information Bulletin, Broadcast Notice to Mariners and Local Notice to Mariners.

安全区,特拉华河疏浚工程;宾夕法尼亚州马库斯胡克。
Safety Zones, Delaware River Dredging; Marcus Hook, PA.
(a) 地点。下列区域为安全区:
(a) Location. The following areas are safety zones:
(1) 安全区一包括:在马库斯胡克航道(Marcus Hook Range)内作业期间,按照《联邦法规汇编》第33编第83.27条规定显示操纵能力受限船舶灯光和号型的疏浚船周围250码范围内的所有水域,以及所有相关疏浚设备所在的水域。就执法目的而言,马库斯胡克航道包括特拉华河的所有可航水域,其范围以在该航道最上游端垂直于航道中心线所画的一条直线,至在该航道最下游端垂直于航道中心线所画的一条直线为界。
(1) Safety zone one includes all waters within 250 yards of the dredge displaying lights and shapes for vessels restricted in ability to maneuver as described in 33 CFR 83.27, as well as all related dredge equipment, while the dredge is operating in Marcus Hook Range. For enforcement purposes, Marcus Hook Range includes all navigable waters of the Delaware River, bound by a line drawn perpendicular to the center line of the channel at the farthest upriver point of the range to a line drawn perpendicular to the center line of the channel at the farthest downriver point of the range.
(2) 安全区二包括马库斯胡克航道外第7号锚地的全部水域,该锚地按照《联邦法规汇编》第33编第110.157(a)(8)条所述。
(2) Safety zone two includes all the waters of Anchorage 7 off Marcus Hook Range, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(8).
(3) 安全区三包括曼图亚溪(Mantua Creek)入口附近第9号锚地的全部水域,该锚地按照《联邦法规汇编》第33编第110.157(a)(10)条所述。
(3) Safety zone three includes all the waters of Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek, as described in 33 CFR 110.157(a)(10).
(b) 定义。就本节而言,指定代表是指经辖区港口长授权协助执行本节第(a)段所述安全区域相关规定的任何海岸警卫队现役军官、准尉或士官。
(b) Definitions. As used in this section, designated representative means any Coast Guard commissioned, warrant, or petty officer who has been authorized by the Captain of the Port to assist with enforcement of the safety zones described in paragraph (a) of this section.
(c) 规定。(1) 除非船舶依照本节规定及《道路规则》(33 CFR第一章E分章)通过甚高频调频(VHF-FM)无线电13频道或16频道与正在作业的挖泥船达成令人满意的通行安排,否则禁止进入或穿越第一安全区。要求通行的船舶应在抵达前至少1小时通过甚高频调频无线电13频道或16频道与正在作业的挖泥船联系。此外,船舶也可通过甚高频调频无线电16频道或拨打电话(833) 453-1007,征得特拉华湾辖区港口长(COTP)的许可。
(c) Regulations. (1) Entry into or transiting within safety zone one is prohibited unless vessels make satisfactory passing arrangements via VHF-FM radio channel 13 or 16 with the operating dredge per this section and the rules of the Road (33 CFR chapter I, subchapter E). Vessels requesting to transit shall contact the operating dredge via VHF-FM radio channel 13 or 16 at least 1 hour prior to arrival. Alternatively, vessels may obtain permission from the Captain of the Port, Sector Delaware Bay (COTP) via VHF-FM radio channel 16 or via phone at (833) 453-1007.
(2) 欲在第二安全区,即马库斯胡克航道外的第7号锚地抛锚的船舶,船长必须不小于650英尺,并须至少提前24小时拨打电话(833) 453-1007征得港口长(COTP)许可。港口长将按“先到先得”的原则,最多同时允许两艘船舶抛锚。船舶抛锚时间仅限12小时。船长不足650英尺的船舶可通过上述电话号码联系港口长,申请在马库斯胡克航道外的第7号锚地抛锚,并将视具体情况逐案审批。需要接受公共卫生局、海关或移民当局检查的船舶,将由港口长指引至指定锚地接受所需检查。(印刷页45207)
(2) Vessels desiring to anchor in safety zone two, Anchorage 7 off Marcus Hook Range, must be at least 650 feet in overall length and must obtain permission from the COTP at least 24 hours in advance by calling (833) 453-1007. The COTP will permit, at maximum, two vessels at a time to anchor on a “first-come, first-served” basis. Vessels will only be allowed to anchor for a 12-hour period. Vessels under 650 feet in overall length may request to anchor in Anchorage 7, off Marcus Hook Range by contacting the COTP at the phone number detailed above and may be approved on a case-by-case basis. Vessels that require an ( printed page 45207) examination by the Public Health Service, Customs, or Immigration authorities will be directed to an anchorage for the required inspection by the COTP.
(3) 欲在第三安全区,即曼图亚溪入口附近的第9号锚地抛锚的船舶,船长必须不小于500英尺。船长不足500英尺的船舶可申请在曼图亚溪入口附近的第9号锚地抛锚,并将视具体情况逐案审批。
(3) Vessels desiring to anchor in safety zone three, Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek, must be at least 500 feet in overall length. Vessels under 500 feet in overall length may request to anchor in Anchorage 9, near entrance to Mantua Creek, and may be approved on a case-by-case basis.
(4) 本节适用于所有船舶,但从事下列作业的船舶除外:执法、助航设施维护服务以及应急响应。
(4) This section applies to all vessels except those engaged in the following operations: enforcement of laws, service of aids to navigation, and emergency response.
(d) 执法。美国海岸警卫队在对相关区域进行巡逻和执法时,可获得联邦、州及地方机构的协助。
(d) Enforcement. The U.S. Coast Guard may be assisted by Federal, State, and local agencies in the patrol and enforcement of the zones.
(e) 执行期限。本节自2026年7月15日起至2026年10月31日止施行,除非特拉华湾辖区港口长提前取消。
(e) Enforcement period. This section will be enforced from July 15, 2026, through October 31, 2026, unless cancelled earlier by the Captain of the Port, Sector Delaware Bay.
