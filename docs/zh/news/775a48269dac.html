DHS宣布特朗普政府执法创纪录,边境拦截与遣返数字大幅上升

发布日期 2026年11月3日

美国国土安全部(DHS)发布新闻稿,总结特朗普总统重返白宫以来的边境管控与移民驱逐统计数据。该新闻稿强调边境墙建设进度、逮捕与遣返总数以及毒品查获量,以展示其执法成果。

要点

  • DHS称边境已连续17个月实现移民零释放。
  • 2026财年初步数据显示,边境拦截人数和全国遭遇人数远低于拜登政府时期的水平。
  • 本届政府上任以来已完成约330英里新边境屏障,已动用346亿美元边境墙资金。
  • 截至10月,DHS报告已完成近120万人遣返,逮捕超过125万人,其中包括逮捕2,288名涉嫌恐怖分子。
  • 新闻稿还提到与帮派相关的逮捕情况,包括MS-13和特伦德阿拉瓜(Tren de Aragua)成员,并宣传名为'Worst of the Worst'的公开数据库网站。
  • 这是一份总结执法统计数据的新闻稿,并非新的法规、政策变化或法律要求。

影响对象

无合法身份人士、受移民执法影响者,以及关注美国边境与遣返政策的公众。

建议行动

该新闻稿本身不要求采取任何具体行动,如对自身移民身份有疑问,应咨询律师。

让美国再度安全:在特朗普总统领导下,国土安全部实现了历史上最安全的边境,并以创纪录的速度驱逐犯罪非法外国人
MAKING AMERICA SAFE AGAIN: Under President Trump, DHS Delivered the Most Secure Border in History and is Removing Criminal Illegal Aliens at a Record Pace
发布日期:2026年11月3日
Release Date: November 3, 2026
通过国土安全部,特朗普总统和部长马克韦恩·穆林正在让美国再度安全
Through the Department of Homeland Security, President Trump and Secretary Markwayne Mullin are Making America Safe Again
华盛顿消息,在唐纳德·J·特朗普总统和马克韦恩·穆林部长的领导下,美国国土安全部(DHS)正在恢复法治,实现有史以来最安全的边境,驱逐危险的犯罪非法外国人,创造更安全的社区,并为子孙后代打造一个更加安全的美国。
WASHINGTON – Under the leadership of President Donald J. Trump and Secretary Markwayne Mullin, the United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is restoring the rule of law, delivering the most secure border ever, deporting dangerous criminal illegal aliens, creating safer communities, and making America safer for generations to come.
“在特朗普总统重返白宫不到两年的时间里,他已经兑现了其标志性承诺,即确保边境安全、逮捕并驱逐非法外国人,让美国再度安全,”穆林部长表示。“在特朗普总统的领导下,已有超过300万名移民离开美国。我们已连续17个月实现零非法外国人被释放进入我国。有些人曾试图声称我们的移民系统已经崩溃。但我们并不需要新的法律或新的体系,我们只需要一位新总统。”
“Less than two years after President Trump returned to office, he has fulfilled his signature promise to secure our border, arrest and deport illegal aliens, and Make America Safe Again,” said Secretary Mullin. “Under President Trump’s leadership, more than 3 million migrants have left the U.S. We have had 17 consecutive months with ZERO illegal aliens released into our country. Some people tried to say that our immigration system was broken. But we didn’t need new laws or a new system; we just needed a new president.”
得益于国土安全部的努力,美利坚合众国变得更加自由、更加安全、更加繁荣。以下是本部门为美国人民取得的部分胜利成果:
Thanks to DHS, the United States of America are freer, safer, and more prosperous. Below are some of the Department’s victories for the American people:
有史以来最安全的美国边境
The Most Secure American Border EVER
特朗普总统的竞选承诺之一就是确保边境安全,并解决其前任乔·拜登执政期间出现的边境危机。
President Trump ran on securing the border and fixing the border crisis that took place under his predecessor Joe Biden.
乔·拜登执政期间的边境
The border under Joe Biden
自重返白宫以来,特朗普总统已实现美国历史上最安全的边境。
Since his return to office, President Trump has delivered the most secure border in U.S. history.
边境执法:由于终结了“抓了就放”的做法、持续运用拘留和驱逐权力,并明确传达非法进入美国不会得到纵容或奖励的信息,美国历史上首次在一个财政年度内实现边境零释放。
Border enforcement: For the first time in American history, there have been ZERO releases at the border during a fiscal year, thanks to the end of catch-and-release, consistent use of detention and removal authorities, and a clear message that illegal entry into the United States will not be rewarded or tolerated.
美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)已连续17个月实现边境零释放的记录。
Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has recorded 17 consecutive months of ZERO releases at the border.
国土安全部近期公布的2026财年初步数据显示,2026财年的总抓获人数(98,708人)比拜登政府单月最高抓获人数(249,740人)低60%。
DHS recently announced preliminary Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 data showcasing that total FY26 apprehensions (98,708) were 60% lower than the Biden Administration’s single highest month alone (249,740).
此外,初步数据显示,2026财年全国范围内的遇见总人数为379,243人,为该机构历史上最低水平,相比之下,拜登执政期间每年为270万人次(高出七倍)。2026财年的遇见人数比拜登执政期间最高的2023财年低88%。
Additionally, preliminary data shows total nationwide encounters in FY26 were the lowest in agency history at 379,243, compared with 2.7 million per year under Biden (seven times higher). FY26 encounters were 88% lower than FY23 under Biden, which was the highest fiscal year ever.
智能边境墙建设:CBP和国土安全部正利用《一个美丽大法案》(One Big Beautiful Bill Act)拨付的资金,全力完成边境墙建设,以彻底封堵边境。
Smart Wall Construction: CBP and DHS are working to seal the border by finishing the border wall with funds from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
从本届政府执政之初到2026年10月30日,CBP已完成约330英里的新边境屏障建设,其中包括约139.5英里的主要边境墙、约73.9英里的水上屏障,以及约116.2英里的次级边境墙。
From the beginning of this Administration through October 30, 2026, CBP has completed ~330 miles of new border barriers, including ~139.5 miles of primary wall, ~73.9 miles of waterborne barrier, and ~116.2 miles of secondary wall.
截至目前,CBP已动用《工薪家庭减税法案》(Working Families Tax Cut Act)拨款中的346亿美元(占比75%)用于边境墙建设。
To date, CBP has obligated $34.6B (75%) of the Working Families Tax Cut Act funding for border wall.
毒品查缉:作为国家边境安全的第一线执法机构,CBP运用尖端的非侵入式检查技术和情报驱动战略,查缉致命毒品,打击目标不仅限于成品毒品,还包括前体化学品、制毒工具和压片机。
Drug Seizures: As the nation’s frontline border security agency, CBP utilizes cutting-edge nonintrusive inspection technology and intelligence-driven strategies to seize deadly drugs, targeting not just finished products but precursor chemicals, tools, and pill presses.
因此,初步数据显示,CBP在2026财年查获的毒品总量近100万磅,比过去四年年均600,000磅高出63%。
As a result, preliminary data shows CBP’s drug seizures in FY26 totaled nearly 1 million pounds – 63% higher than the 600,000-pound annual average of the last four years.
特朗普总统执政下的边境
The border under President Trump
保护美国人民免受最危险的非法外国人和罪犯的侵害
Protecting Americans from the Worst of the Worst Illegal Aliens and Criminals
在穆林部长的领导下,得益于美国移民与海关执法局(ICE)、CBP以及其他执法和移民执法机构的英勇工作人员,国土安全部已在全国范围内逮捕并驱逐了数以百万计的非法外国人,其中包括帮派成员、强奸犯、绑架犯和毒品贩运者。
Under Secretary Mullin's leadership, DHS has arrested and deported millions of illegal aliens across the country, including gang members, rapists, kidnappers, and drug traffickers, thanks to the brave men and women of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), CBP, and other law enforcement and immigration enforcement agencies.
在特朗普总统的领导下,已有超过300万名移民离开美国。截至10月,我们已驱逐近120万名非法外国人,并逮捕了超过125万名非法外国人。
Under President Trump’s leadership, more than 3 million migrants have left the U.S. As of October, we have now deported nearly 1.2 million illegal aliens and arrested over 1.25 million illegal aliens.
ICE在预防恐怖威胁方面发挥了关键作用,已逮捕2,288名已知或疑似恐怖分子(KST),并驱逐了2,143名已知或疑似恐怖分子。
ICE has been instrumental in preventing terror threats, with over 2,288 known or suspected terrorists (KSTs) being arrested, and 2,143 KSTs removed.
ICE和CBP已在全国各城市开展行动,共实施了超过一百万次逮捕。
ICE and CBP have conducted operations in cities nationwide, resulting in more than a million arrests.
自特朗普总统重新就职以来,移民及海关执法局(ICE)已实施12,018起涉及帮派的行政逮捕行动。其中包括1,965名MS-13帮派成员和1,553名阿拉瓜火车帮(Tren de Aragua,简称TDA)成员。
Since President Trump returned to office, ICE has made 12,018 gang-related administrative arrests. This includes 1,965 MS-13 affiliates and 1,553 Tren de Aragua (TDA) affiliates.
在特朗普总统就职第一年,国土安全部(DHS)还推出了广受好评的开创性新网页“头号通缉要犯”(“Worst of the Worst”,网址为wow.dhs.gov),访问者可通过该网页查询自特朗普政府执政以来在执法行动中被捕的数十万名犯罪非法外国人中的部分人员信息。
In President Trump’s first year, DHS also launched the critically acclaimed, groundbreaking new webpage, “Worst of the Worst” (wow.dhs.gov), which allows visitors to search through some of the hundreds of thousands of criminal illegal aliens arrested during enforcement operations since the start of the Trump Administration.
在唐纳德·特朗普总统和穆林部长的领导下,国土安全部的工作才刚刚开始。美国黄金时代的回归才刚刚拉开序幕。
DHS is just getting started under President Donald Trump and Secretary Mullin. The return to America’s Golden Age is only just beginning.
