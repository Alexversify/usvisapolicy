美国国土安全部终止对索马里的临时保护身份（TPS）指定

发布日期 2026年1月14日生效日期 2026年3月17日

美国国土安全部（DHS）宣布终止对索马里的临时保护身份（TPS）指定，自2026年3月17日当地时间晚上11时59分起生效。此后，依据该指定持有TPS的索马里人将不再享有该身份。

要点

  • 国土安全部部长在审查索马里国内状况并与相关机构磋商后，认定索马里已不再符合TPS指定的法定条件。
  • 终止将于2026年3月17日当地时间晚上11时59分正式生效。
  • 此后，索马里籍人士以及最后惯常居住地为索马里的无国籍人士将不再依据该指定享有TPS身份。
  • TPS终止后，受益人将恢复为获得TPS之前所持有的移民身份（如仍然有效），或恢复为在TPS登记期间合法取得且仍然有效的其他身份。
  • 根据《移民与国籍法》，部长关于TPS终止的决定不受司法审查。
  • 本公告为已在联邦公报刊登的最终通知，并非拟议规则。

影响对象

目前依据该指定持有TPS身份的索马里籍人士，以及最后惯常居住地为索马里的无国籍人士将受到影响。

建议行动

受影响人士应查阅联邦公报全文或咨询移民律师，以确认2026年3月17日终止后自身的身份及工作许可情况。

Through this notice, the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) announces that the Secretary of Homeland Security (Secretary) is terminating the designation of Somalia for Temporary Protected Status. The designation of Somalia is set to expire on March 17, 2026. After reviewing country conditions and consulting with appropriate U.S. Government agencies, the Secretary determined that Somalia no longer continues to meet the conditions for designation for Temporary Protected Status. The Secretary, therefore, is terminating the Temporary Protected Status designation of Somalia as required by statute. This termination is effective March 17, 2026. After March 17, 2026, nationals of Somalia (and aliens having no nationality who last habitually resided in Somalia) who have been granted Temporary Protected Status under Somalia's designation will no longer have Temporary Protected Status.

什么是临时保护身份?
What Is Temporary Protected Status?
《移民与国籍法》(INA)授权国土安全部部长在与美国政府相关机构协商后,如认定存在特定的国家状况,可指定某外国(或其部分地区)适用临时保护身份(TPS)。参见INA第244(b)(1)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)。部长可自行决定,向该外国的符合条件的国民(或最后惯常居住在该被指定外国的无国籍外国人)授予临时保护身份。参见INA第244(a)(1)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(1)(A)。
The Immigration and Nationality Act (INA) authorizes the Secretary of Homeland Security, after consultation with appropriate agencies of the U.S. Government, to designate a foreign state (or part thereof) for Temporary Protected Status (TPS) if the Secretary determines that certain country conditions exist. See INA sec. 244(b)(1), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1). The Secretary, in her discretion, may grant Temporary Protected Status to eligible nationals of that foreign state (or aliens having no nationality who last habitually resided in the designated foreign state). See INA sec. 244(a)(1)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(1)(A).
在某外国的临时保护身份指定或延期到期前至少60天,部长在与美国政府相关机构协商后,必须审查该被指定享有临时保护身份的外国的状况,并确定该临时保护身份指定的条件是否继续得到满足。参见INA第244(b)(3)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)。如果部长认定该外国的状况继续符合临时保护身份指定的具体法定标准,临时保护身份将延长6个月,或由部长酌情决定延长12个月或18个月。参见INA第244(b)(3)(A)、(C)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)、(C)。如果部长认定该外国已不再符合临时保护身份指定的条件,部长必须终止该指定。参见INA第244(b)(3)(B)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B)。对于“部长就某外国的临时保护身份指定、终止或延期所作出的任何决定”,不受司法审查。参见INA第244(b)(5)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(5)(A)。
At least 60 days before the expiration of a foreign state's Temporary Protected Status designation or extension, the Secretary—after consultation with appropriate U.S. Government agencies—must review the conditions in the foreign state designated for Temporary Protected Status and determine whether the conditions for the Temporary Protected Status designation continue to be met. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A). If the Secretary determines that the conditions in the foreign state continue to meet the specific statutory criteria for the Temporary Protected Status designation, Temporary Protected Status will be extended for an additional period of 6 months or, in the Secretary's discretion, 12 or 18 months. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(A), (C), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A), (C). If the Secretary determines that the foreign state no longer meets the conditions for the Temporary Protected Status designation, the Secretary must terminate the designation. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(B), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B). There is no judicial review of “any determination of the [Secretary] with respect to the designation, or termination or extension of a designation of a foreign state” for Temporary Protected Status. See INA sec. 244(b)(5)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(5)(A).
临时保护身份是授予符合条件的、来自根据《移民与国籍法》被指定为临时保护身份国家的国民的临时移民福利,或授予最后惯常居住在该指定国家的无国籍合格外国人的临时移民福利。在指定期间内,临时保护身份受益人有资格留在美国境内,且只要其持续符合临时保护身份的各项要求,即不得被遣返。此外,临时保护身份受益人获授权工作,并可在提出申请后获得就业许可文件(EAD)。临时保护身份受益人亦可申请并自由裁量地获准获得旅行许可。授予临时保护身份并不会导致或引致获得合法永久居民身份或任何其他移民身份。
Temporary Protected Status is a temporary immigration benefit granted to eligible nationals of a country designated for Temporary Protected Status under the Immigration and Nationality Act, or to eligible aliens without nationality who last habitually resided in the designated country. During the designation period, Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries are eligible to remain in the United States and may not be removed so long as they continue to meet the requirements of Temporary Protected Status. In addition, Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries are authorized to work and obtain an Employment Authorization Document (EAD), if requested. Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries may also apply for and be granted travel authorization as a matter of discretion. The granting of Temporary Protected Status does not result in or lead to lawful permanent resident status or any other immigration status.
为符合临时保护身份资格,受益人必须符合《移民与国籍法》第244(c)(2)条、《美国法典》第8编第1254a(c)(2)条所规定的资格标准,并依照《联邦法规汇编》第8编第244部分及第1244部分的实施细则执行。当部长终止某一国家的指定时,受益人将恢复其在获得临时保护身份之前所维持的相同移民身份或类别(如有)(除非该身份或类别此后已过期或被终止),或恢复其在登记临时保护身份期间以合法方式获得的任何其他移民身份或类别,只要该身份或类别在临时保护身份终止之日仍然有效。
To qualify for Temporary Protected Status, beneficiaries must meet the eligibility standards at INA section 244(c)(2), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(c)(2) in accordance with the implementing regulations at 8 CFR parts 244 and 1244. When the Secretary terminates a country's designation, beneficiaries return to the same immigration status or category that they maintained before Temporary Protected Status, if any (unless that status or category has since expired or been terminated), or any other lawfully obtained immigration status or category they received while registered for Temporary Protected Status, as long as it is still valid on the date Temporary Protected Status terminates.
索马里的临时保护身份指定
Designation of Somalia for Temporary Protected Status
索马里最初于1991年9月16日被指定为临时保护身份国家,依据的认定是索马里存在“特殊和临时状况”,致使索马里国民无法安全返回,且允许索马里国民暂时留在美国不违反美国的国家利益。[1] 索马里最初的临时保护身份指定其后延长了九次,分别为1992年9月17日至1993年9月17日,[2] 1993年9月18日至1994年9月17日,[3] 1994年9月18日至1995年9月17日,[4] 1995年9月18日至1996年9月17日,[5] 1996年9月18日至1997年9月(印刷页第1548页)17日,[6] 1997年9月18日至1998年9月17日,[7] 1998年9月18日至1999年9月17日,[8] 1999年9月18日至2000年9月17日,[9] 以及2000年9月18日至2001年9月17日。[10]
Somalia was initially designated for Temporary Protected Status on September 16, 1991, based on a determination that there were “extraordinary and temporary conditions” in Somalia that prevented Somali nationals from returning in safety and that permitting nationals of Somalia to remain temporarily in the United States is not contrary to the national interest of the United States.[1] The initial Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia was extended nine times, from September 17, 1992-September 17, 1993,[2] September 18, 1993-September 17, 1994,[3] September 18, 1994-September 17, 1995,[4] September 18, 1995-September 17, 1996,[5] September 18, 1996-September ( printed page 1548) 17, 1997,[6] September 18, 1997-September 17, 1998,[7] September 18, 1998-September 17, 1999,[8] September 18, 1999-September 17, 2000,[9] and September 18, 2000-September 17, 2001.[10]
2001年9月,索马里的临时保护身份指定获得延长,且索马里获得了新的指定(称为重新指定)。[11] 此后,索马里的临时保护身份指定延长了八次,分别为2002年9月17日至2003年9月17日,[12] 2003年9月17日至2004年9月17日,[13] 2004年9月17日至2005年9月17日,[14] 2005年9月17日至2006年9月17日,[15] 2006年9月17日至2008年3月17日,[16] 2008年3月18日至2009年9月17日,[17] 2009年9月18日至2011年3月17日,[18] 以及2011年3月18日至2012年9月17日。[19] 尽管索马里最初被指定为临时保护身份国家仅基于特殊和临时状况这一单一依据,但2002年7月的延长决定将索马里的临时保护身份指定依据改为武装冲突持续存在以及特殊和临时状况这两项并行依据。[20] 此后的各项指定均将这两项并行依据作为继续指定的理由。
In September 2001, the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia was extended, and Somalia was newly designated (referred to as a redesignation).[11] Thereafter, the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia was extended eight times, from September 17, 2002-September 17, 2003,[12] September 17, 2003-September 17, 2004,[13] September 17, 2004-September 17, 2005,[14] September 17, 2005-September 17, 2006,[15] September 17, 2006-March 17, 2008,[16] March 18, 2008-September 17, 2009,[17] September 18, 2009-March 17, 2011,[18] and March 18, 2011-September 17, 2012.[19] Although Somalia was initially designated for Temporary Protected Status on the sole basis of extraordinary and temporary conditions, the July 2002 extension designated Somalia for Temporary Protected Status on the dual bases of ongoing armed conflict and extraordinary and temporary conditions.[20] Subsequent designations included these dual bases as justification for continuing designation.
2012年5月,索马里的临时保护身份指定获得延长,且索马里再次获得了新的指定。[21] 此后,索马里的临时保护身份又额外延长了五次,分别为2014年3月18日至2015年9月17日,[22] 2015年9月18日至2017年3月17日,[23] 2017年3月18日至2018年9月17日,[24] 2018年9月18日至2020年3月17日,[25] 以及2020年3月18日至2021年9月17日。[26]
In May 2012, the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia was extended, and Somalia again was newly designated.[21] Temporary Protected Status for Somalia was subsequently extended an additional five times, from March 18, 2014-September 17, 2015,[22] September 18, 2015-March 17, 2017,[23] March 18, 2017-September 17, 2018,[24] September 18, 2018-March 17, 2020,[25] and March 18, 2020-September 17, 2021.[26]
在上述延长之后,索马里的临时保护身份指定又延长并重新指定了三次,分别为2021年9月18日至2023年3月17日,[27] 2023年3月18日至2024年9月17日,[28] 以及2024年9月18日至2026年3月17日。[29]
After these extensions, the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia was extended and Somalia was newly designated three times, from September 18, 2021-March 17, 2023,[27] March 18, 2023-September 17, 2024,[28] and September 18, 2024-March 17, 2026.[29]
部长终止索马里临时保护身份指定的权力
Secretary's Authority To Terminate the Designation of Somalia for Temporary Protected Status
在外国的临时保护身份指定或延期到期前至少60天,国土安全部部长(以下简称“部长”)须在与美国政府相关机构协商后,对被指定临时保护身份的外国状况进行审查,以确定该国是否继续符合该项指定的条件。见《移民与国籍法》(INA)第244(b)(3)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)。如果部长认定该外国不再符合临时保护身份指定的条件,部长必须终止该项指定。见INA第244(b)(3)(B)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B)。终止生效日期不得早于终止通知在《联邦公报》上公布之日起60天,或者,如果更晚,则不得早于该国最近一次指定延期的到期日。见同上。部长可以确定终止生效的适当日期,以及在终止生效日期之后签发或续发的任何与临时保护身份相关文件(例如就业许可文件,EAD)的到期日。见同上;另见INA第244(d)(3)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(d)(3)(规定部长在其认为适当的情况下,可自由裁量决定是否给予一定的“有序过渡”期)。
At least 60 days before the expiration of a foreign state's Temporary Protected Status designation or extension, the Secretary—after consultation with appropriate U.S. Government agencies—must review the conditions in the foreign state designated for Temporary Protected Status to determine whether the country continues to meet the conditions for the designation. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A). If the Secretary determines that the foreign state no longer meets the conditions for the Temporary Protected Status designation, the Secretary must terminate the designation. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(B), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B). The termination may not take effect earlier than 60 days after the date the Federal Register notice of termination is published, or if later, the expiration of the most recent previous extension of the country designation. See id. The Secretary may determine the appropriate effective date of the termination and expiration of any Temporary Protected Status-related documentation, such as EADs, issued or renewed after the effective date of termination. See id.; see also INA sec. 244(d)(3), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(d)(3) (providing the Secretary the discretionary “option” to allow for a certain “orderly transition” period if she determines it to be appropriate).
部长终止索马里临时保护身份指定的理由
Reasons for the Secretary's Termination of the Temporary Protected Status Designation for Somalia
根据INA第244(b)(3)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)的规定,在与美国政府相关机构协商后,部长审查了索马里的国家状况,并考虑索马里是否继续符合INA第244(b)(1)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A)和第244(b)(1)(C)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C)规定的指定条件。此项审查包括根据INA第244(b)(1)(A)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A),审查该国是否存在持续的武装冲突,以及由于该冲突,是否要求该国国民返回将对其人身安全构成严重威胁。部长还根据INA第244(b)(1)(C)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C),审查索马里是否继续存在使索马里国民无法安全返回的特别且暂时性的状况,以及允许索马里国民暂时留在美国是否有悖于美国的国家利益。
Consistent with INA section 244(b)(3)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A), after consulting with appropriate U.S. Government agencies, the Secretary reviewed country conditions in Somalia and considered whether Somalia continues to meet the conditions for the designation under INA sections 244(b)(1)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A) and 244(b)(1)(C), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C). This review included examining under INA section 244(b)(1)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A), whether there is ongoing armed conflict within the state, and whether, due to such conflict, requiring aliens who are nationals of that state to return would pose a serious threat to their personal safety. The Secretary also examined under INA section 244(b)(1)(C), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C), whether extraordinary and temporary conditions in Somalia that prevent Somali nationals from returning in safety continue to exist, and if permitting Somali nationals to remain temporarily in the United States is contrary to the national interest of the United States.

根据法律规定，如果国土安全部长认定“允许外国人暂时留在美国有悖于美国国家利益”，则禁止其以存在特殊和临时状况为由指定某国适用临时保护身份，或延长对某国的临时保护身份指定。[48] 国土安全部长已得出结论，认为允许索马里公民暂时留在美国有悖于美国国家利益。
By statute, the Secretary is prohibited from designating a country for Temporary Protected Status or extending a Temporary Protected Status designation on the basis of extraordinary and temporary conditions if she finds that “permitting the aliens to remain temporarily in the United States is contrary to the national interest of the United States.” [48] The Secretary has concluded that permitting Somali nationals to remain temporarily in the United States would be contrary to the national interest of the United States.
“国家利益”是一项宽泛的标准，可涵盖一系列广泛的考量因素，包括外交政策、公共安全（例如，与犯罪团伙成员身份的潜在关联）、国家安全、移民因素（例如，拉力因素）、移民政策（例如，执法优先事项），以及经济考量因素（例如，对美国工人的不利影响、对美国社区的影响）。[49] 因此，判定是否允许某一类外国人暂时留在美国有悖于美国国家利益，需要国土安全部长运用其专业知识和自由裁量判断，并参考与相关美国政府机构的协商意见。
“National interest” is an expansive standard that may encompass an array of broad considerations, including foreign policy, public safety ( e.g., potential nexus to criminal gang membership), national security, migration factors ( e.g., pull factors), immigration policy ( e.g., enforcement prerogatives), and economic considerations ( e.g., adverse effects on U.S. workers, impact on U.S. communities).[49] Determining whether permitting a class of aliens to remain temporarily in the United States is contrary to the U.S. national interest therefore calls upon the Secretary's expertise and discretionary judgment, informed by consultations with appropriate U.S. Government agencies.

There are significant public safety and national security risks associated with the continued designation of Temporary Protected Status for Somalia. The United States named Al Shabaab a Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) on March 18, 2008.[50] In Executive Order 13536, former President Obama found “that the deterioration of the security situation and the persistence of violence in Somalia, and acts of piracy and armed robbery at sea off the coast of Somalia, which have repeatedly been the subject of United Nations Security Council resolutions . . . constitute an unusual and extraordinary threat to the national security and foreign policy of the United States” and, declared “a national emergency to deal with that threat.” [51] Although the United States never formally severed diplomatic relations with Somalia, the U.S. Embassy in Somalia was closed in 1991. The United States formally recognized the new Federal Government of Somalia on January 17, 2013, following the adoption of a provisional constitution.[52] This lack of diplomatic engagement severely limits the U.S. government's ability to access reliable Somali records. Furthermore, the caliber of the civil and criminal history records is not comprehensive, accurate, or reliable, making meaningful vetting virtually impossible. In a January 20, 2025 Executive Order, President Trump instructed the Secretary of State, Attorney General, Secretary of Homeland Security, and the Director of National Intelligence to “vet and screen to the maximum degree possible all aliens who intend to be admitted, enter, or are already inside the United States, particularly those aliens coming from regions or nations with identified security risks.” [53] Due to this lengthy gap in U.S. diplomatic engagement, the United States cannot adequately vet Somali nationals, particularly aliens who were approved for TPS during this period of 1991-2013, for identity, criminal history, or potential terrorist affiliations, posing an ongoing threat to public safety and national security of the United States.

这些威胁和担忧仍在持续。2025年10月,现年23岁的阿卜迪萨塔尔·艾哈迈德·哈桑(Abdisatar Ahmed Hasan)承认犯有企图向一个被指定的外国恐怖组织提供物质支持和资源的罪行。哈桑在多个社交媒体帖子中公开支持“青年党”(Al Shabaab),后来又支持“伊斯兰国”(ISIS),并赞扬了在全球及美国境内发生的恐怖袭击。2024年12月,他曾两次试图从明尼苏达州单程前往索马里,意图加入“伊斯兰国”并为其作战。[57] 一名在美国拥有诸多机会的索马里青年[58]竟会选择支持索马里境内的恐怖组织“青年党”,这一情况令人深感不安。此案还引发了关于审查程序不足的严重担忧,反映出在外国人获得此类机会之前,相关甄别工作可能存在漏洞。2025年12月1日,财政部长斯科特·贝森特(Scott Bessent)宣布,美国财政部“正在调查有关……明尼苏达州纳税人的税款可能被转移给恐怖组织‘青年党’的指控”。[59] 国土安全部(DHS)的记录显示,存在一些索马里国民(或最后惯常居住地为索马里的外国人),他们是临时保护身份(Temporary Protected Status)受益人或申请人,目前或曾经因欺诈、公共安全及国家安全问题而成为行政调查的对象。部长据此在作出其决定时考虑了这些案例,因为欺诈行为及严重的公共安全违规行为有悖于国家利益。
These threats and concerns remain ongoing. In October 2025, Abdisatar Ahmed Hasan, 23, pled guilty to attempting to provide material support and resources to a designated foreign terrorist organization. Hasan publicly supported Al Shabaab and later ISIS in multiple social media posts and praised terrorist attacks globally and in the United States. In December 2024, he twice attempted to travel one-way from Minnesota to Somalia with the intent to join and fight on behalf of ISIS.[57] It is deeply troubling that a young Somali man [58] with so many opportunities in the United States would choose to support Al Shabaab, a terrorist organization in Somalia. This case also raises serious concerns about inadequate vetting processes, reflecting potential gaps in how aliens were screened before being granted access to such opportunities. On December 1, 2025, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced that the U.S. Treasury is “investigating allegations that . . . Minnesotans' tax dollars may have been diverted to the terrorist organization Al-Shabaab.” [59] DHS records indicate there are Somali nationals (or aliens who last habitually resided in Somalia) who are Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries or applicants who are or have been the subject of administrative investigations for fraud, public safety, and national security. The Secretary accordingly took account of those cases in making her determination, as fraud and egregious public safety violations are contrary to the national interest.
在题为《限制外国国民入境以保护美国免受外国恐怖分子及其他国家安全和公共安全威胁》的总统公告中,特朗普总统根据国务卿对相关国家的认定,并在综合考虑美国外交政策、国家安全及反恐目标后,对索马里国民连同另外11个国家的国民实施了全面入境暂停令。[60] 关于索马里,公告列明的理由包括:“索马里缺乏有能力或愿意合作签发护照或民事文件的中央政府当局,且不具备适当的审查和甄别措施”,以及“索马里境内也持续存在恐怖主义威胁的根源”。[61] 此举是对2025年1月20日行政命令的直接回应,该命令中特朗普总统指示国务卿、司法部长、国土安全部部长及国家情报总监“在最大程度上对所有意图获准入境、入境或已身处美国境内的外国人,特别是来自已识别出安全风险的地区或国家的外国人,进行甄别和审查”。[62] 2025年12月16日,特朗普总统发布了一项新的总统公告,题为《限制和限定外国国民入境以保护美国安全》,在该公告中,他决定继续全面限制和限定索马里国民的入境。[63]
In Presidential Proclamation “Restricting the Entry of Foreign Nationals to Protect the United States From Foreign Terrorists and Other National Security and Public Safety Threats” President Trump imposed a full suspension on the admission of Somali nationals along with nationals from 11 other countries, upon country identification by the Secretary of State and after accounting for the United States' foreign policy, national security, and counterterrorism objectives.[60] For Somalia, reasons included: “Somalia lacks a competent or cooperative central authority for issuing passports or civil documents and it does not have appropriate screening and vetting measures” and “[a] persistent terrorist threat also emanates from Somalia's territory.” [61] This was in direct response to the January 20, 2025 Executive Order, in which President Trump instructed the Secretary of State, Attorney General, Secretary of Homeland Security, and the Director of National Intelligence to “vet and screen to the maximum degree possible all aliens who intend to be admitted, enter, or are already inside the United States, particularly those aliens coming from regions or nations with identified security risks.” [62] On December 16, 2025, President Trump issued a new Presidential Proclamation “Restricting and Limiting the Entry of Foreign Nationals to Protect the Security of the United States,” in which he determined to continue to fully restrict and limit the entry of nationals from Somalia.[63]
根据2024财年国土安全部《入境/出境逾期停留报告》,索马里在非签证豁免计划国家中,商务或旅游访客(B-1/B-2)签证的逾期停留率为21.3%,学生及交流访问者(F、M、J类)签证的逾期停留率为25%。[64] 这些比率大大超过了B-1/B-2签证2.3%及F、M、J类签证3.2%的全球平均逾期停留率,商务或旅游访客的比率高出全球平均水平九倍以上,学生及交流访问者的比率高出七倍以上。[65] 自2018财年至2024财年,索马里的签证逾期停留率相较于全球平均水平一直居高不下。[66] 在非移民身份下超过所获准的停留期限,属于违反美国移民法律的行为,并给移民执法和资源分配带来挑战。签证逾期停留会分散本应用于其他关键执法重点的资源,例如应对非法越境问题。上届政府治下的大规模边境环境助长了未经授权的入境,加之在获取索马里国民可核实信息方面存在的差距,使美国无法对索马里国民进行充分的审查。
According to the Fiscal Year (FY) 2024 DHS Entry/Exit Overstay Report, Somalia had a non-visa waiver program countries business or pleasure visitors (B-1/B-2) visa overstay rate of 21.3% and a student and exchange visitors (F, M, J) visa overstay rate of 25%.[64] These rates exceed by a large margin the global average overstay rates of 2.3% for B-1/B-2 visas and 3.2% for F, M, J visas—over nine times higher for business or pleasure visitors and seven times higher for student and exchange visitors.[65] Somalia's visa overstay rates have remained high compared to the global average from FY 2018 to FY 2024.[66] Overstaying the authorized period of admission in nonimmigrant status is a violation of U.S. immigration laws and presents challenges for immigration enforcement and resource allocation. Visa overstaying diverts resources from other critical enforcement priorities such as addressing illegal border crossings. The high-volume border environment under the previous administration, which facilitated unauthorized entries, coupled with gaps in access to verifiable information for Somali nationals, has left the United States unable to adequately vet nationals from Somalia.
与该群体相关的福利欺诈问题已被充分记录。最近,美国司法部指控一名被告从事电信欺诈,该欺诈系一项1400万美元自闭症欺诈计划的一部分。根据对被告阿莎·法尔汉·哈桑(Asha Farhan Hassan)提起的指控,她及其同伙接触索马里社区的家长,招募其子女加入Smart Therapy机构,并设法使这些儿童无论是否确有需要均被认定符合自闭症治疗资格。随后,“作为提高入学人数的招募手段,哈桑及其同伙向将子女注册入Smart Therapy接受自闭症服务的家长支付每月现金回扣。”[67] 2023年,一对夫妇就其在一项2.5亿美元欺诈计划中的角色认罪,该计划在新冠疫情期间利用了一项联邦资助的儿童营养项目。根据美国司法部的说法,“阿里(Ali)和侯赛因(Hussein)分别将其公司Lido Restaurant和Somali American Faribault Education(SAFE)注册加入由Feeding Our Future担任赞助方的联邦儿童(印刷页1552)营养项目。注册后,侯赛因和阿里开始提交虚假夸大的报销发票,谎称每天为数千名儿童提供餐食。”[68] 2024年,另外三人,哈吉·奥斯曼·萨拉德(Haji Osman Salad)、沙马克·伊萨(Sharmarke Issa)和哈德拉·阿布迪(Khadra Abdi),就其在同一项利用新冠疫情期间联邦资助的儿童营养项目实施的2.5亿美元欺诈计划中的角色认罪。根据美国司法部的说法,“在2020年6月至2022年期间,萨拉德虚假声称Haji's Kitchen是明尼苏达州多个联邦儿童营养项目点1500多万份餐食的食品供应商……根据法院文件,伊萨作为明尼苏达索马里社区组织(Minnesota's Somali Community)和瓦坎餐厅有限责任公司(Wacan Restaurant LLC)的负责人,分别在赞助方A和Feeding Our Future的赞助下,骗取联邦儿童营养项目资金。”[69]
There are well-documented concerns related to benefit fraud with this population. Recently, the Department of Justice charged a defendant with engaging in wire fraud as part of a $14 million autism fraud scheme. Per the charges filed against the defendant, Asha Farhan Hassan, she and her partners approached parents in the Somali community to recruit their children into Smart Therapy and worked to get children qualified for autism treatment regardless of need. Then, “as a recruitment tactic to drive up enrollment, Hassan and her partners paid monthly cash kickback payments to the parents of children who enrolled their children in Smart Therapy to receive autism services.” [67] In 2023, a couple pleaded guilty to their roles in a $250 million fraud scheme that exploited a federally funded child nutrition program during the COVID-19 pandemic. Per the Department of Justice, “Ali and Hussein enrolled their companies, Lido Restaurant and Somali American Faribault Education (SAFE) respectively, in the Federal Child ( printed page 1552) Nutrition Program under the sponsorship of Feeding Our Future. 美国移民申请中的欺诈和虚假陈述也一直是一个令人担忧的问题。2017年,国土安全部(DHS)宣布正对四名从事欺诈和虚假陈述行为的索马里国民寻求剥夺其入籍资格。在该案中,索马里本土居民福西亚·阿布迪·阿丹(Fosia Abdi Adan)申请并获得了多元化签证,并利用该签证非法为三名外国人获取受益人签证,她谎称这三人分别是其丈夫和子女,但实际上与其毫无关系,且这些人使用的是完全虚假的身份。[70] 2025年7月,一名与索马里伊斯兰国(ISIS-Somalia)有家庭联系的索马里国民因实施庇护欺诈在联邦法院被判刑。[71]
Fraud and misrepresentation with U.S. immigration petitions have also been a concern. In 2017, DHS announced that it was seeking denaturalization against four Somali nationals who engaged in fraud and misrepresentation. In that case, Fosia Abdi Adan, a native of Somalia, applied for and received a diversity visa and used her visa to unlawfully obtain beneficiary visas for three aliens who she purported to be her husband and children despite having no relationship to them, and their using completely false identities.[70] In July 2025, a Somali national with familial ties to ISIS-Somalia was sentenced in federal court for committing asylum fraud.[71]
终止索马里临时保护身份(Temporary Protected Status)的指定存在令人信服的外交政策理由。在题为《致国务卿的美国优先外交政策指示》的行政命令中,特朗普总统宣布“从今天起,美国的外交政策应捍卫美国的核心利益,并始终将美国和美国公民置于首位。”此外,该命令指示“国务卿应尽快发布指导意见,使国务院的政策、项目、人员和运作与将美国及其利益置于首位的美国优先外交政策保持一致。”[72] 在近期发言中,美国常驻联合国代表团政治参赞约翰·凯利(John Kelley)表示,“美国仍致力于在索马里打击恐怖主义的斗争,但最终是时候让其他在索马里的安全、稳定和未来中拥有既得利益的伙伴增加其财政贡献了。”[73] 鉴于这些指示和声明,显而易见,终止索马里的临时保护身份与美国优先考虑本国利益并鼓励更多国际责任承担的外交政策相一致。
There are compelling foreign policy reasons for ending the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia. In Executive Order “America First Policy Directive to the Secretary of State,” President Trump declared “from this day forward, the foreign policy of the United States shall champion core American interests and always put America and American citizens first.” Moreover, it instructed “as soon as practicable, the Secretary of State shall issue guidance bringing the Department of State's policies, programs, personnel, and operations in line with an America First foreign policy, which puts America and its interests first.” [72] In recent remarks, John Kelley, a Political Counselor at the U.S. Mission to the United Nations stated “[t]he United States remains committed to the fight against terrorism in Somalia, but it is ultimately time for other partners with vested interests in Somalia's security, stability, and future to increase their financial contributions.” [73] In light of these directives and statements, it is clear that ending Temporary Protected Status for Somalia aligns with U.S. foreign policy by prioritizing American interests and encouraging greater international responsibility.
总之,国务卿决定终止索马里的临时保护身份指定,其依据是对国家利益因素进行的全面评估,包括公共安全、国家安全、移民诚信、外交政策,以及对该国当前暴力性质的分析。自1991年9月索马里首次获得临时保护身份指定以来,武装冲突已发生重大变化,表明暴力行为已不再构成持续的武装冲突,也不再完全妨碍索马里国民安全返回该国。此外,鉴于持续存在的国家安全和公共安全风险,加上欺诈和外交政策方面的考量,这些因素无论单独考虑还是综合考虑,均表明索马里已不再符合临时保护身份的法定条件。
In sum, the Secretary's decision to terminate the Temporary Protected Status designation for Somalia is grounded in a comprehensive assessment of national interest factors including public safety, national security, immigration integrity, foreign policy, as well as an analysis of the nature of the violence in the country today. The significant change in armed conflict from the initial designation of Temporary Protected Status for Somalia in September 1991 to the present shows that violence no longer constitutes an ongoing armed conflict nor does it fully prohibit Somali nationals from returning in safety. Moreover, in light of the continued national security and public safety risks combined with the fraud and foreign policy considerations, these factors considered individually and cumulatively establish that Somalia no longer meets the statutory basis for Temporary Protected Status.

终止指定的生效日期
Effective Date of Termination of the Designation
《临时保护身份》法规定,终止一国临时保护身份指定的生效日期,不得早于通知在《联邦公报》上公布之日起60天,或者(如晚于该日期)最近一次延期届满之日。参见《移民与国籍法》(INA)第244(b)(3)(B)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B)。
The Temporary Protected Status statute provides that the termination of a country's Temporary Protected Status designation may not be effective earlier than 60 days after the notice is published in the Federal Register or, if later, the expiration of the most-recent previous extension. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(B), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B).
《临时保护身份》法授权国务卿自行决定,针对终止及任何与临时保护身份相关文件(如就业许可文件,EAD)的失效,给予延长的“有序过渡”期。国务卿已自行决定,鉴于其认定继续允许索马里国民暂时留在美国有悖于美国国家利益,此处60天的过渡期已属充分且合理。参见INA第244(d)(3)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(d)(3)。因此,索马里临时保护身份指定的终止将于本通知公布之日起60天后生效。[75]
The Temporary Protected Status statute authorizes the Secretary, at her discretion, to allow for an extended “orderly transition” period with respect to the termination and the expiration of any Temporary Protected Status-related documentation, such as EADs. The Secretary has determined, in her discretion, that a 60-day transition period is sufficient and warranted here given the Secretary's finding that continuing to permit Somali nationals to remain temporarily in the United States is contrary to the U.S. national interest. See INA sec. 244(d)(3), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(d)(3). Accordingly, the termination of the Somalia Temporary Protected Status designation will be effective 60 days from this notice's publication date.[75]
DHS承认,索马里临时保护身份指定下的受益人在60天过渡期内仍具有就业授权资格。[76]因此,DHS通过本联邦公报通知,自动将先前根据索马里临时保护身份指定签发的某些就业许可文件(EAD)的有效期延长至2026年3月17日。因此,作为截至2026年3月17日持续具有就业授权的证明,临时保护身份受益人可出示其“类别”栏标注为A-12或C-19、且“卡片到期”日期为2023年3月17日、2024年9月17日或2026年3月17日的EAD。
DHS recognizes that Somalia Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries under the designation continue to be employment authorized during the 60-day transition period.[76] Accordingly, through this Federal Register notice, DHS automatically extends the validity of certain EADs previously issued under the Temporary Protected Status designation of Somalia through March 17, 2026. Therefore, as proof of continued employment authorization through March 17, 2026, Temporary Protected Status beneficiaries can show their EADs that have the notation A-12 or C-19 under Category and a “Card Expires” date of March 17, 2023, September 17, 2024, and March 17, 2026.
国务卿已考虑了索马里临时保护身份指定下的所谓信赖利益,(印刷页第1553页)特别是在考虑是否应如此前某些临时保护身份终止案例那样给予额外过渡期时。顾名思义,临时保护身份本质上是一种临时性身份。临时保护身份的指定具有时限性,必须定期审查,某些情况下甚至每六个月审查一次,临时保护身份通知也明确告知外国人该指定的到期日期。此外,是否给予有序过渡期完全由国务卿自行裁量决定。参见INA第244(b)(3)、(d)(3)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)、(d)(3)。该法规本身即要求及时公布国务卿的决定,并将终止生效日期延后至《联邦公报》终止通知公布之日起至少60天,或(如晚于该日期)现有到期日之后,由此本质上已预先设置了终止通知的提前通知期。参见INA第244(b)(3)(A)(B)、(d)(3)条,8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)(B)、(d)(3)。
The Secretary has considered putative reliance interests in the Somalia Temporary Protected Status designation, ( printed page 1553) especially when considering whether to allow for an additional transition period akin to that allowed under certain previous Temporary Protected Status terminations. Temporary Protected Status, as the name itself makes clear, is an inherently temporary status. Temporary Protected Status designations are time-limited and must be periodically reviewed, as frequently as every six months in some cases, and Temporary Protected Status notices clearly notify aliens of the designations' expiration dates. Further, whether to allow for an orderly transition period is left to the Secretary's unfettered discretion. See INA sec. 244(b)(3), (d)(3); 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3), (d)(3). The statute inherently contemplates advance notice of a termination by requiring timely publication of the Secretary's determination and delaying the effective date of the termination by at least 60 days after publication of a Federal Register notice of the termination or, if later, the existing expiration date. See INA sec. 244(b)(3)(A)-(B), (d)(3); 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(A)-(B), (d)(3).
关于终止索马里临时保护身份指定的通知
Notice of the Termination of the Temporary Protected Status Designation of Somalia
根据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第244(b)(3)条,即8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)条赋予本人作为部长的权力,本人已与相关美国政府机构协商,对以下事项进行了审查:(a)索马里的状况;(b)允许索马里国民(以及最后惯常居住地为索马里、无任何国籍的外国人)暂时留在美国是否违背美国国家利益;以及(c)索马里是否正经历对索马里国民人身安全构成严重威胁的持续武装冲突。基于本人的审查,本人已确定索马里不再继续符合《移民与国籍法》第244(b)(1)(A)条(即8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A)条)和第244(b)(1)(C)条(即8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C)条)规定的临时保护身份条件。
By the authority vested in me as Secretary under INA section 244(b)(3), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3), I have reviewed, in consultation with the appropriate U.S. Government agencies, (a) conditions in Somalia; (b) whether permitting nationals of Somalia (and aliens having no nationality who last habitually resided in Somalia) to remain temporarily in the United States is contrary to the national interest of the United States; and (c) whether Somalia is experiencing ongoing armed conflict that poses a serious threat to the personal safety of Somali nationals. Based on my review, I have determined that Somalia no longer continues to meet the conditions for Temporary Protected Status under INA sections 244(b)(1)(A), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(A) and 244(b)(1)(C), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(1)(C).
因此,本人命令如下:

(1) Pursuant to INA section 244(b)(3)(B), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(b)(3)(B), and considering INA section 244(d)(3), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(d)(3), the designation of Somalia for Temporary Protected Status is terminated effective at 11:59 p.m., local time, on March 17, 2026.
(2)关于终止根据该指定给予索马里国民(以及最后惯常居住地为索马里、无任何国籍的外国人)临时保护身份的信息,将在本通知发布后在美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)各地方办事处提供,也可通过USCIS联络中心电话1-800-375-5283查询。该信息也将发布在USCIS网站www.uscis.gov上。
