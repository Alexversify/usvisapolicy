EOIR要求OCAHO案件律师强制使用电子提交系统

发布日期 2026年3月2日生效日期 2026年3月2日

移民上诉事务执行办公室(EOIR)发布一项临时最终规则,要求律师和授权代表以及国土安全部(DHS)和移民及雇员权利科(IER)在首席行政听证官办公室(OCAHO)的案件中通过新的OCAHO门户进行电子文件提交。该规则已于2026年3月2日起生效,但司法部仍接受公众意见至2026年4月1日。

要点

  • OCAHO负责审理依据移民国籍法274A条和274C条的雇主制裁案件,以及依据274B条的歧视案件。
  • DHS、IER以及律师或授权代表现在必须通过OCAHO门户进行电子提交,适用于新案件和待审案件。
  • 无律师代理的申诉人和被申诉人可自愿选择电子提交,但一旦加入,未经法官或首席行政听证官许可通常不得退出。
  • 注册需要创建DOJ Login账户,与移民法院电子案件访问系统所使用的流程类似。
  • 这是一项临时最终规则,意味着已经生效,但司法部仍接受公众意见至2026年4月1日。

影响对象

在OCAHO处理雇主制裁或歧视案件的律师、授权代表、DHS、IER以及相关当事方。

建议行动

在OCAHO办理案件的律师和代表应通过DOJ Login在OCAHO门户注册,有意见者可在2026年4月1日前通过regulations.gov提交意见。

移民事务执行办公室(“EOIR”)正在为首席行政听证官办公室(“OCAHO”)受理的所有案件实施电子提交及记录申请系统。本临时最终规则(“IFR”)更新了实施上述电子提交及记录申请系统所需的相关法规,包括要求特定用户以电子方式提交文件,以及变更送达程序的方法。本IFR还对OCAHO的执业与程序规则作出若干其他细微修改,以澄清并完善现行法规文字。
The Executive Office for Immigration Review (“EOIR”) is implementing electronic filing and records applications for all cases before the Office of the Chief Administrative Hearing Officer (“OCAHO”). This interim final rule (“IFR”) updates the relevant regulations necessary to implement these electronic filing and records applications, including by requiring certain users to file documents electronically and changing service of process methods. This IFR also includes several additional minor changes to OCAHO's rules of practice and procedure to clarify and improve upon the existing regulatory language.

二、背景
II. Background
A. 引言
A. Introduction
2014年5月,行政听证首席法官办公室(OCAHO)开始试行一项自愿参与的计划,允许各方在OCAHO受理的案件中通过电子邮件提交和送达案件相关文件。见《行政听证首席法官办公室电子提交试点计划》,联邦公报第79卷第31143页(2014年5月30日)。该成功的试点计划被无限期延长,期间移民审查执行办公室(EOIR)开发了完整的电子提交申请系统。见EOIR,《OCAHO提交》(2021年6月4日),https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​ocaho-filing [https://perma.cc/​8HPG-QFR2]。
In May 2014, OCAHO began piloting a voluntary program in which parties may file and serve case-related documents by email in cases before OCAHO. See Office of the Chief Administrative Hearing Officer Electronic Filing Pilot Program, 79 FR 31143 (May 30, 2014). That successful pilot was extended indefinitely while EOIR developed a complete electronic filing application. See EOIR, OCAHO Filing (June 4, 2021), https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​ocaho-filing [ https://perma.cc/​8HPG-QFR2].
EOIR现已开发出一套电子提交申请系统,称为“OCAHO门户”,各方可通过该系统以电子方式提交和接收案件文件的送达,并可在线查看其OCAHO案件中的所有文件。见EOIR,《OCAHO门户》,https://ocaho.eoir.justice.gov/​。随着该系统的发布,EOIR正在修订为有效实施该电子提交申请系统所需的相关法规条款,包括对大多数用户强制要求电子提交。本临时最终规则(IFR)还包括对OCAHO执业和程序规则的若干其他细微修改,以澄清和改进现行法规用语。
EOIR has now developed an electronic filing application, known as the OCAHO Portal, through which parties can file and receive service of case documents electronically and view all documents in their OCAHO cases online. See EOIR, OCAHO Portal, https://ocaho.eoir.justice.gov/​. With its release, EOIR is amending the regulatory sections necessary to effectively implement this electronic filing application, including by mandating electronic filing for most users. This IFR also includes several additional minor changes to OCAHO's rules of practice and procedure to clarify and improve upon the existing regulatory language.
B. 历史沿革
B. History
OCAHO是EOIR三个裁决机构之一,另外两个是移民法院和移民上诉委员会(“委员会”)。自2003年起,EOIR开始长期致力于为移民法院和委员会开发和实施电子案件查阅与提交申请系统,该工作随着EOIR法院与上诉系统(“ECAS”)的全面实施而完成。详见《移民审查执行办公室电子案件查阅与提交》总论,联邦公报第85卷第78240页,第78241页(2020年12月4日)(详细记述了EOIR在此领域所作努力的历史)。除此更广泛的努力之外,并与之协同进行,EOIR一直致力于为OCAHO受理的案件实施一套完全电子化的案件查阅与提交系统。
OCAHO is one of three EOIR adjudicatory components, along with the Immigration Courts and the Board of Immigration Appeals (“Board”). Beginning in 2003, EOIR engaged in a long-term effort to develop and implement electronic case access and filing applications for the Immigration Courts and the Board, which was completed with the full implementation of the EOIR Courts & Appeals System (“ECAS”). See generally Executive Office for Immigration Review Electronic Case Access and Filing, 85 FR 78240, 78241 (Dec. 4, 2020) (detailing the history of EOIR's efforts in this area). In addition to and in concert with this broader effort, EOIR has been working to implement a fully electronic case access and filing system for cases before OCAHO.
首先,如前所述,2014年5月,OCAHO开始运行一项基于电子邮件的自愿电子提交试点计划。见联邦公报第79卷第31143至31144页。根据该试点计划,已注册案件的各方可通过电子邮件向OCAHO提交案件文件,同样也会通过电子邮件接收OCAHO行政法官(“ALJs”)和行政听证首席法官(“CAHO”)所发布命令和裁决的送达。联邦公报第79卷第31144页。该基于电子邮件的试点计划本质上是临时性的,其目标是利用所获得的经验开发出“更全面且永久性的电子提交系统”。联邦公报第79卷第31143页。
First, as previously noted, in May 2014, OCAHO began operating a voluntary email-based electronic filing pilot program. See79 FR 31143-44. Under this pilot program, parties in enrolled cases could file case documents with OCAHO by email, and would similarly receive service by email of orders and decisions issued by OCAHO's Administrative Law Judges (“ALJs”) and the Chief Administrative Hearing Officer (“CAHO”). 79 FR 31144. This email-based pilot program was intended to be temporary in nature, with a goal of using the experience gained to develop a “more comprehensive and permanent electronic filing system.” 79 FR 31143.
随后,EOIR在移民法院和委员会成功实施了ECAS系统,使该机构在开发和实施电子提交与记录申请系统方面积累了丰富经验。详见EOIR,《ECAS:律师与认证代表》,https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​ecas-attorneys-and-accredited-representatives [https://perma.cc/​5E6X-GGEL](计划概述及常见问题解答)。
Next, EOIR successfully implemented ECAS before the Immigration Courts and the Board, which allowed the agency to gain significant experience in developing and implementing electronic filing and records applications. See generally EOIR, ECAS: Attorneys and Accredited Representatives, https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​ecas-attorneys-and-accredited-representatives [ https://perma.cc/​5E6X-GGEL] (program overview and FAQs).
得益于这些项目所积累的经验,行政审查执行办公室(EOIR)开发了OCAHO门户网站,这是一个安全的公共应用程序,为OCAHO提供案件查阅和电子提交功能,并与OCAHO的内部案件管理系统相集成。EOIR已为OCAHO所有案件部署了OCAHO门户网站。OCAHO门户网站将取代OCAHO的电子邮件提交试点计划。
With the benefit of the experience gained from these projects, EOIR has developed the OCAHO Portal, a secure public application with case access and electronic filing capabilities for OCAHO, to integrate with OCAHO's internal case management system. EOIR has deployed the OCAHO Portal for all cases before OCAHO. The OCAHO Portal will take the place of OCAHO's email filing pilot program.
三、关于中期最终规则(IFR)变更的说明
III. Explanation of IFR Changes
本中期最终规则规定在OCAHO案件中实施OCAHO门户网站,用于电子案件查阅和提交。根据本中期最终规则,通过OCAHO门户网站进行电子提交对OCAHO案件中的所有律师和其他授权代表而言属于强制性要求。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(1)。这包括,例如,国土安全部(“DHS”)在依据《移民与国籍法》(“INA”)第274A条和第274C条(8 U.S.C. 1324a和1324c)提起的案件中强制提交新诉状;或在适用情况下,本部移民与雇员权益科(“IER”)或私人律师或授权代表在依据INA第274B条(8 U.S.C. 1324b)提起的案件中强制提交新诉状。为完成本电子提交应用程序的实施,EOIR对OCAHO的相关法规作出以下修改。(印刷页第9991页)
This IFR provides for implementation of the OCAHO Portal for electronic case access and filing in OCAHO cases. Under this IFR, electronic filing through the OCAHO Portal is mandatory for all attorneys and other authorized representatives in OCAHO cases. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(1). This includes, for example, mandatory electronic filing of new complaints by the Department of Homeland Security (“DHS”) in cases under sections 274A and 274C of the Immigration and Nationality Act (“INA”), 8 U.S.C. 1324a and 1324c, or by the Department's Immigrant and Employee Rights Section (“IER”) or private attorneys or authorized representatives, when applicable, in cases under section 274B of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1324b. In order to complete implementation of this electronic filing application, EOIR is making the following changes to OCAHO's regulations. ( printed page 9991)
A. 电子提交的资格、注册及其他要求
A. Eligibility, Registration, and Other Requirements for Electronic Filing
1. 谁可以进行电子提交
1. Who May File Electronically
本中期最终规则规定,对于代表OCAHO案件中申诉人或被申诉人的DHS、IER以及律师和其他授权代表而言,通过OCAHO门户网站进行电子提交属于强制性要求,该规定适用于新案件以及目前在OCAHO待审的案件。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(1)。
This IFR makes electronic filing through the OCAHO Portal mandatory for DHS, IER, and attorneys and other authorized representatives who represent complainants or respondents in OCAHO cases, and applies to both new cases and cases currently pending before OCAHO. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(1).
通过对律师和授权代表强制实行电子提交,OCAHO最终将能够为许多案件维护从始至终完整的电子记录。与EOIR在通过ECAS进行电子提交和记录方面的经验类似,OCAHO预计这将为各方当事人及OCAHO本身带来显著的效率提升。例如,已注册的当事人将能够在任何可连接互联网的地点以电子方式提交文件,并能够在线随时查看其案件中提交的所有文件。此外,电子提交将实现案件提交文件近乎即时的传递和接收,从而避免了通过实体邮寄方式提交所带来的延误。同样,OCAHO也将能够通过电子系统快速处理电子提交文件并维护案件记录。
By mandating electronic filing for attorneys and authorized representatives, OCAHO will eventually be able to maintain a complete electronic record for many cases from beginning to end. Similar to EOIR's experience with electronic filings and records through ECAS, OCAHO anticipates that this will create significant efficiencies for the parties and OCAHO. For example, registered parties will be able to file documents electronically from any location with internet access, and will be able to view all of the documents filed in their case online and on-demand. Furthermore, electronic filing will allow for near-instantaneous delivery and receipt of case filings, without the delays associated with delivery times for filings sent by physical mail. Similarly, OCAHO will be able to quickly process electronic filings and maintain case records through an electronic system.
OCAHO同时也向无代理人(“本人出庭”)的申诉人和被申诉人提供自愿性电子提交服务,因为如果这些个人选择使用电子提交,上述所有效率提升也可能惠及他们。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(2)。尽管对本人出庭的申诉人和被申诉人而言,选择启用电子提交属于自愿行为,但一旦选择启用,即在其案件存续期间持续适用,未经主审行政法官(ALJ)或首席行政法官(CAHO)许可,不得退出。同上。
OCAHO is also making electronic filing available on a voluntary basis to unrepresented (“pro se”) complainants and respondents because all of the efficiencies listed above may also flow to those individuals if they choose to use electronic filing. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(2). Although opting in to electronic filing is voluntary for pro se complainants and respondents, individuals who choose to opt in will do so for the life of their case and may not opt out without leave from the presiding ALJ or the CAHO. Id.
本人出庭的申诉人和被申诉人自愿选择电子提交的规定存在一项例外情形。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(1)。具体而言,在纸质提交不可行或不切实际的情况下,行政法官或首席行政法官可以要求本人出庭的申诉人和被申诉人以电子方式提交文件。该项权限仅适用于个别案件及有限的情形,例如存在邮件投递问题,或需要加快文件交换速度,特别是在当事人居住于美国大陆以外地区的情况下。
Voluntary electronic filing for pro se complainants and respondents is subject to one exception. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(1). Specifically, ALJs or the CAHO may require pro se complainants and respondents to electronically file documents where paper filing is infeasible or impracticable. This authority only applies to individual cases and in limited scenarios, such as where there are issues with mail delivery or there is a need for the expedited exchange of documents, particularly where parties reside outside the continental United States.
2. 注册流程
2. Registration Process
为了向OCAHO进行电子提交,律师、授权代表或无律师代理的申诉人或被申诉人必须使用一款DOJ身份验证应用程序(目前为DOJ Login)向OCAHO进行注册,该流程与ECAS注册流程类似。参见,例如,美国移民上诉事务执行办公室(EOIR),《ECAS:律师和获认证代表》,https://www.justice.gov/eoir/ecas-attorneys-and-accredited-representatives [https://perma.cc/5E6X-GGEL](在“常见问题”标题下,“DOJ Login与ECAS账户创建”子标题中,就“什么是DOJ Login?”的问题回答称:“DOJ Login是一种基于云的身份管理和身份验证服务。司法部对最终用户的身份进行验证,以便其访问司法部的应用程序和网络,包括远程访问。”)。注册流程要求每位个人提供基本的身份识别和联系信息,包括用户姓名、电子邮件地址、电话号码以及用户类型(DHS、IER或私人个体)。随后,注册系统使用多因素身份验证工具验证用户身份,并允许用户登录该应用程序。已拥有ECAS用的DOJ Login账户的用户也可以使用该账户。
In order to file electronically with OCAHO, an attorney, authorized representative, or pro se complainant or respondent must register with OCAHO using a DOJ authentication application—currently DOJ Login—similar to the process for ECAS registration. See, e.g., EOIR, ECAS: Attorneys and Accredited Representatives, https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​ecas-attorneys-and-accredited-representatives [ https://perma.cc/​5E6X-GGEL] (under the heading “Frequently Asked Questions” and the subheading “DOJ Login and ECAS Account Creation,” answering the question “What is DOJ Login?” by stating that “DOJ Login is a cloud-based identity management and authentication service. The Department of Justice authenticates the identity of end users for access to DOJ applications and networks, including remote access.”). The registration process requires each individual to provide basic identifying and contact information, including the user's name, email address, phone number, and user type (DHS, IER, or private individual). The registration system then uses a multi-factor authentication tool to validate the user's identity and allow the user to log in to the application. Users with existing DOJ Login accounts for ECAS may also use those accounts.
3. 符合电子提交条件的案件
3. Cases Eligible for Electronic Filing
所有OCAHO案件均符合电子提交条件,无论该案件是由国土安全部(DHS)、移民雇佣权利办公室(IER)还是由私人申诉人提交。参见28 CFR 68.6(a)。无论案件中的对方当事人是否已选择参与电子提交,电子提交功能均可供用户使用。但是,对于案件中一方或多方当事人选择不参与电子提交的情形,已注册电子提交的当事人须以另一种经批准的送达方式,向未注册电子提交的当事人送达以电子方式提交的文件,详见本序言第III.B节的进一步讨论。参见28 CFR 68.6(c)(2)。
All OCAHO cases will be eligible for electronic filing, regardless of whether the case is filed by DHS, IER, or by a private complainant. See28 CFR 68.6(a). Electronic filing will be available for users regardless of whether the opposing party or parties in the case have elected to participate in electronic filing. However, for those cases in which one or more parties to the case have chosen not to participate in electronic filing, those parties who are enrolled in electronic filing will be required to serve electronically filed documents on parties not enrolled in electronic filing by another approved method of service, as discussed further in Section III.B of this preamble. See28 CFR 68.6(c)(2).
4. 电子提交申请系统的可用性
4. Electronic Filing Application Availability
本临时最终规则(IFR)就在OCAHO电子提交申请系统不可用时,负有电子提交义务的当事人应如何处理提供了指引。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(3)。如果在某一特定案件的提交截止日当天,OCAHO电子提交申请系统因计划外系统中断而不可用,OCAHO将评估总体影响,并作出适当的提交截止日期调整(例如,将提交截止日期延长至电子提交申请系统恢复可访问状态后的下一个工作日)。同上。OCAHO将判定电子提交申请系统是否因系统中断而不可用,且该中断是否足以触发延长的提交截止日期,OCAHO将通过电子邮件、EOIR网站或其他可用的通信方式,就此类中断事项与外部用户进行沟通。同上。
This IFR provides guidance for how a party subject to electronic filing requirements should proceed if OCAHO's electronic filing application is unavailable. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(3). If OCAHO's electronic filing application is unavailable due to an unplanned system outage on the last day for filing in a specific case, OCAHO will evaluate the overall impact and make appropriate filing deadline adjustments ( e.g., extension of filing deadlines to the next business day that the electronic filing application becomes accessible). Id. OCAHO will determine whether the electronic filing application is unavailable due to a system outage sufficient to trigger the extended filing deadline, and OCAHO will communicate such outages to external users through email, EOIR's website, or other methods of communication, as available. Id.
各方当事人仍可酌情向行政法官(ALJ)或首席行政法官(CAHO)请求延长截止日期,或提交动议请求接受逾期提交的文件。参见,例如,Tingling诉里士满市案,13 OCAHO no. 1324c(2021年)(讨论OCAHO案件中批准延期的标准)。行政法官和首席行政法官均有权自行决定接受逾期提交的文件。参见,例如,美国诉Bhattacharya案,14 OCAHO no. 1380b, 第3页脚注3,第5-6页,第6页脚注8(2021年)(讨论OCAHO裁决者接受逾期或不当提交或送达文件的权力)。此外,如遇OCAHO电子提交申请系统不可用的情形,当事人可以通过纸质方式或电子邮件方式提交动议或延期请求。
Parties will maintain the ability to request an extension of deadlines from the ALJ or the CAHO, as appropriate, or to submit a motion to accept an untimely filing. See, e.g., Tingling v. City of Richmond, Va., 13 OCAHO no. 1324c (2021) (discussing standards for granting extensions of time in OCAHO cases). Both the ALJs and the CAHO have the discretion to accept untimely filings. See, e.g., United States v. Bhattacharya, 14 OCAHO no. 1380b, 3 n.3, 5-6, 6 n.8 (2021) (discussing the authority of OCAHO adjudicators to accept filings that were untimely or improperly filed or served). Additionally, in the event that OCAHO's electronic filing application is unavailable, parties are permitted to file paper or email motions or requests for extension of time.
此项有关计划外不可用情形的政策与移民法院及移民上诉委员会针对ECAS所采用的政策保持一致,参见85 FR 78244-45,并且与联邦法院针对其电子提交系统所采用的政策相似,参见《联邦民事诉讼规则》第6(a)(3)(A)条;《联邦上诉程序规则》第26(a)(3)(A)条。
This unplanned unavailability policy tracks the policy of the Immigration Courts and the Board for ECAS, see85 FR 78244-45, and is similar to the Federal courts' policy for their electronic filing system, see Fed. R. Civ. P. 6(a)(3)(A); Fed. R. App. P. 26(a)(3)(A).
另一方面,如果OCAHO的电子备案申请系统因计划性、事先已公布的系统停机而在特定案件的最后备案日当天无法使用,本临时最终规则规定,用户必须相应地做好计划,在系统可用期间以电子方式提交文件,或准备以纸质或电子邮件形式向OCAHO提交文件,以便满足任何适用的备案截止日期。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(3)。OCAHO将通过电子邮件、EOIR网站或其他可用的通信方式,将这些计划性停机告知外部用户。同上。本临时最终规则不会改变行政法官(ALJ)或首席行政法官(CAHO)决定如何处理逾期备案的权力,也不会阻止当事人提出接受逾期备案的动议。
On the other hand, if OCAHO's electronic filing application is unavailable due to a planned, previously announced system outage on the last day for filing in a specific case, this IFR provides that the user must plan accordingly to electronically file their documents during system availability or be prepared to file the documents in paper or email form with OCAHO in order to meet any applicable filing deadlines. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(3). OCAHO will communicate these planned outages to external users through email, EOIR's website, or other methods of communication, as available. Id. This IFR will not change the authority of the ALJs or the CAHO to determine how to treat an untimely filing, nor will it prevent parties from making a motion to accept an untimely filing.
最后,在所有情况下,本临时最终规则还赋予行政法官和首席行政法官自行决定接受纸质或电子邮件备案的权力。
Lastly, in all cases, the IFR also provides ALJs and the CAHO with the authority to accept paper or email filings at their discretion.
5. 涉密信息的备案
5. Filing Classified Information
OCAHO的电子备案申请系统未获得处理涉密信息的认证。(印刷页第9992页)参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(6)。用户不应通过OCAHO的电子备案申请系统提交涉密信息,该申请系统不会改变用户或本机构在涉密信息方面的责任。同上。用户需要以纸质方式提交任何涉密信息,并遵循现行的涉密信息备案程序。参见28 CFR 68.42(b)(详细说明在OCAHO案件中提交涉密或敏感信息的程序)。对于包含涉密信息的任何案件,OCAHO工作人员将保留纸质记录。
OCAHO's electronic filing application is not rated for classified information. ( printed page 9992) See28 CFR 68.6(b)(6). Users should not file classified information through OCAHO's electronic filing application, and the application does not change the users' or the agency's responsibilities related to classified information. Id. Users will need to file any classified information by paper and follow existing procedures for the filing of classified information. See28 CFR 68.42(b) (detailing procedures for filing classified or sensitive information in OCAHO cases). OCAHO staff will maintain a paper record for any case that contains classified information.
6. 滥用行为
6. Misuse
本临时最终规则包含相关规定,允许OCAHO在用户反复滥用或不当使用该系统时暂停其对OCAHO电子备案申请系统的使用权限。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(8)。这将确保OCAHO有能力处理对该系统的反复违规行为,例如不当或骚扰性备案、大量“垃圾”备案或欺诈活动等。参见,例如,Ravines de Schur诉Easter Seals-Goodwill Northern Rocky Mountain, Inc.案,15 OCAHO no. 1388a(2021年)(详细说明一方当事人无法或不愿遵守OCAHO电子邮件备案试点计划条款的情况);美国诉Pasquel Hermanos, Inc.案,18 OCAHO no. 1506a(2024年)(同上)。被暂停权限的用户可以选择不通过OCAHO电子备案申请系统进行电子备案,例如向OCAHO提交纸质文件。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(4)。
This IFR includes language allowing OCAHO to suspend a user's access to the OCAHO electronic filing application for repeated misuse or abuse of the system. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(8). This will ensure that OCAHO has the ability to address repeated violations of the system, such as inappropriate or harassing filings, “spamming” large numbers of filings, or fraudulent activity, among others. See, e.g., Ravines de Schur v. Easter Seals-Goodwill Northern Rocky Mountain, Inc., 15 OCAHO no. 1388a (2021) (detailing a party's inability or unwillingness to comply with the terms of the OCAHO email filing pilot); United States v. Pasquel Hermanos, Inc., 18 OCAHO no. 1506a (2024) (same). In lieu of electronic filings through the OCAHO electronic filing application, suspended users may, for example, paper file documents with OCAHO. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(4).
B. 备案与送达
B. Filing and Service
本临时最终规则还对OCAHO受理案件中备案和诉讼文书送达的完成方式作出了修改和说明。一般参见28 CFR 68.6。在本临时最终规则中,行政复议办公室(EOIR)正在更新OCAHO的法规,通过将电子备案列入OCAHO案件中允许的备案和送达方式清单,明确允许对所有诉状进行电子备案和送达。参见28 CFR 68.6(a)(2)。此外,如本序言第III.A.1节所述,EOIR正在修订OCAHO的法规,要求所有有代理人的当事人以电子方式提交所有案件文件(包括起诉书)。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(1)。
This IFR also changes and clarifies how filing and service of process is accomplished in cases before OCAHO. See generally28 CFR 68.6. In this IFR, EOIR is updating OCAHO's regulations to expressly allow for electronic filing and service of all pleadings by adding electronic filing to the list of permissible methods of filing and service in OCAHO cases. See28 CFR 68.6(a)(2). Moreover, as explained in Section III.A.1 of this preamble, EOIR is amending OCAHO's regulations to require all represented parties to file all case documents (including complaints) electronically. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(1).
为了简化备案和送达流程,对于所有当事人均使用电子备案的案件,OCAHO还将代表各方完成电子送达。参见28 CFR 68.6(c)(1)。当一方当事人成功将文件上传至OCAHO的电子备案申请系统,且所有当事人均使用电子备案时,OCAHO的电子备案申请系统将向各方发送关于新备案的电子通知。同上。这将简化电子备案方用户的备案流程,使其在符合条件的案件中只需向OCAHO提交文件,而无需为完成送达要求而进行多次邮寄。
In order to simplify the filing and service process, OCAHO will also complete service electronically on behalf of the parties for all cases in which all parties are using electronic filing. See28 CFR 68.6(c)(1). When a party successfully uploads a document to OCAHO's electronic filing application and all parties are using electronic filing, OCAHO's electronic filing application will send the parties an electronic notification of the new filing. Id. This will simplify the filing process for electronic filers by only requiring them to file their documents with OCAHO in eligible cases rather than needing to execute multiple mailings to complete service requirements.
但是,如果OCAHO案件中有一方或多方当事人未对该特定案件采用电子备案,电子备案方将需要通过其他经授权的送达方式完成对对方当事人的诉讼文书送达。参见28 CFR 68.6(c)(2)。同样,在备案方因系统不可用或根据OCAHO的自由裁量权而被允许在OCAHO电子备案申请系统之外进行备案的情况下,该备案方将需要完成对对方当事人的送达。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(3)至(4)。
However, if one or more parties to an OCAHO case are not participating in electronic filing for that particular case, the electronic filer will have to complete service of process on the opposing party by another authorized means of service. See28 CFR 68.6(c)(2). Similarly, in circumstances where the filer is otherwise allowed to file outside of OCAHO's electronic filing application, either due to system unavailability or pursuant to OCAHO's discretion, the filer will need to complete service on the opposing party. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(3)-(4).
在所有情况下,根据现行惯例,所有提交人均须在每次提交文件时附上送达证明,以证明已向对方当事人完成送达。参见28 CFR 68.6(c)(1)-(2)。例如,如果所有当事人均使用行政听证首席法官办公室(OCAHO)的电子提交申请系统,则电子提交人可附上如下送达证明:“本文件已通过OCAHO电子提交申请系统进行电子提交,且双方当事人均参与电子提交。因此,无需另行完成送达。”
In all cases, consistent with existing practice, all filers must include a certificate of service with each filing as proof of completed service on the opposing party or parties. See28 CFR 68.6(c)(1)-(2). For example, if all parties are using OCAHO's electronic filing application, then electronic filers can include a certificate of service that states: “This document was electronically filed through OCAHO's electronic filing application and both parties are participating in electronic filing. Therefore, no separate service was completed.”
当当事人需要自行完成送达时,本临时最终规则(IFR)还将为当事人提供以电子方式完成送达的选项。参见28 CFR 68.6(c)(2)(iii)。根据本IFR,在当事人需要在OCAHO电子提交申请系统之外完成送达的情况下,当事人可通过电子邮件、邮政邮寄或亲自递送的方式完成送达。28 CFR 68.6(c)(2)。为此,本IFR取消了目前在某些情况下允许通过传真提交文件的有限权限,鉴于现可通过OCAHO电子提交申请系统进行电子提交,或在允许的情况下通过电子邮件提交,该传真提交方式现已无必要。
When parties need to complete service themselves, this IFR will also provide parties with the option to complete service electronically. See28 CFR 68.6(c)(2)(iii). Under this IFR, in situations where the parties need to complete service outside of the OCAHO electronic filing application, the parties may complete service by email, by postal mail, or by personal delivery. 28 CFR 68.6(c)(2). In doing so, this IFR removes the current, limited ability to file by facsimile in certain circumstances, which is now unnecessary in light of the ability to file electronically through OCAHO's electronic filing application, or by email where allowed.
OCAHO还将通过电子邮件通知的方式,向参与电子提交的当事人送达OCAHO行政法官(ALJ)或首席行政法官(CAHO)作出的通知、命令和决定。参见28 CFR 68.3(a)(4)。该通知即构成完成送达,并(如适用)启动行政复议或上诉期限。同上。如果某一当事人未参与电子提交,OCAHO将继续通过邮寄或其他亲自递送方式向该当事人送达此类通知、命令和决定。参见28 CFR 68.3(a)(1)-(3)。在一方当事人参与电子提交而另一方未参与的情况下,OCAHO将以电子方式向参与电子提交的当事人送达,并以邮寄或其他亲自递送方式向未参与的当事人送达。
OCAHO will also serve notices, orders, and decisions issued by OCAHO ALJs or the CAHO by email notification to parties that are participating in electronic filing. See28 CFR 68.3(a)(4). This notification will constitute completed service and begin the administrative review or appeal period, if applicable. Id. If a party is not participating in electronic filing, OCAHO will continue to serve such notices, orders, and decisions on that party by mail or other personal delivery. See28 CFR 68.3(a)(1)-(3). In cases where one party is participating in electronic filing and another party is not, OCAHO will serve the participating party electronically and the non-participating party by mail or other personal delivery.
为使OCAHO能够有效完成电子送达,当事人必须在OCAHO电子提交申请系统中维持一个有效的电子邮件地址。28 CFR 68.6(c)(3)。如果用户的电子邮件地址发生变更,该用户必须立即联系移民审查执行办公室(EOIR)以更新其账户信息。同上。
In order for OCAHO to effectuate electronic service, the parties must maintain a valid email address within the OCAHO electronic filing application. 28 CFR 68.6(c)(3). If a user's email address changes, the user must immediately contact EOIR to update their account. Id.
OCAHO认为,当电子通知送达至用户所提供的档案中最新电子邮件地址时,即视为送达完成。同上。
OCAHO considers service completed when the electronic notification is delivered to the last email address on file provided by the user. Id.
C. 签名
C. Signatures
本IFR还为OCAHO案件中提交的诉状及其他文件的签名规定了标准。参见28 CFR 68.6(b)(7)、68.7(a)(2)。根据本IFR,OCAHO将允许四种不同类型的签名,具体取决于所提交文件的类型以及文件提交的方式:(1)手写墨迹原始签名;(2)加密数字签名;(3)电子签名;以及(4)格式化签名(conformed signature)。[1] 同上。
This IFR also provides standards for signatures on pleadings and other documents submitted in OCAHO cases. See28 CFR 68.6(b)(7), 68.7(a)(2). Under this IFR, OCAHO will allow four different types of signatures, depending on the type of document being filed and the method by which the document is being filed: (1) original, handwritten ink signatures; (2) encrypted, digital signatures; (3) electronic signatures; and (4) conformed signatures.[1] Id.
首先,无论是以电子方式提交还是以纸质方式提交,OCAHO均将接受带有手写墨迹原始签名、加密数字签名或电子签名的文件。同上。如果以电子方式提交,文件可使用加密数字签名、电子签名签署,或者使用手写墨迹原始签名签署后经扫描上传至OCAHO电子提交申请系统。28 CFR 68.6(b)(7)。如果用户使用加密数字签名对文件进行签署,但OCAHO电子提交申请系统当时不可用,则该用户可打印带有该数字签名的文件,并以纸质方式向OCAHO提交该文件。以纸质方式向OCAHO提交的文件,也可使用电子签名或手写墨迹原始签名签署。
First, whether filing electronically or on paper, OCAHO will accept documents with original, handwritten ink signatures; encrypted, digital signatures; or electronic signatures. Id. If filed electronically, the document may be signed with an encrypted, digital signature; an electronic signature; or an original, handwritten ink signature which is then scanned for upload to the OCAHO electronic filing application. 28 CFR 68.6(b)(7). If a user signs a document using an encrypted digital signature but the OCAHO electronic filing application is unavailable, the user may print the document with the digital signature and paper file the document with OCAHO. Paper documents filed by OCAHO may also be signed by electronic signature or original, handwritten ink signature.

Conformed signatures will not be accepted for anyone other than the user who is submitting the document. Conformed signatures typically consist of the user typing “/s/” followed by the user's name into the signature block. By signing into the electronic filing application, the user has demonstrated that they have completed the registration and identity verification process for the electronic filing application, thereby allowing the use of a conformed signature.

E. 其他修订
E. Other Amendments
本临时最终规则还对首席行政官听证办公室(OCAHO)的相关规定作出若干其他细微修订。
This IFR also makes a number of other minor amendments to OCAHO's regulations.
第一,本临时最终规则在28 CFR 68.2中新增“听证通知”的定义,以反映该文件在OCAHO现行实务中的名称,并为今后该文件名称可能发生变更提供灵活性。
First, the IFR adds a definition of “notice of hearing” to 28 CFR 68.2 to reflect the document's current name in OCAHO's practice as well as to provide flexibility to OCAHO in the event the document changes title in the future.
第二,本临时最终规则修订28 CFR 68.5(关于听证通知),以赋予行政法官(ALJ)在安排听证及庭前会议方面更大的灵活性,并明确允许在适当情形下以视频电话会议方式进行OCAHO听证。具体而言,本临时最终规则删除了行政法官须在收到被告对起诉状的答辩后30日内安排听证、庭前会议或二者兼有的要求。除极少数例外情形外,几乎所有OCAHO案件均通过裁决性动议或和解方式了结,因此在案件处理如此早的阶段(通常在证据开示尚未开始之前)即确定听证日期,会降低案件处理效率。此外,OCAHO的一贯做法是先发布总体排期令并要求当事人提交庭前陈述(见28 CFR 68.12),然后再安排庭前会议,因此在28 CFR 68.5所设想的阶段安排庭前会议同样效率低下。对28 CFR 68.5的修订仅删除了该30日的要求,以便OCAHO行政法官能够根据每起案件的具体情况,以最有效率的方式管理案件。
Second, the IFR revises 28 CFR 68.5 (regarding notices of hearings) to allow ALJs increased flexibility in the scheduling of hearings and prehearing conferences, and to expressly allow for OCAHO hearings to be conducted by video teleconference in appropriate circumstances. In particular, the IFR removes the requirement that an ALJ schedule a hearing, a pre-hearing conference, or both within 30 days of receiving a respondent's answer to a complaint. With rare exceptions, nearly all OCAHO cases are decided through dispositive motions or settlements, making the setting of a hearing date at such an early stage of the case—and typically before discovery has commenced—inefficient for case processing. Further, OCAHO's usual practice is to issue a general scheduling order and seek prehearing statements from the parties, see28 CFR 68.12, before scheduling a prehearing conference, making the setting of a prehearing conference at the stage contemplated by 28 CFR 68.5 similarly inefficient. The revision to 28 CFR 68.5 simply removes the 30-day requirement to provide full flexibility for OCAHO ALJs to manage their cases in the most efficient manner based on the particular circumstances of each case.
第三,本临时最终规则修订28 CFR 68.13(关于会议),明确赋予行政法官依照OCAHO和解官员计划,将适当案件转交和解官员处理的权力。见《OCAHO实务手册》第4.7章(2025年4月3日),网址:https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​reference-materials/​ocaho/​chapter-4/​7 [https://perma.cc/​44ZU-UTRK]。
Third, the IFR revises 28 CFR 68.13 (regarding conferences) to expressly include the authority for ALJs to refer appropriate cases to a settlement officer, in accordance with OCAHO's Settlement Officer Program. See OCAHO Practice Manual, Chapter 4.7 (Apr. 3, 2025), https://www.justice.gov/​eoir/​reference-materials/​ocaho/​chapter-4/​7 [ https://perma.cc/​44ZU-UTRK].
第四,本临时最终规则明确规定,证据开示请求、答复或回应不应向行政法官提交。相反,行政法官可依当事人申请或依职权主动下令,要求提交此类证据开示请求、答复或回应。见28 CFR 68.18(a)。
Fourth, the IFR clarifies that discovery requests, answers, or responses should not be filed with the ALJ. Rather, upon motion of a party or on the ALJ's own initiative, the ALJ may order that such requests for discovery, answers, or responses thereto be filed. 28 CFR 68.18(a).
第五,本临时最终规则删除了28 CFR 68.52中关于以“3.5英寸软盘”向行政法官提供拟定最终裁定副本的过时表述。
Fifth, the IFR replaces the outdated reference to providing a copy of a proposed final order to the ALJ “on a 3.5” microdisk” in 28 CFR 68.52.
最后,本临时最终规则明确了28 CFR 68.54中关于提交行政复核请求及相关文件的要求,增加了电子提交方式的选项,并删除了对28 CFR 68.6(c)的交叉引用,转而在该条款中直接规定与行政复核相关文件的提交及送达要求。
Finally, the IFR clarifies the requirements in 28 CFR 68.54 for filing a request for administrative review and related documents by adding an electronic filing option and removing a cross-reference to 28 CFR 68.6(c) in favor of simply stating the filing and service requirements for documents related to administrative review in that section.

A. Administrative Procedure Act
《行政程序法》(“APA”)一般要求各机构在《联邦公报》上公布拟议规则制定通知,并在公布后,“使利害关系人有机会通过提交书面资料、意见或论据参与该规则制定程序”。见5 U.S.C. 553(b)及(c)。《行政程序法》进一步规定,除某些特定情形外,实体性规则的必要公布或送达应在其生效日期前不少于30日作出。同上,553(d)。但是,如下文详述,《行政程序法》对上述一般性要求规定了例外情形。
The Administrative Procedure Act (“APA”) generally requires agencies to publish notice of a proposed rulemaking in the Federal Register and, after such notice, “give interested persons an opportunity to participate in the rulemaking through submission of written data, views, or arguments.” 5 U.S.C. 553(b) and (c). The APA further provides that the required publication or service of a substantive rule shall be made not less than 30 days before its effective date, except in certain circumstances. Id. 553(d). 根据《行政程序法》(APA),并基于下文所述理由,本临时最终规则(IFR)因属于机构程序和实务规则而豁免于通知和评议程序以及延迟生效日期的要求。5 U.S.C. 553(b)(A)、(d)(3)。与此同时,本部欢迎并征求对本临时最终规则在颁布后提出的意见。
Consistent with the APA and for the reasons explained below, this IFR is exempt from notice-and-comment procedures and the delayed-effective-date requirement, because it is a rule of agency procedure and practice. 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(A), (d)(3). At the same time, the Department seeks and welcomes post-promulgation comments on this IFR.
《行政程序法》的通知和评议要求不适用于“机构组织、程序或实务规则”。5 U.S.C. 553(b)(A)。法院“已将术语‘程序例外'作为该豁免的简称”。AFL-CIO诉美国国家劳资关系委员会案,57 F.4th 1023, 1034 (D.C. Cir. 2023)(内部引用标记省略)。“符合……程序例外规定的规则的关键特征在于,其所涵盖的机构行为本身并不改变当事方的权利或利益,尽管它可能改变当事方向机构陈述自己或其观点的方式。”同上(内部引用标记省略);另参见得克萨斯州诉美国案,809 F.3d 134, 176 (5th Cir. 2015)(认定当规则“修改实体性权利和利益”时该规则不属于程序性规则)(引用美国劳工部诉卡斯特金属公司案,744 F.2d 1145, 1153 (5th Cir. 1984))。
The APA's notice-and-comment requirements do not apply to “rules of agency organization, procedure, or practice.” 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(A). Courts “have used the term `procedural exception' as shorthand for that exemption.” AFL-CIO v. NLRB, 57 F.4th 1023, 1034 (D.C. (cleaned up); cf. Texas v. United States, 809 F.3d 134, 176 (5th Cir. 2015) (holding that a rule is not procedural when it “modifies substantive rights and interests” (quoting U.S. Dep't of Lab. v. Kast Metals Corp., 744 F.2d 1145, 1153 (5th Cir. 1984))).

1. Costs and Savings Associated With Electronic Filing

Under this IFR, OCAHO will be able to efficiently: (1) intake electronic documents, which will be automatically added to an electronic record of proceeding; (2) maintain and review fully electronic records of proceeding; [2] and (3) issue electronic orders and decisions that will be electronically served on participating parties.
美国移民法院行政办公室(EOIR)通过全面实施电子案件接入系统(ECAS),在移民法院及移民上诉委员会同样实现了所有这些效率提升。例如,参见86 FR 70714(其中说明,在最初的ECAS试点项目中,该机构处理指控性文件的速度“几乎快了10倍”,处理非指控性文件的速度比纸质提交“快约25%”)。
EOIR similarly realized all of these efficiency gains at the Immigration Courts and the Board through the full implementation of ECAS. See, e.g.,86 FR 70714 (explaining for example that, in the initial ECAS pilot program, the agency processed charging documents “nearly 10 times faster” and non-charging documents “approximately 25 percent faster” than paper filings).
除ECAS取得的巨大成功外,OCAHO还通过一项基于电子邮件的试点项目尝试了电子提交。尽管该试点项目的范围更为有限,例如并未实现自动送达程序,但OCAHO报告称该电子邮件试点带来了明显的效率提升,而随着OCAHO电子提交应用程序的实施,这种提升还将进一步增强。既然OCAHO电子提交应用程序已经实施,且本临时最终规则已经发布,OCAHO将能够转向以电子方式接收文件,并完全以电子方式维护诉讼记录,从而大幅节省与打印、扫描、存储及其他方式使用纸质案卷相关的人工成本。
In addition to ECAS's resounding success, OCAHO has also piloted electronic filing through an email-based pilot program. Although that pilot was more limited—for instance, by not having automatic service of process—OCAHO has reported noticeable efficiency gains from the email pilot, which will only further increase with the implementation of OCAHO's electronic filing application. Now that the OCAHO electronic filing application has been implemented and this IFR has been issued, OCAHO will be able to move towards electronically intaking documents and maintaining the record of proceeding fully electronically, which will save significant labor costs associated with printing, scanning, storing, and otherwise using paper case files.
以电子申请取代纸质邮寄申请,也将解决行政听证与申诉办公室(OCAHO)目前面临的若干问题。例如,即时电子申请将消除大部分邮寄延误,包括涉及位于美国大陆以外当事人的延误。参见,例如,Zajradhara诉Kang Corp.案,19 OCAHO no. 1555(2024年)(描述塞班岛案件中“邮寄申请所固有的重大延误”)。案件的截止日期也可以迅速确定,因为采用电子申请( printed page 9995)的案件截止日期无需额外预留时间以应对纸质邮件投递延误。参见28 CFR 68.8(c)(3)(要求凡通过普通邮件发送文件时,相关答复截止日期需顺延五天)。电子申请还将解决围绕申请日期和截止日期产生的歧义和混淆。参见,例如,Y.Y.诉Zuora, Inc.案,15 OCAHO no. 1402(2021年)(讨论通过邮寄方式送达起诉书副本时当事人收到时间的不确定性和延误问题);美国诉Terrapower, LLC案,19 OCAHO no. 1548e(2025年)(“电子申请注册有可能,甚至很可能,减少动议在案件已裁决后才送达的情形。”);美国诉Silverado Stages, Inc.案,10 OCAHO no. 1185(2013年)(首席行政法官命令认定“送达证明书上的日期与实际提交和送达的日期相矛盾”);In re Investigation of Space Expl. Techs. Corp.案,14 OCAHO no. 1378(2020年)(认定一项修改或撤销传票的申请逾期提出,因该申请虽在截止日期前通过电子邮件发送给另一方当事人,但邮寄至OCAHO,因此直至截止日期之后才被收到并视为已提交)。
Replacing paper mail filing with electronic filing will also address a number of issues currently faced by OCAHO. For instance, instantaneous electronic filing will eliminate most mail delays, including those involving parties located outside of the continental United States. See, e.g., Zajradhara v. Kang Corp., 19 OCAHO no. 1555 (2024) (describing the “significant delays inherent with mail filing” for case based out of Saipan). Case deadlines can also be set quickly, as deadlines in cases with electronic ( printed page 9995) filing do not require additional time to account for paper mail delivery. See28 CFR 68.8(c)(3) (requiring the addition of five days to relevant response deadlines whenever documents are sent by ordinary mail). Electronic filing will also resolve ambiguities and confusion surrounding filing dates and deadlines. See, e.g., Y.Y. v. Zuora, Inc., 15 OCAHO no. 1402 (2021) (discussing uncertainty and delay in a party's receipt of a copy of the complaint when it was sent by mail); United States v. Terrapower, LLC, 19 OCAHO no. 1548e (2025) (“It is possible, or perhaps even likely, that registration in e-filing would minimize instances of motions arriving after a matter has already been adjudicated.”); United States v. Silverado Stages, Inc., 10 OCAHO no. 1185 (2013) (CAHO order finding that “[t]he date on the certificate of service is contradicted by the actual date on which filing and service was made”); In re Investigation of Space Expl. Techs. Corp., 14 OCAHO no. 1378 (2020) (finding a petition to modify or revoke a subpoena untimely where it was emailed to the other party by the deadline but mailed to OCAHO and thus not received and considered filed until after the deadline).
因此,基于从这些项目中获得的重要经验,本部预期OCAHO电子申请应用程序的实施将带来类似的效益。
As such, based on the significant experience gained from these projects, the Department anticipates similar benefits to OCAHO with the implementation of OCAHO's electronic filing application.
本部同样预期将为公众,主要是律师和获授权代表人,包括国土安全部(DHS)和移民相关就业歧视事务办公室(IER),节省成本,这些主体根据本规则将被要求向OCAHO进行电子申请。首先,这些当事人将能够免除纸质申请以及随之而来的邮寄或快递费用。无律师代理的当事人同样将从电子申请的可用性中受益。参见Zajradhara诉Costa World Corp.案,19 OCAHO no. 1546c(2024年)(行政法官命令允许一名无律师代理的申诉人电子提交文件,因为“他正经历经济困难,这可能影响他邮寄申请文件的能力”)。
The Department similarly anticipates costs savings for the public, mainly for attorneys and authorized representatives—including DHS and IER—who will be required to electronically file with OCAHO under this rule. Primarily, these parties will be able to forgo paper filings and any attendant mailing or courier costs. Pro se parties will similarly benefit from the availability of electronic filing. See Zajradhara v. Costa World Corp., 19 OCAHO no. 1546c (2024) (ALJ order allowing a pro se complainant to electronically file a document because “he was experiencing financial difficulty, which could impact his ability to mail a filing”).
此外,本部预期与熟悉OCAHO电子申请应用程序相关的任何成本都将微不足道,因为该应用程序操作简便,且大多数用户应普遍熟悉在线电子上传文件的操作。此外,移民上诉行政办公室(EOIR)已在其网站上发布了逐步指导,说明在线注册和电子申请流程。参见EOIR,《OCAHO电子申请门户用户指南》(2025年6月13日),https://ocaho.eoir.justice.gov/​Content/​OCAHOPortalUserGuide.pdf [https://perma.cc/​63PD-RVN7]。
Additionally, the Department anticipates that any costs related to familiarization with OCAHO's electronic filing application will be de minimis, as the application is straightforward and most users should be generally familiar with electronically uploading documents online. Moreover, EOIR has published step-by-step guidance on its website explaining the online registration and electronic filing process. See EOIR, OCAHO Portal for E-Filing User Guide (June 13, 2025), https://ocaho.eoir.justice.gov/​Content/​OCAHOPortalUserGuide.pdf [ https://perma.cc/​63PD-RVN7].
2. 与其他变更相关的成本和节约
2. Costs and Savings Related to Other Changes
最后,本部认为与本临时最终规则(IFR)序言第III.E节所载的微小变更相关的任何成本均微不足道,且该节所述的效益超过这些成本。也许最为重要的是,本临时最终规则将OCAHO受理案件中使用和解会议的程序予以成文化。然而,此类和解会议属自愿性质,需经所有当事方同意。因此,本部预期不会给各方带来任何额外成本,反而可能因参与和解程序而非在OCAHO经历完整裁决程序而获得潜在的效率节约。
Finally, the Department believes any costs related to the IFR's minor changes contained in Section III.E of this preamble to be de minimis, and are outweighed by the benefits described in that section. Perhaps most significantly, the IFR codifies procedures for the use of settlement conferences in cases before OCAHO. However, such settlement conferences are voluntary and require the consent of all parties. Therefore, the Department does not anticipate any additional costs on the parties but, rather, potential efficiency savings by engaging in the settlement process rather than proceeding through a full adjudication before OCAHO.
