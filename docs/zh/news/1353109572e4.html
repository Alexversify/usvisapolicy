ICE宣布不到一周内逮捕逾100名帮派成员

发布日期 2026年10月19日

美国国土安全部(DHS)宣布,ICE在2026年10月4日至10日期间逮捕了131名有帮派关联的人员,其中包括MS-13和特伦德阿拉瓜(TDA)成员。DHS表示,自2025年1月20日以来,ICE在全国已实施与帮派相关的行政拘捕共12,018起。

要点

  • 2026年10月4日至10日期间,ICE执法与遣返行动局(ERO)逮捕129人,国土安全调查局(HSI)逮捕2人,共计131名帮派关联人员被捕。
  • 被捕人员中包括MS-13成员22人,特伦德阿拉瓜(TDA)成员13人。
  • DHS表示,自2025年1月20日以来,ICE已实施与帮派相关的行政拘捕12,018起,其中包括MS-13成员1,965人和TDA成员1,553人。
  • DHS列举了部分具体案例,涉及此前曾被驱逐出境、非法再入境,或此前被边境巡逻队逮捕后在前几届政府时期被释放的人员。
  • DHS部长马克韦恩·穆林(Markwayne Mullin)表示,政府正致力于逮捕和驱逐帮派及贩毒集团成员。

影响对象

此次公告涉及被DHS认定为MS-13和特伦德阿拉瓜等帮派成员或关联人员,且在美国没有合法移民身份的个人。

ICE在不到一周内逮捕100多名帮派成员
ICE Arrests More Than 100 Gang Members in Less Than a Week
发布日期：2026年10月19日
Release Date: October 19, 2026
被捕人员中包括MS-13和阿拉瓜火车帮(Tren de Aragua)成员
Among those arrested were members of MS-13 and Tren de Aragua
华盛顿 - 美国国土安全部（Department of Homeland Security，简称DHS）发布以下声明，宣布美国移民及海关执法局（U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement，简称ICE）在不到一周的时间内逮捕了100多名帮派成员，其中包括MS-13和阿拉瓜火车帮(TDA)等帮派的成员。
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement announcing that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has arrested more than 100 gang members in less than a week, including members of gangs such as MS-13 and Tren de Aragua (TDA).
自10月4日至10月10日，ICE逮捕了131名有帮派关联的人员，其中包括ICE执法与遣返行动局（Enforcement and Removal Operations，简称ERO）逮捕的129人，以及ICE国土安全调查局（Homeland Security Investigations，简称HSI）逮捕的2人。在被捕人员中，有22人为MS-13成员，13人为TDA成员。
From October 4 to October 10, ICE arrested 131 gang-affiliated individuals, including 129 arrests by ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO) and two arrests by ICE Homeland Security Investigations (HSI). Among those arrested were 22 affiliates of MS-13 and 13 affiliates of TDA.
自唐纳德·J·特朗普总统于2025年1月20日重新上任以来，ICE已进行了12,018起与帮派相关的行政逮捕。其中包括1,965名MS-13成员和1,553名TDA成员。
Since President Donald J. Trump returned to office on January 20, 2025, ICE has made 12,018 gang-related administrative arrests. This includes 1,965 MS-13 affiliates and 1,553 TDA affiliates.
“在不到一周的时间里，ICE的英勇爱国者们在全国各地逮捕了100多名帮派成员，其中包括MS-13和阿拉瓜火车帮的成员，”国土安全部部长马克韦恩·穆林（Markwayne Mullin）表示。“美国最优秀的人才每天都奋战在第一线，确保人性中最恶劣的一面无法威胁或伤害无辜的美国人。在特朗普总统的领导下，我们正在打击帮派和贩毒集团，使他们无法在美国从事活动，并将其成员驱逐出我们的国家。逮捕并驱逐非法外国人，尤其是帮派成员，是我们让美国再次安全的方式。”
“In less than a week, the brave patriots of ICE have arrested more than 100 gang members across the country, including members of MS-13 and Tren de Aragua,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “The best that America has to offer are on the frontlines every day to ensure that the worst of humanity cannot threaten or harm innocent Americans. Under President Trump’s leadership, we are smashing the gangs and cartels, making it impossible for them to do business in America, and removing their members from our country. Arresting and deporting illegal aliens, especially gang members, is how we make America safe again.”

Among the gang members arrested in recent days are:
古斯塔沃·阿道夫·弗洛雷斯-拉米雷斯（Gustavo Adolfo Flores-Ramirez），来自萨尔瓦多的非法外国人，MS-13成员，于10月14日在拉斯维加斯被ICE ERO逮捕。他在本国因加重谋杀罪、加重杀害女性罪、强奸未成年人或丧失行为能力者罪、非法或不负责任地操作、携带或持有枪支罪、非法结伙罪以及涉及公共治安的罪行而被通缉。弗洛雷斯-拉米雷斯最初于未知日期和地点非法进入美国，于2021年9月被美国边境巡逻队逮捕并被驱逐至墨西哥。此后，他在未知日期和地点再次非法入境，此行为构成重罪。
Gustavo Adolfo Flores-Ramirez, an illegal alien from El Salvador and MS-13 member, arrested by ICE ERO in Las Vegas on October 14. He is wanted in his home country for charges of aggravated homicide, aggravated femicide, rape of a minor or incapacitated person, illegal or irresponsible handling, carrying, or possession of firearms, illicit grouping, and crimes involving public peace. Flores-Ramirez first illegally entered the United States at an unknown date and location, and was arrested by the U.S. Border Patrol in September 2021 and deported to Mexico. He then illegally re-entered the country – a felony – at an unknown date and location.
何塞·路易斯·佩雷斯-阿里亚斯（Jose Luis Perez-Arrias），来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人，阿拉瓜火车帮成员，于10月14日在佛罗里达州塔拉哈西被ICE迈阿密办公室和ERO塔拉哈西办公室逮捕。他被怀疑是TDA的职业杀手。佩雷斯-阿里亚斯于2023年10月经德克萨斯州非法进入美国，并被美国边境巡逻队逮捕。随后，他被拜登政府释放进入美国境内。
Jose Luis Perez-Arrias, an illegal alien from Venezuela and Tren de Aragua member, arrested by ICE Miami and ERO Tallahassee in Tallahassee, Florida on October 14. He is a suspected hitman for TDA. Perez-Arrias illegally entered the United States through Texas in October 2023 and was arrested by the U.S. Border Patrol. He was then RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration.
埃默森·约西埃尔·卡尔德隆-埃尔南德斯（Emerson Jossiel Calderon-Hernandez），来自萨尔瓦多的非法外国人，MS-13成员，于10月13日在华盛顿特区被ICE逮捕。他的犯罪记录包括一项二级袭击罪定罪，以及因一级谋杀未遂、二级谋杀未遂、持械抢劫、一级袭击、鲁莽危害行为和非法拘禁而被逮捕的记录。卡尔德隆-埃尔南德斯于2012年8月经德克萨斯州非法进入美国，并被美国边境巡逻队逮捕。随后，他被奥巴马政府释放进入美国境内。
Emerson Jossiel Calderon-Hernandez, an illegal alien from El Salvador and MS-13 member, arrested by ICE Washington D.C. on October 13. His criminal history includes a conviction for second-degree assault and arrests for attempted first-degree murder, attempted second-degree murder, armed robbery, first-degree assault, reckless endangerment, and false imprisonment. Calderon-Hernandez illegally entered the United States through Texas in August 2012 and was arrested by the U.S. Border Patrol. He was then RELEASED into the country by the Obama Administration.
10月13日，HSI丹佛办公室和ERO丹佛办公室在科罗拉多州丹佛市逮捕了一名14岁的委内瑞拉籍非法外国人，该少年与阿拉瓜火车帮的一个派系“海盗帮”（Los Piratas Gang）有关联。被捕时，他非法持有一把格洛克48手枪。该嫌疑人目前面临的指控包括：一级谋杀罪，极端漠视态度下的犯罪未遂、极端漠视态度下的一级袭击罪、一级绑架罪、加重犯罪勒索罪、非法开枪罪以及共谋罪。
On October 13, HSI Denver and ERO Denver arrested a 14-year-old illegal alien from Venezuela who is associated with Los Piratas Gang, a faction of Tren de Aragua, in Denver, Colorado. At the time of his arrest, he unlawfully had a Glock 48 pistol in his possession. The suspect is currently facing charges of first-degree murder – criminal attempt with extreme indifference, first-degree assault with extreme indifference, first-degree kidnapping, aggravated criminal extortion, illegal discharge of a weapon, and conspiracy.
