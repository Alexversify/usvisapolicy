美国国土安全部将43家企业列入《防止强迫维吾尔人劳动法》实体清单

发布日期 2026年8月3日

美国国土安全部(DHS)作为强迫劳动执法工作组(FLETF)主席,发布了更新后的《防止强迫维吾尔人劳动法》(UFLPA)实体清单,新增43家企业,并对两家已列入企业的信息作出技术性更正。清单所列企业生产的商品将被推定为强迫劳动制品,禁止进口美国。

要点

  • 本通知涉及贸易和海关执法事项,与签证或移民身份无关。
  • 4家企业因涉嫌配合新疆政府进行强迫劳动转移而被列入清单。
  • 41家企业因涉嫌通过政府劳务项目从新疆获取原材料而被列入清单。
  • 两家原有上榜企业的信息得到技术性更正。
  • 自2022年6月21日起实施的可反驳推定原则规定,清单所列企业生产的商品被视为强迫劳动制品,依据19 U.S.C. 1307禁止进口美国。
  • 上榜企业可向FLETF主席提交支持材料,申请从清单中移除。

影响对象

与新疆维吾尔自治区供应链有关联的企业,以及进口其产品的美国进口商受到影响。

建议行动

进口商应对照最新清单审查自身供应链,并可考虑咨询律师以评估进口合规事宜。

背景
Background
A. 强迫劳动执法工作组
A. The Forced Labor Enforcement Task Force
《美墨加协定实施法》第741条设立了强迫劳动执法工作组(FLETF),负责监督美国对经修订的1930年《关税法》第307条(19 U.S.C. 1307)所规定禁令的执行情况。参见19 U.S.C. 4681。根据美国国土安全部(DHS)第23034号授权令,国土安全部负责战略、政策与规划事务的副部长担任强迫劳动执法工作组主席。该工作组是一个跨部门工作组,成员机构包括国土安全部、美国贸易代表办公室,以及劳工部、国务院、司法部、财政部和商务部。1 参见19 U.S.C. 4681;第13923号行政命令(2020年5月15日)。此外,强迫劳动执法工作组还包括五个观察员机构:能源部、农业部、国家安全委员会、美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)以及美国移民与海关执法局(ICE)国土安全调查处。
Section 741 of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act established the FLETF to monitor United States enforcement of the prohibition under section 307 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1307). See19 U.S.C. 4681. Pursuant to DHS Delegation Order No. 23034, the DHS Under Secretary for Strategy, Policy, & Plans serves as Chair of the FLETF, an interagency task force that includes the Department of Homeland Security, the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative, and the Departments of Labor, State, Justice, the Treasury, and Commerce as member agencies. 1 See19 U.S.C. 4681; Executive Order 13923 (May 15, 2020). In addition, the FLETF includes five observer agencies: the Departments of Energy and Agriculture, the National Security Council, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement Homeland Security Investigations.
B. 《维吾尔强迫劳动预防法》:防止中华人民共和国使用强迫劳动生产的商品进入美国
B. The Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act: Preventing Goods Made With Forced Labor in the People's Republic of China From Being Imported Into the United States
《维吾尔强迫劳动预防法》(UFLPA)要求,除其他事项外,强迫劳动执法工作组应在与商务部长及国家情报总监协商后,制定一项战略(UFLPA第2(c)条),以支持执行1930年《关税法》第307条,防止全部或部分使用中华人民共和国强迫劳动开采、生产或制造的货物、制品、物品和商品进口至美国。根据UFLPA的要求,《防止进口在中华人民共和国以强迫劳动开采、生产或制造的货物战略》已于2022年6月17日在国土安全部网站上发布(参见https://www.dhs.gov/uflpa-strategy),其中包括初始的UFLPA实体清单,这是根据UFLPA要求制定并维护的四份清单的合并登记册。参见UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条。
The UFLPA requires, among other things, that the FLETF, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce and the Director of National Intelligence, develop a strategy (UFLPA section 2(c)) for supporting enforcement of section 307 of the Tariff Act of 1930, to prevent the importation into the United States of goods, wares, articles, and merchandise mined, produced, or manufactured wholly or in part with forced labor in the People's Republic of China. As required by the UFLPA, the Strategy to Prevent the Importation of Goods Mined, Produced, or Manufactured with Forced Labor in the People's Republic of China, which was published on the DHS website on June 17, 2022 ( see https://www.dhs.gov/​uflpa-strategy), includes the initial UFLPA Entity List, a consolidated register of the four lists required to be developed and maintained pursuant to the UFLPA. See UFLPA section 2(d)(2)(B).
C. UFLPA实体清单
C. UFLPA Entity List
UFLPA实体清单针对UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条第(i)、(ii)、(iv)和(v)款中规定的不同要求,要求强迫劳动执法工作组识别并公布以下四份清单:
The UFLPA Entity List addresses distinct requirements set forth in clauses (i), (ii), (iv), and (v) of section 2(d)(2)(B) of the UFLPA that the FLETF identify and publish the following four lists:
(1) 新疆维吾尔自治区内全部或部分使用强迫劳动开采、生产或制造任何货物、制品、物品和商品的实体清单;
(1) a list of entities in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region that mine, produce, or manufacture wholly or in part any goods, wares, articles, and merchandise with forced labor;
(2) 与新疆维吾尔自治区政府合作,招募、运输、转移、窝藏或接收强迫劳动人员或维吾尔族、哈萨克族、柯尔克孜族或其他受迫害群体成员,将其带离新疆维吾尔自治区的实体清单;
(2) a list of entities working with the government of the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region to recruit, transport, transfer, harbor or receive forced labor or Uyghurs, Kazakhs, Kyrgyz, or members of other persecuted groups out of the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region;
(3) 将清单1和清单2中实体所生产的产品从中华人民共和国出口至美国的实体清单;以及
(3) a list of entities that exported products made by entities in lists 1 and 2 from the People's Republic of China into the United States; and
(4) 为“扶贫”计划、“对口支援”计划或任何其他使用强迫劳动的政府劳动安排目的,从新疆维吾尔自治区或与新疆政府或新疆生产建设兵团合作的人员处采购材料的设施和实体清单,包括新疆生产建设兵团本身。
(4) a list of facilities and entities, including the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, that source material from the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region or from persons working with the government of Xinjiang or the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps for purposes of the “poverty alleviation” program or the “pairing-assistance” program or any other government-labor scheme that uses forced labor.
UFLPA实体清单是上述四份清单的合并登记册。根据UFLPA第3(e)条,自2022年6月21日起生效,UFLPA实体清单上的实体(以下简称“被列名实体”)适用UFLPA规定的可反驳推定,即其全部或部分生产的产品被推定为使用强迫劳动制造,并依据19 U.S.C. 1307禁止进入美国。UFLPA实体清单作为本通知的附录1提供。UFLPA实体清单不应被解释为从事UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条第(i)、(ii)、(iv)或(v)款所述行为的实体的详尽清单。
The UFLPA Entity List is a consolidated register of the above four lists. In accordance with section 3(e) of the UFLPA, effective June 21, 2022, entities on the UFLPA Entity List (listed entities) are subject to the UFLPA's rebuttable presumption that products they produce, wholly or in part, are made with forced labor and prohibited from entry into the United States under 19 U.S.C. 1307. The UFLPA Entity List is provided as appendix 1 to this notice. The UFLPA Entity List should not be interpreted as an exhaustive list of entities engaged in the practices described in clauses (i), (ii), (iv), or (v) of section 2(d)(2)(B) of the UFLPA.
《维吾尔强迫劳动预防法》（UFLPA）实体清单的修订，包括所有新增、删除和技术性更正，将公布在国土安全部（DHS）UFLPA网站（https://www.dhs.gov/​uflpa-entity-list）上，并作为将在《联邦公报》上发布的通知的附录予以公布。见附录1。打击强迫劳动执法工作组（FLETF）将依据UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条第(i)、(ii)、(iv)或(v)款所述标准，考虑今后对UFLPA实体清单的新增或删除（第48915页）。任何FLETF成员机构均可提交建议，对UFLPA实体清单条目进行新增、删除或技术性更正。FLETF成员机构将据此对UFLPA实体清单的修订进行审议并投票表决。
Revisions to the UFLPA Entity List, including all additions, removals, and technical corrections, will be published on the DHS UFLPA website ( https://www.dhs.gov/​uflpa-entity-list) and as an appendix to a notice that will be published in the Federal Register . See appendix 1. The FLETF will consider ( printed page 48915) future additions to, or removals from, the UFLPA Entity List based on criteria described in clauses (i), (ii), (iv), or (v) of Section 2(d)(2)(B) of the UFLPA. Any FLETF member agency may submit a recommendation(s) to add, remove or make technical corrections to an entry on the UFLPA Entity List. FLETF member agencies will review and vote on revisions to the UFLPA Entity List accordingly.
实体清单的新增
Additions to the Entity List
FLETF将依据UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条第(i)、(ii)、(iv)或(v)款所述标准，考虑今后对UFLPA实体清单的新增。任何FLETF成员机构均可向FLETF主席提交建议，将某实体列入UFLPA实体清单。经FLETF成员机构审议该建议后，是否将某实体列入UFLPA实体清单的决定将由FLETF成员机构以多数票作出。

从实体清单中删除的请求
Requests for Removal From the Entity List
任何被列入清单的实体均可向FLETF主席提交删除请求（removal request）及相关佐证信息，提交地址为FLETF.UFLPA.EntityList@hq.dhs.gov。在删除请求中，该实体（或其指定代表）应提供能够证明该实体不再符合或不符合UFLPA第2(d)(2)(B)条相应款项（第(i)、(ii)、(iv)或(v)款）所述标准的信息。FLETF主席将把所有此类删除请求及佐证信息转交FLETF成员机构。收到删除请求后，FLETF主席或主席指定的代表可代表FLETF就删除请求向该实体提出问题，并可要求提供补充信息。经FLETF成员机构审议删除请求后，是否将某实体从UFLPA实体清单中删除的决定将由FLETF成员机构以多数票作出。
Any listed entity may submit a request for removal (removal request) from the UFLPA Entity List along with supporting information to the FLETF Chair at FLETF.UFLPA.EntityList@hq.dhs.gov. In the removal request, the entity (or its designated representative) should provide information that demonstrates that the entity no longer meets or does not meet the criteria described in the applicable clause ((i), (ii), (iv), or (v)) of section 2(d)(2)(B) of the UFLPA. The FLETF Chair will refer all such removal requests and supporting information to FLETF member agencies. Upon receipt of the removal request, the FLETF Chair or the Chair's designated representative may contact the entity on behalf of the FLETF with questions on the removal request and may request additional information. Following review of the removal request by the FLETF member agencies, the decision to remove an entity from the UFLPA Entity List will be made by majority vote of the FLETF member agencies.
被列入清单的实体在向FLETF主席（地址：FLETF.UFLPA.EntityList@hq.dhs.gov）书面提交删除请求后，可要求与FLETF举行会议。FLETF在完成对删除请求的审议后，可在审议期结束时接受会议请求，若接受，将在就该实体的删除请求进行表决之前举行会议。FLETF主席将以书面形式告知该实体FLETF就其删除请求所作的决定。尽管FLETF就删除请求作出的决定不可上诉，但如附有新信息，FLETF将考虑受理新的删除请求。
Listed entities may request a meeting with the FLETF after submitting a removal request in writing to the FLETF Chair at FLETF.UFLPA.EntityList@hq.dhs.gov. Following its review of a removal request, the FLETF may accept the meeting request at the conclusion of the review period and, if accepted, will hold the meeting prior to voting on the entity's removal request. The FLETF Chair will advise the entity in writing of the FLETF's decision on its removal request. While the FLETF's decision on a removal request is not appealable, the FLETF will consider new removal requests if accompanied by new information.
