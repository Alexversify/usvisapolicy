国土安全部就两名非法移民刺伤休班纽约警局侦探发表声明，嫌犯在逃

发布日期 2026年10月6日

美国国土安全部（DHS）就10月2日发生在布�朗克斯的一起袭击事件发表声明，两名非法移民涉嫌在抢劫未遂过程中刺伤一名休班的纽约警察局（NYPD）侦探。移民与海关执法局（ICE）确认两名嫌疑人均为多米尼加共和国籍非法移民，此前均曾被当地当局释放。

要点

  • 该名侦探被刺七刀，目前据报正在医院接受治疗并逐渐康复。
  • 两名嫌疑人丹杰洛·拉斐尔·金·卡拉瓦约和斯奈德·梅希亚·包蒂斯塔分别于2023年和2022年经亚利桑那州非法入境，并在拜登政府时期被释放。
  • 两人此前在纽约均有多次被捕记录，但在ICE发出拘留请求前均被当地执法部门释放。
  • 国土安全部长马克韦恩·穆林批评纽约的“庇护”政策，并呼吁当局与ICE展开合作。
  • 包蒂斯塔已于2025年5月8日被移民法官下达最终驱逐令。
  • 截至声明发布时，两名嫌疑人仍在逃。

影响对象

该声明涉及ICE与纽约地方当局之间的执法合作问题，不直接影响签证或移民申请程序。

DHS Issues Statement After Two Illegal Aliens Stab Off-Duty NYPD Detective; Suspects Remain At-Large
发布日期:2026年10月6日
Release Date: October 6, 2026

Both of the suspects were RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration and had prior arrests in New York, only to be released by sanctuary politicians
华盛顿,美国国土安全部(DHS)在两名非法外国人被控持刀刺伤一名休班纽约警察局(NYPD)警探后发表以下声明,目前两名嫌疑人仍在逃。
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement after two illegal aliens were accused of stabbing an off-duty New York Police Department (NYPD) detective, with both suspects remaining at-large.
据当地媒体报道,该事件发生在10月2日的布朗克斯区。两名嫌疑人接近该警探并试图抢劫他,当他自卫时将其刺伤七刀。该警探被送往当地医院,据报道正在康复中。嫌疑人骑电动自行车逃离现场。
According to local reporting, the incident occurred on October 2 in the Bronx. The two suspects approached the detective and attempted to rob him, stabbing him seven times when he defended himself. The detective was taken to a local hospital, where he is reportedly recovering. The suspects fled the scene on an e-bike.
美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)执法与遣返行动局(ERO)纽约市分局已查明两名嫌疑人身份:丹杰洛·拉斐尔·让·卡拉巴洛(Dangelo Rafael Jean Caraballo)和斯奈德·梅希亚·鲍蒂斯塔(Sneider Mejia Bautista),两人均为来自多米尼加共和国的非法外国人。
U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement’s (ICE) Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO New York City has since identified both suspects: Dangelo Rafael Jean Caraballo and Sneider Mejia Bautista, both illegal aliens from the Dominican Republic.

卡拉巴洛的犯罪记录包括此前因鲁莽危害他人安全、危险驾驶、驾车非法逃避警察、持有撬盗工具、刑事持有管制物质、刑事持有涉及装填武器的枪支以及涉及武器的二级恐吓而遭逮捕。在他于2024年12月因持有撬盗工具被捕后,当地执法部门在ICE能够提出拘留请求之前将其释放。
Caraballo’s criminal history includes prior arrests for reckless endangerment, reckless driving, unlawful fleeing from a police officer in a motor vehicle, possession of burglar’s tools, criminal possession of a controlled substance, criminal possession of a weapon involving a loaded firearm, and second-degree menacing involving a weapon. Following his arrest in December 2024 for possession of burglar’s tools, he was released by local law enforcement before ICE could lodge a detainer.
鲍蒂斯塔的犯罪记录包括此前因刑事持有赃物、持有撬盗工具、重大盗窃以及刑事持有武器而遭逮捕。在他于2025年10月因刑事持有武器被捕后,ICE提出了拘留请求,但遭到纽约警察局(NYPD)拒绝。他其余的所有逮捕都在ICE能够提出拘留请求之前被当地当局释放。
Bautista’s criminal history includes prior arrests for criminal possession of stolen property, possession of burglar’s tools, grand larceny, and criminal possession of a weapon. Following his arrest in October 2025 for criminal possession of a weapon, ICE lodged a detainer which was then declined by the NYPD. All of his other arrests led to him being released by local authorities before ICE could lodge detainers.
国土安全部部长马克韦恩·穆林(Markwayne Mullin)表示:“这两名来自多米尼加共和国的非法外国人是刺伤并企图抢劫一名休班纽约警察局警探案件的嫌疑人。这两名嫌疑人都是被拜登政府放入境内的,尽管他们在纽约曾多次被捕,但庇护城市政客拒绝与ICE合作,将他们释放。如果不是拜登的开放边境政策和纽约庇护城市政客的‘美国优先’倒退政策,这起可怕的罪行本可以避免。这些嫌疑人仍然在逃。国土安全部呼吁纽约当局与我们合作,以便我们能将这些危险罪犯逐出街头。我们的心与祈祷与这名警探同在,祝他早日康复。”
“These illegal aliens from the Dominican Republic are suspects in the stabbing and attempted robbery of an off-duty NYPD detective,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Both of these suspects were RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration, and despite multiple prior arrests in New York, sanctuary politicians refused to cooperate with ICE and released them. This horrific crime could have been prevented if it weren’t for Biden’s open borders and the America Last policies of New York sanctuary politicians. These suspects remain at-large. DHS is calling on New York authorities to cooperate with us so we can get these dangerous criminals off our streets. Our thoughts and prayers are with the detective for a speedy recovery.”
卡拉巴洛于2023年2月通过亚利桑那州非法进入美国,被美国边境巡逻队逮捕。随后他被拜登政府放入境内。鲍蒂斯塔于2022年12月通过亚利桑那州非法进入美国,被美国边境巡逻队查获。随后他被拜登政府放入境内。鲍蒂斯塔于2025年5月8日被移民法官下达最终驱逐令。
Caraballo illegally entered the United States through Arizona in February 2023 and was arrested by the U.S. Border Patrol. He was then RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration. Bautista illegally entered the United States through Arizona in December 2022 and was encountered by U.S. Border Patrol. He was then RELEASED into the country by the Biden Administration. Bautista was issued a final order of removal by an Immigration Judge on May 8, 2025.
