美国国土安全部发布临时最终规则,落实H.R.1法案要求的移民费用

发布日期 2026年4月29日生效日期 2026年5月29日

美国国土安全部(DHS)发布一项临时最终规则,将H.R.1法案(One Big Beautiful Bill Act)要求的移民费用及相关规定纳入正式法规。该规则自2026年5月29日起生效,涉及庇护申请费、新设的年度庇护费、与I-94表格相关的费用,以及临时保护身份(TPS)工作许可有效期的新限制。该规则作为临时最终规则已经生效,但DHS将在2026年6月29日前接受公众意见。

要点

  • 庇护申请人须在提交I-589表格时缴纳申请费,该费用不可退还,即使申请被拒绝,USCIS也将予以保留。
  • 案件仍在审理中的庇护申请人,每年须缴纳一次年度庇护费。
  • 若在收到通知后30天内未缴纳年度庇护费,待审的庇护申请将被拒绝,相关的工作许可申请也会被拒。
  • H.R.1要求的费用适用于I-102表格(非移民入境/离境记录补发或初次申请表),用以落实USCIS的I-94表格费用。
  • 基于临时保护身份(TPS)签发的工作许可文件,有效期将被限制为一年,或TPS指定剩余期限(以较短者为准)。
  • 该规则自2026年5月29日起生效,但DHS将就规则各方面接受公众意见,截止日期为2026年6月29日。

影响对象

影响庇护申请人、临时保护身份(TPS)持有者以及I-102表格申请人。

建议行动

受影响的申请人及其律师应在提交申请前了解新的费用和有效期要求,并可在2026年6月29日前通过regulations.gov向DHS提交意见。

II. 行政摘要
II. Executive Summary
A. 监管行动的目的
A. Purpose of the Regulatory Action
2025年7月4日,总统签署了H.R.1,《一个美丽大法案》,公法119-21号,139 Stat. 72(“H.R.1”),使其成为法律。H.R.1是一项全面的立法方案,修改了许多法律并增加了涉及美国政府诸多领域的新法律。在这些修改中,该法律为《移民与国籍法》(INA)设立了若干新规定和费用。参见H.R.1第十章,A分章,第一部分,第100001至1000018条。本临时最终规则将H.R.1中若干移民费用规定及对外国人的其他限制纳入法规。
On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law H.R.1—One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (“H.R.1”). H.R.1 was a comprehensive legislative package that changed many laws and added new laws that touch many areas of the United States government. Among those changes, the law established several new provisions and fees to the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA). See H.R.1, Title X, Subtitle A, Part I, Sections 100001 through 1000018. This IFR codifies several of the H.R.1 immigration fee provisions and other limitations on aliens.
具体而言,该临时最终规则(IFR)执行以下事项:(1)将《美国法典》第8编第1807条中规定的适用于美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)的I-94表格费用要求编入《联邦法规汇编》(CFR);(2)将《美国法典》第8编第1808条规定的年度庇护费(AAF)要求编入法规,包括未缴纳年度庇护费的后果以及《美国法典》第8编第1810条(b)款所要求的与工作许可相关的限制;(3)将《美国法典》第8编第1802条要求的每份庇护申请在提交时均须缴纳规定费用的要求编入法规,无论该申请是否被驳回,且该费用不予退还;以及(4)将《美国法典》第8编第1803条(c)款和第1811条(a)款所要求的H.R.1对临时保护身份(TPS)工作许可有效期的限制编入法规。
Specifically, the IFR does the following: (1) codification in the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) of the Form I-94 fee requirement set forth in 8 U.S.C. 1807 as it applies to USCIS; (2) codification of the Annual Asylum Fee (AAF) requirement in 8 U.S.C. 1808, including consequences for failure to pay the AAF and limitations related to employment authorization required by 8 U.S.C. 1810(b); (3) codification of the requirement that every asylum application include the fee required by 8 U.S.C. 1802 at filing regardless of whether the application is rejected, and is not refundable; and (4) codification of the H.R.1 limits on the validity of Temporary Protected Status (TPS) employment authorization required by 8 U.S.C. 1803(c) and 8 U.S.C. 1811(a).
B. 法律授权
B. Legal Authority
本规则依据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第208(d)(3)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1158条(d)款;《2002年国土安全法》(HSA)第102条,即《美国法典》第6编第112条;以及H.R.1第100002条至第100018条,编入《美国法典》第8编第1802条至第1815条而制定。上述法律授权国土安全部(DHS)管理庇护程序,并依法征收特定费用。
This rule is issued under section 208(d)(3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), 8 U.S.C. 1158(d); section 102 of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (HSA), 6 U.S.C. 112; and sections 100002 through 100018 of H.R.1, codified at 8 U.S.C. 1802 through 1815. These statutes authorize DHS to administer the asylum process, and collect certain fees as required by law.
国土安全部依据《美国法典》第5编第553条(b)款(B)项规定的“正当理由”例外情形发布本规则作为临时最终规则,因为事先征求公众意见将不切实际且有悖于公共利益。H.R.1要求立即实施,以确保遵守法定授权,且在本规则所实施的各项规定方面,国土安全部并无自由裁量权。
DHS is issuing this rule as an interim final rule under the “good cause” exception of 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B), as prior notice and comment would be impracticable and contrary to the public interest. H.R.1 requires immediate implementation to ensure compliance with the statutory mandate and provides no discretion to DHS on the provisions implemented in this rule.

C. Summary of the Regulatory Action
本规则将H.R.1中适用于美国公民及移民服务局的若干费用规定编入法规:
This rule codifies certain H.R.1 fee provisions applicable to USCIS:

III. 背景与授权
III. Background and Authority
A. H.R.1,《一项伟大的大法案》
A. H.R.1—One Big Beautiful Bill Act
《2025年H.R.1协调法案》(H.R.1),《公法》119-21号,建立了一套新的移民费用框架,国会指示美国国土安全部(DHS)自2025财年起开始实施该框架。[1] 国会在制定H.R.1时旨在确保申请或维持移民福利资格的外国人承担更多管理移民系统的成本。[2] 国会在解释其决定时明确表示,这些新费用早该征收,且对于弥补在美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS,DHS的下属机构)和美国司法部移民审查执行办公室(EOIR)两处积压的数百万份待审庇护申请所产生的日益增长的裁决成本而言是必要的。[3] H.R.1要求这些费用自2025财年起开始征收,且“在法律授权的任何其他费用之外”另行征收。[4](原文第22954页)除本规则针对特定费用另有说明外,H.R.1规定的费用是在8 CFR第106部分规定的费用,或DHS根据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第286(m)条,即8 U.S.C. 1356(m)制定的任何其他费用之外另行征收的,且不可退还。参见89 FR 6194(2024年1月31日);90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。2025年7月22日,USCIS发布了一份《联邦公报》通知(FRN),宣布实施由USCIS管理的、H.R.1规定的若干项费用(H.R.1费用通知)。90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。[5] 该通知实施了根据8 U.S.C. 1802自2025财年起征收的最低100美元庇护申请受理费,以及根据8 U.S.C. 1808自2025财年起,针对庇护申请每一个待决历年征收的最低100美元年度庇护费(AAF)。90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。该通知还宣布了临时保护身份(TPS)、根据8 U.S.C. 1805规定的特殊移民青少年(SIJ),以及根据8 U.S.C. 1803(a)至(c)规定的某些类别就业授权的相关费用。90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。
The H.R.1 Reconciliation Act of 2025 (H.R.1), Public Law 119-21, established a new framework of immigration fees that Congress directed DHS to implement beginning FY 2025.[1] Congress intended H.R.1 to ensure that aliens who apply for or maintain eligibility for immigration benefits bear more of the costs of administering the immigration system.[2] In explaining its decision, Congress made clear that these new fees were long overdue and necessary to recover the growing costs of adjudicating the millions of pending asylum applications before both USCIS, a component agency of DHS, and the Department of Justice, Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR).[3] H.R.1 requires that these fees be applied commencing in FY 2025 “in addition to any other fee authorized by law.” [4] ( printed page 22954) Unless otherwise described in this rule with respect to a specific fee, the fees set forth in H.R.1 are imposed in addition to fees in 8 CFR part 106, or any other fee promulgated by DHS under INA sec. 286(m), 8 U.S.C. 1356(m), and are not refundable. See89 FR 6194 (Jan. 31, 2024); 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). On July 22, 2025, USCIS published a Federal Register notice (FRN) announcing the implementation of several fees administered by USCIS mandated by H.R.1 (H.R.1 Fee notice). 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).[5] That notice implemented a minimum $100 asylum application filing fee commencing in FY 2025 under 8 U.S.C. 1802 and a minimum $100 annual asylum fee (AAF) starting in FY 2025 for each calendar year an asylum application remains pending under 8 U.S.C. 1808. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). The notice also announced fees for Temporary Protected Status (TPS), special immigrant juveniles (SIJs) under 8 U.S.C. 1805, and certain categories of employment authorization under 8 U.S.C. 1803(a)-(c). 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).
USCIS的通知还规定,USCIS将向每一位在USCIS处有待决申请、且需缴纳年度庇护费(AAF)的庇护申请人发出个人化的单独通知,该通知将载明费用金额、缴费的时间和方式,以及未缴费的后果。[6] 对于2025财年应缴纳的年度庇护费,DHS已发出的年度庇护费通知仅说明在截止日期前未缴费可能对申请产生不利影响,但未明确说明若未缴费将发生何种具体后果。[7]
The USCIS notice also provides that USCIS will issue personal, individualized notice to each asylum applicant with an application pending with USCIS from whom the AAF is required, and that the notice will include the amount of the fee, when and how the fee must be paid, and the consequences of failure to pay.[6] For the AAF due for FY 2025, DHS has issued AAF notices that only state that failure to pay the fee before the deadline may negatively affect the application, but do not specify what will occur if the fee is not paid.[7]
H.R.1费用通知明确推迟公布了多项法定费用。其中包括:(1)8 U.S.C. 1804所规定的移民假释费(IPF),该条款包含多项列明的法定例外情形;以及(2)8 U.S.C. 1807所规定的、适用于任何提交I-94表格《入境/出境记录》申请的外国人的I-94表格费用。90 FR 34511,第34516页(2025年7月22日)。在这两种情况下,USCIS均说明在实施之前有必要作出进一步的解释和指导。90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。2025年10月16日,DHS发布了另一份《联邦公报》通知,以处理8 U.S.C. 1804所规定的移民假释费(IPF)问题。90 FR 48317(2025年10月16日)。该IPF通知宣布了由包括USCIS在内的DHS下属机构负责管理的新费用,并明确了适用该费用的申请人类别、新规定的生效日期以及缴费的相关说明。90 FR 48317(2025年10月16日)。该通知还依照H.R.1所规定的法定例外情形,说明了费用例外可能适用的情形,并阐明了未缴费的后果。90 FR 48317(2025年10月16日)。该IPF通知履行了H.R.1费用通知中作出的承诺,即该通知曾表示,8 U.S.C. 1804所规定的、因涉及多项需经机构解释的法定例外情形而需另行处理的费用,将在未来的出版物中予以公布。90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。通过提供这一后续指导,DHS确保公众获得了必要的信息,以全面遵守这一新的法定要求。
The H.R.1 Fee notice expressly deferred announcement of multiple statutory fees. Among them were: (1) the Immigration Parole Fee (IPF) required by 8 U.S.C. 1804, which includes multiple enumerated statutory exceptions, and (2) the Form I-94 fee required by 8 U.S.C. 1807 applicable to any alien who submits an application for a Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record. 90 FR 34511 at 34516 (July 22, 2025). In both cases, USCIS explained that further interpretation and guidance were necessary before implementation could proceed. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). On October 16, 2025, DHS published an additional FRN to address the Immigration Parole Fee (IPF) required by 8 U.S.C. 1804. 90 FR 48317 (Oct. 16, 2025). The IPF notice announced the new fee to be administered by DHS components, including USCIS, and specified the classes of applicants to whom the fee applies, the effective date of the new requirement, and instructions for remitting payment. 90 FR 48317 (Oct. 16, 2025). It also described the circumstances under which the fee exceptions may apply in accordance with the statutory exceptions provided in H.R.1 and clarified the consequences for failure to pay. 90 FR 48317 (Oct. 16, 2025). The IPF notice fulfilled the commitment made in the H.R.1 Fee notice, which stated that the fee mandated by 8 U.S.C. 1804, subject to multiple statutory exceptions requiring agency interpretation, would be announced in a future publication. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). By providing this follow-up guidance, DHS ensured that members of the public received the necessary information to comply fully with the new statutory mandate.
H.R.1法案要求国土安全部(DHS)自2026财政年度起及其后每个财政年度,根据通货膨胀对移民相关费用进行调整。H.R.1法案规定,国土安全部应使用当年7月城镇消费者消费价格指数(CPI-U)相对于前一个日历年同期的百分比变化,并按照H.R.1法案授权的方式,将每项费用向下舍入至最接近的10美元整数倍或最接近的1美元。为推进H.R.1法案文本的实施,国土安全部各部门发布了多份联邦公报通知,宣布新的H.R.1通货膨胀调整费用(90 FR 52085(2025年11月19日)、90 FR 52425(2025年11月20日)和90 FR 52693(2025年11月21日))。
H.R.1 requires that DHS, beginning in FY 2026 and continuing for each subsequent fiscal year, adjust the immigration-related fees for inflation. H.R.1 prescribes that DHS use the percentage change to the CPI-U for the month of July in the current year compared to the preceding calendar year, and round each fee to the next lowest multiple of $10 or down to the nearest dollar as authorized by H.R.1. In furtherance of enacting the text of H.R.1, DHS components published multiple FRNs announcing the new H.R inflationary increases (90 FR 52085 (Nov. 19, 2025), 90 FR 52425 (Nov. 20, 2025), and 90 FR 52693 (Nov. 21, 2025)).
B. 国土安全部一般规则制定权限
B. DHS General Rulemaking Authority
国土安全部部长就本次法规修订所依据的权限,见于《移民与国籍法》(INA)第8编美国法典第1101节及以下条款,以及《2002年国土安全法》(HSA)第6编美国法典第101节及以下条款的多个条款。颁布本临时最终规则(IFR)的一般权限见于INA第103(a)节,即8 U.S.C. 1103(a),该条款授权部长管理和执行移民与国籍法律,并制定部长认为执行该权限所必需的法规,[8] 以及HSA第102节,即6 U.S.C. 112,该条款将国土安全部的所有职能赋予部长,并授权部长颁布法规。[9] 除上述一般权限外,INA第208(d)(5)(B)节即8 U.S.C. 1158(d)(5)(B)规定的庇护专项权限指出:“司法部长可通过法规规定与本章不相抵触的、对庇护申请审议的任何其他条件或限制。” [10]
The Secretary of Homeland Security's authority for the regulatory amendment is found in various sections of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq. and the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (HSA), 6 U.S.C. 101 et seq. General authority for issuing this IFR is found in section 103(a) of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1103(a), which authorizes the Secretary to administer and enforce the immigration and nationality laws and establish such regulations as the Secretary deems necessary for carrying out such authority,[8] as well as sections 102 of the HSA, 6 U.S.C. 112, which vests all of the functions of DHS in the Secretary and authorizes the Secretary to issue regulations.[9] In addition to the general authority, the asylum-specific authority at INA sec. 208(d)(5)(B), 8 U.S.C. 1158(d)(5)(B), states that “the Attorney General may provide by regulation for any other conditions or limitations on the consideration of an application for asylum not inconsistent with this chapter.” [10]
H.R.1法案文本中“在法律授权的任何其他费用之外”这一表述是明确的。[11] H.R.1法案规定的费用并不取代或替代国土安全部在8 CFR第106部分及相关条款中颁布的收费表,也不限制国土安全部根据INA第286(m)节即8 U.S.C. 1356(m)所享有的收回提供裁决和入籍服务成本的权限。H.R.1法案规定的费用是一套独立的法定要求,旨在筹集收入以支持执法优先事项、改善公共安全,并为财政部提供收入,同时美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)继续通过其现有收费权限为裁决职能提供资金。若将H.R.1法案解释为取代国土安全部在8 CFR第106部分中编纂的USCIS费用,将导致USCIS运营收入出现巨大缺口,损害USCIS为其运营提供资金的能力,并危及国会并未表示应予削减的服务水平。
The H.R.1 text “in addition to any other fee authorized by law” is clear.[11] H.R.1 fees do not supersede or replace the fee schedule DHS promulgated in 8 CFR part 106 and related provisions, nor do they limit DHS's authority under INA sec. 286(m), 8 U.S.C. 1356(m), to recover the costs of providing adjudication and naturalization services. The H.R.1 fees are a distinct set of statutory requirements intended to raise revenue to support enforcement priorities, improve public safety, and provide revenue to the Treasury while USCIS continues to fund adjudicatory functions through its existing fee authority. To interpret H.R.1 as supplanting the USCIS fees DHS codified in 8 CFR part 106 would produce a large shortfall in USCIS' operating revenue, compromise USCIS' ability to fund its operations, and jeopardize service levels Congress has not indicated should be curtailed.
IV. 本临时最终规则所作修改的讨论
IV. Discussion of Changes Made in This IFR
本临时最终规则在H.R.1费用通知和临时政策冻结(IPF)通知的基础上进一步扩展,以更全面地实施与USCIS相关的H.R.1费用。国会的立法意图,无论是从H.R.1法案的法定文本还是从相关立法历史中均可清楚看出,这些法定条款的制定旨在迅速加强移民执法并改善公共(印刷页22955)安全。[12] 具体修改内容如下:
This IFR expands upon the H.R. 1 Fee notice and the IPF notice to more fully implement the H.R.1 fees related to USCIS. Congressional intent, reflected in both the statutory text of H.R.1 and in the accompanying legislative history, makes clear that these statutory provisions were enacted to expeditiously strengthen immigration enforcement and improve public ( printed page 22955) safety.[12] The specific changes are as follows:
A. I-94表格移民费
A. Form I-94 Immigration Fee
H.R.1法案要求“任何提交I-94表格入境/离境记录申请的外国人须缴纳最低24美元的费用。” [13] 该新费用应每年根据通货膨胀进行调整,且须“在法律授权的任何其他费用之外”另行征收。[14] 该法规定此项费用“不得豁免或减免。” [15] 本临时最终规则将新的8 U.S.C. 1807节费用要求编纂成法规,适用于提交I-94表格入境/离境记录的申请人,且该费用必须在申请人申请I-94表格时提交,此费用是在8 CFR 106.2(a)(2)所要求费用之外另行征收的。[16]
H.R.1 requires “any alien who submits an application for a Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record to pay a minimum of $24.” [13] This new fee shall be adjusted annually for inflation and is to be collected “in addition to any other fee authorized by law.” [14] The statute specifies that this fee “shall not be waived or reduced.” [15] This IFR codifies the new 8 U.S.C. 1807 fee requirement for applicants that submit a Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record and that the fee must be submitted when the applicants request the Form I-94 in addition to the fee required by 8 CFR 106.2(a)(2).[16]
USCIS将8 U.S.C. 1807节中“提交I-94表格申请”这一表述解释为仅适用于申请人明确提交申请以请求I-94表格入境/离境记录的情形。根据这一解释,USCIS征收I-94表格费用的范围仅限于I-102表格《更换/初次签发非移民入境离境文件申请书》或其后续表格,此系8 U.S.C. 1807节所要求的。[17]
USCIS interprets the language “submits an application for a Form I-94” in 8 U.S.C. 1807 to apply exclusively to scenarios where an applicant files an application explicitly requesting a Form I-94 Arrival/Departure record. This interpretation limits USCIS' collection of the Form I-94 fee solely to Form I-102, Application for Replacement/Initial Nonimmigrant Arrival-Departure Document, or successor form, as required by 8 U.S.C. 1807.[17]
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)认识到,在许多裁决中,该机构在批准授予、延长或变更合法居留期限的申请或申请书时,可能会在其系统中创建或更新电子版I-94表格记录。但是,这一流程不同于通过提交I-102表格直接申请I-94表格。因此,就USCIS的收费而言,8 U.S.C. 1807条所要求的I-94表格费仅限于直接提交I-102表格的情形,不适用于因裁决而附带产生或修改I-94表格记录的情形。[18]
USCIS recognizes that in many adjudications, the agency may create or update an electronic Form I-94 record in its systems when approving an application or petition that confers, extends, or changes a period of authorized stay. However, this process is distinct from a direct request for a Form I-94 through the filing of Form I-102. Accordingly, as it pertains to USCIS' collection, the Form I-94 fee required by 8 U.S.C. 1807 is limited to cases involving the direct filing of Form I-102 and does not extend to an adjudication that results in the incidental creation or amendment of a Form I-94 record.[18]
I-102表格,《更换/初次非移民入境-离境记录申请表》,是外国人申请签发、更换或更正I-94表格所使用的申请表。根据USCIS表格说明以及8 CFR 264.6条的规定,I-102表格可因多种原因提交。[19]其中包括“一般申请”类别,适用于申请人需要更换遗失、被盗、损毁或损坏的I-94表格,或因其他特定例外情形未涵盖的原因而需要I-94表格的情况。此外,申请人还可在下列情形下提交I-102表格:美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)在陆路边境、机场或海港入境时未签发I-94表格;需要更正I-94表格、I-94W《非移民签证豁免入境-离境记录》或I-95《外国人机组人员登陆许可证》,且该错误并非申请人的过失所致;或无法通过CBP网站以电子方式检索该记录。[20]其他类别反映了针对美国武装部队非移民成员、北大西洋公约组织(NATO)部队或“和平伙伴关系”计划成员的特殊规定,在这些情形下,初次申请可免除相应的USCIS申请费,但后续申请仍须缴纳该费用。[21]
Form I-102, Application for Replacement/Initial Nonimmigrant Arrival-Departure Document, is the application an alien requests the issuance, replacement, or correction of a Form I-94. Form I-102 may be filed for a range of reasons, as outlined in the USCIS form instructions and codified at 8 CFR 264.6.[19] These include the “general filing” category, which applies where an applicant requires replacement of a lost, stolen, mutilated, or damaged Form I-94 or otherwise needs a Form I-94 not covered by one of the specific exceptions. In addition, applicants may file Form I-102 where U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) did not issue a Form I-94 at the time of admission at a land border, airport, or seaport, where a correction of a Forms I-94, I-94W, Nonimmigrant Visa Waiver Arrival/Departure Record, or I-95, Alien Crew Landing Permit, is necessary through no fault of the applicant, or where the record cannot be retrieved electronically through CBP's website.[20] Other categories reflect special provisions for nonimmigrant members of the U.S. Armed Forces, North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) forces, or Partnership for Peace programs, where initial requests may be exempt from the underlying USCIS filing fee but subsequent requests remain subject to it.[21]
根据8 U.S.C. 1807条和新增的8 CFR 103.7(d)(4)条,通过I-102表格《更换/初次非移民入境-离境记录申请表》提交的福利申请,除须缴纳现行的美国国土安全部(DHS)申请费外,还须缴纳新的I-94表格费用。即使在DHS申请费被豁免或定为0美元的情况下,H.R.1法案规定的I-94表格费用仍然适用。[22] H.R.1法案I-94表格费用的唯一例外情形是,当I-102表格的提交是为更正DHS的错误时。根据8 CFR 106.3(a)条可申请费用豁免的费用范围不包括H.R.1法案规定的I-94表格费用。

Even in instances where the DHS filing fee is waived or set at $0, the H.R.1 Form I-94 fee remains applicable.[22] The only exception to the H.R.1 Form I-94 fee is for Form I-102 filings when the application is submitted to correct a DHS error. The fees eligible for a waiver request under 8 CFR 106.3(a) do not include the H.R.1 Form I-94 fee.

B. 庇护申请费
B. Asylum Application Fee
1. 背景与法定依据
1. Background and Statutory Context
在本规则中,美国国土安全部(DHS)将8 U.S.C. 1802条[25]所规定的庇护申请费要求予以法典化,并规定,若I-589表格因任何原因依据8 CFR 103.2条被退回,USCIS将保留庇护申请费。目前,若I-589表格未包含签名、未回答表格中的问题,或缺少所需证据或材料,则该表格将作为不完整申请处理。参见8 CFR 208.3(c)条。DHS正在8 CFR 208.3(c)条中增加规定,即所提交的I-589表格若未包含庇护申请费,亦视为不完整,此举与DHS其他法规的规定保持一致。DHS还将庇护申请费增列入8 CFR 103.7条,该条须遵循8 CFR 103.2条规定的提交要求。
In this rule, DHS codifies the asylum application fee requirement set forth in 8 U.S.C. 1802,[25] and provides that USCIS will retain the asylum application fee when a Form I-589 is rejected for any reason consistent with 8 CFR 103.2. Currently, a Form I-589 is filed as an incomplete application if it does not include a signature, does not include a response to the questions on the form, or is missing required evidence or materials. 8 CFR 208.3(c). DHS is adding in 8 CFR 208.3(c) that a filed Form I-589 is also incomplete if it does not include the asylum application fee, which is consistent with DHS's other regulations. DHS is also adding the asylum application fee to 8 CFR 103.7 which is subject to submission requirements in 8 CFR 103.2.
《移民与国籍法》(INA)第208条确立了庇护申请的法定框架,并要求政府对“受理庇护申请”征收“费用”。[26] 8 U.S.C. 1802要求的庇护申请费应在申请提交时缴纳,且规定该费用不得被豁免或减免。[27] 在H.R.1费用通知中,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)通过公布最初最低100美元的庇护申请费,实施了8 U.S.C. 1802的规定,并澄清I-589表格申请必须随附新费用,若缺少该费用,申请将被驳回。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。然而,该通知并未论及USCIS已收到因而已视为提交[28],但随后依据8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)被驳回或依据8 CFR 208.3(c)被退回的申请。
INA sec. 208 establishes the statutory framework for asylum applications and requires the Government to impose “fees for the consideration of an application for asylum.” [26] The asylum application fee required by 8 U.S.C. 1802 is due at the time the application is filed and provides that the fee may not be waived or reduced.[27] In the H.R.1 Fee notice, USCIS implemented 8 U.S.C. 1802 by announcing the initial minimum $100 asylum application fee and clarifying that I-589 filings must include the new fee or they will be rejected if the fee is missing. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). However, that notice did not address applications that are received by USCIS and therefore filed,[28] but subsequently rejected under 8 CFR 103.2(a)(7) or returned under 8 CFR 208.3(c).
美国国土安全部(DHS)的法规要求表格须按照表格说明填写,并须随附法规规定的费用一并提交,除非USCIS另行酌情决定,否则申请费一律不予退还。见8 CFR 103.2(a)(1)。此外,法规规定,USCIS以实际收到福利申请的日期作为收件日期;被驳回的申请不保留收件日期;若未随附正确费用提交,申请将被驳回。见8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)(ii)(D)。这些提交要求与收件规则自1964年起即已适用于福利申请的提交。[29] 就8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)而言,USCIS长期以来将“驳回”一词定义为:因未满足全部提交要求而将福利申请及所缴费用退回,且未经全面审查;申请人可在正确完整填写后重新提交。参见,例如,《移民福利业务转型,第一阶段》,76 FR 53764,53770(2011年8月29日)。[30] 然而,“驳回”一词并未在DHS法规中被编入法典、予以定义或正式颁布。另一方面,“拒绝”(Denied)通常是指申请已经过全面裁决和审查,申请人被认定不符合所寻求福利的资格。同上。“拒绝”一词同样仅在实践中有所界定,而未被编入法典。[31]
DHS regulations require that a form must be executed in accordance with the form instructions and filed with the fee(s) required by regulation, and filing fees are non-refundable, except at the discretion of USCIS. 8 CFR 103.2(a)(1). In addition, regulations provide that USCIS records the receipt date as of the actual date of physical receipt of a benefit request, a rejected request will not retain a receipt date, and a request will be rejected if not submitted with the correct fee. 8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)(ii)(D). Those filing requirements and receipt rules have been in place for benefit request filings since at least 1964.[29] For the purpose of 8 CFR 103.2(a)(7), USCIS has long defined the term “rejected” to mean that the benefit request and fee payment are returned for failure to comply with all filing requirements without being fully considered, and can be re-filed when properly completed. See, e.g., Immigration Benefits Business Transformation, Increment I, 76 FR 53764, 53770 (August 29, 2011).[30] However, the term “rejection” is not codified, defined, or promulgated in DHS regulations. “Denied,” on the other hand, generally means that the request is fully adjudicated and considered, and the requestor is determined ineligible for the benefit sought. Id. Denied is also defined only in practice and not codified.[31]
根据8 CFR 103.2(a)(1)所赋予的关于费用不予退还的自由裁量权,DHS规定:若庇护申请被驳回,则在已提交的庇护申请被驳回时,所缴庇护申请费将予以保留,不予退还或返还。见新增的8 CFR 106.2。
Consistent with the discretion provided in 8 CFR 103.2(a)(1) regarding the non-refundability of fees, DHS is providing that if an asylum application is rejected, the asylum application fee will be retained and not returned or refunded when a filed asylum application is rejected. See new 8 CFR 106.2.
DHS出于法律和实际两方面的原因而保留该费用。首先,此种处理方式是H.R.1所要求的,因为8 U.S.C. 1802规定,每份申请在提交时必须随附该费用。H.R.1规定,DHS应要求任何提交庇护申请的外国人在提交该申请时缴纳100美元的费用。见8 U.S.C. 1802(a)。该条款要求在申请提交时即缴纳费用,而不考虑DHS必须提供的服务或申请人应获得的与该费用相对应的回报,也不考虑若申请被拒绝、被驳回、被放弃、被延迟等情形下该费用应如何处理。同上。[32] 另一方面,H.R.1中缺乏与DHS须提供服务相关联的法定依据,这与INA第286(m)条(即8 U.S.C. 1356(m))形成对比,该条规定DHS可设定福利申请费用以弥补提供此类服务的成本。DHS将第1356(m)条中“提供裁决及入籍服务的费用”解释为:该费用是为提供服务而征收的,实质上即是对所提交申请进行裁决的费用。当申请被驳回,且所执行的唯一“服务”是判断该申请是否达到最低可受理标准时,则无需缴纳费用,该费用应予退还。[33]
DHS is retaining the fee both for legal and practical reasons. First, such treatment is directed by H.R.1 given the requirement in 8 U.S.C. 1802 that each application when filed must include the fee. H.R.1 provides that DHS shall require the payment of a $100 fee by any alien who files an application for asylum at the time such application is filed. 8 U.S.C. 1802(a). That provision requires a fee at the time of the application without regard for the services DHS must provide or the applicant must receive in exchange for the fee or how the fee is treated if the application is denied, rejected, abandoned, delayed, etc. Id.[32] On the other hand, the lack of a statutory link in H.R.1 to the services DHS must provide contrasts with INA 286(m), 8 U.S.C. 1356(m) that provides that DHS may set benefit request fees to recover the costs of providing such services. DHS has interpreted “fees for providing adjudication and naturalization services” in section 1356(m) as meaning the fee is required for the provision of a service, in effect, an adjudication of the filed request. When the request is rejected and the only service performed is to determine if it is minimally acceptable, no fee is due, and the fee is returned.[33]
其次,保留H.R.1规定的庇护申请费有助于遏制有缺陷的申请提交,弥补受理成本,并切实落实国会明确表达的意图,即为执法提供资源,并确保由外国人而非美国纳税人承担特定的行政成本。[34] H.R.1费用通知中有关实施细节(生效日期、缴费方式及因缺少费用而驳回申请)的规定与此种解读相一致。在本临时最终规则(IFR)中,DHS制定了具有法律效力的法规,规定在庇护申请被驳回时保留申请费,包括因未缴纳庇护申请费(AAF)而被驳回的情形。[35]
Next, retention of the H.R.1 asylum application fee promotes deterrence of defective filings, recoups intake costs, and concretely advances Congress's expressed intent to resource enforcement and ensure aliens, not American taxpayers, bear specified administrative costs.[34] The H.R.1 Fee notice implementation details (effective dates, payment mechanics, and rejection for missing fees) are consistent with this reading. In this IFR, DHS establishes regulations with the force and effect of law to provide for retention of filing fees when asylum applications are rejected, to include when an asylum application is rejected due to nonpayment of the AAF.[35]
DHS还援引8 CFR 103.2,该条款为美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)在认定适当的情况下退还费用提供了一定的行政裁量权。例如,过去USCIS曾在极少数情况下错误地要求个人连同相关费用一并提交非必要的表格。另一个例子是,个人多次缴纳所需费用,或以其他方式缴纳超过应付金额的费用,而USCIS接受了该不正确的超额付款。因此,DHS援引8 CFR 103.2指出,虽然在所提交的庇护申请被驳回时,所收费用将被保留而不予退还,但DHS现行法规在DHS自由裁量权范围内提供有限的退款。(印刷页22957)
DHS is also referencing 8 CFR 103.2 which provides some administrative discretion to USCIS to refund a fee if the agency determines that is appropriate. For example, in the past, USCIS on a rare occasion has erroneously requested that an individual file an unnecessary form along with the associated fee. Another example is where an individual pays a required fee more than once or otherwise pays a fee in excess of the amount due and USCIS accepts the incorrect overpayment. Therefore, DHS references 8 CFR 103.2 noting that while the fee will be retained and not returned or refunded when a filed asylum application is rejected, DHS's existing regulations provide limited refunds at DHS discretion. ( printed page 22957)

C. 年度庇护费的实施与管理
C. Implementation and Administration of the Annual Asylum Fee
1. 年度庇护费(AAF)背景
1. AAF Background
2025年7月22日,USCIS发布了H.R.1费用通知,实施8 U.S.C. 1808,宣布2025财年及以后年度年度庇护费(AAF)的管理方式。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。H.R.1要求所有待决庇护申请的申请人,在申请“仍处于待决状态”的每个日历年,除其他适用费用外,还须缴纳最低100美元的年费。见8 U.S.C. 1808。
On July 22, 2025, USCIS published the H.R.1 Fee notice implementing 8 U.S.C. 1808 by announcing how the AAF would be administered for FY 2025 and beyond. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). H.R.1 requires all asylum applicants with pending asylum applications to pay a minimum $100 annual fee for each calendar year the application “remains pending,” in addition to any other applicable fee. 8 U.S.C. 1808.
在H.R.1费用通知中,DHS将法定用语“仍处于待决状态”解释为涵盖任何已向USCIS或美国司法部(DOJ)提交、且仍在任何对庇护申请具有管辖权的联邦政府机构、法院或实体处待决的I-589表格。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。该通知进一步明确,凡在2024年10月1日或之前向USCIS提交申请、且在2025年9月30日2025财年结束时仍处于待决状态的庇护申请人,必须缴纳最初的最低100美元年度庇护费。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。通过这种方式,USCIS向受影响的申请人提前数月发出通知,告知他们若选择在2025年9月及以后继续推进其申请,将须缴纳该费用。
In the H.R.1 Fee notice, DHS interpreted the statutory phrase “remains pending” to encompass any Form I-589 filed with USCIS or DOJ and that remains pending with any federal government agency, court, or entity with jurisdiction over asylum claims. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). The notice further clarified that the initial minimum $100 AAF must be paid by asylum applicants whose applications had been filed with USCIS on or before October 1, 2024, and that were still pending at the close of FY 2025 on September 30, 2025. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). In doing so, USCIS provided several months' notice to impacted applicants that they would be subject to a fee if they chose to pursue their application through and beyond September 2025.
H.R.1费用通知还说明了年度庇护费在后续年度的适用方式。对于在整个2025财年内处于待决状态的庇护申请,此后每个该申请仍处于待决状态的年度,年度庇护费均应于9月30日到期缴纳。对于在2024年10月1日之后提交、且自提交之日起365天仍处于待决状态的I-589表格,在该申请仍处于待决状态的每一年,年度庇护费均应于提交之日一周年当天到期缴纳。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。USCIS认定,H.R.1并未对2025财年之前的年份追溯适用年度庇护费,但8 U.S.C. 1808的明文规定要求将最低100美元的费用适用于在2025财年开始时已处于待决状态的申请。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。在得出此结论时,USCIS援引了成熟的制定法解释原则以及美国最高法院判例Landgraf诉USI Film Products案,该判例区分了不被允许的溯及既往规则与前瞻性的程序变更。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。由于H.R.1明确规定年度庇护费“应当”自2025财年起及在该财年内适用,因此将该要求适用于截至2024年10月1日仍处于待决状态的案件,被认定既符合国会意图,也符合判例法。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。
The H.R.1 Fee notice also explained how the AAF applies in subsequent years. For asylum applications pending during the entirety of FY 2025, the AAF would become due on September 30 for each subsequent year that the application remains pending. For Forms I-589 filed after October 1, 2024 that remain pending for 365 days after filing, the AAF becomes due annually on the one-year anniversary of the filing date each year the application remains pending. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). USCIS determined that H.R.1 does not impose the AAF retroactively for years prior to FY 2025, but that the plain language of 8 U.S.C. 1808 requires applying the minimum $100 fee to applications that were already pending at the start of FY 2025. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). In reaching this conclusion, USCIS cited established principles of statutory interpretation and U.S. Supreme Court case law, Landgraf v. USI Film Products, which distinguishes between impermissible retroactive rules and prospective procedural changes. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). Because H.R.1 expressly mandated that the AAF “shall” apply beginning in and for FY 2025, applying the requirement to pending cases as of October 1, 2024, was deemed consistent with both congressional intent and case law. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).
最后,7月通知确立了USCIS征收年度庇护费的行政程序。就2025财年而言,根据H.R.1费用通知的规定,USCIS向有待决案件的庇护申请人逐一发送个性化通知,载明应缴金额、缴费期限、缴费方式以及未缴费的后果。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。USCIS要求通过该机构的电子收费系统在线缴纳年度庇护费。USCIS建立的这一框架旨在便利申请人遵守规定,使年度庇护费缴纳流程尽量清晰明了、负担尽量小,并确保政府能够对所有待决庇护申请可靠地实施这一新的法定要求。见90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。
Finally, the July notice established USCIS' administrative process for collecting the AAF. For FY 2025, consistent with the H.R.1 Fee notice, USCIS sent individual, personalized notices to asylum applicants with pending cases, identifying the amount owed, the time period in which to pay the fee, the method of payment, and the consequences of failure to pay. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). USCIS requires the AAF to be paid online through the agency's electronic fee payment system. The framework established by USCIS was designed to facilitate compliance by applicants, to make the AAF process unconfusing and as un-burdensome as possible, and to ensure the government can reliably administer the new statutory requirement across all pending asylum applications. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).
2. 未缴纳年度庇护费的后果
2. Consequences of Failure To Pay the AAF
i. 后果概述
i. Summary of Consequences
H.R.1法案要求,且本临时最终规则(IFR)将其纳入法规,自2025财年及以后,凡庇护申请处于待决状态的每个日历年均须缴纳年度庇护费(AAF)。8 U.S.C. 1808。该法规要求征收并强制执行年度庇护费,且禁止豁免。8 U.S.C. 1808。将未缴纳年度庇护费的后果以法规形式明确规定,对于使申请人明确了解相关要求、落实国会立法意图至关重要。美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)此前从未对其待决的申请或请愿要求缴纳年费。因此,USCIS无法依据现行或既往做法来确定未缴纳年度庇护费的后果。明确规定的法规后果能够最有效地落实该法定要求,因为若无此规定,该法定要求将面临失效的风险,使申请人得以在无限期维持庇护申请待决状态的同时规避其应尽义务。[36]这种结果将削弱H.R.1法案的立法目的,造成遵规申请人与不遵规申请人之间的不公平,并且未能将相关成本转嫁给申请人本身,而是由合法移民缴纳的费用来予以补贴。[37]根据H.R.1法案第100018条,《移民与国籍法》(INA)第208(d)(3)条已被修订,要求USCIS“就受理庇护申请征收费用”。年度庇护费即属于该等强制性费用之一。8 U.S.C. 1808。若不确立未缴纳年度庇护费的相应后果,USCIS将被迫在无法定权限的情况下审理庇护申请,或者使未缴纳年度庇护费的申请无限期滞留在积压案件之中。
H.R.1 requires, and this IFR codifies in regulation, for FY 2025 and beyond, payment of the AAF for each calendar year that an asylum application remains pending. 8 U.S.C. 1808. The statute mandates collection and enforcement of the AAF and prohibits waivers. 8 U.S.C. 1808. Regulatory codification of the consequences for failure to pay the AAF is essential to give clarity to applicants and give effect to Congress's mandate. USCIS has never required an annual fee for an application or petition that is pending with USCIS. Therefore, USCIS cannot rely on current or past practice to determine the consequences for nonpayment of the AAF. A clearly defined regulatory consequence implements the statutory requirement most effectively because the statutory requirement would risk becoming ineffective, allowing applicants to avoid their obligations while maintaining pending asylum claims indefinitely.[36] Such a result would undercut H.R.1's purpose, create inequities between compliant and non-compliant applicants, and fail to place costs on applicants rather than being subsidized by fees paid by legal immigrants.[37] Pursuant to H.R.1 sec. 100018, INA sec. 208(d)(3) was amended to require that USCIS “impose fees for the consideration of an application for asylum.” One of those mandatory fees is the AAF. 8 U.S.C. 1808. Without established consequences for failure to pay the AAF, USCIS would be required to adjudicate an asylum application without statutory authority or keep applications with unpaid AAFs in the backlog indefinitely.
国土安全部(DHS)在本规则中明确规定,在发出个别通知并给予30天在线缴费期限后,未缴纳年度庇护费将导致待决的I-589表格被驳回。[38]驳回意味着该庇护申请在USCIS终止,即USCIS将不再对该申请采取任何进一步行动。若外国人希望重新申请庇护,须重新提交新的I-589表格,并按照8 U.S.C. 1802的要求缴纳新的强制性申请费。若该外国人维持合法身份,USCIS将不会仅因未缴纳年度庇护费而签发出庭通知书(NTA)或启动遣返程序。[39]若该外国人不具有合法身份,国土安全部将根据《移民与国籍法》第235(b)条,即8 U.S.C. 1225(b),在申请人符合加速遣返条件时启动加速遣返程序,或在其他情形下根据《移民与国籍法》第239条,即8 U.S.C. 1229,签发出庭通知书。[40]根据现行庇护管辖规则,一旦在驳回后提交了出庭通知书,任何其后以辩护方式提交的I-589表格将提交移民法官,并依据8 CFR 1208.2(b)进行审理。若该外国人维持合法身份,USCIS将因未缴费而驳回I-589表格,但仅在事实支持可遣返指控的情况下才会签发出庭通知书。[41]
DHS codifies in this rule that, following individualized notice and a 30-day window for online payment, failure to pay the AAF results in rejection of the pending Form I-589.[38] Rejection results in the termination of the asylum application with USCIS, meaning that USCIS will take no further action on the application. If the alien wishes to reapply for asylum, he or she will need to file a new Form I-589, including a new mandatory filing fee as required by 8 U.S.C. 1802. If the alien maintains lawful status, USCIS will not issue a Notice to Appear (NTA) or initiate removal solely based on the AAF nonpayment.[39] If the alien lacks lawful status, DHS will either initiate expedited removal under INA sec. 235(b), 8 U.S.C. 1225(b), where the applicant is amenable to expedited removal, or issue an NTA under INA sec. 239, 8 U.S.C. 1229, in other cases.[40] Consistent with existing asylum jurisdiction rules, once the NTA is filed after rejection, any subsequent defensively filed Form I-589 is submitted to and adjudicated by an immigration judge under 8 CFR 1208.2(b). If the alien maintains lawful status, USCIS will reject Form I-589 for nonpayment but will only issue an NTA if the facts support a charge of removability.[41]
此外,根据新增的8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(i)条,因未缴费而被驳回将导致庇护就业授权时钟停止计时,因为该申请将不再处于待决状态。此外,根据新增的8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(iii)和(iv)条,根据8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8)条提出的任何待决就业授权申请都将被驳回或拒绝。(原文第22958页)一旦USCIS驳回庇护申请,根据新增的8 CFR 208.7(b)(1)条,依据8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8)条所获得的任何现有就业授权将立即终止。根据8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(2)和新增的8 CFR 208.7(b)(2)条,若行政审查办公室(EOIR)的移民法官拒绝或驳回庇护申请,就业授权将在拒绝或驳回之日起满30天的当日立即终止,除非该外国人在此期限内及时向移民上诉委员会提出上诉。根据8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(3)和新增的8 CFR 208.7(b)(3)条,若移民上诉委员会驳回针对庇护申请拒绝或驳回决定的上诉,就业授权将立即终止。[42]
Further, rejection for nonpayment will stop the asylum employment-authorization clock under new 8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(i) as the application will no longer be pending. Further, any pending application for employment authorization under 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8) would be rejected or denied per new 8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(iii) and (iv). Upon ( printed page 22958) rejection of the asylum application by USCIS, any existing employment authorization pursuant to 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8) will terminate immediately per new 8 CFR 208.7(b)(1). Per 8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(2) and new 8 CFR 208.7(b)(2), if the asylum application is denied or rejected by an immigration judge at EOIR, the employment authorization will terminate immediately on the date that is 30 days after the date of denial or rejection, unless the alien makes a timely appeal to the Board of Immigration Appeals. Per 8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(3) and new 8 CFR 208.7(b)(3), if the Board of Immigration Appeals denies an appeal of a denial or rejection of an asylum application, employment authorization will terminate immediately.[42]

三、因未缴纳庇护申请费(AAF)而拒收待决庇护申请
iii. Rejection of a Pending Asylum Application for Failure To Pay the AAF
甲、H.R.1法案及《移民与国籍法》第208条项下国土安全部的职权
a. DHS Authority Under H.R.1 and INA Sec. 208
《移民与国籍法》(INA)第208条,即8 U.S.C. 1158条,确立了管辖庇护申请及相关程序的法定框架。第208(a)条作出一般性规定,即任何身在美国境内或抵达美国的外国人均可依据第208条或(在适用情况下)第235条申请庇护,同时也规定了若干例外情形,说明某些外国人不具备申请庇护的资格。第208(b)条界定了批准庇护的标准,并允许国土安全部(DHS)制定额外的限制条件,规定在何种情形下外国人将不具备庇护资格。[44] 第208(d)条规定了程序性要求,并明确授权政府征收“审理庇护申请的费用”以及“本条项下就业授权的费用”,并允许国土安全部“以法规形式规定对庇护申请审理的任何其他条件或限制。”[45]
Section 208 of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1158, establishes the statutory framework governing asylum applications and related procedures. Section 208(a) provides a general statement that any alien physically present in or arriving at the United States may apply for asylum, either pursuant to section 208 or section 235 where applicable, and also provides exceptions describing certain aliens who are not eligible to apply for asylum. Section 208(b) defines the standards for granting asylum and permits DHS to establish additional limitations and conditions under which an alien will be ineligible for asylum.[44] Section 208(d) sets procedural requirements and expressly authorizes the Government to impose “fees for the consideration of an application for asylum” and “fees for employment authorization under this section,” and permits DHS to “provide by regulation for any other conditions or limitations on the consideration of an application for asylum.” [45]
由此可见,国会已认识到,外国人在国土安全部寻求庇护身份须以缴纳合理的行政费用为前提条件,且未能遵守此类程序性要求将导致任何已提交的庇护申请归于无效,因为国土安全部无法对其进行审理。[46] 因此,《移民与国籍法》第208条允许国土安全部将庇护申请的持续待决状态,以及与之相关权益的保留,以遵守既定缴费义务为条件。[47] H.R.1法案要求国土安全部就申请待决的每一年征收庇护申请费(AAF),这完全符合《移民与国籍法》第208条的范围。[48] 因此,国土安全部将以下规定予以成文化:未能在30天缴费期限内缴纳庇护申请费,将产生与庇护法规定的程序性要求以及H.R.1法案此前在其H.R.1费用通知中所述的移民执法目的相一致的后果。[49] 见联邦公报第90卷第34511页(2025年7月22日)。
Congress thus recognized that an alien's pursuit of asylum status before DHS is contingent on the payment of reasonable administrative fees and that failure to comply with such procedural requirements renders any attempted asylum application a nullity because DHS is unable to consider it.[46] As such, section 208 of the INA permits DHS to condition the continued pendency of an asylum application—and retention of benefits associated with it—on compliance with established fee obligations.[47] H.R.1's requirement that DHS collect the AAF for each year an application remains pending operates squarely within the bounds of section 208 of the INA.[48] Thus, DHS codifies that failure to pay the AAF within a 30-day payment period will result in consequences consistent with both the procedural requirements of the asylum statute and the immigration enforcement purpose of H.R.1 previously described in its H.R.1 Fee notice.[49] 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).
乙、拒收与美国公民及移民服务局申请规则及行政惯例相符
b. Rejection Aligns With USCIS Filing Rules and Administrative Practice
美国公民及移民服务局的一般申请法规要求,任何权益申请均须“按照表格说明及适用法规”提交,其中包括缴费要求。[50] 美国公民及移民服务局的法规还规定,如权益申请提交时所附费用不正确、未附费用,或未能满足所要求的缴费条件,该申请“将被拒收”,这一规定将拒收未附正确费用的申请这一长期惯例予以成文化。[51] 缴费系申请的前提条件,美国公民及移民服务局可拒收未满足法定、法规或表格说明所规定缴费要求的申请。[52] 由于8 U.S.C. 1808(b)条将缴费义务与申请“持续待决”的每一年挂钩,因此在经个别通知并给予30天缴费期限后,对未缴费情形而言,可操作、合乎逻辑且统一的后果应为拒收申请,而非使其继续待决以供审理,否则将与适用法律相抵触。[53] 见联邦公报第90卷第34511页(2025年7月22日)。
USCIS' general filing regulations require that any benefit request be submitted “in accordance with the form instructions and applicable regulations,” which include fee requirements.[50] USCIS regulations also provide that a benefit request “will be rejected” if it is submitted with an incorrect fee, no fee, or otherwise fails to satisfy required fee payment conditions—codifying the longstanding practice of rejecting filings that do not include proper fees.[51] Fees are a filing prerequisite and USCIS may reject filings that do not meet fee requirements prescribed by statute, regulation, or form instructions.[52] Because 8 U.S.C. 1808(b) ties fee payment to each year the application “remains pending,” the administrable, logical, and uniform consequence for nonpayment—after individualized notice and a 30-day payment period—is rejection rather than allowing it to remain pending for consideration contrary to the applicable statutes.[53] 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025).
丙、因未缴费而拒收申请的做法符合对8 U.S.C. 1808(b)条的最佳解释
c. Rejection for Failure To Pay Is Consistent With the Best Interpretation of 8 U.S.C. 1808(b)
国会意图使8 U.S.C. 1808条具有实际效力,使未缴纳年度申请费(AAF)产生实质性后果。根据既定的制定法解释原则,包括反冗余规则,法规的解释必须使每一条款都具有效力。[54] 若将8 U.S.C. 1808条解释为仅规定强制性年度费用而对不缴费不设任何后果,将使该条款形同虚设,这与国会的意图相悖。[55]
Congress intended 8 U.S.C. 1808 to have operative effect such that failure to pay the AAF would carry meaningful consequences. Under settled principles of statutory construction, including the rule against superfluities, a statute must be interpreted so that each provision is given effect.[54] Reading 8 U.S.C. 1808 to impose a mandatory annual fee without consequence for nonpayment would render the provision a nullity, contrary to Congress' intent.[55]
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)进一步根据其长期以来的实践,解释H.R.1法案对未缴纳年度申请费(AAF)具体后果的沉默。USCIS一贯要求福利申请必须随附适当的费用缴纳作为受理申请的条件,并(印刷页22959)惯常拒收未附带适当费用的福利申请。[56] 这一要求缴纳适当费用的既有框架早于H.R.1法案,并体现在现行的美国国土安全部(DHS)法规中。[57] 因此,DHS认为对该法规的最佳解读是,国会在制定年度申请费要求时,假定USCIS将适用其现有的费用合规实践,并认为无需在法规中明确规定不缴费的后果。据此,DHS将8 U.S.C. 1808条解释为与USCIS现行的费用实践协同运作,即不遵守费用要求将导致福利申请被拒收。[58]
DHS further interprets H.R.1's silence regarding the specific consequence for nonpayment of the AAF consistent with longstanding USCIS practice. USCIS has consistently required that benefit requests be accompanied by proper fee payment as a condition of filing and ( printed page 22959) routinely rejects benefit requests that do not include the proper fee.[56] This well-established framework for requiring payment of the proper fee predates H.R.1 and is reflected in existing DHS regulations.[57] As such, DHS thinks the best reading of the statute is that Congress, in enacting the AAF requirement, presumed that USCIS would apply its existing practice for fee compliance and did not see a need to codify the consequences for nonpayment explicitly in the statute. Accordingly, DHS interprets 8 U.S.C. 1808 to operate in concert with existing USCIS fee practice, under which noncompliance with fee requirements results in rejection of the benefit request.[58]
因此,DHS认为,结合移民与国籍法(INA)第208条解读,H.R.1法案年度申请费规定的最佳解释是,将费用合规作为庇护申请持续待决及USCIS继续审理该申请的条件。[59] 将INA第208(d)(3)条和8 U.S.C. 1808(b)条解释为授权在发出通知及30天缴费期限后因未缴费而拒收I-589表格,是最佳解读,因为国会将年度费用与申请“持续待决”的每一年相关联,从而使该年度之后申请的持续待决及USCIS对申请的继续审理取决于是否缴费。[60] 90 FR 34511(2025年7月22日)。这一解释也符合合理决策标准,因为它通过明确、可操作的触发机制,直接推进了国会的成本回收和威慑目标。[61] 此外,本临时最终规则不具有溯及力,因为它不会“损害当事方行为时所享有的权利,增加当事方对过去行为的责任,或对已完成的交易施加新的义务。”[62] 触发事件是申请在法案颁布后某一日期已待决满一年,且该申请持续待决。任何年度申请费最早的到期日为2025财政年度结束之时,本规则所规定的具体后果不会适用,除非申请人已根据本规则收到相关后果通知,并获得30天履行该要求的机会。因此,及时缴费是一项前瞻性要求,而非不被允许的溯及既往处罚。据此,将因未缴纳年度申请费而拒收申请予以法典化,是对INA第208(d)(3)条和8 U.S.C. 1808(b)条的最佳解释之落实。[63]
Thus, DHS believes the best interpretation of H.R.1's AAF mandate, when read together with INA sec. 208, is to require fee compliance as a condition of continued pendency and USCIS' consideration of the asylum application.[59] Interpreting INA sec. 208(d)(3) and 8 U.S.C. 1808(b) to authorize rejection of a Form I-589 for nonpayment after notice and a 30-day payment period is the best reading because Congress tied an annual fee to each year an application “remains pending,” thereby making ongoing pendency and USCIS' consideration of the application after such year contingent on payment.[60] 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). This construction also satisfies the reasoned decisionmaking standard because it directly advances Congress's cost-recovery and deterrence objectives through a clear, administrable trigger.[61] Further, this interim final rule will not have retroactive effect because it does not “impair rights a party possessed when he acted, increase a party's liability for past conduct, or impose new duties with respect to transactions already completed.” [62] The triggering event is the pendency of the application for one year as of a date after enactment and the continued pendency of the application. The earliest any AAF payments became due was the close of FY 2025 and the specific consequences articulated in the rule will not be applied until an applicant receives notice of such consequences under the rule and a 30-day opportunity to fulfill the requirement. As such, timely payment is a prospective requirement, not an impermissible retroactive penalty. Accordingly, codifying rejection for AAF nonpayment implements the best interpretation of INA sec. 208(d)(3) and 8 U.S.C. 1808(b).[63]
d. 未缴费将导致工作许可申请被拒收或拒绝,或已批准工作许可立即终止
d. Nonpayment Will Result in the Rejection or Denial of Applications for Employment Authorization or Immediate Termination of Previously Approved Employment Authorization
根据本最终规则,未按8 U.S.C. 1808条要求缴纳年度申请费,将依据新增的8 CFR 106.2(c)(15)(ii)条和新增的8 CFR 208.3(c)(3)条,导致待决庇护申请被拒收。与此认定相一致,因未缴纳年度申请费而被拒收,将依据新增的8 CFR 208.3(c)(3)(i)条和8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(i)条,导致工作许可资格累计时间的终止。此外,因未缴纳年度申请费而拒收I-589表格,将依据新增的8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(iii)或(iv)条,导致任何仍待决的相关工作许可申请被拒收或拒绝,或依据8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(1)条及新增的8 CFR 208.7(b)(1)条的要求,导致相关工作许可立即终止。[64] 这些后果将要求庇护申请人在维持其待决庇护申请的同时,履行法律规定的费用义务。这些变更确保了I-589表格的拒绝和拒收,在8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8)条下的工作许可资格方面产生相同的后果。
Under this final rule, failure to pay the AAF required by 8 U.S.C. 1808 results in rejection of the pending asylum application pursuant to new 8 CFR 106.2(c)(15)(ii) and new 8 CFR 208.3(c)(3). Consistent with that determination, rejection for failure to pay the AAF will result in the termination of accrual of time toward employment authorization eligibility under new 8 CFR 208.3(c)(3)(i) and 8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(i). Further, rejection of the Form I-589 for failure to pay the AAF will result in the rejection or denial of any related application for employment authorization still pending under new 8 CFR 208.7(a)(1)(iii) or (iv), or the immediate termination of related employment authorization as required by 8 U.S.C. 1810(b)(1) and new 8 CFR 208.7(b)(1).[64] These consequences will require asylum applicants to meet the fee obligations in the law while still maintaining their pending asylum claims. These changes ensure that both a denial and a rejection of a Form I-589, result in the same consequence for purposes of employment authorization eligibility under 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(8).
四、已考虑的未缴纳年度申请费的替代后果
iv. Alternative Consequences for Nonpayment of the AAF Considered
国土安全部(DHS)评估了若干替代方案,以替代所通过的后果框架,从而确保本规则体现对H.R.1和《移民与国籍法》(INA)的最佳解释,并满足《行政程序法》(APA)关于合理决策的要求。[65] 基于下文所述理由,国土安全部得出结论,所选方法,即因未缴纳资产评估费(AAF)而予以驳回(并在适用情形下可能签发NTA或启动快速遣返程序),最能有效实现H.R.1的执法和威慑目的,并且在行政管理上优于以下所考虑的各项替代方案。
DHS evaluated several alternatives to the adopted consequence framework to ensure the rule reflects the best interpretation of H.R.1 and the INA and satisfies the Administrative Procedure Act (APA)'s requirement for reasoned decisionmaking.[65] For the reasons explained below, DHS concluded that the selected approach—rejection for nonpayment of the AAF (and possibly NTA issuance or initiation of expedited removal where applicable) best effectuates H.R.1's enforcement and deterrence purposes and is administratively superior to the following alternatives considered.
a. 因未缴纳资产评估费而予以拒绝,而非驳回
a. Denial for AAF Nonpayment in Lieu of Rejection
国土安全部曾考虑依据8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)(ii)(D)(1)将未缴费作为拒绝的理由,而非将其视为与法定申请条件相关的驳回。将驳回用于费用合规缺陷,符合现行8 CFR 103.2框架,该框架规定申请“必须按照表格说明和适用法规执行并提交”,包括费用要求,如提交时费用不正确或缺失,则“将被驳回”。[66] 此外,以拒绝取代驳回可能会触发8 CFR 103.3和103.5项下的动议程序,增加程序层级和延误,这与H.R.1要求资产评估费须在申请“继续待决”的每一年份一并缴纳的规定不符。[67] 最后,现行庇护法规[68]规定,如申请人维持合法移民身份或处于有效假释期内,可对I-589表予以拒绝。
DHS considered treating nonpayment as grounds for denial under 8 CFR 103.2(a)(7)(ii)(D)(1) rather than a rejection tied to a statutory filing condition. Using rejection for a fee-compliance defect fits the existing 8 CFR 103.2 framework, in which filings “must be executed and filed in accordance with the form instructions and applicable regulations,” including fee requirements, and “will be rejected” if submitted with an incorrect or missing fee.[66] Further, a denial in lieu of a rejection could trigger motions processes under 8 CFR 103.3 and 103.5, adding layers of procedure and delay inconsistent with H.R.1's direction that the AAF accompany each year an application “remains pending.” [67] Finally, current asylum regulations [68] contemplate denial of an I-589 where the applicant is maintaining lawful immigration status or is in a valid period of parole.
b. 驳回且不签发NTA,并禁止重新提交庇护申请
b. Rejection With No NTA and a Bar on Refiling Asylum
国土安全部还考虑了另一种替代方案,即未缴纳资产评估费将导致驳回,不随后签发NTA,但禁止向USCIS重新提交I-589表。国土安全部否决了该替代方案,因为一律禁止重新提交可能需要一项行政负担沉重的程序来评估例外情形,以便与INA第208(a)(1)条,即8 U.S.C. 1158(a)(1)条保持一致。此外,一概禁止重新提交将难以公平执行,且可能排除真实的保护申请,这与该法规的人道主义目的相悖。[69] 签发NTA或启动快速遣返程序,为外国人提供了一条途径,使其能够向有权批准暂缓遣返或《禁止酷刑公约》保护的移民法官,或有权进行恐惧筛查的庇护官员提出其真实的保护申请。[70]
DHS also considered an alternative where an unpaid AAF would result in rejection, with no subsequent NTA but with a prohibition on refiling a Form I-589 with USCIS. DHS rejected this alternative because a categorical bar on refiling would potentially require an administratively burdensome process for evaluating exceptions in order to maintain consistency with INA section 208(a)(1), 8 U.S.C. 1158(a)(1). Additionally, a blanket refile ban would be difficult to administer fairly and could preclude bona fide protection claims, contrary to the statute's humanitarian purpose.[69] Issuing an NTA or initiating expedited removal provides a path for aliens to present their bona fide protection claims either before an immigration judge with authority to grant withholding of removal or protection under the Convention against Torture, or an ( printed page 22960) asylum officer with authority to conduct a fear screening.[70]
c. 在缴纳资产评估费之前将申请搁置
c. Holding Applications in Abeyance Until AAF Is Paid
国土安全部还考虑了在未缴费情形下将申请搁置的做法(即不作出决定、不予驳回、不计息),从而使案件保持待决状态,直至缴纳强制性资产评估费为止。该方案被否决,因为这将允许在不合规的情况下无限期维持待决状态,从而违背H.R.1关于年度费用须在申请“继续待决”的每一年份一并缴纳的要求。[71] 资产评估费的结构也表明,国会关注的是庇护申请人提交I-589表以毫无根据地逃避遣返并获得工作授权,同时依赖庇护积压或采用拖延策略以避免对其申请作出最终裁决的问题。若国土安全部因未缴纳资产评估费而将I-589表申请无限期搁置,这将加剧国会意图解决的这一问题本身。搁置还将加剧积压和资源紧张,而非推进H.R.1的执法和成本回收目标,这与APA对迅速作出行政处置的期望相悖。[72] 简言之,搁置将保留甚至恶化H.R.1的资产评估费旨在抵制的那些激励因素,因此并不适当。8 U.S.C. 1808条。
DHS also considered holding applications in abeyance upon nonpayment ( i.e., no decision, no rejection, and no charging interest), thereby keeping the case pending until the mandatory AAF fee is paid. This approach was rejected because it would allow indefinite pendency notwithstanding noncompliance, frustrating H.R.1's requirement that the annual fee accompany each year an application “remains pending.” [71] The structure of the AAF also demonstrates Congress's concern with asylum applicants filing Form I-589 to baselessly avoid removal and obtain employment authorization while relying on the asylum backlog or using dilatory tactics to avoid a final adjudication of their application. Were DHS to hold Form I-589 applications in indefinite abeyance for failure to pay the AAF, this would exacerbate the very problem that Congress intended to address. Abeyance also would exacerbate backlogs and resource strain rather than advance H.R.1's enforcement and cost-recovery objectives, contrary to the APA's expectation of prompt administrative disposition.[72] In short, abeyance would preserve or worsen the very incentives H.R.1's AAF was designed to counteract and therefore not appropriate. 8. U.S.C. 1808.
D. 限制基于临时保护身份的工作授权的实施
D. Implementation of the Limits on Employment Authorization Based on Temporary Protected Status
1. H.R.1限制工作授权有效期的条文
1. H.R.1 Text Limiting Validity of Employment Authorization
国会制定《H.R.1法案》,其部分目的在于明确与临时保护身份(TPS)相关的就业授权及就业授权期限的新统一限制和费用。[73] 《美国法典》第8编第1803(c)条规定,每份基于TPS的初次就业授权“有效期应为1年,或临时保护身份的持续期间,以较短者为准”,并对提交初次TPS就业授权文件(EAD)申请的外国人加征一项不得豁免或减免的额外费用。《美国法典》第8编第1811(a)条同样规定,对获得TPS的外国人授予的任何就业授权,或该授权的任何续期或延长,“有效期应为1年或临时保护身份指定的持续期间,以较短者为准”,并设立了一项不得豁免或减免的续期或延长费用。根据其条文规定,这些条款将与TPS相关的工作授权上限设定为1年或剩余指定期间(以较短者为准),且未针对TPS设置可超出该上限的特殊例外情形。[74]
Congress enacted H.R.1, in part, to specify new, uniform limits and fees for employment authorization and employment authorization periods in connection with TPS.[73] 8 U.S.C. 1803(c) provides that each initial TPS-based employment authorization “shall be valid for a period of 1 year, or for the duration of the alien's temporary protected status, whichever is shorter,” and imposes an additional fee for an alien who files an initial TPS EAD application that may not be waived or reduced. 8 U.S.C. 1811(a) likewise provides that any employment authorization for an alien granted TPS, or any renewal or extension of such authorization, “shall be valid for a period of 1 year or for the duration of the designation of temporary protected status, whichever is shorter,” and establishes a renewal or extension fee that may not be waived or reduced. By their terms, these provisions cap TPS-related work authorization at one year or the remaining designation, whichever is shorter, with no TPS-specific exception to exceed the cap.[74]
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)在其《H.R.1费用》通知中,概述了这些TPS就业授权费用条款,并重申了初次申请和续期/延长申请适用的“1年或指定期间”有效期规则,同时指出法律禁止豁免《H.R.1法案》规定的费用,但依据《联邦法规汇编》第8编第106部分,另有一项可豁免的现有法规费用仍然适用。见《联邦公报》第90卷第34511页(2025年7月22日)。此后,USCIS开始将该有效期规则适用于基于TPS的就业授权。[75]本临时最终规则(IFR)对《联邦法规汇编》第8编第244部分和第274a部分中的国土安全部(DHS)法规进行了更新,以符合《H.R.1法案》的法定要求。
In its H.R.1 Fee notice, USCIS summarized these TPS employment authorization fee provisions and restated the one-year-or-duration of designation validity rules for initial and renewal/extension filings, noting the statutory prohibition on waiver of the H.R.1 fees and the separate preexisting regulatory fee that may still be waived under 8 CFR part 106. 90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025). Thereafter, USCIS began applying the validity rules to TPS-based employment authorization.[75] This IFR updates DHS regulations at 8 CFR parts 244 and 274a to conform to the statutory mandate in H.R.1.
二、《移民与国籍法》(INA)第244条及既往实践
2. INA Sec. 244 and Prior Practice
《移民与国籍法》第244条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a条,授权部长指定TPS适用国家,对符合资格的该国国民授予TPS,并为处于TPS身份的外国人提供福利,包括获得就业授权的资格。《移民与国籍法》第244(a)(1)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(1)条规定,部长“可授予该外国人临时保护身份”,并“应授权该外国人在美国境内从事工作,并向其提供就业授权批注或其他适当的工作许可”。《移民与国籍法》第244(a)(2)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(2)条进一步规定,与TPS相关的工作授权“在该外国人依据本条处于临时保护身份期间应持续有效”。《移民与国籍法》第244(a)(4)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(4)条进一步要求,对初步证明符合TPS资格条件的外国人提供临时待遇福利,包括就业授权,直至作出最终决定,或在适用情况下,直至开启合理登记机会为止。
INA sec. 244, 8 U.S.C. 1254a, authorizes the Secretary to designate countries for TPS, to grant TPS to eligible nationals, and to provide benefits to an alien in TPS status including eligibility for employment authorization. INA sec. 244(a)(1), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(1) states that the Secretary “may grant the alien temporary protected status” and “shall authorize the alien to engage in employment in the United States and provide the alien with an employment authorized endorsement or other appropriate work permit.” INA sec. 244(a)(2), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(2), adds that the work authorization associated with TPS “shall be effective throughout the period the alien is in temporary protected status under this section.” INA sec. 244(a)(4), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(4), further requires provision of temporary treatment benefits—including employment authorization—to an alien who establishes a prima facie case of eligibility for TPS until a final determination is made or until a reasonable opportunity to register opens, as applicable.
国土安全部(DHS)关于提供就业授权类别的相关法规,体现了《移民与国籍法》第244(a)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)条的各项要求。根据《联邦法规汇编》第8编第274a.12(a)(12)条,获得TPS的外国人作为其移民身份的一项条件,被授权“随身份自动获得”就业授权。外国人可通过提交I-765表格《就业授权申请书》申请就业授权文件(EAD),USCIS将向已获得TPS并提交I-765表格的个人签发(a)(12)类别的就业授权文件。根据《联邦法规汇编》第8编第274a.12(c)(19)条,USCIS将向经其认定初步符合TPS资格条件、因而在申请待决期间获得临时待遇福利的TPS申请人,授予就业授权并签发(c)(19)类别的就业授权文件。《联邦法规汇编》第8编第244.5条与《移民与国籍法》第244(a)(4)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(4)条相呼应,规定在TPS申请裁决待决期间,为初步符合资格条件的申请人提供临时待遇福利,包括就业授权。自TPS制度设立以来,国土安全部一直将TPS受益人和初步符合资格的TPS申请人视为在指定期间及任何指定延长期间内持续获得就业授权,并通过《联邦公报》通知自动延长现有基于TPS的就业授权文件的有效期,通常为六个月或十二个月,以便在重新登记人等待其I-765表格获得裁决从而取得续签就业授权文件期间,持续提供就业授权的证明。[76]
DHS's regulations governing the regulations that provide the employment-authorization categories reflect the requirements of INA sec. 244(a), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a). Under 8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12), aliens granted TPS are authorized for employment “incident to status” as a condition of their immigration status. An alien may request an EAD by filing Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, and USCIS will issue EADs in category (a)(12) to individuals who have been granted TPS and file Form I-765. Under 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(19), USCIS will grant employment authorization and issue EADs in category (c)(19) to TPS applicants whom USCIS has determined are prima facie eligible for TPS and therefore receive temporary treatment benefits while their applications are pending. The regulation at 8 CFR 244.5 mirrors INA sec. 244(a)(4), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(4), by providing temporary treatment benefits, including employment authorization, for prima facie-eligible applicants during the pendency of adjudication of the TPS application. Since the inception of TPS, DHS has treated both TPS beneficiaries and prima facie-eligible TPS applicants as employment authorized continuously during the designation and through any designation extensions, using Federal Register notices to automatically extend the validity of existing TPS-based EADs—typically for six or twelve months—to provide continued evidence of employment authorization while re-registrants await adjudication of their Forms I-765 to obtain renewal EADs.[76]

三、《H.R.1法案》对《移民与国籍法》第244条下既往TPS就业授权期限实践具有管控效力并取代该等实践
3. H.R.1 Controls and Supersedes Prior TPS Employment Authorization Duration Practices Under INA Sec. 244
从文本上看,《美国法典》第8编第1803条(c)款及第1811条(a)款似乎与《移民与国籍法》(INA)第244(a)(2)条即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(2)条关于基于临时保护身份(TPS)的就业许可“在整个”TPS期间“有效”的表述存在冲突,因为前述条款对与TPS相关的就业许可规定了具体的一年或指定期限上限(以较短者为准),而第244(a)(2)条,即第1254a(a)(2)条,仅以身份为基础进行表述,并未规定具体期限。[77] 国土安全部(DHS)必须以同时忠实于新旧法律文本的方式,执行《H.R.1法案》对TPS相关就业许可期限所作的明确上限规定。
Textually, 8 U.S.C. 1803(c) and 8 U.S.C. 1811(a) appear to conflict with the phrasing of INA sec. 244(a)(2), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(2) that TPS-based employment authorization is “effective throughout” the period of TPS, because they impose a specific one-year-or-duration of designation cap on the TPS-related employment authorization, whereas section 244(a)(2), 1254a(a)(2), speaks in status-based terms without prescribing a duration.[77] DHS must implement H.R.1's explicit caps for TPS-related employment authorization periods in a manner faithful to both the new and previously existing statutory text.
《H.R.1法案》中的期限规定系后颁布的条款,直接针对基于TPS的就业许可的时间范围;该法案对任何一次授权或续期中就业许可可保持有效的时长设定了明确上限,即一年或指定期限(以较短者为准)。[78] 依据通常的解释工具,将《美国法典》第8编第1803条(c)款及第1811条(a)款与《移民与国籍法》第244条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a条一并解读,国土安全部通过为两部法律分配互补作用,使两者均得以实施：《移民与国籍法》第244条,即第1254a条,继续界定谁有资格获得就业许可(即依据8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12)条获得许可的TPS受益人,以及依据8 CFR 274a.12(c)(19)条及8 CFR 244.5条获得临时待遇利益的表面符合资格申请人),而《H.R.1法案》则为上述两类人员的每一次授权期限规定最长时限。[79]
H.R.1's duration provisions are later-enacted and speak directly to the temporal scope of TPS-based employment authorization; they impose an express ceiling—one year or the duration of the designation (whichever period is shorter)—on how long employment authorization may remain valid in any single grant or renewal.[78] Reading 8 U.S.C. 1803(c) and 8 U.S.C. 1811(a) together with INA sec. 244, 8 U.S.C. 1254a, under the ordinary tools of construction, DHS gives effect to both statutes by assigning them complementary roles: INA sec. 244, 8 U.S.C. 1254a, continues to define who is eligible to be employment-authorized (TPS beneficiaries under 8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12) and prima facie-eligible applicants receiving temporary treatment benefits under 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(19) and 8 CFR 244.5), while H.R.1 supplies the maximum duration for each period of authorization across both categories.[79]
在《移民与国籍法》第244(a)(2)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1254a(a)(2)条规定就业许可“在整个”TPS期间“有效”之处,国土安全部将其理解为:在TPS指定存续期间,具备获得许可资格的一项基于身份的权利,而非保证任何单次授权期限可超过《H.R.1法案》规定的具体上限。[80] 适用“后法优于前法”的解释原则,可在允许《移民与国籍法》第244条,即第1254a条所赋予的权利持续存在的同时,由《H.R.1法案》这一在后、更为具体的规定来规制每次授权可持续的时长,从而化解文本上的张力。[81] 这种协调一致的解读提供了一项清晰、可操作的规则,并与《H.R.1法案》将初次及续期费用与有时限的就业许可期限相挂钩的制度结构相契合。[82]
Where INA sec. 244(a)(2), 8 U.S.C. 1254a(a)(2), states that employment authorization is “effective throughout” TPS, DHS reads that as a status-based entitlement to be eligible for authorization during the life of the TPS designation, not as a guarantee that any single authorization period may exceed H.R.1's specific limit.[80] Applying the later-in-time canon resolves the textual tension by allowing the INA sec. 244, 8 U.S.C. 1254a entitlement to persist while H.R.1's later, more specific command regulates how long each authorization may run.[81] This harmonized reading provides a clear, administrable rule that aligns with H.R.1's structure of initial and renewal fees tied to time-limited employment authorization periods.[82]
国土安全部正在执行《H.R.1法案》规定的就业许可期限上限,要求对8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12)条下的受益人及8 CFR 274a.12(a)(19)条下的表面符合资格申请人,均须按一年一次或按TPS指定剩余期限(以较短者为准)的间隔续期其TPS就业许可。[83] 国土安全部认识到,在TPS指定期限超过12个月的情况下,这一续期要求可能产生的影响是,部分TPS受益人或表面符合资格申请人在获得就业许可续期之前,可能面临就业许可出现中断并暂时失业的风险。就目前而言,现行TPS指定期限尚不会使TPS受益人及申请人面临就业许可出现中断的风险。[84] 此外,在TPS指定被自动延长6个月的情形下,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)已自动延长了就业许可文件(EAD)的有效期,以防止就业许可出现中断。若未来的TPS指定、重新指定或延期设定为超过12个月,则希望避免就业许可及EAD有效期出现中断的申请人,须及时提交续期申请,以在指定或延期的剩余期间内维持就业许可,且每次续期仅可按照《H.R.1法案》所允许的最长期限获得批准,即一年或TPS指定剩余期限,以较短者为准。[85] 通过要求TPS受益人及表面符合资格申请人定期续期其就业许可申请,该做法为身份核验、资格审查及安全审查提供了可预见的检查节点。[86] 这有助于确保外国人不会持有基于TPS、但其所依据的TPS指定已不复存在的、表面有效的EAD,包括在指定临近结束或被终止的情形下。[87] 任何证明就业许可的文件(例如EAD)均须反映且不得超过《H.R.1法案》所确定的基础授权期限,从而使文件的有效期与其所证明的法律授权保持一致。[88]
DHS is enforcing the H.R.1 employment authorization period limits by requiring that TPS employment authorization be renewed at intervals of one year—or the remaining TPS designation, if shorter—for both 8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12) beneficiaries and 8 CFR 274a.12(a)(19) prima facie-eligible applicants.[83] DHS recognizes that, where a TPS designation is longer than 12 months, a possible impact of this renewal requirement may be that some TPS beneficiaries or prima facie-eligibility applicants could face gaps in employment authorization and suffer temporary job loss until they receive a renewal of employment authorization. At present, current TPS designation timeframes do not place TPS beneficiaries and applicants at risk of experiencing gaps in employment authorization.[84] In addition, in instances where TPS designations have been automatically extended for 6 months, USCIS has automatically extended EADs in order to prevent gaps in employment authorization. Should future TPS designations, redesignations, or extensions be set for more than 12 months, applicants seeking to avoid gaps in employment authorization and EAD validity would need to file timely renewals to maintain employment authorization for the remainder of the designation or extension, and each renewal will be approved only for the maximum period permitted under H.R.1—that is, for one year or the remaining duration of the TPS designation, whichever is shorter.[85] By requiring TPS beneficiaries and prima facie eligible applicants to regularly renew their requests for employment authorization, this approach provides predictable checkpoints for identity, eligibility, and security vetting.[86] It helps ensure that aliens do not possess facially-valid EADs based on TPS when the underlying TPS designations no longer exist, including when a designation nears conclusion or is terminated.[87] Any document evidencing employment authorization ( e.g., an EAD) must reflect—and may not exceed—the underlying authorization period established by H.R.1, so that documentary validity and the legal authorization it evidences remain aligned.[88]

F. Fee Waivers and Exemptions
DHS在本规则中不对8 CFR第106部分中的任何费用减免和豁免作出变更。《H.R.1法案》征收的费用不得豁免或减免。尽管《移民与国籍法》(INA)第245(l)(7)条,即8 U.S.C. 1255(l)(7)条,要求DHS允许就某些移民福利申请所需费用提出豁免请求,但《H.R.1法案》取代了该要求。因此,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)不得豁免《H.R.1法案》所要求的此类费用,亦不得提交此类豁免请求。无法豁免《H.R.1法案》费用并不要求对8 CFR第103和106部分的DHS费用法规作出任何变更。8 CFR 106.3(a)(3)条中的费用豁免和减免规定列出了可豁免或可减免的USCIS费用,由于这些规定保持不变,《H.R.1法案》费用既未被明示也未被默示纳入可豁免或可减免的费用范围。此外,8 CFR 106.3(c)条授权的公共利益费用豁免和减免,必须与适用法律保持一致。因此,该条款不授权豁免缴纳《H.R.1法案》费用,亦无需作出任何修订或澄清。
DHS is not changing any fee exemptions and fee waivers in 8 CFR part 106 in this rule. Fees imposed by HR-1 cannot be waived or reduced. While INA section 245(l)(7), 8 U.S.C. 1255(l)(7) requires DHS to allow a request for waiver of the fees required for certain immigration benefit requests, H.R.1 supersedes that requirement. Therefore, USCIS cannot waive such a fee required by H.R.1 and a request for such may not be submitted. The inability to waive an H.R.1 fee requires no changes to the DHS fee regulations at 8 CFR parts 103 and 106. Fee waivers and exemptions in 8 CFR 106.3(a)(3) list the USCIS fees that may be waived or are exempt, and by leaving those provisions unchanged, H.R.1 fees are neither explicitly nor implicitly included as fees that may be waived or exempt. In addition, public interest fee waivers and exemptions authorized by 8 CFR 106.3(c) must be consistent with the applicable law. Thus, that provision does not authorize relief from the payment of H.R.1 fees and no amendment or clarification is required.
8 CFR第106部分提供的费用豁免不适用于本规则中编纂的新费用。因此,例如,8 CFR 106.2(a)(7)(v)和(vi)条为USCIS I-131表格提供的费用豁免,适用于I-131表格的基本USCIS费用,而因被准予假释入境美国而产生的《H.R.1法案》费用仍须缴纳。
The fee exemptions provided by 8 CFR part 106 do not apply to the new fees codified in this rule. Thus, for example, the fee exemptions provided for USCIS Form I-131 by 8 CFR 106.2(a)(7)(v) and (vi) apply to the base USCIS fee for Form I-131, while the H.R.1 fee for being paroled into the United States is still required.

1. 法定要求的变更
1. Statutorily Required Changes
正如本序言其他部分所述,国土安全部(DHS)正在使其法规与赋予该机构在解释和颁布方面极小自由裁量权的法律变更保持一致。当法规仅是重申其所实施的制定法时(即,该规则不改变既定的法律秩序),《行政程序法》(APA)并不要求该机构采用通知和评议程序。参见5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B);Gray Panthers Advocacy Comm. 诉 Sullivan案,936 F.2d 1284, 1291(哥伦比亚特区巡回上诉法院,1991年)。只要该机构不扩大制定法的实质性范围以施加新的义务、处罚或实质性资格要求,即,只要该机构“仅仅是重申”制定法,通知和评议即属不必要。参见World Duty Free Americas, Inc. 诉 Summers案,94 F. Supp. 2d 61, 65(哥伦比亚特区地区法院,2000年)。
As noted elsewhere in the preamble, DHS is conforming its regulations to statutory changes that provide little agency discretion in its interpretation and promulgation. When regulations merely restate the statute they implement ( i.e., when the rule does not change the established legal order), the APA does not require the agency to use notice-and-comment procedures. See5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B); Gray Panthers Advocacy Comm. v. Sullivan, 936 F.2d 1284, 1291 (D.C. Cir. 1991). So long as the agency does not expand the substantive reach of the statute to impose new obligations, penalties, or substantive eligibility requirements— i.e., so long as the agency “merely restate[s]” the statute—notice and comment are unnecessary. See World Duty Free Americas, Inc. v. Summers, 94 F. Supp. 2d 61, 65 (D.D.C. 2000).
此外,法院一贯认定,根据《行政程序法》关于通知和评议的豁免规定,当延误将不切实际、无必要或有悖于公共利益时,即存在“正当理由”。[89]当采用制定规则程序对该行业而言无关紧要且该规则属于常规性质时,制定规则程序即被视为“无必要”。[90]法院认定,例如,当规则的废止或变更是为了与立法或司法裁决保持一致,且对该机构行动方针不存在公众辩论余地时,即满足“无必要”这一条件。[91]
In addition, courts have consistently held that “good cause” under the APA's notice-and-comment exemption exists where delay would be impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest.[89] Rulemaking procedures are deemed “unnecessary” when the use of rulemaking would be inconsequential to the industry and the rule is routine.[90] Courts have found that the unnecessary prong is satisfied if, for example, the rescission or change of a rule is needed for consistency with legislation or judicial decision and there is no room for public debate over the agency's course of action.[91]

《H.R.1法案》于2025年7月4日签署成为法律,规定了国土安全部在2025财政年度收取新的法定费用、限制就业许可文件(EAD)有效期限并执行此类费用之强制要求。[92]本最终规则既将支付和收取费用的法定要求成文化,又规定此类费用不得豁免或减免。国土安全部承认新费用对受影响各方具有实质性影响,但国土安全部别无裁量余地,必须施加这些费用,并必须规定其实施方式、相关程序以及未能遵守的后果。[93]《H.R.1法案》自颁布之日起即生效,国土安全部必须通过为申请人及美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)提供支付、收取费用以及未付费情形的程序来实施该法。[94]
H.R.1, signed into law on July 4, 2025, sets forth the requirement that DHS collect new statutory fees during FY 2025, limit the length of EADs, and execute enforcement of such fees.[92] This final rule both codifies statutory requirements to pay and collect fees, and provides that the fees cannot be waived or reduced. 因此,美国国土安全部(DHS)认定有正当理由以临时最终规则(IFR)的形式发布本规则。为满足国会的指令并符合公共利益,立即生效实属必要。尽管《H.R.1法案》规定了这些要求,美国国土安全部仍认识到公众意见的价值,因此以临时最终规则的形式发布本规则,并征求公众意见。美国国土安全部拟发布最终规则,并将在制定该规则以及发布与《H.R.1法案》相关的指导意见时,考虑在联系方式部分所述的公众意见征询期内提交的所有及时意见。
Accordingly, DHS finds good cause to issue this rule as an IFR. Immediate effect is necessary to meet Congress's directive and in the public interest. Although H.R.1 prescribes these requirements, DHS recognizes the value of public comments and is publishing this rule as an IFR with a request for public comment. DHS intends to publish a final rule and will consider all timely comments submitted during the public comment period as described in the Addresses section in developing that rule as well as in issuing guidance related to H.R.1.

1. 基准
1. Baseline
一般而言,规则制定始于对需要解决的问题的阐述,以及对经修订法规将通过何种机制解决相关问题,并相对于适当基准产生何种后续影响的分析。基准是指在没有该监管行动的情况下,世界将呈现的最佳评估状态,即基准衡量的是世界当前的状态。美国国土安全部相对于该基准评估监管行动的收益和成本。在本规则中,美国国土安全部正在更新《联邦法规汇编》(CFR),以将《H.R.1法案》所要求的细节和变更予以编纂成文。因此,本临时最终规则的适当基准是一个法定基准,例如本案中的《H.R.1法案》,我们可据此评估本规则相对于现行既有法律的经济影响。
Generally, rulemaking begins with the articulation of a problem that needs to be solved and analysis of the mechanisms by which the amended regulations would solve the relevant issues and what the resulting impacts would be relative to the appropriate baseline. A baseline is the best assessment of the way the world would look absent the regulatory action, i.e., a baseline measures the current state of the world. DHS assesses the benefits and costs of a regulatory action relative to the baseline. In this rule, DHS is updating the CFR to codify the details and changes required in H.R.1. Therefore, the proper baseline for this IFR is a statutory baseline, such as H.R.1 in this case, from which we can assess the economic impact of the rule relative to current, existing law.

4. TPS Employment Authorization Validity required by 8 U.S.C. 1803(c) and 8 U.S.C. 1811(a): Limits work authorization and any associated employment authorization document under TPS to one year, or the remaining period of designation if shorter, with conforming changes to ensure consistency across DHS regulations. New 8 CFR 274a.12(a)(12) and 274.12(c)(19).
共有4项明确要求由法律规定。但是,国土安全部(DHS)在实施第1至3项时行使有限的自由裁量权,以制定此类实施所需的程序性变更。因此,本分析讨论了国土安全部如何实施H.R.1要求的I-102表格费用(用于补发I-94表格)的影响、未缴纳年度庇护费(AAF)的后果,以及I-589表格被拒后保留庇护申请费的影响。

Therefore, this analysis discusses the impacts of how DHS implements the Form I-102 fees (for a replacement Form I-94 as required by H.R.1), the consequences of failure to pay the AAF, and the retention of the asylum application fee after Form I-589 rejection.

3. 本临时最终规则的背景和目的
3. Background and Purpose of the IFR
《H.R.1法案》于2025年7月4日签署成为法律,规定了由多个政府机构管理的各类移民相关表格、福利、身份、申请、请愿及请求所需缴纳的特定费用。为推进《H.R.1法案》条文的实施,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)和国土安全部(DHS)发布了多份联邦公报通知(FRN)(第90联邦公报34511页(2025年7月22日)、第90联邦公报42025页(2025年8月28日)、第90联邦公报43223页(2025年9月8日)以及第90联邦公报48317页(2025年10月16日)),公布了由USCIS管理的部分新费用,包括适用对象、新费用生效时间、缴费说明、豁免条件(如有),以及未缴费的后果。本临时最终规则(IFR)旨在在前述联邦公报通知的基础上进一步扩展,并将《H.R.1法案》的法定要求予以编纂成规,本监管影响分析(RIA)对本监管行动的影响进行了分析。
H.R.1 was signed into law on July 4, 2025, and mandated specific fees for various immigration-related forms, benefits, statuses, petitions, applications, and requests administered by multiple government agencies. In furtherance of enacting the text of H.R.1, USCIS and DHS published multiple FRNs (90 FR 34511 (July 22, 2025), 90 FR 42025 (Aug. 28, 2025), 90 FR 43223 (Sept. 8, 2025), and 90 FR 48317 (Oct. 16, 2025)) announcing some of the new fees that are administered by USCIS, to whom those fees apply, when the new fees take effect, instructions on their payment, when and if the fees may be waived, and consequences of the failure to pay. The purpose of this IFR is to expand upon the prior FRNs and codify the statutory requirements of H.R.1, and this Regulatory Impact Analysis (RIA) analyzes the impacts of this regulatory action.

5. 成本效益分析
5. Cost-Benefit Analysis
上述背景和人群部分描述了受本规则影响的人群。在大多数情况下,对这些人群的影响直接源于国会在《H.R.1法案》中明确规定的变更,因此不被视为本次制定规则所产生的影响。然而,在其他情况下,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)寻求通过颁布本临时最终规则(IFR),对《H.R.1法案》中国会指示含糊不清或缺失的部分予以明确规定。以下描述了相关成本、效益、成本节约以及转移支付(无论是定量还是定性的)。
The background and population sections above describe the populations affected by this rule. In most instances, the impacts to these populations come directly from changes Congress articulated in H.R.1 and therefore are not considered impacts from this rulemaking. In other instances, however, USCIS seeks to codify aspects of H.R.1 where congressional direction was ambiguous or missing through the promulgation of this IFR. The costs, benefits, cost savings, and transfers (whether quantitative or qualitative) are described below.
一、成本
i. Costs
本临时最终规则(IFR)的某些方面可能带来定性成本。未能及时(30天内)缴纳庇护申请费(AAF)的无合法身份外国人,其I-589表将被驳回,并将被置于加速遣返程序或签发出庭通知书(NTA)。这必然需要耗费时间参与上述程序,但由于每个个案程序均具有具体事实特性,USCIS无法恰当评估实际成本。
Aspects of this IFR may impose qualitative costs. Aliens without lawful status who fail to pay the AAF in a timely manner (within 30 days) will have their Form I-589 rejected and will either be placed in expedited removal proceedings or will be issued an NTA. This necessarily entails time engaging in those processes, though USCIS cannot properly assess the actual costs given the fact-specific nature of each individual proceeding.
此外,未能及时(30天内)缴纳庇护申请费(AAF)的外国人可能会面临工作许可中断,因此外国人本人及假设中的雇主都将遭受经济损失(外国人表现为工资损失,雇主表现为生产力损失)。同样,部分临时保护身份(TPS)受益人的EAD有效期可能因H.R.1法案而被缩短。这将导致经济损失(受影响外国人表现为工资损失,受影响雇主表现为生产力损失)。这些影响具有具体事实特性(包括(见原文第22968页)程序的持续时间和性质、外国人的工资损失、雇主的生产力损失等)。
Furthermore, aliens who fail to pay the AAF in a timely manner (within 30 days) could experience disruptions to employment authorization and therefore, both the alien and a hypothetical employer would experience economic losses (in the form of lost wages for the alien and lost productivity for the employer). Similarly, the EAD validity period for some TPS recipients may have been shortened by H.R.1. This would entail economic losses (in the form of lost wages for the affected alien and lost productivity for an affected employer). These impacts are fact specific (including the duration and nature of ( printed page 22968) proceedings, lost wages for aliens, lost productivity for employers, etc.).
对特定外国人加速遣返程序使用比例的相对增加,也可能因强制拘留时间延长及若被遣返所产生的运输成本增加,而提高受影响人群的成本。受影响的外国人将因拘留时间延长而遭受工资损失(受上述工资范围约束)。政府在此情形下也将面临更高成本,因为强制拘留的普遍适用需要增加基础设施、管理及监督成本。加速遣返使用的增加也可能提高运输成本。国土安全部(DHS)无法准确估算这些成本,因为其缺乏关于可能受影响人群及庇护申请费不合规比率的充分数据。
The relative increase in the usage of expedited removal (for certain aliens) may also increase costs to affected populations because of the additional time in mandatory detention and transportation costs if removed. Affected aliens will realize costs due to lost wages due to additional time in detention (subject to the wage ranges discussed above). The government will also face increased costs in this instance because of increased infrastructure, administration, and oversight costs required by the increased prevalence of mandatory detention. Increased use of expedited removal may also increase transportation costs. DHS cannot accurately estimate these costs because it lacks sufficient data regarding the possibly affected population and rate of non-compliance with the AAF.
此外,国土安全部预计外国人(在某些情况下为其代理人)将需要阅读并理解本规则,以便成功理解监管变化并作出相应回应。因此,我们预计本规则将产生与阅读本规则相关的熟悉成本。
Additionally, DHS expects that aliens (or their representatives in some circumstances) will need to read and understand this rule in order to successfully understand and be responsive to the regulatory changes. As a result, we expect this rule will impose familiarization costs associated with reading this rule.
为估算规则熟悉成本,我们按每分钟250个单词的阅读速度估算阅读并理解本临时最终规则所需的时间。[105]本规则约有17,000个单词。按每分钟250个单词的阅读速度计算,国土安全部估计阅读并理解本规则大约需要1.13小时。
To estimate the costs of rule familiarization, we estimate the time it would take to read and understand the IFR by assuming a reading speed of 250 words per minute.[105] This rule has approximately 17,000 words. Using a reading speed of 250 words per minute, DHS estimates it will take approximately 1.13 hours to read and understand this rule.
为正确计算这些成本,USCIS必须考虑受影响人群的时间机会成本,这需要有关相关工资的信息。联邦最低工资目前为每小时7.25美元,[106]但许多州已实施更高的最低工资标准。[107]然而,联邦政府并未跟踪全国范围内按人口加权的最低工资估计值。相关分析所涉人群中的个人可能位于美国境内任何地方,并可能因其所居住的州或城市不同而适用不同范围的最低工资标准。
To properly calculate these costs, USCIS must account for the opportunity cost of time for the affected population, which requires information regarding the relevant wages. The Federal minimum wage is currently $7.25 per hour,[106] but many states have implemented higher minimum wage rates.[107] However, the Federal Government does not track a nationwide population-weighted minimum wage estimate. Individuals in the population of interest for an analysis could be located anywhere within the United States and may be subject to a range of minimum wage rates depending on the state or city in which they live.
就本临时最终规则而言,国土安全部使用美国劳工部(DOL)劳工统计局(BLS)全国职业就业及工资估算中的最新工资数据。更具体而言,在估算可能获得入门级工资的相关人群的时间机会成本时,我们使用所有职业第10百分位小时工资估计值作为有效最低工资的合理替代指标。[108]对于失业人员,或无法或选择不参与劳动力市场的个人,我们同样使用第10百分位小时工资估计值,因为这些个人同样承担机会成本,或在决定如何分配其时间时对时间进行价值评估,或两者兼有。
For this IFR, DHS uses the most recent wage data from the U.S. Department of Labor (DOL), Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), National Occupational Employment and Wage Estimates. More specifically, we use the 10th percentile hourly wage estimate for all occupations as a reasonable proxy for the effective minimum wage when estimating the opportunity cost of time for individuals in populations of interest who are likely to earn an entry-level wage.[108] We also use the 10th percentile hourly wage estimate for individuals who are unemployed, or for individuals who cannot, or choose not to, participate in the labor market, as these individuals incur opportunity costs, assign valuation in deciding how to allocate their time, or both.
由于个人可从事的非付薪活动种类繁多,例如照顾儿童、家务劳动或其他没有报酬的活动,因此很难估算该时间的价值,而且即使个人并非为获取工资而工作,其时间仍然具有价值。此外,使用所有职业时薪估算值的某个百分位数,使国土安全部(DHS)能够根据监管影响分析所关注人群的不同,在必要时灵活调整其估算值。而且,劳工统计局(BLS)的估算值考虑了美国劳动力市场工资的变化,其中包括各州最低工资标准未来可能发生的任何变化。
Due to the wide variety of non-paid activities an individual could pursue, such as childcare, housework, or other activities without paid compensation, it is difficult to estimate the value of that time, and even when an individual is not working for wages, their time still has value. In addition, using a percentile of the hourly wage estimate for all occupations allows DHS the flexibility to adjust its estimates, when necessary, depending on the population(s) of interest for regulatory impact analyses. Moreover, BLS estimates account for changes in wages across the United States labor market, which includes any future changes to state minimum wage rates.
此外,国土安全部不排除部分人群可能获得更高工资的可能性。鉴于国土安全部缺乏有关受影响人群的详细信息,采用一个工资区间来计算本临时最终规则各项规定对受影响人群造成的成本是合理的。前述第10百分位工资作为受影响人群的下限,而国土安全部则以劳工统计局的平均工资作为上限。国土安全部将在其监管影响分析中持续评估最适合相关人群的工资假设。
Furthermore, DHS does not rule out the possibility that some portion of the population might earn higher wages. Given that DHS lacks detailed information on the affected population, it is reasonable to use a range of wages to calculate the cost of the IFR's provisions to the affected populations. The aforementioned 10th percentile wage serves as the lower for the affected population, while DHS utilizes the mean wage from BLS as the upper bound. DHS will continue to evaluate the most appropriate wage assumptions for the populations of interest in its regulatory impact analyses.
目前,所有职业的第10百分位时薪估算值为14.42美元,未计入员工福利;而所有职业的平均时薪估算值为32.66美元。[109] 此外,国土安全部认识到,受影响的外国人在其移民程序中可能已选择聘请律师或经认可的代表。劳工统计局估计律师的平均时薪为87.86美元。
The 10th percentile hourly wage estimate for all occupations is currently $14.42, not accounting for worker benefits while the mean hourly wage estimate for all occupations is $32.66.[109] Furthermore, DHS recognizes that affected aliens may have chosen to hire a lawyer or accredited representative during their immigration proceedings. The BLS estimates that the average hourly wage for a lawyer is $87.86.
国土安全部在估算时间机会成本时,还必须通过计算福利与工资的乘数来考虑员工福利。福利与工资乘数是利用劳工统计局最新报告中关于美国所有平民劳动者平均员工总薪酬的数据计算得出的。[110] 国土安全部估计福利与工资乘数为1.46,该乘数包含员工工资薪金以及福利的全部成本,例如带薪休假、保险和退休金。[111] 因此,利用该福利与工资乘数,国土安全部计算出本临时最终规则中个人总薪酬的下限为每小时21.05美元,其中第10百分位时薪估算值为每小时14.42美元,平均福利为每小时6.63美元。[112] 同样,总薪酬的上限为47.68美元,其中平均工资为每小时32.66美元,平均福利为每小时15.02美元。[113] 此外,国土安全部估计律师的平均总时薪为128.28美元。[114]
DHS also must account for worker benefits when estimating the opportunity cost of time by calculating a benefits-to-wage multiplier. The benefits-to-wage multiplier is calculated using the most recent BLS report detailing average total employee compensation for all civilian U.S. workers.[110] DHS estimates the benefits-to-wage multiplier to be 1.46, which incorporates employee wages and salaries and the full cost of benefits, such as paid leave, insurance, and retirement.[111] Therefore, using the benefits-to-wage multiplier, DHS calculates the lower bound of total compensation for individuals as $21.05 per hour for this IFR, where the 10th percentile hourly wage estimate is $14.42 per hour and the average benefits are $6.63 per hour.[112] Similarly, the upper bound for total compensation is $47.68, where the mean wage is $32.66 per hour and the average benefits are $15.02 per hour.[113] Similarly, DHS estimates the average total hourly compensation for a lawyer is $128.28.[114]
如上所述,获得第10百分位工资的外国人的时薪总薪酬为21.05美元,获得平均工资的外国人的时薪总薪酬为47.68美元,律师的时薪总薪酬为128.28美元。因此,对于非在职申请人而言,每类申请人阅读并理解本规则所需时间的估计机会成本,对于获得第10百分位工资的外国人约为23.79美元,对于获得平均工资的外国人约为53.88美元,对于律师则约为144.96美元。[115]
As discussed above, the hourly total compensation for aliens earning the 10th percentile wage is $21.05, the total hourly compensation of aliens earning the average wage is $47.68, and total hourly compensation for lawyers is $128.28. Therefore, the estimated opportunity cost of time for each type of applicant to read and understand the rule for non-working petitioners is approximately $23.79 for aliens earning the 10th percentile wage, $53.88 for aliens earning the average wage, and $144.96 for lawyers.[115]

二、益处
ii. Benefits
国土安全部认为,本规则有若干益处。首先,本临时最终规则通过提高联邦政府合理分配稀缺资源的能力而使联邦政府受益。例如,由于本规则规定未缴纳庇护申请费(AAF)的后果是驳回待决申请,这可能减少美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)须处理至批准或拒绝阶段的庇护申请数量。通过减少待决庇护申请的数量,因未缴纳庇护申请费而被驳回的情形也将减少庇护申请人或其附带申请人提交I-765表格《工作许可申请书》的数量。美国公民及移民服务局对庇护申请人提交的I-765表格免费处理。[116] 因此,由于在一定程度上很可能出现未缴纳庇护申请费的情况,美国公民及移民服务局将因能够把处理资源从本规则生效后将被驳回的I-589表格和I-765表格庇护申请人转移到按要求缴纳庇护申请费的申请人身上而受益。这种资源转移将有助于美国公民及移民服务局更好地分配稀缺资源。
DHS believes that there are several benefits to this rule. First, this IFR benefits the Federal Government by improving the ability to allocate scarce resources. Because the consequence for failure to pay the AAF in this rule is rejection of the pending request, for instance, it could reduce the number of asylum applications USCIS must process to approval or denial. By reducing the number of pending asylum applications, rejection for failure to pay the AAF will also reduce filings of USCIS Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, from an applicant for asylum or their derivatives. USCIS processes a Form I-765 from asylum applicants for free.[116] Thus, because some level of nonpayment of the AAF is quite likely, USCIS will benefit by being able to shift resources from processing Forms I-589 and I-765 for asylum applicants who will be rejected after this rule takes effect to requests from those who pay their AAF as required. Such a shift will facilitate better allocation of scarce resources for USCIS.
该临时最终规则的另一项重要益处是全面加强移民体系。因未缴纳庇护申请费而驳回申请,以及在表格被驳回时保留I-589表格申请费,通过确保不合规行为导致迅速且可预见的后果,推进了H.R.1法案的威慑与执法目标,这与国土安全部(DHS)执行移民法律并确保国家边境安全的使命相一致。
Another important benefit of the IFR is the general strengthening of the immigration system. Rejection for failure to pay the AAF and the retention of the Form I-589 filing fee if the form is rejected advances H.R.1's deterrence and enforcement objectives by ensuring that noncompliance results in swift and predictable consequences consistent with DHS' mission to enforce the immigration laws and ensure the security of the Nation's borders.
四、转移支付
iv. Transfers
本临时最终规则很可能会对不同群体之间的转移支付产生影响。其中部分转移可以量化,另一些则只能作定性描述。
This IFR will likely impact transfer payments between various populations. Some of these transfers can be quantified, while others can be described only qualitatively.
甲、定量转移支付
a. Quantitative Transfers
本临时最终规则将下列规定法典化:I-102表格的申请必须包含对任何“提交I-94表格申请”的外国人依法规定征收的24美元费用。因此,预计每年约有4,120名I-102表格申请人(见表4)将被要求在I-102表格本身所需费用之外缴纳此项费用。因此,根据本规则,受影响外国人向联邦政府转移支付的年度金额预计为每年98,880美元。[117] 在整个分析期内,受影响外国人向联邦政府转移支付的总额预计为988,800美元。[118]
This IFR codifies that Form I-102 filings must include the statutorily mandated $24 fee for any alien who “submits an application for a Form I-94”. As such, an estimated 4,120 Form I-102 filers per year (see Table 4) will be required to submit this fee in addition to any fees required for Form I-102 itself. Therefore, annual transfers from affected aliens to the Federal Government under the rule are estimated to be $98,880 per year.[117] Total transfers from affected aliens to the Federal Government are estimated to be $988,800 over the entire analysis period.[118]
乙、定性转移支付
b. Qualitative Transfers
本临时最终规则将以下规定法典化:如I-589表格被驳回,则H.R.1法案要求缴纳的庇护申请费将由联邦政府予以保留。保留此类费用的决定确实构成了从庇护申请人向联邦政府的转移支付。该规则本身并未创设或改变此类转移支付,而仅是澄清了在申请被驳回情况下此类费用的处理方式。尽管申请费通常被视为转移支付,因为美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)设定费用的目的在于合理覆盖与该表格相关的裁决成本和行政负担,但保留庇护申请费本身构成一种转移支付,因为无论申请是否被受理,该转移均会发生。在这种情况下,本临时最终规则的规定推进了H.R.1法案将移民执法与监管成本从纳税人转移至外国人身上的目标。由于庇护申请费的实施属于新措施,且缺乏足够信息以估算不合规率或包含庇护申请费的被驳回申请的比率,美国公民及移民服务局无法可靠地量化这些转移支付的金额。[119]
This IFR codifies that the asylum application fee required by H.R.1 will be retained by the Federal Government if a Form I-589 is rejected. The decision to retain such fees does represent a transfer from asylum applicants to the Federal Government. The rule itself does not create or change such transfers, but only clarifies their treatment in the case of rejected applications. While filing fees are typically thought of as transfers since USCIS sets fees so that they properly cover the cost of adjudication and administrative burdens associated with the form, the retention of the asylum application fee represents a transfer because the transfer occurs irrespective of the filing's acceptance. In this instance, the IFR's provision furthers H.R.1's goal of shifting immigration enforcement and oversight costs from taxpayers to aliens. USCIS cannot reliably quantify the amount of these transfers since the implementation of the asylum application fee is new and there is a lack of information to estimate the rate of noncompliance or the rate of rejected filings that would include the asylum application fee.[119]
六、所考虑的备选方案
6. Alternatives Considered
如上文所述,本规则主要用于将国会通过H.R.1法案所规定的变更予以法典化。因此,本规则总体而言并不代表美国公民及移民服务局行使自由裁量权以修订《联邦法规汇编》的独立行动。
As discussed above, this rule largely serves to codify the changes that Congress dictated by the passage of H.R.1. Therefore, this rule generally does not represent an independent action by USCIS to assert its discretion to amend the CFR.
如上文所述,国土安全部对本临时最终规则所述程序性规定评估了若干备选方案。对每一方案的评估均旨在确保该规则体现对H.R.1法案和《移民与国籍法》(INA)的最佳解释,并满足《行政程序法》(APA)关于合理决策的要求。[120] 基于本分析中所述理由,国土安全部认定,所选定的做法,即将I-94表格费用适用于I-102表格、因未缴费而驳回I-589表格并在适用情况下导致出庭通知书(NTA)或快速遣返程序以及丧失工作许可、在申请被驳回时保留庇护申请费,以及统一特定临时保护身份(TPS)申请人和受益人的工作许可文件(EAD)有效期,最能实现H.R.1法案的执法与威慑目的,并且在行政管理上优于下文所考虑的备选方案。
As discussed above, DHS evaluated several alternatives to the procedural provisions described in this IFR. Each option was evaluated with the goal of ensuring that the rule reflects the best interpretation of H.R.1 and the INA and satisfies the APA's requirement for reasoned decisionmaking.[120] For the reasons explained in this analysis, DHS concluded that the selected approaches—applicability of the Form I-94 fee to the Form I-102, rejection of a nonpaying I-589 resulting in an NTA or expedited removal where applicable and the loss of employment authorization, retention of the asylum application fees in the event of rejection, and harmonization of EAD validity periods for certain TPS applicants and recipients—best effectuate H.R.1's enforcement and deterrence purposes and are administratively superior to the following alternatives considered.
丙、《管制灵活性法》(认证)
C. Regulatory Flexibility Act (Certification)
经《1996年小企业管制执行公平法》修订的《1980年管制灵活性法》(RFA)要求联邦机构在制定规则过程中考虑法规对小企业、小型政府辖区和小型组织可能产生的影响。“小实体”一词包括小企业、独立拥有和经营且在其所属领域不占主导地位的非营利组织,以及人口少于50,000人的政府辖区。[121]
The Regulatory Flexibility Act of 1980 (RFA), as amended by the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act of 1996, requires Federal agencies to consider the potential impact of regulations on small businesses, small governmental jurisdictions, and small organizations during the development of their rules. The term “small entities” comprises small businesses, not-for-profit organizations that are independently owned and operated and are not dominant in their fields, and governmental jurisdictions with populations of less than 50,000.[121]
该临时最终规则(IFR)不直接规范小实体,预计也不会对小实体产生直接影响。相反,本临时最终规则规范的是个人,而根据《规制灵活性法案》(RFA),个人并不被定义为“小实体”。尽管部分雇主可能会承担成本或产生转移效应,但这些影响属于间接影响。如前所述,本临时最终规则的不作为基线已包含《H.R.1》所规定的费用,因此由这些费用引起的任何行为反应均不应归因于本临时最终规则。例如,根据(a)(12)或(c)(19)类别申请I-765表格的外国人数量如有任何减少,应归因于《H.R.1》,而非本临时最终规则。无论如何,本规则不会影响雇佣符合资格的外国人的能力,也不会影响就业授权的实际资格标准(第22970页)。因此,本规则对小实体的影响将是微不足道的。
The IFR does not directly regulate small entities and is not expected to have a direct effect on small entities. Rather, this IFR regulates individuals, and individuals are not defined as “small entities” by the RFA. While some employers could experience costs or transfer effects, these impacts would be indirect. As discussed previously, the no-action baseline for this IFR includes the fees mandated by H.R.1 so any behavioral response from those fees would not be attributable to this IFR. For instance, any reduction in the number of aliens requesting Forms I-765 under either (a)(12) or (c)(19) classifications would be attributable to H.R.1 and not to this IFR. In any case, this rule would not impact the ability to employ eligible aliens and also would not impact the actual eligibility criteria ( printed page 22970) for employment authorization. As such, the impact of this rule on small entities will be negligible.
基于本分析及本序言通篇所提供的证据,美国国土安全部(DHS)证明本临时最终规则不会对大量小实体产生重大经济影响。尽管如此,美国国土安全部欢迎就本规则对小实体可能产生的影响提出意见,并可能在最终规则中酌情予以考虑。
Based on the evidence presented in this analysis and throughout this preamble, DHS certifies that this IFR would not have a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities. DHS nonetheless welcomes comments regarding potential impacts on small entities, which DHS may consider as appropriate in a final rule.
D. 1995年《不provided资金授权改革法案》
D. Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995
《1995年不provided资金授权改革法案》(UMRA)旨在遏制向州、地方和部落政府施加未提供资金的联邦授权的做法。UMRA第二章要求每个联邦机构就拟议规则或已就其发布拟议规则的最终规则中所含任何联邦授权的影响编写书面声明,如该联邦授权可能导致州、地方和部落政府合计或私营部门在任何一年内支出1亿美元或以上(每年根据通货膨胀进行调整)。[122]
The Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995 (UMRA) is intended, among other things, to curb the practice of imposing unfunded Federal mandates on State, local, and Tribal governments. Title II of UMRA requires each Federal agency to prepare a written statement assessing the effects of any Federal mandate in a proposed rule, or final rule for which the agency published a proposed rule, that includes any Federal mandate that may result in a $100 million or more expenditure (adjusted annually for inflation) in any one year by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector.[122]
