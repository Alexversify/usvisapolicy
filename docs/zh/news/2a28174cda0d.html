国土安全部豁免环保等多项法律以加快得克萨斯州边境墙建设

发布日期 2026年7月27日生效日期 2026年7月27日

国土安全部长根据《非法移民改革和移民责任法》第102条确定,有必要豁免多项联邦法律,以便在得克萨斯州大本德和德尔里奥边境巡逻区内的指定区域加快建设隔离墙和道路。该豁免决定于2026年7月27日生效,涵盖《国家环境政策法》《濒危物种法》《清洁水法》等法律。

要点

  • 国土安全部援引IIRIRA第102(c)条,对得克萨斯州特定项目区域豁免相关法律要求。
  • 项目区域位于大本德和德尔里奥边境巡逻区内两个GPS坐标之间。
  • 此次建设豁免涉及数十项环境保护、历史保护和土地管理相关法律。
  • 该决定不涉及任何签证或移民福利程序的变更,仅针对实体隔离墙和道路的建设。
  • 此次豁免不撤销此前根据第102条作出的其他豁免决定,先前豁免继续有效。

影响对象

该通知主要影响得克萨斯州指定边境地区的建设活动和土地使用,并不直接涉及个人签证或移民申请人。

美国国土安全部（简称“DHS”）的重要使命要求包括边境安全以及对非法进入美国行为的侦测和预防。边境安全对国家安全至关重要。认识到边境安全的极端重要性，国会已要求国土安全部实现并维持对国际陆地边境的有效管控。参见2006年《安全围栏法》，公法第109-367号，第2节，120 Stat. 2638（2006年10月26日）（8 U.S.C. 1701 note）。国会将“有效管控”定义为防止一切非法进入美国的行为，包括恐怖分子、其他非法外国人、恐怖主义工具、麻醉品及其他违禁品的进入。同上。根据该授权，总统关于确保边境安全的行政命令指示本人采取一切适当行动，部署并建造实体屏障，以确保对美国南部边境的完全有效管控。第14165号行政命令，第3节（2025年1月20日）。（印刷版第46940页）
Important mission requirements of the Department of Homeland Security (“DHS”) include border security and the detection and prevention of illegal entry into the United States. Border security is critical to the nation's national security. Recognizing the critical importance of border security, Congress has mandated DHS to achieve and maintain operational control of the international land border. Secure Fence Act of 2006, Public Law 109-367, section 2, 120 Stat. 2638 (Oct. 26, 2006) (8 U.S.C. 1701 note). Congress defined “operational control” as the prevention of all unlawful entries into the United States, including entries by terrorists, other unlawful aliens, instruments of terrorism, narcotics, and other contraband. Id. Consistent with that mandate, the President's Executive Order on Securing Our Borders directs that I take all appropriate action to deploy and construct physical barriers to ensure complete operational control of the southern border of the United States. Executive Order 14165, section 3 (Jan. 20, 2025). ( printed page 46940)
国会已授予国土安全部部长若干必要权力，以执行国土安全部的边境安全使命。其中一项权力见于经修订的1996年《非法移民改革和移民责任法》（简称“IIRIRA”）第102节。公法第104-208号，C分编，第一章，第102节，110 Stat. 3009-546, 3009-554（8 U.S.C. 1103 note），经2005年《真实身份法》修订，公法第109-13号，B分编，第一章，第102节，119 Stat. 302, 306（8 U.S.C. 1103 note），经2006年《安全围栏法》修订，公法第109-367号，第3节，120 Stat. 2638（8 U.S.C. 1103 note），并经《2008年国土安全部拨款法》修订，公法第110-161号，E分编，第五章，第564节，121 Stat. 2042, 2090。在IIRIRA第102(a)节中，国会规定，国土安全部部长应采取必要行动，在美国边境附近安装额外的实体屏障和道路（包括清除妨碍侦测非法入境者的障碍物），以阻止在非法入境高发地区发生的非法越境行为。在IIRIRA第102(b)节中，国会要求，本人在行使第102(a)节授权时，应提供安装额外围栏、屏障、道路、照明、摄像设备和传感器，以实现并维持边境的有效管控。最后，在IIRIRA第102(c)节中，国会授予国土安全部部长权力，可自行酌情豁免本人认定为确保依照IIRIRA第102节授权迅速建造屏障和道路所必需的一切法律要求。
Congress has provided to the Secretary of Homeland Security a number of authorities necessary to carry out DHS's border security mission. One of those authorities is found at section 102 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996, as amended (“IIRIRA”). Public Law 104-208, Div. C, Tit. I, section 102, 110 Stat. 3009-546, 3009-554 (8 U.S.C 1103 note), as amended by the REAL ID Act of 2005, Public Law 109-13, Div. B, Tit. I, section 102, 119 Stat. 302, 306 (8 U.S.C. 1103 note), as amended by the Secure Fence Act of 2006, Public Law 109-367, section 3, 120 Stat. 2638 (8 U.S.C. 1103 note), as amended by the Department of Homeland Security Appropriations Act, 2008, Public Law 110-161, Div. E, Tit. V, section 564, 121 Stat. 2042, 2090. In section 102(a) of IIRIRA, Congress provided that the Secretary of Homeland Security shall take such actions as may be necessary to install additional physical barriers and roads (including the removal of obstacles to detection of illegal entrants) in the vicinity of the United States border to deter illegal crossings in areas of high illegal entry into the United States. In section 102(b) of IIRIRA, Congress mandated that in carrying out the authority of section 102(a), I provide for the installation of additional fencing, barriers, roads, lighting, cameras, and sensors to achieve and maintain operational control of the border. Finally, in section 102(c) of IIRIRA, Congress granted to the Secretary of Homeland Security the authority to waive all legal requirements that I, in my sole discretion, determine necessary to ensure the expeditious construction of barriers and roads authorized by section 102 of IIRIRA.

第1节
Section 1
美国边境巡逻队大弯(Big Bend)和德尔里奥(Del Rio)辖区是非法入境高发地区。在2021财年至2025财年期间,边境巡逻队在大弯辖区的两个边境口岸之间逮捕了超过89,000名试图进入美国的非法外国人。在同一时期,边境巡逻队查获了超过87,574磅大麻、超过867磅可卡因、超过1,156磅甲基苯丙胺、超过12磅海洛因以及超过94磅芬太尼。在2021财年至2025财年期间,边境巡逻队在德尔里奥辖区的两个边境口岸之间逮捕了超过1,410,330名试图进入美国的非法外国人。在同一时期,边境巡逻队查获了超过277磅大麻、超过198磅可卡因、超过353磅甲基苯丙胺以及超过5磅芬太尼。
The United States Border Patrol Big Bend and Del Rio Sectors are areas of high illegal entry. Between fiscal year 2021 and fiscal year 2025, Border Patrol apprehended over 89,000 illegal aliens attempting to enter the United States between border crossings in the Big Bend Sector. In that same time period Border Patrol seized over 87,574 pounds of marijuana, over 867 pounds of cocaine, over 1,156 pounds of methamphetamine, over 12 pounds of heroin, and over 94 pounds of fentanyl. Between fiscal year 2021 and fiscal year 2025, Border Patrol apprehended over 1,410,330 illegal aliens attempting to enter the United States between border crossings in the Del Rio Sector. In that same time period Border Patrol seized over 277 pounds of marijuana, over 198 pounds of cocaine, over 353 pounds of methamphetamine, and over five pounds of fentanyl.
自总统就职以来,美国国土安全部(DHS)已实现了历史上最安全的边境。然而,仍有更多工作可以也必须完成。如上述统计数据所示,大弯和德尔里奥辖区是非法入境高发地区,非法外国人经常试图进入美国并走私违禁毒品。鉴于我负有实现并维持边境行动管控的职责,我必须依据《非法移民改革和移民责任法》(IIRIRA)第102条赋予我的权力,在大弯和德尔里奥辖区增设屏障和道路。因此,美国国土安全部将立即采取行动,在大弯和德尔里奥辖区的边境区段增设屏障和道路。将在此进行此类建设的区段在本文件中称为“项目区域”,其具体描述见下文第2节。
Since the President took office, DHS has delivered the most secure border in history. More can and must be done, however. As the statistics cited above demonstrate, the Big Bend and Del Rio Sectors are areas of high illegal entry where illegal aliens regularly attempt to enter the United States and smuggle illicit drugs, and given my mandate to achieve and maintain operational control of the border, I must use my authority under section 102 of IIRIRA to install additional barriers and roads in the Big Bend and Del Rio Sectors. Therefore, DHS will take immediate action to construct additional barriers and roads in a segment of the border in the Big Bend and Del Rio Sectors. The segment where such construction will occur is referred to herein as the “project area,” which is more specifically described in Section 2 below.

Section 2

I determine that the following area in the vicinity of the United States border, located in the state of Texas within the U.S. Border Patrol Big Bend and Del Rio Sectors, is an area of high illegal entry (the “project area”): Starting at approximately GPS point 29.728522, −102.683945 and extending east to approximately GPS point 29.449892, −101.058017.
根据《非法移民改革和移民责任法》(IIRIRA)第102条(a)款和(b)款的规定,目前迫切且紧急需要在美国边境附近的项目区域内增建物理屏障和道路,以防止非法入境美国。为确保在项目区域内迅速完成物理屏障和道路的建设,我已确定有必要行使《非法移民改革和移民责任法》第102条(c)款赋予我的权力。
There is presently an acute and immediate need to construct additional physical barriers and roads in the vicinity of the border of the United States in order to prevent unlawful entries into the United States in the project area pursuant to section 102(a) and 102(b) of IIRIRA. In order to ensure the expeditious construction of additional physical barriers and roads in the project area, I have determined that it is necessary that I exercise the authority that is vested in me by section 102(c) of IIRIRA.
