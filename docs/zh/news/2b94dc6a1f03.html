EOIR确定2027财年移民法院申请费通胀调整方案

发布日期 2026年8月21日生效日期 2026年10月1日

美国司法部移民复审执行办公室(EOIR)发布最终规则,根据One Big Beautiful Bill Act(OBBBA)的要求,对向移民法院和移民上诉委员会(BIA)提交的上诉及动议费用进行通胀调整。新费用标准将于2026年10月1日(2027财年开始)生效,反映了3.4%的消费者价格指数(CPI-U)涨幅。

要点

  • 这是法律规定的年度强制性通胀调整,因此以最终规则形式直接发布,无需公众意见征询程序。
  • 新费用适用于2026年10月1日及以后向移民法院或BIA提交的申请。
  • EOIR-26和EOIR-29上诉通知表格的总费用均为1,060美元,包括950美元的OBBBA费用和现有的110美元第286(m)条费用。
  • 驱逐暂缓申请(EOIR-40)和永久居民撤销驱逐令申请(EOIR-42A)的总费用均为730美元。
  • 非永久居民撤销驱逐令及调整身份申请(EOIR-42B)的总费用为1,690美元,是费用表中最高的金额。
  • 该规则不改变移民与国籍法第286(m)条下的费用金额,也不涉及任何费用减免或例外规定的变更。

影响对象

向移民法院或BIA提交上诉、重新审理或复议动议,以及驱逐暂缓、撤销驱逐令等申请的当事人将受到影响。

建议行动

自2026年10月1日起提交文件者应确认最新费用金额并按新标准缴费,或在适用情况下一并提交费用减免申请。

一、背景
I. Background
2025年7月4日,国会预算协调法案(H.R. 1)正式成为法律,该法案通常被称为《大而美法案》(“OBBBA”)。公法第119-21号,139 Stat. 72。就本规则所涉事项而言,OBBBA自2025财年起为EOIR的申请、动议和上诉引入了新的必要的移民相关费用(“OBBBA费用”),并要求司法部长每年根据通货膨胀对这些OBBBA费用进行更新。 参见8 U.S.C. 1802、1808、1812。本规则根据OBBBA的规定,更新相关EOIR监管费用表,以对2027财年的OBBBA费用进行通货膨胀调整。
On July 4, 2025, a congressional budget reconciliation bill (H.R. 1), commonly referred to as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (“OBBBA”), became law. Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72. As relevant here, OBBBA introduced new required immigration-related fees for EOIR applications, motions, and appeals beginning in FY 2025 (“OBBBA fees”) and mandated that the Attorney General annually update these OBBBA fees for inflation. See8 U.S.C. 1802, 1808, 1812. This rule updates the relevant EOIR regulatory fee schedules to adjust the OBBBA fees for inflation for FY 2027 in accordance with OBBBA's mandate.
在此过程中,本部指出,本规则不改变根据《移民与国籍法》(“INA”或“该法”)第286(m)条、8 U.S.C. 1356(m)所收取的任何费用金额,本规则也不对费用豁免或减免作出任何变更。此外,EOIR的监管费用表中不包含由国土安全部(“DHS”)发布的申请表格的费用金额,但由EOIR收取的此类费用将在EOIR网站及EOIR支付门户上进行更新。 参见8 CFR 1103.7(d)(4)(ii)(“由国土安全部发布并用于移民诉讼程序的申请表费用,受8 CFR 103.7及8 CFR 第106部分管辖。”)。
In doing so, the Department notes that this rule does not change any fee amounts collected under section 286(m) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (“INA” or “Act”), 8 U.S.C. 1356(m), nor does this rule make any changes to fee exceptions or waivers. Additionally, EOIR's regulatory fee schedule does not contain fee amounts for application forms published by the Department of Homeland Security (“DHS”), though those fees that are collected by EOIR will be updated on EOIR's website and the EOIR Payment Portal. See8 CFR 1103.7(d)(4)(ii) (“The fees for applications published by the Department of Homeland Security and used in immigration proceedings are governed by 8 CFR 103.7 and 8 CFR part 106.”).
二、调整依据
II. Basis for Adjustment
OBBBA要求所有由EOIR收取的OBBBA费用每年根据通货膨胀进行调整,并规定应对每项费用适用特定的调整公式。 参见8 U.S.C. 1802、1808、1812。对于所有OBBBA费用,通货膨胀调整后的金额通过以下方式计算得出:将(1)最近结束的财政年度所要求的OBBBA费用金额,加上(2)将最近结束的财政年度所要求的OBBBA费用金额乘以以下百分比(如有)所得之积:即在调整生效日期之前的7月份(本例中为2026年7月)的所有城市消费者消费价格指数(“CPI-U”),超过上一个日历年同月(本例中为2025年7月)CPI-U的百分比,所得之和向下舍入至最接近的10美元的倍数, 参见8 U.S.C. 1802(c)、1812(a)(2)(B),或者,对于年度庇护费(“AAF”),向下舍入至最接近的整数美元。 参见8 U.S.C. 1808(b)(2)。用于计算2027财年OBBBA费用的CPI-U百分比变化为3.4%。[1]
OBBBA requires that all EOIR-collected OBBBA fees be adjusted annually for inflation and directs that a specific adjustment formula be applied to each fee. See8 U.S.C. 1802, 1808, 1812. For all OBBBA fees, the inflation-adjusted amount is calculated by adding: (1) the amount of the OBBBA fee required for the most recently concluded fiscal year to (2) the product resulting from multiplying the OBBBA fee required for the most recently concluded fiscal year by the percentage (if any) by which the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (“CPI-U”) for the month of July (in this case July 2026), preceding the date on which such adjustment takes effect, exceeds the CPI-U for the same month of the preceding calendar year (in this case July 2025), either rounded down to the nearest multiple of $10, see8 U.S.C. 1802(c), 1812(a)(2)(B), or, for the Annual Asylum Fee (“AAF”), rounded down to the nearest dollar. See8 U.S.C. 1808(b)(2). The CPI-U percent change for calculating FY 2027 OBBBA fees is 3.4 percent.[1]

四、法定与法规要求
IV. Statutory and Regulatory Requirements
A. 行政程序法
A. Administrative Procedure Act
根据《行政程序法》(“APA”),机构通常必须在《联邦公报》上发布“拟议规则制定通知”,并在发布该通知后,“让利害关系人有机会通过提交书面资料、意见或论据参与规则制定”。见5 U.S.C. 553(b)-(c)。《行政程序法》进一步规定,实体性规则的必要公布或送达应在其生效日期前不少于30天进行,但特定情形除外。同上, 553(d)。
Under the Administrative Procedure Act (“APA”), agencies generally must provide “notice of proposed rule making” in the Federal Register and, after such notice, “give interested persons an opportunity to participate in the rule making through submission of written data, views, or arguments.” 5 U.S.C. 553(b)-(c). The APA further provides that the required publication or service of a substantive rule shall be made not less than 30 days before its effective date, except in certain circumstances. Id. 553(d).
然而,根据《行政程序法》,如果机构基于正当理由认定此类程序不可行、不必要或有悖公共利益,则该规则可免除通知与评议程序。见5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B)。如果机构认定存在正当理由放弃该要求,并随该规则一并公布,则该规则也可免除延迟生效日期的要求。同上, 553(d)(3);见Riverbend Farms, Inc. 诉 Madigan案,958 F.2d 1479, 1485(第九巡回上诉法院,1992年)(区分《行政程序法》的两项“正当理由”例外:适用于通知与评议程序的5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B),以及适用于延迟生效日期的5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3))。
However, pursuant to the APA, a rule is excepted from notice-and-comment procedures when an agency for good cause finds that such procedures are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest. 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B). A rule is also excepted from the delayed-effective-date requirement when good cause is found by the agency to forgo this requirement and is published with the rule. Id. 553(d)(3); Riverbend Farms, Inc. v. Madigan, 958 F.2d 1479, 1485 (9th Cir. 1992) (differentiating the APA's two “good cause” exceptions: 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B), which applies to the notice and comment process, and 5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3), which applies to the delayed effective date).
如上文所述,《“一揽子美好法案”》(OBBBA)要求司法部长使用法定规定的公式,每年针对通货膨胀更新由行政性移民复核办公室(EOIR)收取的OBBBA费用。见8 U.S.C. 1802、1808、1812。因此,通知与评议及延迟生效日期的要求是不必要的,因为本部有义务遵守OBBBA的通货膨胀调整要求,故本规则所作的变更纯属程序性事务。参见,例如,Metzenbaum 诉 FERC案,675 F.2d 1282, 1291(哥伦比亚特区巡回上诉法院,1982年)(解释在《行政程序法》正当理由例外下,对于“非自由裁量行为”而言,通知与评议是不必要的,因为这将是一种“徒劳之举”)。
As discussed above, OBBBA requires the Attorney General to annually update EOIR-collected OBBBA fees for inflation using a statutorily prescribed formula. See8 U.S.C. 1802, 1808, 1812. Therefore, the notice-and-comment and delayed-effective-date requirements are unnecessary because the Department is bound to comply with OBBBA's inflation adjustment requirements and, thus, the changes made by this rule are purely ministerial. See, e.g., Metzenbaum v. FERC, 675 F.2d 1282, 1291 (D.C. Cir. 1982) (explaining notice and comment was unnecessary under the APA good cause exception for “nondiscretionary acts” as it would be a “futile gesture”).

本规则不触发《监管灵活性法》的要求,因为如上所述,本部在发布本规则之前无需“发布拟议规则制定的一般通知”。见5 U.S.C. 604(a)。

C. 第12866号行政命令(《监管规划与审查》)及第13563号行政命令(《改进监管与监管审查》)
C. Executive Order 12866 (Regulatory Planning and Review) and Executive Order 13563 (Improving Regulation and Regulatory Review)
行政命令第12866号, 《监管计划与审查》,58 FR 51735(1993年9月30日),以及行政命令第13563号, 《改进监管与监管审查》,76 FR 3821(2011年1月18日),均指示各机构评估现有监管备选方案的成本和收益,并且,如果监管确属必要,应选择能使净收益最大化的监管方法(包括潜在的经济、环境、公共卫生和安全影响、分配影响以及公平性)。行政命令第13563号进一步强调了量化成本和收益、降低成本、协调规则以及促进灵活性的重要性。
Executive Order 12866, Regulatory Planning and Review,58 FR 51735 (Sept. 30, 1993), and Executive Order 13563, Improving Regulation and Regulatory Review,76 FR 3821 (Jan. 18, 2011), each direct agencies to assess the costs and benefits of available regulatory alternatives and, if regulation is necessary, to select regulatory approaches that maximize net benefits (including potential economic, environmental, public health, and safety effects, distributive impacts, and equity). Executive Order 13563 further emphasizes the importance of quantifying both costs and benefits, reducing costs, harmonizing rules, and promoting flexibility.
本部已确定,根据行政命令第12866号第3(f)条,本规则不属于“重大监管行动”,因此,本规则未经管理与预算办公室(OMB)审查。尽管如此,本部证明,本规则的起草符合行政命令第12866号和第13563号所确立的原则。
The Department has determined that this rule is not a “significant regulatory action” under section 3(f) of Executive Order 12866 and, therefore, it has not been reviewed by the Office of Management and Budget. Nevertheless, the Department certifies that this regulation has been drafted in accordance with the principles laid out in Executive Orders 12866 and 13563.
本规则仅根据法定要求的收费变更,更新相关的行政法官执行办公室(EOIR)收费金额。因此,本规则所作的任何变更均不会在法定条款之外给公众带来额外成本。同样,这些监管变更提供了明确性,这有利于公众,并有助于避免对当前收费金额产生混淆。因此,本规则所作的变更不会以与行政命令第12866号或第13563号所确立的原则相抵触的方式影响公众。
This rule merely updates pertinent EOIR fee amounts to be consistent with the fee changes required by statute. Accordingly, any changes made by this rule do not impose a cost upon the public beyond the terms of the statute. Likewise, these regulatory changes provide clarity, which benefits the public and helps avoid confusion over the current fee amounts. Thus, the changes made by this rule do not impact the public in a way that would place it in tension with the principles set forth in Executive Orders 12866 or 13563.

E. 行政命令第14294号(联邦法规的过度刑事化)
E. Executive Order 14294 (Overcriminalization of Federal Regulations)
行政命令第14294号, 《联邦法规的过度刑事化》,90 FR 20363(2025年5月9日),要求制定含有可能受到刑事执法的刑事监管罪行的法规的各机构,明确描述受刑事执法约束的行为、授权制定该法规的法律,以及适用于该等罪行各要素的犯罪意图(mens rea)标准。本规则未设立任何刑事监管罪行,因此不适用行政命令第14294号的要求。
Executive Order 14294, Overcriminalization of Federal Regulations,90 FR 20363 (May 9, 2025), requires agencies promulgating regulations with criminal regulatory offenses potentially subject to criminal enforcement to explicitly describe the conduct subject to criminal enforcement, the authorizing statutes, and the mens rea standard applicable to each element of those offenses. This rule does not create a criminal regulatory offense and is thus exempt from Executive Order 14294 requirements.
F. 1995年《无资金授权改革法》
F. Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995
本规则不会导致州政府、地方政府和部落政府合计,或私营部门,在任何一年内支出1亿美元或以上(经通货膨胀调整),也不会对小型政府产生重大或独特的影响。因此,根据1995年《无资金授权改革法》(公法104-4号,109 Stat. 48,编入《美国法典》第2编第1501至1571条)的规定,本部认定无需采取任何行动。
This rule will not result in the expenditure by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector, of $100 million or more (inflation adjusted) in any one year, and it will not significantly or uniquely affect small governments. Therefore, no actions were deemed necessary under the provisions of the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995, Public Law 104-4, 109 Stat. 48 (codified at 2 U.S.C. 1501-1571).
G. 行政命令第13132号(联邦制)
G. Executive Order 13132 (Federalism)
本规则不会对各州、国家政府与各州之间的关系,或各级政府之间权力与责任的分配产生实质性的直接影响。因此,根据行政命令第13132号, 《联邦制》,64 FR 43255(1999年8月4日)第6条的规定,本部已确定,本规则不具有足以要求编制联邦制影响摘要声明的联邦制含义。
This rule will not have substantial direct effects on the States, on the relationship between the National Government and the States, or on the distribution of power and responsibilities among the various levels of government. Therefore, in accordance with section 6 of Executive Order 13132, Federalism,64 FR 43255 (Aug. 4, 1999), the Department has determined that this rule does not have sufficient federalism implications to warrant the preparation of a federalism summary impact statement.
H. 行政命令第12988号(民事司法改革)
H. Executive Order 12988 (Civil Justice Reform)
本规则符合行政命令第12988号, 《民事司法改革》,61 FR 4729(1996年2月5日)第3(a)条和第3(b)(2)条所规定的适用标准。
This rule meets the applicable standards set forth in sections 3(a) and 3(b)(2) of Executive Order 12988, Civil Justice Reform,61 FR 4729 (Feb. 5, 1996).
I. 《文书削减法》
I. Paperwork Reduction Act
本规则未提出任何新的或修订的“信息收集”,该术语的定义见1995年《文书削减法》(公法104-13号,109 Stat. 163,编入《美国法典》第44编第3501至3521条)及其实施法规(《联邦法规汇编》第5编第1320部分)。但是,本部指出,鉴于《一揽子预算协调法案》(OBBBA)所要求的通货膨胀调整,行政法官执行办公室(EOIR)正在另行对EOIR各表格进行微小变更,以更新表格上的收费金额。
This rule does not propose new or revised “collection[s] of information” as that term is defined under the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, Public Law 104-13, 109 Stat. 163 (codified at 44 U.S.C. 3501-3521), and its implementing regulations, 5 CFR part 1320. However, the Department notes that EOIR is separately making de minimis changes to EOIR forms to update the fee amounts on the forms in light of the inflation adjustments required by OBBBA.

J. Congressional Review Act
根据《国会审查法》第804条的定义,本规则不属于重大规则。《美国法典》第5编第804条。
This rule is not a major rule as defined by section 804 of the Congressional Review Act. 5 U.S.C. 804.
