USCIS与司法部对隐瞒恐怖主义支持、战争罪等罪行的12人提起剥夺公民身份诉讼

发布日期 2026年5月14日

美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)与司法部(DOJ)在多个联邦地区法院对12名入籍公民提起民事剥夺公民身份(denaturalization)诉讼,指控他们在入籍过程中隐瞒支持恐怖主义、战争罪、间谍行为及性侵未成年人等严重罪行。根据《移民与国籍法》,如入籍是通过隐瞒重要事实或故意虚假陈述获得的,其公民身份可被撤销。USCIS强调,这些仅为指控,尚未经法院作出责任认定。

要点

  • 涉案的12人分别来自伊拉克、哥伦比亚、摩洛哥、索马里、冈比亚、玻利维亚、乌兹别克斯坦、肯尼亚、印度、中国和尼日利亚等国。
  • 指控内容包括向基地组织或青年党提供物质支持、参与法外处决、为古巴从事间谍活动、性侵儿童以及欺诈行为。
  • 多起案件涉及虚假婚姻、伪造身份或隐瞒犯罪记录,被指因此不符合入籍所需的良好道德品行或对宪法的忠诚要求。
  • 相关诉讼由司法部移民诉讼办公室积极诉讼股在USCIS协助下提出,涉及美国十个联邦司法辖区的检察官办公室。
  • USCIS指出,这些均为民事诉讼中的指控,尚未经法院最终裁定。

影响对象

此次行动直接影响被列为被告的12名入籍公民。

建议行动

公众无需采取特别行动,但发现移民福利欺诈或滥用行为的人士可通过USCIS举报表格(Tip Form)进行举报。

华盛顿,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)近日与美国司法部合作,在多个美国联邦地区法院对12名被控犯有严重罪行的个人提起褫夺国籍诉讼,所涉罪行包括向恐怖组织提供物质支持、实施战争罪行以及性侵未成年人。
WASHINGTON –U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services partnered with the Department of Justice in its recent filing of denaturalization actions in various U.S. district courts against 12 individuals accused of serious offenses—including providing material support to a terrorist group, committing war crimes, and sexually abusing a minor.
根据《移民与国籍法》,归化美国公民如系通过非法手段取得国籍,或通过隐瞒重大事实或故意虚假陈述取得国籍,其公民身份可被撤销,其归化证书可被注销。
Under the Immigration and Nationality Act, a naturalized U.S. citizen’s citizenship may be revoked, and certificate of naturalization canceled, if the naturalization was illegally procured or procured by concealment of a material fact or by willful misrepresentation.
阿里·优素福·艾哈迈德·阿尔努里(48岁/伊拉克):5月8日,美国司法部与亚利桑那州联邦检察官在凤凰城美国联邦地区法院对伊拉克裔人士阿里·优素福·艾哈迈德提起民事褫夺国籍诉讼。艾哈迈德于2009年入境美国,声称他本人及家人在伊拉克曾遭基地组织恐怖分子袭击。2019年,伊拉克共和国要求美国将艾哈迈德引渡至伊拉克,以就其涉嫌于2006年蓄意谋杀两名伊拉克警察的刑事指控受审。伊拉克指控艾哈迈德以基地组织头目身份实施了该谋杀行为。艾哈迈德在申请入境美国及归化入籍时就其犯罪和家庭背景作虚假宣誓陈述,从而于2015年非法取得归化入籍资格。
Ali Yousif Ahmed Al-Nouri (Age 48/Iraq): On May 8, DOJ and the U.S. Attorney for the District of Arizona filed a civil denaturalization complaint in the U.S. District Court in Phoenix against Ali Yousif Ahmed, a native of Iraq. Ahmed entered the U.S. in 2009, claiming that he and his family were attacked by Al-Qaida terrorists in Iraq. In 2019, the Republic of Iraq asked the U.S. to extradite Ahmed to Iraq to face criminal charges for the premeditated murder of two Iraqi police officers in 2006. Iraq alleges that Ahmed committed the murders in his role as a leader in Al-Qaida. Ahmed illegally procured his naturalization in 2015 by lying under oath about his criminal and family history when seeking admission to the U.S. and when naturalizing.
奥斯卡·阿尔贝托·佩拉埃斯(75岁/哥伦比亚):美国政府已对奥斯卡·阿尔贝托·佩拉埃斯提起褫夺国籍诉讼。佩拉埃斯是一名哥伦比亚裔天主教神父,曾在1998年至2000年间多次性侵一名儿童,该儿童受害时年仅14岁,持续至其17岁。2002年,佩拉埃斯对13项针对儿童的性侵指控认罪并被判有罪,其中包括两项对18岁以下人员实施口交的指控,以及两项对18岁以下人员实施鸡奸的指控。佩拉埃斯在申请归化入籍时就其所犯罪行作虚假陈述。美国政府已就佩拉埃斯提出四项褫夺国籍诉求,包括其不具备成为美国公民所需的良好品德,以及其明知故犯地向移民当局作虚假陈述。
Oscar Alberto Pelaez (Age 75/Colombia): The U.S. filed to denaturalize Oscar Alberto Pelaez, a Colombian Roman Catholic priest who sexually abused a child on multiple occasions from 1998 to 2000, beginning when the child was 14 until he was 17 years old. In 2002, Pelaez pleaded guilty to and was convicted of 13 counts of sexual assault against a child, including two counts of oral copulation with a person under 18 years of age, and two counts of sodomy of a person under 18 years of age. Pelaez lied about committing these crimes in connection with his naturalization application. The U.S. has brought four claims against Pelaez seeking his denaturalization, including that he lacked the good moral character to become a U.S. citizen and that he knowingly lied to immigration authorities.
哈立德·瓦扎尼(48岁/摩洛哥):摩洛哥裔人士哈立德·瓦扎尼于2005年申请美国公民身份、并于2006年归化入籍时,宣誓效忠宪法原则。然而该誓言系虚假陈述,因为早在2003年,瓦扎尼便与另外两名后因企图炸毁纽约证券交易所而被定罪的人士共同策划向基地组织提供支持的方式。到2007年,即其归化入籍仅一年后,他便利用通过欺诈手段获取的资金向基地组织提供了数万美元的财务支持,并于2008年宣誓效忠基地组织。2010年5月,瓦扎尼对银行欺诈罪、洗钱罪以及向基地组织提供物质支持罪表示认罪。针对瓦扎尼提起的褫夺国籍诉讼指控其在归化入籍程序中就其效忠宪法一事作出虚假证词。其在归化入籍后五年内与基地组织的关联及成员身份,足以证明其并不具备所要求的对宪法的效忠。
Khalid Ouazzani (Age 48/Morocco): When Khalid Ouazzani, a native of Morocco, applied for U.S. citizenship in 2005 and again when he naturalized in 2006, he swore to his attachment to the principles of the Constitution. Those oaths were false because, as early as 2003, Ouazzani was planning ways to support Al-Qaida with two men who were later convicted of trying to bomb the New York Stock Exchange. By 2007, just one year after he naturalized, he sent Al-Qaida tens of thousands of dollars in financial support with money that he had fraudulently obtained, and in 2008, he took an oath of allegiance to Al-Qaida. In May 2010, Ouazzani pleaded guilty to bank fraud, money laundering, and providing material support to Al-Qaida. The denaturalization complaint filed against Ouazzani alleges that he gave false testimony during his naturalization proceedings about his attachment to the Constitution. His affiliation and membership with Al-Qaida within five years of his naturalization is evidence that he lacked the required attachment to the Constitution.
萨拉赫·奥斯曼·艾哈迈德(43岁/索马里):艾哈迈德于2007年归化入籍时并未效忠美国宪法原则。在归化入籍仅数月后,艾哈迈德便开始向恐怖分子提供物质支持。艾哈迈德前往索马里参与战斗并杀害埃塞俄比亚人,并加入恐怖组织青年党(Al-Shabab)。2009年7月28日,他对向恐怖分子提供物质支持罪表示认罪。艾哈迈德在归化入籍后五年内与青年党的成员身份或关联关系本应使其无法获得归化入籍资格。他在归化入籍后不久即加入青年党,表明艾哈迈德系通过隐瞒重大事实及故意虚假陈述取得公民身份。
Salah Osman Ahmed (Age 43/Somalia): Ahmed was not attached to the principles of the U.S. Constitution when he naturalized in 2007. Just months after he naturalized, Ahmed began providing material support to terrorists. Ahmed traveled to Somalia to fight and kill Ethiopians and joined the terrorist group Al-Shabab. On July 28, 2009, he pleaded guilty to providing material support to terrorists. Ahmed’s membership in or affiliation with Al-Shabab within five years of naturalization would have prevented him from naturalizing. Joining Al-Shabab shortly after his naturalization shows that Ahmed obtained his citizenship by concealing material facts and willful misrepresentation.
巴布卡尔·姆布(58岁/冈比亚):1994年11月11日,冈比亚本土公民、冈比亚军队宪兵巴布卡尔·姆布伙同其他15名士兵,按照其指挥官的命令处决了六名军官,该指挥官认定这些受害者正在密谋针对时任总统叶海亚·贾梅的反政变。这些处决行为是在未给予受害者接受审判权利的情况下实施的。姆布在2002年入境美国并于2011年入籍后,在其移民及入籍程序中始终隐瞒自己参与战争罪行和迫害行为的事实。在2019年4月9日冈比亚真相、和解与赔偿委员会举行的听证会上作证时,姆布承认处决了他的六名同僚军官。美国正寻求撤销其入籍证书,因为他通过隐瞒重大事实并故意虚报其军事背景而非法获得美国公民身份。
Baboucarr Mboob (Age 58/Gambia): On Nov. 11, 1994, Baboucarr Mboob, a native of The Gambia and a military police officer in the Gambian army, participated—along with 15 other soldiers—in the execution of six officers, following orders from his commanding officer, who believed the victims were plotting a counter-coup against then-President Yahya Jammeh. The executions were carried out without giving the victims the benefit of a trial. Mboob concealed his involvement in war crimes and acts of persecution throughout his immigration and naturalization proceedings after entering the U.S. in 2002 and naturalizing in 2011. In testimony before a hearing of The Gambian Truth, Reconciliation, and Reparations Commission held on April 9, 2019, Mboob admitted to executing his six fellow officers. The U.S. is seeking to revoke his certificate of naturalization because he obtained his citizenship illegally by concealing material fact and willfully misrepresenting his military background.
凯文·罗宾·苏亚雷斯(31岁/玻利维亚):自2016年5月起,并在其2017年1月入籍后持续十个月,凯文·罗宾·苏亚雷斯参与了一项阴谋,通过“稻草人”购买者购买枪支,最终将这些枪支出口至玻利维亚及其他拉丁美洲国家。苏亚雷斯与其姐妹合作,招募他人代为向持有联邦许可证的枪支经销商购买枪支,并将这些枪支提供给其父亲。这些枪支是玻利维亚籍人士在美国组织的从南佛罗里达向玻利维亚贩运枪支的更大网络的一部分。枪支运抵玻利维亚后,往往被转运至巴西、巴拉圭和秘鲁的贩毒组织,加剧了当地的毒品暴力活动。苏亚雷斯于2020年2月承认犯有串谋致使联邦持证枪支经销商收到虚假陈述罪。他非法获得入籍资格,因为其犯罪行为表明他不具备入籍所要求的良好品德。此外,苏亚雷斯在宣誓作证时作出虚假陈述,并歪曲和隐瞒了对确定其入籍资格具有重大意义的事实。
Kevin Robin Suarez (Age 31/Bolivia): Beginning in May 2016, and continuing for 10 months after his January 2017 naturalization, Kevin Robin Suarez engaged in a conspiracy to purchase firearms through straw purchasers to eventually export the firearms to Bolivia and other Latin American countries. Working with his sister, Suarez solicited individuals to purchase firearms on their behalf from licensed federal firearms dealers and provided those firearms to his father. These firearms were part of a larger network of gun trafficking from South Florida to Bolivia by Bolivian nationals in the U.S. Once the guns were in Bolivia, they were often sent to drug trafficking organizations in Brazil, Paraguay, and Peru, fueling drug violence there. Suarez pled guilty to conspiracy to cause false statements to be made to federally licensed firearms dealers in February 2020. He illegally obtained his naturalization because his crime means he did not have the required good moral character to naturalize. Also, Suarez falsely testified under oath and misrepresented and concealed facts that were material to determining his naturalization eligibility.
阿布杜沃西特·拉济科夫(46岁/乌兹别克斯坦):2005年,乌兹别克斯坦本土公民阿布杜沃西特·拉济科夫出钱与一名美国公民缔结虚假婚姻,以便获得在美国的永久居留权。2007年,拉济科夫又出钱让另一名美国公民与拉济科夫的真正恋爱伴侣缔结虚假婚姻,使她得以从乌兹别克斯坦入境美国。2010年,拉济科夫与其美国公民“妻子”离婚,并于2012年入籍成为美国公民。几个月后,拉济科夫又安排了第三次虚假婚姻,这次是与另一名乌兹别克斯坦妇女(并非他本人的恋爱伴侣)结婚,以便她获得移民利益。针对拉济科夫提起的撤销国籍诉讼指控称,由于其虚假婚姻及移民欺诈行为,他从未合法取得入籍所需的永久居民身份;其非法行为及虚假证词使他无法证明具备入籍所需的良好品德;并且他是通过隐瞒或故意虚报重大事实而获得公民身份的。
Abduvosit Razikov (Age 46/Uzbekistan): In 2005, Abduvosit Razikov, a native of Uzbekistan, paid a U.S. citizen to enter into a sham marriage with him so that he could obtain permanent residency in the U.S. In 2007, Razikov paid another U.S. citizen to enter into a sham marriage with Razikov’s actual romantic partner, enabling her to enter the U.S. from Uzbekistan. In 2010, Razikov divorced his U.S. citizen “wife,” and in 2012, he became a naturalized citizen. Months later, Razikov arranged a third sham marriage, this time marrying another Uzbekistani woman (not his own romantic partner) so she could obtain immigration benefits. The denaturalization complaint filed against Razikov alleges that he never lawfully acquired the permanent resident status required to naturalize due to his sham marriage and immigration fraud; that his unlawful acts and false testimony prevented him from establishing the good moral character required for naturalization; and that he obtained his citizenship by concealing or willfully misrepresenting material facts.
阿卜杜拉·奥斯曼·谢赫(28岁/肯尼亚):阿卜杜拉·奥斯曼·谢赫通过隐瞒重大事实或故意虚报而非法获得入籍资格。2019年7月,在其入籍之前,但在美国海军陆战队服役期间,谢赫持有两名未成年人的不雅数字图像,并将其中一人的不雅数字图像发布至其社交媒体账户。这些犯罪行为,连同他在入籍程序中向政府隐瞒这些行为的努力,构成了对谢赫予以撤销国籍的正当理由。此外,谢赫是基于其军事服役记录而获得入籍资格的,但其后因未能连续五年诚实服役而以“非光荣”(因不当行为)方式从海军陆战队退役,这进一步构成了对其予以撤销国籍的正当理由。
Abdallah Osman Sheikh (Age 28/Kenya): Abdallah Osman Sheikh unlawfully obtained his naturalization by concealing a material fact or through willful misrepresentation. In July 2019, before he naturalized, but while he was serving in the U.S. Marine Corps, Sheikh possessed indecent digital images of two minor individuals and posted an indecent digital image of one of them to his social media account. These crimes, along with his efforts to conceal them from the government during his naturalization proceedings, warrant Sheikh’s denaturalization. Additionally, Sheikh obtained naturalization based on his military service, but later received an other than honorable discharge (for misconduct) from the Marine Corps after failing to serve honorably for five years, further warranting his denaturalization.

Debashis Ghosh (Age 62/India): Before Debashis Ghosh naturalized, he conspired to defraud investors of $2.5 million intended for the construction of an aircraft maintenance facility. After naturalizing, Ghosh, a native of India, continued the fraudulent scheme, misrepresenting the location and safekeeping of the investor funding. 平和(53岁/中国):在以春迪·和(Chun Di He)的姓名于1992年被下令驱逐出境后,平和于次年以不同身份申请了移民福利。该申请获得批准,他于2007年获得永久居留权。2013年,他以平和(Pin He)的身份及其移民记录入籍,从未披露其曾以春迪·和之名被下达驱逐令的事实。他是在隐瞒其先前身份并谎报其入籍资格的情况下获得公民身份的。

That application was granted, and in 2007, he obtained permanent residence. In 2013, he naturalized under the identity and immigration history of Pin He, without ever disclosing his prior removal order under the name Chun Di He. He obtained his citizenship after concealing his prior identity and misrepresenting his eligibility for citizenship.
乔治·奥富安·奥亚基雷(66岁/尼日利亚):乔治·奥富安·奥亚基雷出生于尼日利亚,于1986年10月18日首次以其真实姓名所签发的签证入境美国。1988年9月2日,奥亚基雷使用化名“奥利弗·贝内特·奥亚基雷”(Oliver Bennett Oyakhire)及虚假出生日期获得临时居民身份。1990年12月1日,奥亚基雷以该虚假身份成为合法永久居民。1995年9月12日,奥亚基雷以该虚假身份提交了入籍申请。该申请于1996年3月22日获得批准。1996年4月22日,奥亚基雷以化名奥利弗·贝内特·奥亚基雷成为归化公民。他是以虚假身份入籍的。

On Sept. 2, 1988, Oyakhire obtained temporary resident status using a false name, “Oliver Bennett Oyakhire,” and a false date of birth.
