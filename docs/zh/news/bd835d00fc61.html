伊利诺伊州释放致命醉驾非法移民后 ICE再次将其逮捕

发布日期 2026年11月13日

美国国土安全部（DHS）于2026年11月13日宣布，ICE在11月11日逮捕了墨西哥籍非法移民Vicente Torres-Vasquez。此前伊利诺伊州惩教部门在未通知ICE的情况下将其假释释放，未遵守ICE的拘留请求（detainer）。Torres-Vasquez因2011年醉驾致一人死亡、两人受伤被判处25年监禁，此次被捕时尚在服刑期间。

要点

  • 2012年他被判定犯有加重醉驾致死罪及两项加重醉驾致重伤罪，获刑25年。
  • ICE分别于2017年1月和2026年提出拘留请求，但Stateville惩教中心于2026年11月2日未通知ICE即将其假释释放。
  • ICE芝加哥执法与遣返行动处（ERO）于2026年11月11日将其逮捕，目前他被ICE拘留，等待遣返程序。
  • 他此前曾四次因醉驾被捕，其中三次被定罪，并曾多次非法入境美国，2010年已被移民法官下达最终遣返令。
  • DHS在声明中引用了国土安全部长Markwayne Mullin对伊利诺伊州庇护政策的批评，并列举了该州近期发生的其他涉及非法移民的犯罪案件。

影响对象

涉及正被ICE拘留或面临遣返程序的个人，以及ICE与限制配合移民拘留请求的州和地方辖区之间的执法互动。

ICE Arrests Illegal Alien Responsible for Fatal DUI After Illinois Sanctuary Politicians Released Him
发布日期:2026年11月13日
Release Date: November 13, 2026
该非法外国人此前曾因酒后驾车被逮捕四次
This illegal alien had previously been arrested FOUR TIMES for driving under the influence
华盛顿,美国国土安全部(DHS)发布以下声明,宣布美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕了一名此前因酒后驾车致人死亡而服刑,后被伊利诺伊州庇护政客释放的非法外国人。
WASHINGTON – The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement announcing that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) arrested an illegal alien who had been RELEASED by sanctuary politicians in Illinois, after serving a prison sentence for causing a fatal accident while driving under the influence.
文森特·托雷斯瓦斯克斯(Vicente Torres-Vasquez)是一名来自墨西哥的非法外国人,于2011年7月24日酒后驾车(DUI)导致一起致命车祸后,在伊利诺伊州沃康达(Wauconda)被捕。他的受害者是55岁的格雷戈里·埃罗尔·霍莫拉(Gregory Errol Homola),一名美国海军陆战队退伍军人,当时他正骑摩托车,被托雷斯瓦斯克斯撞上。另一辆车上的两名女性也在事故中受伤。
Vicente Torres-Vasquez, an illegal alien from Mexico, was arrested in Wauconda, Illinois after causing a fatal crash on July 24, 2011, while driving under the influence (DUI). His victim was 55-year-old Gregory Errol Homola, a veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps, who was riding his motorcycle when he was struck by Torres-Vasquez. Two other women in another vehicle were also injured in the accident.
受害者:格雷戈里·埃罗尔·霍莫拉
The victim: Gregory Errol Homola
托雷斯瓦斯克斯于2012年被判犯有加重酒后驾车致人死亡罪,以及两项加重酒后驾车致人重伤罪。他被判处25年监禁。
Torres-Vasquez was convicted in 2012 for aggravated driving under the influence resulting in the death of another person and TWO counts of aggravated driving under the influence resulting in great bodily injury. He was sentenced to 25 years in prison.
托雷斯瓦斯克斯此前曾因酒后驾车被逮捕四次,其中三次被定罪。
Torres-Vasquez had previously been arrested FOUR TIMES for driving under the influence, being convicted on three of the four counts.
凶手:文森特·托雷斯瓦斯克斯
The killer: Vicente Torres-Vasquez
2017年1月16日,ICE向位于伊利诺伊州坎顿(Canton)的伊利诺伊河畔惩教中心(IRCC)发出拘留令。2026年3月3日,IRCC在预期其假释释放日期之前,将托雷斯瓦斯克斯转移至位于伊利诺伊州克雷斯特希尔(Crest Hill)的斯泰特维尔惩教中心(SCC)。ICE随后又发出了另一份拘留令。但在2026年11月2日,SCC拒绝执行ICE的拘留令,在未通知ICE的情况下将其假释释放。
On January 16, 2017, ICE lodged a detainer with the Illinois River Correctional Center (IRCC) in Canton, Illinois. On March 3, 2026, IRCC transferred Torres-Vasquez to the Stateville Correctional Center (SCC) in Crest Hill, Illinois, in anticipation of his parole release date. ICE then lodged another detainer. But on November 2, 2026, SCC refused to honor the ICE detainer and RELEASED him on parole without notifying ICE.
11月11日,ICE执法与遣返行动局(ERO)芝加哥办事处逮捕了在逃的文森特·托雷斯瓦斯克斯。他将继续被ICE拘留,等待遣返程序。
On November 11, ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO) Chicago arrested Vicente Torres-Vasquez at large. He will remain in ICE custody pending removal proceedings.
“这名非法外国人酒后驾车致人死亡,被判处25年监禁。当ICE在其假释释放前发出拘留令时,J.B.普里茨克(J.B. Pritzker)治下伊利诺伊州的庇护政客拒绝与我们合作,在未通知ICE的情况下将其释放,”国土安全部部长马克韦恩·马林(Markwayne Mullin)表示。“幸运的是,ICE的英勇爱国者们最终还是成功逮捕了这名杀人犯,他现在将被驱逐出我国。这些冷酷无情的庇护政客必须停止将政治权力置于其选民安全之上。他们欠'天使家庭'(Angel Families)一个交代,应当与我们合作,以防止更多美国人的生命因非法外国人犯罪而丧失。”
“This illegal alien was sentenced to 25 years in prison after killing a man while drunk behind the wheel. When ICE lodged a detainer ahead of his release on parole, sanctuary politicians in J.B. Pritzker’s Illinois refused to cooperate with us and released him without notifying ICE,” said Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Thankfully, the brave patriots of ICE managed to arrest this killer anyway, and he will now be deported from our country. These heartless sanctuary politicians need to stop prioritizing political power over the safety of their constituents. They owe it to the Angel Families to work with us so that we can prevent any more American lives from being lost to illegal alien crime.”
托雷斯瓦斯克斯于2004年3月两次通过亚利桑那州非法入境美国,两次均被准予自愿离境。随后他在未知的日期和地点第三次非法入境该国。一名移民法官于2010年11月11日对其签发了最终遣返令。
Torres-Vasquez illegally entered the United States through Arizona TWICE in March 2004, and was granted voluntary departures both times. He then illegally entered the country for a third time at an unknown date and location. An Immigration Judge issued him a final order of removal on November 11, 2010.

Under Governor J.B. Pritzker, Illinois has become a hotbed of illegal alien crime. Some of the recent illegal alien crimes in the sanctuary state include:
8月,ICE逮捕了来自约旦的非法外国人哈米德·穆罕默德·哈米德·阿尔赛迪(Hamed Mohammed Hamed Alsaidi),此前他因在伊利诺伊州莫基纳(Mokena)持有八枚燃烧瓶而被捕。威尔县(Will County)的庇护政客拒绝执行ICE的拘留令将其释放,两天后ICE将其逮捕。
In August, ICE arrested Hamed Mohammed Hamed Alsaidi, an illegal alien from Jordan, after he had been arrested for having EIGHT Molotov cocktails in his possession in Mokena, Illinois. He had been released by sanctuary politicians in Will County who refused to honor ICE’s detainer, and ICE arrested him two days later.
6月,ICE对来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人哈尼奥·吉奥内尔·贝拉斯克斯穆罗(Janio Gionel Velasquez-Muro)发出拘留令,此前他因在伊利诺伊州凯恩县(Kane County)绑架并性侵一名9岁女童而被捕。
In June, ICE lodged a detainer for Janio Gionel Velasquez-Muro, an illegal alien from Venezuela, after he was arrested for kidnapping and sexually assaulting a 9-year-old girl in Kane County, Illinois.
6月,ICE逮捕了来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人、前教师焦万娜·梅赛德斯·莫雷诺·奥奇皮恩蒂(Giovanna Mercedes Moreno Occhipinti),她参与了2024年12月阿拉瓜火车帮(Tren de Aragua)在伊利诺伊州芝加哥一场住宅派对上制造的大规模枪击事件,该事件造成三人死亡,多人受伤。
In June, ICE arrested Giovanna Mercedes Moreno Occhipinti, an illegal alien from Venezuela and former teacher, for her involvement in a Tren de Aragua mass shooting in December 2024 that killed three people and injured several others at a house party in Chicago, Illinois.
3月,ICE对来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人何塞·梅迪纳梅迪纳(Jose Medina-Medina)发出拘留令,此前他因在伊利诺伊州芝加哥枪杀18岁的洛约拉学院学生谢里登·戈尔曼(Sheridan Gorman)而被捕。
In March, ICE lodged a detainer for Jose Medina-Medina, an illegal alien from Venezuela, after he was arrested for shooting and killing 18-year-old Loyola College student Sheridan Gorman in Chicago, Illinois.
2025年10月,ICE对来自尼加拉瓜的非法外国人雷特·杰斐逊·阿劳斯梅迪纳(Leyter Jeferson Arauz-Medina)发出拘留令,此前他因在伊利诺伊州芝加哥强奸一名54岁女性而被捕。
In October 2025, ICE lodged a detainer for Leyter Jeferson Arauz-Medina, an illegal alien from Nicaragua, after his arrest for raping a 54-year-old woman in Chicago, Illinois.
2025年10月,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕了拉杜勒·博约维奇(Radule Bojovic),这名来自黑山的非法外国人曾在汉诺威公园警察局担任宣誓警察。
In October 2025, ICE arrested Radule Bojovic, an illegal alien from Montenegro, who was working as a sworn police officer with the Hanover Park Police Department.
2025年9月,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)对何塞·哈维尔·科罗纳多梅萨(Jose Javier Coronado-Meza)发出拘留请求,这名来自委内瑞拉的非法外国人因在伊利诺伊州库克县以行刑方式杀害一名男子并将其尸体埋入混凝土中而被捕。
In September 2025, ICE lodged a detainer for Jose Javier Coronado-Meza, an illegal alien from Venezuela, after he was arrested for killing a man execution-style and then burying his body in concrete in Cook County, Illinois.
2025年7月,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕了路易斯·门多萨冈萨雷斯(Luis Mendoza-Gonzalez),这名来自墨西哥的非法外国人此前曾因在其名下房产上藏匿37岁的梅根·博斯(Megan Bos)的尸体而被捕,但此后却被湖县的庇护政客释放。
In July 2025, ICE arrested Luis Mendoza-Gonzalez, an illegal alien from Mexico, after he was released by sanctuary politicians in Lake County, despite being arrested for hiding the body of 37-year-old Megan Bos on his property.
2025年1月,来自危地马拉的非法外国人胡里奥·库库尔博尔(Julio Cucul-Bol)在伊利诺伊州厄巴纳酒后驾车造成车祸,致使20岁的凯蒂·亚伯拉罕(Katie Abraham)死亡。库库尔博尔目前面临肇事逃逸致人死亡、加重酒后驾驶致人死亡以及鲁莽杀人罪的指控。为纪念凯蒂,“中途封锁行动”(Operation Midway Blitz)于2025年9月启动。
In January 2025, Julio Cucul-Bol, an illegal alien from Guatemala, caused a drunk-driving crash that killed 20-year-old Katie Abraham in Urbana, Illinois. Cucul-Bol now faces charges of leaving the scene of a crash resulting in death, aggravated driving under the influence resulting in death, and reckless homicide. Operation Midway Blitz was launched in September 2025 in Katie’s honor.
