美国劳工部公布2026年度H-2A伙食费上限及H-2A/H-2B差旅生活补贴报销标准更新

发布日期 2026年4月7日生效日期 2026年4月7日

美国劳工部(DOL)就业与培训管理局(ETA)发布年度通知,更新了H-2A农业雇主可向工人收取的最高伙食费金额,以及H-2A和H-2B工人适用的差旅相关生活补贴(含住宿)的最低和最高报销金额。此次调整自2026年4月7日起生效。

要点

  • 由于2024年12月至2025年12月食品消费者价格指数(CPI-U for Food)上涨3.1%,H-2A雇主可向工人收取的每日最高伙食费从16.28美元上调至16.78美元。
  • 该上限适用于除放牧或牧场牲畜养殖以外的行业中每日提供三餐的H-2A雇主。
  • 就H-2A和H-2B项目的差旅生活补贴报销而言,雇主凭票据须报销的每日最高金额与2026年美国本土(CONUS)标准每日津贴一致,即伙食及杂项支出68.00美元,住宿110.00美元。
  • 对于不足一整天的伙食报销,雇主凭票据可将报销金额限制为最高伙食标准的75%,即51.00美元。
  • 若工人未提供票据,雇主无需报销超过伙食费规定中所列的最低金额。
  • 这些义务在工人达到特定工作合同条件时适用,例如完成合同期的50%,具体规定见现行H-2A和H-2B相关法规。

影响对象

雇用H-2A农业工人或H-2B非农业临时工人的雇主,以及持这些签证类别在美工作的外籍劳工均受影响。

建议行动

雇主应根据2026年新的最高伙食费标准和差旅补贴报销金额,更新职位要约及薪酬发放流程。

摘要：
SUMMARY:
美国劳工部（DOL）就业和培训管理局（ETA）发布本通知，宣布针对H-2A工人（放牧或牲畜放养以外职业）雇主每日提供三餐时可向工人收取的允许货币费用的年度更新。本通知还宣布H-2A和H-2B项目下所要求的与旅行相关的生活费报销的最低和最高金额。最后，本通知就雇主在必需的生活费用以及往返工作地点的合理旅行费用方面，涉及夜间住宿费用的义务予以提醒。
The Employment and Training Administration (ETA) of the Department of Labor (DOL) is issuing this notice to announce the annual updates to allowable monetary charges employers of H-2A workers, in occupations other than herding or production of livestock on the range, may charge workers when the employer provides three meals per day. This notice also announces the minimum and maximum amount of travel-related subsistence reimbursements required under the H-2A and H-2B programs. Finally, this notice includes a reminder regarding employers' obligations with respect to overnight lodging costs as part of required subsistence and reasonable travel costs to and from the worksite.

The U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services of the Department of Homeland Security will not approve an employer's petition for the admission of H-2A or H-2B nonimmigrant temporary workers in the U.S. unless the petitioner has received an H-2A or H-2B labor certification from DOL. 允许的膳食收费
Allowable Meal Charge
雇用从事放牧或牲畜放养以外职业工人的H-2A农业雇主，必须向工人提供每日三餐或免费且便利的烹饪设施。[1] 参见20 CFR 655.122(g)。若雇主提供膳食，招聘要约须注明向工人收取的膳食费用（如有）。参见同上。膳食收费金额受20 CFR 655.173管辖。
H-2A agricultural employers who are employing workers in occupations other than herding or production of livestock on the range must offer and provide workers three meals per day or free and convenient cooking facilities.[1] See20 CFR 655.122(g). Where the employer provides the meals, the job offer must state the charge, if any, to the worker for such meals. See id. The amount of meal charges is governed by 20 CFR 655.173.
根据法规，劳工部已确立一种方法，用于确定H-2A农业雇主在为工人每日提供三餐时可收取的最高金额。参见20 CFR 655.173(a)。（印刷页第17665页）该方法允许根据经更新的、未经季节性调整的“所有城市消费者食品消费价格指数”（CPI-U for Food）对上一年度的最高允许收费进行年度调整。参见同上。雇主为提供膳食而可向工人收取的最高金额，按上一年度CPI-U for Food的12个月百分比变化（即上一年度12月与再前一年12月之间的变化）进行年度调整。参见同上。外国劳工认证办公室（OFLC）认证官员亦可根据20 CFR 655.173(b)的规定，在雇主提供充分合理的理由和文件证明的情况下，允许雇主就每日提供三餐收取更高金额。
By regulation, DOL has established the methodology for determining the maximum amount that H-2A agricultural employers may charge workers for providing them with three meals per day. See20 CFR 655.173(a). ( printed page 17665) This methodology allows for annual adjustments of the previous year's maximum allowable charge based on the updated Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers for Food (CPI-U for Food), not seasonally adjusted. See id. The maximum amount employers may charge workers for providing meals is adjusted annually by the 12-month percentage change in the CPI-U for Food for the prior year ( i.e., between December of the year just concluded and December of the prior year). See id. The Office of Foreign Labor Certification (OFLC) Certifying Officer may also permit an employer to charge workers a higher amount for providing them with three meals a day if the higher amount is justified and sufficiently documented by the employer, as set forth in 20 CFR 655.173(b).
2024年12月至2025年12月期间，CPI-U for Food的百分比变化为3.1%。[2] 因此，H-2A允许膳食收费的年度更新计算方法为：以当前允许膳食收费（16.28美元）乘以2024年12月至2025年12月期间CPI-U for Food的12个月百分比变化（16.28美元 × 1.031 = 16.78美元）。[3] 据此，根据20 CFR 655.122(g)和655.173更新后的最高允许收费为每日16.78美元，除非OFLC认证官员根据20 CFR 655.173(b)的授权批准更高收费，否则雇主不得向工人收取每日超过16.78美元的费用。
The percentage change in the CPI-U for Food between December 2024 and December 2025 was 3.1 percent.[2] Thus, the annual update to the H-2A allowable meal charge is calculated by multiplying the current allowable meal charge ($16.28) by the 12-month percentage change in the CPI-U for Food between December 2024 and December 2025 ($16.28 × 1.031 = $16.78).[3] Accordingly, the updated maximum allowable charge under 20 CFR 655.122(g) and 655.173 is $16.78 per day, and an employer is not permitted to charge a worker more than $16.78 per day unless the OFLC Certifying Officer approves a higher charge, as authorized under 20 CFR 655.173(b).
与旅行相关的生活费报销
Reimbursement for Travel-Related Subsistence
H-2B和H-2A雇主必须支付工人在合理范围内产生的差旅和生活费用,包括从工人前来为雇主工作之地前往工作地点、以及从工作地点返回工人为雇主工作前所出发之地的差旅期间所产生的膳宿费用,以及工人为获得以H-2A或H-2B就业为目的进入美国的签证而产生的任何此类费用。参见20 CFR 655.122(h)(1)和(2)以及655.20(j)(1)(i)和(ii)。
H-2B and H-2A employers must pay reasonable travel and subsistence costs, including the costs of meals and lodging, incurred by workers during travel to the place of employment from the place from which the worker has come to work for the employer and from the place of employment to the place from which the worker departed to work for the employer, as well as any such costs incurred by the worker incident to obtaining a visa authorizing entry to the United States for the purpose of H-2A or H-2B employment. See20 CFR 655.122(h)(1) and (2) and 655.20(j)(1)(i) and (ii).
具体而言,如果工人完成了工作合同期的50%,H-2A雇主有责任为工人提供、预先支付或报销从工人前来为雇主工作之地前往工作地点所产生的合理交通费用及每日差旅相关生活费用。20 CFR 655.122(h)(1)。一般而言,在工人完成合同或在非因工人过错被解雇时,雇主必须提供(或在离职时支付)工人从工作地点返回其为雇主工作前所出发之地的交通费用及每日差旅相关生活费用。20 CFR 655.122(h)(2)。
Specifically, an H-2A employer is responsible for providing, paying in advance, or reimbursing a worker for the reasonable costs incurred by the worker for transportation and daily travel-related subsistence from the place from which the worker has come to work for the employer, if the worker completes 50 percent of the work contract period. 20 CFR 655.122(h)(1). In general, the employer must provide (or pay at the time of departure) the worker's transportation and daily travel-related subsistence from the place of employment to the place from which the worker departed to work for the employer upon the worker completing the contract or being terminated without cause. 20 CFR 655.122(h)(2).
同样,如果工人完成了招工令期限的50%,H-2B雇主有责任为工人提供、预先支付或报销从工人前来为雇主工作之地前往工作地点的交通费用及每日差旅相关生活费用。20 CFR 655.20(j)(1)(i)。在工人完成招工令期限或(因任何原因)提前被解雇后,雇主通常有责任提供(或在离职时支付)工人从工作地点返回其为雇主工作前所出发之地的返程交通费用及每日差旅相关生活费用。20 CFR 655.20(j)(1)(ii)。
Similarly, an H-2B employer is responsible for providing, paying in advance, or reimbursing a worker for transportation and daily travel-related subsistence from the place from which the worker has come to work for the employer, if the worker completes 50 percent of the job order period. 20 CFR 655.20(j)(1)(i). Upon the worker completing the job order period or being dismissed early (for any reason), the employer is generally responsible for providing (or paying at the time of departure) the worker's cost of return transportation and daily travel-related subsistence from the place of employment to the place from which the worker departed to work for the employer. 20 CFR 655.20(j)(1)(ii).
每日生活费的金额必须至少达到20 CFR 655.173(a)所允许的金额(如有根据20 CFR 655.173(b)批准的更高金额,则以该较高金额为准)。雇主根据收据证明须向工人报销的差旅相关生活费的每日最高金额,等于由总务管理局(GSA)依据41 CFR第301部分制定的标准美国本土(CONUS)每日津贴标准,该标准此前刊载于附录A,现刊载于https://www.gsa.gov/travel/plan-book/per-diem-rates。参见《美国本土(CONUS)每日津贴最高报销标准》,90 FR 40365(2025年8月19日)。2026年标准CONUS餐费及杂项费用标准为每日68.00美元,2026年标准CONUS住宿标准仍为每日110.00美元。参见90 FR 40365、40366。符合生活费报销条件的工人,在提供收据的情况下,有权获得最高至标准CONUS标准的膳宿费报销。在确定不足一整天的餐费报销适当金额时,雇主可根据GSA每日津贴标准,将凭收据报销的餐费限制为最高餐费报销额的75%,即51.00美元。参见https://www.gsa.gov/travel/plan-book/per-diem-rates。如果工人未提供收据,雇主没有义务支付超出上述20 CFR 655.173所规定最低标准的费用。
The amount of the daily subsistence must be at least the amount permitted in 20 CFR 655.173(a) (or the higher amount approved under 20 CFR 655.173(b), if any). The maximum daily amount an employer is required to reimburse workers for travel-related subsistence, as evidenced with receipts, is equal to the standard Continental United States (CONUS) per diem rate, as established by the General Services Administration (GSA) at 41 CFR part 301, formerly published in Appendix A and now found at https://www.gsa.gov/​travel/​plan-book/​per-diem-rates. See Maximum Per Diem Reimbursement Rates for the Continental United States (CONUS), 90 FR 40365 (Aug. 19, 2025). The standard CONUS meals and incidental expenses rate is $68.00 per day for 2026, and the standard CONUS lodging rate remains $110.00 per day for 2026. See90 FR 40365, 40366. Workers who qualify for subsistence reimbursement are entitled to reimbursement for meals and lodging up to the standard CONUS rates when they provide receipts. In determining the appropriate amount of reimbursement for meals for less than a full day, the employer may limit the meal expense reimbursement, with receipts, to 75 percent of the maximum reimbursement for meals, or $51.00, based on the GSA per diem schedule. See https://www.gsa.gov/​travel/​plan-book/​per-diem-rates. If a worker does not provide receipts, the employer is not obligated to reimburse above the minimum stated at 20 CFR 655.173, as specified above.
此外,如果工人完成了工作合同期的50%,雇主有责任承担工人前往工作地点所必需的费用。雇主还须承担返程交通费用。雇主为往返工作地点所须支付的交通费金额不得低于(但也不要求高于)最经济合理的费用。这些要求同样适用于工人在美国境内或国际间前往雇主工作地点的情形。参见20 CFR 655.122(h)(1)和(2)以及655.20(j)(1)(i)和(ii)。
In addition, the employer is responsible for those costs necessary for the worker to travel to the place of employment if the worker completes 50 percent of the work contract period. The employer also is responsible for the costs of return transportation. The amount an employer must pay for transportation to and from the place of employment must be no less than (and is not required to be more than) the most economical and reasonable costs. These requirements apply equally to instances where the worker is traveling within the U.S. or internationally to the employer's worksite. See20 CFR 655.122(h)(1) and (2) and 655.20(j)(1)(i) and (ii).
有关雇主何时须负责住宿费用的进一步信息,请参阅美国劳工部(DOL)外国劳工认证办公室(OFLC)网站上的《膳食费用与差旅生活费》,网址为https://flag.dol.gov/wage-data/subsistence-rates,以及OFLC网站上的《关于工作邀约和雇主义务的H-2B常见问题》,网址为https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/faqs/print。
For further information on when the employer is responsible for lodging costs, please see the DOL's Meal Charges and Travel Subsistence, on OFLC's website at https://flag.dol.gov/​wage-data/​subsistence-rates, and H-2B Frequently Asked Questions on Job Offers and Employer Obligations, on OFLC's website at https://www.dol.gov/​agencies/​eta/​foreign-labor/​faqs/​print.
