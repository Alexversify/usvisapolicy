DHS通报打击庇护欺诈行动，公布多起最新定罪案例

发布日期 2026年11月10日

美国国土安全部(DHS)和美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)通报了过去一年中协助联邦检方打击庇护欺诈的多起案件。DHS表示，2026年5月发布的指令扩大了移民及海关执法局(ICE)根据文件欺诈法律采取执法行动的权限，包括针对提交虚假庇护申请的律师。多名个人及人口走私团伙因庇护欺诈、婚姻欺诈及国家安全问题被定罪、判刑或递解出境。

要点

  • DHS表示，USCIS在过去一年中协助了多起针对庇护欺诈的联邦起诉。
  • 2026年5月的DHS指令要求ICE依据8 U.S.C. 1324c(d)加强执法，包括对提交虚假庇护申请的律师采取行动。
  • 2026年10月29日，五名古巴籍人士和一名美国公民因人口走私、庇护欺诈及洗钱共谋在佛罗里达被判刑。
  • 伊利诺伊州一名商人因涉及虚假庇护申请的移民欺诈罪于2026年5月被判处九年监禁。
  • USCIS的审查工作促成了一名承认曾是MS-13成员并犯有谋杀罪的萨尔瓦多籍人士在USCIS面谈期间被捕。
  • DHS表示，根据8 U.S.C. 1324c(d)、18 U.S.C. 1546及18 U.S.C. 1001，庇护欺诈可被作为重罪起诉。

影响对象

庇护申请人、移民律师以及提供移民服务的机构受到执法力度加大的影响。

建议行动

申请人和律师应确保庇护申请信息的真实准确，任何人均可通过USCIS举报表格(Tip Form)举报可疑欺诈行为。

国土安全部正在打击庇护欺诈者
DHS is Taking Down Asylum Fraudsters
发布日期：2026年11月10日
Release Date: November 10, 2026
庇护欺诈可以作为重罪起诉
Asylum fraud can be prosecuted as a felony
华盛顿, , 美国国土安全部（DHS）发布了以下声明，重点介绍了美国公民及移民服务局（USCIS）在打击庇护欺诈战争中发挥关键作用的近期实例。在过去一年中，USCIS为多起联邦检控案件提供了关键协助，这些案件致力于揪出那些为获得美国保护而说谎的人。
WASHINGTON — The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) released the following statement highlighting recent examples of how U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is playing a key role in the war on asylum fraud. Over the past year, USCIS has provided pivotal assistance to several federal prosecutions focused on ferreting out those who lie to get the protection of the United States.
“拜登政府执政的四年里，边境大开，这对我们国家而言是一场灾难。这场非法移民浪潮的很大一部分涉及对我们庇护制度的欺诈，”国土安全部部长马克韦恩·穆林表示。“USCIS的工作人员每天都在努力追查并打击庇护欺诈者，确保这些企图钻制度空子的罪犯受到法律的制裁。如果我们的庇护制度得不到保障，我们国家的安全就得不到保障。”
“Four long years of wide-open borders under the Biden Administration was a disaster for our nation. A massive part of this illegal migration involved defrauding our asylum system,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “The men and women of USCIS are working every day to go after and take down asylum fraudsters, ensuring that these criminals face justice for trying to game the system. If our asylum system is not secure, our nation is not secure.”
今年5月，国土安全部发布了一项指令，指示美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）采取进一步措施打击虚假庇护申请。其中包括制定反欺诈政策，以进一步执行《美国法典》第8编第1324c(d)条，该法律规定了对文件欺诈行为的处罚。由于该指令的实施，ICE的律师现在拥有更大的权力来执行该法律，包括对在移民法庭提交虚假庇护申请的移民律师采取执法行动。
In May, DHS released a directive instructing U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) to take additional steps to crack down on fraudulent asylum claims. This includes developing anti-fraud policies that will further enforce 8 U.S.C. § 1324c(d), a law that establishes penalties for violations of document fraud. As a result of this directive, ICE attorneys have greater authority to enforce this law, including enforcement actions against immigration attorneys who file false asylum claims in an immigration court.

2026年10月29日，五名古巴籍外国人和一名美国公民在佛罗里达州因参与一项涉及偷渡外国人、庇护欺诈和洗钱计划的国际共谋案而被判刑，该阴谋使数千名外国人非法进入美国。美国司法部长去年首次宣布了在一份补充起诉书中对12名涉案人员的指控，指控其参与该共谋活动。根据法庭文件，被告经营着一个庞大的偷渡网络，通过准备签证申请、洗钱数百万美元款项以及滥用移民程序，协助古巴公民非法进入美国。该活动最初规模很小，后来发展成为一个商业企业。作为该企业的一部分，被告经营着一家名为“Asesoria Y Servicios Migratorios LLC”的虚假移民服务公司，该公司承诺办理庇护申请、工作许可以及合法永久居留申请。
On October 29, 2026, five Cuban aliens and one U.S. citizen were sentenced in Florida for their roles in an international conspiracy involving alien smuggling, asylum fraud, and money laundering schemes that brought thousands of aliens illegally into the United States. The U.S. Attorney General made the initial announcement last year of charges against 12 individuals in a superseding indictment for their roles in the conspiracy. According to court documents, the defendants operated a prolific alien smuggling operation that facilitated the unlawful entry of Cuban nationals into the United States by preparing visa applications, laundering millions of dollars in payment, and exploiting the immigration process. What began as a small operation grew into a commercial enterprise. As part of the enterprise, the defendants operated a sham immigration business, Asesoria Y Servicios Migratorios LLC, which promised asylum filings, work permits, and lawful permanent residency applications.

该公司在客户不知情或未同意的情况下，大量炮制千篇一律的庇护申请，甚至阻止客户访问他们自己的USCIS在线账户。由于这次成功的调查，其他多名人员也已被定罪并判刑。
The company churned out boilerplate asylum claims without clients’ knowledge or consent and even blocked them from accessing their own USCIS online accounts. Several other individuals have been convicted and sentenced because of this successful investigation.
25岁的黄碧琪（“艾希礼”）和35岁的杜文天于2026年9月被起诉。黄碧琪面临一项在入籍程序中作虚假陈述的指控和一项欺诈获取枪支的指控。杜文天面临一项外国人非法持有枪支的指控。根据法庭文件，黄碧琪于2023年持学生签证进入美国，并在2025年的绿卡面谈中就其婚姻状况作出虚假陈述后获得了合法永久居民身份。杜文天于2022年持B1/B2非移民签证进入美国，约一年后申请庇护。今年6月，黄碧琪搬到纽约并结识了杜文天。此后她申请加入美国空军，并告诉招募人员，她想尽快入伍以获得公民身份、与现任配偶离婚并与杜文天结婚。
Biqi “Ashley” Huang, 25, and Wentian Du, 35, were indicted in September 2026. Huang faces one count of making a false statement in a naturalization proceeding and one count of fraudulent acquisition of a firearm. Du faces one count of being an alien in possession of a firearm. According to court documents, Huang entered the U.S. on a student visa in 2023 and received lawful permanent resident status after misrepresenting the status of her marriage during a 2025 Green Card interview. Du entered the U.S. in 2022 on a B1/B2 nonimmigrant visa and applied for asylum about a year later. In June, Huang moved to New York and met Du. She then applied to join the U.S. Air Force, telling a recruiter that she wanted to enlist to obtain citizenship as soon as possible, divorce her current spouse, and marry Du.
现年47岁的纳齐拉·哈吉·扎达是一名阿富汗籍公民，此前居住在德克萨斯州沃斯堡。她于2026年9月在承认自己是外国恐怖分子后被驱逐出美国，这是美国外国恐怖分子驱逐法庭（ATRC）审理的首例案件。扎达曾支持一项于2024年选举日实施受“伊斯兰国”启发的大规模枪击事件的阴谋，其子阿卜杜拉·哈吉·扎达及女婿纳西尔·艾哈迈德·陶赫迪此前已因该案被捕并被定罪。扎达现已被永久禁止入境美国。
Nazira Haji Zada, 47, an Afghan national previously residing in Fort Worth, Texas, was removed from the United States in September 2026 after conceding that she is an alien terrorist, in the first-ever case before the United States Alien Terrorist Removal Court (ATRC). Zada supported a plot to commit an ISIS-inspired mass shooting on Election Day in 2024 for which her son, Abdullah Haji Zada, and son-in-law, Nasir Ahmad Tawhedi, were previously arrested and convicted. Zada is now permanently inadmissible to the U.S.

Nazira Haji Zada
尤尼尔·利马-桑托斯(Yuniel Lima-Santos),31岁,居住在佛罗里达州坦帕市,于2026年8月就偷渡外国人、庇护欺诈和洗钱罪名认罪。利马-桑托斯与他人合谋经营一个偷渡外国人组织,该组织怂恿数千名古巴籍外国人使用欺诈性的电子旅行许可系统(ESTA)申请和签证豁免进入美国。被告在多个社交媒体平台上宣传其偷渡服务和虚假签证服务,招揽古巴籍公民及其他外国人试图非法进入美国并以欺诈手段获得合法身份。利马-桑托斯通过协助将外国人从包括古巴在内的原籍国转移至美国,推进了该共谋行为。这包括购买国际和国内航班机票,将外国人运送进入美国并在美国境内转运。
Yuniel Lima-Santos, 31, of Tampa, Florida, pleaded guilty in August 2026 to alien smuggling, asylum fraud, and money laundering. Lima-Santos conspired with others to operate an alien smuggling organization that encouraged thousands of Cuban aliens to enter the United States using fraudulent Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) applications and visa waivers. The defendants advertised their smuggling services and bogus visa services across multiple social media platforms to solicit Cuban nationals and other aliens to attempt to illegally enter the U.S. and fraudulently obtain lawful status. Lima-Santos furthered the conspiracy by helping move aliens from their countries of origin, including Cuba, into the U.S. This included purchasing international and domestic flights to transport aliens into and throughout the United States.
何塞·格雷戈里奥·索萨·卡尔多纳(Jose Gregorio Sosa Cardona)是芝加哥郊区的一名商人,于2026年5月因移民欺诈、持有儿童色情制品和实施税务欺诈被判处九年监禁。索萨·卡尔多纳经营着德尔塔全球解决方案公司(Delta Global Solutions, Inc.),该公司协助个人申请庇护、移民签证、永久居民卡及其他移民福利。自2020年至2024年,索萨·卡尔多纳与其公司的两名员工及其他人合谋,明知故犯地向美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)提供虚假和欺诈信息,为寻求福利的外籍客户提供便利。索萨·卡尔多纳被指控伪造外国执法报告和外国政党成员资格信,以虚假方式佐证其客户的庇护申请。
Jose Gregorio Sosa Cardona, a suburban Chicago businessman, was sentenced in May 2026 to nine years in prison for immigration fraud, possessing child pornography, and committing tax fraud. Sosa Cardona operated Delta Global Solutions, Inc., which assisted individuals applying for asylum, immigrant visas, lawful permanent residence cards, and other immigration benefits. From 2020 to 2024, Sosa Cardona conspired with two employees of his firm and others to knowingly provide false and fraudulent information to USCIS for his foreign national clients who were seeking benefits. Sosa Cardona was accused of fabricating foreign law enforcement reports and foreign political party membership letters to falsely corroborate his clients’ asylum claims.
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)的审查和审核工作还有助于识别可能对公共安全或国家安全构成威胁的个人。
USCIS screening and vetting also helps identify individuals who may pose public-safety or national-security concerns.
例如,来自萨尔瓦多的埃德温·安东尼奥·埃尔南德斯(Edwin Antonio Hernandez),27岁,于2026年1月在美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)预约期间被美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕。通过对其庇护申请和工作许可申请的审查和审核,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)发现了有关其犯罪记录和移民身份的信息。
For example, Edwin Antonio Hernandez, 27, of El Salvador, was arrested in January 2026 by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement during a USCIS appointment. Through the screening and vetting of his asylum and employment authorization applications, USCIS identified information concerning his criminal history and immigration status.

Edwin Antonio Hernandez
埃尔南德斯此前曾非法进入美国,并于2017年被一名移民法官下令驱逐出境。在羁押期间,埃尔南德斯承认自己是MS-13帮派成员,并供认通过枪击、酷刑、刺伤和肢解的方式在萨尔瓦多杀害了五人。
Hernandez had previously entered the United States unlawfully and was ordered removed by an immigration judge in 2017. While in custody, Hernandez admitted to being an MS-13 member and confessed to murdering five people in El Salvador through shooting, torturing, stabbing, and dismemberment.
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)将一如既往地致力于侦查和震慑移民福利欺诈行为,维护庇护制度的完整性,并支持执法合作伙伴,追究那些为谋取犯罪利益而利用弱势个人和移民程序者的责任。
USCIS remains committed to detecting and deterring immigration benefit fraud, safeguarding the integrity of the asylum system, and supporting law enforcement partners in holding accountable those who exploit vulnerable individuals and immigration processes for criminal gain.
如需向美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)举报涉嫌移民福利欺诈或滥用行为,请使用USCIS举报表(USCIS Tip Form)。
To report suspected immigration benefit fraud or abuse to USCIS, please use the USCIS Tip Form.
除8 U.S.C. § 1324c(d)外,庇护欺诈行为还可依据18 U.S.C. § 1546和18 U.S.C. § 1001作为重罪予以起诉。
In addition to 8 U.S.C. § 1324c(d), asylum fraud can also be prosecuted as a felony under both 18 U.S.C. § 1546 and 18 U.S.C. § 1001.
