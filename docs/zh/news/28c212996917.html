DHS提议取消USCIS对既往批准决定的遵从,影响工作签证延期申请

发布日期 2026年10月14日

美国国土安全部(DHS)提议废除要求USCIS在审理通过Form I-129提交的非移民工作签证延期申请时对涉及相同事实的既往批准给予遵从的规定(8 CFR 214.1(c)(5))。根据现行规定,除非存在重大错误、重大情况变化或新的实质性信息,USCIS通常应遵从此前的批准决定。该提案尚未最终确定或生效,公众可在2026年11月13日前提交意见。

要点

  • DHS提议废除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5),该条款要求USCIS在审理涉及相同事实的Form I-129延期申请时遵从既往批准决定。
  • 该规定适用于包括H-1B在内的通过Form I-129提交的非移民工作签证延期申请。
  • DHS表示,此项修改旨在将证明持续符合资格的全部举证责任重新归还给申请人、请愿人或受益人,即使是此前已获批准类别的延期申请。
  • 该提案尚未最终确定或生效,公众意见提交截止日期为2026年11月13日。
  • 该提案不涉及任何费用变更。

影响对象

为非移民工作签证持有者提交Form I-129延期申请的雇主,以及延期申请一直依赖此前USCIS批准的非移民员工,尤其是H-1B等类别的申请人。

建议行动

受影响的雇主和员工可通过regulations.gov在2026年11月13日前提交意见,并应为未来延期申请面临更严格审查做好准备。

二、背景
II. Background
A. 拟议监管行动的目的和摘要
A. Purpose and Summary of the Proposed Regulatory Action
本拟议规则的目的是删除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)。该法规指示美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)官员在审理涉及相同当事人和相同基础事实的I-129表格(非移民工作者申请书)所提交的请求时,对申请人、请愿人或受益人资格的先前认定给予尊重。[1] 这一要求最常适用于申请雇主在相同的先前已获批准的非移民工作者类别下,寻求延长外国人在美国的雇佣或授权停留期限的申请书。根据现行法规,除非存在重大错误、重大变化或新的重大信息,美国公民及移民服务局通常必须尊重对非移民工作者类别资格的先前认定。参见8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)。
The purpose of this proposed rule is to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5). That regulation instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination of a petitioner's, applicant's, or beneficiary's eligibility when adjudicating a request filed on Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker, involving the same parties and the same underlying facts.[1] This requirement most often applies when a petitioning employer seeks to extend a petition for an alien's employment or authorized stay in the United States under the same previously approved nonimmigrant worker classification. Under the current regulation, USCIS must generally defer to the prior determination of eligibility for the nonimmigrant worker classification unless there was a material error, a material change, or new material information. 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5).
国土安全部在颁布8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)时曾表示,尽管此前已获批准,申请人“仍将负有举证责任,须提交所有必要且相关的证据”,以证明其符合所申请非移民工作者类别的资格。参见《H-1B要求现代化、为F-1项目提供灵活性以及影响其他非移民工作者的项目改进》,89 FR 103054, 103093(2024年12月18日)。该机构还表示,“尊重先前认定并不等同于美国公民及移民服务局不对申请书进行审查。”同上。
When DHS promulgated 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), the agency stated that petitioners would “continue to have the burden to present all required and relevant evidence” to establish eligibility for the requested nonimmigrant worker classification notwithstanding the prior approval. See Modernizing H-1B Requirements, Providing Flexibility in the F-1 Program, and Program Improvements Affecting Other Nonimmigrant Workers,89 FR 103054, 103093 (Dec. 18, 2024). The agency also stated that “deference does not equate to a lack of USCIS review of the petition.” Id.
然而，国土安全部(DHS)现在认为，该遵从规定的实际效果与上述声明不一致。具体而言，遵从规定和政策实际上将证明有理由偏离先前批准的举证责任从申请人转移给了美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)官员。国土安全部还担心，遵从政策产生的实际效果是，促使USCIS官员在裁决延期申请时优先考虑与先前批准保持一致以及裁决效率，而不是充分审查所有事实和证据以确保申请人持续符合非移民工人福利的资格。因此，国土安全部认为，部分申请人已将遵从政策理解为，在就先前批准申请延期时，减轻了其证明持续符合所申请非移民工人类别资格的义务。总体而言，遵从规定和政策可能通过降低对非移民工人类别延期申请的审查力度而损害项目诚信，从而可能使错误的先前批准得以延续，并造成一种使欺诈性和无实质理由的申请在批准前更不易被发现的环境。参见本序言第三部分。
DHS now believes, however, that the practical effect of the deference regulation is inconsistent with those statements. In particular, the deference regulation and policy effectively shift the burden from the petitioner to the USCIS officer to justify departing from a prior approval. DHS is also concerned that the deference policy has had the practical effect of encouraging USCIS officers to prioritize consistency with prior approvals and efficiency in adjudication of extension requests rather than fully exploring all facts and evidence to ensure continued eligibility for the nonimmigrant worker benefit. As a result, DHS believes that some petitioners have interpreted the deference policy as reducing their obligation to demonstrate continued eligibility for the requested nonimmigrant worker classification when seeking an extension of a prior approval. Overall, the deference regulation and policy risk undermining program integrity by potentially reducing scrutiny of extension requests of nonimmigrant worker classifications, thereby potentially perpetuating erroneous prior approvals and creating an environment in which fraudulent and meritless petitions are less likely to be detected before approval. See Section III of this preamble.
因此，国土安全部提议删除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)条。国土安全部认为，这一改动将使国土安全部的法规和USCIS的裁决实践更好地与该机构对《移民与国籍法》（INA或该法）的长期解释保持一致，该解释将证明符合所有移民福利资格的举证责任置于申请人身上。参见INA第291条，8 U.S.C. 1361；Matter of Chawathe案，25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7（USCIS行政上诉办公室，2010年）（“证明符合所寻求福利资格的举证责任完全由申请人承担”）。取消遵从要求还将有助于确保USCIS官员在每个案件中都充分审查事实和证据，以确保持续符合资格。总体而言，国土安全部认为，这一改动将促进更全面的裁决，加强非移民工人福利裁决中的项目诚信，降低错误批准得以延续的风险，并支持本届政府在更好地保护美国工人和更忠实地执行国会制定的移民法方面所做的更广泛努力。[2]
Therefore, DHS proposes to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5). DHS believes that this change would better align DHS regulations and USCIS adjudicatory practice with the agency's longstanding interpretation of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA or Act), which places the burden of proof in establishing eligibility for all immigration benefits on the petitioner. See INA sec. 291, 8 U.S.C. 1361; Matter of Chawathe, 25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7 (USCIS AAO, 2010) (“The burden of proving eligibility for the benefit sought remains entirely with the applicant”). Removing the deference requirement would also help to ensure that USCIS officers fully review the facts and evidence in every case to ensure continued eligibility. Overall, DHS believes that this change would promote more complete adjudications, strengthen program integrity in the adjudication of nonimmigrant worker benefits, reduce the risk of perpetuating erroneous approvals, and support the Administration's broader efforts to better protect U.S. workers and more faithfully enforce the immigration laws enacted by Congress.[2]

C. 停留期限延期与申请有效期延期
C. Extensions of Stay and Petition Validity
许多外国人以非移民签证类别进入美国。其中一些类别允许外国人在美国从事临时性的受雇工作或提供其他服务。见移民与国籍法(INA)第101(a)(15)条和第214条,8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(15)和1184。就在美国从事受雇工作或提供其他服务而言,每一类别都有其各自的资格要求,以及与准入或身份期限、身份维持、变更工作或雇主的能力以及延长非移民身份的能力相关的要求。同上。
Many aliens come to the United States under a nonimmigrant visa classification. Some of these classifications permit aliens to be employed or otherwise provide services in the United States for temporary periods. See INA secs. 101(a)(15) and 214, 8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(15) and 1184. Each classification for purposes of employment or otherwise performing services in the United States has its own eligibility requirements and requirements related to the period of admission or status, maintenance of status, the ability to change jobs or employers, and the ability to extend nonimmigrant status. Id.
对于某些非移民签证类别,寻求外国人服务超过此前获准停留期限的申请人,可以通过表格I-129《非移民工作者申请书》提交延长停留期限的申请。[5] 8 CFR 214.1(c)(1)。延长停留期限通常仅在外国人在提交申请时仍处于并已维持其非移民身份的情况下才予以批准。8 CFR 214.1(c)(6)。在通过表格I-129申请延长停留期限时,申请人必须提交证明外国人已维持其身份的证据,以及法规规定并根据表格说明要求的所有其他初始证据。见8 CFR 214.1(c)(1)、(6);表格I-129说明,《非移民工作者申请书填表说明》,https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-129instr.pdf(2026年9月9日)。
For certain nonimmigrant visa classifications, a petitioner seeking the services of an alien beyond the previously granted period of admission may file an extension of stay request on Form I-129, Petition for Nonimmigrant Worker.[5] 8 CFR 214.1(c)(1). An extension of stay is ordinarily only granted if the alien is still in, and has maintained, his or her nonimmigrant status at the time the request is filed. 8 CFR 214.1(c)(6). When requesting an extension of stay on Form I-129, the petitioner must submit evidence that the alien maintained his or her status along with all other initial evidence specified in the regulations and in accordance with form instructions. See8 CFR 214.1(c)(1), (6); Form I-129 Instructions, Instructions for Petition for Nonimmigrant Worker, https://www.uscis.gov/​sites/​default/​files/​document/​forms/​i-129instr.pdf (Sept. 9, 2026).
延长停留期限的申请通常与延长申请有效期的申请一并提交,后者是请求允许外国人在相同的非移民类别下继续为其现任雇主工作的申请。[6] 申请延期和停留期限延期均通过表格I-129提交,并随附证明继续符合该非移民类别资格的证据,以及法规规定并根据表格说明要求的所有其他初始证据。[7]
An extension of stay request is often submitted together with a request for an extension of petition validity, which is a request to allow the alien to continue to work for his or her current employer in the same nonimmigrant classification.[6] The petition extension and extension of stay request are filed on Form I-129 along with evidence of continued eligibility for the nonimmigrant classification, and all other initial evidence specified in the regulations and in accordance with form instructions.[7]
申请延长申请有效期是以此前对该非移民类别资格的认定为前提的。尽管存在此前的资格认定,但在任何移民申请中,包括申请延长申请有效期的请求,举证责任始终由申请人承担。见INA第291条,8 U.S.C. 1361。[8] 对于在资格未获证明的情况下寻求移民利益的后续申请,移民官员并不因先前已获批准而有义务予以批准。[9] 先前的批准可能存在错误,在这种情况下,如果裁决官员并未意识到该错误,而对该先前的资格认定给予遵从,则可能导致不应获批的申请被持续批准。即使先前的批准并无错误,申请人在每一次利益申请中仍须承担证明其符合资格的举证责任。USCIS根据个案记录中的证据,逐案作出决定。[10]
A request for an extension of petition validity follows a previous finding of eligibility for the nonimmigrant classification. Although there is a previous finding of eligibility, the burden of proof in any immigration request—including a request for an extension of petition validity—remains on the petitioner. See INA sec. 291, 8 U.S.C. 1361.[8] Officers are not bound to approve subsequent petitions seeking immigration benefits where eligibility has not been demonstrated, merely because of a prior approval.[9] The prior approval may have been erroneous, in which case giving deference to that prior determination of eligibility because the adjudicating officer was not aware of the error may perpetuate unmeritorious approvals. Even if the prior approval was not erroneous, the burden of proof remains on the petitioner to establish eligibility in each benefit request. USCIS decides each matter according to the evidence of record on a case-by-case basis.[10]
尽管存在上述已确立的原则,8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)所载法规仍指示USCIS官员,在裁决涉及相同当事方和相同基础事实、通过表格I-129提交的申请时,应对此前的资格认定给予遵从,除非该官员认定先前的批准存在实质性错误、情况或资格要求发生实质性变化,或存在对资格产生不利影响的新的实质性信息。如本序言第III节所述,国土安全部(DHS)认为,该遵从性法规与法定要求(即申请人必须承担证明其符合移民利益资格的举证责任)存在冲突,并且在某些情况下可能不当限制裁决官员固有的事实认定权力。
Despite these well-established principles, the regulation at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination of eligibility when adjudicating a request filed on Form I-129 involving the same parties and the same underlying facts unless the officer determines that there was a material error involved with a prior approval, a material change in circumstances or eligibility requirements, or new, material information that adversely impacts eligibility. As discussed in Section III. of this preamble, DHS believes that the deference regulation is in tension with the statutory requirement that the petitioner must bear the burden to demonstrate eligibility for an immigration benefit, and it may unduly limit adjudicating officers' inherent fact-finding authority in certain cases.
D. USCIS裁决中遵从原则的历史沿革
D. History of Deference in USCIS Adjudications
尽管“遵从原则”（deference）的成文化相对较近，但美国公民及移民服务局（USCIS）自2004年以来通过一系列政策措施一直在实施和废止“遵从原则”，有其历史渊源。首先，在2004年4月，USCIS发布了一份备忘录，讨论了先前USCIS裁决的重要性。参见USCIS，《在随后关于申请延期的资格认定中，CIS先前批准非移民申请的重要性》（“The Significance of a Prior CIS Approval of a Nonimmigrant Petition in the Context（印刷页65122）of a Subsequent Determination Regarding Eligibility for Extension of Petition Validity”），HQPRD 72/11.3（2004年4月23日）。该备忘录虽然承认USCIS有权对先前的认定提出质疑，且每一案件均须根据案卷证据逐案判定，但首次确立了以下政策：指示裁决官员在涉及相同当事方（申请人和受益人）及相同基础事实的非移民申请延期事项中，对先前的资格认定给予遵从，除非存在实质性错误、情况发生重大变化，或出现新的实质性信息。
While the codification of deference was relatively recent, USCIS has had a history of implementing and rescinding deference through a series of policy actions beginning in 2004. First, in April 2004, USCIS issued a memorandum discussing the significance of prior USCIS adjudications. See USCIS, “The Significance of a Prior CIS Approval of a Nonimmigrant Petition in the Context ( printed page 65122) of a Subsequent Determination Regarding Eligibility for Extension of Petition Validity,” HQPRD 72/11.3 (Apr. 23, 2004). This memorandum, while recognizing USCIS' authority to question prior determinations and that each matter must be decided according to the evidence of record on a case-by-case basis, first set out the policy instructing adjudications officers to give deference to a prior determination of eligibility in matters relating to an extension of nonimmigrant petition validity involving the same parties (petitioner and beneficiary) and the same underlying facts, unless there was material error, a substantial change in circumstances, or new material information.
2017年10月，USCIS发布了一份备忘录，废止了2004年的指引，指出2004年备忘录不当地将举证责任从申请人转移至USCIS。参见USCIS，《废止关于在非移民身份延期申请裁决中对先前资格认定给予遵从的指引》（“Rescission of Guidance Regarding Deference to Prior Determinations of Eligibility in the Adjudication of Petitions for Extension of Nonimmigrant Status”），PM-602-0151（2017年10月23日）。在2017年备忘录中，USCIS还表示担忧，认为该“遵从”政策在某些情况下不当地限制了裁决官员固有的事实认定权。
In October 2017, USCIS issued a memorandum which rescinded the 2004 guidance, expressing concerns that the 2004 memorandum improperly shifted the burden from the petitioner to USCIS. See USCIS, “Rescission of Guidance Regarding Deference to Prior Determinations of Eligibility in the Adjudication of Petitions for Extension of Nonimmigrant Status,” PM-602-0151 (Oct. 23, 2017). In the 2017 memorandum, USCIS also expressed concerns that the deference policy unduly limited adjudicating officers' inherent fact-finding authority in certain cases.
2021年4月，USCIS恢复了其“遵从”政策，并将其纳入《USCIS政策手册》。参见《USCIS政策手册》第2卷，A部分，第4章《停留延期、身份变更及申请延期》（“Extension of Stay, Change of Status, and Extension of Petition Validity”）。该政策手册承认，官员不应仅因先前已获批准而必须批准后续申请，但如果资格未得到证明，则无须批准，同时强调对于涉及相同当事方及基础事实的情形，官员应遵从先前的认定。根据该政策手册，如果官员认定对先前批准给予遵从不恰当，则必须在拒绝决定、证据请求（RFE）或拟拒绝通知（NOID）中说明先前的批准情况，并阐明不遵从先前认定的理由（例如，由于实质性错误、情况变化，或出现新的不利实质性信息）。参见同上。官员必须依照8 CFR 103.2(b)(16)(i)的规定，给予申请人就新信息作出回应的机会。参见同上。
In April 2021, USCIS reinstituted its deference policy and incorporated it into the USCIS Policy Manual. See USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 2, Part A, Chapter 4 “Extension of Stay, Change of Status, and Extension of Petition Validity.” The policy manual acknowledges that officers are not required to approve subsequent petitions where eligibility has not been demonstrated strictly because of a prior approval but stressed that they should defer to prior determinations involving the same parties and underlying facts. According to the policy manual, if an officer determines that deference to a prior approval is not appropriate, the officer must acknowledge the previous approval(s) in the denial, Request for Evidence (RFE), or Notice of Intent to Deny (NOID) and articulate the reason for not deferring to the previous determination (for example, due to a material error, change in circumstances, or new adverse material information). See id. Officers must provide the petitioner with an opportunity to respond to the new information consistent with 8 CFR 103.2(b)(16)(i). See id.
2024年12月，国土安全部（DHS）通过最终规则《现代化H-1B要求、提供F-1项目灵活性及影响其他非移民工人的项目改进》（“Modernizing H-1B Requirements, Providing Flexibility in the F-1 Program, and Program Improvements Affecting Other Nonimmigrant Workers”），89 FR 103054（2024年12月18日）（“现代化最终规则”），将其“遵从”政策成文化，纳入8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)。现行8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)规定，在裁决涉及相同当事方及相同基础事实的I-129表（《非移民工人申请书》）时，USCIS应对其先前的资格认定给予遵从，除非存在实质性错误、实质性变化，或出现对资格产生不利影响的新的实质性信息。
In December 2024, DHS codified its deference policy at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) through the final rule Modernizing H-1B Requirements, Providing Flexibility in the F-1 Program, and Program Improvements Affecting Other Nonimmigrant Workers,89 FR 103054 (Dec. 18, 2024) (Modernization Final Rule). Current 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) states that when adjudicating a Form I-129, Petition for Nonimmigrant Worker, involving the same parties and the same underlying facts, USCIS gives deference to its prior determination of eligibility unless there is a material error, a material change, or new material information that adversely impacts eligibility.
三、拟议规则的讨论
III. Discussion of Proposed Rule
美国国土安全部（DHS）提议废除其在8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)中的规定，以及该顺从政策。该政策指示美国公民及移民服务局（USCIS）官员在审理涉及相同当事人和相同基础事实的I-129表格提交的请求时，对申请人或受益人资格的先前认定给予顺从，除非存在实质性错误、实质性变化，或对资格产生不利影响的新的实质性信息。[11]该拟议变更将适用于所有使用I-129表格（非移民工人申请书）提交的福利请求。本提案并不禁止USCIS官员在相关情形下考虑先前的批准。相反，本提案将废除官员须以顺从先前批准为起点的要求。DHS认为，此项变更将更好地确保官员充分考虑所有相关事实和证据，以确保持续符合资格，并对每一份申请书或请求进行逐案审理，以确定申请人是否已履行其举证责任，证明其持续符合该福利的资格。总体而言，DHS认为，取消顺从将增强项目诚信，从而更好地保护美国工人的利益，这符合本届政府的优先事项。
DHS proposes to remove its regulation at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), and the deference policy, which instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination of the petitioner's or beneficiary's eligibility when adjudicating a request filed on Form I-129 involving the same parties and the same underlying facts, unless there was a material error, a material change, or new material information that adversely impacts eligibility.[11] This proposed change would apply to all benefit requests filed using Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker. This proposal would not prohibit USCIS officers from considering a prior approval where relevant. Rather, it would remove the requirement that officers begin from a position of deference to the prior approval. DHS believes that this change would better ensure that officers fully consider all relevant facts and evidence to ensure continued eligibility, and adjudicate each petition or request on a case-by-case basis to determine whether the petitioner has met its burden of proof to demonstrate continued eligibility for the benefit. Overall, DHS believes that removing deference would enhance program integrity and thereby better protect the interests of U.S. workers, consistent with this Administration's priorities.
A. 恢复举证责任的正确分配
A. Restoring the Proper Allocation of the Burden of Proof
取消顺从将使DHS的法规更好地与该机构长期以来对《移民与国籍法》（INA）第291条，即8 U.S.C. 1361条的解释保持一致。该条规定，举证责任由申请人或申请方承担，须证明其符合所寻求的每项移民福利的资格。另参见Matter of Chawathe, 25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7（USCIS行政上诉办公室，2010年）（“证明符合所寻求福利资格的举证责任完全由申请人承担。”）。尽管一个机构可以建立合理的证据推定，参见例如Reno v. Flores, 507 U.S. 292, 313（1993），但任何此类推定必须与制定法文本及体系保持一致，参见Lin v. U.S. Dep't of Justice, 494 F.3d 296, 308（第二巡回上诉法院，2007年，全院庭审）。无论顺从法规是否与该法律相符，DHS现在认为，就国会意图由该机构针对所寻求的每项移民福利确定的资格标准而言，该法规实际上产生了将举证责任从申请人转移至该机构的效果，这与INA第291条，即8 U.S.C. 1361条相抵触。
Removing deference would better align DHS regulations with the agency's longstanding interpretation of section 291 of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1361, which places the burden of proof on the petitioner or applicant to establish eligibility for each immigration benefit sought. See also Matter of Chawathe, 25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7 (USCIS AAO, 2010) (“The burden of proving eligibility for the benefit sought remains entirely with the applicant.”). Although an agency may establish reasonable evidentiary presumptions, see e.g., Reno v. Flores, 507 U.S. 292, 313 (1993), any such presumption must be consistent with the statutory text and scheme, see Lin v. U.S. Dep't of Justice, 494 F.3d 296, 308 (2d Cir. 2007) (en banc). Whether or not the deference regulation is consonant with the statute, DHS now believes that it has the practical effect of shifting the burden of proof from the petitioner to the agency, in tension with section 291 of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1361, with respect to the eligibility criteria that Congress intended the agency to determine for each immigration benefit sought.
当DHS颁布8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)时，该机构曾表示申请人将继续承担证明其持续符合所申请非移民工人类别资格的举证责任。参见89 FR 103094。然而，在实践中，顺从政策实际上将举证责任从申请人转移给了审理官员，后者必须指出实质性错误或情况变化，才能证明偏离先前批准是正当的。[12]实际上，这形成了一种默认立场，即先前的批准是正确的，申请人和受益人仍符合该福利的资格，除非官员认定顺从政策所列明的某项例外情形适用（即存在实质性错误、资格要求或情况的实质性变化，或对资格产生不利影响的新的实质性信息）。这种结构实际上将举证责任从申请人转移给了（印刷页第65123页）审理官员。顺从法规及政策实际上并未要求申请人证明该外国人有资格在美国继续以某非移民类别从事工作，而是要求官员说明为何不应以先前的批准为准。
When DHS promulgated 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), the agency stated that petitioners would continue to have the burden to demonstrate continued eligibility for the requested nonimmigrant worker classification. See89 FR 103094. In practice, however, the deference policy effectively shifts the burden from the petitioner to the adjudicating officer who must identify a material error or change in circumstances to justify deviating from the prior approval.[12] In effect, this creates a default position that the prior approval was correct, and the petitioner and beneficiary remain eligible for the benefit unless the officer finds that an enumerated exception to the deference policy applies ( i.e., that there was a material error, material change in circumstances or eligibility requirements, or new, material information that adversely impacts eligibility). This structure effectively shifts the burden from the petitioner to ( printed page 65123) the adjudicating officer. Instead of requiring the petitioner to establish that the alien is eligible to continue to work in the United States under a nonimmigration classification, the deference regulation and policy effectively require the officer to justify why the prior approval should not control.
B. 增强USCIS官员的事实认定权限
B. Enhancing USCIS Officer Fact-Finding Authority
取消顺从还将更好地确保USCIS官员在批准延期申请及批准延长居留请求之前，对每一案件的事实和证据进行审查，以确保持续符合资格。该顺从政策暗示对每一案件进行非彻底审查或属可接受，并给人以不当限制审理官员固有的事实认定权限之印象。部分官员可能将顺从视为对其全面考虑案件所有事实之能力的一种限制。由于该顺从政策的影响，部分官员可能并未在申请书未证明符合资格时采取适当行动（例如发出补充证据通知，RFE），而是在没有明显错误或变化的情况下直接批准该福利。
Removing deference would also better ensure that USCIS officers review the facts and evidence in every case to ensure continued eligibility before approving a petition extension and granting an extension of stay request. The deference policy suggests that a less than thorough review of each case may be acceptable and gives the impression of unduly limiting adjudicating officers' inherent fact-finding authority. Some officers may view deference as a restriction on their ability to consider all the facts of the case. Rather than taking appropriate action if eligibility was not demonstrated in the petition (such as issuing an RFE), some officers may simply grant the benefit absent glaring errors or changes as a result of the deference policy.
事实上,如下文表3所示,在2017年推迟遵从政策被废止后,要求提供证据(RFE)的比率比对照组的RFE比率增加了2.9个百分点。国土安全部(DHS)承认,其他与推迟遵从政策无关的变化可能在一定程度上导致了这一RFE比率的上升。然而,国土安全部认为,可以合理地得出结论,推迟遵从政策的废止是导致该RFE比率上升的因素之一。这一增长与国土安全部的担忧是一致的,即推迟遵从政策产生了这样的效果:即便在申请人未能就当前请求证明其符合非移民工作者类别资格的案件中,也会使部分官员不愿要求提供额外证据。
Indeed, as shown in Table 3 infra, the RFE rate increased by 2.9 percent more than the RFE rate for the comparison group after the deference policy was rescinded in 2017. DHS acknowledges that other changes unrelated to deference may have, in part, contributed to this increased RFE rate. However, DHS believes that it is reasonable to conclude that the rescission of the deference policy was one of the contributing factors for this increased RFE rate. This increase is consistent with DHS's concern that the deference policy has had the effect of discouraging some officers from requesting additional evidence even in cases where the petitioner has not established eligibility for the nonimmigrant worker classification in connection with the current request.
裁决官员的事实认定权限不应受到任何先前申请批准的限制。相反,官员应根据每个案件的具体情况,全面审查申请是否符合资格条件,并酌情要求提供额外文件,这与美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)关于要求提供证据、拟拒绝通知以及非移民福利申请裁决的政策相一致。由于在开始审查某些申请材料时,推迟遵从可能被视为一种默认立场,因此在某些情况下,推迟遵从政策可能产生了意想不到的效果,即限制了官员对事实进行全面审查的能力,并限制了对每个案件资格的评估。尽管官员当然可以得出与先前裁决相同的结论,但不应被迫将此作为默认的出发点。[13]
An adjudicating officer's fact-finding authority should not be constrained by any prior petition approval. Instead, officers should thoroughly review a petition for eligibility and request additional documentation as appropriate based on the merits of each case, consistent with USCIS policy regarding requests for evidence, notices of intent to deny, and the adjudication of petitions for nonimmigrant benefits. Because deference may be viewed as a default position upon beginning review of certain filings, the deference policy may, in some cases, have had the unintended effect of limiting the ability of officers to conduct a thorough review of the facts and limiting assessment of eligibility in each case. While officers may, of course, reach the same conclusion as in a prior decision, they should not be compelled to do so as a default starting point.[13]
此外,推迟遵从还增加了官员未能发现先前裁决中实质性错误的风险。尽管推迟遵从政策允许官员在存在实质性错误的情况下偏离先前的裁决,但如果官员因假定先前裁决正确而限制了对其面前案件的审查,这必然会限制对实质性错误的发现。对非移民工作者类别资格的认定进行推迟遵从,而该认定可能是在申请延期裁决前数年作出的,且可能基于不准确或过时的事实,或是根据自原始批准作出以来已经发生变化的法规所裁决的,这与本届政府加强非移民工作者制度完整性的努力是不一致的。
In addition, deference increases the risk of officers not discovering material errors in prior adjudications. Although the deference policy allows officers to deviate from the prior decision if there was a material error, if officers limited their review of the cases before them because of an assumption that the prior decision was correct, it would necessarily limit the discovery of material error. Deferring to a determination of eligibility for nonimmigrant worker classification that was made potentially up to several years before the adjudication of the petition extension, and that may be based on inaccurate or outdated facts or adjudicated under regulations which have changed since the original approval was issued, is inconsistent with this Administration's efforts to strengthen the integrity of the nonimmigrant worker system.
美国公民及移民服务局认为,对官员进行额外培训或就如何适用推迟遵从作出澄清,不足以充分解决这些问题。如本序言前文所述,美国公民及移民服务局多年来一直在适用推迟遵从政策。在2024年将其编入法规之前,推迟遵从政策自2004年起在美国公民及移民服务局实施,直至2017年被废止,后于2021年重新实施。因此,美国公民及移民服务局的官员通常已就在推迟遵从政策实施期间如何裁决申请接受过培训和业务指导。然而,培训并非问题所在,也非解决之道。实际上,任何推迟遵从的要求本身都与官员在授予非移民工作者福利之前,必须进行全面审查以确保申请人已履行举证责任的义务存在根本性冲突。事实上,推迟遵从政策的部分初衷是希望通过减少官员重新审视先前资格认定的需要来提高效率。然而,这种效率却产生了意想不到的效果,即在裁决非移民工作者类别的延期申请时,使官员不愿对案件进行全面审查。国土安全部现在认为,推迟遵从政策不利于确保官员在每个案件中都全面评估资格,而这对于保护移民福利制度的完整性以及美国工人的利益是必要的,其方式是确保申请人和受益人符合所申请的非移民工作者福利的资格条件。
USCIS does not believe additional officer training or clarification on how to apply deference would sufficiently address these concerns. As discussed earlier in this preamble, USCIS has a history of applying deference over several years. Prior to its codification in the regulations in 2024, the deference policy was in place at USCIS from 2004 until its rescission in 2017 and was reinstituted in 2021. Therefore, USCIS officers generally have had training and operational guidance on how to adjudicate petitions with the deference policy in place. Training, however, is not the issue or solution. Rather, any deference requirement itself conveys a fundamental tension with an officer's obligation to conduct a fulsome review to ensure that the petitioner met its burden before granting a nonimmigrant worker benefit. Indeed, the deference policy was based in part on a desire to increase efficiency by reducing the officer's need to revisit prior determinations of eligibility. That efficiency, however, has had the unintended effect of discouraging officers from thoroughly reviewing a case when adjudicating extension requests for nonimmigrant worker classifications. DHS now believes that the deference policy is inconsistent with ensuring that officers fully evaluate eligibility in every case, which is necessary to protect the integrity of the immigration benefits system and the interests of U.S. workers by ensuring that petitioners and beneficiaries are eligible for the requested nonimmigrant worker benefit.
C. 加强项目完整性
C. Strengthening Program Integrity
国土安全部（DHS）还认为，遵从原则可能损害项目的完整性，包括为不择手段的申请人提交欺诈性或误导性非移民工人福利申请创造机会。例如，不择手段的申请人可能提交一份是否合理存疑的申请，期望一旦获批，该批准可以作为日后涉及类似事实、相关受益人或同一雇主的申请的依据。他们甚至可能提交多份此类申请，并使用最有利或最快获批的事实情节。同样，申请人可能在首次申请时夸大受益人的职责、薪酬或资质，期望一旦获批，未来的申请将受到较少审查。此外，如果申请人认为之前的批准具有约束力，他们在涉及影响其业务运营或所提供职位的变化方面（例如业务规模缩减、合同损失、工作职责变更或其他合规问题）可能不会如实披露。如果预期可获得遵从，申请人可能会被激励在未充分披露这些变化的情况下提交延期申请。
DHS also believes that deference may undermine program integrity, including by creating opportunities for unscrupulous petitioners to submit fraudulent or misleading petitions for nonimmigrant worker benefits. For example, an unscrupulous filer might submit a petition of questionable merit, hoping that if it is approved, the approval can be used as an anchor for later filings involving similar facts, related beneficiaries, or the same employer. They may even file multiple of these types of petitions and use the fact pattern that is the most advantageous or that is the most quickly approved. Similarly, a petitioner might overstate the beneficiary's role, compensation, or qualifications in the first filing, expecting that once approved, future filings will receive less scrutiny. Further, petitioners may be less forthcoming about changes affecting their business operations or the proffered position (such as reduced operations, loss of contracts, altered job duties, or other compliance issues) if they believe a prior approval is binding. If deference is expected, the petitioner may be incentivized to file extensions without fully disclosing those changes.
事实上，美国公民及移民服务局（USCIS）的数据在一定程度上支持了这样的担忧：遵从原则可能通过在某些情况下降低审查力度，从而损害项目完整性，并助长一种使误导性、不合规或欺诈性的非移民工人申请延期更容易获批的环境。例如，数据显示，自USCIS恢复遵从政策并将其纳入《USCIS政策手册》以来，在H-1B和L-1A项目中发现的欺诈和不合规比例有所上升。[14]（第65124页）具体而言，就2022财年至2025财年的H-1B延期案件而言，在USCIS行政现场访查和定向现场访查中所访查的H-1B场所中，不合规和/或欺诈的总体综合比例为22.39%。相比之下，在USCIS实行不遵从政策的时期，即2018财年至2020财年，访查的H-1B场所中不合规和/或欺诈的总体综合比例为16.77%。[15]就L-1A延期案件而言，2022财年至2025财年期间，在USCIS行政现场访查和定向现场访查中所访查的L-1A场所中，不合规和/或欺诈的总体综合比例为24.46%。相比之下，2018财年至2020财年期间访查的L-1A场所中，不合规和/或欺诈的总体综合比例为18.37%。[16]
Indeed, USCIS data provides some support for the concern that deference may undermine program integrity by reducing scrutiny in some cases and contributing to an environment in which misleading, non-compliant, or fraudulent extensions of nonimmigrant worker petitions are more likely to be approved. For example, the data demonstrate higher percentages of fraud and non-compliance identified in the ( printed page 65124) H-1B and L-1A programs since the time when USCIS reinstituted its deference policy and incorporated it into the USCIS Policy Manual.[14] Specifically, for H-1B extension cases from FY 2022 through FY 2025, the overall combined percentage of non-compliance and/or fraud among H-1B sites visited during USCIS administrative and targeted site visits was 22.39 percent. In comparison, when USCIS had a policy of no deference, the overall combined percentage of H-1B non-compliance and/or fraud among H-1B sites visited during FY 2018 through FY 2020 was 16.77 percent.[15] For L-1A extension cases, the overall combined percentage of non-compliance and/or fraud among L-1A sites visited during USCIS administrative and targeted site visits from FY 2022 through FY 2025 was 24.46 percent. In comparison, the overall combined percentage of L-1A non-compliance and/or fraud among L-1A sites visited from FY 2018 through FY 2020 was 18.37 percent.[16]
换言之，在USCIS实行遵从政策的年份，H-1B和L-1A项目中被访查场所的不合规和/或欺诈综合比例，高于USCIS未实行遵从政策的年份。这一在H-1B和L-1A延期申请子集中反映出的趋势，支持了这样一种担忧，即遵从原则可能限制USCIS逐案全面审查延期申请的能力，从而可能激励那些虚假声称不存在实质性变化的轻率或欺诈性申请。尽管国土安全部承认，除遵从原则之外或在其之外，可能还有其他因素导致了这一较高的欺诈或不合规发现比例，但可以合理地认为，遵从原则是促成因素之一。
In other words, during the years that USCIS had a policy of deference, the combined percentage of non-compliance and/or fraud among sites visited in the H-1B and L-1A programs was higher than in the years that USCIS did not have a policy of deference. This trend reflected in a subset of H-1B and L-1A extension filings supports the concern that deference undermines program integrity in potentially limiting USCIS' ability to thoroughly review an extension petition on a case-by-case basis, which in turn may incentivize frivolous or fraudulent filings that falsely assert no material change. While DHS acknowledges that other factors besides or in addition to deference may have led to this higher percentage of identifying fraud or non-compliance, it is reasonable to conclude that deference was one of the contributing factors.
取消遵从原则有可能遏制欺诈性申请，并有助于恢复合法移民体系的完整性。这反过来也将通过确保非移民工人申请仅在申请人基于每份申请所提交的证据证明其资格的情况下才获批准，从而更好地保护美国工人。
Removing deference would potentially disincentivize fraudulent filings and help restore the integrity of the lawful immigration system. This, in turn, would also better protect U.S. workers by ensuring that petitions for nonimmigrant workers are only approved if eligibility is established by the petitioner based on evidence submitted in support of each petition.
D. 对遵从政策的重新考虑
D. Reconsideration of the Deference Policy
国土安全部(DHS)承认遵从政策的历史沿革及其最近在《现代化最终规则》中于8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)条款中的法典化。国土安全部还承认,本拟议规则中的部分陈述与《现代化最终规则》中的陈述相比有所转变。在《现代化最终规则》中,一些评论者表示担忧,认为将遵从政策法典化会限制美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)官员的事实认定权限。见89 FR 103093。评论者指出,USCIS官员在裁决过程中不应受到限制,无法要求提供补充证据。他们还指出,要求USCIS官员在批准福利申请前确认所有重大事实,将起到欺诈侦测机制和威慑作用。
DHS acknowledges the history of the deference policy and its recent codification at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) in the Modernization Final Rule. DHS also acknowledges that some of the statements in this proposed rule represent a shift from statements made in the Modernization Final Rule. In the Modernization Final Rule, some commenters voiced concern that codifying deference would constrain USCIS officers' fact-finding authority. See89 FR 103093. Commenters stated that USCIS officers should not be constrained in requesting additional evidence in the adjudication process. They also stated that requiring USCIS officers to confirm all material facts before granting a benefit request would serve as a fraud detection mechanism and deterrent.
当时,国土安全部不认同评论者的担忧。同上。然而,自《现代化最终规则》发布以来,国土安全部已对此问题进行了重新审视,目前认为,将遵从政策法典化可能产生了意想不到的后果,即看似限制了USCIS官员的事实认定权限,导致部分USCIS官员未能进行充分全面的审查,从而削弱了该机构侦测和遏制欺诈行为的努力。[17]
At the time, DHS disagreed with the commenters' concerns. Id. However, DHS has reconsidered the issue since publication of the Modernization Final Rule and now believes that codification of the deference policy may have had the unintended effect of appearing to constrain USCIS officers' fact-finding authority, leading some USCIS officers to conduct a less than fulsome review, and thus undermining the agency's efforts to detect and deter fraud.[17]
在提议将遵从政策法典化的《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中,国土安全部指出,遵从政策的益处包括促进USCIS及其利益相关方之间的一致性和效率,并为申请人和受益人创造可预见性。国土安全部现在认为,这些益处被夸大了,且未能充分权衡遵从政策所带来的负面影响。目前尚不清楚遵从政策的法典化是否确实促进了裁决工作的一致性和效率,即便在某种程度上确实如此,这也是以牺牲项目完整性为代价实现的。正如本序言中所述,遵从政策可能会助长无实质理据的申请,给人以不当限制裁决官固有事实认定权限的印象,并增加先前错误批准继续作为批准非移民工人延期申请依据的风险。简而言之,遵从政策与本届政府致力于恢复合法移民体系完整性、通过确保只有符合资格标准并能证明其资格的申请人和受益人方可获得非移民工人福利,从而更好地保护美国工人的工作重心不相符。
In the NPRM proposing to codify deference, DHS stated that the benefits of deference included promoting consistency and efficiency for both USCIS and its stakeholders, and creating predictability for petitioners and beneficiaries. DHS now believes that these benefits were overstated and were not adequately weighed against the negative impacts of deference. It is not clear that the codification of deference actually promoted consistency and efficiency in adjudication, and to any extent that it did, it did so at the expense of program integrity. As discussed in this preamble, deference may incentivize non-meritorious filings, give the appearance of unduly limiting adjudicators' inherent fact-finding authority, and increase the risk that erroneous prior approvals continue to serve as the basis for granting extension petitions for nonimmigrant workers. Quite simply, the deference policy is inconsistent with this Administration's focus on restoring the integrity of the legal immigration system to better protect U.S. workers by ensuring that only petitioners and beneficiaries who meet eligibility criteria and demonstrate eligibility are granted nonimmigrant worker benefits.
E. 信赖利益及已考虑的替代方案
E. Reliance Interests and Alternatives Considered
国土安全部认识到,部分美国雇主和非移民工人可能会主张对遵从政策延续性的信赖利益。例如,预期其I-129表格申请将持续获批的美国雇主,可能已基于这些非移民工人将能够继续为其工作的假设做出了相应的商业安排。同样,非移民工人也可能基于其将能够继续在美国工作及合法居留的假设,制定了职业和/或个人规划。
DHS recognizes that some U.S. employers and nonimmigrant workers may assert reliance interests in the continuation of the deference policy. For example, U.S. employers that expect the continued approval of their Form I-129 petitions may have made business arrangements based on an assumption that these nonimmigrant workers would be able to continue working for them. Similarly, nonimmigrant workers may have made career and/or personal plans in the United States based on the assumption that they would be able to continue their employment and legal presence in the United States.
国土安全部已考虑上述信赖利益,但认为此类利益是有限的。遵从规定本身并不保证延期申请一定获批。反之,取消遵从政策也不会实质性改变所申请移民福利的资格条件。只要美国雇主和非移民工人能够证明持续符合资格要求并获得继续从事此类雇佣所需的必要批准,本拟议变更就不会剥夺美国雇主继续雇用非移民工人的能力,也不会剥夺非移民工人继续在美国工作和生活的能力。申请人和受益人并不具有合法期待,认为此前错误或缺乏依据支持的批准将会在未来继续导致获批。
DHS has considered these reliance interests but believes that any such interests are limited. The deference regulation does not itself guarantee approval of an extension. Conversely, removing deference does not substantively change eligibility for the requested immigration benefit. This proposed change would not deprive U.S. employers of their ability to continue employing nonimmigrant workers, nor would it deprive nonimmigrant workers of their ability to continue working and living in the United States, so long as they are able to establish continued eligibility and obtain necessary approvals to continue such employment. Petitioners and beneficiaries do not have a legitimate expectation that an erroneous or unsupported prior approval would continue to result in future approvals.
在延续遵从政策方面存在合法依赖利益的范围内,美国国土安全部(DHS)认为,正如本序言所讨论的,遵从政策的负面影响超过了可能寻求依赖8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)所规定的遵从政策的雇主和外国人所能获得的潜在利益。DHS认为,其在恢复移民体系完整性方面的利益,包括确保美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)审查员在批准任何福利之前对所有福利申请进行全面审查,远远超过请愿人或受益人对持续获批可能抱有的任何期望利益。当遵从政策增加了USCIS未能识别原本就不符合批准资格的请愿人和受益人的风险时,这一点尤为重要。因此,DHS(印刷页65125)认为,删除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)并终止遵从政策,对于确保合法移民体系的完整性是必要的,这反过来也能更好地保护美国工人。
To the extent there are legitimate reliance interests in the continuation of deference, DHS believes the negative impacts of the deference policy, as discussed in this preamble, outweigh the potential benefits to employers and aliens who may seek to rely on deference as codified at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5). DHS believes that its interest in restoring integrity to the immigration system, including by ensuring that USCIS adjudicators conduct fulsome reviews of all benefits requests before granting a benefit, far outweighs any interest a petitioner or beneficiary may have in expecting continued approvals. This is especially true when deference increases the risk of USCIS failing to identify petitioners and beneficiaries who were not eligible for approval in the first place. DHS ( printed page 65125) therefore believes that removing 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) and ending the deference policy is necessary to ensure the integrity of the legal immigration system, which in turn better protects U.S. workers.
DHS考虑了一种替代方案,即不删除遵从规定,而是修订该规定以更清楚地说明USCIS是否适用遵从政策具有自由裁量权。这可以通过将监管文本修订为USCIS“可自行决定给予遵从”,而不是目前8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)所述的“USCIS给予遵从”来实现。然而,DHS没有提议采用这一替代方案,因为这很可能在请愿人和USCIS官员之间就是否应适用遵从政策产生不必要的混淆和不确定性。此外,这一替代方案也无法充分解决本序言第III.A节至第III.C节上文所讨论的有关遵从政策的问题。总体而言,DHS认为,完全取消遵从政策是增强项目完整性的最实际的替代方案,这包括确保举证责任仍由请愿人承担,以证明其符合所申请移民福利的资格,并确保USCIS审查员在延长该福利之前,根据每份非移民工人延期申请自身的情况进行彻底审查。
DHS considered an alternative to removing the deference regulation whereby DHS would revise the regulation to more clearly state that USCIS has discretion whether to apply deference. This could be accomplished by revising the regulatory text to state that USCIS “may give deference in its discretion” instead of “USCIS gives deference” as currently stated at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5). However, DHS is not proposing this alternative because it would likely create unnecessary confusion and uncertainty for both petitioners and USCIS officers as to whether deference should be applied. Further, this alternative would not adequately address the concerns with deference discussed above in Section III.A through III.C of this preamble. Overall, DHS believes that removing deference entirely is the most practical alternative to enhance program integrity, including by ensuring that the burden remains on the petitioner to establish eligibility for the requested immigration benefit, and that USCIS adjudicators thoroughly review each nonimmigrant worker extension request on its own merits before extending the benefit.

修改摘要
Summary of Changes
该拟议规则将废除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)条规定的遵从原则,该条款现行规定,在当事方和基本事实保持不变的情况下,美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)官员应对Form I-129申请的既往裁定给予遵从,除非存在实质性错误、实质性变化,或对资格产生不利影响的新的实质性信息。由于对每份申请将进行更为全面的审查,预计证据请求(RFE)的数量将会增加,从而导致申请人在提交补充证明文件和证据方面承担额外的时间负担。该规则还将因延长裁决时间及增加持续雇佣方面的不确定性,而给申请人带来成本。国土安全部(DHS)估计,由于RFE数量的增加,该规则将使申请人每年新增约133,987美元的成本。此外,该规则还将通过增加裁决工作量和运营成本,给USCIS带来成本,因为官员将需要花费更多时间重新评估此前已审查过的资格问题,且可能发出更多的RFE,从而增加整体资源需求。
The proposed rule would remove the deference provision at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), which currently instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination for Form I-129 requests when the parties and underlying facts remain the same, unless there was a material error, a material change, or new material information that adversely impacts eligibility. As a result of a more thorough review of each petition, the number of requests for evidence (RFE) is expected to increase, resulting in additional time burden for petitioners to submit additional supporting documentation and evidence. The rule would also impose costs on petitioners by lengthening adjudication times and increasing uncertainty regarding continued employment. DHS estimates that the rule would produce new annual costs to petitioners of approximately $133,987 due to an increase in RFEs. In addition, the rule would impose costs on USCIS by increasing the adjudicative workload and operational costs, as officers would spend more time reassessing eligibility issues previously reviewed and may issue an increased number of RFEs, thereby increasing overall resource demands.
废除遵从政策具有若干主要益处。废除该政策将厘清并强化以下原则,即法定举证责任仍由申请人承担,而非由机构承担。废除该政策还将促使对每份申请及其证明证据进行更为全面的审查。实施更为全面的审查程序预计将提升发现既往错误、不一致之处或欺诈迹象的能力,并消除判断遵从原则是否适用的必要性。就更广泛的公众和移民体系而言,预计此项变更将增强公众对裁决程序完整性和可靠性的信心,并与机构在维护系统完整性及保护美国工人利益方面的优先事项保持一致。表1详细列明了该拟议规则可量化和不可量化影响的估计情况。(印刷页第65126页)
There are primary benefits associated with removing the deference policy. Removal would clarify and reinforce that the statutory burden of proof remains on the petitioner, not the agency. Removal would also encourage a more thorough review of each petition and its supporting evidence. Implementing a more thorough review process is expected to improve the ability to detect prior errors, inconsistencies, or indicators of fraud, and to eliminate the need to determine when deference applies. For the broader public and the immigration system, the change is expected to enhance confidence in the integrity and reliability of the adjudication process and align with agency priorities related to system integrity and protection of U.S. workers' interests. 背景与适用人群
Background and Population
本拟议规则的目的是删除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5),该条款指示美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)官员在审理使用I-129表格(非移民工作者申请)提交的、涉及相同当事方和相同基础事实的请求时,应对申请人、请愿人或受益人资格的先前认定给予遵从,除非存在重大错误、重大变化或对资格产生不利影响的新的重大信息。
The purpose of this proposed rule is to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), which instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination of the petitioner's, applicant's, or beneficiary's eligibility when adjudicating a request filed using Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker, involving the same parties and the same underlying facts, unless there was a material error, a material change, or new material information that adversely impacts eligibility.
USCIS的遵从政策最初于2004年4月实施,并一直适用至2017年10月。2004年4月,USCIS发布一份备忘录,指示官员在审理涉及相同请愿人、相同受益人和相同基础事实的延期申请时,一般应对先前的批准给予遵从。参见USCIS,《在随后确定申请延期资格有效性背景下先前移民局批准非移民申请的意义》,HQPRD 72/11.3号(2004年4月23日)。该政策在承认每个案件均须根据其自身案情裁决、且先前决定可被重新审视的同时,确立了官员应在不存在重大错误、实质性情况变化或新的重大信息的情况下,对该先前资格认定给予遵从的原则。同上。
The USCIS deference policy was first implemented and applied from April 2004 to October 2017. In April 2004, USCIS issued a memorandum directing officers to generally give deference to prior approvals when adjudicating petitions for extensions involving the same petitioner, the same beneficiary, and the same underlying facts. See USCIS, “The Significance of a Prior CIS Approval of a Nonimmigrant Petition in the Context of a Subsequent Determination Regarding Eligibility for Extension of Petition Validity,” HQPRD 72/11.3 (Apr. 23, 2004). While recognizing that each case must be decided on its own merits and that prior decisions could be revisited, the policy established that officers should defer to that prior determination of eligibility absent material error, substantial changed circumstances, or new material information. Id.
2017年10月,USCIS发布一份备忘录,撤销了2004年4月的指导意见。参见USCIS,《撤销关于在审理非移民身份延期申请时遵从先前资格认定的指导意见》,PM-602-0151号(2017年10月23日)。该机构表示担忧,认为该遵从政策不当地将举证责任从请愿人身上转移,并不当地限制了官员的事实调查权限。同上。从2017年10月至2021年4月,官员未被指示遵从先前的批准,而是被指示在不依赖先前决定的情况下作出新的资格认定。
In October 2017, USCIS issued a memorandum rescinding the April 2004 guidance. See USCIS, “Rescission of Guidance Regarding Deference to Prior Determinations of Eligibility in the Adjudication of Petitions for Extension of Nonimmigrant Status,” PM-602-0151 (Oct. 23, 2017). The agency expressed concern that the deference policy improperly shifted the burden of proof away from the petitioner and unduly restricted officers' fact-finding authority. Id. From October 2017 to April 2021, officers were not instructed to defer to prior approvals and instead were instructed to make new determinations of eligibility without relying on earlier decisions.
USCIS于2021年4月恢复了遵从政策,并将其纳入《USCIS政策手册》。参见《USCIS政策手册》第2卷A部分第4章《停留延期、身份变更及申请有效期延期》。该手册指示官员在涉及相同当事方和相同基础事实的情况下,一般应对先前的批准给予遵从,除非先前决定存在重大错误、情况发生重大变化,或出现对请愿人或受益人资格产生不利影响的新的重大信息。同上。随后,2024年12月,国土安全部(DHS)在H-1B现代化最终规则中将恢复后的政策编入8 CFR 214.1(c)(5),要求在此类案件中遵从先前的认定,除非符合其中一项例外情形。参见89 FR 103054(2024年12月18日)。
USCIS reinstated the deference policy in April 2021 and incorporated it into the USCIS Policy Manual. See USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 2, Part A, Chapter 4 “Extension of Stay, Change of Status, and Extension of Petition Validity.” The manual instructs officers to generally defer to prior approvals involving the same parties and the same underlying facts, unless there was a material error in the prior decision, a material change in circumstances, or new material information that adversely impacts the petitioner's or beneficiary's eligibility. Id. Then, in December 2024, DHS codified the reinstated policy at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) in the H-1B Modernization Final Rule, requiring deference to prior determinations in such cases unless one of the exceptions applies. See89 FR 103054 (Dec. 18, 2024).

拟议规则的成本与收益
Costs and Benefits of the Proposed Rule
国土安全部(DHS)预计,相较于现行推定政策继续生效的基准情形,拟议规则将产生可量化及不可量化的成本与收益。
DHS expects that the proposed rule would generate both quantified and unquantified costs and benefits, assessed relative to a baseline in which the existing deference policy remains in effect.
可量化成本
Quantified Costs
可量化成本体现为因补充证据通知(RFE)发生频率增加而给申请人带来的额外时间负担。取消推定政策要求审案官员更为彻底地评估资格条件,这可能导致官员更频繁地要求提交补充材料,以独立核实每一份延期申请的资格。每一份RFE都会增加申请人的时间负担,申请人必须审阅通知内容、收集并整理文件,并准备和提交答复。为估算因取消推定政策而导致的RFE增加幅度,国土安全部使用了2013年10月1日至2021年4月27日期间的历史数据,该期间涵盖了推定政策生效期(2013年10月1日至2017年10月11日)和推定政策未生效期(2017年10月12日至2021年4月27日)。[18]
Quantified costs are captured as the additional time burden on petitioners resulting from more frequent RFEs. Removing the deference policy requires officers to more thoroughly assess eligibility, which may result in officers requesting additional documentation more often to independently verify eligibility for each extension request. Each RFE increases the time burden on petitioners, who must review the notice, collect and organize documents, and prepare and submit a response. To estimate the increase in RFEs attributable to removal of the deference policy, DHS uses historical data from October 1, 2013, to April 27, 2021, covering periods when the deference policy was in effect (October 1, 2013, to October 11, 2017) and when it was not in effect (October 12, 2017, to April 27, 2021).[18]
为了单独分离出取消推定政策对RFE数量的影响,国土安全部采用了简单的双重差分法(DID)。[19] 在本分析中,该DID方法比较了受影响群体(受推定政策影响的申请)在推定政策生效期与未生效期之间RFE比率的变化,并将其与同一时期内对照群体(不受推定政策影响的申请)RFE比率的变化进行比较。
In order to isolate the effect of removal of the deference policy on the number of RFEs, DHS uses a simple difference-in-difference (DID) approach.[19] For this analysis, the DID approach compares changes in the RFE rate for the affected group (petitions affected by deference policy) during the periods when deference policy was in effect and when it was not in effect with changes in the RFE rate for the comparison group (petitions not affected by deference policy) over the same periods.

未量化的成本
Unquantified Costs
申请人在寻求延长停留期限和申请有效期时,可能会经历更长的审理时间,并对能否继续雇佣产生更大的不确定性。根据过去四个财年(2022财年至2025财年)的平均数据,有补充证据请求(RFE)的申请与无补充证据请求的申请之间的处理时间差异为64天。因此,我们采用与收到补充证据请求的申请相关的额外处理时间,即64个日历天。[27]这些较长的时间线可能延迟最终决定,使工人和雇主难以规划项目时间表、人员配置水平和工作安排。在此延长期间,申请人可能面临关于受益人长期雇佣授权和身份的额外不确定性,这可能给雇主带来运营方面的挑战,例如劳动力规划和项目安排,并增加工人的压力,因为他们可能需要等待更长时间才能明确是否能够在已获授权的期限之外继续工作。在已及时提交延长受益人停留期限的申请的情况下,如果该申请在受益人身份到期时仍处于待决状态,受益人可在等待就其提交的申请作出裁决期间,在获授权的停留期内继续留在美国,并可能有资格继续工作。参见,例如,8 CFR 274a.12(b)(20)。此时受益人所承担的成本将更多与其申请何时获得裁决的不可预测性有关。
Petitioners may experience longer adjudication times and increased uncertainty regarding continued employment when seeking extensions of stay and petition validity. Based on the average of the last four fiscal years (FY 2022-FY 2025), the difference in processing time between petitions with and without an RFE is 64 days; therefore, we use the additional processing time associated with petitions that receive an RFE, which is 64 calendar days.[27] These longer timelines can delay the final decision, making it difficult for workers and employers to plan project timelines, staffing levels, and work assignments. During this extended period, petitioners may face added uncertainty as to the beneficiary's longer-term employment authorization and status, which could create operational challenges for employers, such as workforce planning and project scheduling and increase stress for workers who may have to wait significantly longer for clarity on their ability to continue employment beyond that already authorized. In cases where a petition to extend the beneficiary's stay has been timely filed, if the petition remained pending when the beneficiary's status expired, the beneficiary may remain in the United States in an authorized period of stay while awaiting the adjudication of the petition filed on their behalf and may be eligible to continue to work. See, e.g.,8 CFR 274a.12(b)(20). The costs to beneficiaries then would be more related to unpredictability regarding when their petition is adjudicated.
国土安全部认识到,取消遵从可能导致申请人投入更多时间和资源来准备延期申请。美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)未发现有证据表明,因取消遵从而导致G-28表格的提交率有所上升。[28]然而,申请人可能会采取额外措施,以确保从一开始提交的材料就完整且条理清晰,而不是仅仅依赖先前的批准。这些努力可能有助于申请人满足其举证责任,并可能降低出现缺陷的可能性,但也可能带来未量化的成本,表现为额外的员工时间、律师时间或其他准备成本。国土安全部无法根据现有数据对此成本进行量化。
DHS recognizes that removing deference may lead petitioners to devote additional time and resources to preparing extension petitions. USCIS did not find evidence that the Form G-28 filing rate increased as a result of removing deference.[28] However, petitioners may take additional steps to ensure that submissions are complete and well-organized at the outset, instead of merely relying on a prior approval. These efforts may help petitioners satisfy their burden of proof and may reduce the likelihood of deficiencies, but they may also impose unquantified costs in the form of additional staff time, attorney time, or other preparation costs. DHS is unable to quantify this cost with the available data.
未量化的收益
Unquantified Benefits
取消8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)及遵从政策将明确并强化这样一项原则,即法定举证责任仍由申请人承担,而非由机构承担,这与制定法相符。参见《移民与国籍法》(INA)第291条,8 U.S.C. 1361; Matter of Chawathe案,25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7(美国公民及移民服务局行政上诉办公室,2010年)(“证明符合所寻求福利资格的举证责任完全由申请人承担”)。这对该机构是有益的,因为它强调了官员没有义务仅因先前的批准,就批准在尚未证明符合资格的情况下寻求移民福利的后续申请。取消遵从政策还将促使裁决官员不依赖不完整或过时的事实,从而支持作出更准确、以档案记录为基础的决定。明确举证责任的分配也可以促进提交材料更加完整、条理更清晰,并可能降低延期申请被错误批准的风险。
Removing 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) and the deference policy would clarify and reinforce that the statutory burden of proof remains on the petitioner rather than on the agency, consistent with the statute. See INA sec. 291, 8 U.S.C. 1361; Matter of Chawathe, 25 I. & N. Dec. 369, 375 n.7 (USCIS AAO, 2010) (“The burden of proving eligibility for the benefit sought remains entirely with the applicant”). This benefits the agency by underscoring that officers are not obligated to approve subsequent petitions seeking immigration benefits where eligibility has not been demonstrated, merely because of prior approval. Removing the deference policy would also compel adjudication officers not to rely on incomplete or outdated facts, and supports more accurate, record-based decisions. Clear allocation of the burden of proof can also promote more complete, better-organized filings, and may reduce the risk of erroneous approvals of extension petitions.
此外,取消推定效力将强化国土安全部(DHS)全面、及时审查每份申请所附证据的权力,从而提高识别不符合资格情形或欺诈迹象的能力。当官员无需对先前的批准给予推定效力时,他们可以根据可能在此前决定作出时尚不明显的最新信息、政策或模式,更好地评估文件和资格标准。这种全面审查有助于揭示既往申请与当前申请之间的差异,凸显不断演变的风险指标,并识别先前决定中可能存在的错误。这种做法将通过帮助机构纠正错误、发现新出现的欺诈手法,以及根据对重复申请者持续审查中发现的问题来完善指导和培训,从而加强项目的完整性。
Moreover, removing deference would reinforce DHS's authority to conduct a full, current review of the evidence in each petition, improving the ability to identify ineligibility or indicators of fraud. When officers need not defer to prior approvals, they can better evaluate documentation and eligibility criteria based on updated information, policies, or patterns that may not have been apparent at the time of the earlier decision. This full-scope review could help reveal discrepancies between past and current filings, highlight evolving risk indicators, and identify potential mistakes in prior decisions. This approach would strengthen program integrity by helping the agency to correct mistakes, detect emerging fraud schemes, and refine guidance and training based on issues identified through ongoing examination of repeat filers.
取消推定效力还将通过强化每份申请均依据当前记录进行评估这一原则,使公众和更广泛的移民体系受益,这可能会增强公众对裁决完整性的信心。公众将更有把握地相信,获批并非仅仅沿袭先前决定,而是基于当前事实和适用标准作出的。同时,取消推定效力将维护移民体系的完整性,并推动更好地保护美国工人利益的政策,其方式是支持更准确、以证据为依据的决定,并降低过时或错误的既往批准继续作为授予移民福利依据的风险。(印刷页第65130页)
Removing deference would also benefit the public and the broader immigration system by reinforcing that each petition is evaluated on the current record, which could increase confidence in the integrity of adjudications. Members of the public would have more assurance that approvals are not simply carried forward from prior decisions but are based on current facts and applicable standards. At the same time, removing deference would maintain the integrity of the immigration system and advance policies that better protect the interests of U.S. workers by supporting more accurate, evidence-driven decisions and reducing the risk that outdated or erroneous prior approvals continue to serve as the basis for granting immigration benefits. 已考虑的备选方案
Alternatives Considered
本拟议规则旨在明确并强化申请人承担法定举证责任这一原则,鼓励对每份申请及其所附证据进行更全面的审查,并更好地维护合法移民体系的完整性。国土安全部认为,取消推定效力将带来相对较小的额外合规成本,而由此产生的收益足以证明这些成本的合理性。因此,国土安全部认为,取消对延长居留申请的推定效力条款将是实现本次制定规则各项目标最有效的方式。
This proposed rule seeks to clarify and reinforce that the statutory burden of proof remains on the petitioner, encourage a more thorough review of each petition and its supporting evidence, and better protect the integrity of the legal immigration system. DHS believes that removing deference would impose relatively small additional compliance costs and that the resulting benefits it provides justify these costs. Therefore, DHS believes that removing the deference provision for extension of stay petitions would be the most effective way to achieve the goals of this rulemaking.
国土安全部考虑了一种备选方案,即修订推定效力法规,更明确地说明美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)是否适用推定效力具有裁量权。国土安全部并未提议采用此备选方案,因为这可能会给申请人和美国公民及移民服务局带来不必要的困惑和不确定性。相反,国土安全部认为,完全取消推定效力是增强项目完整性的最切实可行的备选方案,包括确保举证责任仍由申请人承担,以证明非移民外国工人有资格在美国继续工作,并确保美国公民及移民服务局的裁决官员在授予该项福利之前,根据每份非移民工人延期申请自身的是非曲直进行全面审查。国土安全部未考虑其他备选方案。国土安全部欢迎公众就此问题提出意见。
DHS considered an alternative to revise the deference regulation to more clearly state that USCIS has discretion whether to apply deference. DHS is not proposing this alternative because it would likely create unnecessary confusion and uncertainty for both petitioners and USCIS. Instead, DHS believes that removing deference entirely is the most practical alternative to enhance program integrity, including by ensuring that the burden remains on the petitioner to establish the nonimmigrant alien worker's eligibility to continue working in the United States, and that USCIS adjudicators thoroughly review each nonimmigrant worker extension request on its own merits before granting the benefit. DHS did not consider other alternatives. DHS welcomes public comments on this issue.

B. 1980年《管制灵活性法案》
B. Regulatory Flexibility Act of 1980
初步管制灵活性分析
Initial Regulatory Flexibility Analysis
《管制灵活性法案》(RFA),公法96-354号,经1996年《小企业管制执行公平法案》(公法104-121号)修订,载于《美国法典》第5编第601条至第612条,要求联邦机构在制定规则的过程中考虑法规对小企业、小型政府辖区和小型组织可能产生的影响。“小实体”一词包括小企业、独立拥有和经营且在其所属领域不占主导地位的非营利组织,以及人口少于50,000人的政府辖区。[30] “个人”不被视为小实体,对个人造成的成本在《管制灵活性法案》的意义上不被视为对小实体的影响。此外,法院已裁定,《管制灵活性法案》仅要求机构在规则直接(第65131页)规制小实体时,才需对小实体影响进行管制灵活性分析。[31] 因此,就《管制灵活性法案》而言,规则对小实体产生的间接影响不被视为成本。本拟议规则将影响提交I-129表格(非移民工人申请表)的小实体。因此,国土安全部已在下文编写初步管制灵活性分析,供公众评议。
The RFA, Public Law 96-354, as amended by the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act of 1996, Public Law 104-121, 5 U.S.C. 601 through 612, requires Federal agencies to consider the potential impact of regulations on small businesses, small governmental jurisdictions, and small organizations during the development of their rules. The term “small entities” comprises small businesses, not-for-profit organizations that are independently owned and operated and are not dominant in their fields, and governmental jurisdictions with populations of less than 50,000.[30] An “individual” is not considered a small entity and costs to an individual are not considered a small entity impact for RFA purposes. In addition, the courts have held that the RFA requires an agency to perform a regulatory flexibility analysis of small entity impacts only when a rule directly ( printed page 65131) regulates small entities.[31] Consequently, indirect impacts from a rule on a small entity are not considered as costs for RFA purposes. This proposed rule would affect small entities that submit Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker. Consequently, DHS has prepared an initial regulatory flexibility analysis below for public comment.
1. 机构考虑采取此项行动的原因说明
1. A Description of the Reason Why the Action by the Agency Is Being Considered
本拟议规则的目的是废除8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)条及遵从政策。该政策指示移民局官员在裁决涉及相同当事人和相同基本事实的I-129表格(非移民工人申请表)申请时,对申请人、请愿人或受益人资格此前的认定给予遵从,除非存在重大错误、重大变化,或存在对资格产生不利影响的新的重大信息。这项拟议变更将使该法规更好地与制定法保持一致,后者将证明资格的举证责任置于请愿人身上。
The purpose of this proposed rule is to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) and the deference policy which instructs USCIS officers to give deference to a prior determination of the petitioner's, applicant's or beneficiary's eligibility when adjudicating a request filed on Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker, involving the same parties and the same underlying facts, unless there was a material error, a material change, or new material information that adversely impacts eligibility. This proposed change would better align the regulation with the statute, which places the burden of proof in establishing eligibility on the petitioner.

在本分析中，美国国土安全部（DHS）使用了2025财年雇主提交H-1B延期申请的内部数据。[32] 拟议规则将影响在Form I-129表格上为多个非移民类别提交的延期申请。为本分析之目的，DHS依赖H-1B申请人数据作为雇主层面信息的最佳可用来源。Form I-129表格用于多个非移民工作者类别，然而，DHS并未掌握针对所有非H-1B类别均足够可靠的雇主层面数据，以便对所有Form I-129申请人开展类似的小实体分析。H-1B申请占所有Form I-129表格提交总数的75%以上，并包含雇主层面数据，使DHS能够评估申请人规模。由于H-1B申请占Form I-129申请的大多数，并提供了评估小实体影响时最可靠的可用数据，DHS将H-1B申请人数据作为更广泛的Form I-129申请人群体的合理替代指标。DHS将内部雇主数据与美国小企业管理局（SBA）规模标准表[33]进行了合并，以识别小实体。
For this analysis, DHS used internal data for employers filing H-1B extension petitions for FY 2025.[32] The proposed rule would affect extension petitions filed on Form I-129 for multiple nonimmigrant classifications. For purposes of this analysis, DHS relied on H-1B petitioner data as the best available source of employer-level information. Form I-129 is used for several nonimmigrant worker classifications; however, DHS does not have sufficiently reliable employer-level data for all non-H-1B classifications to conduct a comparable small entity analysis across all Form I-129 petitioners. H-1B petitions account for more than 75 percent of all Form I-129 filings and include employer-level data that allow DHS to evaluate petitioner size. Because H-1B filings constitute the majority of Form I-129 petitions and provide the most reliable available data for assessing small entity impacts, DHS used H-1B petitioner data as a reasonable proxy for the broader Form I-129 petitioner population. DHS merged the internal employer data with the Small Business Administration (SBA) table of size standards [33] to identify small entities.

Some entities were classified as small based on their annual revenue, and some by their number of employees. Based on FY 2022 through FY 2025 data, petitions with an RFE took an average of 64 additional calendar days to process, which may affect employer planning for staffing, project timelines, and work assignments, as well as workers' uncertainty regarding their longer-term employment authorization and status.
可量化的成本是雇主花费时间准备和提交额外补充证据请求(RFE)所产生的时间机会成本,这是由于预期RFE数量增加所致。正如《监管影响分析》(RIA)中“量化成本”部分所述,美国国土安全部(DHS)估计,为重新确立资格而准备额外证明文件和证据所需的额外成本,将导致所有受影响雇主的年度总成本增加133,987美元,对应约6,044份额外的RFE。DHS确定共有22,748家独立雇主受本规则影响。DHS通过将估计的年度总成本133,987美元除以归因于本规则的RFE增加数量6,044,计算出每份RFE的成本,得出每份RFE的估计成本约为22美元。由于小实体提交申请的件数中位数为一件,[34]因受取消推定政策而收到RFE的小实体需要花费时间提交额外的证明文件和证据,估计将产生约22美元的成本。如表3所示,在推定政策未生效期间,RFE发生率为23.4%。因此,DHS估计,在可能受本规则影响的小实体中,约23.4%,即3,637家,将因本规则而产生成本。每个受影响小实体的估计成本约为22美元。因此,尽管本规则可能影响相当数量的小实体,但DHS预计对受影响小实体的经济影响不会是显著的。DHS欢迎公众就此分析提出意见,尤其欢迎能够进一步揭示本拟议规则量化成本的意见。
The quantifiable cost is the opportunity cost of employer time spent preparing and submitting additional RFEs resulting from the expected increase in RFEs. As discussed in the “Quantified Costs” section of the RIA, DHS estimates that the annual increase in costs to petitioners would be $133,987 across all affected employers for preparing additional supporting documentation and evidence to re-establish eligibility for an estimated 6,044 additional RFEs. DHS identified 22,748 unique employers affected by this rule. DHS estimates the cost per RFE by dividing the total estimated annual cost of $133,987 by the estimated increase in RFEs attributable to this rule, 6044, resulting in an estimated cost of approximately $22 per RFE. Because the median number of petitions filed by small entities is one,[34] a small entity that receives an RFE due to the removal of deference would need to spend time submitting additional supporting documentation and evidence and would incur an estimated cost of approximately $22. As shown in Table 3, the RFE rate during the period when the deference policy was not in effect was 23.4 percent. DHS therefore estimates that approximately 23.4 percent, or 3,637, of the small entities potentially affected by the rule would incur costs as a result of this rule. The estimated cost would be approximately $22 per affected small entity. Therefore, although the rule may affect a substantial number of small entities, DHS does not expect the economic impact on affected small entities to be significant. DHS welcomes public comments on this analysis, particularly those comments that would provide further insight into the quantified costs of this proposed rule.

4. A Description of the Projected Reporting, Recordkeeping, and Other Compliance Requirements of the Proposed Rule, Including an Estimate of the Classes of Small Entities That Will Be Subject to the Requirement and the Types of Professional Skills Necessary for Preparation of the Report or Record
拟议规则将主要通过增加与延长停留期限申请相关的RFE数量来提高合规要求,这是因为官员将不再对先前批准给予推定,而是会对每份申请的资格条件进行更全面的审查。因此,某些申请人将需要更频繁地审阅RFE通知,收集并整理额外的证明文件,并对先前的批准及持续资格情况保留更完善的记录。DHS预计,这些工作通常将由人力资源专员、内部律师或外聘律师负责执行,由其负责分析RFE内容、整理所需文件,并代表申请人及时准备完整的答复。
The proposed rule would increase compliance requirements primarily by increasing the number of RFEs associated with extension of stay petitions, because officers would no longer apply deference to prior approvals and would instead more thoroughly examine eligibility for each request. As a result, certain petitioners would need to review RFE notices more frequently, collect and organize additional supporting documentation, and maintain more robust records of prior approvals and ongoing eligibility. DHS anticipates that these activities would generally be carried out by an HR specialist, an in-house attorney, or an outsourced attorney, who would be responsible for analyzing the RFEs, assembling the required documentation, and preparing timely, complete responses on behalf of the petitioner.

C. 1995年《无资金授权改革法》
C. Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995
1995年《无资金授权改革法》(UMRA)的目的之一,是遏制在没有联邦资金支持的情况下向州、地方及部落政府施加联邦强制要求的做法。UMRA第二章要求各联邦机构就拟议规则,或已就其发布过拟议规则的最终规则中所含的任何联邦强制要求的影响,编制书面说明,该等强制要求可能导致州、地方及部落政府整体,或私营部门,在任何一年内产生1亿美元或以上的支出(每年根据通货膨胀进行调整)。[35]
The Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995 (UMRA) is intended, among other things, to curb the practice of imposing unfunded Federal mandates on State, local, and Tribal governments. Title II of UMRA requires each Federal agency to prepare a written statement assessing the effects of any Federal mandate in a proposed rule, or final rule for which the agency published a proposed rule, that includes any Federal mandate that may result in a $100 million or more expenditure (adjusted annually for inflation) in any one year by State, local, and tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector.[35]

The inflation adjusted value of $100 million in 1995 is approximately $211 million in 2025 based on the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U).[36] This proposed rule does not contain a Federal mandate as the term is defined under UMRA.[37] The requirements of title II of UMRA, therefore, do not apply, and DHS has not prepared a statement under UMRA.

G. 《国家环境政策法》
G. National Environmental Policy Act
国土安全部及其所属部门对拟议监管行动进行分析,以确定《国家环境政策法》(NEPA,《美国法典》第42编第4321条及后续条款)是否适用于这些行动,若适用,则需确定所要求的分析程度。国土安全部第023-01号指令(经第01次修订)《实施国家环境政策法》(第023-01号指令,经第01次修订)及第023-01-001-01号指令手册(经第01次修订)(《指令手册》)[38] 确立了国土安全部及其所属部门用于遵守NEPA的政策和程序。
DHS and its components analyze proposed regulatory actions to determine whether the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA), 42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq., applies to them and, if so, what degree of analysis is required. DHS Directive 023-01 Rev. 01 “Implementing the National Environmental Policy Act” (Dir. 023-01 Rev. 01) and Instruction Manual 023-01-001-01 Rev. 01 (Instruction Manual) [38] establish the policies and procedures that DHS and its components use to comply with NEPA.
NEPA允许联邦机构在其NEPA实施程序中确立若干行动类别(“分类排除”),根据经验,这些类别单独或累积均不会对人类环境产生重大影响,因此不需要进行环境评估或环境影响报告。参见《美国法典》第42编第4336(a)(2)条、第4336e(1)条。《指令手册》附录A列出了国土安全部的分类排除事项。[39]
NEPA allows Federal agencies to establish, in their NEPA implementing procedures, categories of actions (“categorical exclusions”) that experience has shown do not, individually or cumulatively, have a significant effect on the human environment and, therefore, do not require an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement. See42 U.S.C. 4336(a)(2), 4336e(1). The Instruction Manual, Appendix A lists the DHS Categorical Exclusions.[39]
根据国土安全部NEPA实施程序,一项行动若要被归入分类排除,必须满足以下三项条件:(1)整个行动明显符合一项或多项分类排除;(2)该行动不是更大行动的一部分;以及(3)不存在可能产生重大环境影响的特殊情况。[40] 国土安全部拟删除第8 CFR 214.1(c)(5)条中关于对某些资格认定给予遵从的监管条款,以使该法规更好地与制定法保持一致,并更好地确保移民体系的完整性,包括美国工人的利益。对国土安全部现行法规的这一拟议修改纯属行政性质,该规则明显符合分类排除A3(a)。
Under DHS NEPA implementing procedures, for an action to be categorically excluded, it must satisfy each of the following three conditions: (1) the entire action clearly fits within one or more of the categorical exclusions; (2) the action is not a piece of a larger action; and (3) no extraordinary circumstances exist that create the potential for a significant environmental effect.[40] DHS is proposing to remove a regulatory provision at 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5) codifying deference to certain eligibility determinations to better align the regulation with the statute and better ensure the integrity of the immigration system, including the interests of U.S. workers. This proposed change to existing DHS regulations is strictly administrative in nature and the rule clearly fits within categorical exclusion A3(a).
因此,国土安全部认定,对现行法规的拟议修订明显符合国土安全部NEPA实施程序中确立的分类排除A3,属于不改变环境影响的行政性变更,不属于更大联邦行动的一部分,且不存在可能产生重大环境影响的特殊情况。参见《指令手册》023-01第V.B(2)(a)至(c)条。
Accordingly, DHS finds that the proposed amendments to current regulations clearly fits within categorical exclusion A3 established in DHS's NEPA implementing procedures as an administrative change with no change in environmental effect, is not part of a larger Federal action, and does not present extraordinary circumstances that create the potential for a significant environmental effect. See Instruction Manual 023-01 at V.B(2)(a)-(c).
