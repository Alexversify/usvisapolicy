DHS将HR-1规定的两项移民执法费用在2027财年上调至5300美元

发布日期 2026年10月1日生效日期 2026年10月16日

美国国土安全部(DHS)公布了根据HR-1法案(One Big Beautiful Bill Act)设立的两项移民执法相关费用在2027财年的通胀调整结果。这两项费用分别适用于被缺席判决驱逐后又被逮捕的外国人,以及在入境口岸之间被截获的不可入境外国人,费用将从5130美元上调至5300美元。DHS将从2026年10月16日起开始收取新费用。

要点

  • HR-1要求DHS每年根据消费者物价指数(CPI-U)对这两项移民执法费用进行通胀调整。
  • 两项费用均从2026财年的5130美元上调至2027财年的5300美元,向下取整至最接近的10美元倍数。
  • 该费用适用于依据移民与国籍法(INA)第240(b)(5)条被缺席判决驱逐后又被ICE逮捕的外国人,以及在入境口岸之间被截获的不可入境外国人。
  • 如缺席驱逐令依据INA第240(b)(5)(C)条被撤销,则可获得费用豁免。
  • 这两项费用均不提供减免(waiver)。
  • 新费用将自2026年10月16日起生效并开始征收。

影响对象

被缺席判决驱逐后又被ICE逮捕的外国人,以及在入境口岸之间被截获的不可入境外国人受到影响。

建议行动

受影响人员将收到DHS的个别通知及缴费说明,应按通知要求操作。

SUMMARY:
美国国土安全部(DHS)现宣布,根据《一个大而美丽法案》(One Big Beautiful Bill Act,HR-1)设立的两项移民执法相关费用的2027财年通货膨胀调整。HR-1要求DHS每年对某些移民执法相关费用进行通货膨胀调整。针对被缺席判决驱逐出境后又被逮捕的外国人,以及在入境口岸之间被截获的不可入境外国人,2027财年调整后的费用金额为5,300美元。DHS将于2026年10月16日起开始评估和收取这些新金额。
The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is announcing Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 inflationary adjustments for two of the immigration enforcement-related fees established by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (HR-1). HR-1 requires that DHS annually adjust certain immigration enforcement-related fees for inflation. The adjusted fee amounts for FY 2027 for aliens ordered removed in absentia who are subsequently arrested and inadmissible aliens apprehended between ports of entry are $5,300. On October 16, 2026, DHS will begin assessing and collecting these new amounts.

一、背景与授权依据
I. Background and Authority
2025年7月4日,总统签署《一个大而美丽法案》(One Big Beautiful Bill Act),成为《公法》第119-21号,139 Stat. 72(HR-1)。该法案除其他事项外,设立了若干项移民相关费用。[1] 与本通知相关的是,HR-1设立了适用于以下情形的两项移民执法费用:(1)依据《移民与国籍法》(INA)第240(b)(5)条,8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5),被缺席判决驱逐出境、随后被美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕的外国人;以及(2)在入境口岸之间被截获时属于不可入境的外国人。[2] 2025年9月8日,DHS向公众发布通知,宣布DHS将依照法定授权,自2025财年起按两项费用各5,000美元的金额开始评估和收取这些费用。[3] 自2025年9月8日起,DHS已逐一通知适用这些费用的外国人,并提供有关如何缴纳这些费用的说明。[4]
On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (HR-1), which, among other things, established a number of immigration-related fees.[1] Relevant to this notice, HR-1 established two immigration enforcement fees applicable to the following: (1) aliens who are ordered removed in absentia pursuant to section 240(b)(5) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), 8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5), and are subsequently arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE); and (2) inadmissible aliens at the time such aliens are apprehended between ports of entry.[2] On September 8, 2025, DHS provided notice to the public announcing that DHS would begin assessing and collecting these fees in the amount of $5,000 for FY 2025 for both fees, as authorized by statute.[3] As of September 8, 2025, DHS has been individually notifying aliens to whom these fees apply and providing instructions on how to make payments for these fees.[4]
2025年11月20日,DHS发布了2026财年通知,将费用因通货膨胀调整为5,130美元,该调整自2025年12月1日起生效。[5] 根据HR-1规定,这些费用须在每个财政年度根据通货膨胀进行年度调整。[6] 具体而言,这两项费用均规定,通货膨胀年度调整应按以下方式计算:
On November 20, 2025, DHS published a notice for FY 2026 to adjust the fees for inflation to $5,130, which became effective on December 1, 2025.[5] Under HR-1, these fees are subject to annual adjustments for inflation each fiscal year.[6] Specifically, both fees provide that the annual adjustment for inflation will be calculated as follows:

A. Adjustments for Immigration Enforcement-Related Fees Under HR-1
根据现行程序,美国国土安全部(DHS)将单独通知适用这些调整后费用的外国人,并就如何缴纳自2026年10月16日起征收的费用提供说明。根据《HR-1法案》第100016(b)(2)条和第100017(b)(2)条的规定,2027财年的年度调整额计算方式为以下两项之和:本款项下最近一个已结束财年所要求的费用金额(5,130美元),以及将(A)段所述金额乘以2026年7月城镇消费者消费价格指数(CPI-U)(333.918)超过2025年7月CPI-U(323.048)的百分比所得乘积,并向下舍入至最接近的10美元的倍数。将1.03365的乘数(保留小数点后五位)应用于此前5,130美元的费用,得出新金额为5302.62美元(四舍五入至最接近的美分),然后向下舍入至最接近的10美元增量,结果为5,300美元。2027财年的新费用如下:
Consistent with the current process, DHS will individually notify aliens to whom these adjusted fees apply and provide instructions on how to pay the fees levied as of October 16, 2026. In accordance with HR-1 secs. 100016(b)(2) and 100017(b)(2), the annual adjustment for FY 2027 is calculated as the sum of the amount of the fee required under this subsection for the most recently concluded fiscal year ($5,130); and the product resulting from the multiplication of the amount referred to in subparagraph (A) by the percentage by which the Consumer Price Index of All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) (333.918) for the month of July 2026 exceeds the CPI-U for July 2025 (323.048), rounded to the next lowest multiple of $10. The multiplier of 1.03365 (rounded to the fifth decimal place) applied to the previous fee of $5,130 results in a new amount of $5302.62 (rounded to the nearest cent) and then rounded down to the nearest $10 increment is $5,300. B. 第100016条。根据《移民与国籍法》第240(b)(5)条被缺席判令驱逐出境后随后被美国移民及海关执法局逮捕的外国人
B. Section 100016. Aliens Ordered Removed in Absentia Pursuant to INA Section 240(b)(5) and Subsequently Arrested by ICE
根据《HR-1法案》第100016(b)(2)条规定的2027财年通货膨胀调整计算结果,美国国土安全部将自2026年10月16日起,对适用《HR-1法案》第100016条, 即缺席驱逐令外国人费用, 所规定费用的外国人征收并评估5,300美元的费用。该费用适用于因未出席驱逐程序而被移民法官依据《移民与国籍法》第240(b)(5)条缺席判令驱逐出境,随后被美国移民及海关执法局逮捕的外国人。[8] 《HR-1法案》为依据《移民与国籍法》第240(b)(5)(C)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1229a(b)(5)(C)条,撤销缺席驱逐令的外国人规定了该费用的豁免情形。 9 参见公法第119-21号第100016(c)条。《HR-1法案》还规定,此项执法费用不提供任何豁免。 参见公法第119-21号 第100016(e)条。
Based on the calculation for inflation adjustments for FY 2027, as set forth under HR-1 section 100016(b)(2), DHS will collect and assess a fee in the amount of $5,300 for aliens subject to the fee under HR-1, section 100016, Fee for aliens ordered removed in absentia, beginning October 16, 2026. This fee is applicable to aliens who have been ordered removed in absentia by an immigration judge pursuant to INA section 240(b)(5) for failure to attend removal proceedings and are subsequently arrested by ICE.[8] HR-1 provides an exception to this fee for aliens whose removal order in absentia is rescinded pursuant to INA section 240(b)(5)(C), 8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C). 9 SeePublic Law 119-21 sec. 100016(c). HR-1 also provides that no waivers are available for this enforcement fee. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100016(e).
C. 第100017条。在入境口岸之间被抓获的不可入境外国人
C. Section 100017. Inadmissible Aliens Apprehended Between Ports of Entry
根据《HR-1法案》第100017(b)(2)条规定的2027财年通货膨胀调整计算结果,美国国土安全部,包括美国移民及海关执法局和美国海关与边境保护局(CBP),将自2026年10月16日起,对适用《HR-1法案》第100017条, 即不可入境外国人抓获费用, 所规定费用的外国人征收并评估5,300美元的费用。该费用适用于依据《移民与国籍法》第212条,即《美国法典》第8编第1182条,被认定为不可入境,且在入境口岸之间被美国国土安全部抓获的外国人。在入境口岸之间被美国国土安全部抓获的外国人通常依据《移民与国籍法》第212(a)(6)条,即《美国法典》第8编第1182(a)(6)条所规定的不可入境理由,该条规定,任何“未经准许入境或假释而出现在美国境内,或在非司法部长指定的时间或地点抵达美国的”外国人,均属不可入境。[10] 但是,外国人也可能基于《移民与国籍法》第212条,即《美国法典》第8编第1182条所列的其他不可入境理由而被认定为不可入境。
Based on the calculation for inflation adjustments for FY 2027, as set forth under HR-1 section 100017(b)(2), DHS, including ICE and U.S. Customs and Border Protection, will collect and assess a fee in the amount of $5,300 for aliens subject to the fee under HR-1, section 100017, Inadmissible alien apprehension fee, beginning October 16, 2026. This fee is applicable to aliens who are inadmissible under INA section 212, 8 U.S.C. 1182, and are apprehended by DHS between ports of entry. Aliens who are apprehended by DHS between ports of entry are often subject to the ground of inadmissibility under INA section 212(a)(6), 8 U.S.C. 1182(a)(6), which provides that any alien “present in the United States without being admitted or paroled, or who arrives in the United States at any time or place other than as designated by the Attorney General, is inadmissible.” [10] However, an alien could also be inadmissible based on additional grounds of inadmissibility listed under INA section 212, 8 U.S.C. 1182.
