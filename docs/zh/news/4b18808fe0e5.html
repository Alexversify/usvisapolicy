执法与欺诈移民局 (USCIS)#执法·遣返·欺诈

古巴籍人士因国际人口走私及洗钱共谋罪被判有罪

发布日期 2026年9月28日

在USCIS参与的调查下，居住在佛罗里达州海厄利亚的古巴籍人士拉萨罗·阿兰·卡布雷拉-罗德里格斯因共谋经营国际人口走私组织并洗钱而被陪审团裁定有罪。该组织每人收费1,500至40,000美元,并利用虚假的ESTA申请和虚假欧洲国籍声明帮助古巴人非法入境美国。

要点

  • 该走私组织协助数千名古巴公民通过南部边境非法进入美国。
  • 在共谋期间,超过2,000万美元经由被告及其共谋者控制的至少57个银行账户流转。
  • 古巴公民不符合ESTA计划资格,但该组织利用虚假地址和虚假旅行记录提交了数百份欺诈性ESTA申请。
  • 陪审团裁定卡布雷拉-罗德里格斯犯有为谋利共谋人口走私罪,以及共谋国际洗钱和洗钱隐匿罪。
  • 他最高可被判处20年监禁,定于12月17日接受判刑。
  • 共有十二人在替代起诉书中被起诉,多名共同被告已认罪或已被判刑。

影响对象

此案涉及支付费用或参与针对寻求进入美国的古巴公民的有组织走私及欺诈签证计划的人员。

建议行动

如发现涉嫌移民福利欺诈行为,可通过USCIS Tip Form向USCIS举报。

佛罗里达州坦帕报道。美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)在一项调查中发挥了关键作用,该调查最终导致28岁的佛罗里达州海厄利亚市居民拉萨罗·阿兰·卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯(Lazaro Alain Cabrera-Rodriguez)因参与一起国际偷运外国人及洗钱共谋案而被定罪。根据法庭文件及审判中出示的证据,卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯是一名古巴籍外国人,他与他人共谋经营一个偷运外国人组织,协助数千名古巴外国人经由南部边境非法进入美国。该组织的偷运服务收费介于1500美元至40000美元之间,共谋者有时还包租私人飞机运送成批的外国人。佛罗里达州中区美国联邦检察官办公室宣布了此定罪结果。
TAMPA, Fla. — U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services played a critical role in the investigation that led to the conviction of Lazaro Alain Cabrera-Rodriguez, 28, of Hialeah, Florida, for his role in an international alien smuggling and money laundering conspiracy. According to court documents and evidence presented at trial, Cabrera-Rodriguez, a Cuban alien, conspired with others to operate an alien smuggling organization that facilitated the unlawful entry of thousands of Cuban aliens into the United States through the southern border. The organization charged between $1,500 and $40,000 for its smuggling services, and co-conspirators sometimes chartered private planes to transport groups of aliens. The U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Middle District of Florida announced the conviction.
被告人经常使用伪造的旅行许可电子系统(ESTA)签证豁免,并在多个社交媒体平台上宣传其偷运及虚假签证服务。通过这些手段,他们招揽古巴国民及其他外国人,企图非法入境并在美国以欺诈方式获得合法移民身份。财务记录显示,在共谋期间,超过2000万美元通过被告及其共谋者控制的至少57个银行账户流转。
The defendants often used fraudulent Electronic System for Travel Authorization visa waivers and advertised their smuggling and bogus visa services on multiple social media platforms. Through these efforts, they solicited Cuban nationals and other aliens to try to illegally enter the country and fraudulently obtain lawful immigration status in the United States. Financial records show that, over the course of the conspiracy, more than $20 million passed through at least 57 bank accounts controlled by the defendant and his co-conspirators.
该偷运外国人组织所宣传的服务包括协助古巴人虚假声称拥有欧洲国籍,以便凭欧洲旅游签证豁免或ESTA进入美国。该组织还使用虚假地址,向美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)提交了数百份欺诈性ESTA申请。古巴国民不具备参加ESTA项目的资格。被告人虚假陈述称申请人自2011年以来未曾前往古巴,但实际上他们明知申请人在提交ESTA申请时正身处古巴。卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯通过协助将外国人从其原籍国(包括古巴)运送至美国,进一步推动了该共谋行为。这包括购买国际及国内航班机票,将外国人运送至美国境内及境内各地。
The alien smuggling organization advertised services that included helping Cubans make false claims of European citizenship to enter the United States on a European tourist visa waiver or ESTA. The organization also filed hundreds of fraudulent ESTA applications with U.S. Customs and Border Protection using fake addresses. Cuban nationals are not eligible for the ESTA program. The defendants falsely represented that the applicants had not been in Cuba since 2011, despite knowing the applicants were in Cuba when the ESTA applications were submitted. Cabrera-Rodriguez furthered the conspiracy by helping transport aliens from their countries of origin, including Cuba, into the United States. This included purchasing international and domestic flights to transport aliens into and throughout the United States.
卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯及其共同被告还共谋定期将资金转移至美国境外的公司。2021年至2024年间,卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯及其共谋者从美国汇出超过600000美元,用于为外国人非法进入美国购买机票。
Cabrera-Rodriguez and his co-defendants also conspired to regularly move funds to companies outside of the United States. Between 2021 and 2024, Cabrera-Rodriguez and his co-conspirators transmitted more than $600,000 from the United States to purchase flights for aliens to unlawfully enter the United States.
陪审团裁定卡夫雷拉罗德里格斯犯有共谋为谋取经济利益而偷运外国人罪、共谋实施国际洗钱罪及共谋实施洗钱隐匿罪。他定于12月17日接受量刑,最高可面临20年监禁。联邦地区法院法官将在参考美国量刑指南及其他法定因素后,确定最终判决。
A jury convicted Cabrera-Rodriguez of conspiracy to commit alien smuggling for financial gain, conspiracy to commit international money laundering, and conspiracy to commit money laundering concealment. He is scheduled to be sentenced Dec. 17 and faces a maximum penalty of 20 years in prison. A federal district court judge will determine any sentence after considering the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines and other statutory factors.
在补充起诉书中,共有十二人因参与该共谋而被起诉。利安尼斯·雅伊塞利斯·维加佩雷斯(Liannys Yaiselys Vega-Perez),31岁;米格尔·亚历杭德罗·马丁内斯·瓦斯孔塞洛斯(Miguel Alejandro Martinez Vasconcelos),31岁;莱拉·利贝塔德·特雷托·桑托斯(Layra Libertad Treto Santos),30岁;埃曼纽尔·马丁内斯·冈萨雷斯(Emanuel Martinez Gonzalez),29岁;瓦尔比斯·波索杜特尔(Walbis Pozo-Dutel),31岁;路易斯·埃马努埃尔·埃斯卡洛纳马雷罗(Luis Emmanuel Escalona-Marrero),32岁;以及吉斯莱维·佩拉尔塔·孔苏埃格拉(Gisleivy Peralta Consuegra),41岁,均已认罪,正等待量刑。埃里克·文图拉卡斯特罗(Erik Ventura-Castro),24岁,已于5月7日认罪,并于7月29日被判处30个月监禁。
Twelve individuals were charged in a superseding indictment for their roles in the conspiracy. Liannys Yaiselys Vega-Perez, 31; Miguel Alejandro Martinez Vasconcelos, 31; Layra Libertad Treto Santos, 30; Emanuel Martinez Gonzalez, 29; Walbis Pozo-Dutel, 31; Luis Emmanuel Escalona-Marrero, 32; and Gisleivy Peralta Consuegra, 41, have all pleaded guilty and are awaiting sentencing. Erik Ventura-Castro, 24, pleaded guilty on May 7 and was sentenced on July 29 to 30 months in prison.
美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)国土安全调查局坦帕办公室、美国边境巡逻队迈阿密区以及USCIS欺诈检测与国家安全局牵头了美国的调查工作,并获得位于华盛顿特区的国土安全调查局人口偷运科以及CBP国家目标中心国际拦截特别工作组的大力协助。美国司法部国际事务办公室及开曼群岛当局也提供了宝贵的协助。
U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement’s Homeland Security Investigations Tampa, U.S. Border Patrol Miami Sector, and USCIS’ Fraud Detection and National Security Directorate led U.S. investigative efforts, with substantial assistance from HSI’s Human Smuggling Unit in Washington, D.C., and CBP’s National Targeting Center International Interdiction Task Force. The Justice Department’s Office of International Affairs and authorities in the Cayman Islands provided valuable assistance.
本案由司法部刑事司人权与特别检控科审判律师阿曼达·布朗(Amanda Brown)以及佛罗里达州中区助理联邦检察官考特尼·德里(Courtney Derry)负责起诉。
Trial Attorney Amanda Brown of the Criminal Division’s Human Rights and Special Prosecutions Section and Assistant U.S. Attorney Courtney Derry for the Middle District of Florida are prosecuting the case.
