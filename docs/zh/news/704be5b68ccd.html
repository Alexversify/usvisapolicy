DHS豁免多项联邦法律 以加快在得克萨斯州修建边境墙

发布日期 2026年7月2日生效日期 2026年7月2日

美国国土安全部(DHS)援引《非法移民改革与移民责任法案》(IIRIRA)第102条,豁免多项联邦环境、历史保护和野生动物保护法律,以加快在得克萨斯州大弯(Big Bend)区域修建边境屏障和道路。此举修订了此前2026年2月17日发布的决定,新增了被豁免的法律要求。

要点

  • 该决定自2026年7月2日起生效。
  • 项目区域是得克萨斯州边境巡逻队大弯区内由GPS坐标界定的特定边境地段。
  • 被豁免的法律包括《国家环境政策法》《濒危物种法》《清洁水法》等数十部法律。
  • DHS表示,2021至2025财年期间,该区域逮捕非法入境者超过8.9万人,并查获大量毒品。
  • 此次豁免不撤销或影响此前根据IIRIRA第102条作出的其他豁免决定。

影响对象

此举影响得克萨斯州指定边境地段的建设、土地使用及环境审查事项,与个人签证申请者无直接关系。

建议行动

对相关土地或环境审查程序有利益关切的各方,可在联邦公报公告原文中查阅被豁免法律的完整清单及坐标信息。

SUMMARY:
美国国土安全部部长已依法确定，为确保在得克萨斯州境内靠近国际陆地边界的地区迅速建设隔离墙和道路，有必要豁免某些法律、法规及其他法律要求。该决定通知已于2026年2月17日在《联邦公报》上发布。本文件对2026年2月17日的决定通知进行修订，增补了被豁免的其他法律要求。
The Secretary of Homeland Security has determined, pursuant to law, that it is necessary to waive certain laws, regulations, and other legal requirements in order to ensure the expeditious construction of barriers and roads in the vicinity of the international land border in the state of Texas. The notice of determination was published in the Federal Register on February 17, 2026. This document amends the February 17, 2026, notice of determination by including additional legal requirements that are being waived.

补充信息：
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
美国国土安全部（“DHS”）的重要使命要求包括边境安全以及对非法进入美国行为的侦测与防范。边境安全对国家的国家安全至关重要。鉴于边境安全的至关重要性，国会已授权国土安全部实现并维持对国际陆地边界的行动管控。《2006年安全围栏法》，公法109-367号，第2条，120 Stat. 2638（2006年10月26日）（8 U.S.C. 1701 note）。国会将“行动管控”定义为防止一切非法进入美国的行为，包括恐怖分子、其他非法外国人、恐怖主义工具、麻醉品及其他违禁品的进入。同上。根据该授权，总统关于确保边境安全的行政命令指示本人采取一切适当行动，部署和建造实体隔离设施，以确保对美国南部边境实现完全的行动管控。第14165号行政命令，第3条（2025年1月20日）。
Important mission requirements of the Department of Homeland Security (“DHS”) include border security and the detection and prevention of illegal entry into the United States. Border security is critical to the nation's national security. Recognizing the critical importance of border security, Congress has mandated DHS to achieve and maintain operational control of the international land border. Secure Fence Act of 2006, Public Law 109-367, section 2, 120 Stat. 2638 (Oct. 26, 2006) (8 U.S.C. 1701 note). Congress defined “operational control” as the prevention of all unlawful entries into the United States, including entries by terrorists, other unlawful aliens, instruments of terrorism, narcotics, and other contraband. Id. Consistent with that mandate, the President's Executive Order on Securing Our Borders directs that I take all appropriate action to deploy and construct physical barriers to ensure complete operational control of the southern border of the United States. Executive Order 14165, section 3 (Jan. 20, 2025).
国会已授予国土安全部部长若干必要权力，以执行国土安全部的边境安全使命。其中一项权力见于经修订的《1996年非法移民改革和移民责任法》（“IIRIRA”）第102条。公法104-208号，C分部，110 Stat. 3009-546, 3009-554（1996年9月30日）（8 U.S.C 1103 note），经《2005年真实身份法》修订，公法109-13号，B分部，119 Stat. 231, 302, 306（2005年5月11日）（8 U.S.C. 1103 note），又经《2006年安全围栏法》修订，公法109-367号，第3条，120 Stat. 2638（2006年10月26日）（8 U.S.C. 1103 note），再经《2008年国土安全部拨款法》修订，公法110-161号，E分部，第五章，第564条，121 Stat. 2090（2007年12月26日）。在IIRIRA第102(a)条中，国会规定国土安全部部长应采取必要行动，在美国边境附近非法入境高发地区安装额外的实体隔离设施和道路（包括清除妨碍侦测非法入境者的障碍物），以阻止非法越境。在IIRIRA第102(b)条中，国会授权本人在行使第102(a)条权力时，提供额外的围栏、隔离设施、道路、照明、摄像头和传感器的安装，以实现并维持边境的行动管控。最后，在IIRIRA第102(c)条中，国会授予国土安全部部长豁免一切法律要求的权力，只要本人自行酌情确定该豁免对确保IIRIRA第102条所授权的隔离设施和道路的迅速建设属必要。
Congress has provided the Secretary of Homeland Security a number of authorities necessary to carry out DHS's border security mission. One of those authorities is found at section 102 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996, as amended (“IIRIRA”). Public Law 104-208, Div. C, 110 Stat. 3009-546, 3009-554 (Sept. 30, 1996) (8 U.S.C 1103 note), as amended by the REAL ID Act of 2005, Public Law 109-13, Div. B, 119 Stat. 231, 302, 306 (May 11, 2005) (8 U.S.C. 1103 note), as amended by the Secure Fence Act of 2006, Public Law 109-367, section 3, 120 Stat. 2638 (Oct. 26, 2006) (8 U.S.C. 1103 note), as amended by the Department of Homeland Security Appropriations Act, 2008, Public Law 110-161, Div. E, Title V, section 564, 121 Stat. 2090 (Dec. 26, 2007). In section 102(a) of IIRIRA, Congress provided that the Secretary of Homeland Security shall take such actions as may be necessary to install additional physical barriers and roads (including the removal of obstacles to detection of illegal entrants) in the vicinity of the United States border to deter illegal crossings in areas of high illegal entry into the United States. In section 102(b) of IIRIRA, Congress mandated that in carrying out the authority of section 102(a), I provide for the installation of additional fencing, barriers, roads, lighting, cameras, and sensors to achieve and maintain operational control of the border. Finally, in section 102(c) of IIRIRA, Congress granted to the Secretary of Homeland Security the authority to waive all legal requirements that I, in my sole discretion, determine necessary to ensure the expeditious construction of barriers and roads authorized by section 102 of IIRIRA.
决定与豁免
Determination and Waiver
第一节
Section 1
美国边境巡逻队大弯地区(Big Bend Sector)是非法入境高发地区。在2021财年至2025财年期间,边境巡逻队在大弯地区边境口岸之间抓获企图非法进入美国的外国人超过89,000人次。在同一时期内,边境巡逻队查获的毒品包括:大麻超过87,574磅、可卡因超过867磅、甲基苯丙胺超过1,156磅、海洛因超过12磅,以及芬太尼超过94磅。
The United States Border Patrol Big Bend Sector is an area of high illegal entry. Between fiscal year 2021 and fiscal year 2025, Border Patrol apprehended over 89,000 illegal aliens attempting to enter the United States between border crossings in the Big Bend Sector. In that same time period Border Patrol seized over 87,574 pounds of marijuana, over 867 pounds of cocaine, over 1,156 pounds of methamphetamine, over 12 pounds of heroin, and over 94 pounds of fentanyl.
自总统就任以来,美国国土安全部(DHS)已实现了历史上最安全的边境状况。然而,仍有更多工作可以也必须完成。如上述统计数据所示,大弯地区是非法入境高发地区,外国人经常企图非法进入美国并走私违禁毒品。鉴于我负有实现并维持边境实际控制的职责,我必须根据《非法移民改革和移民责任法》(IIRIRA)第102条赋予我的权力,在大弯地区修建额外的隔离设施和道路。因此,国土安全部将立即采取行动,在大弯地区的边境区段修建额外的隔离设施和道路。将进行此类建设的区段在本文件中称为“项目区域”,该区域在下文第2节中有更具体的描述。
Since the President took office, DHS has delivered the most secure border in history. More can and must be done, however. As the statistics cited above demonstrate, the Big Bend Sector is an area of high illegal entry where illegal aliens regularly attempt to enter the United States and smuggle illicit drugs, and given my mandate to achieve and maintain operational control of the border, I must use my authority under section 102 of IIRIRA to install additional barriers and roads in the Big Bend Sector. Therefore, DHS will take immediate action to construct additional barriers and roads in a segment of the border in the Big Bend Sector. The segment where such construction will occur is referred to herein as the “project area,” which is more specifically described in Section 2 below.

目前,根据《非法移民改革和移民责任法》(IIRIRA)第102(a)条和第102(b)条,在项目区域内美国边境附近修建额外的实体隔离设施和道路,以防止非法进入美国,存在迫切且紧急的需要。为确保在项目区域内迅速修建额外的实体隔离设施和道路,我已确定有必要行使《非法移民改革和移民责任法》第102(c)条赋予我的权力。
There is presently an acute and immediate need to construct additional physical barriers and roads in the vicinity of the border of the United States in order to prevent unlawful entries into the United States in the project area pursuant to section 102(a) and 102(b) of IIRIRA.
