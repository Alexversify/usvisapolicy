USCIS暂时恢复2017年关于入籍仪式选民登记的政策指南

发布日期 2026年8月12日生效日期 2026年8月31日

根据法院命令,USCIS暂时恢复2017年6月28日的政策指南,允许非营利、非党派组织在行政入籍仪式上协助新公民进行选民登记。该变化源于League of Women Voters诉USCIS案的法院命令,PA-2025-21号指南将于2026年8月31日起废止。

要点

  • USCIS恢复适用2017年6月28日关于非政府组织参与入籍仪式的政策指南。
  • 此变化源于League of Women Voters v. USCIS案(编号25-cv-3777,马里兰地区法院2025年8月3日)的法院命令。
  • 在州或地方选举机构无法到场的情况下,非营利、非党派组织可在仪式后分发和收集选民登记申请,并为新公民正式办理选民登记。
  • 名为Voter Registration at Administrative Naturalization Ceremonies的PA-2025-21号指南将于2026年8月31日起废止。
  • 恢复的指南内容载于USCIS政策手册第12卷J部分第5章F节和H节。
  • 该变化在相关诉讼最终裁决前适用,不一定是永久性的。

影响对象

影响参加行政入籍仪式的新公民以及参与选民登记工作的非营利组织。

建议行动

申请人无需采取任何行动;希望参与的组织应查阅USCIS政策手册第12卷J部分第5章中恢复的2017年相关规定。

U.S. Department of Homeland Security U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services www.uscis.gov U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services Office of the Director Camp Springs, MD 20588-0009 PA- 2026-08 August 12, 2026 Policy Alert SUBJECT: Participation of Non-Governmental Organizations at Administrative Naturalization Ceremonies Purpose U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is temporarily reverting to the June 28, 2017 policy guidance on non-governmental organization participation in administrative naturalization ceremonies, consistent with the court order in League of Women Voters v. USCIS, No. 25-cv-3777 (D. Md. Aug. 3, 2025). Pending final resolution of the litigation, this guidance permits nonprofit and nonpartisan non-governmental organizations to participate in administrative naturalization ceremonies by distributing and collecting voter registration applications and officially registering new citizens to vote following the ceremony, when state and local election offices are not available to do so.
背景 2025年8月29日，USCIS发布了一项新的政策手册更新及提示，规定仅允许州和地方选举官员（而非非政府组织）在行政入籍仪式上提供选民登记服务。1在2025年8月之前的USCIS政策指南及实践中，非政府组织被允许在行政入籍仪式上提供选民登记申请及一般性信息。2026年8月3日，美国马里兰州地区法院中止了USCIS于2025年8月29日发布的政策提示及更新的实施，待对该政策的法律挑战作出最终裁决。妇女选民联盟诉USCIS案（League of Women Voters v. USCIS, No. 25-cv-3777（D. Md. Aug. 3, 2026））。地区法院还命令USCIS恢复2017年6月28日当时有效的USCIS政策手册相关章节。基于此次更新，USCIS暂时恢复2017年6月28日的政策指南，以便遵守法院的命令。
Background On August 29, 2025, USCIS issued a new Policy Manual Update and Alert that permitted only state and local election officials (and not non-governmental organizations) to provide voter registration services at administrative naturalization ceremonies.1 Under USCIS policy guidance and practice prior to August 2025, non-governmental organizations were permitted to provide voter registration applications and general information at administrative naturalization ceremonies. On August 3, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the District of Maryland stayed the implementation of the USCIS August 29, 2025 Policy Alert and Update pending final resolution of a legal challenge to the policy. League of Women Voters v. USCIS, No. 25-cv-3777 (D. Md. Aug. 3, 2026). The District Court also ordered USCIS to reinstate the relevant sections of the USCIS Policy Manual that were in effect on June 28, 2017. With this update, USCIS is temporarily reinstating the June 28, 2017 policy guidance to facilitate compliance with the Court’s order.
本指南载于政策手册第12卷，自即日起生效，直至诉讼最终解决为止。政策手册中所载指南具有约束力，并取代此前任何相关指南。
This guidance, contained in Volume 12 of the Policy Manual, is effective immediately, pending final resolution of the litigation. The guidance contained in the Policy Manual is controlling and supersedes any related prior guidance.

明确说明被视为可接受的“选民登记服务”活动，包括分发、审核及协助填写选民登记表。
Clarifies activities considered acceptable “voter registration services,” including distribution, review, and assistance in completing voter registration forms.
规定非政府组织必须在任何活动举行前至少60天，以书面形式向当地USCIS办公室提交提供选民登记服务的申请。
Provides that non-governmental organizations must submit requests to provide voter registration services in writing to local USCIS offices at least 60 days prior to any event.
重申非政府组织不得以任何方式更改已完成的选民登记材料，也不得收集或保留在入籍仪式上报名选民登记者的个人信息。
Reaffirms that non-governmental organizations may not alter completed voter registration materials in any manner, nor collect or retain personal information of those who sign up for voter registration at naturalization ceremonies.
规定未能遵守相关要求的非政府组织，其参与资格可能被撤销。
Provides that a non-governmental organization that fails to comply with requirements may have their participation revoked.
