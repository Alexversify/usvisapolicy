ICE请求地方当局暂不释放涉嫌劫机的非法移民

发布日期 2026年11月12日

2026年11月10日,ICE向地方当局发出拘留请求(detainer),要求在通知ICE之前不要释放两次被驱逐出境的洪都拉斯公民罗尼·亚历山大·戈麦斯-阿尔韦托。戈麦斯-阿尔韦托此前曾以自称跨性别者为由获得庇护,11月9日他涉嫌在美联航航班上企图闯入驾驶舱并声称携带炸弹而被捕。DHS将此案与多起无关的庇护及移民欺诈起诉案件一并提及,作为加强庇护审查、打击欺诈行为的例证。

要点

  • 2026年11月9日,戈麦斯-阿尔韦托涉嫌在从纽瓦克飞往新奥尔良的美联航航班上企图闯入驾驶舱,并声称携带炸弹。
  • 飞机降落后他被拘捕,面临重罪指控,包括炸弹威胁、恐怖威胁和扰乱秩序。
  • ICE于11月10日向地方当局发出拘留请求,要求在通知ICE之前不要释放他。
  • 他此前曾两次被驱逐出境,第三次非法入境后以自称跨性别者为由获得庇护。
  • DHS表示该案反映出庇护制度的漏洞,并提及2026年5月发布的指令,要求依据8 U.S.C. 1324c(d)加强打击欺诈行为。
  • 新闻稿还列举了多起与此案无关的庇护及移民欺诈起诉案例,作为执法工作的例证。

影响对象

受影响者包括此次拘留请求所涉人员、负责羁押他的地方当局,以及可能面临更严格欺诈审查的庇护申请人和律师。

建议行动

公众无需采取特定行动,但庇护申请人及移民律师应注意欺诈执法力度的加强,包括依据8 U.S.C. 1324c(d)可能面临的处罚。

ICE Requests Local Authorities Not Release Criminal Illegal Alien Who Attempted To Hijack a Plane
发布日期:2026年11月12日
Release Date: November 12, 2026
这名曾被两次驱逐出境的刑事非法外国人在第三次试图非法入境时,以自己是跨性别者、担心会被杀害为由申请庇护,从而获准进入美国
This twice-deported criminal illegal alien was allowed into the country after he tried to illegally enter for a third time because he claimed asylum, citing he was afraid he would be killed because he is transgender
华盛顿消息,11月9日,来自洪都拉斯的非法外国人罗尼·亚历山大·戈麦斯-阿尔贝托(Rony Alexander Gomez-Alberto)在一架从新泽西州纽瓦克飞往路易斯安那州新奥尔良的美国联合航空公司航班上,声称携带炸弹,企图强行闯入驾驶舱。飞机降落后,他被拘留,并被控重罪炸弹威胁罪、恐怖威胁罪和扰乱秩序罪。11月10日,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)向地方当局发出拘留请求,要求在不通知ICE的情况下不得释放这名对公共安全构成威胁的人员。
WASHINGTON – On November 9, Rony Alexander Gomez-Alberto, an illegal alien from Honduras, attempted to storm the cockpit of a United Airlines plane traveling from Newark, New Jersey to New Orleans, Louisiana, claiming to have a bomb. After the plane landed, he was taken into custody and charged with felony bomb threat, terroristic threats, and disorderly conduct. On November 10, ICE lodged a detainer with local authorities requesting they do not release this public safety threat without notifying ICE.
罗尼·亚历山大·戈麦斯-阿尔贝托
Rony Alexander Gomez-Alberto
此前曾两次被驱逐出境后,他第三次非法进入美国并申请庇护。一名法官在他自称是跨性别者后批准了他的庇护申请,使其得以留在美国境内。
After having previously been deported twice, he illegally entered the country a third time and claimed asylum. A judge granted him asylum after he claimed to be transgender, allowing him to stay in the country.

Gomez-Alberto's asylum application
“这名刑事非法外国人企图强行闯入驾驶舱、劫持飞机,并声称自己携带炸弹。幸亏执法人员的努力,他现已被关押。这名曾被两次驱逐出境的刑事非法外国人之所以能够进入我国,是因为一个薄弱的庇护体系让他得以钻制度的空子,”国土安全部(DHS)部长马克维恩·穆林(Markwayne Mullin)表示。“在特朗普总统的领导下,ICE已逮捕2,312名已知或涉嫌恐怖分子,并正在加强我们的庇护体系,把美国公民的安全放在首位,严厉打击企图钻制度空子的欺诈分子。”
“This criminal illegal alien attempted to storm the cockpit, hijack a plane, and claimed he had a bomb. Thanks to law enforcement, he is now behind bars. This twice-deported criminal illegal alien was allowed into our country because of a weak asylum system that allowed him to game the system,” said DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin. “Under President Trump’s leadership, ICE has arrested 2,312 known or suspected terrorists and is strengthening our asylum system to put the safety of the American citizens first and crack down on fraudsters who try to game the system.”
从2025年1月20日至2026年11月10日,ICE已逮捕2,312名已知或涉嫌恐怖分子(KST),并已将2,157名已知或涉嫌恐怖分子驱逐出境。
From January 20, 2025, to November 10, 2026, ICE has arrested 2,312 known or suspected terrorists (KSTs), and has deported 2,157 KSTs.
在特朗普政府的领导下,国土安全部(DHS)和美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)已采取迅速行动,保障庇护体系安全,严厉打击庇护欺诈,以防止此类危险的非法外国人进入我国。
Under the Trump Administration, DHS and U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) have taken swift action to secure the asylum system and crack down on asylum fraud, so that dangerous illegal aliens like this cannot enter our country.
今年5月,国土安全部发布了一项指令,指示美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)采取进一步措施,打击虚假庇护申请。其中包括制定反欺诈政策,以进一步执行《美国法典》第8编第1324c(d)条,该法律规定了对文件欺诈行为的处罚。由于该指令的出台,ICE律师在执法方面获得了更大的权限,包括对在移民法庭提交虚假庇护申请的移民律师采取执法行动。除《美国法典》第8编第1324c(d)条外,庇护欺诈行为还可依据《美国法典》第18编第1546条和第18编第1001条作为重罪起诉。
In May, DHS released a directive instructing U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) to take additional steps to crack down on fraudulent asylum claims. This includes developing anti-fraud policies that will further enforce 8 U.S.C. § 1324c(d), a law that establishes penalties for violations of document fraud. As a result of this directive, ICE attorneys have greater authority to enforce this law, including enforcement actions against immigration attorneys who file false asylum claims in an immigration court. In addition to 8 U.S.C. § 1324c(d), asylum fraud can also be prosecuted as a felony under both 18 U.S.C. § 1546 and 18 U.S.C. § 1001.

2026年10月29日,五名古巴籍外国人和一名美国公民在佛罗里达州因参与一起涉及外国人偷渡、庇护欺诈和洗钱活动的国际共谋案而被判刑,该共谋活动使数千名外国人非法进入美国。美国司法部长去年首次宣布了对12名涉案人员的补充起诉书中的指控,内容涉及其在该共谋活动中所扮演的角色。根据法庭文件,被告人经营着一个规模庞大的外国人偷渡活动,通过准备签证申请、洗钱数百万美元的款项以及利用移民程序,协助古巴公民非法进入美国。该活动起初规模较小,后来发展成为一个商业企业。作为该企业运作的一部分,被告人经营着一家名为Asesoria Y Servicios Migratorios LLC的虚假移民服务公司,承诺提供庇护申请、工作许可证和合法永久居民申请服务。该公司在客户不知情且未经同意的情况下,大量炮制套话式的庇护申请,甚至阻止客户访问他们自己的USCIS在线账户。由于此次成功调查,另有多名其他人员已被定罪并判刑。
On October 29, 2026, five Cuban aliens and one U.S. citizen were sentenced in Florida for their roles in an international conspiracy involving alien smuggling, asylum fraud, and money laundering schemes that brought thousands of aliens illegally into the United States. The U.S. Attorney General made the initial announcement last year of charges against 12 individuals in a superseding indictment for their roles in the conspiracy. According to court documents, the defendants operated a prolific alien smuggling operation that facilitated the unlawful entry of Cuban nationals into the United States by preparing visa applications, laundering millions of dollars in payment, and exploiting the immigration process. What began as a small operation grew into a commercial enterprise. As part of the enterprise, the defendants operated a sham immigration business, Asesoria Y Servicios Migratorios LLC, which promised asylum filings, work permits, and lawful permanent residency applications. The company churned out boilerplate asylum claims without clients’ knowledge or consent and even blocked them from accessing their own USCIS online accounts. Several other individuals have been convicted and sentenced because of this successful investigation.
黄碧琪(“艾希莉”)，25岁，和杜文天，35岁，于2026年9月被起诉。黄某面临一项在入籍程序中作虚假陈述罪名和一项欺诈获取枪支罪名。杜某面临一项作为外国人持有枪支罪名。根据法庭文件，黄某于2023年以学生签证进入美国，并在2025年绿卡面谈中就其婚姻状况作虚假陈述后获得合法永久居民身份。杜某于2022年以B1/B2非移民签证进入美国，约一年后申请庇护。6月，黄某搬至纽约并结识杜某。此后她申请加入美国空军，并告诉招募官，她希望入伍以尽快获得公民身份，与现任配偶离婚，并与杜某结婚。
Biqi “Ashley” Huang, 25, and Wentian Du, 35, were indicted in September 2026. Huang faces one count of making a false statement in a naturalization proceeding and one count of fraudulent acquisition of a firearm. Du faces one count of being an alien in possession of a firearm. According to court documents, Huang entered the U.S. on a student visa in 2023 and received lawful permanent resident status after misrepresenting the status of her marriage during a 2025 Green Card interview. Du entered the U.S. in 2022 on a B1/B2 nonimmigrant visa and applied for asylum about a year later. In June, Huang moved to New York and met Du. She then applied to join the U.S. Air Force, telling a recruiter that she wanted to enlist to obtain citizenship as soon as possible, divorce her current spouse, and marry Du.
纳齐拉·哈吉·扎达，47岁，阿富汗籍，此前居住在德克萨斯州沃斯堡，于2026年9月在承认自己为外国恐怖分子后被美国遣返，这是美国外国恐怖分子遣返法庭(ATRC)审理的首例案件。扎达曾支持一项受伊斯兰国(ISIS)启发、计划于2024年选举日实施大规模枪击的阴谋，其子阿卜杜拉·哈吉·扎达和女婿纳西尔·艾哈迈德·陶赫迪此前已因此被捕并定罪。扎达现已被永久禁止入境美国。
Nazira Haji Zada, 47, an Afghan national previously residing in Fort Worth, Texas, was removed from the United States in September 2026 after conceding that she is an alien terrorist, in the first-ever case before the United States Alien Terrorist Removal Court (ATRC). Zada supported a plot to commit an ISIS-inspired mass shooting on Election Day in 2024 for which her son, Abdullah Haji Zada, and son-in-law, Nasir Ahmad Tawhedi, were previously arrested and convicted. Zada is now permanently inadmissible to the U.S.
尤尼尔·利马桑托斯，31岁，来自佛罗里达州坦帕市，于2026年8月就外国人偷渡、庇护欺诈和洗钱罪名认罪。利马桑托斯与他人合谋经营一个外国人偷渡组织，怂恿数千名古巴籍外国人使用欺诈性电子旅行许可系统(ESTA)申请和签证豁免进入美国。被告在多个社交媒体平台上宣传其偷渡服务和虚假签证服务，招揽古巴国民及其他外国人尝试非法进入美国并通过欺诈手段获得合法身份。利马桑托斯通过协助将外国人从其原籍国（包括古巴）转移至美国，推进了此项阴谋。这包括购买国际和国内航班机票，以将外国人运送进入美国并在美国境内转运。
Yuniel Lima-Santos, 31, of Tampa, Florida, pleaded guilty in August 2026 to alien smuggling, asylum fraud, and money laundering. Lima-Santos conspired with others to operate an alien smuggling organization that encouraged thousands of Cuban aliens to enter the United States using fraudulent Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) applications and visa waivers. The defendants advertised their smuggling services and bogus visa services across multiple social media platforms to solicit Cuban nationals and other aliens to attempt to illegally enter the U.S. and fraudulently obtain lawful status. Lima-Santos furthered the conspiracy by helping move aliens from their countries of origin, including Cuba, into the U.S. This included purchasing international and domestic flights to transport aliens into and throughout the United States.
何塞·格雷戈里奥·索萨·卡多纳，芝加哥郊区一名商人，于2026年5月因移民欺诈、持有儿童色情制品和逃税罪被判处九年监禁。索萨·卡多纳经营Delta Global Solutions, Inc.公司，该公司协助申请庇护、移民签证、永久居民卡及其他移民福利的个人。从2020年至2024年，索萨·卡多纳与其公司的两名雇员及其他人合谋，故意向美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)提供虚假和欺诈性信息，为其寻求福利的外国籍客户提供服务。索萨·卡多纳被指控伪造外国执法报告和外国政党党员证明信，以虚假证实其客户的庇护申请。
Jose Gregorio Sosa Cardona, a suburban Chicago businessman, was sentenced in May 2026 to nine years in prison for immigration fraud, possessing child pornography, and committing tax fraud. Sosa Cardona operated Delta Global Solutions, Inc., which assisted individuals applying for asylum, immigrant visas, lawful permanent residence cards, and other immigration benefits. From 2020 to 2024, Sosa Cardona conspired with two employees of his firm and others to knowingly provide false and fraudulent information to USCIS for his foreign national clients who were seeking benefits. Sosa Cardona was accused of fabricating foreign law enforcement reports and foreign political party membership letters to falsely corroborate his clients’ asylum claims.
美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)的筛查和审查工作也有助于识别可能对公共安全或国家安全构成威胁的个人。
USCIS screening and vetting also helps identify individuals who may pose public-safety or national-security concerns.
例如，埃德温·安东尼奥·埃尔南德斯，27岁，来自萨尔瓦多，于2026年1月在美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)预约期间被美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕。通过对其庇护和工作许可申请的筛查和审查，美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)发现了有关其犯罪记录和移民身份的信息。埃尔南德斯此前曾非法进入美国，并于2017年被移民法官下令驱逐出境。在羁押期间，埃尔南德斯承认自己是MS-13帮派成员，并供认在萨尔瓦多通过枪击、折磨、刺杀和肢解等手段杀害了五人。
For example, Edwin Antonio Hernandez, 27, of El Salvador, was arrested in January 2026 by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement during a USCIS appointment. Through the screening and vetting of his asylum and employment authorization applications, USCIS identified information concerning his criminal history and immigration status. Hernandez had previously entered the United States unlawfully and was ordered removed by an immigration judge in 2017. While in custody, Hernandez admitted to being an MS-13 member and confessed to murdering five people in El Salvador through shooting, torturing, stabbing, and dismemberment.
