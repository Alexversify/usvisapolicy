佛罗里达13名巴西和古巴籍人士因签证和婚姻欺诈被联邦起诉

发布日期 2026年11月2日

美国奥兰多联邦检察官办公室宣布,13名来自巴西和古巴的外国人因电汇欺诈、签证欺诈、婚姻欺诈及非法雇用外国人等罪名被大陪审团起诉。USCIS协助了此次调查,调查发现一家名为Legacy Imigra Solutions LLC的公司冒充由移民专家和律师经营。

要点

  • 被控行为发生在2023年8月至2026年4月期间,涉及Legacy Imigra Solutions LLC公司及其员工。
  • 被告面临共谋电汇欺诈、电汇欺诈、共谋签证欺诈、共谋婚姻欺诈以及非法雇用外国人等多项指控。
  • 检方称Legacy公司谎称聘有移民专家和律师,并伪造文件以欺诈方式为客户获取移民身份和福利。
  • 部分被告还被指控在明知某些人无合法工作许可的情况下仍雇用其工作。
  • 电汇欺诈最高可判20年监禁,共谋签证欺诈最高10年,共谋婚姻欺诈最高5年,非法雇用最高6个月监禁。
  • 起诉仅为正式指控,所有被告在被证明有罪之前均被推定无罪。

影响对象

此案涉及被起诉的个人以及曾向该公司寻求移民协助的客户。

建议行动

任何人若怀疑存在移民福利欺诈行为,可通过USCIS举报表格向USCIS举报。

佛罗里达州奥兰多讯。十三名来自巴西和古巴的外国人因在一起共谋欺骗美国及其他外国人的案件中的角色而被大陪审团起诉。指控罪名包括共谋实施电信欺诈、电信欺诈、签证欺诈、非法雇用外国人以及婚姻欺诈。美国联邦检察官格雷戈里·W·基霍(Gregory W. Kehoe)宣布了这一消息。美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)为此次调查提供了宝贵的协助。
ORLANDO, Fla. — Thirteen aliens from Brazil and Cuba have been charged by indictment for their roles in a conspiracy to defraud the United States and other aliens. The charges include conspiracy to commit wire fraud, wire fraud, visa fraud, unlawful employment of aliens, and marriage fraud. United States Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement. U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services provided valuable support to the investigation.
瓦格纳·索雷斯·德·阿尔梅达(Vagner Sorares De Almeida),54岁;朱莉安娜·科卢奇(Juliana Colucci),47岁;卢卡斯·费利佩·特林达德·席尔瓦(Lucas Felipe Trindade Silva),34岁;以及罗纳尔多·德·坎波斯(Ronaldo De Campos),35岁,每人均被指控一项共谋实施电信欺诈罪和两项电信欺诈罪。他们还被指控四项共谋实施签证欺诈罪、一项共谋实施婚姻欺诈罪以及一项非法雇用外国人罪。
Vagner Sorares De Almeida, 54; Juliana Colucci, 47; Lucas Felipe Trindade Silva, 34; and Ronaldo De Campos, 35, are each charged with one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and two counts of wire fraud. They are also charged with four counts of conspiracy to commit visa fraud, one count of conspiracy to commit marriage fraud, and one count of unlawful employment of aliens.
埃里克·布鲁诺·贡萨尔维斯·德·阿西斯·平托(Erick Bruno Goncalves De Assis Pinto),34岁;纳塔利娅·达·科斯塔·费雷拉(Nathalia Da Costa Ferreira),43岁;纳塔利娅·戈梅斯·西里亚科(Nathalia Gomes Ciriaco),29岁;以及凯伦·克里斯蒂娜·达·席尔瓦(Kelen Cristina Da Silva),37岁,每人均被指控一项共谋实施电信欺诈罪、两项电信欺诈罪以及四项共谋实施签证欺诈罪。
Erick Bruno Goncalves De Assis Pinto, 34; Nathalia Da Costa Ferreira, 43; Nathalia Gomes Ciriaco, 29; and Kelen Cristina Da Silva, 37, are each charged with charged with one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, two counts of wire fraud, and four counts of conspiracy to commit visa fraud.
伊萨克·安东尼奥·纳瓦埃斯(Issac Antonio Narvaez),69岁,被指控三项共谋实施婚姻欺诈罪。
Issac Antonio Narvaez, 69, is charged with three counts of conspiracy to commit marriage fraud.
罗奈·卡斯蒂略·萨拉萨尔(Ronay Castillo Salazar),51岁;丽贝卡·阿尔达马·加西亚(Rebeca Aldama Garcia),40岁;米哈伊尔·卡贝拉·努涅斯(Mijail Cabera Nunez),52岁;以及洛兰·索雷斯·奥利维拉(Lorraine Soares Oliverira),32岁,每人均被指控一项共谋实施婚姻欺诈罪。
Ronay Castillo Salazar, 51; Rebeca Aldama Garcia, 40; Mijail Cabera Nunez, 52; and Lorraine Soares Oliverira, 32, are each charged with one count of conspiracy to commit marriage fraud.
根据法院记录,自2023年8月18日至2026年4月20日期间,瓦格纳·索雷斯·德·阿尔梅达拥有并经营一家在佛罗里达州注册的名为“Legacy Imigra Solutions LLC”(“Legacy”)的公司实体。朱莉安娜·科卢奇、卢卡斯·费利佩·特林达德·席尔瓦、纳塔利娅·达·科斯塔·费雷拉、纳塔利娅·戈梅斯·西里亚科、罗纳尔多·德·坎波斯、埃里克·布鲁诺·贡萨尔维斯·德·阿西斯·平托以及凯伦·克里斯蒂娜·达·席尔瓦均为该公司的员工。Legacy虚假宣称自己是一家聘用移民专家和律师的专业机构,能够协助个人处理移民相关法律事务。该共谋的一部分内容是向潜在客户提供虚假、不准确和不完整的法律及移民建议,以诱使其聘用Legacy的服务。Legacy会伪造移民文件,以欺诈手段为其客户获取移民身份和福利。
According to court records, from Aug. 18, 2023, through April 20, 2026, Vagner Sorares De Almeida owned and operated a corporate entity registered in Florida called Legacy Imigra Solutions LLC (“Legacy”). Juliana Colucci, Lucas Felipe Trindade Silva, Nathalia Da Costa Ferreira, Nathalia Gomes Ciriaco, Ronaldo De Campos, Erick Bruno Goncalves De Assis Pinto, and Kelen Cristina Da Silva were employees of the company. Legacy falsely purported to be a professional establishment employed by immigration experts and attorneys who could assist individuals with immigration-related legal work. It was part of the conspiracy to give false, inaccurate, and incomplete legal and immigration advice to prospective clients to induce them to retain Legacy’s services. Legacy would falsify immigration documents to fraudulently obtain immigration status and benefits for their clients.
被告人还经常性地雇用他们明知无权在美国就业的外国人。瓦格纳·索雷斯·德·阿尔梅达、朱莉安娜·科卢奇、罗奈·卡斯蒂略·萨拉萨尔、丽贝卡·阿尔达马·加西亚、米哈伊尔·卡贝拉·努涅斯、洛兰·索雷斯·奥利维拉以及伊萨克·安东尼奥·纳瓦埃斯还共谋实施婚姻欺诈。
The defendants also engaged in a regular practice of hiring aliens knowing that the individuals were unauthorized for employment in the United States. Vagner Sorares De Almeida, Juliana Colucci, Ronay Castillo Salazar, Rebeca Aldama Garcia, Mijail Cabera Nunez, Lorraine Soares Oliverira, and Issac Antonio Narvaez also conspired to commit marriage fraud.
如罪名成立,共谋实施电信欺诈罪和电信欺诈罪的每项罪名最高可判处20年监禁。共谋实施签证欺诈罪的每项罪名最高可判处10年监禁。共谋实施婚姻欺诈罪的最高刑罚为联邦监禁5年,非法雇用外国人罪的最高刑罚为监禁6个月。
If convicted, each count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and wire fraud carries a maximum penalty of 20 years’ imprisonment. The conspiracy to commit visa fraud offenses are each punishable up to 10 years’ imprisonment. The maximum penalty for conspiracy to commit marriage fraud is 5 years in federal prison, and unlawful employment of aliens carries a maximum penalty of 6 months’ imprisonment.
起诉书仅为正式指控,指称被告人已触犯一项或多项联邦刑事法律,在依法被证明有罪之前,所有被告人均被推定为无罪。
An indictment is merely a formal charge that a defendant has committed one or more violations of federal criminal law, and every defendant is presumed innocent unless, and until, proven guilty.
本案由美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)国土安全调查处(奥兰多、坦帕和巴西利亚办事处)及执法与遣返行动处、美国国税局刑事调查处、美国劳工部监察长办公室、美国农业部监察长办公室、美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)、美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)、美国联邦法警署、奥兰治县警长办公室、佛罗里达州公路巡逻队以及佛罗里达州执法部共同调查。本案由助理联邦检察官比安卡·班萨尔(Bianca Bansal)负责起诉。
This case is being investigated by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement’s Homeland Security Investigations (Orlando, Tampa, and Brasilia) and Enforcement and Removal Operations, IRS Criminal Investigation, the Department of Labor Office of Inspector General, the Department of Agriculture Office of Inspector General, USCIS, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, the U.S. Marshals Service, the Orange County Sheriff’s Office, Florida Highway Patrol, and the Florida Department of Law Enforcement. It is being prosecuted by Assistant United States Attorney Bianca Bansal.
