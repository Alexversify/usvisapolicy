费用移民海关执法局 (ICE)#执法·遣返·欺诈#费用

DHS拟将缺席遣返令相关费用提高至18,000美元

发布日期 2026年5月20日

美国国土安全部（DHS）与移民及海关执法局（ICE）提议上调对某些被缺席判决遣返、未离境后又被ICE逮捕的外国人所收取的费用。该费用拟从5,130美元提高至18,000美元，此后每年根据通胀进行调整。这仅是一项拟议规则，尚未生效。

要点

  • 该费用适用于依据移民与国籍法第240(b)(5)(A)条被缺席判决遣返、随后被ICE逮捕的外国人。
  • DHS提议将每人收取的费用从5,130美元提高到18,000美元。
  • 该费用依据8 U.S.C. 1814条设立，源自被称为HR-1的预算协调法案。
  • 新规则还明确DHS此后将每年根据消费者价格指数调整该费用。
  • 该费用由受影响的外国人本人支付，而非雇主或担保人。
  • 公众意见须在2026年6月22日前通过联邦电子规则制定门户网站提交。

影响对象

受影响人群是被缺席判决遣返、未离境并随后被ICE逮捕的外国人。

建议行动

相关方可在2026年6月22日前通过regulations.gov网站，使用案卷编号ICEB-2026-0034提交对该拟议规则的意见。

二、背景与目的
II. Background and Purpose
A. 法律授权
A. Legal Authority
国土安全部部长(以下简称“部长”)实施本规则所提议的监管修正案的权力,见于移民法的多项规定之中。部长制定法规的权力主要源自经修订的《移民与国籍法》(INA)。见8 U.S.C. 1101及其后条款。2002年《国土安全法》(公法107-296号,116 Stat. 2135)第102条,即6 U.S.C. 112条,以及INA第103(a)(1)和(3)条,即8 U.S.C. 1103(a)(1)、(3)条,赋予部长负责执行美国移民与归化法律的职责。INA第103(a)(3)条,即8 U.S.C. 1103(a)(3)条,授予部长采取“为执行”其根据INA各项规定所享有权力“所必需”行动的权力。国土安全部(DHS)在采用其合理认定为适当的程序以执行移民法方面,也拥有广泛的自由裁量权,包括评估和征收经授权的费用。[1]参见Vt. Yankee Nuclear Power Corp. 诉 Nat. Res. Def. Council, Inc.案,435 U.S. 519, 543 (1978)(“在不存在宪法限制或极其迫切情形的情况下,行政机构应可自由制定其自身的程序规则,并采用能够使其履行繁多职责的调查方法。”(引号略)(引自FCC 诉 Schreiber案,381 U.S. 279, 290 (1965))。[2]
The authority of the Secretary of Homeland Security (the Secretary) to implement the proposed regulatory amendments in this rule can be found in various provisions of the immigration laws. The Secretary derives the authority to promulgate regulations primarily from the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), as amended. See8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq. Section 102 of the Homeland Security Act of 2002, Public Law 107-296, 116 Stat. 2135, 6 U.S.C. 112, and section 103(a)(1) and (3) of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1103(a)(1), (3), charge the Secretary with the administration and enforcement of the immigration and naturalization laws of the United States. Section 103(a)(3) of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1103(a)(3), grants the Secretary the power to take actions “necessary for carrying out” the Secretary's authority under the provisions of the INA. DHS also has broad discretion to employ the procedures it reasonably concludes are appropriate to enforce immigration laws, including the assessment and collection of authorized fees.[1] See Vt. Yankee Nuclear Power Corp. v. Nat. Res. Def. Council, Inc., 435 U.S. 519, 543 (1978) (“Absent constitutional constraints or extremely compelling circumstances the administrative agencies should be free to fashion their own rules of procedure and to pursue ( printed page 29381) methods of inquiry capable of permitting them to discharge their multitudinous duties.” (quotation marks omitted) (quoting FCC v. Schreiber, 381 U.S. 279, 290 (1965)).[2]
8 U.S.C. 1814条要求对被缺席下令驱逐出境的外国人收取费用,除非该外国人的驱逐令依照第240(b)(5)(C)条(8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C))被撤销。[3]该项不可豁免的费用适用于根据第240(b)(5)(A)条(8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(A))被缺席下令驱逐出境,且其后被美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)逮捕的任何外国人。该费用作为对政府逮捕成本的部分补偿。[4]《HR-1法案》要求该新费用在2026财政年度(FY)每名外国人最低为5,130美元。然而,8 U.S.C. 1814条授权部长通过制定规则调整该费用,并要求根据“所有城市消费者消费价格指数”(CPI-U)每年对该费用进行调整。[5]
8 U.S.C. 1814 requires a fee for aliens ordered removed in absentia, unless the alien's order is rescinded pursuant to section 240(b)(5)(C) (8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C)).[3] This unwaivable fee applies to any alien who is ordered removed in absentia pursuant to section 240(b)(5)(A) (8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(A)) and is subsequently arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). The fee serves as partial reimbursement to the government for the cost of the arrests.[4] HR-1 requires this new fee to be a minimum of $5,130 per alien for Fiscal Year (FY) 2026. However, 8 U.S.C. 1814 authorizes the Secretary to adjust the fee through rulemaking and requires the fee to be adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U).[5]
1. ICE移民执法权力
1. ICE Immigration Enforcement Authority
国土安全部的移民官员拥有广泛的权力,可依据国土安全部签发的逮捕令或在无逮捕令的情况下,对尚未确立合法居留权利、等待移民程序审理或等待被驱逐出美国的外国人进行逮捕和拘留。[6]逮捕和拘留均会给美国纳税人带来巨大开支。当可被驱逐出境的外国人,包括那些已被缺席下达最终驱逐令的外国人未能离境时,这些开支将成倍增加,因为这将迫使ICE采取进一步的执法行动。
DHS immigration officers have broad authority to arrest and detain aliens pending immigration proceedings and for removal from the United States, who have not established a legal right to remain, pursuant to a warrant issued by the Department of Homeland Security, or without a warrant.[6] Both arrest and detention come at great expense to the U.S. taxpayer. Those expenses exponentially increase when removable aliens, including those with final orders of removal issued in absentia, fail to depart, which results in ICE being forced to take further enforcement action.
依照INA第287(a)(1)至(2)条,即8 U.S.C. 1357(a)(1)至(2)条,国土安全部的移民官员有权对有合理依据认为可被驱逐出美国的外国人进行讯问和逮捕。[7]未持逮捕令进行的逮捕,必须伴有对该外国人在能够取得逮捕令之前可能潜逃的认定。[8]某些被逮捕并被置于驱逐程序中的外国人,在就是否应将其驱逐出美国作出决定之前,可被拘留。[9]根据INA第240条,即8 U.S.C. 1229a条,对于在驱逐程序待决期间的所有入境申请人,拘留是强制性的。[10]执法活动的监管标准规定于8 CFR 287.8条中,其中包括实施逮捕的权力和程序。[11]
Pursuant to section 287(a)(1)-(2) of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1357(a)(1)-(2), DHS immigration officers have the authority to interrogate and to arrest aliens for whom there is probable cause to believe are removable from the United States.[7] Arrests made without a warrant must be accompanied by a determination that the alien would be likely to escape before a warrant could be obtained.[8] Certain aliens arrested and placed in removal proceedings may be detained pending a decision on whether they should be removed from the United States.[9] Detention is mandatory for all applicants for admission pending removal proceedings under section 240 of the INA, 8 U.S.C. 1229a.[10] The regulatory standards for enforcement activities are set forth in 8 CFR 287.8, and they include the authority and procedures for the conduct of arrests.[11]
除上述权力外,《移民与国籍法》(INA)第241条,即8 U.S.C. 1231节,还授权国土安全部(DHS)拘留外国人以执行驱逐令,包括缺席作出的驱逐令。[12] 当移民法官在外国人缺席的情况下下令将其驱逐时,该驱逐令自作出之日起即为终局性命令,要求该外国人立即离开美国。[13] 如果该外国人不遵守终局驱逐令,[14] 移民及海关执法局(ICE)通常将依据驱逐令逮捕状将其逮捕羁押,并将其驱逐出美国。[15]
In addition to these authorities, INA 241, 8 U.S.C. 1231, provides DHS with the authority to detain aliens to effectuate a removal order, including a removal order entered in absentia.[12] When an immigration judge orders an alien removed in the alien's absence, the order of removal becomes final upon entry of the order, requiring the alien to immediately depart the United States.[13] If the alien fails to comply with a final order of removal,[14] the alien will be arrested and taken into custody by ICE, generally pursuant to a warrant of removal, and will be removed from the United States.[15]
二、缺席驱逐令及随后由ICE实施的逮捕
2. In Absentia Removal Orders and Subsequent Arrest by ICE
根据INA第240条,即8 U.S.C. 1229a节,当国土安全部向外国人送达出庭通知书(NTA)时,该外国人即获得有关驱逐程序及听证日期的通知。[16] 该出庭通知书告知外国人有关程序的性质、对其提出的指控、程序举行的时间和地点,以及要求该外国人向国土安全部提供地址和电话号码(如有)并在其后如有变更应及时更新的要求。[17] 值得注意的是,该出庭通知书还说明了未能提供更新的地址和电话号码的后果,[18] 以及未能出席程序的后果。[19] 一旦出庭通知书提交至移民法院,移民法院即发出有关未来听证日期和/或日期或地点变更的通知(听证通知)。[20]
Under INA sec. 240, 8 U.S.C. 1229a, an alien receives notice of the removal proceedings and hearing date when DHS serves the alien with a Notice to Appear (NTA).[16] The NTA advises the alien of the nature of the proceedings, the charges against the alien, the time and place at which the proceedings will be held, and the requirements that the alien provide DHS with an address and telephone number (if any) and any changes of address or telephone number thereafter.[17] Notably, the NTA also states the consequences for failing to provide an updated address and telephone number,[18] and for failing to appear at the proceedings.[19] Once the NTA is filed with the Immigration Court, the Immigration Court issues notices of future hearings (hearing notice) dates and/or changes in dates or location.[20]
出庭通知书和听证通知须亲自送达该外国人,如亲自送达不可行,则通过邮寄方式送达该外国人或其有案可查的代理律师。[21] 当外国人未出庭,且国土安全部以“清楚、明确和令人信服的证据”证明该外国人可被驱逐,并证明该外国人或其有案可查的代理律师已获得出庭通知书或听证通知(即关于程序时间和地点以及未出庭后果的书面通知)时,移民法官应下令将该外国人缺席驱逐。[22] 如果外国人未能按照INA第239(a)(1)(F)条的要求提供地址和电话号码(如有),或未能按照INA第239(a)(1)(F)条的要求更新该信息,则不要求书面通知,国土安全部也无义务提供该程序的进一步通知。在收到出庭通知书后发出的任何通知,只要是发送至该外国人提供的最近地址,即为充分通知。如果该外国人未能按照INA第239(a)(1)(F)条提供地址,则“不要求书面通知”。[23]
The NTA and hearing notices are served in person to the alien or, if personal service is not practicable, through service by mail to the alien or the alien's counsel of record.[21] When an alien fails to appear in court and DHS establishes by “clear, unequivocal, and convincing evidence” that the alien is removable and that the alien or the alien's counsel of record was provided the NTA or hearing notice ( i.e., the written notice of the time and place of the proceedings and the consequences of failing to appear), an immigration judge shall order the alien removed in absentia.[22] Where the alien has failed to provide the address and telephone number (if any) as required under INA 239(a)(1)(F), or has failed to update such information in accordance with INA 239(a)(1)(F), written notice is not required, and DHS is not obligated to provide further notice of the proceeding. Any notice issued after the NTA has been received is sufficient if it is given at the most recent address provided by the alien. If the alien fails to provide an address pursuant to INA 239(a)(1)(F), “no written notice shall be required.” [23]

HR-1法案规定,在依据INA第240(b)(5)(C)条,即8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C)节撤销缺席驱逐令的某些情形下,可免除第1814节,即8 U.S.C. 1814节所授权征收的移民执法费。[24] 如果外国人提出重新开庭动议,移民法官可撤销缺席驱逐令,情形如下：(1) 在驱逐令作出后180天内提出动议,且该外国人证明其未出庭是由于“特殊情况”所致；或(2) 在任何时间提出动议,只要该外国人能够证明其未收到所需通知,或该外国人当时处于联邦或州的羁押之中且未出庭并非其过错所致。[25] 《移民与国籍法》给出了“特殊情况”的示例,例如“对该外国人或其子女或父母实施殴打或极端残忍行为、该外国人本人患有严重疾病,或其配偶、子女或父母患有严重疾病或死亡”,但不包括“该外国人可控制范围内”的不那么紧迫的情形。[26] 外国人必须提供充分证据以支持其有关特殊情况的主张,并佐证其未出庭的原因。[27] 在基于缺乏通知而提出的重新开庭以撤销缺席驱逐令的动议待决期间,该外国人的驱逐将自动中止,直至移民法官对该动议作出裁决为止。[28]

B. 背景
B. Background
自2025年9月8日起生效,美国移民及海关执法局(ICE)开始对所有依据《美国法典》第8编第1814条应予收费的外国人征收2025财政年度5,000美元的费用。见90 FR 43223(2025年9月8日)。该费用的征收触发事件为外国人随后被ICE逮捕之日,而不论缺席最终驱逐令的签发日期为何。同上。2025年11月20日,ICE根据通货膨胀对该费用进行调整,自2026财政年度起由5,000美元调整为5,130美元,自2025年12月1日起生效。见90 FR 52425(2025年11月20日)。依据法律规定,所征收款项中50%存入ICE的拘留与遣返办公室费用账户,由ICE支用,其余部分存入美国财政部普通基金。[29]
Effective September 8, 2025, ICE began issuing the FY 2025 fee of $5,000 from any alien who falls under 8 U.S.C. 1814. See90 FR 43223 (Sept. 8, 2025). The triggering event for issuance of the fee is the date the alien was subsequently arrested by ICE, regardless of the date the final removal order in absentia was issued. Id. On November 20, 2025, ICE adjusted the fee for inflation from $5,000 to $5,130 for FY 2026, effective December 1, 2025. 90 FR 52425 (Nov. 20, 2025). As mandated by statute, 50 percent of the funds collected from the fee are deposited into ICE's Detention and Removal Office Fee Account to be expended by ICE, and the remaining amounts are deposited into the General Fund of the U.S. Treasury.[29]
依据《美国法典》第8编第1814条须缴纳该费用的外国人将收到书面费用通知。该费用评估通知向外国人提供逮捕官员所作各项认定的清单,包括该外国人被缺席判决驱逐出境的日期、该外国人随后被逮捕的事实,以及费用金额。该书面费用评估通知明确说明,若未及时缴纳该费用,将依据《美国法典》第31编第3717条、《联邦法规汇编》第6编第11.10条及第31编第901.9条产生利息累积、行政成本及逾期缴款罚金等后果。外国人可在通知发出之日起30日内对该书面费用通知提出异议。[30]
An alien subject to the fee under 8 U.S.C. 1814 receives written notice of the fee. This notice of the fee assessment provides the alien with a list of determinations made by the arresting officer, including the date the alien was ordered removed in absentia, the fact that the alien was subsequently arrested, and the fee amount. The written notice of the fee assessment makes clear that failure to promptly pay the fee results in consequences such as the accrual of interest, administrative costs, and a late payment penalty charge pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3717, 6 CFR 11.10, and 31 CFR 901.9. An alien may dispute the written fee notice within 30 days of issuance of the notice.[30]
C. 制定本规则的必要性
C. Need for the Rulemaking
1. 移民执法行动的成本及对政府的部分补偿
1. Costs for Immigration Enforcement Actions and Partial Reimbursement to the Government
ICE审查了有关ICE移民执法行动成本的数据,确定5,130美元的部分补偿费用过低,不足以充分补偿ICE为逮捕已被缺席判决驱逐出境的外国人所产生的成本。
ICE has reviewed the data on the cost of ICE immigration enforcement actions and determined that the partial reimbursement fee of $5,130 is too low to sufficiently reimburse ICE for the cost of arresting an alien who has been ordered removed in absentia.
逮捕一名被缺席判决驱逐出境的外国人的总成本,除须考虑在缺席驱逐令作出后实施逮捕的直接成本外,还须考虑识别、拘留、处理及遣返该外国人的更广泛成本。ICE还产生了各种间接成本及管理费用,包括但不限于培训、车辆及支持人员等费用。这些成本均包含于逮捕外国人的成本之中。正如逮捕外国人这一行为直接产生拘留、处理及遣返该外国人的成本一样。
The total cost of arresting an alien ordered removed in absentia requires consideration of the broader costs of identifying, detaining, processing, and removing such alien, in addition to the direct cost of the arrest subsequent to the in absentia removal order. ICE also incurs a variety of indirect and overhead costs, including, but not limited to, training, vehicles, and support staff. These costs are subsumed in the cost of arresting an alien. Just as the act of arresting an alien directly imposes costs to detain, process, and remove that alien.
由ICE预算与项目绩效办公室(OBPP)绩效分析与评估(PA&E)部门编制的ICE移民执法生命周期(IEL)成本框架,提供了移民执法生命周期各阶段成本的信息,包括直接成本、间接成本及管理费用。平均而言,一名外国人的移民执法生命周期总成本为18,042美元,此数据证明了国土安全部部长行使职权上调《美国法典》第8编第1814条所要求的5,130美元费用金额的正当性。
ICE's immigration enforcement lifecycle (IEL) cost framework, produced by ICE's Office of Budget and Program Performance (OBPP) Performance Analysis and Evaluation (PA&E) Unit, provides information on the cost of each stage of the IEL, including direct costs, indirect costs, and overhead costs. On average, the total cost of the IEL for an alien is $18,042, justifying the DHS Secretary's exercise of authority to increase the $5,130 fee amount required by 8 U.S.C. 1814.

此外,近年来被缺席判决驱逐出境的外国人数量急剧增加。具体而言,行政移民上诉事务执行办公室(EOIR)的数据显示,2022年至2024年间,缺席驱逐令的签发数量增长了257%,即由2022年的62,510份增至2024年的222,920份。[35] 2025年的数据显示,2024年至2025年间又增长了39%,缺席驱逐令签发总数达到309,700份。[36]
Moreover, during the last several years the number of aliens ordered removed in absentia has drastically increased. Specifically, data from the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR) indicates that between 2022 and 2024 there was a 257 percent increase in the number of in absentia removal orders issued—62,510 in 2022 and 222,920 in 2024.[35] Data for 2025 show an additional increase of 39 percent occurred between 2024 and 2025 for a total of 309,700 in absentia removal orders issued.[36]
本NPRM(拟议规则制定通知)中提议的18,000美元费用,是根据《美国法典》第8编第1814条(a)款对ICE(美国移民及海关执法局)总成本的部分补偿。ICE关于IEL(缺席遣返名单)的数据显示,2025年ICE识别、逮捕、拘留、处理并遣返一名外国人所产生的平均成本为18,040美元。被缺席裁定遣返的外国人数量大幅增加,加之IEL的整体成本,持续给ICE本已捉襟见肘的执法资源造成重大压力,并使这些重要资源从其他移民执法优先事项中被挤占。[37]为缓解资源压力并减少美国纳税人为这些执法活动所支付的金额,国会在《美国法典》第8编第1814条中设立了该费用,以部分补偿ICE逮捕被缺席裁定遣返的外国人所产生的成本,[38]而此过程必然涉及在逮捕前识别此类潜逃外国人。对于已获得最终遣返令(包括缺席遣返令)的外国人而言,逮捕是执行遣返令的第一步,拘留、处理和遣返则是在逮捕之后,依据该机构遣返已获最终遣返令外国人的职权和使命而依次展开的。值得注意的是,拟议的费用评估不包括国土安全部(DHS)在外国人被缺席裁定遣返之前所产生的移民执法成本。此外,《美国法典》第8编第1814条授权对“根据本编第1229a(b)(5)条被缺席裁定遣返,且其后被美国移民及海关执法局逮捕的外国人……逮捕成本进行部分补偿”。国土安全部将此解释为指在缺席最终遣返令作出之后,ICE所产生的逮捕成本。
The $18,000 fee proposed in this NPRM constitutes a partial reimbursement of ICE's total costs in accordance with 8 U.S.C. 1814(a). ICE data on the IEL indicates that the average cost accrued by ICE to identify, arrest, detain, process, and remove a single alien in 2025 is $18,040. The meaningful increase in the number of aliens ordered removed in absentia combined with the overall cost of the IEL continues to put a significant strain on ICE's already stretched enforcement resources and diverts those essential resources from other immigration enforcement priorities. To alleviate the strain on resources and reduce the amount paid by U.S. taxpayers for these enforcement activities,[37] Congress created the fee at 8 U.S.C. 1814 to partially reimburse ICE for the cost of arresting aliens ordered removed in absentia,[38] which necessarily involves identifying such alien fugitives prior to arrest. For aliens with a final removal order—including an in absentia removal order—the arrest is a first step in effectuating the removal order, and detention, processing, and removal flow from the arrest consistent with the agency's authorities and mission to repatriate aliens with final orders of removal. Notably, the proposed fee assessment does not include immigration enforcement costs incurred by DHS prior to an alien being ordered removed in absentia. Furthermore, 8 U.S.C. 1814, authorizes a “partial reimbursement for the cost of arresting an alien . . . who is ordered removed in absentia pursuant to section 1229a(b)(5) of this title; and is subsequently arrested by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.” DHS interprets this to refer to the costs of the arrest incurred by ICE subsequent to the in absentia final order of removal.
此外,由于《美国法典》第8编第1814条规定该费用的50%应存入美国财政部普通基金,因此18,000美元的费用可确保对ICE进行实际且有意义的部分补偿。国土安全部认为,有必要通过规则制定,确保国土安全部收取更为适当的部分补偿费用,以如实反映在那些被缺席裁定遣返却未能离境、其后被ICE逮捕的外国人身上所耗费的巨额资源。[39]
Additionally, because 8 U.S.C. 1814 provides that 50 percent of the fee shall be deposited into the General Fund of the U.S. Treasury, a $18,000 fee ensures actual and meaningful partial reimbursement to ICE. DHS believes rulemaking is needed to ensure that DHS collects a more appropriate partial reimbursement fee that properly reflects the enormous amount of resources expended on aliens ordered removed in absentia who fail to depart and are subsequently arrested by ICE.[39]
二、维护移民执法权威的完整性并促进公共安全
2. Protecting the Integrity of Immigration Enforcement Authority and Public Safety
本NPRM中提议的变更也有助于维护移民执法的完整性并促进公共安全。外国人在被缺席裁定作出最终遣返令后未能离开美国,其行为已经违反了美国的移民法律和程序。[40]一旦外国人未能遵守缺席最终遣返令,ICE就必须耗费额外的时间和资源逮捕该外国人以执行遣返令。[41]逮捕被缺席裁定遣返的外国人,涉及对其进行识别、定位并将其遣送出美国。此类外国人群体已经逃避执法,并通过非法滞留美国而违反移民法院命令。规避最终遣返令的外国人破坏了移民制度的有序运行和法治,造成其行踪的不确定性,并增加了执法接触发生在不可控环境中的可能性,从而加大了对国土安全部人员和公众的风险。因此,国土安全部将这些外国人视为潜逃者,并将其列为遣返美国的高度优先对象。
The changes proposed in this NPRM also protect the integrity of immigration enforcement and help promote public safety. An alien who fails to depart the United States upon issuance of a final order of removal in absentia has already violated U.S. immigration laws and processes.[40] Once an alien fails to comply with the final order of removal in absentia, ICE must expend additional time and resources to arrest the alien to effectuate the removal order.[41] Arrest of aliens ordered removed in absentia involves identifying, locating, and removing them from the United States. This population of aliens have absconded from law enforcement and are violating an immigration court order by remaining unlawfully in the United States. Aliens who evade final removal orders undermine the orderly administration of the immigration system and rule of law, create uncertainty regarding their whereabouts, and increase the likelihood of enforcement encounters occurring in uncontrollable settings, which then heightens risks to DHS personnel and the public. Accordingly, DHS considers these aliens fugitives and a high priority for removal from the United States.
此外,缺席遣返令只有在外国人向移民法官提出重新审理动议后方可撤销,参见Matter of Guzman案,22 I&N Dec. 722(移民上诉委员会,1999年)。当移民法官(印刷版第29384页)作出缺席遣返令后,该命令即成为最终且可执行的命令,该外国人被认定为依指控应予遣返,丧失在该程序中对可遣返性提出异议或寻求救济的机会,并须承担因最终命令而产生的一切法规和法定后果。此外,该外国人将丧失获得某些酌情救济形式的资格,包括自缺席命令作出之日起十(10)年内,无资格获得遣返取消、身份调整、身份变更、登记[42]或自愿离境[43]。如果外国人无正当理由未出席或拒绝出席遣返程序,则该外国人在其离境或被遣返后五年内不得入境。[44]
Furthermore, an in absentia removal order can only be rescinded if the alien files a motion to reopen with the immigration judge, see Matter of Guzman, 22 I&N Dec. 722 (BIA 1999).When an Immigration Judge ( printed page 29384) enters an in absentia order of removal, the order becomes final and enforceable, and the alien is determined to be removable as charged, forfeits the opportunity to contest removability or seek relief in those proceedings, and is subject to all regulatory and statutory consequences flowing from a final order. In addition, the alien becomes ineligible for certain forms of discretionary relief, including ineligibility for a period of ten (10) years from the date of the in absentia order to receive cancellation of removal, adjustment of status, change of status, registry,[42] or voluntary departure.[43] If an alien fails to or refuses to attend a removal proceeding without reasonable cause, the alien is inadmissible for five years after their departure or removal.[44]
外国人未能离境会产生额外的执法成本,而这些成本本可用于美国国土安全部(DHS)和本届政府广泛的移民执法优先事项。[45]相反,国土安全部必须耗费有限的资源来定位、逮捕和将这些外国人驱逐出美国。这些成本是在已经为起诉和裁决外国人的驱逐程序至最终完成而支出的成本之外额外产生的。移民及海关执法局(ICE)资源的低效使用有可能损害移民体系的整体完整性,并有可能进一步诱使外国人干脆无视驱逐令。
An alien's failure to depart creates additional enforcement costs that could otherwise be spent on DHS' and the administration's vast immigration enforcement priorities.[45] Instead, DHS must spend finite resources locating, arresting, and removing these aliens from the United States. These costs are in addition to the costs already expended to prosecute and adjudicate an alien's removal proceedings to completion in the first instance. Inefficient use of ICE resources risks undermining the overall integrity of the immigration system, and risks further incentivizing aliens to simply ignore removal orders.
三、有效实施
3. Effective Implementation
将8 U.S.C. 1814规定的费用纳入法规,可为公众提供明确性,并便于查阅法律。此外,法典化有助于更有效地实施法律,并抑制外国人违反移民法的行为。
Codifying the fee at 8 U.S.C. 1814 into regulation provides clarity to the public and easy access to the law. Additionally, codification assists with a more effective implementation of the law and disincentivizes aliens from engaging in violations of immigration law.
三、关于修改内容的讨论
III. Discussion of Changes
国会在8 U.S.C. 1814中规定了最低费用,并授权部长可自行决定提高该费用。[46]国土安全部提议将8 U.S.C. 1814的规定纳入法规,并行使其自由裁量权,将费用从5,130美元提高到18,000美元,原因是该机构在逮捕被缺席判决驱逐令的外国人方面产生了巨额成本。通过寻求部分补偿与逮捕被缺席判决驱逐令的外国人相关的执法活动费用,拟议的修改是国土安全部运用一切法定可用工具以实现本届政府移民执法和边境安全目标的关键举措之一。
Congress established a minimum fee at 8 U.S.C. 1814 and afforded the Secretary the ability to increase that fee in her discretion.[46] DHS proposes to codify the provisions of 8 U.S.C. 1814 into regulations and to exercise its discretion to increase the fee from $5,130 to $18,000 due to the significant costs that the agency incurs in arresting aliens ordered removed in absentia. By seeking a partial reimbursement of the enforcement activities associated with arresting aliens ordered removed in absentia, the proposed changes are a critical part of DHS's efforts to use all statutorily available tools to achieve the Administration's immigration enforcement and border security objectives.
根据本提案,新的18,000美元费用是对该机构因逮捕被缺席判决驱逐令的外国人所产生成本的部分补偿。该新费用将适用于所有根据第240(b)(5)(A)条(8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(A))被缺席判决驱逐,并在最终规则生效日期当天或之后被移民及海关执法局逮捕的外国人。[47]该费用将发给外国人,通知其该费用的评估方式及缴纳方法。根据8 U.S.C. 1814的规定,该18,000美元的费用将根据城市消费者消费价格指数(CPI-U)每年进行调整,并将在《联邦公报》上予以公布。如果缺席驱逐令根据第240(b)(5)(C)条(8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C))被撤销,则该费用不适用于该外国人。
Under this proposal, the new fee of $18,000 is a partial reimbursement to the agency for its costs associated with arresting aliens ordered removed in absentia. The new fee will apply to all aliens who are ordered removed in absentia pursuant to section 240(b)(5)(A) (8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(A)) and are subsequently arrested by ICE on or after the effective date of the final rule.[47] The fee will be issued to the alien notifying the alien of how the fee was assessed and how to pay the fee. Consistent with 8 U.S.C. 1814, this $18,000 fee will be adjusted annually based on the CPI-U and will be announced in the Federal Register . The fee will not apply to any alien who was ordered removed in absentia if the order was rescinded pursuant to section 240(b)(5)(C) (8 U.S.C. 1229a(b)(5)(C)).
如上文背景部分所述,费用评估通知向外国人提供了逮捕官员所作出的一系列认定,包括该外国人被缺席判决驱逐的日期、其随后被逮捕的事实以及费用金额。费用通知还明确说明,未及时缴纳费用将产生后果,包括根据31 U.S.C. 3717、6 CFR 11.10及31 CFR 901.9所产生的利息、行政费用和滞纳金。外国人可在通知发出后30日内对该通知提出异议。[48]
As described in the background above, the notice of the fee assessment provides the alien with a list of determinations made by the arresting officer, including the date the alien was ordered removed in absentia, the fact that the alien was subsequently arrested, and the fee amount. The notice of fees also makes clear that failure to promptly pay the fee has consequences, including the accrual of interest, administrative costs, and a late payment penalty charge pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3717, 6 CFR 11.10, and 31 CFR 901.9. An alien may dispute the notice within 30 days of issuance.[48]
国土安全部已在8 CFR 103.7(d)中提出了这些修订。8 CFR 103.7(d)规定了与移民执法相关的费用,包括但不限于由美国司法部(DOJ)、美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)、美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)和移民及海关执法局(ICE)评估和收取的费用。因此,国土安全部提议修订《联邦法规汇编》(CFR)的本条款,以增列此项法定要求的费用,并允许该费用在以后的财政年度中继续适用,并按法律授权每年进行通货膨胀调整。
DHS has proposed these amendments at 8 CFR 103.7(d). 8 CFR 103.7(d) describes fees related to immigration enforcement, including but not limited to fees assessed and collected by the Department of Justice (DOJ), U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), and ICE. Therefore, DHS is proposing to amend this section of the CFR to add this statutorily required fee and allow for the continued application of this fee in subsequent fiscal years, with annual adjustments for inflation as authorized by statute.

拟议规则概要
Summary of the Proposed Rule
美国国土安全部(DHS)拟将对被缺席判决驱逐令并随后被ICE逮捕的外国人收取的费用从5,130美元提高至18,000美元，此费用作为对逮捕此类外国人总成本的部分补偿。此次提高费用是由于ICE在逮捕被缺席判决驱逐令的外国人方面产生了巨大成本。在2024财年，ICE平均每名外国人产生18,042美元(以2026年美元计)的成本，这是一项巨大的成本支出，占用了本可用于其他移民执法优先事项的重要移民资源。
DHS proposes raising the fee for aliens who have been ordered removed in absentia and subsequently arrested by ICE from $5,130 to $18,000, which serves as a partial reimbursement for the total cost of arresting such an alien. This increase is due to the significant costs that ICE incurs for the arrest of aliens ordered removed in absentia. In FY 2024, ICE incurred an average of $18,042 per alien (in 2026 dollars), a significant cost and use of crucial immigration resources that could have been applied to other immigration enforcement priorities.
规则制定的必要性
Need for the Rule
ICE审查了有关ICE移民执法行动成本的数据，并确定目前5,000美元的部分补偿费用过低，不足以充分补偿ICE逮捕被缺席判决驱逐令的外国人所产生的成本。
ICE has reviewed the data on the cost of ICE immigration enforcement actions and determined that the partial reimbursement fee of $5,000 is too low to sufficiently reimburse ICE for the cost of arresting an alien who has been ordered removed in absentia.
除逮捕外国人的实际成本外，ICE还产生了各种间接成本和管理费用，包括但不限于培训、车辆和支持人员的费用。这些成本与逮捕外国人的成本是不可分割的，正如逮捕外国人与该外国人的驱逐出境也是不可分割的一样。拟议将费用从5,130美元提高至18,000美元，作为对DHS所产生的重大执法开支的部分补偿，而这些开支因缺席驱逐令数量激增而上升。本拟议规则旨在维护移民执法的完整性，遏制对驱逐令的不遵守行为，并缓解ICE基本而有限资源所承受的压力。
In addition to the actual cost of arresting an alien, ICE also incurs a variety of indirect and overhead costs, including, but not limited to, training, vehicles, and support staff. These costs are inseparable from the cost of the arrest of an alien, just as the arrest of an alien is inseparable from that alien's removal. The proposed fee increase, from $5,130 to $18,000, serves as a partial reimbursement to DHS for the significant enforcement expenses incurred, which have risen due to a surge in in absentia removal orders. This proposed rule aims to protect the integrity of immigration enforcement, discourage noncompliance with removal orders, and alleviate the strain on ICE's essential and limited resources.

DHS faces challenges forecasting the affected population because of data quality issues and several other factors impacting population size. The Covid-19 pandemic appears to have dramatically lowered the number of removal orders issued and arrests made in 2021 affecting what would be a trendline in the future without a pandemic. The announcement of the fee will likely change the behavior of the affected alien population. Aliens may be more likely to appear in immigration court, decreasing the number of removal orders issued in absentia. Additionally, aliens issued removal orders in absentia are more likely to leave the country on their own accord than risk the prospect of arrest and a fee. Significant funding from HR-1 for immigration enforcement, including the hiring of 10,000 ICE officers, will likely lead to more arrests being made. As a result of these countervailing forces, DHS presumes the number of arrests will remain at the same levels as FY 2025, 23,670 for FY 2026 and FY 2027.
转移支付
Transfers
转移支付是指一个群体向另一个群体支付的金钱,不影响社会可获得的资源总量。保险赔付、费用、直接补贴和间接补贴等转移支付除分配效应外,还可能产生重大效率效应,因此不计入本条例的收益和成本估算。本拟议规则对转移支付进行分析,因为拟议收费是从受影响的外国人群体向移民与海关执法局(ICE)和美国财政部的一种转移。
Transfer payments are monetary payments from one group to another that do not affect the total resources available to society. Transfers such as insurance payments, fees, direct subsidies, and indirect subsidies can have significant efficiency effects in addition to distributional effects and are not included in the estimates of the benefits and costs of a regulation. Transfers are analyzed in this proposed rule because the proposed fee is a transfer from the affected alien population to ICE and the U.S. Treasury.
国土安全部(DHS)假定2026财年和2027财年将有23,670次逮捕。那么,转移支付的最大可能金额将等于逮捕人数(23,670)乘以费用(18,000美元),每年产生426,060,000美元的转移支付。本规则产生的净转移支付将等于逮捕人数(23,670)乘以费用变化额(18,000美元减去5,130美元,即12,870美元)。国土安全部(DHS)发现,本规则产生的净转移支付介于15,231,645美元至304,632,900美元之间,中点估计值为159,932,273美元。
DHS assumes there will be 23,670 arrests in FY 2026 and 2027. The maximum possible amount of transfers then would be equal to the number of arrests (23,670) times the fee ($18,000) resulting in $426,060,000 transferred annually. The net transfer resulting from this rule would be equal to the number of arrests (23,670) multiplied by the change in fee ($18,000−$5,130 = $12,870). DHS found the net transfers resulting from the rule to range between $15,231,645 and $304,632,900 with a midpoint estimate of $159,932,273.
移民与海关执法局(ICE)自该费用于2025年9月8日生效之日起开始征收该费用。此后,由于该费用生效时间较短以及当前的实际运作状况,国土安全部(DHS)在估算征收率方面存在不确定性。当前的实际运作节奏在收费流程中带来了一个特殊挑战,即外国人的驱逐并不会因未缴纳费用而被推迟。由于这些因素,国土安全部(DHS)预计,该费用的征收及其影响可能要到外国人日后寻求重新入境美国时才能实现。例如,此前因缺席驱逐令而被驱逐且仍欠缴费用的外国人,可能在日后寻求合法入境美国或申请移民福利。除了考虑先前的非法居留、先前的驱逐令以及先前未出庭应讯之外,[49]审理官员可考虑未偿债务对该外国人入境资格的影响。[50]审理官员可标记该未偿债务,以便在其重新入境时进一步追缴。国土安全部(DHS)认为,真实的征收率可能要等到更多时间过去、使得类似上述情形得以发生后才能确定。
ICE began issuing this fee once the fee became effective on September 8, 2025. Since then, DHS has experienced uncertainty in estimating collection rates due to the short time frame this fee has been in effect and the current operational realities. The current operational pace has introduced a specific challenge in the fee collection process, as the removal of aliens is not being postponed due to unpaid fees. Because of these factors, DHS anticipates that the collection of this fee and its impacts may not be realized until aliens seek future admission to the United States. For example, aliens who were previously removed due to an in absentia removal order, and have outstanding fees, may seek lawful admission to the United States or an immigration benefit at a later point. In addition to considering prior unlawful presence, the prior removal order, and the prior failure to appear for proceedings,[49] the adjudicator may consider the outstanding debt to affect the alien's admissibility.[50] The adjudicator may flag the outstanding debt such that collection would be further pursued upon readmission. DHS believes the true collection rate may not be known until more time has passed to allow for situations such as the aforementioned example to occur.

效益
Benefits
拟议费用将作为对美国国土安全部(DHS)因逮捕被缺席判处驱逐出境的外国人而产生的生命周期成本的部分补偿。该项补偿将使国土安全部能够将其有限的移民资源用于其他移民执法优先事项。
The proposed fee would serve as a partial reimbursement for the lifecycle costs incurred by DHS from the arrest of aliens ordered removed in absentia. This reimbursement will allow DHS to apply its limited immigration resources to other immigration enforcement priorities.
成本
Costs
拟议费用可能导致更多外国人出庭应讯,这可能延长法律诉讼程序并需要额外资源。此外,国土安全部认识到18,000美元的费用对外国人而言可能是一笔高昂的支出,但如上文所述,该费用金额之所以合理,是因为国会意图以此为移民及海关执法局(ICE)提供逮捕被缺席判处驱逐出境外国人所产生的移民执法与诉讼(IEL)总成本的部分补偿。
The proposed fee would likely result in more aliens attending court appearances which may extend legal proceedings and require additional resources. Additionally, DHS recognizes that a fee of $18,000 could be costly for aliens to pay, but as explained above, this fee amount is justified by Congress's intent to provide ICE with partial reimbursement of the total IEL costs of arresting aliens ordered removed in absentia.
备选方案
Alternatives
国土安全部考虑了将费用维持在5,130美元,以及将费用提高至低于18,000美元的金额这两种备选方案。由于这些金额不会对移民及海关执法局在逮捕被缺席判处驱逐出境外国人过程中产生的IEL总成本实现实质性和有意义的部分补偿,因此被认定为不可接受。
DHS considered the alternative of the fee remaining at $5,130 and increasing the fee to amounts less than $18,000. These alternatives were deemed unacceptable as such amounts would not result in actual and meaningful partial reimbursements for the total IEL costs incurred by ICE during the arrest of an alien ordered removed in absentia.
国土安全部还考虑了将费用提高至36,000美元的备选方案,以弥补该费用中有百分之五十被存入美国财政部普通基金这一事实。36,000美元的费用将确保移民及海关执法局从其评估并收取的每笔费用中获得18,000美元的部分补偿,与18,000美元费用的百分之五十相比,这一金额更接近实际的IEL成本。然而,在审查了HR-1 [51] 有限的立法历史后,国土安全部认为18,000美元的费用更符合国会意图,即该费用旨在为美国政府整体提供部分补偿,而非分别为移民及海关执法局和美国财政部提供部分补偿。最终,由于该备选方案(印刷页29387)不符合该费用的国会意图,故未被采纳。
DHS also considered the alternative of increasing the fee to $36,000 to account for the fact that 50 percent of the fee is deposited into the U.S. Treasury General Fund. A fee of $36,000 would ensure that ICE receives $18,000 in partial reimbursement from each fee it assesses and collects, which is closer to the actual IEL costs compared to 50 percent of an $18,000 fee. However, after reviewing the limited legislative history of HR-1 [51] , DHS believes that the $18,000 fee is more closely aligned with congressional intent, which is for the fee to provide a partial reimbursement to the United States Government as a whole, not a partial reimbursement to ICE and a partial reimbursement to the U.S. Treasury. Ultimately, this alternative was not adopted because it ( printed page 29387) does not align with the congressional intent for this fee.

《国家环境政策法》(NEPA)允许联邦机构确定若干类别的行为("类别排除"),经验表明,这些行为无论单独还是累积,均不会对人类环境产生重大影响,因此不需要编制环境评估("EA")或环境影响声明("EIS")。如果"拟议机构行为依据该机构的某项类别排除予以排除",则该机构无需为拟议行为编制EA或EIS。42 U.S.C. 4336(a)(2)。《操作手册》附录A列出了国土安全部(DHS)的类别排除事项。根据DHS《操作手册》,某一行为要被归类排除,必须同时满足以下三个条件:(1)整个行为明显属于一项或多项类别排除范围;(2)该行为不是更大行为的一部分;(3)不存在可能造成重大环境影响的特殊情况。[55]
NEPA allows Federal agencies to establish categories of actions (“categorical exclusions”) that experience has shown do not, individually or cumulatively, have a significant effect on the human environment and, therefore, do not require an environmental assessment (“EA”) or environmental impact statement (“EIS”). An agency is not required to prepare an EA or EIS for a proposed action “if the proposed agency action is excluded pursuant to one of the agency's categorical exclusions.” 42 U.S.C. 4336(a)(2). The Instruction Manual, Appendix A, lists the DHS Categorical Exclusions. For an action to be categorically excluded under DHS's Instruction Manual, the action must satisfy each of the following three conditions: (1) the entire action clearly fits within one or more of the categorical exclusions; (2) the action is not a piece of a larger action; and (3) no extraordinary circumstances exist that create the potential for a significant environmental effect.[55]

This NPRM is categorically excluded from DHS's NEPA implementing procedures, because it satisfies all three relevant conditions. First, the Departments have determined that the NPRM fits clearly within categorical exclusions A3(a) of DHS's Instruction Manual, Appendix A, for the promulgation of rules of a “strictly administrative or procedural nature.” This NPRM increases a fee that was established and authorized by 8 U.S.C. 1814. The fee increase proposed in this NRPM does not result in a change in the fee's environmental impact. Second, this NPRM is a standalone rule and is not part of any larger action. Third, the Department is not aware of any extraordinary circumstances that would cause a significant environmental impact. Therefore, this NPRM is categorically excluded, and no further NEPA analysis or documentation is required.
