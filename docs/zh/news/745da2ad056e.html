政策法规海关与边境保护局 (CBP)

CBP提议修改低价值货物报关规则,与移民事务无关

发布日期 2026年10月8日

美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)提议修改对价值2500美元及以下货物的非正式报关申报要求,并为邮寄进口商品设立新的电子非正式报关类型。该文件属于海关贸易监管事项,与签证、绿卡或入籍等移民程序无关。

要点

  • CBP提议修改价值2500美元及以下货物的非正式报关申报要求。
  • 拟设立新的电子非正式报关类型(Entry Type 13),适用于通过邮件进口的商品。
  • 该提案还拟增加承运人的数据申报要求,并对部分非正式报关设定保证金要求。
  • 该规则目前仅为提议,尚未最终确定或生效。
  • 公众意见截止日期为2026年12月7日。

影响对象

涉及向美国进口低价值货物的进口商、报关行、承运人及电商卖家受此影响。

建议行动

本通知不涉及移民申请人;与海关业务相关方可查阅联邦公报原文并在截止日期前提交意见。

II. 背景
II. Background
根据《美国法典》第19编第1498(a)(1)(A)条,财政部长[1]获授权就总价值不超过2,500美元的货物运输(即非正式报关)制定有关货物入境的规则和条例,包括此前可能符合《美国法典》第19编第1321(a)(2)(C)条规定的微量豁免条件的货物运输,即由一人在同一天进口、在出运国的合计公平零售价值不超过800美元的货物运输。
Under 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A), the Secretary of the Treasury [1] is authorized to prescribe rules and regulations concerning the entry of merchandise when the aggregate value of the shipment is not more than $2,500, i.e., informal entries, including shipments of merchandise that may have previously qualified for the de minimis exemption provided for in 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C) for shipments of merchandise imported by one person on one day having an aggregate fair retail value in the country of shipment of not more than $800.
微量豁免至少自2025年8月29日起已被暂停。[2]2026年6月24日,CBP在《联邦公报》上发布了两项临时最终规则(interim final rules,简称IFR),其中宣布,除其他事项外,CBP法规中规定的微量豁免将被无限期暂停,自2026年6月24日起生效。[3]此外,于2025年7月4日颁布的《大而美法案》(One Big Beautiful Bill Act)终止了微量豁免,自2027年7月1日起生效。[4]
The de minimis exemption has been suspended since at least August 29, 2025.[2] On June 24, 2026, CBP published two interim final rules (IFRs) in the Federal Register announcing, inter alia, the indefinite suspension of the de minimis exemption in CBP's regulations, effective June 24, 2026.[3] Additionally, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which was enacted on July 4, 2025, terminated the de minimis exemption effective July 1, 2027.[4]
本次拟议规则制定活动旨在解决非正式报关环境中与CBP以下工作相关的问题,即保护税收、识别违反美国海关和贸易法律、健康与安全要求、知识产权以及消费者保护规则的行为,以及侦测和阻止芬太尼等非法毒品(包括合成毒品前体及相关化学品和相关制造设备)进入美国境内。尽管其中一些挑战在非正式报关环境中长期存在,但鉴于此前符合微量豁免条件及相关报关程序的货物运输今后很可能将采用 (第64533页) 非正式报关程序,因此制定这些拟议法规对于充分应对低价值货物运输环境中普遍存在的风险和挑战是必要的。具体而言,正如下文更详细讨论的那样,CBP拟对某些非正式报关提出额外的数据和担保要求。此外,CBP拟要求非正式报关须以电子方式提交,且须在进口之日之前或当日提交,并拟为通过邮件渠道进境的货物设立一种新的电子非正式报关类型,即第13类报关。与拟议的新邮件非正式报关第13类相关,CBP同时拟要求承运人提供额外数据。
This proposed rulemaking addresses issues in the informal entry environment concerning CBP's efforts to protect the revenue and to identify violations of U.S. customs and trade laws, health and safety requirements, intellectual property rights, and consumer protection rules, as well as to detect and prevent illicit drugs such as fentanyl (including synthetic drug precursors and related chemicals and related manufacturing equipment) from entering the country. While some of these challenges have long been present in the informal entry environment, now that shipments formerly eligible for the de minimis exemption and associated entry procedures will likely be utilizing ( printed page 64533) informal entry procedures, these proposed regulations are necessary to adequately address the risks and challenges present in the low-value shipment environment generally. Specifically, and as discussed in more detail below, CBP is proposing additional data and bonding requirements for certain informal entries. Further, CBP is proposing to require that informal entries be filed electronically, prior to or upon the date of importation, and to establish a new electronic informal entry type for merchandise entering through the mail environment, i.e., entry type 13. Related to the proposed new informal mail entry type 13, CBP is also proposing to require additional data from carriers.
A. 权限
A. Authority
1. 非正式报关
1. Informal Entry
所有进口至美国关税领土的货物均须办理报关和放行手续,但另有例外规定的除外。参见《美国法典》第19编第1484-85条、第1498条;《联邦法规汇编》第19编第141.4条。这些程序旨在确保对货物进行适当的估价、估值和税则归类,以便征收应缴纳的法定关税金额,并确保遵守CBP执行和实施的所有其他法律法规。根据货物的价值及其他相关标准,不同类型的报关程序适用于货物的入境和放行。
All merchandise imported into the customs territory of the United States is subject to entry and clearance procedures, unless excepted. 19 U.S.C. 1484-85, 1498; 19 CFR 141.4. These procedures ensure the proper appraisement, valuation, and tariff classification of the merchandise for the purpose of collecting the lawful amount of duties owed, as well as compliance with all other laws and regulations administered and enforced by CBP. Different types of entry procedures are used for the entry and clearance of merchandise depending upon its value and other relevant criteria.
正式报关程序,根据《美国法典》第19编第1484条和第1485条设立,通常适用于货值超过2500美元的货物。[5] 非正式报关程序由《美国法典》第19编第1498条(a)(1)(A)款授权,适用于货值在2500美元或以下的货物,并可纳入《美国法典》第19编第1484条和第1485条所规定的正式报关程序。参见《美国法典》第19编第1498条(b)款。非正式报关的相关法规通常见于《联邦法规汇编》第19编第143部分C分部。一般而言,非正式报关程序比正式报关程序负担更轻、也更简便。然而,如美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)认为出于准入许可、税收保护或海关业务高效开展之目的有必要时,可要求对任何货物采用正式报关程序。参见《联邦法规汇编》第19编第143.22条。提交非正式报关的程序通常仅限于有权办理报关的当事方, 即, 货物的所有人或买方,或经该货物的所有人、买方或收货人适当指定的持证海关经纪人。参见《联邦法规汇编》第19编第143.26条(a)款。关于以邮递方式进口货物(包括非正式邮件报关)的具体程序,见第145部分《邮递进口》(《联邦法规汇编》第19编第145部分)。
Formal entry procedures, established by 19 U.S.C. 1484 and 1485, are generally applicable to shipments of merchandise valued in excess of $2,500.[5] Informal entry procedures are authorized by 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A) for shipments of merchandise valued at $2,500 or less, and may incorporate formal entry procedures appearing in 19 U.S.C. 1484 and 1485. 19 U.S.C. 1498(b). Informal entry regulations are generally found in 19 CFR part 143, subpart C. Generally, informal entry procedures are less burdensome and complex than formal entry procedures. However, CBP may require formal entry for any merchandise if deemed necessary for purposes of admissibility, revenue protection, or the efficient conduct of customs business. 19 CFR 143.22. The process for filing informal entry is generally limited to parties with the right to make entry, i.e., an owner or purchaser of the shipment or, when appropriately designated by the owner, purchaser, or consignee of the shipment, a licensed customs broker. 19 CFR 143.26(a). Specific procedures for shipments imported by mail, including informal mail entries, are found in part 145, Mail Importations (19 CFR part 145).
2. 担保金
2. Bonding
国土安全部部长及CBP专员[6] 拥有广泛的法定权力,可在为保护美国税收或确保遵守CBP获授权执行的任何法律、法规或指令而有必要时,“通过法规或具体指示”要求提供担保金或其他担保。参见《美国法典》第19编第1623条(a)款;《联邦法规汇编》第19编第113.1条。部长及专员亦有权规定担保金的条件和形式、担保金的提交方式以及担保金的数额。参见《美国法典》第19编第1623条(b)款;《联邦法规汇编》第19编第113.2条。该项权力包括确定在违反担保条件时应缴纳的金额。 同上。
The Secretary of Homeland Security and the Commissioner of CBP [6] have broad legal authority to require bonds or other security “by regulation or specific instruction” when necessary to protect the revenue of the United States or to assure compliance with any law, regulation, or instruction that CBP is authorized to enforce. 19 U.S.C. 1623(a); 19 CFR 113.1. The Secretary and the Commissioner also have the authority to prescribe the conditions and form of the bond, the manner in which the bond may be filed, and the amount of the bond. 19 U.S.C. 1623(b); 19 CFR 113.2. This authority encompasses setting the amount owed upon breach of a bond condition. Id.
CBP已依据该项权力在《联邦法规汇编》第19编第113部分制定相关法规,详细规定了担保金的签立和提交要求。《联邦法规汇编》第19编第113部分G分部列明了CBP所要求担保金的条款和条件。基本进口报关担保金的条款和条件见于《联邦法规汇编》第19编第113.62条。
CBP has promulgated regulations exercising this authority in 19 CFR part 113, detailing requirements for the execution and filing of bonds. Subpart G of 19 CFR part 113 enumerates the terms and conditions for bonds required by CBP. The terms and conditions for the Basic Importation and Entry Bond are found in 19 CFR 113.62.
3. 舱单要求
3. Manifest Requirements
根据《美国法典》第19编第1431条(b)款,承运人须向CBP提交有关其运往美国货物的信息。根据《美国法典》第19编第1431条(d)款,CBP有权以法规形式规定舱单的格式以及舱单所需载明的信息和数据。
Under 19 U.S.C. 1431(b), carriers are required to submit to CBP information concerning cargo they are transporting to the United States. Under 19 U.S.C. 1431(d), CBP is authorized to specify by regulation the form for, and the information and data required in, a manifest.
B. 新的低值货物环境
B. The New Low-Value Shipment Environment
海关执法对美国的国家安全、外交政策及经济至关重要。有效的海关执法能够防止非法和危险货物的进口,并确保遵守联邦法律,包括有关强迫劳动、原产地规则、原产地标记、知识产权、税收征收和产品安全等方面的法律。其中,电子商务的持续兴盛(互联网使个人能够轻松进行跨国购物)、2016年将 微量豁免 的货值上限提高至800美元[7] ,以及CBP建立的86号报关类型测试[8] (该测试在自动化商业环境(ACE)系统中为符合条件的低值货物授权了一种自愿性电子报关程序),共同导致使用800美元 微量豁免 的货物数量(以及更广义的低值非正式报关, 即, 货值在2500美元或以下的货物)急剧增加。
Customs enforcement is essential to the national security, foreign policy, and economy of the United States. Effective customs enforcement prevents the importation of unlawful and dangerous goods and guarantees compliance with Federal laws, including laws governing forced labor, rules of origin, origin marking, intellectual property, revenue collection, and product safety. Among other things, the continued rise of e-commerce, with the internet empowering individuals to easily make international purchases, the increase of the value cap for the de minimis exemption to $800 in 2016,[7] and the establishment of the Entry Type 86 test [8] in which CBP authorized a voluntary electronic entry process for qualifying low-value shipments in the Automated Commercial Environment (ACE), led to drastic increases in the volume of shipments using the $800 de minimis exemption (and low-value informal entries more generally, i.e., shipments valued at $2,500 or less).
货物数量的急剧增加凸显出CBP在处理合法货物及在低值货物环境中识别违规和危险货物方面所面临的诸多挑战,这些挑战带来了重大且高昂的负担。尽管 微量豁免 的适用已暂停并将于2027年7月1日终止,CBP预计低值货物的数量仍将居高不下,成为问题,因为原本符合 微量豁免 条件的货物将转向其他非正式报关程序,例如11号报关类型,这是一种向CBP提供有关货物若干相关数据的非正式报关类型。[9]
The drastic increase in volume highlighted numerous challenges facing CBP that impose significant and costly burdens related to processing lawful shipments and identifying violative and dangerous merchandise in the low-value environment. Even though the availability of the de minimis exemption is suspended and will be terminated on July 1, 2027, CBP anticipates that the volume of low-value shipments will remain problematically high as merchandise formerly eligible for the de minimis exemption shifts to other informal entry procedures, such as entry type 11, an informal entry type that provides CBP with certain relevant data about the merchandise.[9]
低价值货物通过邮寄方式寄送时,最初会在美国邮政总局(USPS)的国际服务中心(ISC)之一进行处理,该中心负责在国际邮件转交至CBP(美国海关与边境保护局)设施进行进一步查验之前对其进行分拣。然而,在2026年6月24日发布专门针对经由邮政环境到达的货物的临时最终规则之前,法规中规定的邮件简易申报程序一直是一种几乎不提供任何预先申报信息的纸质流程。
Low-value shipments sent via mail are processed initially at one of the U.S. Postal Service's (USPS) International Service Centers (ISCs) that sort international mail before it is transferred to a CBP facility for further examination. However, until the publication on June 24, 2026, of the interim final rule focusing on merchandise arriving via the postal environment, the mail informal entry process set forth in the regulations was a paper process with almost no advance entry information provided.
根据最近更新的邮件简易申报监管框架,[10] 该框架要求具有申报权利的一方以电子方式支付关税,并按月通过电子邮件传送一份工作表,CBP仍须进行繁琐的人工流程以核实 (印刷页64534) 货物被认定为可予进口以及按照所有适用要求妥善完成申报所需的信息。尽管这一更新后的流程相较于此前的监管流程有所改进(此前的流程是由CBP官员人工填写货物的邮件申报表,收件人随后通过邮政总局支付所欠的关税、税款和费用),本规则制定中提议的修订将使CBP能够更有效地管理其执法和处理职责。
Under the recently updated regulatory framework for informal mail entries,[10] which requires the electronic payment of duties and transmission via email of a worksheet on a monthly basis by a party with the right to make entry, CBP still must engage in a burdensome manual process to verify the ( printed page 64534) information necessary for the shipment to be deemed admissible and properly entered in accordance with all applicable requirements. While this updated process is an improvement on the prior regulatory process, where a CBP officer would manually prepare the mail entry form for a shipment and the recipient paid the duties, taxes, and fees owed subsequently through the Postal Service, the proposed amendments in this rulemaking will allow CBP to more effectively manage its enforcement and processing responsibilities.
尽管第86类申报测试被证明不足以充分解决其设计初衷所要应对的问题,但CBP从该测试中吸取的经验教训为近期宣布的针对邮件新电子简易申报程序的第13类申报测试提供了依据。[11] 因此,本规则制定中所包含的提案部分基于第86类申报测试中吸取的经验教训,包括需要强制要求电子申报、哪些数据要素必须为强制性、要求收货人使用持照海关经纪人提交简易申报,以及需要在货物到达之前或到达时接收数据。
Even though the Entry Type 86 test proved insufficient to adequately address the issues for which it was designed, the lessons CBP learned from the test informed the recently announced Entry Type 13 test for the new electronic informal entry process for mail.[11] Accordingly, the proposals included in this rulemaking are based in part on the lessons learned from the Entry Type 86 test, including the need to require electronic filing, which data elements must be mandatory, requiring a consignee to use a licensed customs broker to file an informal entry, and the need to receive the data prior to or upon arrival of the shipment.
简而言之,CBP需要一个电子化的简易申报流程,以便有效处理数据,用于核实申报信息、及时有效地开展目标筛查,并侦测和查扣非法货物,例如非法枪支、违禁物品、非法芬太尼和其他非法毒品。如果没有下文详细论述的这些拟议监管修订,简易申报环境中庞大的进口数量将继续阻碍CBP处理申报及执行美国法律的工作。
Simply put, CBP requires an electronic process for informal entries in order to effectively process data for purposes of validating entry information, conduct targeting in a timely and effective manner, and detect and seize illicit merchandise, such as illegal firearms, prohibited items, illicit fentanyl, and other illicit drugs. Absent these proposed regulatory amendments, discussed in more detail below, the sheer volume of imports in the informal entry environment would continue to hamper CBP's efforts to process entries and enforce U.S. law.
如上所述,低价值货物数量的庞大给CBP带来更大挑战,使其难以开展目标筛查,以识别违反美国海关和贸易法律、健康与安全要求、知识产权以及消费者保护规则的行为,同时也难以防止芬太尼等非法毒品及合成毒品前体、相关化学品和制造设备进入美国境内。此外,许多在线订购商品的消费者没有足够的信息或知识来遵守海关和贸易法律及其他要求,这增加了他们所购买的商品可能不符合美国健康与安全标准或带来其他风险的危险。综合来看,如果不加以解决,当前环境下的执法挑战可能危及美国民众的福祉和生命安全。
As noted above, the overwhelming volume of low-value shipments makes it more challenging for CBP to conduct targeting for purposes of identifying violations of U.S. customs and trade laws, health and safety requirements, intellectual property rights, and consumer protection rules, as well as preventing illicit drugs, such as fentanyl, and synthetic drug precursors, related chemicals, and manufacturing equipment from entering the country. Moreover, many consumers ordering goods online do not have sufficient information or knowledge about the goods to comply with customs and trade laws and other requirements increases the danger that an item they are purchasing may not comply with U.S. health and safety standards or pose other risks. Taken together, if not addressed, the enforcement challenges in the current environment have the capacity to put Americans' well-being and lives at risk.
因此,如下文更详细讨论的那样,美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)拟实施若干项变更,以解决低价值货物运输环境中存在的问题。具体而言,根据19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A)赋予的权力,CBP拟对价值2,500美元或以下货物的非正式报关程序实施变更,要求强制以电子方式提交报关单,同时修改数据要求和提交时限。此外,CBP拟针对价值2,500美元或以下的邮件货物设立新的电子报关程序。CBP还拟修改非正式报关的担保(保证金)要求,并根据19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A)和1498(b)赋予的广泛法定权力,扩大CBP可要求对价值2,500美元或以下货物(包括通过拟议的邮件电子报关程序提交的报关单)实行正式报关的法规标准。最后,针对价值2,500美元或以下邮件货物的新非正式报关程序,CBP拟根据其19 U.S.C. 1431项下的权力,要求承运人在邮件货物舱单中提供有关邮件货物的额外信息。
Accordingly, as discussed in more detail below, CBP is proposing to implement a number of changes to address issues in the low-value shipment environment. Specifically, pursuant to its authority in 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A), CBP is proposing to implement changes to the informal entry process for shipments valued at $2,500 or less by mandating electronic filing as well as modifying data requirements and filing timelines. Additionally, CBP is proposing a new electronic entry process for mail shipments valued at $2,500 or less. CBP is also proposing to modify the bonding requirements for informal entries, and, pursuant to its broad statutory authority in 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A) and 1498(b), to expand the regulatory criteria when CBP may require formal entry for merchandise valued at $2,500 or less (including for entries filed through the proposed electronic process for mail). And finally, related to the new informal entry process for mail shipments valued at $2,500 or less, under its 19 U.S.C. 1431 authority, CBP is proposing to require additional information regarding mail shipments that carriers must provide as part of a manifest for mail shipments.
C. 解决低价值货物运输环境中的问题
C. Addressing Issues in the Low-Value Shipment Environment
1. 第11类非正式报关要求
1. Informal Entry Type 11 Requirements
为应对CBP在低价值货物运输环境中面临的挑战,CBP拟修改价值2,500美元或以下货物(即非正式报关货物)的报关程序。[12] CBP拟修订该程序,要求在进口之日当日或之前以电子方式提交第11类报关单,并要求在报关总结单(entry summary)上标明最终交付方(如与最终收货人不同)。与此相关,且如下文“非正式报关保证金要求”部分进一步讨论的那样,CBP还拟修订第11类报关单提交的担保要求。
To address the challenges CBP faces in the low-value shipment environment, CBP is proposing to modify the entry process for shipments of merchandise valued at $2,500 or less, i.e., informal entries.[12] CBP is proposing to amend this process by requiring the entry to be filed electronically as an entry type 11 filing, upon or prior to the date of importation, and requiring identification of the final deliver-to party if different from the ultimate consignee on the entry summary. Relatedly, and as discussed further below in the Informal Entry Bond Requirements section, CBP is also proposing to amend the bonding requirements for entry type 11 filings.
a. 第11类报关单提交要求
a. Entry Type 11 Filing Requirements
根据19 CFR 141.5的规定,报关必须在货物从船舶、航空器或车辆卸载后15个日历日内办理,如货物系保税运输的,则须在到达目的地口岸后15个日历日内办理。[13] 这种传统的报关时限,即在货物抵达后提供超过两周的时间办理报关,在高数量、低价值货物运输的情境下会产生问题,因为这无法使CBP在此类可能危险或非法的货物进入美国之前,有充分的时间进行风险评估和锁定目标。要求在货物抵达之前或抵达之时即提供确定可否入境及可否放行所需的报关数据,将使CBP能够在货物抵达时或抵达之前作出此类决定。
Pursuant to 19 CFR 141.5, an entry must be made within 15 calendar days after merchandise has landed from a vessel, aircraft, or vehicle, or after arrival at the port of destination in the case of merchandise transported in bond.[13] This traditional entry timeframe, providing over two weeks to make entry after the arrival of the merchandise, poses problems in the context of high-volume, low-value shipments because it does not provide CBP with sufficient time to properly conduct risk assessment and targeting before the arrival of such potentially dangerous or unlawful importations in the United States. Requiring the entry data necessary to determine admissibility and eligibility for release to be provided prior to or upon the arrival of a shipment will enable CBP to make such determinations at or before a shipment arrives.
因此,根据本拟议规则,CBP将第11类非正式报关的报关截止时间设定为进口之日当日或之前,该“进口之日”在19 CFR 101.1中定义为:对于船运抵达的货物,指有意卸载并在口岸范围内到达之日;对于所有其他货物,指在美国关税领土内到达之日。
Accordingly, with this proposed rule, CBP is setting the deadline to make entry for informal entry type 11 to be upon or prior to the date of importation, which is defined in 19 CFR 101.1 as the date of arrival within port limits with intent to unlade for merchandise arriving by vessel, or the date of arrival within the customs territory of the United States for all other merchandise.
此外,CBP拟修订相关法规,要求第11类报关单必须以电子方式提交。目前,根据19 CFR 143.23的规定,第11类报关单的提交人可选择以纸质方式或电子方式办理报关。纸质报关单由CBP员工手动录入自动化商业环境系统(ACE),这在时间和费用上均给CBP带来处理成本。此外,绝大多数第11类报关单已经以电子方式提交,因此CBP预计此项拟议变更不会对公众造成重大负担。例如,从2025年9月1日至2026年5月31日期间,在合计52,450,418份第11类报关单中,仅有73,534份是以纸质方式手动提交的(占比0.14%)。CBP不仅必须在货物抵达之时或之前获得报关数据,而且必须以电子方式接收该数据,以便能够对报关数据进行更自动化的评估,从而确定货物的可入境性并征收税费。
In addition, CBP is proposing to amend the regulations to require electronic filing for entry type 11. Currently, entry type 11 filers have the option to make entry on paper or electronically pursuant to 19 CFR 143.23. Paper entries are manually added to ACE by CBP employees, which imposes a processing cost to CBP both in time and expense. Moreover, the vast majority of type 11 entries are already filed electronically, so CBP does not anticipate this proposed change to pose a significant burden on the public. For example, from September 1, 2025 to May 31, 2026, only 73,534 type 11 entries were submitted manually as paper filings out of 52,450,418 type 11 entries in total (or 0.14%). It is crucial not only for CBP to have entry data at the time of or prior to the arrival of the merchandise, but also for CBP to receive the data electronically to allow for a more automated assessment of entry data for admissibility and revenue collection purposes.
b. 第11类报关数据:最终交付方
b. Entry Type 11 Data: Final Deliver-To Party
海关与边境保护局(CBP)已确定,有效的目标定位需要识别进口货物最终拟交付的当事方,该当事方可能并非货物抵达时负责接收监管的最初收货人或收货人 (印刷页第64535页) 。因此,CBP提议要求提交识别最终交付方(包括该当事方地址)的数据,如果该当事方与报关单(CBP 7501表)上所列的最终收货人不同。增加这一数据要素的目的是使CBP能够识别进口商品在美国的交付对象及交付地点。为避免数据要素的重复,CBP提议仅在最终交付方与报关单上所列最终收货人不同的情况下,才要求识别最终交付方。最终交付方是指在提交报关单时已知的货物最终预定接收方。
CBP has determined that effective targeting requires identifying the party to whom an imported good is ultimately intended to be delivered, who may not be the initial recipient or the consignee ( printed page 64535) taking custody of the good upon arrival. Accordingly, CBP is proposing to require the submission of data identifying the Final Deliver-To Party (including the party's address), if this party is distinct from the ultimate consignee identified on the entry summary (CBP Form 7501). The purpose of this additional data element is to enable CBP to identify to whom and where the imported merchandise is destined to be delivered in the United States. To avoid duplication of data elements, CBP is proposing to only require identifying the final deliver-to party if this party is distinct from the ultimate consignee named on the entry summary. The final deliver-to party is the final intended recipient of the shipment, as known at the time of filing the entry summary.
c. 简易报关与《美国协调关税表》(HTSUS)第99章第三分章
c. Informal Entry and Chapter 99, Subchapter III, HTSUS
CBP提议修订19 CFR 143.21条,删除有关《美国协调关税表》(HTSUS)第99章第三分章和第四分章项下商品适用简易报关资格的250美元限额的相关规定,例如,受临时贸易救济和/或配额限制的商品,相较于简易报关适用的一般2,500美元价值限额。因此,根据拟议修正案,根据HTSUS第99章第三分章和第四分章归类、价值超过250美元但不超过2,500美元的商品,将不再被禁止使用简易报关程序。该拟议修正案将避免要求价值在251美元至2,500美元之间且受第232条关税等措施(经修订的1962年《贸易扩展法》第232条,19 U.S.C. 1862,公法87-794,76 Stat. 872)约束的商品货运必须进行正式报关,从而为申报人提供使用简易报关程序的选择,因为这些措施所征收的附加关税已列于HTSUS第99章第三分章中。该拟议修正案与2012年对其他简易报关法规所作的相同修改相呼应。77 FR 72715(2012年12月6日)。与此相关,CBP提议修订19 CFR 141.82条和143.23条,删除有关HTSUS第99章第三分章和第四分章项下商品250美元限额的相关规定。
CBP is proposing to amend 19 CFR 143.21 by removing references to the $250 limit on eligibility for informal entry for merchandise classified under Chapter 99, Subchapters III and IV, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), e.g., goods subject to temporary trade remedies and/or quotas, as compared to the general $2,500 value limit for informal entry. Accordingly, under the proposed amendment, goods classified under Subchapters III and IV of Chapter 99, HTSUS, valued over $250, but not more than $2,500, would no longer be prohibited from using informal entry procedures. This proposed amendment will avoid requiring shipments of merchandise valued between $251 and $2,500 and subject to measures such as Section 232 duties (Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, as amended; 19 U.S.C. 1862, Public Law 87-794, 76 Stat. 872) to be formally entered, thus providing the option for filers to use informal entry procedures, because the additional duties imposed by these measures are listed in Subchapter III to Chapter 99 of the HTSUS. This proposed amendment mirrors the same change made to other informal entry regulations in 2012. 77 FR 72715 (Dec. 6, 2012). Relatedly, CBP is proposing to amend 19 CFR 141.82 and 143.23 to remove references to the $250 limit on merchandise classified under Subchapters III and IV of Chapter 99, HTSUS.
d. 报关权
d. Right To Make Entry
CBP提议修订19 CFR 143.26(a)条,要求非货物所有人或购买人的收货人(例如外国邮政运营商、美国邮政总局、货运代理或承运人)如拟报关商品,须委任一名持照海关经纪人,由其作为价值2,500美元或以下、且不属于19 CFR 143.26(b)条例外情形货运的记录进口人办理报关手续。该拟议修正案明确表明,适用于正式报关商品的经纪人代表收货人担任记录进口人的要求,[14]也将适用于通过11类报关类型或下文详述的拟新设的13类电子简易邮件报关类型办理的价值2,500美元或以下商品的报关。该拟议修正案系86类报关测试项目的要求之一。[15]根据19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(A)至(B)款免税货运的报关要求,将继续维持19 CFR 143.26(b)条现行规定不变。
CBP is proposing to amend 19 CFR 143.26(a) to require a consignee intending to enter merchandise who is not an owner or purchaser (such as a foreign postal operator, the United States Postal Service, a freight forwarder, or a carrier) to appoint a licensed customs broker who will act as the importer of record for the entry of shipments valued at $2,500 or less that are not covered by the exception in 19 CFR 143.26(b). This proposed amendment makes it clear that the requirement for a broker to act as the importer of record on behalf of a consignee, applicable to formally entered merchandise,[14] would also be applicable to the entry of merchandise valued at $2,500 or less which is entered via entry type 11 or via the newly proposed electronic informal mail entry type 13, discussed in more detail below. This proposed amendment was a requirement of the Entry Type 86 test.[15] The requirements for entering shipments exempt from duty under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(A)-(B) will remain unchanged from the current provisions in 19 CFR 143.26(b).
2. 新设13类简易邮件报关要求
2. New Informal Mail Entry Type 13 Requirements
考虑到上述问题以及原本符合微量豁免资格的邮件量转向非正式报关程序的情况,美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)已确定有必要向全电子化新报关类型过渡,以取代19 CFR 145.12(b)项下现行的邮件货物处理程序。CBP近期宣布了第13类报关(Entry Type 13)测试计划,该计划涉及通过国际邮件渠道进入美国、货值2,500美元或以下货物的非正式报关电子程序。详见91 FR 38007。第13类报关测试旨在使CBP能够评估ACE(自动化商业环境系统)的功能和操作程序,同时使参与方在本规则制定程序进行期间熟悉该程序的整体运作。据此,CBP提议设立新的法规报关类型,即第13类,非正式邮件报关,适用于经邮寄方式运往美国、货值2,500美元或以下的货物,且该货物若非通过国际邮政网络运输至美国,则本应适用第11类报关。
Considering the issues discussed above and the shifting of mail volumes formerly eligible for the de minimis exemption to the informal entry process, CBP has determined it is necessary to transition to a fully electronic new entry type for mail shipments that will replace the current process under 19 CFR 145.12(b). CBP recently announced the Entry Type 13 test regarding electronic procedures for informal entry of shipments valued at $2,500 or less entering the United States through the international mail process. See91 FR 38007. The Entry Type 13 test is designed to allow CBP to evaluate ACE functionality and operational procedures while allowing participants to become familiar with the process generally during the pendency of this rulemaking. Accordingly, CBP is proposing a new regulatory entry type 13—Informal Mail Entry, applicable to shipments of merchandise valued at $2,500 or less, that are sent to the United States via mail, and that would be eligible for entry type 11 if shipped to the United States by a means other than through the international postal network.
此外,CBP提议要求运输邮件至美国的承运人提供更多数据,这些数据在第13类报关测试中仅列为可选项。根据现行法规,此类承运人仅向CBP提供运入美国邮件的重量信息,这不足以使CBP充分应对该环境中存在的风险,因为CBP无法核实某一特定邮件是否已按照所有适用要求完成报关。[16]
Additionally, CBP is proposing to require additional data from carriers transporting mail to the United States, which are listed as optional in the Entry Type 13 test. Under the current regulations, such carriers only provide CBP with the weight of any mail transported into the United States, which does not allow CBP to sufficiently address the risks in this environment as CBP cannot verify that a specific mail article has been entered in accordance with all applicable requirements.[16]
与此相关,正如下文“非正式报关保证金要求”部分进一步讨论的那样,CBP还提议修订非正式报关(包括第13类非正式邮件报关)的保证金要求。
Relatedly, and as discussed further below in the Informal Entry Bond Requirements section, CBP is also proposing to amend the bonding requirements for informal entries—including entry type 13 informal mail entries.
a. 新设第13类报关,非正式邮件报关
a. New Entry Type 13—Informal Mail Entry
CBP提议要求对依照第145.12(b)条进入美国并办理报关的邮件货物实行电子报关申报,此类货物若非通过邮寄方式运往美国,则本应适用第11类报关。CBP提议,此类货值2,500美元或以下的邮件货物必须通过新设立的第13类报关,非正式邮件报关予以申报。在实务中,本提议新设的邮件第13类报关在数据要求和处理流程上将在很大程度上与经本拟议规则修订后的第11类报关保持一致,包括上文所述的申报时限要求以及下文所述的邮件特有附加要求。
CBP is proposing to require electronic entry filing for mail shipments arriving into the United States and entered under section 145.12(b), which would be eligible for entry type 11 if shipped to the United States by means other than mail. CBP is proposing that such mail shipments of merchandise valued at $2,500 or less must be entered via the newly established entry type 13—Informal Mail Entry. c. 未报关的简易邮件报关
c. Unentered Informal Mail Entries
在简易报关邮件环境中,由于新规定要求报关必须在进口之日当日或之前提交,在报关尚未提交之前抵达的货物将不会从CBP的监管中放行。鉴于每日抵达的大量低价值货物,这一拟议的报关要求意味着,美国邮政总局(USPS)和CBP将需要迅速采取行动,识别并处理未及时提交报关的邮件物品,以避免因存储大量未报关邮件而产生的费用和负担。因此,通过本次规则制定,CBP提议,若在进口之日起15日内未提交报关,此类邮件物品将被视为自愿放弃,而此类被视为放弃的物品随后将按照USPS的要求和程序进行处理,这可能包括销毁货物或将货物退回寄件人。
In the informal entry mail environment, as a result of the new deadline to file entry upon or prior to the date of importation, shipments arriving before an entry has been filed will not be released from CBP custody. The effect of this proposed entry requirement on the high volume of low-value shipments arriving daily means that USPS and CBP will need to move quickly to identify and dispose of mail articles for which an entry has not been timely filed to avoid the cost and burden of storing large volumes of unentered mail shipments. Accordingly, with this rulemaking, CBP is proposing that such mail articles will be deemed voluntarily abandoned if no entry is filed within 15 days of importation, and such deemed abandoned articles will then be processed in accordance with USPS requirements and procedures, which may entail destruction or return of the shipment to the sender.
因此,本文件提议增加新的第145.6节(19 CFR 145.6),即未报关包裹,说明价值在2,500美元或以下且未及时正确提交报关的邮件物品,将被CBP视为在自进口之日起算的15日期限届满时自愿放弃,届时该货物将由USPS按相应程序进行处理,此类处理可能包括销毁货物或将货物退回寄件人。
Therefore, this document proposes to add a new section 145.6 (19 CFR 145.6), Unentered Packages, explaining that mail articles valued at $2,500 or less for which an entry has not been timely and properly filed will be considered by CBP to be voluntarily abandoned at the expiration of a 15-day period, beginning on the date of importation, at which time the merchandise will be processed accordingly by USPS, which may include destruction or returning the shipment to the sender.
3. 简易报关担保金要求
3. Informal Entry Bond Requirements
如上文所述,根据19 U.S.C. 1623,CBP拥有广泛的法律授权,在必要时可“通过法规或具体指示”要求提供担保金或其他担保,以保护美国的税收或确保遵守相关法律、法规和合法指示,并规定担保金的条件和形式、担保金的提交方式、担保金的金额,以及违反担保金条件时应缴纳的金额。目前,对于简易报关,包括邮件环境中的简易报关,仅在某些情形下才要求提供担保金。
As discussed above, pursuant to 19 U.S.C. 1623, CBP has broad legal authority to require bonds or other security “by regulation or specific instruction” when necessary to protect the revenue of the United States or to ensure compliance with laws, regulations, and lawful instructions, and to prescribe the conditions and form of the bond, the manner in which the bond may be filed, the amount of the bond, and the amount owed upon breach of a bond condition. Currently, for informal entries, including those in the mail environment, bonding is only required in certain situations.
a. 简易报关所需的基本进口及报关担保金
a. Basic Importation and Entry Bond Required for Informal Entries
为帮助确保税收得到保护,并确保在清关时支付所欠的关税、税费,CBP提议要求所有第11类报关和第13类报关的申报人必须获得基本进口及报关担保金(单次交易担保金(STB)或连续担保金),其条件见19 CFR 113.62。需要担保金的第11类或第13类报关申报人,可以在本拟议规则最终定稿后终止并替换现有的连续性基本进口及报关担保金,或在本拟议规则最终定稿后获得新的基本进口及报关担保金(连续性或单次交易性担保金)。
To help ensure that the revenue is protected and ensure the payment of the duties, taxes, and fees owed at liquidation, CBP is proposing to require that all entry type 11 and entry type 13 filers must obtain a basic importation and entry bond (either a single transaction bond (STB) or a continuous bond), with the conditions found in 19 CFR 113.62. An entry type 11 or entry type 13 filer needing a bond may either terminate and replace an existing continuous basic importation and entry bond after the finalization of this proposed rule or obtain a new basic importation and entry bond (continuous or single transaction) after the finalization of this proposed rule.
在进口货物的入境未办理担保的情况下，如果记录进口商未能履行准确缴纳关税、税款和费用的义务，海关与边境保护局（CBP）几乎没有追索手段来征收应缴税费。通过对第11类和第13类入境要求担保，类似于对须正式报关货物的担保要求，担保人或现金保证金可确保在记录进口商违约的情况下向CBP支付款项。此担保提案旨在确保从此类进口货物中征收的税费得到保障。基本进口和入境担保还可确保货物的交回， 例如， 当需要补充信息以确定货物是否准予入境时，并使记录进口商有义务纠正与准予入境相关的适用法律或法规方面的不合规情形，这有助于CBP确保美国消费者的安全与保障。而且，如上文所述，当非进口货物所有人或购买人的收货人委托报关行办理第11类或第13类入境手续时，CBP提议要求该报关行作为记录进口商，因此该报关行的基本进口和入境担保将承担相应义务。
In circumstances where an entry of imported merchandise is not bonded, there is little recourse for CBP to collect the revenue owed if an importer of record defaults on its obligation to tender accurate duties, taxes, and fees. By requiring a bond for type 11 and type 13 entries, akin to bond requirements for merchandise required to be formally entered, a surety or a cash deposit ensures payment to CBP in the event the importer of record defaults. This bonding proposal is designed to secure the revenue to be collected from such imported merchandise. A basic importation and entry bond also secures redelivery of merchandise, e.g., when additional information is necessary to determine admissibility, and obligates an importer of record to correct non-compliance with an applicable law or regulation pertaining to admissibility, which aids CBP in ensuring the safety and security of American consumers. And, as mentioned above related to when a consignee who is not an owner or purchaser of the imported merchandise appoints a broker to file a type 11 or type 13 entry, CBP is proposing to require the broker to act as the importer of record, and thus the broker's basic importation and entry bond will be obligated.
b. 清算损害赔偿金的最低金额
b. Minimum Amount for Liquidated Damages
清算损害赔偿金是担保主体在违反其海关担保条件时，例如未能交回货物或未能满足其他进口要求时，事先约定支付的预定金额。清算损害赔偿金的索赔以“所发生罚款或清算损害赔偿金通知及付款要求”（CBP表格5955A）形式发出。进口商可在CBP表格5955A签发之日起60个日历日内就清算损害赔偿金索赔申请减免。担保人与担保主体同时被告知承担清算损害赔偿金责任，如果担保主体在60天内未作出回应，则向担保人发出付款要求。
Liquidated damages are predetermined amounts bond principals agree to pay if they breach the conditions of their customs bond, such as failing to redeliver merchandise or meet other import requirements. A claim for liquidated damages is issued on the “Notice of Penalty or Liquidated Damages Incurred and Demand for Payment” (CBP Form 5955A). Importers can petition for relief from a claim for liquidated damages within 60 calendar days of the date of issuance of the CBP Form 5955A. Sureties are notified of liability for liquidated damages at the same time as principals, and if the principal does not respond within 60 days, a demand on the surety is issued.
基本进口和入境担保现有的许多担保条件，包括除19 CFR第113.62(a)、(g)、(i)、(j)、(k)(2)、(k)(3)、(l)和(m)条所规定条件以外的其他条件，将担保项下清算损害赔偿金的金额设定为违约所涉货物的价值，若货物为限制或禁止类货物或酒精饮料，则为违约所涉货物价值的三倍，或为法律或法规可能授权的其他金额。对于本拟议规则制定所涉及的低价值货运，以货物价值为基础评估清算损害赔偿金往往不足以确保遵守所有适用法律和担保条件，也不足以保障税收。因此，结合上文所述的新担保要求，对于除19 CFR第113.62(a)、(g)、(i)、(j)、(k)(2)、(k)(3)、(l)和(m)条以外的基本进口和入境担保条件，CBP提议确立一项新的 （印刷页第64537页） 最低金额，适用于因违反第11类入境或第13类入境担保而评估的清算损害赔偿金。此项拟议的新最低金额将在保障税收、确保遵守适用法律法规的同时，确保CBP征收清算损害赔偿金的成本不超过所追回的金额。因此，CBP提议修订19 CFR第113.62(n)条，修改针对第11类非正式入境和第13类非正式邮件入境货运所评估的清算损害赔偿金，对所列担保条件的每次违反规定新的最低金额为1,000美元。
Many of the existing bond conditions for a basic importation and entry bond, including conditions other than those set forth in 19 CFR 113.62(a), (g), (i), (j), (k)(2), (k)(3), (l), and (m), set the amount of liquidated damages under the bond at the value of the merchandise involved in the default, or three times the value of the merchandise involved in the default if the merchandise is restricted or prohibited merchandise or alcoholic beverages, or at another amount that may be authorized by law or regulation. For low-value shipments subject to this proposed rulemaking, assessment of liquidated damages based on the value of the merchandise will often be insufficient to ensure compliance with all applicable laws, bond conditions, and to protect the revenue. Accordingly, in conjunction with the new bonding requirements discussed above, for the basic importation and entry bond conditions other than 19 CFR 113.62(a), (g), (i), (j), (k)(2), (k)(3), (l), and (m), CBP is proposing to establish a new ( printed page 64537) minimum amount for liquidated damages assessed due to a breach of the bond for an entry type 11 or entry type 13. This proposed new minimum amount will protect the revenue and ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations while also ensuring that the cost to CBP from collecting liquidated damages does not exceed the amount recovered. Therefore, CBP is proposing to amend 19 CFR 113.62(n) to modify the liquidated damages assessed for informal entry type 11 and informal mail entry type 13 shipments to impose a new minimum amount of $1,000 for each breach of a listed bond condition.

CBP拟修订19 CFR 143.22条,以符合其依据19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A)和1498(b)条[18]对价值2,500美元或以下货物非正式报关事项进行广泛监管的权限,扩大CBP可要求对原本符合非正式报关资格的货物实行正式报关的标准。具体而言,通过此项拟议修订,CBP将扩大其酌情决定权,使其可以基于其认为适当的任何理由要求办理正式报关,包括要求特定报关进口商、特定类型或类别的货物,或由特定制造商或销售商生产的货物办理正式报关。因此,拟议修订明确,CBP并不仅限于针对特定货物逐案要求办理正式报关。相反,根据本拟议条款,CBP可根据其认为相关的标准,对货物实行统一要求办理正式报关,例如基于报关进口商、制造商或货物类别。

背景
Background
在2025年之前,大多数低价值邮政货物符合《美国法典》第19编第1321(a)(2)(C)条规定的行政性关税豁免(即“微量豁免”)条件,并且根据适用于此类低价值货物的入境法规,无需向CBP提交详细的入境信息。2025年7月4日,总统签署了《一个美丽大法案》(One Big Beautiful Bill Act)并使其成为法律,该法案除其他事项外,还规定自2027年7月1日起终止微量豁免。[19] 然而,即使在该终止日期之前,总统已于2025年7月30日签署了第14324号行政命令(《暂停对所有国家实施免税微量待遇》),该命令宣布总统认定,为应对已查明的、源于美国境外全部或实质部分的持续存在的异常且特殊的威胁(此等威胁危及美国的国家安全、外交政策和经济),有必要且适当暂停《美国法典》第19编第1321(a)(2)(C)条项下对大多数进口商品的免税微量待遇。[20] 除暂停微量豁免外,该行政命令还为通过国际邮政网络寄往美国的符合条件的产品建立了临时程序和新的关税税率。
Before 2025, most low-value postal shipments were eligible for the administrative exemption from duty at 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C) ( de minimis exemption) and, consistent with the entry regulations for such low-value shipments, the submission of detailed entry information to CBP was not required. On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which, among other things, enacted the termination of the de minimis exemption effective July 1, 2027.[19] Even before that termination date, however, the President signed Executive Order 14324 (Suspending Duty-Free De Minimis Treatment For All Countries) on July 30, 2025, which announced that the President determined that it was necessary and appropriate to suspend duty-free de minimis treatment under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C) for most imports to deal with the identified continuing unusual and extraordinary threats, which have their source in whole or substantial part outside the United States, to the national security, foreign policy, and economy of the United States.[20] Besides suspending the de minimis exemption, the Executive Order also established an interim process and new duty rate for eligible products sent to the United States through the international postal network.
2026年2月20日,总统签署了第14388号行政命令(《继续暂停对所有国家实施免税微量待遇》),该命令除其他事项外,继续暂停《美国法典》第19编第1321(a)(2)(C)条项下的免税微量待遇,包括通过国际邮政网络寄送的货物,并确认CBP应继续对此类货物征收适用的关税、税款、规费、征费和费用。该行政命令根据1974年《贸易法》第122条(《美国法典》第19编第2132条,以下简称“第122条”)授权的权限,对邮政货物征收10%的从价关税。除该统一的10%关税外,通过临时邮政程序入境的邮政货物不再征收其他关税。在该程序下,报关人无需提交邮政货物的产品归类数据,这意味着CBP没有必要的信息来核定大多数其他关税。报关人转而提交月度国际邮件关税工作表,在其中报告其当月邮政货物的价值以及根据10%税率计算所欠的关税。另一方面,非邮政的非正式货物既需缴纳第122条项下的10%关税,也需缴纳其他任何适用的关税。
On February 20, 2026, the President signed Executive Order 14388 (Continuing the Suspension of Duty-Free De Minimis Treatment For All Countries), which, among other things, continued the suspension of duty-free de minimis treatment under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C), including for shipments sent through the international postal network, and confirmed that CBP should continue to collect applicable duties, taxes, fees, exactions, and charges on such shipments. That Executive Order imposed an ad valorem duty rate of 10% on postal shipments, as authorized by section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2132) (Section 122). Aside from that flat 10% duty, no other duties were collected from postal shipments entered through the interim postal process. Filers were not required to submit product classification data for postal shipments under that process, meaning that CBP did not have the necessary information to assess most other duties. Instead, filers submitted a monthly international mail duty worksheet, in which they reported the value of their postal shipments that month and the duties they owed based on the 10% rate. Informal non-postal shipments, on the other hand, were subject to both the 10% duty under Section 122 and any other applicable duties.
为消除邮政与非邮政非正式报关之间的关税差异,美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)于2026年6月24日发布了《无限期暂停邮件货物最低限额豁免及新邮政非正式报关程序临时最终规则》(IFR),该规则修订了邮件非正式货物的报关程序,改进了经修订的第14324号行政命令所确立的临时程序。[21] 在本分析中,CBP将该临时最终规则所确立的邮件货物非正式报关程序称为“现行邮政非正式报关程序”。根据该规则,月度国际邮件关税工作表被扩展,要求提供产品分类及其他数据要素,CBP开始对邮政非正式货物适用所有相应关税。因此,邮政非正式报关与ET11报关现已须遵守相同的产品分类报告要求。但是,尽管现行邮政非正式报关程序使CBP能够对邮政货物征收与ET11报关相同的关税,但在邮政环境中执行关税及其他法规仍然更为困难。根据现行邮政非正式报关程序,国际邮件关税(IMD)工作表须于次月第7日前提交,且大多数邮政报关货物在IMD工作表提交之前即已自动放行。IMD工作表所要求的信息也不如用于ET11报关的CBP表格3461和7501那样详尽。大多数国际邮件关税工作表由报关行提交,而报关行无需说明进口商或收货人的身份。IMD工作表的这些局限性妨碍了CBP在邮政环境中执行与非邮政环境相同合规标准的能力。因此,CBP持续评估开发一种能够通过ACE系统提交、并具有更一致的提交时限和数据要求的邮件非正式货物报关程序,以应对邮件环境中的各种挑战。
To eliminate the disparity in duties between postal and non-postal informal entries, CBP published the Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for Mail Shipments and New Postal Informal Entry Process IFR on June 24, 2026, which amended the process for entering informal mail shipments, improving upon the interim process established by Executive Order 14324, as amended.[21] Throughout this analysis, CBP refers to the informal entry process for mail shipments established by that IFR as the “current informal postal entry process.” With that rule, the monthly international mail duty worksheet was expanded to require product classification and other data elements, and CBP began applying all applicable duties to postal informal shipments. Hence, postal informal entries and ET11 entries are now subject to the same product classification reporting requirements. But while the current postal informal entry process enables CBP to impose the same duties on postal shipments as on ET11 entries, enforcement of duties and other regulations remains more difficult in the postal environment. Under the current informal postal entry process, the international mail duty (IMD) worksheet is not due until the 7th day of the following month, and most postal entries are automatically released before the IMD worksheet is submitted. The IMD worksheet also does not require as much information as CBP Forms 3461 and 7501, which are used for ET11 entries. Most international mail duty worksheets are submitted by a broker, and the broker does not have to identify the importer or the consignee. The limitations of the IMD worksheet hamper CBP's ability to enforce the same standard of compliance with regulations in the postal environment as in the non-postal environment. Therefore, CBP has continued to evaluate development of an entry process for informal mail shipments that enables filing through ACE with more aligned filing timeframes and data requirements to address challenges in the mail environment.

此外,许多邮政货物在寄送时从未提交IMD工作表或其他报关表格。在2025年9月至2026年5月期间,仅有67%的邮政货物通过非正式或正式报关程序寄送。[22] 其余33%的邮政货物很可能是在未缴纳关税的情况下寄送的。此外,受其他要求和法规约束的货物,例如受合作政府机构(PGA)数据要求或贸易执法行动约束的货物,在经过短期延迟合规窗口后,不得使用现行邮政非正式报关程序,但CBP并不具备充分的能力来执行这些例外规定,因为相关数据是在货物抵达后很久才发送的,其中包括能够揭示该货物受这些要求和法规约束的数据。因此,如果CBP未能主动查获,一些货物可能通过现行邮政非正式报关程序进入,从而规避贸易执法行动或PGA数据要求。
Moreover, many postal shipments are sent without an IMD worksheet or other entry form ever being submitted for them. Between September 2025 and May 2026, only 67% of postal shipments were sent through an informal or formal entry process.[22] The remaining 33% of postal shipments were likely shipped without the payment of duties. Moreover, merchandise subject to other requirements and regulations, such as partner government agencies' (PGA) data requirements or trade enforcement actions is disallowed, after a short-term delayed compliance window, from using the current informal postal entry process, but CBP is not well positioned to enforce these exceptions, as the data is sent well after arrival, including data that would reveal that the merchandise is subject to these requirements and regulations. Therefore, some goods could enter under the current informal postal entry process and thereby avoid trade enforcement actions or PGA data requirements if not affirmatively caught by CBP.
在暂停最低限额(de minimis)待遇之前,邮政环境与非邮政环境在两个重要方面存在差异。第一个方面是从邮政运输货物中征收的关税收入要少得多,第二个方面是邮政运输货物的扣押率更高。如表2所示,在2024财年,邮政最低限额运输货物的扣押率远高于非邮政入境类型。美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)在邮政最低限额运输货物中查获麻醉品的比率为每百万件运输货物277.8次扣押,相比之下,正式报关货物的比率仅为每百万件运输货物2.6次扣押,非正式报关的第11类运输货物的比率为每百万件运输货物15.8次扣押。邮政最低限额运输货物在所有其他扣押类别中也具有最高的扣押率,包括健康与安全、假冒商品和违禁物品类别。邮政与非邮政扣押率之间的部分差异,可能是由于非邮政运输货物有时缺乏最终收件人姓名和地址,以及非邮政环境中自动化程度不足所致。大多数非邮政运输货物的进口商缺乏及时识别海关扣留的自动化能力,这使得进口商和CBP官员都难以将此类扣留货物与其余货物区分开来,从而给CBP增加了额外负担。尽管如此,CBP认为,邮政与非邮政扣押率之间的差异中,相当一部分是由于邮政运输货物中含有更多违规商品所致。邮政最低限额运输货物相对于非邮政最低限额环境含有更多违规商品的一个可能原因是,违规商品的出口商更倾向于选择邮政环境,因为他们认为在该环境中更容易逃避查获。
Before de minimis treatment was suspended, the postal environment differed from the non-postal environment in two significant ways. The first was that much less duty revenue was collected from postal shipments, and the second was that postal shipments had higher rates of seizures. As Table 2 shows, postal de minimis shipments had much higher seizure rates than non-postal entry types in FY 2024. CBP seized narcotics in postal de minimis shipments at a rate of 277.8 seizures per million shipments, compared to only 2.6 seizures per million shipments for formally entered shipments and 15.8 seizures per million shipments for informally entered type 11 shipments. Postal de minimis shipments had the highest seizure rates in all other seizure categories as well, including health and safety, counterfeit goods, and prohibited items. Some of the disparity between postal and non-postal seizure rates could be due to non-postal shipments sometimes lacking the final recipient name and address, as well as the lack of automation in the non-postal environment. Most importers of non-postal shipments lack the automation capacity to identify customs holds in a timely manner, which makes it difficult for both importers and CBP officers to separate such holds from the rest of cargo, thereby adding additional burden to CBP. Nevertheless, CBP believes that a fair share of the disparity between postal and non-postal seizure rates is due to postal shipments containing more violative merchandise. One likely reason that postal de minimis shipments contained more violative merchandise in relation to the non-postal de minimis environment is that the exporters of violative merchandise preferred the postal environment because they perceived it as one in which it was easier to avoid detection.
目前的非正式邮政入境程序并未充分解决CBP在努力识别违反美国海关和贸易法律、健康与安全要求、知识产权以及消费者保护规则的行为时所面临的数据限制问题。与第11类运输货物相比,CBP在用于锁定邮政非正式运输货物目标方面所获得的信息仍然少得多,而且提交给CBP的邮政运输货物信息往往比提交的第11类运输货物信息到达得更晚、可信度更低,这进一步削弱了CBP的目标锁定能力。
The current informal postal entry process does not fully address the data limitations that CBP faces in working to identify violations of U.S. customs and trade laws, health and safety requirements, intellectual property rights, and consumer protection rules. CBP still receives much less information to use in targeting postal informal shipments than ET11 shipments, and the information that is submitted to CBP for postal shipments tends to arrive later and be less trustworthy than the information submitted for ET11 shipments, weakening CBP's targeting abilities further.
CBP寻求以一种新的程序取代目前的非正式邮政入境程序,该新程序将使邮政入境申报承受与非邮政入境申报类似的要求,并且还可用于使CBP能够更好地识别受贸易执法行动或参与政府机构(PGA)数据要求约束的货物。为此,CBP针对邮政非正式运输货物制定了ET13程序。与ET11程序一样,拟议的ET13程序要求以电子方式提交入境申报,使用CBP表格3461和7501,而非使用IMD工作表。CBP制定了拟议ET13程序的简化版本,称为ET13测试,该测试将于2026年9月22日开始实施。[23] 参与ET13测试的申报人必须在自动化商业环境系统(ACE)中提交以下内容:(1)申报人代码;(2)记录进口商(IOR)编号;(3)货物描述;(4)原产国;(5)所有适用的10位数《协调关税表》(HTSUS)分类编码,包括第1章至第97章的主要分类编码以及第98章和/或第99章中任何适用的次要分类编码;(6)如使用从量税率,则需提供数量和重量;(7)税率;(8)价值;(9)应缴关税总额;(10)承运人名称;(11)外国邮政运营商生成的追踪编号;以及(12)抵达口岸。CBP表格3461和7501上出现的其他数据字段在ET13测试下仍为自愿填写。然而,根据本拟议规则,这些其他数据字段将成为强制性要求,与ET11入境申报的要求相同。ET13测试的目的是让CBP和贸易相关方有机会在本拟议规则使ET13成为邮政非正式运输货物的强制性要求之前, (印刷页第64542页) 对该新程序进行测试。
CBP seeks to replace the current informal postal entry process with a process that subjects postal entries to requirements similar to those applicable to non-postal entries and that can also be used to enable CBP to better identify goods that are subject to trade enforcement actions or PGA data requirements. To that end, CBP has developed the ET13 process for postal informal shipments. Like the ET11 process, the proposed ET13 process requires that entry be filed electronically using CBP Forms 3461 and 7501, not the IMD worksheet. CBP created a simplified version of the proposed ET13 process, referred to as the ET13 test, which will commence on September 22, 2026.[23] A filer participating in the ET13 test must submit in ACE the following: (1) filer code; (2) Importer of Record (IOR) number; (3) description of merchandise; (4) country of origin; (5) all applicable 10-digit HTSUS classification(s), including both primary classifications in Chapters 1-97 as well as any applicable secondary classifications in Chapters 98 and/or 99 of the HTSUS; (6) quantity and weight, if using specific duty rates; (7) duty rate; (8) value; (9) total duty owed; (10) carrier name; (11) tracking number generated by the foreign post operator; and (12) arrival port. Other data fields appearing on CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 remain voluntary under the ET13 test. Under this proposed rule, however, those other data fields would be required, just as they are for ET11 entries. The purpose of the ET13 test is to give CBP and trade members the opportunity to test out the new process before this proposed rule ( printed page 64542) makes ET13 required for postal informal shipments.
规则目的
Purpose of Rule
拟议规则旨在终止现行非正式邮政入境程序,以第13类入境类型取而代之,使邮政非正式货物运输适用与非邮政非正式货物运输类似的要求,从而改善美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)在邮政环境中的执法。此外,该规则还将对第11类入境类型(ET11)程序进行更新,这些更新也将适用于第13类入境类型(ET13)程序,以解决非正式环境中的执法问题,即非正式环境中违规货物的比率高于正式环境。
The proposed rule aims to terminate the current informal postal entry process and replace it with entry type 13 so that postal informal shipments will be subject to similar requirements as non-postal informal shipments, thereby improving CBP's enforcement in the postal environment. Furthermore, the rule would make updates to the ET11 process, which would also apply to the ET13 process, to address enforcement issues in the informal environment, namely the higher rate of violative goods in the informal environment than in the formal environment.
第11类非正式入境类型要求
Informal Entry Type 11 Requirements
为使CBP有时间妥善评估非正式入境货物的可入境性,本拟议规则拟将第11类入境类型的申报截止期限从货物抵达后15日内,提前至货物抵达入境口岸时或抵达之前。本拟议规则还将调整ET11的数据要素要求,增加最终交付方作为必填数据要素(如与最终收货人不同)。《美国协调关税表》(HTSUS)第99章第III和第IV分章项下归类、价值在2,500美元或以下的货物,将不再局限于正式入境程序,而可通过ET11入境程序办理入境。
To give CBP time to properly assess the admissibility of informal entries, this proposed rule would move the filing deadline for type 11 entries from within 15 days of arrival to upon, or prior to, arrival at a port of entry. This proposed rule would also adjust data element requirements for ET11 by adding the final deliver-to party as a required data element, if distinct from the ultimate consignee. Goods classified under Chapter 99, Subchapters III and IV, HTSUS, that are valued at or below $2,500 would no longer be limited to formal entry; they would be allowed to enter through the ET11 entry process.
新设第13类非正式邮件入境类型及舱单要求
New Informal Mail Entry Type 13 and Manifest Requirements
鉴于上文“背景”部分所述问题,CBP拟针对经由邮政渠道寄送、价值为2,500美元或以下的所有符合条件的货物运输,过渡至全新的全电子化第13类入境类型(ET13),即“非正式邮件入境”。拟对ET11程序作出的变更,例如提前的申报截止期限和担保金要求,也将适用于ET13程序。ET13程序的数据要求与更新后的ET11程序几乎相同,但为适应邮政环境,存在若干值得注意的差异。除ET11入境通常所需的数据要素外,ET13入境还必须包括发货人或寄件人以及包裹追踪号码。承运人还须在进口舱单上提交包裹追踪号码,以便CBP核实邮政货物运输的进口日期。对于未通过ET13妥善办理入境手续的物品,CBP将视为在15日期限届满时自愿放弃。
Considering the issues discussed in the Background section above, CBP is proposing to transition to the new, fully-electronic entry type 13 (ET13)—Informal Mail Entry, for all eligible shipments sent via the mail environment that are valued at $2,500 or less. The proposed changes being made to the ET11 process, such as the earlier filing deadline and bond requirement, would also apply to the ET13 process. The data requirements would be almost the same for the ET13 process as the updated ET11 process, but with a couple noteworthy differences to account for the mail environment. In addition to the data elements generally required for an ET11 entry, an ET13 entry must also include the shipper or sender and the package tracking number. The carrier would be also required to submit the package tracking number on the inward manifest to allow CBP to verify the date of importation for postal shipments. CBP would deem articles not properly entered through ET13 to be voluntarily abandoned at the expiration of a 15-day period.
在第14324号行政命令发布之前,根据《美国法典》第19编第1321(a)(2)(C)条主张适用行政性免税的邮政货物运输可免税入境。第14324号行政命令暂停了对大多数进口货物的 微量 待遇,设立了临时邮政程序,大多数非正式邮政入境货物,包括此前有资格享受免税 微量 待遇的货物,首次须缴纳关税。该临时邮政程序其后经《无限期暂停邮件运输微量免税待遇及新设邮政非正式入境程序临时最终规则》修订,要求在月度国际邮件关税工作表上提交HTSUS税则号,以便CBP征收所有应缴关税。[24] 本规则将终止现行非正式邮政入境程序,并要求所有非正式邮政货物运输办理ET13入境手续。由于申报人须在货物运输抵达时而非可能延迟数周后提交入境数据,新的ET13入境程序将使CBP能够更有效地执行针对邮政货物运输的法规。因此,CBP通过设立ET13,将保护美国政府的税收收入,并更有效地锁定违规货物运输目标。
Prior to Executive Order 14324, postal shipments claiming the administrative exemption under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C) were admitted duty-free. Executive Order 14324, which suspended de minimis treatment for most importations, established an interim postal process, and most informal postal entries, including those previously eligible for duty-free de minimis treatment, were subject to duty payments for the first time. The interim postal process was later amended by the Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for Mail Shipments and New Postal Informal Entry Process IFR to require the submission of HTSUS codes on the monthly international mail duty worksheet so that CBP could collect all applicable duties.[24] This rule would terminate the current informal postal entry process and require an ET13 entry for all informal postal shipments. Because filers would have to submit the entry data by the time the shipment arrives instead of potentially weeks later, the new ET13 entry process would enable CBP to more effectively enforce the regulations for postal shipments. Therefore, CBP would protect the revenue of the U.S. Government and more effectively target violative shipments by establishing ET13.
非正式入境担保金要求
Informal Entry Bond Requirements
CBP依赖申报人提供的数据来确定可入境性和入境要求,但众所周知,非正式入境的数据一直不可靠。为确保数据准确性,CBP拟根据拟议规则,对所有第11类和第13类入境要求提供基本进口及入境担保金。由于通过现行非正式邮政入境程序进口已须提供担保金,此项要求对ET11入境的影响将大于对邮政入境的影响。
CBP relies on data provided by filers to make determinations on admissibility and entry requirements, but data has been notoriously unreliable for informal entries. To ensure data accuracy, CBP would require basic importation and entry bonds under the proposed rule for all type 11 and type 13 entries. This requirement would affect ET11 entries more than postal entries, as a bond is already required to import through the current informal postal entry process.
该规则还拟对违反担保非正式报关单的海关保证金条款所产生的清算损害赔偿设定1,000美元的下限。因此,所有非正式报关单基于价值计算的清算损害赔偿金额将为1,000美元或该批货物的价值两者中的较高者,按每一事项计算。[25] 如果没有此下限,因低价值货运而导致的保证金违约仅能获得低额的清算损害赔偿。因此,这项拟议的下限将有助于提高合规性,并确保美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)征收清算损害赔偿的成本不会超过所征收的金额。
The rule also proposes to set a $1,000 floor for liquidated damages arising from a breach of the terms of a customs bond securing an informal entry. Hence, the liquidated damages based on value for all informal entries would be $1,000 or the value of the merchandise for each instance, whichever is greater.[25] Without the floor, breaches of the bond resulting from low-value shipments receive low-value liquidated damages. Therefore, this proposed floor would improve compliance and ensure that the cost to CBP to collect the liquidated damages does not outweigh the amount collected.
基准情景与监管替代情景
Baseline and Regulatory Alternative Scenarios
本监管影响分析通过比较基准情景与监管替代情景来估算该规则的净效应。在基准情景中,ET11报关流程尚未更新,邮政货运继续通过2026年6月实施的《无限期暂停邮件货运小额豁免及新邮政非正式报关流程临时最终规则》(IFR)所建立的非正式邮政报关流程进行报关。在基准情景中不存在ET13测试,因为现实中的ET13测试是CBP为预期实施本拟议规则而开发的。在监管替代情景中,ET11报关流程将包括上文所述的新要求,非正式邮政货运将需要通过新的ET13流程进行报关。若该规则最终定稿,现行的非正式邮政报关流程将不再可用。该规则的效益和成本即为从基准情景转变为监管替代情景所产生的效益和成本。由于CBP和美国邮政总局(USPS)自2026年起即开始承担该规则的编程成本,本分析考虑该规则在2026年至2035年这十年监管期内的影响。CBP在本分析的预测中使用的是日历年,而非财政年度。
This regulatory impact analysis estimates the net effects of the rule by comparing the baseline scenario with the regulatory alternative scenario. In the baseline, the ET11 entry process has not been updated, and postal shipments continue to be entered through the informal postal entry process that was implemented in June 2026 by the Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for Mail Shipments and New Postal Informal Entry Process IFR. The ET13 test does not exist in the baseline, as the real-world ET13 test was developed by CBP in anticipation of implementing this proposed rule. In the regulatory alternative scenario, the ET11 entry process would include new requirements, as described above, and informal postal shipments would need to be entered through the new ET13 process. The current informal postal entry process would no longer be available under the rule if finalized. The benefits and costs of the rule are the benefits and costs of changing from the baseline to the regulatory alternative scenario. Because CBP and USPS began incurring programming costs for the rule in 2026, this analysis considers the effects of the rule over a 10-year regulatory period from 2026 to 2035. CBP uses calendar years, rather than fiscal years, in its projections for this analysis.

美国邮政管理局(USPS)
USPS
该规则将在多个方面对USPS产生影响。USPS需要更新其电子系统以处理ET13入境申报,USPS估计这将产生1200万美元至2000万美元之间的初始编程成本。[29] CBP在计算中使用该区间的中点,即1600万美元,同时指出该估算存在不确定性。USPS预计这些编程变更在最短情况下需要4至6个月,在最长情况下可能需要长达8个月。在完成初始开发成本后,USPS预计每年将产生约100万美元的运营和维护成本。[30] 除编程成本外,USPS还将承担人工成本,以根据该规则更新其货运处理流程。
The rule would affect USPS in several ways. USPS would need to make updates to its electronic systems to handle ET13 entries, which USPS estimates would result in an initial programming cost of between $12 million and $20 million.[29] CBP uses the midpoint of this range, $16 million, for its calculations while noting the uncertainty around the estimate. USPS expects these programming changes to take 4 to 6 months on the low end and up to 8 months on the high end. After the initial development costs, USPS expects to incur operation and maintenance costs around $1 million per year.[30] Besides the programming costs, USPS would also incur labor costs to update its process for handling shipments under the rule.
邮政总局还可能遇到仓储能力问题。虽然基线邮政运输流程允许邮政运输件得到快速处理,但美国邮政总局(USPS)预计,根据本规则,平均滞留期将延长。因此,某一时间点滞留在USPS设施内的邮政入境件数量将增加,从而给USPS带来更高的人工和仓储成本。USPS的交换局(Office of Exchanges)设计初衷是用于流转处理,不具备仓储能力,USPS预计根据本规则,其最初将需要相当于进口量80%或以上的仓库容量。除了获取仓库空间的成本外,还会产生设计和实施存储与检索流程的额外成本。租赁费率为每平方英尺25美元至50美元不等,而场外仓储还会带来额外成本。[31]
USPS may also encounter storage capacity issues. While the baseline postal shipment process allows for postal shipments to be processed quickly, USPS expects that the average holding period would lengthen under the rule. As a result, a larger volume of postal entries would be held at USPS facilities at a given time, imposing larger labor and storage costs on USPS. USPS Office of Exchanges were designed for flow-through processing, without warehousing capacity, and USPS anticipates that it would need an initial warehouse capacity equivalent to 80% or more of the import volume under the rule. Besides the cost of sourcing warehouse space, there would be additional costs from designing and implementing the storage and retrieval processes. Lease rates range from $25/sq. ft to $50/sq. ft, though offsite storage would impose additional costs.[31]
根据本规则,USPS预计,由于未能正确办理入境手续,将有更多邮政运输件在15天后被放弃。退回无法投递货物的成本取决于许多变量,但USPS粗略估计,平均退货成本每公斤超过6.00美元。在本分析中,美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)纳入了USPS最初预估的编程成本,但CBP尚缺乏对本规则下仓储要求成本以及退回不合规邮政运输件所耗费资源的量化估算。CBP已对贸易界进行了广泛的外展宣传,以帮助其为本规则做好准备,并将继续开展此项工作。CBP预计,到最终规则生效之时,贸易界将已调整其流程,以尽可能减少退回无法投递邮件的成本。CBP就退回无法投递邮件的成本征求意见。
Under the rule, USPS expects that more postal shipments would be abandoned after 15 days due to not being properly entered. The cost of returning undeliverable goods depends on many variables, but USPS roughly estimates that the average cost of returns is greater than $6.00 USD per kilogram. In this analysis, CBP includes the initial estimated programming cost to USPS, but CBP lacks quantified estimates of the costs of the warehousing requirements and the resources spent returning non-compliant postal shipments under the rule. CBP has undergone extensive outreach to the trade to prepare them for this rule and will continue to do so. CBP expects that by the time the final rule becomes effective, the trade will have adjusted its processes to minimize the cost to return undeliverable mail to the extent possible. CBP requests comment on the cost to return undeliverable mail.
如上文CBP分节所述,根据本规则,检查次数可能会增加,但也不一定。如果邮政运输件的检查次数确实增加,那么CBP将要求USPS滞留更多此类运输件,以便CBP进行检查。滞留量的增加可能会给USPS带来进一步的成本,这是由于需要更大的仓库空间以及需要改变USPS流程以滞留更多运输件。
As discussed in the CBP subsection above, the number of inspections might increase under the rule, though not necessarily. If inspections of postal shipments do increase, then CBP would ask USPS to hold more of these shipments so that CBP can inspect them. An increase in holds could result in further costs to USPS, due to the greater need for warehouse space and the change in USPS processes needed to hold more shipments.
申报人
Filers
本小节描述本规则对办理入境申报的个人和企业所产生的成本。CBP预计,在基线情形和监管情形下,大多数入境申报都将由货主、购买人或收货人聘请的报关行办理。但是,货物的货主或购买人若愿意,也可以自行办理入境申报。
This subsection describes the costs of the rule to individuals and businesses that file entries. CBP expects that in both the baseline and regulatory scenario most entries would be filed by a broker hired by the owner, purchaser, or consignee. However, an owner or purchaser of a shipment is allowed to file entry themselves if they so choose.
更早的申报截止期限
Earlier Filing Deadline
根据本规则,申报人须遵守ET11入境申报的更早截止期限。在基线情形下,ET11入境申报必须在货物抵达后15天内办理。根据本规则,入境数据须在货物抵达时或抵达之前提交。CBP从2025年9月至11月期间随机抽取了392,473份ET11入境申报样本,并计算了货物抵达美国与入境申报提交日期之间的天数。[32] 仅有0.4% (第64546页) 的申报是在抵达当日之前提交的。然而,26.6%的申报是在抵达当日提交的,25.0%是在抵达次日提交的。因此,略超过一半的ET11入境申报仅需提前一两天提交,即可符合更早的截止期限要求。
Under this rule, filers would have to comply with an earlier filing deadline for ET11 entries. In the baseline, entry must be made for ET11 within 15 days of the shipment's arrival. Under the rule, the entry data would need to be submitted upon or prior to the shipment's arrival. CBP took a random sample of 392,473 ET11 entries from September to November of 2025 and calculated the number of days between the entries' arrival in the United States and their entry filing dates.[32] Only 0.4% ( printed page 64546) of entries were filed before the day of arrival. However, 26.6% of entries were filed the day of arrival, and 25.0% were filed the day after. Therefore, just over half of ET11 entries need to be submitted only a day or two earlier to conform with the earlier deadline.
至于较晚申报的入境单,海关及边境保护局(CBP)尚不清楚新截止日期将产生何种成本。根据CBP专业领域专家的意见,在大多数情况下,申报人在货物抵达前即可获得入境数据,因此申报人只需提前向CBP提交该数据即可。然而,在某些情况下,提前获取数据可能存在困难,申报人及其客户可能需要对其业务流程作出实质性调整,以遵守提前的申报截止日期。提前的截止日期甚至可能导致申报人需要在海外增加人员投入。如果申报人在基准情形下的标准做法是查看已抵达美国的货物包装标签,然后利用该信息提交入境数据,那么在本规则下该流程将不再适用。相反,申报人可能需要在海外雇用更多人员,以便在货物离开前往美国之前对其进行查验,从而能够在货物抵达前完成申报。尽管CBP无法量化这些成本,但承认这些成本可能相当可观,即便对于在基准情形下通常在货物抵达后不久即完成申报的申报人而言也是如此。CBP请求公众就拟议的提前申报截止日期所带来成本的大小提交意见。
As for the entries that are filed later, CBP does not know what the costs of the new deadline would be. According to CBP subject matter experts, most of the time, the entry data is available to filers before arrival, so filers simply need to submit the data to CBP earlier. However, there could be cases in which obtaining the data earlier is difficult, and the filers and their clients may need to adjust their business processes in meaningful ways to comply with the earlier filing deadline. The earlier deadline could even result in filers needing to invest in more staffing overseas. If a filer's standard procedure in the baseline is to read the package labeling of a shipment that has arrived in the United States and then use that information to submit entry data, then that process would no longer work under the rule. Instead, the filer may need to employ more people abroad so that they can look at the shipment before it leaves for the United States to be able to make entry before the shipment's arrival. While CBP is unable to quantify these costs, it acknowledges that they could be significant, even for filers who typically make entry shortly after arrival in the baseline. CBP requests comment from the public on the magnitude of the costs of the proposed earlier filing deadline.
本规则对运输时间的影响尚不明确。如果托运人认为运输时间不足以收集和提交所需数据,他们可能会选择推迟发货,例如将货物存放在原产国的仓库中,同时各方准备入境数据。这种前端仓储将增加总运输时间和成本。另一方面,提前的申报截止日期可能具有使货物更早放行从而更早交付的优势。然而,即便后一种效果占主导,这种变化所带来的任何收益也将小于提前申报入境所产生的成本。如果提前于或在货物抵达时申报入境对贸易相关方的收益超过其成本,那么申报人本可以在基准情形下已经选择这样做,因为他们一直拥有这一选择。尽管如此,这种效果可能会在一定程度上减轻提前提交数据所带来的成本。CBP请求就提前申报截止日期对运输时间的影响,以及该影响是否因运输方式不同而有所差异提交意见。
The rule would have an ambiguous effect on shipping times. If shippers do not believe that the transit time will be sufficient to gather and submit the necessary data, they could choose to delay shipment, perhaps by holding the goods in warehouses in the country of origin while the different parties prepare the entry data. This warehousing on the front end would increase the total shipping time and costs. On the other hand, the earlier filing deadline could have the advantage of causing the merchandise to be released sooner and therefore be delivered sooner. However, even if the latter effect prevails, any benefit from this change will be less than the cost of filing entry earlier. If the benefit to trade members exceeded the cost of filing entry upon or prior to arrival, then filers would have already chosen to do so in the baseline, as they have always had that option. Still, this effect may mitigate the costs of having to file data sooner. CBP requests comment on the impact of the earlier filing deadline on shipping times and whether it varies by mode of transport.
入境截止日期的变化也可能影响申报人需要多快完成入境汇总单的提交。入境汇总单的截止日期仍为入境放行后10天,与本规则实施前相同,但由于货物放行部分取决于入境申报的提交情况,部分取决于货物的抵达情况,因此提前的入境申报截止日期将意味着部分货物会更早放行。因此,在某些情况下,入境汇总单的提交期限将比基准情形下更早到期。如果需要更早提交入境汇总单会给申报人及其客户带来不便,这种影响将是 (印刷页第64547页) 本规则的另一项成本,不过CBP无法对其进行量化。
The change to the entry deadline could also affect how soon filers need to complete entry summary. The entry summary deadline would still be 10 days after release of the entry, just as before the rule, but since the release of merchandise is conditioned partly on the entry being filed and partly on the merchandise's arrival, the earlier deadline for filing entry would mean that some merchandise would be released sooner. As a result, entry summary would sometimes be due sooner than it is under the baseline. To the extent that having to submit entry summary sooner would be an inconvenience to filers and their clients, this effect would be another cost of the ( printed page 64547) rule, though one that CBP cannot quantify.
电子提交
Electronic Submission
本规则将要求ET11和ET13类型的入境单必须以电子方式提交,而非纸质方式提交,这将使CBP无需再将信息手动录入自动商业环境系统(ACE)。大多数非正式入境单已经通过电子方式提交,但仍有部分未采用电子方式。从2025年9月1日至2026年5月31日期间,在总计52,450,418份ET11入境单中,有73,534份是以人工方式提交的(占比0.14%)。[33] 正如“成本节约”部分所讨论的,以电子方式提交表格对申报人而言具有一定优势。然而,部分申报人选择以人工方式提交表格这一事实表明,电子提交并非其首选方式,CBP要求强制实行电子申报可能会给他们带来净成本。否则,这些申报人本应在本规则实施前就已转为电子申报方式,尽管也有可能仅仅是由于惯性原因,他们尚未转换为电子申报。CBP请求就此事项提交意见。
The rule would require ET11 and ET13 entries to be submitted electronically instead of on paper, which would let CBP avoid manually entering information into ACE. Most informal entries are already filed electronically, but some are not. From September 1, 2025, to May 31, 2026, 73,534 ET11 entries were submitted manually, out of 52,450,418 ET11 entries in total (or 0.14%).[33] As discussed in the Cost Savings section, there are some advantages to filers of submitting forms electronically. However, the fact that some filers choose to submit forms manually means that electronic submission is not their first choice and that CBP requiring electronic filing could result in a net cost to them. Otherwise, those filers would likely have already switched to electronic filing before the rule, though it is possible that inertia is the only reason they had not already converted to electronic filing. CBP requests comment on this matter.
附加数据要求
Additional Data Requirements
根据该规则,ET11报关单须额外包含一项数据要素,即最终收货方,该要素须包括最终收货方的名称和地址(如与最终收货人不同)。报关人须更新其业务流程,以便从其客户或其他实体处获取此名称和地址,连同通常的报关信息一并获取。美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)预计,过渡到这一新方法将产生一定成本,但一旦报关人完成向新流程的过渡,为每笔ET11报关单收集和提交数据所需的时间负担将保持不变。CBP预计,报关人将在向其客户索取并接收其余报关数据的同时,一并索取并接收这一新数据要素。因此,将最终收货方作为必填数据要素,对报关所需时间负担的影响可忽略不计。
Under the rule, ET11 entries would be required to include one additional data element, the final deliver-to party, which would include both the name and address of the final deliver-to party if distinct from the ultimate consignee. Filers would have to update their business practices to obtain this name and address from their clients or other entities, along with the usual entry information. CBP expects that there would be some costs of transitioning to this new method but that the time burden of gathering and submitting data for each ET11 entry would be unchanged once filers have transitioned to the new process. CBP expects that filers would request this new data element from their clients and receive it at the same time that they request and receive the rest of the entry data. Therefore, adding the final deliver-to party as a required data element would have a negligible effect on the time burden of making entry.
ET13报关单所需数据与ET11相同,此外还需另外两项数据要素:追踪号码以及发货人或寄件人。因此,ET13流程所需数据将多于基准情形下所采用的非正式邮政流程。例如,国际邮件关税工作表并不要求提交该工作表的报关经纪人identify货运中涉及的不同当事方,例如记录进口商或收货人。ET13报关单的报关人很可能从外国邮政运营商(FPO)处获取追踪号码,而外国邮政运营商通常已经向美国邮政总局(USPS)提交追踪号码。同样,CBP预计报关人在调整其运营以常规性获取追踪号码及发货人或寄件人信息方面将产生成本,但CBP假设ET13报关单的平均时间负担将与ET11报关单相同。
ET13 entries would require the same data as ET11, as well as two additional data elements: the tracking number and the shipper or sender. The ET13 process would therefore require more data than the informal postal process used in the baseline. For example, the international mail duty worksheet does not require brokers submitting the worksheet to identify the different parties involved in a shipment, such as the importer of record or consignee. The filers of ET13 entries would likely obtain the tracking numbers from the foreign postal operators (FPOs), which generally already submit the tracking numbers to USPS. Again, CBP expects that filers would incur costs in changing their operations to regularly obtain the tracking number and shipper or sender, but CBP assumes that the average time burden for ET13 entries would be the same as for ET11 entries.
报关人还需对其电子系统进行调整,以便能够传输ET13新流程所需的数据,以及该规则项下ET11所要求的新增数据要素。CBP目前没有数据可用以估算报关人因此类编程调整而产生的总成本,但指出,报关人及其软件提供商为遵守不断变化的要求,通常会定期进行系统更新,而此项变更很可能可与这些更新一并完成,从而产生的额外成本相对较低。新的数据要求还可能延长邮政货物的运送时间,因为外国邮政运营商可能要求客户在其接受包裹之前向报关人提供额外数据,从而延误发货日期。
Filers would also need to make changes to their electronic systems so that they can transmit data for the new ET13 process and the added data elements required for ET11 under the rule. CBP does not have data to estimate what the total costs of the programming changes would be for filers, but notes that filers and their software providers regularly make updates to comply with shifting requirements and this change could likely be done in conjunction with those changes at a relatively low additional cost. The new data requirements could also increase the time it takes to ship postal shipments, as the FPOs might require customers to provide the additional data to a filer before accepting the package, thereby delaying the shipping date.
进口商与寄件人
Importers and Senders
某些成本可能直接由进口商或寄件人承担,具体视情况而定。例如,进口商或寄件人均有可能是雇用报关经纪人办理报关的一方,而雇用经纪人的一方将支付经纪费用,尽管另一方最终也可能承担部分此类成本。因此,CBP将进口商所承担的成本与寄件人所承担的成本归并于本小节中一并讨论。
Certain costs may fall directly on the importer or on the sender, depending on the circumstances. For example, either the importer or the sender could be the one to hire a broker to file entry, and the party that hires the broker would pay the broker fees, though the other party could ultimately bear some share of those costs. Therefore, CBP groups the costs to importers and the costs to senders into this single subsection.
经纪费用与数据要求
Broker Fees and Data Requirements
由于该规则,邮政货物的进口商或寄件人将需要提交更多数据,并在报关行服务上花费更多费用。根据该规则,非正式邮政报关单须以ET13的形式申报,其数据要求与ET11类似。美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)假设,所有ET13报关单的进口商或寄件人都将转而聘请报关行,因为报关行具备确定产品归类及处理各种报关要求所需的专业知识。在基准情形下,大多数IMD工作表由报关行提交,而在监管情形下,这些IMD工作表将被ET13流程取代。ET13流程对报关行的要求比现行非正式邮政报关流程更高,因为在ET13流程下,报关行必须提交更多数据,数据必须更早提交,必须为每一笔报关单单独提交表格,而且CBP能够更严格地监控数据,要求申报人达到较高的准确性标准。因此,CBP假设报关行将向其客户收取远高于目前每月IMD工作表收费的单笔报关费用,以补偿其增加的工作量。对于每笔报关单平均报关行费用的增幅,CBP采用Fajgelbaum与Khandelwal(2024年)文献中[34]所列快递商业承运人报关费用估计值作为估算依据,即每票货物30美元。[35] 在2025年9月至2026年5月期间,价值不超过30美元的邮政货物按数量计占所有邮政货物的79%,但按价值计仅占16%。[36] 对于大额正式报关单,报关行费用可能高得多,但由于本规则仅影响非正式报关单(该类报关单货值必须为2,500美元或以下),CBP采用了较低的费用估算值。报关行费用上涨的根本原因在于邮政货物报关所需的劳动力增加,而正是通过报关行费用,这一成本才从实际从事相关工作的报关行转嫁给了报关行的客户。此外,邮政货物的进口商或寄件人还必须开始提供填写CBP第3461号表格(用于报关)和第7501号表格(用于报关汇总单)所需的额外数据。CBP第3461号表格的时间负担为10分钟,[37] CBP第7501号表格的时间负担为5分钟。CBP对进口商及寄件人平均时薪的估算为38.88美元。[38] (第64548页) 因此,提交CBP第3461号表格和第7501号表格的时间负担的平均成本分别为6.48美元和3.24美元。由此,就单笔ET13报关单而言,报关行费用与提交CBP第3461号表格及第7501号表格的时间负担成本合计为39.72美元。CBP将ET13报关单的平均报关行费用及时间负担成本,应用于表5中所列监管期内邮政货物的预计数量,以计算与新ET13流程相关的进口商年度总成本。表9列示了自2027年该规则生效之日起的年度成本。
As a result of the rule, the importer or sender of a postal shipment would have to submit more data and spend more on broker services. Under the rule, informal postal entries would need to be entered as ET13, which has similar data requirements as ET11. CBP assumes that all importers or senders of ET13 entries would resort to hiring a broker, since brokers have the expertise to determine product classification and handle various entry requirements. In the baseline, most IMD worksheets are submitted by brokers, and these IMD worksheets would be replaced by the ET13 process in the regulatory scenario. The ET13 process is more demanding for brokers than the current informal postal entry process because under the ET13 process the broker has to submit more data, the data must be submitted earlier, a form must be submitted for each individual entry, and CBP is able to more carefully monitor the data, holding the filers to a high standard of accuracy. As a result, CBP assumes that brokers would charge their customers a significantly higher fee per entry than what they charge for the monthly IMD worksheet as compensation for their higher effort. For the increase in the average broker fee per entry, CBP uses as its estimate the express commercial carriers' brokerage fee estimate found in Fajgelbaum and Khandelwal (2024),[34] which is $30 per shipment.[35] From September 2025 through May 2026, postal shipments with a value of less than or equal to $30 made up 79% of all postal shipments by volume but only 16% by value.[36] Brokerage fees can be much higher for large formal entries, but because this rule would only affect informal entries, which must be $2,500 or less, CBP uses a lower fee estimate. The ultimate cause of the increase in broker fees would be the increased labor necessary to make entry for postal shipments, and it is through the broker fees that this cost would be shifted from the brokers who are doing the work to the brokers' clients. Additionally, importers or senders of postal shipments would have to begin providing the additional data needed to fill out CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 for entry and entry summary, respectively. The time burden for CBP Form 3461 is 10 minutes,[37] and the burden for CBP Form 7501 is 5 minutes. CBP's estimate for the average hourly wage of importers and senders is $38.88.[38] The average ( printed page 64548) costs of the time burdens for submitting CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 are therefore $6.48 and $3.24. Therefore, the combined cost of the broker fee and the time burdens of submitting CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 would total $39.72 for an ET13 entry. 非正式报关所需的基本进口与报关保证金
Basic Importation and Entry Bond Required for Informal Entries
根据本规则，ET11和ET13类入境将需要提供基本进口和入境保证金。由于目前的非正式邮政入境程序已经要求提供保证金，美国海关与边境保护局（CBP）预计ET13类入境的保证金要求不会产生额外成本。另一方面，ET11类入境的保证金要求将是新增的。因此，这一变化将对ET11类入境的记录进口商产生成本，因为记录进口商是其保证金承担义务的一方。尽管目前并非所有ET11类入境都要求提供基本进口和入境保证金，但大多数ET11类入境已由保证金担保。从2025年8月1日至2026年7月31日，约有230万笔ET11类入境在没有保证金的情况下办理，[39] 这仅占CBP预计2026年ET11类入境总量的3.31%。保证金之所以已经普遍存在，是因为希望按月定期结算方式缴纳关税、税费的记录进口商需要提供连续保证金。
A basic importation and entry bond would be required under the rule for ET11 and ET13 entries. Because a bond is already required under the current informal postal entry process, CBP does not expect the bond requirement for ET13 entries to result in additional costs. The bond requirement for ET11 entries, on the other hand, would be new. This change would therefore result in costs for the importers of record of ET11 entries, as the importer of record is the party whose bond is obligated. Although a basic importation and entry bond is not currently required for all ET11s, most ET11 entries are secured by a bond. From August 1, 2025, to July 31, 2026, about 2.3 million ET11 entries were entered without a bond,[39] which is only 3.31% of CBP's projected annual ET11 entries for 2026. Bonds are already common because a continuous bond is required for IORs who want to pay duties, taxes, and fees on a periodic monthly statement.
记录进口商可以选择使用连续保证金或单次交易保证金。连续保证金可为长达一年期间内的所有入境提供担保，并自动续展为连续的一年期，而单次交易保证金只能用于单笔入境。对于使用连续保证金的进口商，CBP要求保证金金额至少为上一年度关税、税费总额的10%，若该进口商为新进口商，则为下一年度预计应缴关税、税费的10%。连续保证金金额还必须至少为50,000美元。单次交易保证金一般不得低于货物总申报价值加上任何应缴关税、税费的总额。在基线情形下，单次交易保证金必须至少为100美元。根据拟议规则，CBP可将下限提高至1,000美元，这将与本规则下的最低清算损害赔偿金额相同。
An importer of record can choose to use either a continuous bond or a single transaction bond. A continuous bond secures all entries during a period of up to one year and renews automatically for successive one-year periods, while a single transaction bond can only be used for a single entry. For importers using a continuous bond, CBP requires a bond equal to at least 10% of last year's duties, taxes, and fees or, if the importer is new, 10% of the anticipated duties, taxes, and fees to be paid in the upcoming year. The continuous bond must also be at least $50,000. A single transaction bond is generally an amount not less than the total entered value of the merchandise, plus any duties, taxes, and fees. In the baseline, a single transaction bond must be at least $100. Under the proposed rule, CBP could raise the floor to $1,000, which would equal the minimum liquidated damages under the rule.
保证金对进口商造成的成本并非保证金本身的金额，而是进口商向担保人支付的保证金保费。根据从12个经纪人网站收集的信息，50,000美元保证金的平均保费为441.52美元。因此，最低连续保证金的平均保费率为保证金金额的0.883%。CBP假设该比率同样适用于超过50,000美元的连续保证金。根据从六个经纪人网站收集的信息，经纪人对单次交易保证金收取的平均保费为每1,000美元4.917美元，最低保费为54.167美元。CBP在计算中假设每1,000美元4.917美元（即0.4917%）这一比率是连续适用的，而非按每1,000美元区间分段适用。由于存在54.167美元的最低保费，1,000美元的单次交易保证金与100美元的单次交易保证金所需保费相同。因此，无论本规则下单次交易保证金的下限维持在100美元还是提高至1,000美元，均不会对保证金要求的估计成本产生影响。 （印刷页第64549页）
The cost of a bond to an importer is not the amount of the bond but rather the bond premium that the importer would pay to the surety. Based on information gathered from 12 broker websites, the average premium for a $50,000 bond is $441.52. The average premium is therefore 0.883% of the bond amount for minimum continuous bonds. CBP assumes that this rate holds for continuous bonds over $50,000 as well. Based on information gathered from six broker websites, the average broker charges a premium of $4.917 per $1,000 for single transaction bonds, with a minimum premium of $54.167. CBP assumes for its calculations that the rate of $4.917 per $1,000, or 0.4917%, is applied continuously rather than at intervals of $1,000. Due to the minimum premium of $54.167, a single transaction bond of $1,000 has the same premium as one of $100. Hence, whether the floor for a single transaction bond stays at $100 or is raised to $1,000 under the rule has no effect on the estimated cost of the bond requirement. ( printed page 64549)

未申报的非正式邮政报关单
Unentered Informal Postal Entries
本规则将把ET13报关单的自愿放弃截止期限设定为15天期限届满之时，即邮政货运在到达后15天内未完成报关的，将被视为自愿放弃。该期限比ET11报关单的放弃期限(到达后6个月)更为严格。在基线流程下，邮政货运有30天的窗口期，在此期间美国邮政总署(USPS)将货件暂存以待领取，若未被领取，货件将被视为放弃或予以退回。目前，在此窗口期内未被领取的货件数量非常少。然而，在本规则下，USPS预计将有大量货运因在新流程下未为其提交报关单而在15天后被视为放弃。因此，在本规则下，由于15天自愿 (印刷页64550) 放弃期限叠加更为严苛的报关流程，部分进口商可能无法收到其货运。
The rule would set the voluntary abandonment deadline for ET13 entries at the expiration of a 15-day period, meaning that postal shipments that are unentered 15 days after arrival would be deemed to be voluntarily abandoned. This deadline is stricter than the abandonment deadline for ET11 entries, which is 6 months after arrival. Postal shipments under the baseline process have a 30-day window for which USPS holds items for pickup, and if not picked up, the items are abandoned or returned. Currently, only a very small volume of items are not picked up during this window. Under the rule, however, USPS anticipates a significant number of shipments would be deemed abandoned after 15 days because no entry would be filed for them under the new process. Hence, under the rule some importers may not receive their shipments due to the 15-day voluntary ( printed page 64550) abandonment deadline combined with the more demanding entry process.

无谓损失
Deadweight Loss
尽管本分析在计算中假设进口数量不会因本规则而受到影响，但邮政货运完税率的提高(将在“转移”部分进一步讨论)以及本规则对邮政报关单和ET11报关单造成的各种成本，很可能导致进口商减少进口。这种进口减少将对应一种以无谓损失形式表现的社会成本。尽管在本规则下部分货物将不再被进口，但这些货物在基线情形下被进口这一事实表明，这些进口对进口商的收益超过了生产这些货物、运输这些货物以及遵守基线报关流程所需的成本。如果进口商因本规则而决定不再进口其中部分货运，社会将失去本应从这些货运中获得的贸易净收益。本规则造成的无谓损失即为这部分损失剩余的总价值。尽管CBP在本分析中缺乏估算无谓损失所需的数据，但这可能是本规则的一项重要成本。此外，若本规则导致进口减少从而产生无谓损失，则政府收入、保证金保费、报关行费用支付以及CBP在此估算的任何其他按单位或按价值计算的成本的增加幅度也将相应减小，因为届时货运数量将会减少。
Although this analysis assumes in its calculations that the quantity of imports would be unaffected by the rule, it is likely that the improved duty collection rate for postal shipments (discussed further in the Transfers section) and the various costs for postal and ET11 entries resulting from the rule would cause importers to import less. This decrease in imports would correspond to a cost to society in the form of deadweight loss. While some of these goods would not be imported under the rule, the fact that they would be imported in the baseline means that the benefit of those imports to importers exceeds the cost of producing those goods, shipping them, and complying with the baseline entry process. Were importers to decide not to import some of those shipments as a result of the rule, society would be missing out on the net gains from trade that would have occurred with those shipments. The deadweight loss from the rule is the total value of this lost surplus. While CBP lacks the data to estimate deadweight loss in this analysis, this could be an important cost of the rule. Also, to the extent that there would be deadweight loss under the rule due to a decrease in imports, there would be a smaller increase in government revenue, bond premiums, broker fee payments, and any other per-unit or per-value costs that CBP estimates here, as there would be fewer shipments.
尽管CBP无法估算本规则造成的无谓损失，也无法估算进口数量变化将如何影响其他成本估算，但有一篇研究论文的结果可用于近似估算在考虑无谓损失的情况下消费者所承担成本的大小。在Fajgelbaum和Khandelwal（2024年）（在此简称为F&K）的研究中，作者估算了如果在2021年终止小额豁免（de minimis exemption），本应给消费者福利造成的损失，其中包括无谓损失。[43] 在该论文的模型中，终止小额豁免将导致更多商品需缴纳关税，消费者需向物流公司支付“行政费”（类似于本分析中的报关代理费），据其部分计算估计该费用为30美元。 （第64551页） 与F&K不同，本分析所考虑的监管行动并非暂停小额豁免（该豁免已被暂停），而是以ET13流程取代现行的非正式邮政处理流程，这将导致对非正式邮政货运提交入境单证信息需支付估计30美元的报关代理费，以及总价值为9.72美元的时间负担，同时对于在基线情形下不合规、但在本规则下合规的邮政货运，还将增加关税缴纳额。因此，F&K所考虑的监管行动与本文提出的监管变化并不相同。然而，两者之间存在相似之处。两种变化都涉及每票货物新增30美元费用，且都适用于低价值货运。2021年小额豁免货运的平均价值为56.38美元，而2025年9月至2026年5月期间邮政货运的平均价值为48.29美元。非正式邮政入境新增时间负担的价值为9.72美元，占邮政货运平均价值的20%，这与F&K根据CBP数据所采用的平均关税税率接近，F&K表3显示，平均关税税率范围从最贫困邮政编码地区的约23%到最富裕邮政编码地区的约16%不等。除了具体成本增加幅度略有不同外，F&K与本分析之间的关键区别在于，本规则制定中的监管变化仅适用于邮政货运，而且F&K中的关税收入将返还给消费者，而提交CBP 3461表和7501表所产生的时间负担成本则属于纯粹损失。尽管如此，所考虑的这两项监管行动之间的相似性意味着，F&K关于消费者福利损失的研究结果可为评估本规则制定对消费者福利造成损失的规模提供参考信息。
While CBP cannot estimate the deadweight loss from the rule or how the change in import quantity would affect the other cost estimates, the results from one research paper could be used to approximate the magnitude of the cost to consumers when factoring in deadweight loss. In Fajgelbaum and Khandelwal (2024) (denoted here by F&K) the authors estimate the loss to consumer welfare, including deadweight loss, that would have occurred in 2021 had the de minimis exemption been terminated that year.[43] In the paper's model, ending de minimis would have resulted in more goods being subject to duties and in consumers paying an “administrative fee” to a logistics company (similar to broker fees in this analysis), with the fee ( printed page 64551) estimated at $30 for some of their calculations. Unlike in F&K, the regulatory action considered in this analysis is not the suspension of de minimis, which has already been suspended, but the replacement of the current informal postal process with the ET13 process, which would result in an estimated $30 broker fee and a time burden with a total value of $9.72 to submit entry form information for informal postal shipments, along with an increase in duty payments for the postal shipments that are noncompliant in the baseline but compliant under the rule. The regulatory action considered in F&K is therefore not the same as the regulatory changes proposed here. However, there are similarities. Both changes would involve a new per-shipment fee of $30, and both would apply to low-value shipments. The average value of a de minimis shipment in 2021 was $56.38, while the average value of postal shipments from September 2025 to May 2026 was $48.29. The value of the new time burden for informal postal entries, $9.72, is 20% of the average postal values, which is close to the average duty rate used by F&K based on CBP data, with Table 3 in F&K showing the average duty rate range from about 23% for the poorest zip codes to about 16% for the richest zip codes. Besides the precise cost increases being slightly different, the key differences between F&K and this analysis are that the regulatory changes in this rulemaking would only apply to postal shipments and that the duty revenue in F&K would be rebated back to consumers, whereas the cost of the time burden to submit CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 would be a pure loss. Still, the similarities between the two regulatory actions considered mean that F&K results about consumer welfare loss could be informative for assessing the scale of consumer welfare loss from this rulemaking.
根据CBP的进口数据和估计为30美元的行政费用,F&K估计,如果在2021年终止小额豁免(de minimis),当年的消费者福利损失将等于208亿美元。该年小额豁免进口货物的价值为435亿美元,这意味着估计的消费者福利损失占小额豁免货物基准消费支出的47.8%。CBP在此将该47.8%的比例应用于非正式邮政进口。从2025年9月至2026年5月,非正式邮政货运的价值为1,179,693,422美元。按此比率计算,2026年的进口总价值将为1,577,245,784美元。应用在“预计入境数量”部分讨论的−11.63%的复合年增长率(CAGR),CBP估计基准情形下2027年非正式邮政进口的价值将等于1,392,823,929美元。如果此处的相对消费者福利损失与F&K对2021年终止小额豁免的估计相同,那么将邮政非正式进口价值乘以47.8%,2027年的损失将等于6.66亿美元。需要明确的是,这6.66亿美元的消费者福利损失并非应加到2027年估计给进口商和寄件人造成的12亿美元成本之上的额外成本。相反,它是对该群体总成本的另一种估计。虽然由于本规则制定与F&K所考虑的监管行动之间存在差异,这6.66亿美元的数字过于不准确而无法用于本分析,但确实,一个将进口减少所致无谓损失纳入考虑的成本估计,其总成本估计结果应低于本规则制定中所估计的结果。这是因为CBP在本分析中假设该规则不会影响进口数量。如果该规则导致进口减少,规则的总成本将低于CBP的估计值,因为进口减少意味着支付的经纪费用减少,以及花在提交CBP 3461表和7501表上的时间减少。由进口减少所产生的无谓损失不会大到足以抵消这些影响,否则进口商一开始就不会选择减少进口。
Using CBP import data and an estimated administrative fee of $30, F&K estimate that the consumer welfare losses of ending de minimis in 2021 would have equaled $20.8 billion that year. The value of de minimis imports that year was $43.5 billion, meaning that the estimated consumer welfare losses were 47.8% of baseline consumer spending on the de minimis goods. CBP applies that 47.8% figure to informal postal imports here. From September 2025 to May 2026, the value of informal postal shipments was $1,179,693,422. At that rate, the total value imported in 2026 would be $1,577,245,784. Applying the CAGR of −11.63% discussed in the Projected Entry Counts section, CBP estimates that the value of informal postal imports in 2027 in the baseline would equal $1,392,823,929. If the relative consumer welfare losses were the same here as in F&K's estimate for ending de minimis in 2021, then, multiplying the value of postal informal imports by 47.8%, the losses would equal $666 million in 2027. To be clear, this consumer welfare loss of $666 million is not an additional cost that should be added to the estimated $1.2 billion cost to importers and senders in 2027. Rather, it is an alternative estimate of the total cost to that group. And while the $666 million is too inaccurate to use in this analysis due to the differences between this rulemaking and the regulatory action considered in F&K, it is true that a cost estimate that factors in deadweight loss from decreased imports should produce a total cost estimate less than the one estimated in this rulemaking. That is because CBP assumes in this analysis that the rule would not affect import quantity. To the extent that imports would fall as a result of the rule, the total cost of the rule would be lower than CBP's estimate because fewer imports would mean less money spent on broker fees and less time spent submitting CBP Forms 3461 and 7501. The resulting deadweight loss from decreased imports would not be large enough to outweigh those effects, or else importers would not choose to import less in the first place.

效益
Benefits
本规则将有助于防止非法进口,加强贸易执法,并改善贸易合规。这些益处将源于美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)在识别违规货运方面的改进,以及贸易成员在遵守法规方面的提高。由于CBP将接收到更多的数据,且入境数据将更早到达并更加可靠,CBP将能够更准确、更高效地识别违规货运。在当前的非正式邮政入境程序下,进口商无需像提交ET11入境单那样向CBP提供大量信息。根据新的适用于邮政货运的ET13程序,进口商将必须提供与ET11入境单相同的信息,以及邮政追踪号码和托运人或寄件人信息。除其他数据要素外,CBP还将收到有关参与邮政货运的各方的信息,这将有助于识别高风险货运。对于ET11入境单,如果最终收货方与最终收货人不同,进口商也将开始提供最终收货方信息。由于新的入境申报截止时间将是货物抵达之时,而非抵达后15天,CBP将更早收到入境数据,这也将有助于识别违规货运。
This rule would help prevent unlawful importations, increase trade enforcement, and improve trade compliance. These benefits would result from the improvements in CBP's targeting of violative shipments and in trade members' increased compliance with regulations. CBP would be able to target violative shipments more accurately and efficiently because CBP would receive more data and because entry data would arrive sooner and be more reliable. Under the current informal postal entry process, importers do not have to provide as much information to CBP as they would have to if they had been filing ET11 entries. Under the new ET13 process for postal shipments, importers would have to provide the same information as for ET11 entries, as well as the postal tracking number and the shipper or sender. Among other data elements, CBP would receive information regarding the various parties involved with the postal shipment, which would be helpful in targeting higher risk shipments. Importers would also start providing the final deliver-to party for ET11 entries if distinct from the ultimate consignee. Because the new entry filing deadline would be the time of arrival rather than 15 days after arrival, CBP would receive the entry data sooner, which would also help with targeting.
CBP认为,由于对非正式入境新增了担保金要求,以及对非正式入境设定了1,000美元的清算损害赔偿最低金额,本规则将使入境数据更加可靠。过去,对于未以担保金担保的非正式入境,当CBP发现申报人提交的数据与货运的实际内容存在差异时,例如分类错误或低报价值,CBP无法就违反担保金义务的行为评估清算损害赔偿。随着新增担保金要求的实施,清算损害赔偿将对违反担保金义务的行为施加财务后果,因此CBP预计入境数据将更加可靠。清算损害赔偿通常是根据商品价值计算的,这意味着当商品价值较低时,赔偿金额也较小。随着对非正式入境新设定的1,000美元清算损害赔偿最低金额,低价值货运对入境法规的遵守情况可能会有所改善。
CBP believes entry data would become more reliable under the rule due to the new bond requirement for informal entries and the new minimum liquidated damages amount of $1,000 for informal entries. In the past, for informal entries not secured by a bond, when CBP discovered a discrepancy between the data submitted by the filer and the actual contents of the shipment, such as misclassification or undervaluation, CBP was unable to assess liquidated damages for the breach of the bond. With the added bond requirement, liquidated damages would impose a financial consequence for breaching the bond, so CBP expects that the entry data would be more reliable. Liquidated damages are often calculated based on the value of the merchandise, which means that the damages are small when the merchandise has a low value. With the new minimum liquidated damages amount of $1,000 for informal entries, compliance with entry regulations could improve for low-value shipments.
由于ET13入境单将提交目前非正式邮政入境程序中所不要求的所有新数据,预计本规则带来的最大安全改进将出现在邮政环境中。承运人将提供与经纪人在ET13入境表格上提供的相同的邮政货运追踪号码。通过将承运人在舱单上提供的追踪号码与申报人为该入境单提供的追踪号码进行匹配,CBP将能够将入境信息与舱单信息相关联,并知晓不同的邮政货运何时抵达美国。
Because of all the new data that would be submitted for ET13 entries that is not required in the current informal postal entry process, the largest security improvements under the rule are expected to occur in the postal environment. Carriers would provide the same tracking numbers for postal shipments that the brokers would be providing on the ET13 entry forms. By matching the tracking number provided by the carrier on the manifest to the tracking number provided by the filer for the entry, CBP would be able to connect the entry information to the manifest information and know when different postal shipments arrived in the United States.
邮政环境是一个值得重点关注的领域,因为邮政货运的扣押率远高于其他入境类型。根据2024财年的数据,表15显示了邮政小额豁免、非邮政小额豁免、ET11和ET01货运每百万份入境单的扣押数量。邮政小额豁免的总扣押率比扣押率次高的ET11高出6.6倍。特别是,邮政小额豁免入境单的毒品扣押率比ET11高出17.6倍。非邮政小额豁免入境单的毒品扣押率与ET11相近,但许多非邮政小额豁免入境单属于ET86入境单,其数据要求与ET11入境单相似。CBP认为邮政扣押率之所以偏高,是因为不法分子知道邮件的数据要求较低,这限制了CBP识别应检查哪些包裹的能力。根据本规则,CBP将获得比基准情形下多得多的邮政货运数据,并将更早获得这些数据。通过要求提供与ET11历来要求相同的数据要素并增加新的数据要素,CBP将获得有效识别毒品和其他违禁物品所需的信息。同样,CBP认为担保金要求和更早的申报截止时间将有助于CBP在邮政和ET11环境中识别毒品和其他非法货物。通过弥合邮政货运中的数据缺口, (第64553页打印版) 本规则将显著改善对毒品及其他违规商品的查缉成效。
The postal environment is a useful one to target, as postal shipments have a much higher seizure rate than other entry types. Based on data from FY 2024, Table 15 shows the number of seizures per million entries for postal de minimis, non-postal de minimis, ET11, and ET01 shipments. The total seizure rate is 6.6 times higher for postal de minimis than ET11 shipments, which has the next highest seizure rate. The narcotics seizure rate in particular is 17.6 times higher for postal de minimis entries than for ET11. Non-postal de minimis entries had a narcotics seizure rate similar to that of ET11, but many of the non-postal de minimis entries were ET86 entries, which had similar data requirements as ET11 entries. CBP believes the postal seizure rates are high because bad actors knew that mail had lower data requirements, which limited CBP's ability to identify which packages to search. Under the rule, CBP would receive much more data for postal shipments than it did in the baseline and would receive this data sooner. By requiring the same data elements as ET11 historically did and by adding new data elements, CBP would have the information needed to effectively target for narcotics and other prohibited items. Similarly, CBP believes that the bond requirements and the earlier filing deadlines would aid CBP in targeting narcotics and other illicit goods in both the postal and ET11 environments. By closing the data gap in postal ( printed page 64553) shipments, this rule would lead to a significant improvement in the interdiction of drugs and other violative merchandise.
该规则还将迫使更多邮政货物的寄件人和进口商遵守CBP(美国海关与边境保护局)的报关要求。目前,在已提交表格的邮政货物中,几乎全部是通过非正式邮政程序,使用IMD工作表办理报关,而通过正式报关方式办理的邮政货物数量微乎其微。从2025年9月至2026年5月,共有2440万件邮政货物发往美国,但仅有1640万件邮政货物(占67%)通过非正式或正式报关程序办理了报关手续。如果邮政货物符合19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(A)项下作为礼品的资格,并有资格享受 微量 礼品豁免,则无需通过现行非正式邮政报关程序或正式报关办理报关手续,该豁免将使其免于数据录入要求。然而,CBP认为,符合礼品资格的邮政货物比例远低于33%,并且大多数未提交报关数据的邮政货物并未遵守现行法规。CBP自动放行大多数邮政货物,并期望该货物此后会在IMD工作表上进行申报,但寄件人或进口商并不总是这样做。但根据该规则,报关数据必须在货物抵达前提交,承运人必须在舱单上提交追踪号码,而该追踪号码已是报关表格中的必填项。因此,CBP可以利用舱单上的追踪号码来核查该邮政货物是否已办理报关手续,并可以拒绝自动放行尚未办理报关手续的货物。CBP随后还可以要求某些符合礼品资格的邮政货物,只有在官员审核货物舱单后才能以人工方式放行。这些变化将导致提交报关数据的邮政货物比例大幅提高,从而进一步提升CBP甄别违规货物的能力。
The rule would also force more senders and importers of postal shipments to comply with CBP's entry requirements. Currently, among the postal shipments that have forms submitted, almost all are entered through the informal postal process, using the IMD worksheet, while a negligible number of postal shipments are entered through formal entry. From September 2025 to May 2026, 24.4 million postal shipments were sent to the United States, but only 16.4 million postal shipments, or 67%, were entered through an informal or formal entry process. Postal shipments do not have to be entered through the current informal postal entry process or formal entry if they qualify as gifts and are eligible for the de minimis gift exemption under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(A), which would exempt them from data entry requirements. However, CBP believes that the share of postal shipments that qualify as gifts is much lower than 33% and that most of the postal shipments sent without entry data were not complying with the existing regulations. CBP automatically releases most postal shipments with the expectation that the shipment will later be reported on an IMD worksheet, but the senders or importers do not always do so. Under the rule, however, entry data would have to be submitted prior to arrival, and carriers would have to submit the tracking number on the manifest, which is already required on the entry forms. Thus, CBP could use the tracking number on the manifest to check whether entry has been made for that postal shipment, and CBP could refuse to automatically release shipments that have not yet made entry. CBP could then also require that some postal shipments qualifying as gifts only be released manually after an officer reviews the shipment manifest. These changes would lead to a much higher share of postal shipments submitting entry data to CBP, which would further improve CBP's ability to screen for violative shipments.

该规则将使CBP的进口流程更加高效。ET11报关单及新增的ET13报关单均须以电子方式提交。在基线情景下,除快递承运人外,以人工方式提交报关表格的申报人需缴纳更高费用,因为这会增加CBP的工作负担。部分申报人愿意支付该费用并以人工方式提交报关单,这一事实表明,该规则为CBP带来的负担减轻程度将小于申报人为此承担的成本。因此,CBP未将非快递承运人申报人转为电子提交所带来的成本节约计入其中,CBP预计在基线情景下,此类申报人每年将以人工方式提交201份报关单。[44] 至于快递承运人,CBP对其收取的费用无论是以人工方式还是以电子方式提交报关表格均相同,尽管人工报关单的处理给CBP带来的额外负担可能与其他申报人的情形相同。因此,快递承运人并未将其给CBP带来的人工报关处理额外成本内部化,因此这些承运人选择以人工方式提交表格,并不意味着人工提交对其而言的便利性大于CBP所承担的成本。因此,CBP将快递承运人转为电子提交后给CBP带来的负担减轻计为该规则的成本节约。
This rule would make the importation process more efficient for CBP. ET11 entries and the new ET13 entries would have to be submitted electronically. In the baseline, filers (except for express carriers) who submit entry forms manually are charged a higher fee because of the increased burden for CBP. The fact that some filers are willing to pay the fee and submit entry manually means that the burden reduction for CBP under the rule would be less than the cost to the filers. Hence, CBP does not count the cost savings from the shift to electronic submissions by non-express carrier filers, who CBP projects would submit 201 entries manually per year in the baseline.[44] As for express carriers, CBP charges them the same fee regardless of whether they submitted the forms manually or electronically, even though the added burden to CBP of handling manual entries is probably the same as with other filers. Hence, the express carriers do not internalize the added cost to CBP of processing manual entries, and therefore those carriers' decision to submit forms manually does not mean that the convenience of manual submission to them is greater than the cost to CBP. Therefore, CBP counts the burden reduction to CBP of express carriers switching to electronic submission as cost savings of the rule.
自2025年9月1日至2026年5月31日,快递承运人就ET11申报单提交了73,384份人工申报。[45] 按此比率计算,快递承运人每年将提交98,114份人工ET11申报单。美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)假定人工ET11申报单的增长率与基线中所有ET11申报单的增长率相同,预计为3.14%,具体讨论见“预计申报数量”一节。CBP对非快递承运人收取人工提交费用8.06美元,电子提交费用2.69美元。[46] CBP将这两项费用之差5.37美元作为CBP处理人工申报额外成本的替代指标。将5.37美元乘以预计每年以人工方式提交的ET11申报单数量,CBP计算出快递承运人改用电子提交方式为CBP带来的成本节约,详见表16。该成本节约将自2027年该规则生效之时开始产生。

要求以电子方式而非人工方式提交ET11申报单,也将为非快递承运人的申报人带来一定的时间节省。美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)估计,与人工提交相比,以电子方式提交CBP表格3461[47] 的时间负担减少5分钟,提交CBP表格7501[48] 的时间负担减少10分钟。此外,电子提交所需的CBP处理时间较少,这一点可从电子提交的商品处理费(MPF)低于人工提交的商品处理费中得到印证。然而,此类申报人目前已经可以选择以电子方式提交。事实上,尽管电子提交能够节省时间,仍有部分申报人选择以人工方式提交,这意味着对这些申报人而言,转为电子提交存在隐性成本,且该隐性成本甚至高于其所节省的成本。因此,CBP不会将因要求电子提交而产生的成本节省计入本规则的成本节省之中。总体节省将来自以下事实:与人工提交相比,以电子方式提交CBP表格3461的时间负担减少5分钟,提交CBP表格7501的时间负担减少10分钟;此外,电子提交所需的CBP处理时间较少,这一点可从电子提交的商品处理费比人工提交低5.37美元中得到印证。电子提交的商品处理费与人工提交的商品处理费之间的这一差异目前已经存在,本规则不会影响CBP对提交内容适用商品处理费的方式。
The requirement to submit ET11 entries electronically instead of manually would also result in some time savings for filers who are not express carriers. CBP estimates that the time burden to submit forms electronically is 5 minutes lower for CBP Form 3461 [47] and 10 minutes lower for CBP Form 7501 [48] compared to manual submission. Moreover, electronic submissions require less time to handle on CBP's part, as indicated by the merchandise processing fee (MPF) being lower for electronic submissions than for manual submissions. However, such filers already have the option to file electronically. The fact that some choose to file manually despite the time savings of electronic submission means there are hidden costs to these filers of switching to electronic submission that are even higher than the cost savings. Hence, CBP does not count the cost savings that would result from requiring electronic submission as part of the cost savings of this rule. Gross savings would result from the fact that the time burden to submit forms electronically is 5 minutes lower for CBP Form 3461 and 10 minutes lower for CBP Form 7501 compared to manual submission, as well as the fact that electronic submissions require less time to handle on CBP's part, as indicated by the MPF being $5.37 lower for electronic submissions than for manual submissions. This difference between the MPF for electronic submissions and the MPF for manual submissions already exists, and the rule would not affect how CBP applies the MPF to submissions.
国际邮件关税计算表
International Mail Duty Worksheet
活跃于邮政环境中的经纪人、货主和购买人将不再需要像目前非正式邮政入境程序所要求的那样,每月向美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)提交一份IMD工作表,这一变化将带来一定的时间节省。[49] 在现行非正式邮政入境程序下,货主、购买人或经纪人每月必须向CBP报告该月进口货物的价值、原产国和产品分类等数据项,并缴纳相应的应付关税。CBP估计,在基线情形下,每月将提交约100份IMD工作表,每份工作表填写需耗时6小时。[50] 工作表提交数量远低于邮政入境数量,原因在于一名经纪人可以为多个客户提交一份IMD工作表。尽管CBP预计在整个监管期内邮政货运量将下降,但其假设在基线情形下,每年IMD工作表提交的数量将保持不变。因此,在基线情形下,每年将花费7,200小时用于提交该工作表。大多数IMD工作表的提交人为经纪人,其平均时薪为38.88美元。[51] 按此时薪计算, (印刷页第64555页) 提交人所承担的时间成本价值为每年279,936美元。由于该规则将要求非正式邮政货运以ET13条目方式申报,IMD工作表将不再是征收邮政关税所必需的文件,提交人将停止提交该表。因此,根据该规则,IMD工作表的提交人每年将节省279,936美元的时间成本。正如经纪人及其他提交人将不再需要提交IMD工作表一样,CBP也将不再需要处理IMD工作表。CBP估计处理一份IMD工作表需耗时2小时。[52] CBP贸易与税收员工的平均时薪为95.34美元。[53] 由于基线情形下每月有100份IMD工作表提交,CBP估计在基线情形下,每年处理IMD工作表给CBP带来的负担成本为228,816美元,即该规则下CBP每年可节省的成本。
Brokers, owners, and purchasers who are active in the postal environment would no longer have to submit a monthly IMD worksheet to CBP, as is required for the current informal postal entry process, and this change would yield some time savings.[49] Under the current informal postal entry process, each month, the owner, purchaser, or broker must report to CBP, among other data elements, the value, country of origin, and product classification of that month's imports and pay the corresponding duties owed. CBP estimates that, in the baseline, about 100 IMD worksheets will be submitted each month, with the worksheet taking 6 hours to complete.[50] The number of worksheet submissions is much lower than the number of postal entries because a broker can submit one IMD worksheet for multiple clients. Although CBP expects the number of postal shipments to fall throughout the regulatory period, it assumes that the annual number of IMD worksheet submissions would remain constant in the baseline scenario. Therefore, 7,200 hours would be spent each year submitting the worksheet in the baseline. Most filers of IMD worksheets are brokers, who have an average hourly wage of $38.88.[51] At that wage, the ( printed page 64555) value of the time burden to the filers would be $279,936 per year. Because the rule would require informal postal shipments to be entered as ET13 entries, the IMD worksheet would no longer be necessary for the collection of postal duties, and filers would stop submitting it. Filers of IMD worksheets would therefore experience time savings of $279,936 per year under the rule. Just as brokers and other filers would no longer have to submit the IMD worksheet, CBP would no longer have to process the IMD worksheet. CBP estimates that processing an IMD worksheet takes 2 hours.[52] The average hourly wage of a CBP trade and revenue employee is $95.34.[53] As there would be 100 IMD worksheet submissions per month in the baseline, CBP estimates $228,816 to be the annual burden to CBP of processing IMD worksheets in the baseline or the annual cost savings to CBP under the rule.

转移支付
Transfers
如上文“效益”部分所述,CBP预计该规则将促使邮政环境中的入境要求合规性提高。从2025年9月至2026年5月,仅有67.02%的邮政货运通过非正式或正式入境程序完成申报,且已提交入境表格的邮政货运价值占全部邮政货运总价值的74.96%。[54] 未通过CBP提交入境数据的邮政货运,很可能也未向CBP缴纳关税。因此,如果有更多邮政货运得到适当申报,将会有更多关税被缴纳,CBP的关税收入也将因此增加。为估算关税收入的增长情况,CBP假设在该规则下,提交入境申报的邮政货运比例将从67.02%上升至100%,并假设在基线情形下未提交入境申报的邮政货运与已提交入境申报的邮政货运具有相同的平均关税税率。若第一项假设不准确,则本计算将高估关税收入的增长;若第二项假设不准确,则CBP的计算结果可能高估或低估收入增长。
As discussed in the Benefits section above, CBP expects that the rule would lead to greater compliance with entry requirements in the postal environment. From September 2025 to May 2026, only 67.02% of postal shipments were entered through an informal or formal entry process, and the value of those postal shipments that had entry forms submitted was 74.96% of the total value of all postal shipments.[54] The postal shipments that did not have entry data submitted through CBP also most likely did not have duties paid to CBP. Therefore, if more postal shipments were entered properly, more duty payments would be made, and CBP would see an increase in duty revenue. To estimate the increase in duty revenue, CBP assumes that the share of postal shipments for which an entry is filed would rise from 67.02% to 100% under the rule and that the average duty rate is the same for postal shipments that would not file entry in the baseline as it is for postal shipments that would. To the extent that the first assumption is inaccurate, these calculations will overestimate the increase in duty revenue. To the extent that the second assumption is inaccurate, CBP's calculations could overestimate or underestimate the revenue increase.
由于直到最近,邮政非正式货运才需缴纳所有非第122条关税,因此CBP尚不清楚在IMD工作表中报告的邮政货运所缴纳的平均关税税率。因此,CBP假设邮政类报关单的有效平均税率与ET11类报关单相同。CBP抽取了2025年9月至11月期间392,312份ET11报关单作为样本,并计算了已缴纳的关税总额。[55] 用这些关税总额(23,846,464美元)除以样本中货物的总价值(160,675,653美元),得出14.84%,即这些关税的有效平均税率。从2025年9月至2026年5月,价值1,179,693,422美元的邮政货运抵达美国。按此比率计算,CBP估计一年内将有价值1,577,245,784美元的邮政货运抵达美国。[56] 将14.84%的关税税率应用于此总额,CBP估计在完全合规的情况下,邮政货运的年度关税将为234,084,842美元。如果按价值计算仅有74.96%的邮政报关单符合关税要求,则邮政货运每年征收的关税总额将为175,465,755美元。因此,合规率在本规则下提升至完全合规所带来的关税收入增加额将为58,619,087美元。CBP假设2021年至2024年邮政报关单的复合年增长率(CAGR)为负11.63%,即为邮政货运年度关税在监管期间变化的比率。将负11.63%的增长率应用于58,619,087美元的额外年度关税,CBP预测本规则下监管期间邮政货运的额外关税情况,如表18所示,自2027年本规则生效之日起计算。 (印刷页第64556页) 在邮政货运量可能因更高的关税征收率而受到影响,或邮政货运的构成与ET11报关单的构成不同的情况下,实际收入增加额将与我们的估计值有所不同。CBP还指出,在本分析中并未量化未来可能征收的任何额外关税,因为CBP尚未掌握关于未来关税税率及适用性的足够具体信息。

净影响
Net Impact
本规则将提高报关流程的安全性并增加关税收入,同时也会给报关行、进口商、寄件人和美国政府带来成本。ET11报关单的安全性将得到提升,因为CBP将收到最终收货方的姓名和地址,报关数据将更早送达,并且由于对ET11实施了保证金要求以及1,000美元清算损害赔偿金下限的规定,数据的可靠性也将提高。要求邮政非正式报关单采用ET13流程,将使CBP获得关于邮政货运的大量额外信息,并对邮政货运施加与正在添加到ET11上相同的要求,例如更早的申报截止日期。承运人还将向CBP提供邮政货运的追踪编号,这将有助于CBP确定邮政货运何时抵达美国。有了这些改进,CBP将能够更有效地锁定高风险的ET11和邮政货运目标,从而查获更多违禁物品,包括非法芬太尼及其他麻醉品。本规则将为CBP以及先前提交IMD工作表的申报人带来一定的成本节省。本规则还将增加来自邮政货运的政府收入,因为CBP将能够更严格地执行报关要求,从而使更多邮政货运得到适当申报并缴纳适用关税。预计增加的关税收入情况如表19所示,自2027年本规则生效之日起计算。

The rule would result in some cost savings to CBP and to filers who previously submitted IMD worksheets. 监管替代方案
Alternative Regulatory Options
CBP可以不实施本《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中讨论的拟议监管变更,而是实施更严格或更不严格的监管替代方案,尤其是在入境申报截止日期方面。在基准情形下,申报截止日期为到达后15天,而拟议规则将把该截止日期改为到达时。如上所述,这一变更可能造成干扰,特别是对于通常使用已抵达美国的包裹本身来收集必要入境数据的申报人而言。一种不太严格的替代方案是保持申报截止日期不变,同时推进本规则制定中提议的其他变更。该版本的规则成本将较低,因为申报人可以继续使用其正常程序收集数据。另一方面,将截止日期定为到达后15天将意味着CBP在货物到达时无法获得入境数据,这将妨碍该数据用于筛查目的的有效性。除了保持申报截止日期不变之外,CBP可能还有其他方式可以使该规则不那么严格。CBP欢迎公众提交意见,如果他们认为存在可以降低该规则净成本的监管替代方案。
Instead of the proposed regulatory changes discussed in this NPRM, CBP could implement a stricter or a less strict regulatory alternative, especially with regard to the entry filing deadline. In the baseline, the filing deadline is 15 days after arrival, while the proposed rule would change the deadline to the time of arrival. As discussed above, this change could cause disruptions, especially for filers who typically use the package itself that has arrived in the United States to collect the necessary entry data. A less strict alternative would be to leave the filing deadline as is, while moving forward with the other changes proposed in this rulemaking. This version of the rule would be less costly, as filers could continue collecting data using their normal procedures. On the other hand, having the deadline be 15 days after arrival would mean that CBP would not have the entry data at the time of the merchandise's arrival, which would hamper the data's usefulness for screening purposes. There are likely other ways that CBP could make the rule less strict besides leaving the filing deadline unchanged. CBP invites the public to submit comments if they believe there are regulatory alternatives that would reduce the net cost of the rule.
另一方面,CBP可以使申报截止日期更加严格,例如要求在到达前24小时或货物离开原产国之前提交入境申报。到达前的这段额外时间将使CBP能够在货物到达之前分析数据,从而使CBP在货物一到达即可做好更充分的处理准备,并有助于在货物到达时拒收不合规物品。然而,这种更严格的替代方案将加剧拟议规则中更早申报截止日期所带来的成本。如果截止日期是包裹寄出的时间,申报人将无法利用运输时间来收集数据并提交入境申报。如果截止日期为到达前24小时,只要航程超过24小时,申报人至少可以在货物运输途中提交入境申报,但这一替代方案可能给申报人带来不确定性。申报人可能无法提前24小时确切知道货物何时抵达美国,这将使截止日期本身变得不确定。
In the other direction, CBP could make the filing deadline stricter, such as by requiring that the entry be filed 24 hours before arrival or before the shipment leaves the country of origin. This extra time before arrival would mean that CBP could analyze the data before shipments arrive, making CBP all the more prepared to process merchandise as soon as it arrives and facilitating refusal of non-compliant items upon arrival. However, this stricter alternative would exacerbate the costs of the proposed rule's earlier filing deadline. If the deadline is the time that the package is sent, the filer would not be able to use the transit time to gather data and file entry. If the deadline were 24 hours before arrival, the filer could at least file entry while the merchandise is in transit if the voyage is longer than 24 hours, but this alternative could create uncertainty for the filer. The filer may not know when exactly the shipment will arrive in the United States 24 hours in advance, making the deadline itself uncertain.
B. 《监管灵活性法案》
B. Regulatory Flexibility Act
根据《监管灵活性法案》(5 U.S.C. 601及以下条款,简称RFA)经《1996年小企业监管公平法》(SBREFA)修订后的要求,本节审查拟议规则对小实体的影响。小实体可以是小企业(根据《小企业法》的规定,定义为任何独立拥有和经营、在其所属领域不占主导地位且符合小企业资格的企业) (印刷版第64558页) ;小型非营利组织;或小型政府辖区(人口少于50,000人的地方行政区域)。
This section examines the impact of the proposed rule on small entities, per the requirements of the Regulatory Flexibility Act (5 U.S.C. 601 et seq.) (RFA), as amended by the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act of 1996 (SBREFA). A small entity may be a small business (defined as any independently owned and operated business not dominant in its field that qualifies as a small business ( printed page 64558) per the Small Business Act); a small not-for-profit organization; or a small governmental jurisdiction (locality with fewer than 50,000 people).
根据《规制灵活性法》(RFA)和《小企业规制执行公平法》(SBREFA),如果某机构能够(通常通过筛选分析)证明某项规则不会对“大量小实体产生重大经济影响”,则无需对该规则对小实体的影响进行详细评估。否则,该机构必须完成一份初步规制灵活性分析(IRFA),探讨拟议规则制定对小实体的影响。
Under the RFA and SBREFA, if an agency can certify (typically through a screening analysis) that a rule will not have a “significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities,” a detailed assessment of the rule's impact on small entities is not required. Otherwise, an agency must complete an initial regulatory flexibility analysis (IRFA) exploring the impact of the proposed rulemaking on small entities.

Screening Analysis
该规则将对邮政及非邮政简易报关流程作出的变更,将直接影响报关申报人、寄件人、进口商及承运人。非邮政简易报关的申报人将需要提交一项额外的数据元素,即最终交付方(如与最终收货人不同),并将受到更早的申报截止时间的约束,即货物到达时。邮政简易报关的申报人将必须开始提交CBP表格3461和7501,并且也必须在货物到达之前提交该报关数据。大多数邮政报关的申报人将是报关行,而报关行很可能会从寄件人处获取邮政数据,因此寄件人在该规则下将面临更高的时间负担。非邮政简易报关的申报人可能会向进口商询问最终交付方信息,部分进口商将必须为ET11类报关获取保证金。承运人将必须在舱单上提交邮政货物的追踪号码。
The changes that the rule would make to the postal and non-postal informal entry processes would directly affect entry filers, senders, importers, and carriers. Filers of non-postal informal entries would need to submit an extra data element, the final deliver-to party if distinct from the ultimate consignee, and would be subject to an earlier filing deadline, the time of the shipment's arrival. Filers of postal informal entries would have to start submitting CBP Forms 3461 and 7501, and they would also have to submit this entry data before the shipment's arrival. Most filers of postal entries would be brokers, and a broker would likely obtain the postal data from the sender, who would therefore face a higher time burden under the rule. Filers of non-postal informal entries might ask the importer for the final deliver-to party, and some importers would have to obtain bonds for ET11 entries. Carriers would have to submit the tracking numbers for postal shipments on the manifests.
这些群体在多大程度上拥有大量会受到拟议规则直接重大影响的美国小企业方面存在差异。寄件人中很少有美国实体,因为该群体由向美国寄送邮政货物的外国个人和实体组成。承运人将受到在舱单上提交邮政追踪号码要求的约束,CBP估计22.11%的承运人为美国小企业。为计算该比例,CBP从374家空运承运人总体中抽取了190家样本,发现其中42家根据D&B Hoovers数据[57]及小企业管理局规模标准符合美国小企业的资格。基于样本结果,CBP估计374家空运承运人中有83家将是受该规则影响的美国小企业,这属于相当数量。然而,该规则对承运人的影响并不显著。如表12所示,CBP估计2027年承运人的总负担为18,701,580美元,平均每家承运人为50,004美元。根据D&B Hoovers数据,样本中42家美国小型空运承运人的平均收入为11,768,452美元。因此,对所有承运人而言的平均成本仅为美国小型承运人平均收入的0.425%。此外,由于美国小型承运人平均处理的报关数量较少,其平均成本很可能低于所有承运人的平均成本。因此,不会有大量美国小型承运人受到该规则的重大影响。
These groups differ in how likely they are to have a substantial number of small U.S. businesses that would be significantly affected in a direct way by the proposed rule. Few of the senders would be American, as this group comprises individuals and entities in foreign countries sending postal shipments to the United States. Carriers would be subject to the requirement to submit the postal tracking number on the manifest, and CBP estimates that 22.11% of carriers are small U.S. businesses. To calculate this share, CBP took a sample of 190 air carriers out of a population of 374 and found that 42 out of the 190 qualified as small U.S. businesses, based on D&B Hoovers data [57] and Small Business Administration size standards. Based on the sample results, CBP estimates that 83 out of the 374 air carriers would be small U.S. businesses affected by the rule, which is a substantial number. However, the effect of the rule on carriers would not be significant. As shown in Table 12, CBP estimates that the total burden to carriers would be $18,701,580 in 2027, which equates to $50,004 per carrier on average. According to D&B Hoovers data, the average revenue of the 42 small U.S. air carriers in the sample was $11,768,452. Hence, the average cost to all carriers would be only 0.425% of the average small U.S. carrier's revenue. Moreover, the average cost to small U.S. carriers would likely be even smaller than the average cost to all carriers, as the former handle fewer entries on average. Therefore, there would not be a substantial number of small U.S. carriers significantly affected by the rule.
对进口商的直接影响将是,对于ET11入境而言,如果最终收货方与收货人不同,则需要向申报人提供最终收货方信息所带来的负担,以及在基准情形下本应无需担保的ET11入境所产生的额外担保成本。美国海关及边境保护局（CBP）预计,申报人将在向进口商索取并接收其余入境数据的同时,一并向其索取这一新的数据要素。因此,将最终收货方列为必填数据要素对办理入境所需时间的影响将微乎其微。根据本规则,由于ET13入境流程将取代目前的非正式邮政入境流程,最终收货方及其他数据要素将成为邮政货运的必填项,但据一位CBP主题专家介绍,在邮政环境下,通常是寄件人而非进口商向申报人提供货运数据。根据本规则新增的担保要求,在2025年8月1日至2026年7月31日期间,记录进口商将因此多支付9,276,484美元的担保保费。[58] 这一总额仅占同期无担保ET11入境所缴纳关税、税费总额的3.15%。然而,并非基准情形下所有无担保ET11入境的进口商都会受到同等程度的影响。有些记录进口商进口的是低价值货物,且进口频率很低。根据本规则,这些进口商会发现使用单次担保而非连续担保更为经济。在59,148名在无担保情况下进口ET11入境、且在拟议规则下更愿意使用单次担保而非连续担保的记录进口商中,担保要求所产生的总成本将占其ET11入境所缴关税、税费总额的72.66%。对于这些记录进口商而言,担保要求可能导致成本大幅增加。此外,这些进口商之所以更倾向于使用单次担保而非连续担保,是因为他们进口的货物数量较少,这意味着这类进口商很可能包含大量小型企业。因此,CBP无法证明本规则不会对作为记录进口商的大量美国小型企业产生重大影响。
The direct effect on importers would be the burden of providing the filer with the final deliver-to party if distinct from the consignee for ET11 entries and the added bond cost for ET11 entries that would be bondless in the baseline. CBP expects that filers would request the new data element from the importer at the same time that the filers request and receive the rest of the entry data. Therefore, adding the final deliver-to party as a required data element would have a negligible effect on the time burden of making entry. Both the final deliver-to party and other data elements would become required for postal shipments under the rule, due to the ET13 entry process replacing the current informal postal entry process, but according to a CBP subject matter expert, it is the sender, not the importer, that generally provides the shipping data to the filer in the postal environment. The added bond requirement under the rule would have resulted in importers of record paying an extra $9,276,484 in bond premiums between August 1, 2025, and July 31, 2026.[58] This total is only 3.15% of the total duties, taxes, and fees paid for bondless ET11 entries over that period. However, not all importers of bondless ET11 entries in the baseline would be affected equally. Some importers of record import low-value shipments and only do so infrequently. These importers would find it cheaper to use single transaction bonds rather than continuous bonds, under the rule. Among the 59,148 importers of record who imported an ET11 entry without a bond and would have preferred to use a single transaction bond rather than a continuous bond under the proposed rule, the total cost of the bond requirement would have been 72.66% of the total duties, taxes, and fees on their ET11 entries. For these importers of record, the bond requirement could result in a significant increase in costs. Moreover, these importers would prefer single transaction bonds over continuous bonds because they import a smaller amount of goods, which means that this category of importer is likely to include many small businesses. Therefore, CBP is unable to certify that the rule would not have a significant impact on a substantial number of small U.S. businesses that are importers of record.
根据本规则,非邮政非正式入境的申报人将面临新的要求,包括以电子方式提交入境表格,以及更早的提交截止日期。具体而言,入境数据须在货物抵达前提交,而非当前规定的货物抵达后15天内提交。虽然对申报人而言,其他新要求可能微不足道,但CBP认为,这一新的提交截止日期可能会造成干扰。如果申报人在基准情形下的标准做法是在货物抵达美国后查看货物包装标签,然后利用该信息提交入境数据,那么在本规则下这一流程将不再适用。相反,申报人可能需要在海外雇佣更多人员,以便在货物启运赴美之前对其进行查验,从而在货物抵达前完成入境申报。这一新流程所产生的成本可能是重大的。因此,CBP无法证明本规则不会对非邮政环境下的大量美国小型企业产生重大影响。
Filers of non-postal informal entries would be subject to new requirements, including electronic submission of entry forms and an earlier filing deadline. Specifically, entry data would need to be submitted before arrival of the merchandise, as opposed to the current deadline of 15 days after arrival. While the other new requirements for filers may be insignificant, CBP believes that this new filing deadline could be disruptive. If a filer's standard procedure in the baseline is to read the package labeling of a shipment that has arrived in the United States and then use that information to submit entry data, then that process would no longer work under the rule. Instead, the filer may need to employ more people abroad so that they can look at the shipment before it leaves for the United States in order to make entry before the shipment's arrival. The costs of this new procedure could be significant. Therefore, CBP is unable to certify that the rule would not have a significant impact on a substantial number of small U.S. businesses in the non-postal environment.
根据本规则,邮政非正式入境的申报人将须向CBP提交更多数据,并须在更严格的条件下完成提交,例如更早的提交截止日期。就本次筛选分析而言,CBP重点关注已提交国际邮件数据（IMD）工作表的邮政非正式入境申报人。上述经济分析假定,从长期来看,基准情形下将有100名申报人提交月度IMD工作表,但迄今为止仅有10名申报人实际提交。这些申报人在2026年6月确立当前非正式邮政入境流程之前的过渡性邮政 (印刷页 64559) 流程期间提交了IMD工作表,CBP预计这些申报人将在当前非正式邮政入境流程下继续提交IMD工作表。这10名申报人都将受到影响,因为他们将不再能够提交IMD工作表,而必须改为通过ET13流程提交入境数据。ET13入境必须使用CBP 3461表和7501表提交,这两种表格的数据要求均高于IMD工作表。
Under the rule, filers for postal informal entries would have to submit more data to CBP and would do so under stricter conditions, such as the earlier filing deadline. For this screening analysis, CBP focuses on the filers of postal informal entries who have already filed IMD worksheets. The economic analysis above assumes that in the long run 100 filers would submit the monthly IMD worksheet in the baseline, but so far only 10 filers have done so. These filers submitted an IMD worksheet during the interim postal ( printed page 64559) process that preceded the current informal postal entry process that was established in June 2026, and CBP expects these filers to continue submitting IMD worksheets under the current informal postal entry process. These 10 filers would all be affected because they would no longer be able to submit IMD worksheets and would instead have to switch to submitting entry data through the ET13 process. ET13 entries must be filed using CBP Forms 3461 and 7501, which entail higher data requirements than the IMD worksheet.
CBP(美国海关与边境保护局)利用邓白氏惠誉数据库(D&B Hoovers database)收集了关于10份IMD工作表申报人的信息。[59] 在这10个申报人中,有9个出现在该数据库中,其中6个符合美国小型企业的资格。如果某公司由母公司所有,CBP会查看最终母公司的所在地,以确定该公司是否为美国公司。为评估某公司是否为小型企业,CBP将该公司的销售额或雇员人数(视适用情形而定)与小企业管理局(Small Business Administration)针对该公司北美行业分类系统(NAICS)代码所对应的规模标准进行了比较。由于目前仅有10个IMD工作表的现行申报人,CBP认为6个属于数量可观的美国小型企业。
CBP used the D&B Hoovers database to gather information on the 10 IMD worksheet filers.[59] Of those 10 filers, 9 appeared in the database, and 6 of those 9 qualify as small U.S. businesses. If a company was owned by a parent company, CBP looked at the location of the ultimate parent company to determine whether the company is American. To assess whether a company is small, CBP compared the company's sales or number of employees (whichever was called for) to the Small Business Administration size standard corresponding to the company's North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code. As there are only 10 active filers of IMD worksheets, CBP considers 6 to be a substantial number of small U.S. businesses.
CBP预计,已提交IMD工作表的美国小型企业将在本规则下面临重大经济影响。本规则的一项成本是提交非正式邮政报关单额外数据所带来的负担。在经济影响分析中,CBP估计,报关行将因本规则而开始就每份邮政报关单加收30美元的费用。这30美元的费用也可视为申报人因额外数据要求而面临的成本增加的估计值。将该30美元费用乘以CBP预计2027年邮政报关单的总数,即28,860,463份,得出总成本为8.658亿美元。[60] 相对于在邓白氏惠誉数据库中出现的九个申报人(仅缺一个申报人)的总收入而言,该金额是巨大的。因此,本规则总体上将对现有IMD工作表申报人产生重大影响。由于美国小型企业在这些申报人中占多数,CBP认为被视为“小型”的相当数量的申报人可能因本拟议规则而受到重大影响。因此,CBP无法证明本规则不会对邮政环境中相当数量的小型实体产生重大经济影响。CBP已编制以下初步监管灵活性分析(IRFA),评估本规则对小型实体的潜在影响。CBP欢迎公众就本IRFA所含数据和结论提出意见。对CBP最有帮助的意见应引用IRFA的具体部分,说明建议变更的理由,并提供支持该建议变更的数据、信息或依据。
CBP expects that the small U.S. businesses that have filed IMD worksheets would face a significant economic impact under the rule. One cost of the rule would be the burden of submitting additional data for informal postal entries. In the economic impact analysis, CBP estimates that brokers would begin charging an additional $30 fee to file a postal entry under the rule. This $30 fee can also be seen as an estimate of the cost increase that filers would face as a result of the additional data requirements. Multiplying that $30 fee by the total number of postal entries that CBP projects for 2027, which is 28,860,463, yields a total cost of $865.8 million.[60] This amount is significant relative to the total revenue of the nine filers appearing in the D&B Hoovers database, with only one filer missing. Therefore, the rule would have a significant impact on current IMD worksheet filers as a whole. As small U.S. businesses make up a majority of these filers, CBP believes that a substantial number of filers considered “small” could be significantly affected by this proposed rule. For this reason, CBP cannot certify that the rule would not have a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities in the postal environment. 1. 关于本机构为何考虑采取此行动之原因的说明。
1. A description of the reasons why the action by the agency is being considered.
为消除邮政与非邮政非正式报关之间关税待遇的差异,美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)于2026年6月24日发布了《无限期暂停邮件货运小额豁免及新邮政非正式报关程序临时最终规则》(IFR),该规则针对进入美国的某些商品建立了新的邮政非正式报关程序,改进了根据第14324号行政令(经修订)所建立的临时邮政程序。[61] 根据该规则,月度国际邮件关税工作表被扩大,要求提供产品归类及其他数据要素,CBP开始对邮政非正式报关适用所有相关关税。邮政非正式报关与ET11报关现须遵守相同的产品归类申报要求,但尽管新的邮政程序使CBP能够对邮政报关征收与ET11报关相同的关税,在邮政环境中执行关税及相关法规仍更为困难。CBP需要报关数据以确保申报人缴纳应缴税款,但国际邮件关税(IMD)工作表的申报人无需像ET11申报人那样提交同等数量的数据,也无需在同样的时限内提交IMD工作表。
To eliminate the disparity in duties between postal and non-postal informal entries, CBP published the Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for Mail Shipments and New Postal Informal Entry Process IFR on June 24, 2026, which established a new postal informal entry process for certain merchandise entering the United States, improving upon the interim postal process established pursuant to Executive Order 14324, as amended.[61] With that rule, the monthly international mail duty worksheet was expanded to require product classification and other data elements, and CBP began applying all relevant duties to postal informal entries. Postal informal entries and ET11 entries are now subject to the same product classification reporting requirements, but while the new postal process enables CBP to impose the same duties on postal entries as on ET11 entries, enforcement of duties and regulations is more difficult in the postal environment. CBP needs entry data to make sure that filers are paying what they should, but filers of the international mail duty (IMD) worksheet do not have to submit as much data as ET11 filers, nor do they have to submit the IMD worksheet within the same timeframe.
IMD工作表所要求的有限数据也限制了CBP对邮政货运中违规货物的筛查能力,而邮政货运恰恰最需要严格的筛查。2024财年,CBP在邮政小额豁免货运中查获毒品的比率为每百万件货运277.8次查获,相比之下,正式报关货运的比率仅为每百万件货运2.6次查获,非正式报关第11类货运(ET11)的比率为每百万件货运15.8次查获。[62] 邮政小额豁免货运在所有其他查获类别中也具有最高的查获率,包括健康与安全、假冒商品及违禁物品类别。
The limited data required on the IMD worksheet also limits CBP's ability to screen postal shipments for violative goods, yet postal shipments need strong screening the most. In FY 2024, CBP seized narcotics in postal de minimis shipments at a rate of 277.8 seizures per million shipments, compared to only 2.6 seizures per million shipments for formally entered shipments and 15.8 seizures per million shipments for informally entered type 11 shipments.[62] Postal de minimis shipments had the highest seizure rates in all other seizure categories as well, including health and safety, counterfeit goods, and prohibited items.
2. 拟议规则的目标及法律依据的简要说明。
2. A succinct statement of the objectives of, and legal basis for, the proposed rule.
本规则旨在以第13类报关(ET13)取代现行邮政非正式报关程序,使邮政非正式报关适用与非邮政非正式报关类似的要求,从而改善CBP在邮政环境中的执法能力。与ET11程序相同,ET13程序将要求使用CBP第3461号和第7501号表格进行报关申报,而非使用IMD工作表。本规则还将对ET11程序进行更新,这些更新同样适用于ET13程序。这些更新,例如新的担保要求和更早的申报截止日期,旨在解决非正式环境中的执法问题,这一问题可从非正式环境中违规货物查获率高于正式环境的情况中得到印证。 ( 第64560页 )
The rule aims to replace the current informal postal process with entry type 13 so that postal informal entries are subject to similar requirements as non-postal informal entries, thereby improving CBP's enforcement in the postal environment. Like the ET11 process, the ET13 process would require that entry be filed using CBP Forms 3461 and 7501, not the IMD worksheet. The rule would also make updates to the ET11 process, which would apply to the ET13 process as well. These updates, such as the new bonding requirements and earlier filing deadlines, would aim to address the enforcement issues in the informal environment, as evidenced by the ( printed page 64560) higher rate of seizures of violative goods in the informal environment than the formal environment.
3. 对拟议规则所适用的小实体的描述,并在可行的情况下估算其数量。
3. A description—and, where feasible, an estimate of the number—of small entities to which the proposed rule would apply.
本规则将影响一直在邮政和非邮政环境中提交非正式报关的美国小型企业。目前在邮政环境中,这些企业提交月度IMD工作表,但根据本规则,该工作表将不再被接受。相反,邮政非正式报关将需要通过ET13程序进行申报。截至目前,共有10个实体提交过IMD工作表。在这10个提交过IMD工作表的实体中,有6个为美国小型企业。这六家公司的年平均销售额为1100万美元,但其中三家的销售额低于30万美元。该销售额估算数据取自D&B Hoovers数据库,该数据库使用模型对部分公司的销售额数字进行估算。本规则还将影响ET11报关的申报人。在非邮政环境中,2020年10月1日至2026年2月6日期间共有2428名报关行提交了报关总结单。[63] 所有这些报关行均为美国企业,其中一部分为提交ET11报关的小型企业。
The rule would affect small U.S. businesses that have been filing informal entries, both in the postal and non-postal environment. Currently in the postal environment, these businesses submit a monthly IMD worksheet, but this worksheet would no longer be accepted under the rule. Instead, informal postal entries would need to be entered through the ET13 process. So far, 10 entities have filed an IMD worksheet. Of these 10 entities that have filed an IMD worksheet, 6 of them are small U.S. businesses. The average annual sales for these six companies is $11 million, but three of them have sales under $300,000. The sales estimates are taken from the D&B Hoovers database, which uses a model to estimate some companies' sales figures. The rule would also affect filers of ET11 entries. In the non-postal environment, there were 2,428 brokers that filed an entry summary between October 1, 2020, and February 6, 2026.[63] All of these brokers were U.S. businesses, and some fraction were small businesses that filed ET11 entries.
也可能有大量属于美国小型企业的记录进口商会因非正式入境保证金要求而受到该规则的重大影响。ET13入境和ET11入境均将被要求提交保证金。由于目前的非正式邮政入境程序已经要求提交保证金,美国海关与边境保护局(CBP)预计ET13入境的保证金要求不会导致邮政货运成本增加。然而,对于ET11入境而言,存在一些无需保证金即可进口的记录进口商,这些进口商在该规则下将需要获得保证金。一些记录进口商进口的货物数量足够多,采用连续保证金将是更便宜的选择,这些进口商可能不会受到重大影响。而在进口少量货物的进口商中,更便宜的保证金选择将是依赖单次交易保证金。这一群体包含59,148名记录进口商,其中一些进口商的进口成本可能会显著增加,因为他们将承担相当于关税、税费总额73%的保证金成本增加。此外,这些进口商中可能有相当数量属于小型企业。
There could also be a substantial number of importers of record that are U.S. small businesses that would be significantly affected by the rule as a result of the bonding requirement for informal entries. Bonds would be required for both ET13 and ET11 entries. As bonds are already required for the current informal postal entry process, CBP does not expect that the bond requirement for ET13 entries would result in an increase in costs for postal shipments. For ET11 entries, however, there are importers of record that import without a bond and would need to obtain a bond under the rule. Some importers of record import enough merchandise that a continuous bond would be the cheaper option, and these importers would probably not be significantly affected. Among importers that import small amounts of goods, the cheaper bond option would be to rely on single transaction bonds. This group comprises 59,148 importers of record, and some of these importers could see a significant increase in their importing costs, as they would incur an increase in bond costs equal to 73% of duties, taxes, and fees. Moreover, a substantial number of these importers could be small businesses.
该规则还会影响其他类别的小实体,但影响程度不大。承运人将需要在舱单上提交邮政追踪编号,据估计其中有83家承运人属于美国小型企业。已经持有保证金的ET11入境进口商仅会受到提供最终收货方要求的影响。在2020年10月1日至2026年2月6日期间,共有999,938名美国记录进口商进行了某种形式的入境申报,其中26%为ET11入境,这些美国记录进口商中有一些属于小型企业。[64]
Other classes of small entities would be affected by the rule, but not in a significant way. Carriers would have to submit the postal tracking number on the manifest, and an estimated 83 of these carriers are small U.S. businesses. Importers of ET11 entries who already have bonds would only be affected by the requirement to provide the final deliver-to party. Between October 1, 2020 and February 6, 2026, there were 999,938 U.S. importers of record who made entry of some kind, 26% of which were ET11 entries, and some of those U.S. IORs are small businesses.[64]

拟议规则将增加报告要求,因为邮政非正式入境和ET11入境需要提交更多的入境数据。由于邮政非正式入境的申报人将需要向CBP提交CBP 3461表和7501表,而不是IMD工作表,这些申报人将需要提供更多数据。例如,CBP 3461表和7501表要求申报人标明收货人、记录进口商以及货物制造商。该规则将对ET11入境要求提交一项附加数据元素,即最终收货方(如与收货人不同),ET13入境也将同样被要求提交该数据元素。除要求提交更多数据外,该规则还将要求更早提交这些数据。目前ET11入境的入境申报截止日期为到货日期后15天,邮政非正式入境的申报截止日期为次月第7日,而根据新规则,ET11入境和ET13入境均须在到货时提交申报。
The proposed rule would increase reporting requirements because more entry data would need to be submitted for postal informal entries and ET11 entries. Because filers of postal informal entries would have to submit CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 instead of an IMD worksheet to CBP, these filers would have to provide more data. For example, CBP Forms 3461 and 7501 require the filer to identify the consignee, the importer of record, and the manufacturer of the merchandise. The rule would require an additional data element for ET11 entries, the final deliver-to party if distinct from the consignee, which would be required of ET13 entries as well. In addition to requiring more data to be submitted, the rule would also require that this data be submitted sooner. Instead of the entry filing deadline being 15 days after the date of arrival for ET11 entries and the 7th day of the following month for postal informal entries, both ET11 entries and ET13 entries would need to be filed by arrival.
将受这些新要求约束的小实体主要是提交IMD工作表或ET11入境申报的报关行。为获取CBP所要求的附加数据,这些报关行将需要与拥有相关数据的各方(如发件人或进口商)进行沟通,并且报关行将需要在货运流程的更早阶段获取这些数据,以便能够在到货时提交入境数据。
The small entities that would be subject to these new requirements would mostly be brokers who file IMD worksheets or ET11 entries. To obtain the additional data that CBP would be requiring, these brokers would need to communicate with the parties that have the data, such as the sender or importer, and the brokers would need to obtain this data earlier in the shipping process so that the entry data can be submitted by arrival.

拟议规则不包含对州、地方或部落政府的任何无资金拨付之强制性规定。然而,该规则将导致私营部门每年产生超过1亿美元的成本。尤为值得注意的是,进口商和寄件人将因非正式邮政报关的时间负担和经纪费增加,以及ET11报关新增的担保要求,而产生相关成本。这些成本在该规则实施的第一年总计将超过10亿美元。美国海关及边境保护局(CBP)已在上文第12866号行政命令分析中对该规则的成本进行了更详细的讨论。

Most notably, importers and senders would incur costs from increased time burdens and broker fees for informal postal entries and from the new bond requirement for ET11 entries. These costs would total more than $1 billion in the first year of the rule. CBP discusses the costs of the rule in more detail in the E.O. 12866 analysis above.
