USCIS更新政策手册中关于L-1签证申请费的指导

发布日期 2026年10月27日

USCIS修订了政策手册中有关L-1公司内部调动人员申请费的指导内容,此次修订反映了H-1B和L-1签证9-11应对及生物识别出入境费最终规则所带来的监管变化。来源未说明具体费用金额或生效日期。

要点

  • USCIS修订了政策手册中关于L-1公司内部调动人员申请的章节。
  • 此次修订源于H-1B和L-1签证9-11应对及生物识别出入境费最终规则。
  • 受影响章节为政策手册2 USCIS-PM L.7,第7章,申请部分。
  • 具体费用金额和生效日期在本次公告中未予说明。

影响对象

提交L-1公司内部调动人员申请的雇主及相关L-1签证申请人受到影响。

建议行动

申请人应查阅更新后的政策手册2 USCIS-PM L.7章节,以获取最新的申请费指导信息。

U.S. Department of Homeland Security U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services www.uscis.gov U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services Office of the Director Camp Springs, MD 20588-0009 PA-2026-16 October 27, 2026 Policy Alert SUBJECT: Fee G uidance for L-1 Intracompany Transferees Purpose U.S . Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is revising guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual regarding filing fees for L-1 petitions and the regulatory changes made by the 9-11 Response and Biometric Entry-Exit Fee for H-1B and L-1 Visas final rule (9-11 Biometric Fee final rule).1 Background The Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2016 (referred to as Public Law 114-113)2 imposes a fee for certain L-1A and L-1B petitions. DHS initially applied this fee only for petitions filed by covered employers3 seeking initial grants of L-1 status or seeking a change of employers. On August 10, 2026, DHS published the 9-11 Biometric Fee final rule (effective September 9, 2026) that extends the fee to petitions requesting extensions of L-1 status.
因此,自2026年9月9日起,根据9-11生物识别费最终规则,第114-113号公法规定的费用适用于受涵盖雇主在2027年9月30日或之前邮戳提交的所有L-1申请,但申请人提交的修正申请不涉及延长外国人当前获准的L-1身份的除外。该规则还将长期以来的做法成文化,即对雇员人数达到50人或以上,且其中50%或以上(含兼职和全职雇员在内)处于H-1B或L-1身份的申请人适用该费用。USCIS正在更新其政策指南以反映这一变化。
Accordingly, as of September 9, 2026, under the 9-11 Biometric Fee final rule, the Public Law 114- 113 fee applies to all L-1 petitions filed by covered employers postmarked on or before September 30, 2027, unless the petitioner is filing an amended petition that does not seek an extension of the alien’s currently authorized L-1 status. The rule also codifies longstanding practice applying the fee to petitioners with 50 or more employees if 50 percent or more of those employees are in H-1B or L-1 status in the aggregate, including part-time and full-time employees. USCIS is updating its policy guidance to reflect this change.
此外,本次更新删除了具体费用金额的提法,转而参照《收费表》(G-1055),该表列出了申请人在为L-1受益人提交《非移民工作者申请表》(I-129表)时必须缴纳的所有费用,包括欺诈预防和侦测费以及第114-113号公法规定征收的费用。
In addition, this update removes references to specific fee amounts, referring instead to the Fee Schedule (G-1055), which lists all of the fees petitioners must pay when filing a Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker (Form I-129) for an L-1 beneficiary, including the Fraud Prevention and Detection fee and the fee imposed by Public Law 114-113.
