USCIS更新政策手册,删除有关外交官'蓝名单'的内容

发布日期 2026年8月6日

USCIS发布政策指南,从政策手册中删除有关外交名单(又称蓝名单)的内容,因为美国国务院已不再发布该名单。此次更新涉及关于在美国出生的外国政府雇员子女公民身份认定的章节。

要点

  • 美国国务院已不再发布曾用于确认外国认证外交官身份的外交名单。
  • USCIS修订了7 USCIS-PM O.3(关于在美国出生的外国政府雇员子女)和12 USCIS-PM H.3(关于依据INA第301条和第309条出生取得美国公民身份)两个章节。
  • 此次修订属于删除过时参考资料的技术性调整,并未改变实质性资格要求。
  • 来源未说明具体的生效日期。

影响对象

在美国出生的外国外交官或政府官员子女,以及处理相关公民身份认定事务的律师受到影响。

建议行动

处理相关案件的人员应查阅USCIS政策手册中更新后的相关章节。

U.S. Department of Homeland Security U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services www.uscis.gov U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services Office of the Director Camp Springs, MD 20588-0009 August 6, 2026 PA-2026-07 Policy Alert SUBJECT: Lists of Foreign Diplomatic Officers Accredited to the United States Purpose U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is issuing policy guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual that removes references to the Diplomatic List (also known as the “Blue List”), which is no longer published by the U.S. Department of State (DOS).
背景 一般而言，外国外交官员及其配偶和子女根据国际法和美国法律享有某些豁免权，这影响到他们在美国境内的法律身份。由于这些豁免权意味着他们不受美国法律管辖，经认证派驻美国的外国外交官员在美国出生的子女在出生时不是美国公民。1 该子女可能有资格根据8 CFR 101.3登记获得合法永久居民（LPR）身份。如果父母中一方是外国外交官员，另一方是美国公民，则该子女在出生时即为美国公民，无资格登记LPR身份。
Background Generally, foreign diplomatic officers, their spouses, and children enjoy certain immunities under international and U.S. law, which affects their legal status while present in the United States. Because these immunities mean that they are not subject to the jurisdiction of U.S. law, children born in the United States to a foreign diplomatic officer accredited to the United States are not U.S. citizens at birth.1 The children may be eligible to register for lawful permanent residence (LPR) status under 8 CFR 101.3. If one parent is a foreign diplomatic officer and the other is a U.S. citizen, the child is a U.S. citizen at birth and ineligible to register LPR status.

This guidance, contained in Volumes 7 and 12 of the Policy Manual, is effective immediately and applies to requests pending or filed on or after the publication date. The guidance contained in the Policy Manual is controlling and supersedes any related prior guidance.
