USCIS出台临时最终规则,允许外国政府雇员子女登记永久居留权

发布日期 2026年9月9日生效日期 2026年9月4日

美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)发布一项临时最终规则,自2026年9月4日起生效,修订相关规定,允许在美国出生、非美国公民的外国政府雇员子女登记为合法永久居民,而不再被视为美国公民。该规则是为落实第14418号行政命令,扩大了此前仅适用于外交官的范围,USCIS同时就此规则公开征求意见,截止日期为2026年10月5日。

要点

  • 该规则仅适用于2026年9月4日及以后在美国出生的子女,此前出生者适用出生当时有效的规定。
  • USCIS现将外国政府雇员的定义扩大为包括大使、属于该国国民的外国使领馆雇员、受雇于外国政府的官员,以及享有国际组织豁免权的国际组织雇员。
  • 符合条件的子女不被承认为美国公民,但可自愿提交I-485表格申请登记为合法永久居民,获批后其永久居民身份可追溯至出生之日。
  • USCIS正相应修订I-485表格和G-325R表格。
  • 由于Casa Inc. v. Trump案中存在初步禁令,国土安全部表示在获得该禁令的解除之前,不会对该案认证集体成员适用此规则。
  • 该规则已作为临时最终规则生效,但USCIS仍接受公众意见,截止日期为2026年10月5日。

影响对象

受影响的是在外国政府、使领馆或特定国际组织工作、非美国公民的父母,以及他们在2026年9月4日及以后在美国出生的子女。

建议行动

受影响家庭及其律师在提交I-485表格前应先确认新的资格类别,如有意见可于2026年10月5日前通过regulations.gov提交。

II. Purpose and Background
A. 监管行动的目的
A. Purpose of the Regulatory Action
本次规则制定的目的在于澄清,某些在美国出生但非美国公民的外国人可登记其永久居民身份。这与第14418号行政命令(E.O.)《继续保护美国公民身份的含义和价值》(91 FR 51991,2026年8月6日)相一致,该命令指示国土安全部部长(部长)采取适当行动,防止向某些在美国出生的人签发承认其美国公民身份的文件,前提是该人的父母均非美国公民,且至少一方父母符合某些条件。
The purpose of this rulemaking is to clarify that certain aliens who are born in the United States but are not United States citizens may register their permanent resident status. This is consistent with Executive Order (E.O.) 14418, Continuing to Protect the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship, 91 FR 51991 (August 6, 2026), which directs the Secretary of Homeland Security (Secretary) to take appropriate action to prevent the issuance of documents recognizing United States citizenship to certain individuals born in the United States, where neither parent of that person is a U.S. citizen and at least one parent meets certain conditions.
B. 背景
B. Background
第十四修正案规定,凡“在美国出生......并受其管辖”的所有人,在出生时即获得美国公民身份。最高法院对“受其管辖”一词进行了解释,确立了若干出生公民权的例外情形,特别是“印第安部落成员的子女......在敌对占领期间由敌侨所生的子女,以及外国外交代表的子女”。参见美国诉黄金德案(U.S. v. Wong Kim Ark),169 U.S. 649, 682 (1898);另见特朗普诉巴巴拉案(Trump v. Barbara),609 U.S. ---, 146 S.Ct. 2438, 2456 (2026)(“......一个人『受』其所在国政府『管辖』......除非他属于若干常见例外情形之一,例如大使。”)。
The Fourteenth Amendment grants United States citizenship at birth to “all persons born . . . in the United States, and subject to the jurisdiction thereof.” The Supreme Court has interpreted the words “subject to the jurisdiction thereof” to create certain exceptions to birthright citizenship, notably “children of members of the Indian tribes . . . children born of alien enemies in hostile occupation, and children of diplomatic representatives of a foreign state.” See U.S. v. Wong Kim Ark, 169 U.S. 649, 682 (1898); see also Trump v. Barbara, 609 U.S. ---, 146 S.Ct. 2438, 2456 (2026) (“[A] person is `subject to the jurisdiction' of the government of the country in which he is physically present . . . unless he falls under one of the familiar exceptions, such as for ambassadors.”).
自至少20世纪40年代起,前移民归化局(INS)以及近年来的美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS),一直将出生于美国境内、父母为外国外交官且出生时未取得美国国籍的子女,视为有资格在美国登记合法永久居民身份的人员。参见Matter of Huang, 11 I&N Dec. 190 (BIA 1965)(该案将此做法追溯至“该局总法律顾问于1946年12月10日发布的一项意见书”);Matter of Chu, 14 I&N Dec. 241 (BIA 1972)。1982年,前移民归化局在8 CFR 101.3、101.4及264.2条发布法规,为出生于美国境内、父母为经美国认可的外国外交官的人员建立正式的合法永久居民身份记录创建程序。[1]
Since at least the 1940s, the former Immigration and Naturalization Service (INS), and more recently USCIS, have treated children born in the United States to foreign diplomatic officers who do not acquire citizenship at birth as eligible to register lawful permanent residence in the United States. See Matter of Huang, 11 I&N Dec. 190 (BIA 1965) (tracing this practice back to “an opinion dated December 10, 1946, by the General Counsel of the Service”); Matter of Chu, 14 I&N Dec. 241 (BIA 1972). In 1982, the former INS published regulations, at 8 CFR 101.3, 101.4, and 264.2, to create a formal procedure for creation of records of lawful permanent residence for individuals born in the United States to foreign diplomatic officers accredited to the United States.[1]
这些法规至今仍然有效,其规定经美国认可的外国外交官的子女,根据美国宪法第十四修正案不属于美国公民,但可自愿登记为美国合法永久居民。为进行登记,申请人必须提交I-485表,即《登记永久居留身份或调整身份申请表》,并证明其未放弃在美国的居留权。如申请获批准,申请人将被视为自其出生之日起即为合法永久居民。
These regulations, which are still in force today, state that a child of a foreign diplomatic officer accredited to the United States is not a U.S. citizen under the Fourteenth Amendment but may voluntarily register as a lawful permanent resident of the United States. To register, the applicant must file Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status, and establish that he or she has not abandoned his or her residence in the United States. If the application is granted, the applicant is considered a lawful permanent resident as of his or her date of birth.
2026年8月6日,特朗普总统签署第14418号行政命令,题为《继续维护美国公民身份的意义与价值》。参见91 FR 51991。该行政命令确定了某些不属于最高法院在Barbara案中所宣布的出生公民权规则范围内、出生于美国境内的儿童类别。该行政命令第2节所列类别中,包括没有美国公民父母、且其父母一方为“外国政府雇员”的人员,该术语的定义包括:(一)大使;(二)受雇于外国使馆或领事馆、且为该外国国民的人员;(三)以官方身份受雇于外国政府的人员;以及(四)受雇于具有国际组织豁免权的国际组织的人员。参见同上第2(b)节。该行政命令规定,国土安全部(DHS)不得“为上述人员签发承认美国公民身份的文件”,也不得“接受州、地方或其他政府或机构出具的、旨在承认美国公民身份的文件”。参见同上。
On August 6, 2026, President Trump issued E.O. 14418, Continuing to Protect the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship. See91 FR 51991. The E.O. identifies certain categories of children born in the United States who do not fall within the rule of birthright citizenship as announced by the Supreme Court in Barbara. Among these categories identified in Section 2 of the E.O. are persons who do not have a U.S. citizen parent, and either of their parents is a “foreign government employee, defined to include: (i) ambassadors; (ii) persons employed by a foreign embassy or consulate who are nationals of that foreign country; (iii) persons employed by a foreign government in an official capacity; and (iv) persons employed by an international organization that possess international-organization immunity.” See id. at Sec. 2(b). 1. 外交事务
1. Foreign Affairs
本规则因涉及美国外交事务职能而免于事先通知和征求意见以及延迟生效日期的要求。见5 U.S.C. 553(a)条。如果某项行为“明确且直接”涉及外交事务职能,则属于该例外情形。[6] 直接涉及外交事务处理的案件包括规范在美外国外交官的规则。[7] 在“纽约市诉印度常驻联合国代表团案”(City of N.Y. v. Permanent Mission of India to the U.N.)中,第二巡回上诉法院认定,美国国务院(DOS)关于州和地方政府征收不动产税豁免事项的联邦公报通知,因其对外交关系的规范直接涉及美国的外交事务职能,而有效地援引了外交事务例外。[8]
This rule is exempt from notice and comment and the delayed effective date because it pertains to the foreign affairs function of the United States. 5 U.S.C. 553(a). An action falls within the exception if it “clearly and directly” involves a foreign affairs function.[6] Cases that directly involve the conduct of foreign affairs include rules that regulate foreign diplomats in the United States.[7] In City of N.Y. v. Permanent Mission of India to the U.N., the Second Circuit found that a State Department Federal Register Notice regarding exemptions from real property taxes imposed by state and local governments validly invoked the foreign affairs exemption because the regulation of diplomatic relations directly involves a foreign affairs function of the United States.[8]
本规则规范外国政府雇员所生子女永久居民身份的登记事宜,这些子女此前原本会被视为在出生时即为美国公民。由于这些子女是外国政府雇员在美国境内所生,其身份具有国际影响,包括极有可能对美国驻外外交、政府及国际组织雇员子女的待遇产生重大对等后果。若无规则规范此类子女的身份,这些子女在未持有护照或签证的情况下,可能难以随其父母在美国境内及境外旅行,并可能因此面临被美国递解出境的风险。由于本规则规范外国政府雇员子女的待遇,涉及各国之间多项外交及国际协定与安排,因此直接涉及外交事务职能。若美国国土安全部(DHS)不迅速采取行动处理在美出生的外国政府雇员子女的身份问题,与美国在外交、政府及国际组织人员方面存在对等安排的其他国家,可能会相应修改其本国规则,从而对美国人员与外国伙伴接触及开展对外和国际关系工作的能力产生不利影响。此外,由于本规则涉及外国政府雇员在美国所生子女的身份问题,援引外交事务例外的做法亦得到美国国务院《外交事务职能认定》(DOS Determination: Foreign Affairs Functions of the United States),90 FR 12200(2025年3月14日)的支持。[9]
This rule governs the registration of permanent resident status for children born to foreign government employees who previously would have been considered United States citizens at birth. Because these children are born in the United States to foreign government employees, the status of these children has international implications, including likely significant reciprocal consequences for the treatment of children of U.S. diplomatic, government, and international organization employees overseas. In the absence of a rule governing the status of these children, these children may have a difficult time traveling within and outside of the United States with their parents if they do not possess a passport or a visa and may otherwise be subject to removal from the United States. Because this rule regulates the treatment of children of foreign government employees which implicates various diplomatic and international agreements and arrangements between countries, it directly involves a foreign affairs function. In the absence of DHS moving expeditiously to address the status of children of foreign government employees born in the United States, other countries with whom the United States has reciprocal arrangements regarding diplomatic, government, and international organization personnel may change their own rules in a way that adversely affects the ability of U.S. personnel to engage with foreign partners and conduct the work of foreign and international relations. Additionally, because this rule pertains to the status of children born to foreign government employees in the United States, the reliance on the foreign affairs exception is also supported by the DOS Determination: Foreign Affairs Functions of the United States, 90 FR 12200 (Mar. 14, 2025).[9]

2. Good Cause

In addition to the foreign affairs exception, DHS may forgo notice and comment rulemaking when the agency “for good cause finds . . . that notice and public procedure thereon are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest.” See5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B). Likewise, section 553(d)'s requirement of 30-day delayed effective date may be waived by the agency for good cause found and published with the rule. See5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3).
正当理由例外中的“不切实际”要件,适用于豁免紧急情形下的事先通知和征求意见程序。[10] 尽管正当理由例外须“从严解释,且仅在不得已的情况下方予认可”,[11] 但它是一项重要的安全阀,适用于即便并非紧急情形,因事先通知和征求意见程序造成的延误将带来实质性损害的情形。[12] 是否构成事先通知和征求意见制定规则程序“不切实际”,须根据具体制定规则事项所涉及的特定事实和背景加以判断。[13] 就本案而言,国土安全部认定,鉴于本规则其他部分所述,外国政府雇员所生且父母中无美国公民的子女,并非美国公民,因此开展事先通知和征求意见程序将不切实际。有鉴于此,国土安全部力求尽快建立一项程序,使本规则生效日期当日或之后出生的子女能够自愿登记为合法永久居民,以确保其在美国具有合法身份。
The “impracticable” prong of the good cause exception excuses notice and comment in emergency situations.[10] Although the good cause exception is “narrowly construed and only ( printed page 57253) reluctantly countenanced,” [11] it is an important safety valve to be used where delay caused by notice and comment would do real harm (even absent an emergency situation).[12] Whether undergoing notice and comment rulemaking is impracticable is based on the particular facts and context present with respect to a specific rulemaking.[13] In this case, DHS finds that engaging in notice and comment rulemaking would be impracticable because as discussed elsewhere in this rule, children born to foreign government employees who do not have a U.S. citizen parent are not U.S. citizens. Therefore, DHS is aiming to put in place a procedure for children born on or after the effective date of this rule to have the ability to voluntarily register as lawful permanent residents as soon as possible in order to ensure that they have a lawful status in the United States.
善因例外情形适用于在给予事先通知和征求意见会损害公共利益的情形下。见5 U.S.C. 553(b)。当《行政程序法》(APA)规定的常规程序,即通常被推定为符合公共利益的程序,实际上会损害公共利益时,即满足本项要件。[14] 当常规程序的时间安排和披露要求会破坏该提案的目的并损害公共利益时,适用该例外情形是适当的。[15] 善因例外情形的这一项要件与“不可行”这一项要件密切相关。正如在外交事务例外情形的背景下所讨论的那样,进行通知和征求意见的规则制定程序并延迟生效日期将有悖于公共利益,因为使外国政府雇员子女在美国登记合法移民身份方面缺乏明确途径,将影响这些子女及其父母,进而可能影响身处国外、处境相似的美国外交官、政府和国际组织雇员及其子女所受到的待遇。基于上述原因,美国国土安全部(DHS)援引善因例外情形,不进行通知和征求意见的规则制定程序,也不延迟生效日期。
The good cause exception applies when affording prior notice and comment would be contrary to the public interest. See5 U.S.C. 553(b). This prong is met when the ordinary procedures under the APA—generally presumed to serve the public interest—would in fact harm the interest of the public.[14] The exception is appropriately invoked when the timing and the disclosure requirement of the usual procedures would defeat the purpose of the proposal and harm the public interest.[15] This prong of the good cause exception is closely related to the impracticable prong. As discussed in the context of the foreign affairs exception, undergoing notice and comment rulemaking and a delayed effective date would be contrary to public interest because leaving children of foreign government employees without a clear path to register lawful immigration status in the United States would affect those children and their parents and in turn would likely impact the treatment of similarly situated United States diplomats, government and international organization employees and their children abroad. For these reasons, DHS is invoking the good cause exceptions to forgo notice and comment rulemaking and a delayed effective date.

DHS is updating its regulations to provide a way for additional children who are born in the United States, but are not U.S. citizens, to register as lawful permanent residents. Specifically, this IFR impacts children born in the United States where neither parent is a U.S. citizen and at least one parent is a foreign government employee. This rule amends the existing regulations at 8 CFR 101.3, 101.4, and 264.2 to apply more broadly to children born to certain foreign government employees.

DHS is extending the existing process for registering permanent residence (Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status) to other children of foreign government employees who lack birthright citizenship. DHS is also making related updates to alien registration requirements to reflect this expanded category. The changes in this rule are discussed in depth above, in Section III—Discussion of Interim Final Rule.
本规则所影响的人群是出生于美国的、某些外国政府雇员的子女,这些子女除非父母中至少一方为美国公民,否则不会在出生时取得美国公民身份。这包括外国外交官、某些使馆或领事馆雇员、外国政府官员以及享有国际组织豁免权的国际组织雇员的子女。美国国土安全部(DHS)无法量化受本规则影响的这一新人群,因为美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)历来未将这一人群作为一个独立、结构化的类别加以追踪。美国国务院(DOS)外国使团办公室报告称,美国接待的外国使团成员及其家属超过124,000人。[17]然而,DHS无法利用这一数字来估算受本规则影响的人群,因为现有数据并未显示有多少外国政府雇员拥有美国公民配偶、在子女出生前离开美国,或在子女出生后离开美国且不再返回。因此,DHS缺乏足够的信息来更准确地估算将受本规则影响的在美出生儿童的数量。故此,DHS对本规则所影响的人群进行定性讨论。
The impacted population of this rule is children born in the United States to certain foreign government employees who would not acquire U.S. citizenship at birth unless at least one parent is a U.S. citizen. This includes children of foreign diplomats, certain embassy or consulate employees, foreign government officials, and employees of international organizations with international-organization immunity. DHS is unable to quantify this new population of individuals who will be impacted by the rule because USCIS has not historically tracked this population as a distinct, structured category. The DOS Office of Foreign Missions reports that the United States hosts more than 124,000 foreign mission members and dependents.[17] However, DHS is unable to use this figure to estimate the population affected by this rule because available data does not identify how many foreign government employees have a U.S. citizen spouse, depart the United States prior to the birth of a child, or depart the United States after the birth of a child and do not return. Accordingly, DHS lacks sufficient information to more accurately estimate the number of U.S. born children who would be affected by this rule. Therefore, DHS discusses the affected population of this rule qualitatively.
本规则的变化将使新受影响的个人须遵守其在现行法规下本不适用的移民相关登记和文件要求。新受影响的个人可能因使用G-325R表格《传记信息(登记)》(printed page 57254)履行外国人登记要求而产生费用,[18]对于那些自愿选择使用I-485表格登记为合法永久居民的人,也可能产生相关费用。
The changes in this rule will subject newly affected individuals to immigration-related registration and documentation requirements that would not apply to them under the current regulations. Newly affected individuals may incur costs associated with alien ( printed page 57254) registration requirements using Form G-325R, Biographic Information (Registration) [18] and for those who voluntarily choose to do so, registering as lawful permanent residents using Form I-485.
新受影响的外国人群体可能因填写和提交G-325R表格而产生费用。这些费用包括父母或法定监护人创建在线账户、收集必要信息、填写并提交表格以及遵守任何适用的生物识别要求所需的时间。对于14岁以下儿童,父母或法定监护人必须确保该儿童完成登记,并必须为该儿童创建个人USCIS在线账户并代其提交G-325R表格。个人在年满14岁后的30天内必须重新登记并提供指纹。USCIS目前估计申请人填写G-325R表格所需的时间负担为2.5小时。预计本规则不会改变这一时间负担,且G-325R表格目前无需缴纳申请费。

该规则的益处包括为外国政府雇员在美国出生子女的合法永久居民身份登记建立统一的监管框架。该规则将为受影响个人和联邦机构提供关于公民身份认定、合法永久居民登记以及外国人登记要求的更明确指导。通过将现行I-485表格程序扩展至新受影响的外国人群体,该规则还将为符合条件的个人提供一条既定途径,使其能够自愿登记成为合法永久居民。
The benefits of this rule include establishing a consistent regulatory framework for registering lawful permanent resident status of U.S.-born children of foreign government employees. C. Regulatory Flexibility Act (RFA)
《监管灵活性法案》(RFA),《美国法典》第5编第601-612节,经1996年《小企业监管执法公平法案》(SBREFA)(公法104-121号,1996年3月29日)修订,要求联邦机构在制定规则过程中考虑规则对小实体的潜在影响,以确定是否会对相当数量的小实体产生重大经济影响。“小实体”一词包括小型企业、在其所属领域不占主导地位的非营利组织,以及人口少于50,000人的政府管辖区。“个人”不被视为小实体,因此就RFA而言,不考虑规则对个人的影响。[45] 参见《美国法典》第5编第601节、第632节。此外,法院已裁定,RFA仅要求机构在规则直接监管小实体时,针对小实体影响进行监管灵活性分析。[46] 因此,就RFA而言,不考虑规则对小实体的间接影响。
The Regulatory Flexibility Act (RFA), 5 U.S.C. 601-612, as amended by the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act of 1996 (SBREFA), Public Law 104-121 (Mar. 29, 1996), requires Federal agencies to consider the potential impact of regulations on small entities during the development of their rules to determine whether there will be a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities. The term “small entities” comprises small businesses, not-for-profit organizations that are not dominant in their fields, and governmental jurisdictions with populations of less than 50,000. An “individual” is not considered a small entity and therefore a rule's impact on individuals is not considered for RFA purposes.[45] See5 U.S.C. 601, 632. In addition, the courts have held that the RFA requires an agency to perform a regulatory flexibility analysis of small entity impacts only when a rule directly regulates small entities.[46] Consequently, a rule's indirect impacts on a small entity is not considered for RFA purposes.
本规则不直接规范小实体,预计也不会对其产生直接影响。本规则规范的是个人,而根据《管制弹性法》(RFA),个人不属于“小实体”的定义范围。根据上文监管影响分析(RIA)及整个前言中所述信息,国土安全部(DHS)证明本规则不会对大量小实体产生重大经济影响。
This rule does not directly regulate small entities and is not expected to have a direct effect on them. The rule regulates individuals, and individuals are not defined as “small entities” by the RFA.
