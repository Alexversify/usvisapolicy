USCIS出台临时最终规则,允许外国政府雇员子女登记永久居留权

发布日期 2026年9月9日生效日期 2026年9月4日

美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)发布一项临时最终规则,自2026年9月4日起生效,修订相关规定,允许在美国出生、非美国公民的外国政府雇员子女登记为合法永久居民,而不再被视为美国公民。该规则是为落实第14418号行政命令,扩大了此前仅适用于外交官的范围,USCIS同时就此规则公开征求意见,截止日期为2026年10月5日。

要点

  • 该规则仅适用于2026年9月4日及以后在美国出生的子女,此前出生者适用出生当时有效的规定。
  • USCIS现将外国政府雇员的定义扩大为包括大使、属于该国国民的外国使领馆雇员、受雇于外国政府的官员,以及享有国际组织豁免权的国际组织雇员。
  • 符合条件的子女不被承认为美国公民,但可自愿提交I-485表格申请登记为合法永久居民,获批后其永久居民身份可追溯至出生之日。
  • USCIS正相应修订I-485表格和G-325R表格。
  • 由于Casa Inc. v. Trump案中存在初步禁令,国土安全部表示在获得该禁令的解除之前,不会对该案认证集体成员适用此规则。
  • 该规则已作为临时最终规则生效,但USCIS仍接受公众意见,截止日期为2026年10月5日。

影响对象

受影响的是在外国政府、使领馆或特定国际组织工作、非美国公民的父母,以及他们在2026年9月4日及以后在美国出生的子女。

建议行动

受影响家庭及其律师在提交I-485表格前应先确认新的资格类别,如有意见可于2026年10月5日前通过regulations.gov提交。

二、目的与背景
II. Purpose and Background
A. 监管行动的目的
A. Purpose of the Regulatory Action
本次规则制定的目的是明确规定,某些在美国出生但非美国公民的外国人可以登记其永久居民身份。这与第14418号行政命令《继续维护美国公民身份的意义与价值》(91 FR 51991,2026年8月6日)保持一致,该行政命令指示国土安全部部长(部长)采取适当行动,防止向某些在美国出生的人士颁发承认美国公民身份的文件,前提是该人的父母均非美国公民,且父母中至少一方符合特定条件。
The purpose of this rulemaking is to clarify that certain aliens who are born in the United States but are not United States citizens may register their permanent resident status. This is consistent with Executive Order (E.O.) 14418, Continuing to Protect the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship, 91 FR 51991 (August 6, 2026), which directs the Secretary of Homeland Security (Secretary) to take appropriate action to prevent the issuance of documents recognizing United States citizenship to certain individuals born in the United States, where neither parent of that person is a U.S. citizen and at least one parent meets certain conditions.
B. 背景
B. Background
美国宪法第十四修正案规定,凡在美国出生并受其管辖的所有人,在出生时即获得美国公民身份。最高法院对“受其管辖”一词作出解释,确立了出生公民权的若干例外情形,特别是“印第安部落成员的子女……在敌对占领期间由敌国侨民所生的子女,以及外国外交代表的子女”。见美国诉黄金德案,169 U.S. 649, 682 (1898);另见特朗普诉巴巴拉案,609 U.S. ---, 146 S.Ct. 2438, 2456 (2026)(“一个人'受'其所在国政府的'管辖',除非其属于若干常见例外情形之一,例如大使等。”)。
The Fourteenth Amendment grants United States citizenship at birth to “all persons born . . . in the United States, and subject to the jurisdiction thereof.” The Supreme Court has interpreted the words “subject to the jurisdiction thereof” to create certain exceptions to birthright citizenship, notably “children of members of the Indian tribes . . . children born of alien enemies in hostile occupation, and children of diplomatic representatives of a foreign state.” See U.S. v. Wong Kim Ark, 169 U.S. 649, 682 (1898); see also Trump v. Barbara, 609 U.S. ---, 146 S.Ct. 2438, 2456 (2026) (“[A] person is `subject to the jurisdiction' of the government of the country in which he is physically present . . . unless he falls under one of the familiar exceptions, such as for ambassadors.”).
自20世纪40年代以来,前移民归化局(INS)以及近期的美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)一直将出生在美国但出生时未获得公民身份的外国外交官员子女视为有资格在美国登记获得合法永久居民身份。参见Matter of Huang, 11 I&N Dec. 190 (BIA 1965)(将此做法追溯至“该局总法律顾问于1946年12月10日发表的一项意见”);Matter of Chu, 14 I&N Dec. 241 (BIA 1972)。1982年,前移民归化局在8 CFR第101.3条、第101.4条及第264.2条中发布法规,为在美国出生且其父母为获美国认可之外国外交官员的个人建立合法永久居民身份记录创设了正式程序。[1]
Since at least the 1940s, the former Immigration and Naturalization Service (INS), and more recently USCIS, have treated children born in the United States to foreign diplomatic officers who do not acquire citizenship at birth as eligible to register lawful permanent residence in the United States. See Matter of Huang, 11 I&N Dec. 190 (BIA 1965) (tracing this practice back to “an opinion dated December 10, 1946, by the General Counsel of the Service”); Matter of Chu, 14 I&N Dec. 241 (BIA 1972). In 1982, the former INS published regulations, at 8 CFR 101.3, 101.4, and 264.2, to create a formal procedure for creation of records of lawful permanent residence for individuals born in the United States to foreign diplomatic officers accredited to the United States.[1]
这些至今仍然有效的法规规定,获美国认可之外国外交官员的子女根据第十四修正案不属于美国公民,但可自愿登记为美国合法永久居民。为进行登记,申请人必须提交I-485表格《登记永久居留或调整身份申请书》,并证明其未放弃其在美国的居留权。若该申请获得批准,申请人将被视为自其出生之日起即为合法永久居民。
These regulations, which are still in force today, state that a child of a foreign diplomatic officer accredited to the United States is not a U.S. citizen under the Fourteenth Amendment but may voluntarily register as a lawful permanent resident of the United States. To register, the applicant must file Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status, and establish that he or she has not abandoned his or her residence in the United States. If the application is granted, the applicant is considered a lawful permanent resident as of his or her date of birth.
2026年8月6日,特朗普总统发布了第14418号行政命令《继续保护美国公民身份的含义与价值》。参见91 FR 51991。该行政命令确定了若干类别的出生在美国的儿童,这些儿童不属于最高法院在Barbara案中所宣布的出生地公民身份规则所涵盖的范围。该行政命令第2节所确定的这些类别中,包括没有美国公民父母、且其父母中任一方为“外国政府雇员”的人员,该术语的定义包括:(i)大使;(ii)受雇于外国大使馆或领事馆且为该外国国民的人员;(iii)以官方身份受雇于外国政府的人员;以及(iv)受雇于享有国际组织豁免权的国际组织的人员。参见同上,第2(b)节。该行政命令规定,国土安全部(DHS)不得向上述人员“签发承认美国公民身份的文件”,也不得接受州、地方或其他政府或当局为承认这些人员具有美国公民身份而签发的文件。参见同上。
On August 6, 2026, President Trump issued E.O. 14418, Continuing to Protect the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship. See91 FR 51991. The E.O. identifies certain categories of children born in the United States who do not fall within the rule of birthright citizenship as announced by the Supreme Court in Barbara. Among these categories identified in Section 2 of the E.O. are persons who do not have a U.S. citizen parent, and either of their parents is a “foreign government employee, defined to include: (i) ambassadors; (ii) persons employed by a foreign embassy or consulate who are nationals of that foreign country; (iii) persons employed by a foreign government in an official capacity; and (iv) persons employed by an international organization that possess international-organization immunity.” See id. at Sec. 2(b). 本规则规范此前原本会被视为出生即具有美国公民身份的外国政府雇员子女的永久居民身份登记事宜。由于这些子女是在美国出生的外国政府雇员的子女,其身份具有国际影响,包括极有可能对海外美国外交、政府及国际组织雇员子女的待遇产生重大的对等后果。如果没有一项规则来规范这些子女的身份,则这些子女在没有护照或签证的情况下可能难以随父母在美国境内及境外旅行,并可能因此面临被驱逐出美国的风险。由于本规则涉及外国政府雇员子女的待遇问题,而该问题关系到各国之间的多项外交和国际协议及安排,因此本规则直接涉及外交事务职能。如果国土安全部(DHS)不迅速采取行动,处理在美国出生的外国政府雇员子女的身份问题,那么与美国就外交、政府及国际组织人员安排有对等协议的其他国家,可能会改变本国规则,从而对美国人员与外国伙伴开展交往以及开展外交和国际关系工作的能力产生不利影响。此外,由于本规则涉及在美国出生的外国政府雇员子女的身份问题,援引外交事务例外规定的做法同样得到美国国务院《决定:美国的外交事务职能》(90 FR 12200,2025年3月14日)的支持。[9]
This rule governs the registration of permanent resident status for children born to foreign government employees who previously would have been considered United States citizens at birth. Because these children are born in the United States to foreign government employees, the status of these children has international implications, including likely significant reciprocal consequences for the treatment of children of U.S. diplomatic, government, and international organization employees overseas. In the absence of a rule governing the status of these children, these children may have a difficult time traveling within and outside of the United States with their parents if they do not possess a passport or a visa and may otherwise be subject to removal from the United States. Because this rule regulates the treatment of children of foreign government employees which implicates various diplomatic and international agreements and arrangements between countries, it directly involves a foreign affairs function. In the absence of DHS moving expeditiously to address the status of children of foreign government employees born in the United States, other countries with whom the United States has reciprocal arrangements regarding diplomatic, government, and international organization personnel may change their own rules in a way that adversely affects the ability of U.S. personnel to engage with foreign partners and conduct the work of foreign and international relations. Additionally, because this rule pertains to the status of children born to foreign government employees in the United States, the reliance on the foreign affairs exception is also supported by the DOS Determination: Foreign Affairs Functions of the United States, 90 FR 12200 (Mar. 14, 2025).[9]

In addition to the foreign affairs exception, DHS may forgo notice and comment rulemaking when the agency “for good cause finds . . . that notice and public procedure thereon are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest.” See5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B). Likewise, section 553(d)'s requirement of 30-day delayed effective date may be waived by the agency for good cause found and published with the rule. See5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3).
正当理由例外中的“不可行”这一要件适用于在紧急情况下豁免事先通知和征求意见程序。[10] 尽管正当理由例外规定应“从严解释,且仅在不得已的情况下才予以认可”(第57253页印刷版), [11] 但它仍是一项重要的安全阀机制,适用于因事先通知和征求意见程序造成的延误会带来实际损害的情形(即便并非紧急情况)。[12] 是否进行事先通知和征求意见的立法程序属于不可行,须根据具体规则制定所涉及的特定事实和背景加以判断。[13] 在本案中,国土安全部(DHS)认定,鉴于本规则其他部分所述,不具有美国公民父母的外国政府雇员子女并非美国公民,因此进行事先通知和征求意见的立法程序将不可行。因此,国土安全部(DHS)力求为在本规则生效日期当日或之后出生的子女尽快建立一项程序,使其能够自愿登记为合法永久居民,以确保其在美国拥有合法身份。
The “impracticable” prong of the good cause exception excuses notice and comment in emergency situations.[10] Although the good cause exception is “narrowly construed and only ( printed page 57253) reluctantly countenanced,” [11] it is an important safety valve to be used where delay caused by notice and comment would do real harm (even absent an emergency situation).[12] Whether undergoing notice and comment rulemaking is impracticable is based on the particular facts and context present with respect to a specific rulemaking.[13] In this case, DHS finds that engaging in notice and comment rulemaking would be impracticable because as discussed elsewhere in this rule, children born to foreign government employees who do not have a U.S. citizen parent are not U.S. citizens. Therefore, DHS is aiming to put in place a procedure for children born on or after the effective date of this rule to have the ability to voluntarily register as lawful permanent residents as soon as possible in order to ensure that they have a lawful status in the United States.
善因例外适用于事先给予通知和征求意见将不利于公共利益的情形。参见5 U.S.C. 553(b)。当《行政程序法》(APA)下的通常程序(通常被推定为有利于公共利益)实际上会损害公共利益时,即满足该要件。[14] 当通常程序所要求的时间安排和披露要求将破坏该提案的目的并损害公共利益时,援引该例外是适当的。[15] 善因例外的这一要件与不可行要件密切相关。正如在外交事务例外的背景下所讨论的,进行通知和征求意见的规则制定以及延迟生效日期将不利于公共利益,因为若外国政府雇员的子女在美国缺乏明确的登记合法移民身份的途径,将影响这些子女及其父母,进而可能影响境外处于类似情形的美国外交官、政府和国际组织雇员及其子女所受到的待遇。基于上述原因,国土安全部(DHS)援引善因例外,免于进行通知和征求意见的规则制定以及延迟生效日期。
The good cause exception applies when affording prior notice and comment would be contrary to the public interest. See5 U.S.C. 553(b). This prong is met when the ordinary procedures under the APA—generally presumed to serve the public interest—would in fact harm the interest of the public.[14] The exception is appropriately invoked when the timing and the disclosure requirement of the usual procedures would defeat the purpose of the proposal and harm the public interest.[15] This prong of the good cause exception is closely related to the impracticable prong. As discussed in the context of the foreign affairs exception, undergoing notice and comment rulemaking and a delayed effective date would be contrary to public interest because leaving children of foreign government employees without a clear path to register lawful immigration status in the United States would affect those children and their parents and in turn would likely impact the treatment of similarly situated United States diplomats, government and international organization employees and their children abroad. For these reasons, DHS is invoking the good cause exceptions to forgo notice and comment rulemaking and a delayed effective date.

E.O. 13563 emphasizes the importance of quantifying both costs and benefits, of reducing costs, of harmonizing rules, and of promoting flexibility. The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) has designated this rule a “significant regulatory action” under section 3(f) of E.O. 12866, although not economically significant under section 3(f)(1). Accordingly, the rule has been reviewed by OMB. This rule is not an E.O. 14192 regulatory action because it is being issued with respect to an immigration-related function of the United States. The rule's primary direct purpose is to implement or interpret the immigration laws of the United States (as described in INA 101(a)(17), 8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(17)) or any other function performed by the U.S. Federal Government with respect to aliens.[16]
国土安全部(DHS)正在更新其法规,为在美国出生但非美国公民的更多儿童提供登记为合法永久居民的途径。具体而言,本临时最终规则(IFR)影响的是在美国出生、父母双方均非美国公民、且至少一方父母为外国政府雇员的儿童。本规则修订了8 CFR 101.3、101.4和264.2中的现行法规,使其更广泛地适用于某些外国政府雇员所生的子女。
DHS is updating its regulations to provide a way for additional children who are born in the United States, but are not U.S. citizens, to register as lawful permanent residents. Specifically, this IFR impacts children born in the United States where neither parent is a U.S. citizen and at least one parent is a foreign government employee. This rule amends the existing regulations at 8 CFR 101.3, 101.4, and 264.2 to apply more broadly to children born to certain foreign government employees.
国土安全部(DHS)正将现有的登记永久居留程序(I-485表,《登记永久居留或调整身份申请表》)扩展适用于缺乏出生公民身份的外国政府雇员的其他子女。国土安全部(DHS)还对外国人登记要求作出相关更新,以反映这一扩大后的类别。本规则所作的变更已在上文第三部分“临时最终规则讨论”中详细阐述。
DHS is extending the existing process for registering permanent residence (Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status) to other children of foreign government employees who lack birthright citizenship. DHS is also making related updates to alien registration requirements to reflect this expanded category. The changes in this rule are discussed in depth above, in Section III—Discussion of Interim Final Rule.
本规则所影响的人群是出生在美国的某些外国政府雇员子女,这些子女除非父母中至少有一方为美国公民,否则不会在出生时自动获得美国国籍。此类人群包括外国外交官的子女、某些使馆或领事馆雇员的子女、外国政府官员的子女,以及享有国际组织豁免权的国际组织雇员的子女。由于美国公民及移民服务局(USCIS)历来并未将此类人群作为一个独立、结构化的类别加以追踪,因此国土安全部(DHS)无法对将因本规则而受影响的这一新人群进行量化。美国国务院外国使团办公室报告称,美国境内有超过124,000名外国使团成员及其家属。[17] 然而,国土安全部无法利用该数字来估算受本规则影响的人群,因为现有数据并未显示有多少外国政府雇员拥有美国公民配偶、在子女出生前离境,或在子女出生后离境且未再返回。因此,国土安全部缺乏足够信息来更准确地估算将受本规则影响的美国出生子女人数。故此,国土安全部对本规则所涉受影响人群仅作定性讨论。
The impacted population of this rule is children born in the United States to certain foreign government employees who would not acquire U.S. citizenship at birth unless at least one parent is a U.S. citizen. This includes children of foreign diplomats, certain embassy or consulate employees, foreign government officials, and employees of international organizations with international-organization immunity. DHS is unable to quantify this new population of individuals who will be impacted by the rule because USCIS has not historically tracked this population as a distinct, structured category. The DOS Office of Foreign Missions reports that the United States hosts more than 124,000 foreign mission members and dependents.[17] However, DHS is unable to use this figure to estimate the population affected by this rule because available data does not identify how many foreign government employees have a U.S. citizen spouse, depart the United States prior to the birth of a child, or depart the United States after the birth of a child and do not return. Accordingly, DHS lacks sufficient information to more accurately estimate the number of U.S. born children who would be affected by this rule. Therefore, DHS discusses the affected population of this rule qualitatively.
本规则的变化将使新受影响的个人须遵守移民相关的登记和文件要求,而根据现行法规,这些要求原本并不适用于他们。新受影响的个人可能需要承担与外国人登记要求相关的费用,使用G-325R表格《人口统计信息(登记)》(第57254页)[18],对于那些自愿选择登记为合法永久居民的人,则需使用I-485表格进行登记。
The changes in this rule will subject newly affected individuals to immigration-related registration and documentation requirements that would not apply to them under the current regulations. Newly affected individuals may incur costs associated with alien ( printed page 57254) registration requirements using Form G-325R, Biographic Information (Registration) [18] and for those who voluntarily choose to do so, registering as lawful permanent residents using Form I-485.
新受影响的外国人群体可能需要承担与填写和提交G-325R表格相关的费用。这些费用包括父母或法定监护人创建在线账户、收集必要信息、填写并提交表格,以及遵守任何适用的生物特征识别要求所需的时间。对于14岁以下的儿童,父母或法定监护人必须确保该儿童完成登记,并须代表该儿童创建个人USCIS在线账户并提交G-325R表格。个人在年满14岁后的30天内必须重新登记并提供指纹。USCIS目前估计申请人完成G-325R表格所需的时间负担为2.5小时。预计本规则不会改变该时间负担,且G-325R表格目前无需缴纳申请费。
The newly impacted alien population may incur costs associated with completing and filing Form G-325R. These costs include the time required for a parent or legal guardian to create an online account, gather the necessary information, complete and submit the form, and comply with any applicable biometric requirements. For children under 14 years of age, a parent or legal guardian must ensure that the child is registered and must create an individual USCIS online account and submit Form G-325R on the child's behalf. Individuals must re-register and provide fingerprints within 30 days after reaching the age of 14. USCIS' current estimated time burden for an applicant to complete Form G-325R is 2.5 hours. This time burden is not expected to change as a result of this rule and Form G-325R currently has no filing fee.
国土安全部按如下方式计算完成和提交G-325R表格以及提交所需生物特征信息的费用。对于受影响人群,我们采用所有职业的平均时薪(33.54美元)。[19] 为估算总薪酬,我们将平均时薪乘以民用雇员的薪酬与工资比率(1.46)。[20] 由此得出的估计平均时薪总薪酬为48.97美元。[21] 按总薪酬费率计算,完成G-325R表格的时间机会成本为122.43美元。[22]
DHS calculates the costs to complete and submit Form G-325R as well as the cost to submit the required biometrics as follows. For the affected population, we use the mean hourly wage of all occupations ($33.54).[19] To estimate total compensation, we multiply the mean hourly wage by the compensation to wage ratio for civilian employees (1.46).[20] This results in an estimated mean hourly total compensation of $48.97.[21] The opportunity cost of time to complete Form G-325R is $122.43 at the total compensation rate.[22]
为估算提交生物特征信息的费用,我们考虑了提交生物特征信息所需的时间负担、往返申请支持中心(ASC)的时间负担,以及往返申请支持中心的车辆费用。提交G-325R表格生物特征信息的估计时间负担为1.17小时。[23] 提交生物特征信息的估计时间机会成本为57.29美元。[24] 往返申请支持中心的估计平均旅行距离为50英里;预计总旅行时间为2.5小时。[25] 往返申请支持中心的估计时间机会成本为122.43美元。[26] 往返申请支持中心的车辆费用基于总务管理局(General Service Administration)就使用私人车辆出行的每英里报销费率,目前为每英里0.76美元。[27] 往返申请支持中心的估计车辆费用为38.00美元。[28] 在申请支持中心提交生物特征信息的估计费用为217.72美元。[29] 登记并提交G-325R表格生物特征信息的估计每人合规总费用为340.15美元。[30]
To estimate the costs of submitting biometrics, we consider the time burden to submit biometrics, the time burden to travel to and from an Application Support Center (ASC), and the vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC. The estimated time burden to submit biometrics for Form G-325R is 1.17 hours.[23] The estimated opportunity cost of time to submit biometrics is $57.29.[24] The estimated average travel distance to and from an ASC is 50 miles; the expected total travel time is 2.5 hours.[25] The estimated opportunity cost of time to travel to and from an ASC is $122.43.[26] The vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC are based on the General Service Administration's per mile reimbursement rate for traveling in a privately owned vehicle—currently $0.76 per mile.[27] The estimated vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC are $38.00.[28] The estimated cost to submit biometrics at an ASC is $217.72.[29] The estimated total per person compliance cost to register and submit biometrics for Form G-325R is $340.15.[30]
本规则还将使新受影响的个人有资格自愿提交I-485表以登记成为合法永久居民。如果此类个人在提交申请时未满十八周岁，其父母或法定监护人应代表申请人填写并签署I-485表。选择此方式的个人将承担相应的申请费用，用以收集必要信息、填写并提交表格，并遵守任何适用的生物特征信息要求。美国公民及移民服务局（USCIS）目前估计申请人完成I-485表所需时间为6.86小时（纸质和PDFi方式）和6.09小时（电子提交方式）。预计本规则不会改变上述时间负担。31
The rule will also make newly affected individuals eligible to voluntarily file Form I-485 to register as lawful permanent residents. If such an individual is under eighteen years old at the time of filing, his or her parent or legal guardian shall prepare and sign Form I-485 on the applicant's behalf. Individuals who choose this option will incur the applicable filing costs to gather the necessary information, complete and submit the form, and comply with any applicable biometric requirements. USCIS' current estimated time burdens for an applicant to complete Form I-485 are 6.86 hours (paper and PDFi) and 6.09 hours (e-file). These time burdens are not expected to change as a result of this rule.31
国土安全部（DHS）按如下方式计算完成并提交I-485表以及提交所需生物特征信息的总成本。对于受影响人群，我们采用所有职业的平均每小时工资（33.54美元）。[32] 为估算总薪酬，我们将平均每小时工资乘以民用雇员的薪酬与工资比率（1.46）。[33] 由此得出估计的平均每小时总薪酬为48.97美元。[34] 按总薪酬比率计算，完成I-485表所需的时间机会成本为335.93美元（纸质和PDFi方式）和298.23美元（电子提交方式）。[35]

For the affected population, we use the mean hourly wage of all occupations ($33.54).[32] To estimate total compensation, we multiply the mean hourly wage by the compensation to wage ratio for civilian employees (1.46).[33] This results in an estimated mean hourly total compensation of $48.97.[34] The opportunity cost of time to complete Form I-485 is $335.93 (paper and PDFi) and $298.23 (e-file) at the total compensation rate.[35]
为估算提交生物特征信息的成本，我们考虑了提交生物特征信息所需的时间负担、往返申请支持中心（ASC）的出行时间负担，以及往返申请支持中心的车辆费用。估计提交I-485表生物特征信息所需的时间负担为1.17小时。[36] 估计提交生物特征信息的时间机会成本为57.29美元。[37] 估计往返申请支持中心的平均出行距离为50英里，预计总出行时间为2.5小时。[38] 估计往返申请支持中心的出行时间机会成本为122.43美元。[39] 往返申请支持中心的车辆费用依据（印刷页第57255页）总务管理局（General Service Administration）针对私人车辆出行的每英里报销标准计算，目前为每英里0.76美元。[40] 估计往返申请支持中心的车辆费用为38.00美元。[41] 估计在申请支持中心提交生物特征信息的成本为217.72美元。[42] I-485表目前的申请费用为纸质提交（纸质和PDFi方式）1,440美元，电子提交方式为1,390美元。[43] 国土安全部估计，完成并提交I-485表（包括申请费用）以及提交生物特征信息的人均总成本为1,993.65美元（纸质和PDFi方式）和1,905.95美元（电子提交方式）。[44] 就本分析而言，国土安全部将G-325R表和I-485表视为初始登记的替代途径，尽管部分个人日后可能在通过G-325R表登记后选择再提交I-485表。
To estimate the costs of submitting biometrics, we consider the time burden to submit biometrics, the time burden to travel to and from an ASC, and the vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC. The estimated time burden to submit biometrics for Form I-485 is 1.17 hours.[36] The estimated opportunity cost of time to submit biometrics is $57.29.[37] The estimated average travel distance to and from an ASC is 50 miles; the expected total travel time is 2.5 hours.[38] The estimated opportunity cost of time to travel to and from an ASC is $122.43.[39] The vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC are based on the ( printed page 57255) General Service Administration's per mile reimbursement rate for traveling in a privately owned vehicle—currently $0.76 per mile.[40] The estimated vehicle costs of traveling to and from an ASC are $38.00.[41] The estimated cost to submit biometrics at an ASC is $217.72.[42] Form I-485 currently has filing fees of $1,440 paper filing (paper and PDFi) and $1,390 (e-file).[43] DHS estimates that the total per person cost to complete and submit a form including filing fees and submit biometrics for Form I-485 are $1,993.65 (paper and PDFi) and $1,905.95 (e-file).[44] For purposes of this analysis, DHS treats Form G-325R and Form I-485 as alternative pathways for initial registration, although some individuals may later choose to file Form I-485 after registering through Form G-325R.
本规则的益处包括为外国政府雇员在美出生子女的合法永久居民身份登记建立统一的监管框架。该规则将为受影响个人和联邦机构在公民身份认定、合法永久居民登记以及外国人登记要求方面提供更清晰的指导。通过将现行的I-485表程序扩展适用于新受影响的外国人群体，本规则还将为符合条件的个人提供一条既定的自愿登记合法永久居民身份的途径。
The benefits of this rule include establishing a consistent regulatory framework for registering lawful permanent resident status of U.S.-born children of foreign government employees. It will provide affected individuals and federal agencies with clearer guidance on citizenship recognition, lawful permanent resident registration, and alien registration requirements. By extending the existing Form I-485 process to the newly impacted alien population, the rule will also provide eligible individuals with an established pathway to voluntarily register as lawful permanent residents.
C. 《监管灵活性法》（RFA）
C. Regulatory Flexibility Act (RFA)
根据1996年《小企业监管公平法》（SBREFA，公法104-121，1996年3月29日）修订的《监管灵活性法》（5 U.S.C. 601-612）要求联邦机构在制定规则过程中考虑规则对小实体的潜在影响，以确定是否会对相当数量的小实体产生重大经济影响。“小实体”一词包括小型企业、在其所属领域不占主导地位的非营利组织，以及人口少于50,000人的政府辖区。“个人”不被视为小实体，因此就《监管灵活性法》而言，不考虑规则对个人的影响。[45] 参见5 U.S.C. 601、632。此外，法院已裁定，《监管灵活性法》仅要求机构在规则直接规制小实体时才对小实体影响进行监管灵活性分析。[46] 因此，就《监管灵活性法》而言，不考虑规则对小实体的间接影响。
The Regulatory Flexibility Act (RFA), 5 U.S.C. 601-612, as amended by the Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act of 1996 (SBREFA), Public Law 104-121 (Mar. 29, 1996), requires Federal agencies to consider the potential impact of regulations on small entities during the development of their rules to determine whether there will be a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities. The term “small entities” comprises small businesses, not-for-profit organizations that are not dominant in their fields, and governmental jurisdictions with populations of less than 50,000. An “individual” is not considered a small entity and therefore a rule's impact on individuals is not considered for RFA purposes.[45] See5 U.S.C. 601, 632. In addition, the courts have held that the RFA requires an agency to perform a regulatory flexibility analysis of small entity impacts only when a rule directly regulates small entities.[46] Consequently, a rule's indirect impacts on a small entity is not considered for RFA purposes.
本规则不直接监管小实体,预计不会对其产生直接影响。本规则所规范的对象是个人,而根据《监管灵活法案》(RFA),个人不属于“小实体”的定义范围。基于上述监管影响分析(RIA)及前言全文所述信息,国土安全部(DHS)证明本规则不会对大量小实体产生重大经济影响。
This rule does not directly regulate small entities and is not expected to have a direct effect on them. The rule regulates individuals, and individuals are not defined as “small entities” by the RFA. Based on the information presented above in the RIA and throughout the preamble, DHS certifies that this rule will not have a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities.

J. 国家环境政策法(NEPA)
J. National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA)
国土安全部(DHS)及其下属机构分析最终行动,以确定《国家环境政策法》(National Environmental Policy Act,简称NEPA),42 U.S.C. 4321及其后续条款,是否适用于这些行动,如适用,则需要何种程度的分析。《国土安全部第023-01号指令》修订版01(DHS Directive 023-01 Rev. 01)和《第023-01-001-01号指导手册》修订版01(Instruction Manual,以下简称“指导手册”)确立了国土安全部及其下属机构用于遵守NEPA(42 U.S.C. 4321及其后续条款)的政策和程序。
DHS and its components analyze final actions to determine whether the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA), 42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq., applies to them and, if so, what degree of analysis is required. DHS Directive 023-01 Rev. 01 and Instruction Manual 023-01-001-01 Rev. 01 (Instruction Manual) establish the policies and procedures that DHS and its components use to comply with NEPA, 42 U.S.C. 4321 et seq.
NEPA允许联邦机构根据以往经验,将单独或累积均不会对人类环境产生重大影响的行动类别设定为“分类排除”(categorical exclusions),因此此类行动无需进行环境评估(EA)或环境影响声明(EIS)。参见42 U.S.C. 4336(a)(2)、4336e(1)。《指导手册》附录A列出了国土安全部的分类排除事项。
NEPA allows Federal agencies to establish categories of actions as categorical exclusions that experience has shown do not, individually or cumulatively, have a significant effect on the human environment and, therefore, do not require an environmental assessment (EA) or environmental impact statement (EIS). See 42 U.S.C. 4336(a)(2), 4336e(1). The Instruction Manual, Appendix A lists the DHS Categorical Exclusions.
根据国土安全部实施NEPA的程序,一项行动若要被归为分类排除,必须满足以下三项条件:(1)该行动整体明确符合一项或多项分类排除;(2)该行动并非一项更大行动的组成部分;且(3)不存在可能导致重大环境影响的特殊情况。参见《指导手册》023-01第V.B(2)(a)至(c)条。
Under DHS NEPA implementing procedures, for an action to be categorically excluded, it must satisfy each of the following three conditions: (1) The entire action clearly fits within one or more of the categorical exclusions; (2) the action is not a piece of a larger action; and (3) no extraordinary circumstances exist that create the potential for a significant environmental effect. See Instruction Manual 023-01 at V.B(2)(a)-(c).
通过本临时最终规则,国土安全部对允许在美国出生、父母为“外国外交官员”的子女登记为合法永久居民的相关法规进行修订,将同等待遇扩展适用于在美国出生、父母为“外国政府雇员”的子女。
With this interim final rule, DHS is amending the regulations that permit children born in the United States to “foreign diplomatic officers” to register as lawful permanent residents by extending the same treatment to children born in the United States to parents who are “foreign government employees.”
NEPA允许联邦机构针对以往经验表明单独或累积均不会对人类环境产生重大影响的活动,设立分类排除(CATEX)。国土安全部实施NEPA的程序,包括《指导手册》附录A,列出了国土安全部的分类排除事项。参见《指导手册》023-01附录A。国土安全部已对本规则进行审查,认定本规则属于CATEX A3所规定的分类排除。CATEX A3适用于诸如纯属行政性或程序性性质的规则制定,以及对现有法规进行解释或修订但不改变其环境影响的规则制定。本最终规则修订现行法规,扩大了可登记为合法永久居民的在美国出生儿童的范围。这些儿童不被视为美国公民。此项变更纯属行政性质,不改变现行法规的环境影响。
NEPA allows Federal agencies to establish categorical exclusions (CATEXs) for activities that experience has shown do not, individually or cumulatively, have a significant effect on the human environment. DHS's NEPA implementing procedures, including Appendix A of the Instruction Manual, list DHS's categorical exclusions. See Instruction Manual 023-01, Appendix A. DHS has reviewed the rule and finds that the rule is categorically excluded under CATEX A3. CATEX A3 pertains to the promulgation of rules that are, among others, strictly administrative or procedural in nature and those that interpret or amend an existing regulation without changing its environmental effect. This final rule amends existing regulations to expand the population of children born in the United States that may register as lawful permanent residents. These children are not considered United States citizens. This change is strictly administrative in nature and does not change the environmental effect of the existing regulation.
本规则并非某项更大联邦行动的组成部分,且不存在可能导致重大环境影响的特殊情况。
The rule is not part of a larger federal action and does not present extraordinary circumstances that create the potential for a significant environmental effect.
因此,国土安全部认定,本规则明确符合国土安全部实施NEPA的程序中所确立的分类排除A3,无需进行进一步的NEPA分析。
Accordingly, DHS finds that the rule clearly fits within categorical exclusion A3 established in DHS's NEPA implementing procedures, and no further NEPA analysis is required.
