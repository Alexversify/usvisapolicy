美国劳工部发布最终规则,要求各州向联邦官员披露失业补偿数据

发布日期 2026年9月16日生效日期 2026年11月16日

美国劳工部发布最终规则,要求各州失业补偿(UC)机构向联邦官员披露机密数据,以便进行监督和欺诈审查。该规则与签证或移民事务无直接关系。

要点

  • 该规则将于2026年11月16日起生效。
  • 各州须在2027年9月16日前修订相关法律以符合规定。
  • 目的是帮助劳工部监察长办公室等联邦机构发现并打击欺诈行为。
  • 此规则不涉及移民或签证政策。

影响对象

各州失业补偿机构及联邦监督部门受到影响。

SUMMARY:
劳工部(DOL,或称“本部”)发布本最终规则,要求向联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿(UC)信息,以用于失业补偿项目监督与审计目的。本规则将确保包括劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)在内的联邦官员,能够从各州失业补偿机构获得所需信息,以确保对失业补偿项目进行适当监督,并促使各州失业补偿机构对识别和解决失业补偿项目中的欺诈行为负责。
The Department of Labor (DOL or the Department) is issuing this final rule to require the disclosure of confidential unemployment compensation (UC) information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. This rule will ensure that Federal officials, including the Department's Office of Inspector General (DOL-OIG), are able to obtain the information needed from State UC agencies to ensure proper oversight of the UC programs and to hold State UC agencies accountable for identifying and addressing fraud in UC programs.

二、背景
II. Background
A. 监管沿革
A. Regulatory History
本次修订的授权来源于《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条中的“管理方法”条款,该条款要求各州在其法律中规定劳工部长(以下简称“部长”)认定为“经合理计算可确保在到期时足额支付失业补偿金”的“管理方法”,作为获得行政拨款认证的条件。20 CFR第603部分规定了维护失业补偿(UC)信息保密性的要求,以及关于此类信息强制性和允许性披露的标准。现行法规第603.5(i)条,最后一次更新于2006年劳工部《联邦-州失业补偿计划(UC);州失业补偿信息的保密与披露;最终规则》(以下简称“2006年最终规则”),规定州失业补偿机构出于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿信息是被允许的,且该披露不受第603部分所规定的保密要求约束。见71 FR 56830, 56837(2006年9月27日)。在2006年最终规则之前,劳工部曾提议为第603部分设立一项专门针对联邦法律要求披露的例外规定。[1] 2006年最终规则对第603.5(i)条所提议的规定作出了修改,将其限定为出于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的的披露,因为“根据联邦法律要求”向联邦官员披露的情形已由第603部分中的其他规定涵盖,包括第603.5(e)条中允许向公职人员披露的规定。劳工部在2006年最终规则中解释称,之所以纳入关于出于联邦监督和审计目的允许披露的规定,是因为“劳工部认为有必要明确说明,保密要求不适用于为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的向联邦政府作出的任何披露。”见2006年最终规则。劳工部现对第603部分进行修订,将此类披露规定为强制性要求。
The authority for this amendment is derived from the “methods of administration” provision in sec. 303(a)(1) of the Social Security Act (SSA), which requires States to provide in their laws, as a condition to be certified to receive administrative grants, such “methods of administration” as the Secretary determines to be “reasonably calculated to insure full payment of unemployment compensation when due.” Title 20 CFR part 603 establishes requirements for maintaining the confidentiality of UC information along with standards for required and permissible disclosures of such information. The existing regulation at § 603.5(i), last updated in 2006 by the Department's Federal-State Unemployment Compensation Program (UC); Confidentiality and Disclosure of State UC Information; Final Rule (hereinafter, the 2006 Final Rule), provides that State UC agency disclosures of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits are permissible and excepted from the confidentiality requirement set forth in part 603. 71 FR 56830, 56837 (Sept. 27, 2006). Preceding the 2006 Final Rule, the Department proposed an exception to part 603 specifically for disclosures required by Federal law.[1] The 2006 Final Rule changed the provision proposed at § 603.5(i) to limit it to disclosures for UC program oversight and audits because disclosures to Federal officials as “required by Federal Law” was already covered by other provisions in part 603, including the provision allowing disclosure to public officials at § 603.5(e). The Department explained in the 2006 Final Rule that it included the provision regarding permissible disclosures for purposes of Federal oversight and audits because “the Department believe[d] it [was] necessary to explicitly address the inapplicability of the confidentiality requirement to any disclosure to the Federal Government for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.” See 2006 Final Rule. The Department now revises part 603 to make these disclosures required.
修订本法规以要求此类披露,使劳工部能够继续开展重要工作,即追究各州失业补偿机构在识别和预防失业补偿计划欺诈方面的责任。自本法规最初颁布以来,随着各州失业补偿运营的发展演变,各州所面临的欺诈事件日益增多,其中包括有组织犯罪分子实施的复杂跨州欺诈计划。《CARES法案》为应对2019冠状病毒病(COVID-19)疫情设立了若干新的失业补偿计划,在此期间,该计划遭遇了欺诈活动的大量增加,根据劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)的估计,这使失业补偿系统损失了数十亿美元。DOL-OIG在失业补偿计划的六个高风险领域中识别出456亿美元的潜在欺诈性支付福利,[2] 并估计在疫情期间,失业补偿计划下约有1910亿美元的福利可能被不当支付,其中相当一部分可归因于欺诈。[3] 自2020年以来,DOL-OIG多次向劳工部表达其担忧,即其出于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的而获取保密失业补偿信息的权限可能随时终止,并建议对法规进行修改,以明确规定此类披露属于强制性要求。[4] 劳工部长(部长)必须拥有必要的工具,以确保失业补偿计划的实施符合联邦法律的要求。劳工部、DOL-OIG及其他联邦官员对失业补偿计划进行的监督和审计有助于发现欺诈风险漏洞并找出可能的解决方案,这对于追究各州失业补偿机构按照联邦法律要求实施失业补偿计划的责任是必要的。
Amending the regulation to require these disclosures allows the Department to continue the important work of holding State UC agencies accountable for identifying and preventing fraud in the UC program. As State UC operations have evolved since this regulation was first promulgated, States have faced increased fraud incidents, including sophisticated multistate fraud schemes by organized criminals. The CARES Act created a number of new UC programs in response to the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic and during this period the program was exposed to increased fraudulent activity, costing the UC system billions of dollars according to estimates by DOL-OIG. DOL-OIG identified $45.6 billion in potentially fraudulent benefits paid in six high-risk areas under UC programs,[2] and an estimated $191 billion in benefits under UC programs during the pandemic period may have been paid improperly, with a significant portion attributable to fraud.[3] Since 2020, DOL-OIG has repeatedly expressed its concern to the Department that its access to confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits could end at any time and recommended a change to the regulations to make clear that such disclosures are required.[4] The Secretary of Labor (Secretary) must have the tools necessary to ensure that UC programs are administered consistently with the requirements of Federal law. Oversight and audits of UC programs by the Department, DOL-OIG, and other Federal officials help detect fraud vulnerabilities and identify possible solutions, which is necessary to hold State UC agencies accountable for administering UC programs consistent with Federal law requirements.
2025年8月29日,劳工部(the Department)发布了一项拟议规则制定通知(NPRM),题为《联邦与州失业补偿(UC)计划;数据可获得性;拟议规则》(90 FR 42143)(以下简称“2025年8月NPRM”),拟议要求向联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿信息,以用于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的。共有40位评论者作出回应,代表州失业补偿机构、维权组织、个人及匿名评论者、工会、一个专业协会以及一个智库。有关评论的讨论,请参见本序言第三节。
On August 29, 2025, the Department published a notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM), Federal-State Unemployment Compensation (UC) Program; Data Availability; Proposed Rule (90 FR 42143) (hereinafter referred to as the August 2025 NPRM), that proposed requiring the disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. In total, 40 commenters responded, representing State UC agencies, advocacy organizations, individual and anonymous commenters, unions, a professional association, and a think tank. See section III of this preamble for discussion of the comments.
2025年8月NPRM引用了一项信息征询请求(RFI),题为《联邦与州失业补偿(UC)计划;州失业补偿信息的保密性与披露》,该请求于2023年7月25日发布(88 FR 47829)(以下简称“2023年RFI”),当时正在考虑对第603部分进行全面更新。在2025年8月NPRM中,劳工部承认,当前最为关键的步骤是通过确保劳工部(包括劳工部监察长办公室,DOL-OIG)能够获取数据以开展监督并打击欺诈行为,从而解决失业补偿计划中的欺诈问题。
The August 2025 NPRM referenced a Request for Information (RFI) entitled Federal-State Unemployment Compensation (UC) Program; Confidentiality and Disclosure of State UC Information that was published on July 25, 2023 (88 FR 47829) (hereinafter referred to as the 2023 RFI), considering comprehensive updates to part 603. In the August 2025 NPRM, the Department acknowledged that the most critical step needed at this time was to address fraud in UC programs by ensuring the Department, including DOL-OIG, has access to data to conduct oversight and combat fraud.
2025年8月NPRM还就对第603部分的一项潜在修订征求意见,该修订将要求各州定期向就业和培训管理局(ETA)提交所有失业补偿申领数据,作为用于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的的国家失业补偿申领数据库的一部分,包括关于在此类要求下保护个人数据的适当保障措施和安全措施的意见。
The August 2025 NPRM also sought comment on a potential amendment to part 603 that would require States to submit all UC claims data on a regular basis to the ETA as part of a national UC claims database for purposes of UC program oversight and audits, including comments about appropriate safeguards and security measures to protect individual data under such a requirement.
B. 针对国家失业补偿申领数据库计划发布补充NPRM
B. Supplemental NPRM Planned for National UC Claims Database
劳工部已确定,就国家失业补偿申领数据库这一主题进一步征求意见是适当的。因此,如果决定推进建立此类数据库,劳工部将就该主题发布一项补充拟议规则制定通知(SNPRM)。劳工部在今后起草有关该主题的SNPRM时,将考虑就2025年8月NPRM收到的有关国家失业补偿申领数据库的评论意见。本最终规则不涉及建立国家失业补偿申领数据库的事宜。(印刷页第58595页)
The Department has determined it is appropriate to solicit further input on the topic of a national UC claims database. Therefore, if such a database is to be pursued, the Department will issue a Supplemental Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (SNPRM) on this topic. The Department will consider the comments received regarding a national UC claims database in response to the August 2025 NPRM when drafting the future SNPRM on this topic. This final rule does not address the creation of a national UC claims database. ( printed page 58595)
C. 关于公众参与和规则制定程序的评论
C. Comments on Public Involvement and the Rulemaking Process
2025年8月NPRM设有30天的评论期,供公众就该NPRM提供意见,评论期于2025年9月29日结束。若干评论者要求将评论期至少延长30天,以便有更多时间分析该NPRM的潜在影响并据此准备评论意见。一个维权组织辩称,第13563号行政命令(E.O. 13563)《改进监管和监管审查》通常要求设有60天的评论期。
The August 2025 NPRM included a 30-day comment period for the public to provide input on the NPRM, which ended on September 29, 2025. Several commenters requested that the comment period be extended at least 30 days to allow for more time to analyze the potential impacts of the NPRM and prepare comments accordingly. An advocacy organization argued that Executive Order (E.O.) 13563, Improving Regulation and Regulatory Review, generally requires a 60-day comment period.
一些评论者还就劳工部应如何进一步征求公众意见以指导规则制定程序提出了额外建议。一个维权组织指出,就减少欺诈性支付这一主题,2023年RFI仅收到两份回复,因此主张2025年8月NPRM应获得“来自专家和倡导者的更为充分、更为及时和当前的反馈意见”。该评论者还批评劳工部搁置了公众就2023年RFI提出的其他关切事项。一个维权组织声称该NPRM的拟定方式实为最终规则,另一个评论者则声称劳工部未能且本应与受影响方,例如州、地方政府或部落政府进行协商。另一个维权组织建议劳工部与受影响的移民群体接触,就数据收集和使用保持透明,并承诺保护隐私和问责制。
Some commenters also offered additional suggestions for the Department to solicit further input from the public to inform the rulemaking process. An advocacy organization identified only two responses to the 2023 RFI on the subject of reducing fraudulent payment, and therefore argued for the August 2025 NPRM to have “more robust, current, and timely feedback from experts and advocates.” The commenter also critiqued the Department for setting aside the other concerns identified in public comments on the 2023 RFI. An advocacy organization asserted that the NPRM was styled as a final rule, and another asserted that the Department failed to and should consult with impacted parties, such as States, local governments, or Tribal governments. Another advocacy organization suggested the Department engage with impacted immigrant populations, provide transparency about data collection and use, and commit to privacy and accountability.
劳工部拒绝延长评论期,并在案卷中发布了一封信函,告知利益相关方该决定。[5] 正如2025年8月的《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)和发布在案卷中的驳回评论期延长请求的信函所述,劳工部此前已就2025年8月NPRM所涵盖的议题与受监管群体进行过沟通,包括通过2023年信息征询(RFI)。劳工部当时还宣布,其正在考虑对失业补偿(UC)保密法规进行全面更新,并且对2023年RFI作出回应时收到的信息将用于为该潜在规则制定提供参考并加以考虑。2025年8月NPRM的范围比2023年RFI更为有限,并设有30天的评论期,供利益相关方就劳工部提议对第603部分法规作出的修改进一步提出评论。因此,劳工部拒绝延长2025年8月NPRM的30天评论期,因为其认定已就该NPRM向公众提供了充分的通知、审阅时间和评论机会。针对评论者关于本次规则制定搁置了2023年RFI公众评论中提出的其他关切的担忧,劳工部已对收到的反馈进行了考虑,并认定将向联邦官员(包括劳工部监察长办公室,DOL-OIG)披露保密UC信息以用于UC计划监督和审计目的予以法典化,是现阶段最为关键的步骤。
The Department declined to extend the comment period and posted a letter to the docket to inform stakeholders of this decision.[5] As explained in the August 2025 NPRM and in the letter denying the comment period extension requests posted to the docket, the Department previously engaged with the regulated community on the topics covered in the August 2025 NPRM, including through the 2023 RFI. The Department also announced at that time that it was considering comprehensive updates to the UC confidentiality regulations and that information received in response to the 2023 RFI would inform and be considered by the Department as part of that potential rulemaking. The August 2025 NPRM was more limited in scope than the 2023 RFI and included a 30-day comment period for stakeholders to further provide comment on the changes the Department proposed to make to the part 603 regulation. Therefore, the Department declined to extend the August 2025 NPRM's 30-day comment period because it determined that it had provided the public with sufficient notice, time to review, and opportunity to comment on the NPRM. To the commenter's concern that this rulemaking sets aside the other concerns identified in public comments on the 2023 RFI, the Department considered the feedback it received and determined that codifying the required disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials, including DOL-OIG, for purposes of UC program oversight and audits was the most critical step needed at this time.
三、讨论
III. Discussion
A. 最终规则概述
A. Overview of Final Rule
劳工部正在删除第603.5(i)条,该条允许州失业补偿(UC)机构为UC计划监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密UC信息,并在第603.6条(载有强制性披露事项)中增加一项要求为UC计划监督和审计目的披露保密UC信息的规定。将向联邦官员披露信息用于UC计划监督和审计目的的规定移至第603.6条,使这些披露成为20 CFR第603部分下的一项强制性要求。本最终规则通过将第603.6条第(c)款重新编号为第(d)款,并在第603.6条中插入新的第(c)款,来实现这一变更。此外,针对相关评论,劳工部正在第603.10条中增加一项新规定,要求某些请求方在请求获取属于UC计划监督和审计范围内的保密UC信息时,向各州提供额外信息。本最终规则还对第603.5条引言部分和第603.8条第(b)款作出相应修改。最后,本最终规则更新了授权引用条款,以更准确地反映劳工部对第603部分的法定授权。
The Department is removing § 603.5(i), which permits State UC agencies to disclose confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits, and adding a provision requiring the disclosure of confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits to § 603.6, which contains required disclosures. Moving the disclosure to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits to § 603.6 makes these disclosures a requirement under 20 CFR part 603. This final rule effectuates this change by redesignating paragraph (c) of § 603.6 as paragraph (d) and inserting a new paragraph (c) in § 603.6. Additionally, in response to comments, the Department is adding a new provision to § 603.10 that will require certain requestors to provide additional information to States as part of their request for confidential UC information within the scope of UC program oversight and audits. This final rule also makes conforming amendments to the introductory matter of § 603.5 and to paragraph (b) of § 603.8. Finally, this final rule updates the authority citation to more accurately reflect the Department's statutory authority for part 603.
根据针对2025年8月NPRM收到的评论,劳工部对NPRM作出了两项修改,内容如下所述。首先,如果继续推进建立全国性UC申领数据库,劳工部将发布一份补充拟议规则制定通知(SNPRM),以就2025年8月NPRM中提及的全国性UC申领数据库提供更大的意见征询机会。这一修改使得在考虑到针对该提案收到的评论的情况下,能够进一步征求意见并完善政策。
Based on comments received in response to the August 2025 NPRM, the Department made two changes to the NPRM that are reflected below. First, if a national UC claims database is pursued, the Department will publish an SNPRM to provide a greater opportunity for input on the national UC claims database referenced in the August 2025 NPRM. This change allows for further input and policy refinement in consideration of comments received about the proposal.
第二,劳工部在第603.10条中新增一项规定,要求依据第603.6(c)条请求信息的联邦官员(劳工部、劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)及政府问责署(GAO)除外)说明该信息将如何使用,以及该用途如何属于失业补偿(UC)项目监督和审计目的,并引用该联邦官员进行失业补偿项目监督和审计的权限依据。这是针对评论者对透明度、数据使用及潜在滥用权限的担忧所作出的回应。劳工部、劳工部监察长办公室及政府问责署已拥有接收保密失业补偿信息的单独授权,并基于其职责和法定权限经常性地为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的请求信息,因此无需提供理由说明。劳工部认为,要求其他联邦官员在请求信息时提供书面理由说明将有助于解决评论者提出的担忧,因为这将提高联邦官员所请求数据的范围和用途的透明度。本最终规则第603.10(e)段要求,请求保密失业补偿信息的其他联邦官员必须将其对信息的使用限制在提交给州失业补偿机构的书面请求中所述的用途范围内,除非联邦法律另有要求。
Second, the Department added a new provision to § 603.10 that requires Federal officials (other than the Department, DOL-OIG, and the Government Accountability Office (GAO)) who are requesting information pursuant to § 603.6(c) to state how the information will be used and how that use is for purposes of UC program oversight and audits, and to cite to the Federal official's authority for UC program oversight and audits. This was in response to concerns from commenters over transparency, data use, and potential overreach. The Department, DOL-OIG, and GAO already have separate authority to receive confidential UC information and routinely request information for purposes of UC program oversights and audits based on their duties and statutory authority, thus a justification is not needed. The Department believes that requiring a written justification for requests from other Federal officials will address the concerns raised by commenters because it increases transparency as to the scope and use of the data requested by Federal officials. Paragraph 603.10(e) of this final rule requires that other Federal officials requesting confidential UC information must limit their use of the information to the uses stated in the written request submitted to the State UC agency, except as otherwise required by Federal law.
此项修改在问责制和透明度与新要求披露所促进的重要监督之间取得了平衡。劳工部认为,为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的而对这些披露所规定的范围,为此类披露提供了重要的限制,并且这种限制使这些新要求的披露与州目前向劳工部监察长办公室所作的披露保持一致,这仅是其中一例。出于失业补偿项目监督和审计以外目的寻求获取保密失业补偿信息的联邦官员不在本最终规则的适用范围之内。第603.2(d)条所定义的公职人员,可依据管辖向公职人员披露保密失业补偿信息的第603.5(e)条请求此类信息。其他官员可依据其他适用权限,出于非监督和审计目的请求保密失业补偿信息,前提是该权限可用于该目的。本规则仅涉及出于失业补偿项目监督和审计目的而请求保密失业补偿信息的联邦官员。(印刷页第58596页)
This change balances accountability and transparency with the important oversight facilitated by the newly required disclosures. The Department believes that the prescribed scope of these disclosures, for purposes of UC program oversight and audits, provides an important limitation on the disclosures and that such a limitation aligns these new required disclosures with those States currently make to DOL-OIG, as one example. Federal officials that seek to obtain confidential UC information for purposes other than UC program oversight and audits are outside the scope of this final rule. Public officials, as defined in § 603.2(d), may request such information pursuant to § 603.5(e), which governs disclosure of confidential UC information to public officials. Other officials may request confidential UC information for non-oversight and audit purposes based on other applicable authority that is available for that purpose. This rule is only concerned with Federal officials requesting confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. ( printed page 58596)
本修正案的权限源自《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条中的“管理方法”要求,该条要求各州在其法律中规定部长认定为“经合理计算足以确保在到期时足额支付失业补偿金”的“管理方法”,作为获得认证以接受行政拨款的条件。劳工部将该要求中“到期时”一语解释为,不仅要确保支付及时,还要确保向符合条件的个人作出准确支付。该要求还要求各州不得在不应支付时作出支付,即不得向因欺诈或其他原因而不符合条件的个人作出支付。本最终规则还符合第14243号行政命令《通过消除信息孤岛阻止浪费、欺诈和滥用》的目标,因为本规则要求(而非仅仅允许)为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密失业补偿信息。
The authority for this amendment is derived from the “methods of administration” requirement of sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, which requires States to provide in their laws, as a condition to be certified to receive administrative grants, such “methods of administration” as the Secretary determines to be “reasonably calculated to insure full payment of unemployment compensation when due.” The Department interprets the phrase “when due” in this requirement to mean accurate payments are made to eligible individuals in addition to ensuring that the payments are timely. It also requires that a State not make payments when payments are not due, i.e., to individuals not eligible due to fraud or otherwise. This final rule is also consistent with the objectives of E.O. 14243, Stopping Waste, Fraud, and Abuse by Eliminating Information Silos, because it requires, rather than permits, the disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.
劳工部指出,其根据《社会保障法》所承担的职责之一包括对各州失业补偿项目进行认证。只有在劳工部有能力获取某些信息的情况下,才能履行这一职责。劳工部目前并不寻求补充其信息收集工作,但本规则制定确保劳工部今后具备这样做的能力。
The Department notes that part of its responsibilities under the SSA include certifying State UC programs. This responsibility can only be upheld if the Department has the ability to access certain information. The Department is not seeking to supplement its information collections at this time; however, the capacity for the Department to do so in the future is guaranteed by this rulemaking.
B. 制定规则的必要性
B. Need for Rulemaking
根据《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条,劳工部长期以来一直要求各州披露为妥善管理失业补偿项目所必需的全部信息(第603.6(a)条)。劳工部还特别认识到监督和审计在妥善管理失业补偿项目中所发挥的关键作用,尤其是在确保不作出不应支付的款项方面。劳工部通过明确允许为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的向联邦官员进行披露,并将此类披露排除在第603.5(i)条规定的保密要求之外,从而体现了对这一关键作用的认可。
Pursuant to sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, the Department has long required States to disclose all information that is necessary for the proper administration of UC programs (§ 603.6(a)). The Department has also specifically recognized the critical role that oversight and audits play in the proper administration of UC programs, particularly in ensuring payments are not made when they are not due. The Department has recognized this critical role by explicitly making disclosures to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits permissible and excepting such disclosures from the confidentiality requirement under § 603.5(i).
多年来，劳工部一直将《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条解释为要求向某些联邦机构披露保密的失业补偿(UC)信息，因为此类数据的披露依据由这些机构所执行的法规获得授权，例如，为失业补偿税务管理目的向国内税务局(Internal Revenue Service)披露信息。参见《联邦与州失业补偿计划(UC)；州失业补偿信息的保密与披露；拟议规则》，69 FR 50022, 50027（2004年8月12日）；《2006年最终规则》，71 FR 56830, 56838（2006年9月27日）。同样，劳工部长期以来认识到，为欺诈调查目的向劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)披露保密的失业补偿信息，对于该计划的妥善管理是必要的。参见20 CFR 603.6(a)；另见《失业保险计划通知》(UIPL)第04-17号（2016年12月16日）（“监察长办公室对欺诈的调查对于该计划的妥善管理是必要的”）以及第04-17号，第1号修改（2021年8月3日）。劳工部监察长办公室依据第603.6(a)条进行的信息收集，受涵盖此类收集的《记录系统通知》(SORN)规范。参见DOL-OIG 12，“监察长办公室仓储与学习系统(OWLS)”，85 FR 60833（2020年9月28日）。此外，在新冠疫情期间，劳工部要求根据《冠状病毒援助、救济和经济安全法》(CARES Act)下的失业补偿计划，定期向劳工部监察长办公室披露相关的保密失业补偿信息，以便根据《CARES法案》赋予的职权以及作为失业补偿行政拨款的条件，对失业补偿计划进行监督和审计。参见，例如，《失业保险计划通知》第04-17号，第1号修改（2021年8月3日）（“在管理大流行失业援助(PUA)、联邦大流行失业补偿(FPUC)、大流行紧急失业补偿(PEUC)、混合收入者大流行失业补偿(MEUC)以及根据经修正的《CARES法案》第2105条对无等待期各州普通失业补偿首周给予全额联邦资助报销的背景下，各州必须向劳工部监察长办公室披露所有信息，以供其调查失业补偿欺诈和对失业补偿计划进行审计之用。”）以及《失业保险计划通知》第22-21号（2021年8月11日）（“作为接受本通知项下拨款的条件，各州必须同意向[劳工部监察长办公室]披露信息”）。
For years, the Department has interpreted sec. 303(a)(1) as requiring the disclosure of confidential UC information to certain Federal agencies because disclosure of such data is authorized under statutes administered by those agencies, including, for example, the Internal Revenue Service for purposes of UC tax administration. See Federal-State Unemployment Compensation Program (UC); Confidentiality and Disclosure of State UC Information; Proposed Rule,69 FR 50022, 50027 (Aug. 12, 2004); 2006 Final Rule, 71 FR 56830, 56838 (Sept. 27, 2006). Similarly, the Department has long recognized that disclosures of confidential UC information to DOL-OIG for fraud investigations are necessary for the proper administration of the program. See20 CFR 603.6(a); see, e.g., Unemployment Insurance Program Letter (UIPL) Nos. 04-17 (Dec. 16, 2016) (“OIG investigations of fraud are necessary for the proper administration of the program”) and 04-17, Change 1 (Aug. 3, 2021). DOL-OIG's collections pursuant to § 603.6(a) occur under a System of Records Notice (SORN) that covers such collections. See DOL-OIG 12, “Office of Inspector General Warehouse and Learning System (OWLS),” 85 FR 60833 (Sept. 28, 2020). Additionally, during the pandemic, the Department required the regular disclosure of confidential UC information related to the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security (CARES) Act UC programs to DOL-OIG for purposes of UC program oversight and audits under its authority under the CARES Act and as a condition of UC administrative grants. See, e.g., UIPL No. 04-17, Change 1 (Aug. 3, 2021) (“Within the context of administering PUA, FPUC, PEUC, MEUC, and full federal funding of the first week of regular UC for States with no waiting week that is reimbursed in accordance with Section 2105 of the CARES Act, as amended, states must disclose all information to DOL-OIG for the purposes of investigating UC fraud and for audits of UC programs.”) and UIPL No. 22-21 (Aug. 11, 2021) (“as a condition of receiving a grant under this UIPL, states must agree to information disclosure with [DOL-OIG]”).
各州配合这些请求，已成为有效管理失业补偿计划的关键所在。自2006年首次颁布保密法规以来，各州的失业补偿运作已发生变化，各州面临在线申请件数的大幅增长，以及欺诈事件的急剧增加，尤其是涉及有组织犯罪分子实施的复杂跨州欺诈计划的事件。《CARES法案》为应对2019冠状病毒病(COVID-19)大流行创设了若干新的失业补偿计划，在此期间，该计划遭受了日益增多的欺诈活动，根据劳工部监察长办公室的估计，这使失业补偿体系损失了数十亿美元。劳工部监察长办公室认定，在六个高风险领域中，存在456亿美元的潜在欺诈性失业补偿给付，[6]并估计在疫情期间支付的1910亿美元失业补偿金可能存在不当支付，其中很大一部分可归因于欺诈。[7]劳工部长必须掌握必要的工具，以确保失业补偿计划的管理符合联邦法律的要求。监督和审计对于追究各州失业补偿机构减少不当支付的责任，以及确保各州失业补偿机构对失业补偿计划进行妥善和高效的管理，是必要的。如上文所述，劳工部监察长办公室已通过多次向劳工部提出修订本规则的建议，强调了这一点，即应将为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的而进行的信息披露，由许可性规定改为强制性规定。因此，鉴于该计划内对欺诈识别和预防的需求日益紧迫，劳工部将第303(a)(1)条解释为，要求为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的，向包括劳工部监察长办公室在内的联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿信息。
State cooperation with these requests has become critical to the effective administration of UC programs. State UC operations have evolved since the confidentiality regulation was first promulgated in 2006, as States have faced a large increase in online claims filing, as well as a stark increase in fraud incidents, particularly those involving sophisticated multistate fraud schemes by organized criminals. The CARES Act created a number of new UC programs in response to the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic and during this period the program was exposed to increased fraudulent activity, costing the UC system billions of dollars according to estimates by DOL-OIG. DOL-OIG identified $45.6 billion in potentially fraudulent UC benefits paid in six high-risk areas,[6] and estimated $191 billion in UC benefits during the pandemic period could have been paid improperly, with a significant portion attributable to fraud.[7] The Secretary must have the tools necessary to ensure that the UC programs are administered consistently with the requirements of Federal law. Oversight and audits are necessary to hold State UC agencies accountable for reducing improper payments and for ensuring that State UC agencies provide proper and efficient administration of the UC program. As discussed above, DOL-OIG has reinforced this point through repeated recommendations to the Department to amend this rule to make disclosures for purposes of UC program oversight and audits required rather than permissive. Accordingly, in recognition of the growing urgency of the need for fraud identification and prevention within the program, DOL interprets sec. 303(a)(1) as requiring the disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials, including DOL-OIG, for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.
在下文各节中，劳工部将对所收到的有关2025年8月《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)中提议的要求，即各州失业补偿机构必须为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密失业补偿信息（该要求已在本规则中最终确定）的相关意见予以归纳并作出回应。
In the sections that follow, the Department summarizes and responds to the comments received about the August 2025 NPRM's proposed requirement—finalized in this rule—that State UC agencies must disclose confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.

1. 法定背景
1. Statutory Background
a. 劳工部根据《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条就本规则享有法定授权
a. DOL Has Statutory Authority Under Section 303(a)(1), SSA, for This Rule
评论:多名评论者反对劳工部将《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条规定的“管理方法”要求作为本次规则制定的法定依据。一个州和一位个人评论者表示反对劳工部依据《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条中的措辞,将第603.5(i)条从“允许性数据共享条款”重新定性为第603.6(c)条下的强制性要求。该个人评论者(印刷版第58597页)指出,在没有国会授权的情况下,将一项许可性规定转变为具有约束力的强制要求,超出了劳工部的法定权限。同样,也有一些评论者指出,由于向联邦官员披露信息以用于失业补偿(UC)项目监督和审计的目的已被现行法规列为允许性披露,这表明此类披露本不打算作为强制性要求,劳工部现在不能主张这些相同的披露是强制性的。此外,有评论者认为“管理方法”条款仅旨在处理付款及资格认定的及时性问题。一个倡导组织表示,2025年8月的《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)声称《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条要求向劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)披露信息,这与劳工部此前承认其缺乏要求获取机密信息权限的立场相矛盾,因为该法规中所体现的许可性框架正是对法定限制的一种体现。
Comments: A number of commenters objected to the Department using the “methods of administration” requirement of sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, as a statutory basis for the rulemaking. A State and an individual commenter expressed opposition to the Department's reliance on the language in sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, for authority to recast § 603.5(i) from a “permissive data-sharing provision” into a mandatory requirement under § 603.6(c). The individual commenter ( printed page 58597) stated that without congressional authority it would be beyond the Department's statutory authority to convert something permissible into a binding mandate. Similarly, a few commenters argued that because disclosures to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits were included in the existing regulation as permissible disclosures, such disclosures were not intended to be required and the Department may not now assert that these same disclosures are mandatory. Further, a commenter argued the methods of administration provision is meant to only address timeliness of payments and eligibility. One advocacy organization stated that the August 2025 NPRM's claim that sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, requires disclosure to DOL-OIG reverses the Department's previous acknowledgement that it lacked the authority to require access to confidential information because the permissible framework in the regulation was a recognition of statutory constraints.
回应:劳工部已考虑上述意见,但不予认同。劳工部在发布2006年最终规则时,并未采取其无权要求此类披露的立场。现行法规在此情形下实施的是一种许可性披露,劳工部在发布该规则时并未就其是否有权要求此类披露发表意见。自现行法规颁布以来,各州失业补偿业务日益遭受欺诈计划的侵害,获取机密失业补偿信息对于支持联邦官员履行监督职责,确保各州失业补偿机构对失业补偿项目的完整性负责,已变得愈发关键。因此,本最终规则对该法规进行了调整,以体现联邦官员的监督职责,并明确为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的向联邦官员进行披露,对于该项目的妥善管理是必要的。
Response: The Department has considered these positions and does not agree with them. The Department did not take the position that it does not have the authority to require this type of disclosure when it issued the 2006 Final Rule. The current regulation implemented a permissible disclosure in this instance, and the Department issued that rule without opining on its authority to require those same disclosures. Since the promulgation of the current regulation, State UC operations have been increasingly subjected to fraud schemes, and access to confidential UC information has increasingly become critical for supporting Federal officials in their oversight responsibilities to hold State UC agencies accountable for ensuring the integrity of the UC programs. As a result, this final rule conforms the regulation to reflect both the oversight responsibility of Federal officials and that disclosures to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits are necessary for the proper administration of the program.
只要机构提供了合理的解释,就可以自由改变其现行政策。参见Encino Motorcars, LLC诉Navarro案,579 U.S. 211, 221 (2016)。劳工部已就依据其在《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条下确定何种管理方法属于必要事项的自由裁量权限,将第603.5(i)条的许可性披露修改为第603.6(c)条下的强制性披露一事,提供了合理的解释。因此,本规则是机构合理行使其法定权限,以确定何种管理方法经合理计算可确保在到期时支付福利金的一种体现。
Agencies are free to change their existing policies as long as they provide a reasoned explanation for the change. Encino Motorcars, LLC v. Navarro, 579 U.S. 211, 221 (2016). The Department has provided a reasoned explanation for amending the permissible disclosure at § 603.5(i) to be a mandatory one at § 603.6(c), pursuant to its discretionary authority to determine what methods of administration are necessary under sec. 303(a)(1), SSA. Accordingly, this rule is a reasonable exercise of the agency's statutory authority to determine what methods of administration are reasonably calculated to ensure payment of benefits when due.
b. 第14243号行政命令不是本法规的依据
b. Executive Order No 14243 Is Not the Basis for This Regulation
评论:多名评论者主张,《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条的措辞与第14243号行政命令之间存在冲突,并认为该行政命令不能作为本法规的依据。一个倡导组织对第14243号行政命令的合法性提出质疑,但同时也指出,即便该行政命令本身也承认,不受限制的获取权限仅限于《社会保障法》所允许的范围之内。
Comments: A number of commenters asserted that there is a conflict between the language of sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, and E.O. 14243, and argued that the E.O. cannot be the basis for this regulation. An advocacy organization questioned the legality of E.O. 14243 but also noted that even the E.O. acknowledges that the authority for unfettered access is only to the extent it is allowed under the SSA.
回应:劳工部并未依赖第14243号行政命令作为制定本法规的权限依据。如上文所述,劳工部为本最终规则所依据的权限,系恰当地源自《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条。
Response: The Department does not rely on E.O. 14243 for authority to promulgate the regulation. As explained above, the Department properly derives authority for this final rule from sec. 303(a)(1), SSA.
c. 本规则不涉及重大问题原则问题,也不缺乏国会授权
c. This Rule Does Not Present a Major Questions Doctrine Issue and Does Not Lack Congressional Authorization
评论意见:一些评论者不同意劳工部依据《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条作为授权依据,认为该规则构成“重大问题”,且缺乏明确的国会授权。一家倡导组织断言,劳工部对第303(a)(1)条的解释过于宽泛,并表示“法院已反复认定,一般性行政条款在缺乏明确国会授权的情况下不授权推行全面性的新规定”。此外,该评论者主张本规则涉及重大问题原则,指出“美国最高法院强调,在涉及具有`重大经济和政治意义'的`重大问题'的案件中,行政机构需要`明确的国会授权'”。该评论者认为,本规则在没有明确国会授权的情况下提出了一个重大问题。
Comments: A few commenters disagreed with the Department's reliance on sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, for authority, arguing that this rule constitutes a “major question” and lacks clear congressional authorization. An advocacy organization asserted that the Department's interpretation of sec. 303(a)(1) is overbroad and stated that “[c]ourts have repeatedly held that general administrative provisions do not authorize sweeping new mandates absent clear congressional authorization.” Further, the commenter argues that this rule implicates the major questions doctrine, stating that “[t]he United States Supreme Court emphasized that in cases involving `major questions' of `vast economic and political significance,' agencies require `clear congressional authorization.'” According to the commenter, this rule presents a major question without clear congressional authorization.
答复:劳工部不同意上述意见。重大问题原则规定,如果法院在审查中认定某项行政机构行为具有重大的政治和经济意义,并涉及在重大问题上的政策转变,审查法院将严格评估国会是否有意授权该机构采取此种非同寻常的行动。West Virginia v. EPA, 597 U.S. 697, 700 (2022)(引用 FDA v. Brown & Williamson Tobacco Corp., 529 U.S. 120, 159-60 (2000))。在此类案件中,只有在制定法文本中有“明确的国会授权”支持的情况下,该行政机构的行为才会被维持。同上(引用 Utility Air Regulatory Grp. v. EPA, 573 U.S. 302, 324 (2014))。
Response: The Department does not agree. The major questions doctrine provides that if on review a court finds that an agency action has vast political and economic significance and involves a shift in policy regarding a major issue, a reviewing court will critically assess whether Congress meant to confer authority on the agency to take such an extraordinary action. West Virginia v. EPA, 597 U.S. 697, 700 (2022) (citing FDA v. Brown & Williamson Tobacco Corp., 529 U.S. 120, 159-60 (2000)). In such cases, the agency action will only be upheld if it is supported by “clear congressional authorization” in the statutory language. Id. (citing Utility Air Regulatory Grp. v. EPA, 573 U.S. 302, 324 (2014)).
本最终规则并非非同寻常的行政机构行为的实例。如上文详细所述,劳工部长期以来一直将披露保密的失业补偿(UC)信息解释为在为项目的适当和有效管理所必需时所要求的行为(参见,例如,第603.6(a)条)。鉴于本序言前述监管历史部分所解释的解决失业补偿项目欺诈行为日益增长的需求,以及联邦官员就此问责各州的责任,劳工部已确定,根据《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条,为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的要求披露信息对于该项目的适当管理是必要的,因此这是获得失业补偿行政资金认证的一项条件。劳工部已确定此种监督在部分程度上是必要的,因为项目绩效指标尚未得到充分改善。本规则仅将各州通常已经在进行的某些披露从可自由裁量的披露变为强制性披露,并非重大政策变化,在经济上或政治上也不具有重大意义。因此,就重大问题原则而言,本规则不构成非同寻常的行政机构行为或重大问题。
This final rule is not an instance of extraordinary agency action. As described in detail above, the Department has long interpreted the disclosure of confidential UC information to be required when necessary for proper and efficient administration of the program ( see, e.g., § 603.6(a)). Due to the growing need to address fraud in UC programs, as explained previously in the Regulatory History section of this preamble, and the responsibility of Federal officials to hold States accountable for doing so, the Department has determined that requiring disclosures for purposes of UC program oversight and audits is necessary for the proper administration of the program under sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, and, therefore, a condition of certification for UC administrative funding. The Department has determined that this oversight is necessary in part because program performance metrics have not sufficiently improved. This rule, which merely makes certain disclosures States are generally already making a required rather than a permissible disclosure, is not a major policy change, nor is it economically or politically significant. Accordingly, it does not constitute an extraordinary agency action or major question for the purpose of the major questions doctrine.
d. 本规则并非向联邦政府不当转移权力
d. This Rule Is Not an Improper Shift of Power to the Federal Government
评论意见:一家倡导组织写道,本规则是向联邦政府转移权力的举措,是对《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条的新解释,尽管该条款允许部长要求各州实施为失业补偿项目的适当和有效管理所必需的管理方法,但该条款从未被解释为要求强制向劳工部共享保密的失业补偿数据。该评论者指出,劳工部必须为其所声称的权力提供明确的国会授权依据,尤其是在该权力涉及将重大权力从各州转移至联邦政府的情况下。另一家倡导组织则主张,制定法中没有任何内容授权劳工部“强制各州提供对工资记录、社会安全号码或雇主数据的全面访问权限”。相反,该倡导组织主张,“制定法的目的是确保工人`按时'获得其福利,而不是建立一个全国性的监控系统”。该评论者强调,失业补偿体系旨在成为一种合作式联邦制体系,并主张(印刷页第58598页)劳工部在制定本规则时,所主张的权力远远超出了授权制定法所授予的权力。另一位评论者得出结论认为,2025年8月的《拟议规则制定通知》(NPRM)将对各州施加新的义务,从而改变管理失业补偿的联邦与州之间的伙伴关系,并超越劳工部在该制定法项下的权限。
Comments: An advocacy organization wrote that this rule is a shift of power toward the Federal Government, that it is a new interpretation of sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, and that although the provision allows the Secretary to require States to implement methods of administration necessary for the proper and efficient administration of the UC program, it has not been interpreted to require mandatory sharing of confidential UC data with the Department. The commenter stated the Department must point to clear congressional authorization for its claimed authority, particularly when it imposes a significant shift of power from the States to the Federal Government. And another advocacy organization argued that nothing in the statute authorizes the Department to “compel States to provide wholesale access to wage records, Social Security numbers or employer data.” Instead, the advocacy organization argues “[t]he statutory purpose is to ensure workers receive their benefits `when due,' not to build a national surveillance system.” Emphasizing that the UC system is intended to be a cooperative federalism system, the commenter argues that the ( printed page 58598) Department, in promulgating this rule, asserts authority that is much broader than the authority granted in the authorizing statute. Another commenter concluded that the August 2025 NPRM would place new obligations on States that would alter the Federal-State partnership governing UC and exceed the Department's authority under the statute.
答复:如上文详述,本最终规则是对劳工部(Department)现行预期和惯例做法的成文化规定。根据《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条,劳工部拥有明确的国会授权,可认定此类信息披露是失业补偿(UC)计划适当有效管理所必需的管理方法之一。根据现行《联邦法规汇编》(CFR)第603.5(i)条的规定,各州已经定期向劳工部和劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)提供信息,以用于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的。此外,欺诈行为会损害一州“按时”支付福利的能力,因为它会耗费本可用于该州为其他工作者管理福利的政府资源。
Response: As stated above in detail, this final rule is the codification of current Department expectation and State practice. Under sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, the Department has clear congressional authority to determine such disclosure is a method of administration necessary for the proper and efficient administration of the UC program. States are already providing information to the Department and DOL-OIG regularly for purposes of UC program oversight and audits under the existing regulation set forth at § 603.5(i). Furthermore, fraud detrimentally impacts a State's ability to pay benefits “when due,” because it drains government resources that would otherwise serve the State's administration of benefits to other workers.
e. 法定授权来源于《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条,而非第303(a)(6)条
e. Statutory Authority Comes From 303(a)(1), Not 303(a)(6), SSA
意见:有评论者主张,应以《社会保障法》第303(a)(6)条作为本规则的法定依据,该条款涉及各州作为获得行政拨款条件,须向部长提交报告的要求。
Comments: Commenters asserted that sec. 303(a)(6), SSA, which relates to the requirement that States, as a condition for receiving administrative grants, provide reports to the Secretary, should be used as the statutory basis for this rule.
答复:劳工部不同意评论者关于本最终规则的授权应来自《社会保障法》第303(a)(6)条而非第303(a)(1)条的论点。本最终规则要求向联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿信息,以用于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的。第303(a)(6)条要求各州失业补偿法须包含以下规定:“按劳工部部长不时要求的形式和内容编制此类报告。”第303(a)(6)条中的“报告”一词,历来被解释为有别于原始数据,而报告与原始数据的提供均有助于支持失业补偿计划的监督与审计。在本最终规则中,劳工部不就其根据第303(a)(6)条收集“报告”形式的失业补偿计划信息的权限发表意见。
Response: The Department disagrees with comments that argue the authority for this final rule should come from sec. 303(a)(6), SSA, instead of 303(a)(1), SSA. This final rule requires disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. Section 303(a)(6) requires that State UC laws include provisions for “[t]he making of such reports, in such form and containing such information, as the Secretary of Labor may from time to time require.” The word “reports” in 303(a)(6) has historically been interpreted as distinct from raw data and both reports and the provision of raw data support UC program oversight and audits. In this final rule, the Department does not opine on the Department's authority to collect UC program information in the form of “reports” under sec. 303(a)(6).
为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿数据这一规定,有助于推动关键工作,使各州失业补偿机构对识别和防止失业补偿计划中的欺诈活动负责,从而减少不当的福利支付。因此,第303(a)(1)条是要求为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露信息的依据。
The provision of disclosing confidential UC data to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits facilitates critical efforts in holding State UC agencies accountable for identifying and preventing fraud activities in UC programs, which in turn reduces improper payments of benefits. Therefore, sec. 303(a)(1) stands as the basis for requiring disclosures to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.
2. 法律背景
2. Legal Background
a. 1974年《隐私法》
a. Privacy Act of 1974
意见:评论者对遵守《隐私法》表达了多项担忧,包括劳工部可能在未经同意的情况下不当披露联邦持有的个人记录,超出其已公布的系统记录通知(SORN)范围,超出“惯常用途”例外情形,以及违反最小化原则。一家倡导组织担心,拟议规则将不遵守《隐私法》规定的机构义务,即就个人信息将如何被收集、保存和披露向个人提供明确通知,且该规则也不包含要求向失业补偿申请人发出其个人数据将与联邦官员共享的通知的必要条款。该倡导组织认为,失业补偿申请人的数据是根据《社会保障法》为管理失业福利这一有限目的而收集的,而非用于联邦调查或潜在的执法目的。因此,该评论者认为,强制要求为超出该目的的用途披露数据,有可能违反《隐私法》关于机构只能收集和保存实现其法定使命所“必需”的数据的要求。
Comments: Commenters expressed several concerns regarding compliance with the Privacy Act, including that the Department risks improperly disclosing federally held personal records without consent, exceeding the scope of its published SORN, exceeding the “routine use” exception, and violating the minimization principle. An advocacy organization was concerned that the proposed rule would not follow the Privacy Act's agency obligation to provide clear notice to individuals regarding how their information would be collected, maintained, and disclosed, nor would it contain a required provision requiring notice to UC claimants that their personal data would be shared with Federal officials. The advocacy organization argued that UC claimant data is collected under the SSA for the limited purpose of administering unemployment benefits, not for Federal investigations or potential law enforcement purposes. Therefore, the commenter reasoned, mandating disclosure for use beyond that purpose risks violating the Privacy Act's requirement that agencies collect and maintain only that data that is “necessary” to accomplish their statutory mission.
答复:劳工部并不打算使本最终规则取代或抵触任何联邦法律的要求,包括经修订的1974年《隐私法》(5 U.S.C. 552a)。劳工部必须依照经修订的1974年《隐私法》(5 U.S.C. 552a)以及所有其他适用的联邦法律的各项要求,保护其根据本最终规则收集和保存的保密失业补偿信息。长期以来,包括劳工部内部人员和劳工部监察长办公室在内的联邦官员,一直为失业补偿计划监督和审计目的收集和保存保密的失业补偿信息,以确保失业补偿计划的完整性,并确保按照《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条的要求按时支付福利。本规则项下任何获取保密失业补偿信息以用于失业补偿计划监督和审计目的的劳工部以外的联邦官员,也须遵守所有适用的联邦法律,包括《隐私法》。
Response: The Department does not intend this final rule to supersede or conflict with the requirements of any Federal law, including the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552a, as amended. The Department must protect the confidential UC information it collects and maintains pursuant to this final rule in accordance with all requirements under the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552a, as amended, as well as all other applicable Federal laws. Federal officials, including those within the Department and DOL-OIG, have long collected and maintained confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits to ensure the integrity of the UC program and that benefits are paid when due as required by sec. 303(a)(1), SSA. Any Federal official outside the Department that obtains confidential UC information under this rule for purposes of UC program oversight and audits will also be required to comply with all applicable Federal laws, including the Privacy Act.

评论意见:一位评论者写道,允许将失业数据重新用于其他用途,将剥夺个人获得信息披露通知的权利,使其无法对数据使用提出异议,并违反第五修正案规定的正当程序。一个倡导组织同样认为,在不考虑隐私权的情况下允许不受限制地访问保密失业数据,将违反第四修正案。
Comments: A commenter wrote that allowing unemployment data to be repurposed would deny individuals notice of the information disclosure, remove their ability to contest data use, and violate due process under the Fifth Amendment. An advocacy organization similarly argued that allowing unfettered access to confidential unemployment data without regard for the right to privacy would violate the Fourth Amendment.
一个倡导组织认为,该部的规则缺乏支出条款所要求的“明确通知”,并构成一种非法的“溯及既往”拨款条件。该评论者进一步声称,“该提案中关于各州为不明确的‘监督和审计’目的而共享数据的未明确要求”将是各州无法预见的“项目的根本性转变”,并将超出宪法支出条款所施加的限制。在提出这一论点时,该评论者引用了失业补偿(UC)项目保密规则,根据该评论者的说法,这些规则“仅包含与联邦机构共享[数据]的有限规定”,包括1986年最初的法规,该法规仅允许各州为确定资格或福利金额的目的与劳工部(DOL)共享信息,以及现行法规,该法规“仅要求在‘失业补偿项目妥善管理所必需’的情况下,或为特定目的向特定机构披露信息”。该倡导组织进一步声称,对联邦资金附加溯及既往条件将“违反第十修正案的反强制原则”,因为该规则不仅要求各州共享数据,还要求各州建立共享数据所需的基础设施。

The commenter further asserted that “the proposal's undefined requirement for States to share data for unspecified `oversight and audits'” would be a “fundamental shift in the program” that the States could not anticipate and would exceed the limitations imposed by the Constitution's Spending Clause. In making this argument, the commenter referenced UC program confidentiality rules that, according to the commenter “have included only limited provisions for sharing [data] with Federal agencies,” including the original regulations from 1986 that permitted States to share information with DOL only for the purpose of determining eligibility or benefit amounts and the existing regulations, which “require disclosure only as `necessary for the proper administration of the UC program' or to specific agencies for specific purposes.” The same advocacy organization further asserted that retroactive conditioning of Federal funds would “violate anti-commandeering principles of the Tenth Amendment” because it commands States to share data as well as to build the infrastructure to do so.
答复:本部不认同该规则未能提供支出条款所要求的“明确通知”这一说法。在南达科他州诉多尔案中,最高法院阐述了国会根据支出条款对联邦资助项目附加资金条件的权力所适用的框架。483 U.S. 203 (1987)。就相关部分而言,该框架要求“如果国会希望对各州接受联邦资金附加条件,必须明确无误地这样做,使各州能够在知悉参与后果的情况下,自主做出选择。”见该案判决。(印刷版第58599页)
Response: The Department disagrees with the assertion that the Department's rule fails to provide the “clear notice” required by the Spending Clause. In South Dakota v. Dole, the Supreme Court outlined the framework governing Congress's authority under the Spending Clause to attach funding conditions to Federal award programs. 483 U.S. 203 (1987). In relevant part the framework requires that “if Congress desires to condition the States' receipt of federal funds, it must do so unambiguously, enabling the States to exercise their choice knowingly, cognizant of the consequences of their participation.” See id. ( printed page 58599)
本最终规则符合这一标准。该规则清晰明确。它在20 CFR第603部分的法规文本中明确规定了此前允许但现已成为各州获得失业补偿行政资金所必须满足的强制性条件的信息披露。因此,与评论者的说法相反,本规则满足了联邦拨款项目附加的资金条件必须使接受方能够“知情”接受相关规定这一要求。
This final rule meets this standard. It is clear and unambiguous. It plainly describes the disclosure in the regulatory text of 20 CFR part 603 that was permissible but is now a required condition upon which State receipt of UC administrative funding depends. Accordingly, contrary to the commenter's claim, this rule satisfies the requirement that a funding condition attached to a Federal grant program allow recipients to “knowingly” accept the provisions.
同一评论者还认为,本部的规则在支出条款和反强制原则下具有不被允许的“溯及力”。然而,与该评论者的说法相反,本规则不会具有溯及力;已经提供给各州的行政资金不会受到本规则所确立的新条件的影响。即使在本规则生效日期(即公布后60天)之后,合规日期也为各州提供了1年的时间,以在必要时修订州法律,确保其获得行政资金的认证。因此,本规则不施加溯及既往的条件,并且与支出条款相关判例法一致,本规则的公布使各州知悉一项新要求,未来获得行政资金认证将取决于该要求,从而使各州能够在知情的情况下选择今后是否接受该条款。
The same commenter also argues that the Department's rule is impermissibly “retroactive” under the Spending Clause and the anti-commandeering doctrine. However, counter to the commenter's assertion, this rule will not apply retroactively; administrative funding already provided to States will not be impacted by the new condition it establishes. Even after the effective date of this rule, which is 60 days after publication, the compliance date gives States 1 year to amend their State laws, if needed, to ensure they receive certification for receipt of administrative funding. Accordingly, this rule does not impose a retroactive condition and, consistent with Spending Clause jurisprudence, the publication of this rule puts States on notice of a new requirement upon which future certification for administrative funding will depend so that they may knowingly choose to accept the term going forward.
最后,基于上述原因,本规则所施加的强制性披露要求并未对各州构成强制。事实上,只要各州对是否接受联邦拨款条件拥有真正的选择权,此类条件就是合法的。见全国独立企业联合会诉西贝利厄斯案,567 U.S. 519, 579 (2012)。各州可以在知情的情况下选择今后是否接受这些条款。
Finally, for the reasons set out above, the required disclosure imposed by this rule does not commandeer the States. Indeed, Federal grant conditions are lawful so long as the States have a genuine choice whether to accept them. Nat'l Fed'n of Indep. Bus. v. Sebelius, 567 U.S. 519, 579 (2012). States may knowingly choose to accept the terms, going forward.
此外,劳工部不认同评论者关于本规则对该计划现行运作方式构成重大变革的说法。根据第603.5(i)条规定,各州已在应联邦官员要求时,出于失业补偿(UC)项目监督和审计目的提供失业补偿信息,该条款已明确此类披露为可允许行为,并将其排除在第603部分所规定的保密要求之外。鉴于解决失业补偿项目欺诈问题的需求日益增长,劳工部已确定,根据《社会保障法》(SSA)第303(a)(1)条的规定,要求出于失业补偿项目监督和审计目的进行披露,对该项目的妥善管理是必要的,因此构成获得失业补偿项目行政管理资金认证的一项条件。本规则是该机构行使其法定权力的合理举措,旨在确定各州为获得认证以接受资金管理失业补偿项目所需采取的管理方法,并不构成对各州的非法强制接管。
Moreover, the Department does not agree with the commenter's claim that this rule is a significant shift in the program as it currently exists. All States already provide UC information to Federal officials as requested for purposes of UC program oversight and audits under § 603.5(i), which made the disclosure permissible and excepted such disclosure from the confidentiality requirement set forth in part 603. Due to the growing need to address fraud in the UC program, the Department has determined that requiring disclosures for purposes of UC program oversight and audits is necessary for the proper administration of the program under sec. 303(a)(1), SSA, and, therefore, a condition of certification for administrative funding for the UC program. This rule is a reasonable exercise of the agency's statutory authority to determine what methods of administration are required in order for a State to be certified to receive funding to administer the UC program and does not constitute an unlawful commandeering of the States.

Effective program integrity in the UC system increasingly depends on the ability to identify risks and fraud schemes. Responding to requests from Federal officials seeking confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits enables nationwide analysis, coordination, and oversight that individual States, acting alone, are not positioned to perform. Federal access to this information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits supports the detection of multistate fraud patterns, the assessment of systemic vulnerabilities, and the development of targeted guidance and corrective actions that strengthen the integrity of the UC system as a whole. Requiring disclosure of this information removes any barriers to access that might arise: (1) when the requirement for such disclosure is subject to time-limited conditions of individual grants; or (2) States choosing not to disclose due to the disclosure being permissive—thus streamlining access to data now and into the future. 1. 需求说明
1. Statement of Need
劳工部修订《联邦法规汇编》第20编第603部分,将向联邦官员披露保密失业补偿(UC)信息用于失业补偿项目监督和审计的行为由许可性规定改为强制性要求。自本法规首次颁布以来,随着各州失业补偿业务的发展,各州面临的欺诈事件日益增多,包括由有组织犯罪分子实施的复杂的跨州欺诈计划。在2019冠状病毒病(COVID-19)大流行期间,欺诈活动大幅增加,根据劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)的估计,这使失业补偿项目损失数十亿美元。劳工部监察长办公室在六个高风险领域中查明了456亿美元的潜在欺诈性失业补偿项目福利,[9]并估计在大流行期间,失业补偿项目中可能有1910亿美元的福利被不当支付,其中很大一部分可归因于欺诈。[10]
The Department amends 20 CFR part 603 to require, rather than permit, the disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. Since this regulation was first promulgated, and as State UC operations have evolved, States have faced increased fraud incidents, including sophisticated multistate fraud schemes by organized criminals. During the COVID-19 pandemic, there was a sizable increase in fraudulent activity costing UC programs billions of dollars according to estimates by DOL-OIG. DOL-OIG identified $45.6 billion in potentially fraudulent benefits under UC programs in six high-risk areas,[9] and it estimated $191 billion in benefits under UC programs during the pandemic period could have been paid improperly, with a significant portion attributable to fraud.[10]
部长必须拥有必要的工具,以确保失业补偿项目的管理符合联邦法律的要求。为进行失业补偿项目监督和审计而强制向包括劳工部监察长办公室在内的联邦官员披露保密的失业补偿信息,对于确保失业补偿项目的管理符合联邦法律以及识别和预防欺诈至关重要。将此类披露要求编入法规,可使联邦官员充分利用失业补偿项目监督和审计手段,追究各州失业补偿机构在识别和预防失业补偿项目欺诈方面的责任。
The Secretary must have the tools necessary to ensure that UC programs are administered consistent with the requirements of Federal law. Mandatory disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials, including DOL-OIG, for purposes of UC program oversight and audits is essential to ensure the UC program is being administered consistent with Federal law and to identify and prevent fraud. Codifying the requirement for such disclosures allows Federal officials to fully utilize UC program oversight and audits to hold State UC agencies accountable in identifying and preventing fraud in UC programs.
2. 已考虑的备选方案
2. Alternatives Considered
概述监管分析最佳实践的《行政管理和预算局第A-4号通告》要求各机构分析拟议监管行动的合理监管备选方案。据此,劳工部就为失业补偿项目监督和审计目的向联邦官员披露保密失业补偿信息事宜考虑了两种备选方案。
OMB Circular A-4, which outlines best practices in regulatory analysis, directs agencies to analyze reasonable regulatory alternatives to the proposed regulatory action. Accordingly, the Department considered two alternatives regarding disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.
第一种备选方案是对第603部分进行全面更新,包括要求各州向劳工部监察长办公室披露保密失业补偿信息,用于失业补偿项目监督和审计。劳工部所考虑的、并在2024年秋季《监管与去监管行动统一议程》中所述的全面更新,原本将包括针对多年来利益相关方所提出问题的其他修订,包括解决有关在整个劳动力系统中共享信息的问题、在劳工部内各机构之间为分析和评估目的共享信息的可行性及使用场景、向联邦承认的印第安部落披露保密失业补偿信息的可行性、数据仓储,以及承包商和分包商的使用等问题。尽管劳工部从与利益相关方的接触及2023年信息征询书(RFI)中获得了宝贵的信息,但劳工部最终认为,当前最关键的步骤是通过确保包括劳工部监察长办公室在内的联邦官员能够获取数据以开展监督和打击欺诈,来解决失业补偿项目中的欺诈问题。
The first alternative was to make comprehensive updates to part 603, including to require States to disclose confidential UC information to DOL-OIG for purposes of UC program oversight and audits. The comprehensive updates the Department considered and that were described in the Fall 2024 Unified Agenda of Regulatory and Deregulatory Actions would have included additional amendments regarding issues raised by stakeholders over the years, including addressing questions around sharing information across the workforce system, the permissibility and use cases of sharing information with agencies within the Department for analysis and evaluation, the permissibility of disclosing confidential UC information to federally recognized Indian Tribes, data warehousing, and the use of contractors and subcontractors. While the Department gained valuable information from the engagement with stakeholders and the 2023 RFI, the Department ultimately decided that the most critical step needed at this time was to address fraud in UC programs by ensuring that Federal officials, including DOL-OIG, have access to data to conduct oversight and combat fraud.
所考虑的另一种方案是对第603部分中有关向包括劳工部监察长办公室在内的联邦官员披露保密失业补偿信息的规定不作任何修改。劳工部决定不维持现状,因为欺诈事件的增加以及复杂的跨州欺诈计划要求劳工部立即采取行动,以消除可能出现的任何障碍,从而确保联邦官员目前及未来继续能够获取保密失业补偿信息用于失业补偿项目监督和审计,这些障碍包括:(1)此类披露要求受限于个别拨款的有时限的条件时;或(2)各州因披露属于许可性规定而选择不予披露时。因此,推进此项监管变更将加强联邦官员督促各州确保福利仅支付给合格个人、并对不合格者不予支付的整体项目完整性,从而有助于实现《社会保障法》第303(a)(1)条中关于准确、及时支付的法定要求。
Another option considered was to make no change to part 603 concerning disclosure of confidential UC information to Federal officials, including to DOL-OIG. The Department decided against maintaining the status quo because the rise of fraud incidents and sophisticated multistate fraud schemes demand immediate action by the Department to ensure that Federal officials continue to have the access now and into the future to confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits by removing any barriers that might arise: (1) when the requirement for such disclosure is subject to time-limited conditions of individual grants; or (2) States choosing not to disclose due to the disclosure being permissive. Proceeding with this regulatory change, thus, will strengthen the overall program integrity for Federal officials to hold States accountable for ensuring benefits are paid only to eligible individuals and withheld from those who are ineligible, thus assisting with the statutory requirement for accurate and timely payments under sec. 303(a)(1), SSA.
3. 经济分析
3. Economic Analysis
劳工部（Department of Labor，以下简称劳工部）开展了一项经济分析，以确定本最终规则的成本，并考虑本规则项下的收益和转移支付的影响。劳工部认识到，本规则可能产生与所需的一次性技术升级、合规成本以及与数据提交相关的经常性成本有关的潜在成本。然而，由于缺乏可用数据，劳工部无法对这些成本进行估算。尽管劳工部通过对九个州的调查、2023年信息征询通知（RFI）以及2025年8月的拟议规则制定通知（NPRM）努力收集信息，但仍未获得足以量化现实成本估算的数据。各州失业补偿（UC）机构在很大程度上已经向劳工部及劳工部监察长办公室（DOL-OIG）披露本最终规则所规范化的信息，从而将任何新增成本降至最低。
The Department conducted an economic analysis to determine the costs of this final rule and to consider the benefits and the impact of transfers under this rule. The Department recognizes potential costs of this rule for required one-time technological upgrades, compliance costs, and recurring costs related to data submission. However, data availability prevents the Department from estimating these costs. Despite its efforts to gather information through a survey of nine States, the 2023 RFI, and the August 2025 NPRM, the Department did not receive enough data that would allow for the quantification of realistic cost estimates. State UC agencies are largely already disclosing the information that this final rule codifies to the Department and DOL-OIG, minimizing any new costs.
此外，本最终规则对53个州失业补偿机构施加了一次性的法规熟悉成本。这些成本与州失业补偿机构工作人员审阅新法规并进行内部讨论相关，其估算方法采用了劳工统计局（BLS）职业就业与工资统计（OEWS）数据以及对熟悉本规则所需时间的估算。
Additionally, this final rule imposes a one-time regulatory familiarization cost on the 53 State UC agencies. These costs are associated with State UC agency staff reviewing the new regulation and conducting internal discussions and are determined using BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) data and estimates of the time required to become familiar with this rule.
劳工部认为本最终规则的收益是实质性的，包括确保项目的完整性，以及建立和维护公众对失业补偿制度的信任。具体收益包括加强欺诈预防、识别和调查能力，并通过及时审计和评估提供有力的监督和问责。由于数据可用性和不确定性的限制，劳工部无法对本规则的收益进行量化。
The Department considers the benefits of this final rule to be substantial, including ensuring program integrity and building and maintaining public trust in the UC system. Specific benefits include enhancement of fraud prevention, identification, and investigation and providing strong oversight and accountability through timely audits and evaluations. Data availability and uncertainty limit the Department's ability to quantify the benefits of this rule.

B. Regulatory Flexibility Act
《监管灵活性法案》（RFA），《美国法典》第5编第6章，要求本部门评估本规则对小实体的经济影响。该法案将小实体定义为包括小型企业、小型组织（包括非营利组织）以及小型政府辖区。本部门必须确定本规则是否对大量此类小实体造成重大经济影响。本部门认定本规则（印刷页第58608页）不直接规制任何小实体，因此对小实体的任何监管影响均属间接影响。据此，本部门确定本规则不会在《监管灵活性法案》所指含义范围内对大量小实体产生重大经济影响。
The Regulatory Flexibility Act (RFA), 5 U.S.C. chapter 6, requires the Department to evaluate the economic impact of this rule on small entities. The RFA defines small entities to include small businesses, small organizations (including not-for-profit organizations), and small governmental jurisdictions. The Department must determine whether this rule imposes a significant economic impact on a substantial number of such small entities. The Department concludes that this rule ( printed page 58608) does not regulate any small entities directly, so any regulatory effect on small entities will be indirect. Accordingly, the Department has determined this rule will not have a significant economic impact on a substantial number of small entities within the meaning of the RFA.

C. Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995
1995年《文书削减法案》（PRA），《美国法典》第44编第3501条及其后条款，其宗旨包括最大限度地减轻受影响实体的文书负担。该法案要求机构在采用或修订信息收集之前采取某些行动，包括就信息收集事项的摘要以及拟议使用该信息的必要性和用途的简要说明发布公告以征求公众意见。
The purposes of the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 (PRA), 44 U.S.C. 3501 et seq., include minimizing the paperwork burden on affected entities. The PRA requires certain actions before an agency can adopt or revise a collection of information, including publishing for public comment a summary of the collection of information and a brief description of the need for and proposed use of the information.
作为持续努力减少文书工作和填报人负担的一部分，本部门依照《文书削减法案》开展预先审批咨询程序，为公众和联邦机构提供就拟议及持续性信息收集事项发表评论的机会。参见《美国法典》第44编第3506条(c)(2)(A)款。此项活动有助于确保公众理解本部门的收集说明，填报人能够以所需格式提供所要求的数据，将报告负担（时间和财务资源）降至最低，收集工具能够被清楚理解，并使本部门能够恰当评估收集要求对填报人的影响。
As part of its continuing effort to reduce paperwork and respondent burden, the Department conducts a preclearance consultation program to provide the public and Federal agencies with an opportunity to comment on proposed and continuing collections of information in accordance with the PRA. See44 U.S.C. 3506(c)(2)(A). This activity helps to ensure that the public understands the Department's collection instructions, respondents can provide the requested data in the desired format, reporting burden (time and financial resources) is minimized, collection instruments are clearly understood, and the Department can properly assess the impact of collection requirements on respondents.
除非经管理与预算办公室（OMB）依照《文书削减法案》批准并显示现行有效的OMB控制编号，联邦机构不得开展或资助信息收集活动。除非显示现行有效的OMB控制编号，公众亦无需对信息收集作出回应。此外，尽管存在其他法律规定，若某项信息收集未显示现行有效的OMB控制编号，任何人均不因未遵守该信息收集要求而受到处罚（《美国法典》第44编第3512条）。
A Federal agency may not conduct or sponsor a collection of information unless it is approved by OMB under the PRA and it displays a currently valid OMB control number. The public is also not required to respond to a collection of information unless it displays a currently valid OMB control number. In addition, notwithstanding any other provisions of law, no person will be subject to penalty for failing to comply with a collection of information if the collection of information does not display a currently valid OMB control number (44 U.S.C. 3512).
意见：一位个人评论者表示，缺乏成本效益分析对《文书削减法案》具有相关影响。
Comments: An individual commenter said that the lack of a cost-benefit analysis has implications for the PRA.
答复:本最终规则不要求收集受《文书削减法》(PRA)约束的任何特定数据。如果根据本授权收集任何信息,该收集行为将受《文书削减法》的约束,并须符合任何适用的要求。
Response: This final rule does not require any specific data collection subject to the PRA. If any information is to be collected under this authority, it would be subject to the PRA and must meet any applicable requirements.
D. 第13132号行政命令(联邦制)
D. Executive Order 13132 (Federalism)
第13132号行政命令《联邦制》,64 FR 43255(1999年8月10日),对制定和实施优先于州法律的政策或法规,或具有联邦制影响的政策或法规的联邦机构施加了某些要求。第13132号行政命令要求各机构审查支持任何将限制各州政策制定自由裁量权的行动的宪法和法定权力,并仔细评估采取此类行动的必要性。第13132号行政命令还要求各机构建立问责机制,以确保州和地方官员能够对具有联邦制影响的监管政策的制定提供有意义且及时的意见。劳工部已依据这些要求对本最终规则进行了审查,并得出结论认为本规则符合第13132号行政命令的要求。
E.O. 13132, “Federalism,” 64 FR 43255 (Aug. 10, 1999), imposes certain requirements on Federal agencies formulating and implementing policies or regulations that preempt State law or that have Federalism implications. E.O. 13132 requires agencies to examine the constitutional and statutory authority supporting any action that would limit the policymaking discretion of the States and to carefully assess the necessity for such actions. E.O. 13132 also requires agencies to have an accountable process to ensure meaningful and timely input by State and local officials in the development of regulatory policies that have Federalism implications. The Department has reviewed this final rule in light of these requirements and has concluded that it meets the requirements of E.O. 13132.
因此,劳工部已对本最终规则进行了审查,并得出结论认为,如第13132号行政命令所述,本规则制定对各州、联邦政府与各州之间的关系,或各级政府之间权力与责任的分配均不产生实质性直接影响。因此,劳工部得出结论认为,本最终规则不具有足以要求采取进一步机构行动或分析的联邦制影响。
Accordingly, the Department has reviewed this final rule and has concluded that the rulemaking has no substantial direct effects on States, the relationship between the Federal Government and the States, or the distribution of power and responsibilities among the various levels of government as described by E.O. 13132. Therefore, the Department has concluded that this final rule does not have a sufficient federalism implication to require further agency action or analysis.

The disclosures required by this final rule are already permissible disclosures under the existing regulations. Since this final rule does not substantially alter the scope of the disclosures, E.O. 13132 does not require further consultation with State and local officials beyond the normal comment period within the rulemaking process. Such disclosures are part of the normal business of the administration of UC programs and would be allowable uses of UC administrative funding.
本最终规则也未修改适用于强制性披露的数据使用保护或限制。本规则仅是将州被允许作出的披露,改为州必须作出的披露。如上文所述,劳工部拥有将这些披露从许可性披露改为强制性披露的法定权力。
This final rule also does not modify the protections or limitations on use of data that are applicable to required disclosures. This rule merely changes the disclosure from one that a State is permitted to make to one that the State is required to make. As discussed above, the Department has the statutory authority to change these disclosures from permissive disclosures to mandatory disclosures
尽管本规则不具有联邦制影响,且在规则制定程序的正常意见征询期之外不要求进行进一步协商,但劳工部仍然开展了此类协商。劳工部发布了《2023年信息征询通知》(RFI),其中指出,“劳工部正在考虑对20 CFR第603部分进行监管更新,以纳入要求各州向[劳工部监察长办公室(DOL-OIG)]披露保密失业补偿信息以供监督和审计的规定。”参见88 FR 47829(2023年7月25日)。《2023年信息征询通知》就该主题提出了问题,并为受监管群体提供了发表意见的机会。此外,劳工部与各州之间的独特关系要求与州合作伙伴进行定期协商与协作,包括涉及根据第603部分披露保密失业补偿信息的相关事宜。
Although this rule does not have Federalism implications and does not require further consultation with State and local officials beyond the normal comment period within the rulemaking process, the Department has nevertheless pursued such consultation. The Department published the 2023 RFI, which said “[t]he Department is considering a regulatory update to 20 CFR part 603 that would incorporate a requirement for States to disclose confidential UC information to [DOL-OIG] for oversight and audits.” See88 FR 47829 (July 25, 2023). The 2023 RFI included questions on the topic and afforded the regulated community an opportunity to comment. Additionally, the Department's unique relationship with the States requires regular consultation and collaboration with State partners, including on matters pertaining to disclosures of confidential UC information under part 603.
E. 《无资金授权改革法》
E. Unfunded Mandates Reform Act
1995年《无资金授权改革法》(UMRA)(Pub. L. 104-4)第二章要求各联邦机构评估联邦监管行动对州、地方和部落政府以及私营部门的影响。2 U.S.C. 1531。对于可能导致某项规则包含联邦授权,从而可能使州、地方和部落政府合计或私营部门在任何一年内(每年根据通货膨胀进行调整)支出1亿美元或以上的监管行动,UMRA要求联邦机构发布书面声明,估算由此产生的成本、收益以及对国民经济的其他影响。(2 U.S.C. 1532(a)、(b))。关于小型政府,UMRA要求机构制定计划,在确立任何可能对潜在受影响的小型政府产生重大或独特影响的要求之前,向其发出通知并给予及时提供意见的机会。此类计划必须包括一个有效程序,以允许州、地方和部落政府的民选官员就(印刷页第58609页)重大联邦政府间授权及时提供意见。
Title II of the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995 (UMRA) (Pub. L. 104-4) requires each Federal agency to assess the effects of Federal regulatory actions on State, local, and Tribal governments and the private sector. 2 U.S.C. 1531. For a regulatory action likely to result in a rule that includes any Federal mandate that may cause the expenditure by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector of $100 million or more in any one year (adjusted annually for inflation), UMRA requires a Federal agency to publish a written statement that estimates the resulting costs, benefits, and other effects on the national economy. (2 U.S.C. 1532(a), (b)). Relating to small governments, UMRA requires an agency plan for giving notice and opportunity for timely input to potentially affected small governments before establishing any requirements that might significantly or uniquely affect them. Such plan must include an effective process to permit timely input by elected officers of State, local, and Tribal governments on ( printed page 58609) significant Federal intergovernmental mandates.
劳工部根据UMRA及其政策声明对本最终规则进行了审查,并确定本最终规则不包含联邦政府间授权,预计也不会要求州、地方和部落政府合计或私营部门在任何一年内支出1亿美元或以上。此外,由于本规则未施加联邦政府间授权,本规则不会对小型政府产生重大或独特影响。因此,UMRA的要求不适用于本规则。
The Department examined this final rule according to UMRA and its statement of policy and determined that this final rule does not contain a Federal intergovernmental mandate, nor is it expected to require expenditures of $100 million or more in any one year by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector. Furthermore, because this rule does not impose a Federal intergovernmental mandate, this rule does not significantly or uniquely affect small governments. As a result, the requirements of UMRA do not apply.

To the commenter's concern that “[c]ertifying compliance without quantification is arbitrary and capricious under the APA,” the Department notes that the scope of this final rule is limited. All State UC agencies already provide confidential UC information for purposes of UC program oversight and audits to Federal officials, including the Department and DOL-OIG, through permissible disclosures. Regarding certification under UMRA, the Department conducted an economic analysis to determine the costs of this final rule as provided in section IV.A.3 of this preamble. Although data availability prevents the Department from fully estimating these costs, the Department does not expect this final rule to require expenditures of $100 million or more in any one year by State, local, and Tribal governments, in the aggregate, or by the private sector. States are currently processing permissible disclosure requests from Federal officials, so any additional costs would arise from the change to these being required disclosures, which are expected to be roughly equivalent to the current number of permissible disclosures for purposes of UC program oversight and audits.
基于上述原因,《无资金授权法》第二编的要求不适用于本规则。因此,劳工部未依据《无资金授权法》编写相关声明。
For these reasons, the requirements of title II of UMRA do not apply. Therefore, DOL has not prepared a statement under UMRA.
